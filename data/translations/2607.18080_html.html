Haochen Zhao1,2, Yongxiu Xu1,2\corresponding, Xinkui Lin1,2, Dong Xie3,
Jiarui Lu1,2, Yuqi Qian1,2, Yubin Wang1,2, Hongbo Xu1, Gaopeng Gou1
摘要Abstract

多模态视频错误信息检测通常被表述为整体视频理解任务，其中整个视频及其相关内容在一次传递中进行处理和判断。然而，现实世界的错误信息通常表现出稀疏且组合的证据结构：可靠的决策可能仅取决于几个耦合的线索，而大多数视频内容提供的附加信息有限。因此，详尽的多模态推理可能会引入大量冗余和模糊的决定性证据。这促使证据获取与验证脱钩：首先识别稀疏的、与决策相关的线索，然后根据获取的证据判断准确性。据此，我们建议Multimodal video misinformation detection is commonly formulated as a holistic video-understanding task, where the entire video and its associated content are processed and judged in a single pass. However, real-world misinformation often exhibits a sparse and compositional evidence structure: a reliable decision may depend on only a few coupled clues, while most video content contributes limited additional information. Exhaustive multimodal reasoning may therefore introduce substantial redundancy and obscure decisive evidence. This motivates decoupling evidence acquisition from verification: first identifying sparse, decision-relevant clues and then judging veracity based on the acquired evidence. An evidence-seeking agent actively explores the available multimodal evidence and constructs a compact evidence package, which is then used by a verifier to determine veracity. The agent is trained with supervised evidence-seeking trajectories and an evidence-aware reinforcement learning objective that promotes informative evidence acquisition while discouraging unnecessary or invalid interactions. Experiments on multiple video misinformation benchmarks show that SIEVE consistently outperforms the evaluated baselines and supports reliable verification using compact evidence packages. Moreover, the resulting acquisition process provides an explicit and inspectable evidence trail, improving the transparency and groundedness of multimodal misinformation detection.

简介Introduction

视频已成为网络信息消费的主导媒介，尤其是在TikTok、YouTube等短视频平台上，用户生成的视频可以快速创作并广泛传播Videos have become a dominant medium for online information consumption, especially on short-video platforms such as TikTok and YouTube, where user-generated videos can be rapidly created and widely disseminated(Bu et al. 2023; Venkatagiri et al. 2023)。然而，视频创作和传播的便利性也加速了错误信息的传播，威胁到在线信息生态系统和公共话语的可信度. However, the same ease of video creation and dissemination also accelerates the spread of misinformation, threatening the credibility of online information ecosystems and public discourse(Bu et al. 2023; Lazer et al. 2018)。由于在线视频的数量和速度远远超出了人工事实核查的能力，自动视频错误信息检测已成为越来越重要的研究问题. Since the volume and velocity of online videos far exceed the capacity of manual fact-checking, automatic video misinformation detection has become an increasingly important research problem(Venkatagiri et al. 2023; Bu et al. 2023).

与基于文本或图像的错误信息相比，视频错误信息更具挑战性，因为视频包含异构的视觉、听觉、文本和社交信号，以及时间动态和微妙的跨模式不一致Compared with text- or image-based misinformation, video misinformation is more challenging because videos contain heterogeneous visual, acoustic, textual, and social signals, as well as temporal dynamics and subtle cross-modal inconsistencies(Bu et al. 2023; Shang et al. 2021; Liu et al. 2023)。早期的视频错误信息检测方法主要依赖于特定于任务的编码器和多模态融合策略，例如对跨模态相关性、社会背景、创作过程模式或事件相关视频之间的邻域关系进行建模. Early video misinformation detection methods mainly rely on task-specific encoders and multimodal fusion strategies, such as modeling cross-modal correlations, social context, creative-process patterns, or neighborhood relations among event-related videos(Choi and Ko 2021; Liu et al. 2023; Qi et al. 2023a; Li et al. 2025; Bu et al. 2024; Qi et al. 2023b)。尽管这些方法取得了有希望的结果，但它们通常被优化为端到端判别分类器，为开放世界验证场景中的显式、可解释和灵活的多步骤证据推理提供有限的支持. Although these methods have achieved promising results, they are typically optimized as end-to-end discriminative classifiers, offering limited support for explicit, interpretable, and flexible multi-step evidence reasoning in open-world verification scenarios(Wang et al. 2025b; Zheng et al. 2025; Wang et al. 2026; Lang et al. 2026).

最近，视觉语言模型和多模态大语言模型利用其广泛的多模态知识和推理能力，在虚假短视频新闻检测方面表现出了强大的潜力Recently, vision-language models and multimodal large language models have shown strong potential for fake short-video news detection by leveraging their broad multimodal knowledge and reasoning capabilities(Zheng et al. 2025; Wang et al. 2025b; Hong et al. 2025; Zhang et al. 2025)。一些方法引入思维链推理来提高可解释性. Some methods introduce chain-of-thought reasoning to improve explainability(Hong et al. 2025)，而其他人则进一步探索用于视频错误信息检测的长推理轨迹和强化学习, while others further explore long reasoning trajectories and reinforcement learning for video misinformation detection(Zhang et al. 2025; Li et al. 2026)。尽管如此，大多数现有方法仍然遵循单遍或固定深度推理范例，其中模型直接从预先提取的输入中预测标签。当关键证据稀疏、支离破碎或不充分时，此类模型可能依赖于内部假设，而不是主动获取有针对性的证据，从而导致决策不可靠或缺乏依据. Nevertheless, most existing methods still follow a single-pass or fixed-depth inference paradigm, where the model directly predicts a label from pre-extracted inputs. When critical evidence is sparse, fragmented, or insufficient, such models may rely on internal assumptions rather than actively acquiring targeted evidence, leading to unreliable or poorly grounded decisions(Yao et al. 2023; Zhang et al. 2023; Li et al. 2026; Wang et al. 2026).

因此，我们将视频错误信息检测制定为预算受限的证据寻求和验证过程，其中获取预算鼓励代理优先考虑一小组潜在的决定性和补充线索，而不是详尽地阅读所有可用内容。具体来说，平台提供的“描述”字段被视为初始声明上下文，而不使用数据集提供的“事件”字段。寻证代理有选择地读取视频衍生的证据通道，包括视觉帧、OCR 和 ASR，并构建一个紧凑的证据包，其中可以联合解释互补的线索。然后验证者根据描述和获得的证据做出判断。这种设计将证据获取与验证分离，并公开所获取的证据及其构成基础以供检查。Accordingly, we formulate video misinformation detection as a budget-constrained evidence-seeking and verification process, where an acquisition budget encourages the agent to prioritize small sets of potentially decisive and complementary clues rather than exhaustively reading all available content. Specifically, the platform-provided Description field is treated as the initial claim context, while the dataset-provided Event field is not used. An evidence-seeking agent selectively reads video-derived evidence channels, including visual frames, OCR, and ASR, and constructs a compact evidence package in which complementary clues can be jointly interpreted. A verifier then makes the judgment from the Description and the acquired evidence. This design decouples evidence acquisition from verification and exposes both the acquired evidence and its compositional basis for inspection.

相关工作Related Work

现有的视频错误信息检测方法通常将任务制定为整体多模态分类，联合编码视觉、文本、声音和社交信号以进行预测。早期研究调查了假新闻或 COVID-19 错误信息视频的主题建模、对抗性学习和特定领域的多模态融合Existing video misinformation detection methods usually formulate the task as holistic multimodal classification, jointly encoding visual, textual, acoustic, and social signals for prediction. Early studies investigate topic modeling, adversarial learning, and domain-specific multimodal fusion for fake news or COVID-19 misinformation videos(Choi and Ko 2021; Shang et al. 2021; Liu et al. 2023)。后来的作品建立了短视频基准并引入了更丰富的上下文建模。 FakeSV 结合多模态内容和社交背景来检测虚假短视频新闻. Later works build short-video benchmarks and introduce richer contextual modeling. FakeSV incorporates multimodal content and social context for fake short-video news detection(Qi et al. 2023a)，而后续方法则利用事件相关视频之间的邻域关系, while subsequent methods exploit neighborhood relations among event-related videos(Qi et al. 2023b), 创作过程线索, creative-process cues(Bu et al. 2024)，或异质社会潜在结构, or heterogeneous social latent structures(Li et al. 2025)。尽管取得了这些进步，大多数方法还是通过单个判别管道处理预先收集的视频输入，为明确识别哪些稀疏多模态线索足以进行接地验证提供了有限的支持。. Despite these advances, most methods process pre-collected video inputs through a single discriminative pipeline, offering limited support for explicitly identifying which sparse multimodal clues are sufficient for grounded verification.

最近的工作利用视觉语言模型和多模态大语言模型来改进错误信息检测中的语义推理和可解释性。基本原理增强的 VLM 方法从直接预测转向面向分析的检测Recent works leverage vision-language models and multimodal large language models to improve semantic reasoning and explainability in misinformation detection. Rationale-augmented VLM methods move from direct prediction to analysis-oriented detection(Zheng et al. 2025)，而 FakeSV-VLM 通过渐进式专家和事件级一致性检查来调整 VLM, while FakeSV-VLM adapts VLMs through progressive experts and event-level consistency checking(Wang et al. 2025b)。 ExMRD 引入了精炼-检索-推理链来生成可解释的微视频谣言预测. ExMRD introduces a refining–retrieving–reasoning chain to produce explainable micro-video rumor predictions(Hong et al. 2025)，Fact-R1进一步结合长链推理、偏好对齐和强化学习进行视频错误信息检测, and Fact-R1 further combines long-chain reasoning, preference alignment, and reinforcement learning for video misinformation detection(Zhang et al. 2025)。最近的代理框架，例如 CSI 和 FactGuard，使用多代理调查或工具增强推理来增强证据获取和决策可靠性. More recent agentic frameworks, such as CSI and FactGuard, use multi-agent investigation or tool-augmented reasoning to strengthen evidence acquisition and decision reliability(Wang et al. 2026; Li et al. 2026)。检索引导的适应也被探索用于测试时的假新闻视频检测. Retrieval-guided adaptation has also been explored for test-time fake news video detection(Lang et al. 2026)。尽管取得了这些进步，大多数方法仍然对预先构建的全局输入或外部检索的信息进行推理，而不是学习如何从密集的多模态视频内容中寻找紧凑的、本地化的和与声明相关的证据。. Despite these advances, most methods still reason over preconstructed global inputs or externally retrieved information, rather than learning how to seek compact, localized, and claim-relevant evidence from dense multimodal video content.

我们的工作还涉及以证据为中心的事实验证和选择性感知。文本事实验证长期以来一直强调在做出主张层面的判断之前检索支持证据的重要性Our work is also related to evidence-centered fact verification and selective perception. Textual fact verification has long emphasized the importance of retrieving supporting evidence before making a claim-level judgment(Thorne et al. 2018)，后来的工作表明，证据检索应该针对下游验证效用进行优化，而不仅仅是相关性, and later work shows that evidence retrieval should be optimized for downstream verification utility rather than relevance alone(Zhang et al. 2023)。在语言代理研究中，ReAct 表明，交错的推理和动作使模型能够获取缺失的信息，而不是依赖于内部假设. In language-agent research, ReAct demonstrates that interleaving reasoning and actions enables models to acquire missing information instead of relying on internal assumptions(Yao et al. 2023)。在视频理解中，自适应帧选择方法表明，许多视频任务可以通过仅观察信息帧的子集来解决. In video understanding, adaptive frame selection methods show that many video tasks can be solved by observing only a subset of informative frames(Wu et al. 2019)。与这些工作不同的是，SIEVE 将视频错误信息检测作为一个预算内的交互式证据寻求问题进行研究。它仅使用平台提供的“描述”字段作为初始声明上下文，显式排除数据集提供的“事件”字段，并在基于证据的验证之前主动从 OCR、ASR 和视觉通道获取紧凑的声明相关证据，将任务从固定输入分类转变为预算主动证据获取。. Unlike these lines of work, SIEVE studies video misinformation detection as a budgeted interactive evidence-seeking problem. It uses only the platform-provided Description field as the initial claim context, explicitly excludes the dataset-provided Event field, and actively acquires compact, claim-relevant evidence from OCR, ASR, and visual channels before evidence-based verification, shifting the task from fixed-input classification to budgeted active evidence acquisition.
