摘要Abstract

贡献：Contribution:本文提出了一种基于课程即代码范式的六阶段人工智能（AI）辅助教学设计架构。它将生成式 AI 与 LaTeX 和 Python 集成在一起，自动为科学、技术、工程和数学 (STEM) 教育创建可重复、视觉一致且技术精确的材料。This paper presents a six-phase Artificial Intelligence (AI)-assisted instructional design architecture based on the Curriculum as Code paradigm. It integrates Generative AI with LaTeX and Python to automate the creation of reproducible, visually consistent, and technically precise materials for Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) education.背景：Background:定制教学材料的创建，特别是针对主动学习环境的定制材料，给教师带来了沉重的工作负担。标准演示工具缺乏对复杂技术内容的强大支持，而当前的人工智能应用程序经常出现幻觉，无法将教学创作过程形式化，从而限制了它们在严格学术设计中的实用性。The creation of customized instructional materials, particularly for active learning environments, imposes a heavy workload on faculty. Standard presentation tools lack robust support for complex technical content, while current AI applications often suffer from hallucinations and fail to formalize the instructional authoring process, limiting their utility for rigorous academic design.预期成果：Intended Outcomes:所提出的框架旨在减少备课时间，同时确保数学准确性、严格遵守机构视觉识别，并通过明确的规则保存教师的隐性教学知识。The proposed framework aims to reduce class preparation time while ensuring mathematical accuracy, strict adherence to institutional visual identity, and the preservation of the instructor’s tacit pedagogical knowledge through explicit rules.应用设计：Application Design:该解决方案由结构化的六阶段管道组成，用系统的工作流程取代临时提示工程。它利用基于文本的界面和代码驱动的生成（用于结构化幻灯片的 LaTeX/Beamer 和用于技术图形的 Python），受明确的教学约束、上下文校准和自动审核周期的约束。The solution consists of a structured six-phase pipeline that replaces ad-hoc prompt engineering with a systematic workflow. It utilizes text-based interfaces and code-driven generation (LaTeX/Beamer for structured slides and Python for technical figures), governed by explicit pedagogical constraints, contextual calibrations, and automated review cycles.调查结果：Findings:该架构在要求严格的基于项目的学习 (PBL) 环境中经过一年的实施，在 8 个不同的学术模块和 28 个项目环境中进行了验证，与教师工作量的大幅减少有关。生成的资产经过了独立的同行评审，并由六名不同的教员成功部署在课堂上，证实该方法捕获了隐性知识，其规模超出了单个作者的范围。此外，根据 600 多名学生自愿评估，这些材料获得了一致的高质量评分，从 8.5 到 9.9（满分 10 分）不等。结果表明，技术可重复性高、AI 幻觉最小化以及持续的教学和视觉保真度，表明其在广泛的 STEM 教育应用中的可行性。Validated over a one-year implementation period across 8 distinct academic modules and 28 project contexts in a demanding Project-Based Learning (PBL) environment, the architecture was associated with a substantial decrease in instructor workload. The generated assets underwent independent peer review and were successfully deployed in the classroom by six different faculty members, confirming that the methodology captures tacit knowledge and scales beyond a single author. Furthermore, based on over 600 voluntary student evaluations, the materials achieved consistently high quality ratings, ranging from 8.5 to 9.9 out of 10. The results indicate high technical reproducibility, minimized AI hallucinations, and sustained pedagogical and visual fidelity, suggesting its viability for broad STEM educational applications.

I 简介Introduction

主动学习方法，特别是基于项目的学习（PBL）Active learning methodologies, particularly Project-Based Learning (PBL)[1, 2, 3]和翻转课堂and Flipped Classroom[4, 5]通过将教师的角色从传统的知识传播者转变为学习的促进者，从根本上重新定义了工程教育。这种教学范式要求教学材料高度定制，深深扎根于实际项目环境，并精心设计以促进学生的自主性和批判性思维。, have fundamentally redefined engineering education by shifting the instructor’s role from a traditional transmitter of knowledge to a facilitator of learning. This pedagogical paradigm requires instructional materials to be highly customized, deeply anchored in practical project contexts, and deliberately designed to promote student autonomy and critical thinking.

传统的可视化创作工具，通常称为所见即所得工具（Traditional visual authoring tools, commonly referred to as WYSIWYG tools (所见即所得What You See Is What You Get），为通用文档和演示材料提供直观的界面，但为复杂的数学符号、算法伪代码和结构化科学内容提供有限的本机支持。因此，教师经常花费大量精力手动调整格式并保持幻灯片之间的一致性，从而减少了教学设计的时间), provide intuitive interfaces for general-purpose documents and presentation materials but offer limited native support for complex mathematical notation, algorithmic pseudocode, and structured scientific content. As a result, instructors often spend considerable effort manually adjusting formatting and maintaining consistency across slides, reducing the time available for instructional design[6].

虽然生成人工智能（GenAI）的出现为教育技术提供了有前途的途径，但当前的文献主要强调孤立的内容生成While the advent of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) offers promising avenues for educational technology, the current literature predominantly emphasizes isolated content generation[7]、智能辅导系统, intelligent tutoring systems[8]，以及个性化的学习路径, and personalized learning paths[9]。教学创作流程的形式化方面存在明显差距。具体来说，人工智能辅助的工作流程很少受到关注，因为人工智能辅助的工作流程可以系统地加强教学的一致性，维护机构的视觉识别，并保证整个课程的数学和技术可重复性。. There is a noticeable gap regarding the formalization of instructional authoring processes. Specifically, there is little attention given to AI-assisted workflows that can systematically enforce pedagogical coherence, maintain institutional visual identity, and guarantee mathematical and technical reproducibility across an entire curriculum.

为了弥补这一差距，本文调查了以下研究问题（RQ）：To address this gap, this paper investigates the following research questions (RQs):

  • •

    问题一：RQ1:如何构建人工智能辅助的教学设计工作流程，以有效地将教师的隐性教学知识转化为明确的、可复制的规则，同时保持教学的一致性？How can an AI-assisted instructional design workflow be structured to effectively translate an instructor’s tacit pedagogical knowledge into explicit, replicable rules while preserving pedagogical consistency?

  • •

    问题2：RQ2:基于代码的创作工具与生成式人工智能的集成如何减轻技术幻觉并确保 STEM 教学材料的数学和视觉再现性？How does the integration of code-based authoring tools with Generative AI mitigate technical hallucinations and ensure the mathematical and visual reproducibility of STEM instructional materials?

  • •

    问题3：RQ3:当部署在高度情境化的学习环境中时，这种自动化架构对教师准备时间和机构视觉识别的维护有何实际影响？What is the practical impact of this automated architecture on faculty preparation time and the maintenance of institutional visual identity when deployed in a highly contextualized learning environment?

值得注意的是，由于实际项目中对理论概念的语境化的严格要求，PBL 模型本质上增加了教学设计的复杂性。由于所提出的架构成功地解决了这种高度复杂的场景，因此其在传统的基于讲座的课程中的应用变得更加简单。It is important to note that the PBL model inherently adds a layer of complexity to instructional design due to the strict requirement of contextualizing theoretical concepts within practical projects. Because the proposed architecture successfully resolves this highly complex scenario, its application to traditional, lecture-based classes becomes more straightforward.

本文的其余部分组织如下。第二节和第三节回顾了主动学习和课程即代码的理论基础和相关工作。第四节详细介绍了拟议的人工智能辅助教学设计工作流程，包括其阶段和使用 LaTeX 和 Python 的技术实现。第五部分介绍了一年实施期间收集的验证和结果。第六节讨论了实际影响和未来的工作，然后是第七节的结论。The remainder of this paper is organized as follows. Sections II and III review the theoretical foundations and related work regarding active learning and Curriculum as Code. Section IV details the proposed AI-assisted instructional design workflow, including its phases and technical implementation using LaTeX and Python. Section V presents the validation and results gathered over a one-year implementation period. Section VI discusses the practical implications and future work, followed by the concluding remarks in Section VII.

II 相关工作Related Work

生成式人工智能在教育中的应用在最近的文献中得到了广泛的探索，研究涵盖了智能辅导系统The application of Generative AI to education has been extensively explored in recent literature, with studies spanning intelligent tutoring systems[10]、自动评估, automated assessment[11]，以及个性化的学习途径, and personalized learning pathways[12, 13]。然而，这些贡献大部分集中在教育中面向学生的方面，通过对话、反馈或自适应内容交付来支持学习者。面向教师的方面，特别是教学材料制作的自动化，仍然相对未得到充分探索。本节回顾了与我们的提案相关的现有工作主体，围绕三个轴组织：（i）使用法学硕士来生成教育内容，（ii）用于教学设计的结构化提示工程框架，以及（iii）将基于代码的创作工具与生成模型集成的技术方法。. However, the majority of these contributions focus on the student-facing side of education, supporting learners through dialogue, feedback, or adaptive content delivery. The instructor-facing side, particularly the automation of instructional material production, remains comparatively underexplored. This section reviews the existing body of work relevant to our proposal, organized around three axes: (i) the use of LLMs for generating educational content, (ii) structured prompt engineering frameworks for instructional design, and (iii) technical approaches that integrate code-based authoring tools with generative models.

最近的几项研究调查了大型语言模型生成教育材料（例如讲座摘要）的能力Several recent studies have investigated the capacity of large language models to generate educational materials, such as lecture summaries[14], 选择题, multiple-choice questions[15]，以及 STEM 主题的解释性文本, and explanatory texts for STEM topics[16]。这些作品表明法学硕士可以制作出连贯且适合上下文的内容。然而，他们也一致报告了重大局限性：幻觉、事实不准确，以及生成过于通用或冗长的输出的倾向，需要大量的人工修改. These works demonstrate that LLMs can produce content that is often coherent and contextually appropriate. However, they also consistently report significant limitations: hallucinations, factual inaccuracies, and a tendency to generate overly generic or verbose outputs that require extensive human revision[17, 18]。在定量学科的背景下，数学符号和算法精度至关重要，这些限制尤其成问题。对生成内容的格式、布局和视觉层次结构缺乏细粒度的控制进一步限制了这些方法在专业教学设计中的适用性。. In the context of quantitative disciplines, where mathematical notation and algorithmic precision are paramount, these limitations are particularly problematic. The lack of fine-grained control over the format, layout, and visual hierarchy of generated content further restricts the applicability of these approaches to professional instructional design.

为了解决这些局限性，最近的研究转向促进工程作为提高法学硕士输出的可靠性和特异性的机制。研究提出了几次提示、思维链推理和角色扮演等技术来提高教育任务的表现To address these limitations, recent research has turned to prompt engineering as a mechanism for improving the reliability and specificity of LLM outputs. Studies have proposed techniques such as few-shot prompting, chain-of-thought reasoning, and role-playing to enhance performance in educational tasks[19]。尽管如此，这些努力中的大部分仍然. Nevertheless, most of these efforts remain临时的ad hoc并且针对具体问题，缺乏可以在不同课程或教学环境中复制的系统架构。值得注意的是，多阶段管道的概念已在通用 LLM 应用中提出，其中模型首先被校准，然后规划，最后执行and problem-specific, lacking a systematic architecture that can be replicated across different courses or pedagogical contexts. Notably, the concept of a multi-phase pipeline, where the model is first calibrated, then plans, and finally executes, has been proposed in general-purpose LLM applications[20, 21, 22]，但它对教学设计的适应，具有明确的教学约束和视觉一致性要求，在当前的文献中是不存在的。我们的工作通过提出一种端到端架构来解决这一差距，该架构将即时工程从短暂的实践转变为可重复的、整个课程范围的过程。, but its adaptation to instructional design, with explicit pedagogical constraints and visual consistency requirements, is absent from the current literature. Our work addresses this gap by proposing an end-to-end architecture that transforms prompt engineering from an ephemeral practice into a repeatable, curriculum-wide process.

并行的工作探索了基于代码的创作工具与生成式人工智能的集成。使用 LaTeX 和 Beamer 进行学术幻灯片制作已经很成熟A parallel body of work has explored the integration of code-based authoring tools with generative AI. The use of LaTeX and Beamer for academic slide production is well established[23]，最近的工作研究了使用 LLM 生成 LaTeX 代码的可行性, and recent efforts have investigated the feasibility of using LLMs to generate LaTeX code[7, 24, 25]。这些研究报告称，虽然法学硕士可以为简单文档生成语法正确的 LaTeX，但在面对自定义文档类、复杂宏或特定包交互时，它们常常会失败. These studies report that, while LLMs can produce syntactically correct LaTeX for simple documents, they often fail when faced with custom document classes, complex macros, or specific package interactions[24, 25]。同样，通过 Matplotlib 和 Seaborn 等 Python 库生成科学图形，在简单可视化方面也显示出了可喜的结果；然而，生成的代码通常需要手动优化才能正确配置可视化参数并生成出版质量的图形. Similarly, the generation of scientific figures through Python libraries such as Matplotlib and Seaborn has shown promising results for simple visualizations; however, the generated code often requires manual refinement to correctly configure visualization parameters and produce publication-quality figures[26]。我们的架构通过将图形的 Python 代码的生成与 LaTeX 内容分开，并通过使用讲师提供的示例进行几次校准来解决这个问题，以确保两种输出都遵循机构模板和教学规则。. Our architecture addresses this by separating the generation of Python code for figures from the LaTeX content, and by using few-shot calibration with instructor-provided examples to ensure that both outputs adhere to institutional templates and pedagogical rules.

据我们所知，之前的工作还没有提出一种全面的、可复制的架构，可以同时解决 STEM 主动学习教学材料制作中的教学校准、视觉一致性和技术可重复性问题。现有框架要么专注于内容生成，而不强制执行结构或视觉限制，要么依赖手动后处理，这会破坏自动化所带来的效率提升。我们的贡献是六阶段流程的形式化：情境注入、教学校准、技术校准、规划、迭代实施和审查。它系统地嵌入教师的隐性知识，应用课程即代码原则，并强制执行认知负荷理论约束，从而形成一个显着减少准备时间的工作流程，同时保持教学和数学的保真度。To the best of our knowledge, no previous work has proposed a comprehensive, replicable architecture that simultaneously addresses pedagogical calibration, visual consistency, and technical reproducibility in the production of instructional materials for active learning in STEM. Existing frameworks either focus on content generation without enforcing structural or visual constraints, or they rely on manual post-processing that undermines the efficiency gains promised by automation. Our contribution is the formalization of a six-phase pipeline: Context Injection, Pedagogical Calibration, Technical Calibration, Planning, Iterative Implementation, and Review. It systematically embeds the instructor’s tacit knowledge, applies Curriculum as Code principles, and enforces cognitive load theory constraints, resulting in a workflow that significantly reduces preparation time while preserving pedagogical and mathematical fidelity.

三、III理论基础Theoretical Foundation

所提出的架构基于五个相互关联的理论和技术支柱：主动学习方法、生成式人工智能在教育中的现状和局限性、课程即代码的概念、知识启发技术和认知负荷理论。这些基础共同证明了提示中嵌入的教学规则以及有关 LaTeX 和 Python 的技术选择的合理性。The proposed architecture is grounded on five interrelated theoretical and technical pillars: active learning methodologies, the current state and limitations of generative AI in education, the concept of Curriculum as Code, knowledge elicitation techniques, and cognitive load theory. Together, these foundations justify both the pedagogical rules embedded in the prompts and the technical choices regarding LaTeX and Python.

主动学习方法，特别是基于项目的学习（PBL）和翻转课堂，通过将教师的角色从单纯的知识传播者转变为自主学习的促进者，重塑了工程教育Active learning methodologies, particularly Project-Based Learning (PBL) and the Flipped Classroom, have reshaped engineering education by shifting the instructor’s role from a mere transmitter of knowledge to a facilitator of autonomous learning[1, 2, 3, 4, 5]。在 PBL 中，理论概念必须始终扎根于真实、实际的项目环境中，要求教学材料不仅在数学上精确，而且要深入情境化。同样，翻转课堂模式需要简洁、可供参考的材料，学生可以在课前自主学习，从而腾出面对面解决问题和讨论的时间。这些教学方法对教学设计提出了严格的要求：理论必须分解为易于理解的模块，直接与项目背景联系起来，并立即进行应用活动。这种结构化流程（理论、关键要点、活动和答案）正是我们的架构寻求自动执行的。. In PBL, theoretical concepts must be consistently anchored in authentic, practical project contexts, requiring instructional materials that are not only mathematically precise but also deeply contextualized. Similarly, the Flipped Classroom model demands concise, reference-ready materials that students can study autonomously before class, freeing in-person time for problem-solving and discussion. These pedagogical approaches impose strict requirements on instructional design: the theory must be fragmented into digestible modules, directly connected to the project context, and immediately followed by applied activities. This structured flow (theory, key takeaways, activity, and answer) is precisely what our architecture seeks to enforce automatically.

生成人工智能（GenAI），特别是大型语言模型的出现，为教育内容创作开辟了新的可能性。目前的研究已经探索了它们在智能辅导系统、个性化学习路径和自动问题生成中的应用。然而，当 GenAI 应用于定量学科教学材料的制作时，文献仍然存在显着的方法学局限性。两个关键问题是幻觉（生成数学上或事实上不正确的内容）和低再现性，即重复请求类似材料会产生不一致的输出The emergence of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI), particularly large language models, has opened new possibilities for educational content creation. Current studies have explored their use in intelligent tutoring systems, personalized learning paths, and automated question generation. However, the literature still presents significant methodological limitations when GenAI is applied to the production of instructional materials for quantitative disciplines. Two critical issues are hallucinations (the generation of mathematically or factually incorrect content) and low reproducibility, where repeated requests for similar materials yield inconsistent outputs[17, 18]。此外，大多数研究关注的是孤立的内容生成，而不是结构化的工作流程，后者可以系统地强化整个课程的教学一致性、机构视觉识别和技术严谨性。这一差距促使我们提出一种端到端架构，将即时工程从短暂的、临时的实践转变为可重复的工程过程。. Moreover, most studies focus on isolated content generation rather than on structured workflows that can systematically enforce pedagogical coherence, institutional visual identity, and technical rigor across an entire curriculum. This gap motivates our proposal of an end-to-end architecture that transforms prompt engineering from an ephemeral, ad-hoc practice into a repeatable engineering process.

为了解决可重复性和精度差距，我们采用了“课程即代码”的概念，这种方法通过使用编程和标记语言将教学材料表示为可版本化、可编译和可重复的工件。To address the reproducibility and precision gaps, we adopt the concept of Curriculum as Code, an approach that represents instructional materials as versionable, compilable, and reproducible artifacts through the use of programming and markup languages[27, 28, 29, 30]。在此框架中，幻灯片不是使用所见即所得工具进行编辑，而是使用 Beamer 使用 LaTeX 编写，而技术数据则通过 Python 脚本生成。这种方法具有三个基本优点：首先，数学表达式以印刷精度呈现，减少了标准演示工具中常见的错误；其次，源代码可以进行版本控制，允许协作改进和历史跟踪；第三，编译过程自动强制执行一致的视觉识别，使教师无需手动调整布局。将教育内容视为代码创建了一个环境，使自动化不仅成为可能，而且变得可靠。. In this framework, slides are not edited in WYSIWYG tools but written in LaTeX with Beamer, while technical figures are generated through Python scripts. This approach offers three fundamental advantages: first, mathematical expressions are rendered with typographic precision, mitigating errors common in standard presentation tools; second, the source code can be version-controlled, allowing for collaborative improvement and historical tracking; third, the compilation process automatically enforces a consistent visual identity, freeing the instructor from manual layout adjustments. Treating educational content as code creates an environment where automation becomes not only possible but also reliable.

IV-A 拟议的架构：人工智能辅助教学设计工作流程The Proposed Architecture: AI-Assisted Instructional Design Workflow

所提出的架构将教学材料的创建概念化为一个工程过程。该工作流程与软件工程中的持续集成和持续部署 (CI/CD) 管道直接类比，用系统化、阶段控制的管道取代了非结构化的人机交互。该架构由六个连续阶段组成，旨在加强整个课程的教学一致性、技术精度和视觉一致性。The proposed architecture conceptualizes the creation of instructional materials as an engineering process. Drawing a direct analogy to Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipelines in software engineering, this workflow replaces unstructured human-AI interactions with a systematic, stage-gated pipeline. The architecture is composed of six sequential phases designed to enforce pedagogical coherence, technical precision, and visual consistency across the curriculum.

∙\bullet第一阶段：上下文注入和范围界定。Phase 1: Context Injection and Scoping.该管道从摄取规定课程范围的基础文档开始。在传统课程中，这可能是教学大纲或教科书章节摘要。在高度情境化的环境中，例如 PBL，这是项目章程或技术描述文档。法学硕士的专门任务是理解此输入并生成核心概念和约束的简明三段文本摘要 (.txt)。一个重要的技术设计是，只有简洁的文本工件被转移到阶段5。这种刻意的遗忘机制可以防止上下文窗口溢出和注意力稀释，减少后期生成复杂代码时出现幻觉焦点丢失的可能性。The pipeline begins with the ingestion of a foundational document that dictates the lesson’s scope. In traditional courses, this might be a syllabus or a textbook chapter summary. In highly contextualized environments, such as PBL, this is the Project Charter or a technical descriptive document. The LLM is tasked exclusively with understanding this input and generating a concise, three-paragraph textual summary (.txt) of the core concepts and constraints. An important technical design is that only the concise text artifact is carried forward to Phase 5. This deliberate forgetting mechanism prevents context window overflow and attention dilution, reducing the likelihood of hallucinations focus loss when generating complex code in later stages.

∙\bullet第二阶段：教学校准。Phase 2: Pedagogical Calibration.此阶段的重点是引出和标准化教师的隐性知识。利用之前经过验证的教学材料的几次提示，人工智能可以接受所需的教学风格的培训。这包括强制实施约束，例如认知负荷限制、每张幻灯片的时间限制以及将理论概念与实际应用相结合的必要性。This phase focuses on eliciting and standardizing the instructor’s tacit knowledge. Utilizing few-shot prompting with previously validated teaching materials, the AI is trained on the desired pedagogical style. This includes enforcing constraints such as cognitive load limits, time restrictions per slide, and the necessity to anchor theoretical concepts to practical applications.

∙\bullet第三阶段：技术校准。Phase 3: Technical Calibration.在生成任何内容之前，必须建立技术边界。在这个阶段，AI被注入机构所需的特定语法规则、所需的包和自定义类调用。这样做的目的是促进所有后续输出遵守既定的视觉识别和印刷标准，从而绕过手动格式调整的需要。Before any content is generated, the technical boundaries must be established. In this stage, the AI is injected with the specific syntax rules, required packages, and custom class calls required by the institution. This is intended to promote adherence of all subsequent outputs to the established visual identity and typographical standards, bypassing the need for manual formatting adjustments.

∙\bullet第四阶段：结构规划。Phase 4: Structural Planning.人工智能会生成与预定义的学习目标一致的综合课程计划（结构框架）。为了最大限度地减少语言模型的认知负担，此阶段不会生成任何代码。输出是严格的分层大纲，定义了章节、小节以及理论模块和主动学习练习的顺序。The AI generates a comprehensive lesson plan (a structural skeleton) aligned with the predefined learning objectives. To minimize cognitive load on the language model, no code is generated during this phase. The output is strictly a hierarchical outline defining sections, subsections, and the sequence of theoretical blocks and active learning exercises.

∙\bullet第 5 阶段：迭代实施。Phase 5: Iterative Implementation.此阶段作为算法“while”循环运行。尝试在单个提示中生成整个幻灯片必然会导致令牌耗尽和代码截断。为了避免这些问题，一代人被严重分割。 AI 会收到第 4 阶段的结构骨架以及第 1 阶段的简要上下文摘要。然后，它会逐节迭代生成 LaTeX 代码和相应的 Python 脚本（用于数据可视化或数学图表）。循环一直持续到整个结构计划实施为止。通过不断参考第一阶段的总结，该模型不太可能陷入密集的通用理论，从而使内容严格锚定于特定的课程范围。This phase operates as an algorithmic “while” loop. Attempting to generate the entire slide deck in a single prompt would inherently lead to token exhaustion and code truncation. To avoid these problems, the generation is heavily segmented. The AI is fed the structural skeleton from Phase 4 alongside the condensed context summary from Phase 1. It then iteratively generates the LaTeX code and the corresponding Python scripts (for data visualization or mathematical graphs) section by section. The loop continues until the entire structural plan has been implemented. By constantly referencing the Phase 1 summary, the model is much less likely to diverge into dense, generic theory, keeping the content strictly anchored to the specific lesson scope.

∙\bullet第 6 阶段：审查和管理。Phase 6: Review and Curation.最后阶段引入双层验证过程。最初，生成的代码和文本由辅助独立人工智能代理进行自动审查，其任务是识别语法错误或教学偏差。在这种自动管理之后，主要讲师充当人在循环验证者，编译代码并批准最终材料。在我们的实证验证中，为了确保严谨性，此审查有选择地扩展到两名独立的同行讲师。The final stage introduces a dual-layer validation process. Initially, the generated code and text undergo an automated review by a secondary, independent AI agent tasked with identifying syntax errors or pedagogical deviations. Following this automated curation, the primary instructor acts as the human-in-the-loop validator, compiling the code and approving the final material. In our empirical validation, to ensure rigor, this review was optionally extended to two independent peer instructors.

VI 讨论Discussion

为期一年的实施结果为自动化、代码驱动的教学设计的可行性提供了充分的证据。通过构建教师和生成人工智能之间的交互，所提出的架构有效地解决了技术可重复性和教学一致性的核心挑战。The results of this year-long implementation provide substantial evidence for the viability of automated, code-driven instructional design. By structuring the interaction between the instructor and the Generative AI, the proposed architecture effectively addressed the core challenges of technical reproducibility and pedagogical coherence.

六一AVI-A 研究结果的综合Synthesis of Findings

实证验证提供了解决指导本研究的三个研究问题的证据。Empirical validation provided evidence addressing the three research questions that guided this study.

∙\bulletRQ1（教学框架）：RQ1 (Pedagogical Framework):该架构表明，通过小样本校准（第 2 阶段）和结构隔离（第 4 阶段）的组合，可以将隐性教学知识系统地转化为显式规则。通过允许教师在生成任何代码之前快速审查和调整基于文本的课程计划，该管道确保了教师的个人教学签名得以保留，同时自动化了最劳动密集型的任务。The architecture suggests that tacit pedagogical knowledge can be systematically translated into explicit rules through a combination of few-shot calibration (Phase 2) and structural isolation (Phase 4). By allowing the instructor to quickly review and tweak the text-based Lesson Plan before any code was generated, the pipeline ensured that the instructor’s personal teaching signature was preserved while automating the most labor-intensive tasks.

∙\bulletRQ2（技术再现性）：RQ2 (Technical Reproducibility):基于代码的工具（LaTeX/Beamer 和 Python）与生成式 AI 的集成缓解了数学幻觉的问题。实现这一目标的关键机制是第一阶段的大幅上下文修剪和第五阶段的迭代生成循环。仅向模型提供课程计划的孤立部分以及严格浓缩的项目摘要，使人工智能能够集中注意力，确保高度准确的代码输出。The integration of code-based tools (LaTeX/Beamer and Python) with Generative AI mitigated the problem of mathematical hallucinations. The critical mechanism enabling this was the drastic context pruning in Phase 1 and the iterative generation loop in Phase 5. Feeding the model only isolated sections of the lesson plan alongside a strictly condensed project summary kept the AI’s attention focused, ensuring highly accurate code outputs.

∙\bulletRQ3（实际影响）：RQ3 (Practical Impact):实际部署证实了教师准备时间的显着减少。此外，由于视觉格式集中在自定义 Beamer 类中，因此机构视觉标识在 24 个不同的项目和多个学科中得到维护。六位不同教授成功交付这些材料提供了证据，表明这些资产可以在多个讲师之间采用，从而支持其可扩展性。The practical deployment confirmed a significant reduction in faculty preparation time. Furthermore, because the visual formatting was centralized within a custom Beamer class, the institutional visual identity was maintained across 24 distinct projects and multiple disciplines. The successful delivery of these materials by six different professors provided evidence that the assets could be adopted across multiple instructors, supporting their scalability.

六-BVI-B 超越即时工程：建筑的首要地位Beyond Prompt Engineering: The Primacy of Architecture

验证阶段的一个关键观察结果是，与工作流程本身的结构相比，提示的确切措辞或使用的特定基础模型（例如 Gemini 或 DeepSeek）对最终质量具有次要影响。当前的文献非常强调即时工程，但我们的研究结果表明了向教学架构的范式转变。A critical observation from the validation phase is that the exact phrasing of the prompts or the specific foundational model used (e.g., Gemini or DeepSeek) had a secondary impact on the final quality compared to the structure of the workflow itself. The current literature heavily emphasizes prompt engineering, but our findings suggest a paradigm shift towards instructional architecture.

这种架构的优势在于它的限制：上下文的彻底修剪、结构规划与代码生成的隔离以及一次实现一个部分的迭代循环。尝试在单个提示中生成完整的幻灯片不可避免地会导致象征性的疲惫和幻觉。此外，将人机交互界面限制为纯文本、降价和源代码被证明是一种高度精确的通信协议，成功地避免了人工智能模型尝试输出富文本或专有文件格式时常见的格式故障。The strength of this architecture lies in its constraints: the drastic pruning of context, the isolation of structural planning from code generation, and the iterative loop that implements one section at a time. Attempting to generate a full slide deck in a single prompt inevitably leads to token exhaustion and hallucinations. Furthermore, restricting the human-AI interface to pure text, markdown, and source code proved to be a highly precise communication protocol, successfully avoiding the formatting glitches common when AI models attempt to output rich text or proprietary file formats.

六-CVI-C 实际意义Practical Implications

这一框架促进了教育者角色的根本演变。讲师从幻灯片的手动格式化者转变为教学设计的架构师和工程师。This framework promotes a fundamental evolution in the role of the educator. The instructor transitions from being a manual formatter of slides to acting as an architect and engineer of instructional design.

应强调所提出的解决方案的广泛适用性。该架构在基于项目的学习 (PBL) 环境中进行了特意的压力测试，由于不断需要将理论概念锚定到不同的实际项目，这增加了严重的复杂性。由于该管道成功解决了这一要求极高的场景，因此其在不同高等教育机构传统的、以讲座为基础的课程中的应用变得简单且高度可扩展。强制实施的教学风格并不是硬性要求；该架构完全适用于任何教学方法。The broad applicability of the proposed solution should be emphasized. The architecture was deliberately stress-tested in a Project-Based Learning (PBL) environment, which adds a severe layer of complexity due to the continuous need to anchor theoretical concepts to varying practical projects. Because the pipeline successfully resolved this highly demanding scenario, its application to traditional, lecture-based courses across diverse higher education institutions becomes straightforward and highly scalable. The pedagogical style enforced is not a rigid requirement; the architecture is entirely adaptable to any instructional methodology.

该架构的一个非常有价值的含义是它对国际化的原生支持。由于内容严格以代码形式（基于文本）生成，因此利用法学硕士为交换生翻译整个模块被证明是微不足道的。与传统的所见即所得工具不同，更改语言通常会破坏视觉布局，编译后的 LaTeX 代码会自动适应文本扩展或收缩，从而在不同语言之间保持完美的视觉一致性。A highly valuable implication of this architecture is its native support for internationalization. Because the content is generated strictly as code (text-based), utilizing the LLM to translate entire modules for exchange students proved trivial. Unlike traditional WYSIWYG tools, where changing the language often disrupts the visual layout, the compiled LaTeX code automatically accommodates text expansion or contraction, maintaining perfect visual consistency across different languages.

VI-D 挑战和限制Challenges and Limitations

尽管取得了成功，但实施仍面临实际挑战。生成模型在其提供者进行系统更新后会受到行为漂移的影响，有时需要在提示说明中进行细微的重新校准。此外，虽然概念上的幻觉被消除了，但轻微的语法异常仍然发生，例如 LaTeX 中偶尔出现括号不匹配的情况。Despite its successes, the implementation faced practical challenges. Generative models are subject to behavioral drift following systemic updates by their providers, occasionally requiring minor recalibrations in the prompt instructions. Additionally, while conceptual hallucinations were eliminated, minor syntactic anomalies still occurred, such as the occasional mismatch of brackets in LaTeX.

虽然第一年的基础模块成功地展示了该架构在多个教师和大量学生群体中的高可扩展性（While the first-year foundational modules successfully demonstrated the architecture’s high scalability across multiple instructors and large student cohorts (n>200n>200），高级专业化模块（第二年和第三年）仅限于较小的班级规模和单讲师部署。另一方面，需要这些高级模块来对架构处理高密度、专业数学内容而不会产生幻觉的能力进行压力测试。), the advanced specialization modules (Years 2 and 3) were limited to smaller class sizes and single-instructor deployment. On the other hand, these advanced modules were required for stress-testing the architecture’s capacity to handle highly dense, specialized mathematical content without hallucinations.

这项研究的主要局限性在于，尽管生成的材料被部署在多个课程中并由不同的教授使用，但创作过程是由单个机构内的单个讲师进行的。未来的多地点研究有必要观察不同的教员如何适应操作管道。The primary limitation of this study is that, although the generated materials were deployed across multiple courses and utilized by various professors, the authoring process was conducted by a single instructor within a single institution. Future multisite studies are necessary to observe how different faculty members adapt to operating the pipeline.

七VII结论Conclusion

本文提出了一种强大且可扩展的人工智能辅助教学设计架构，专为满足 STEM 和工程教育的严格要求而量身定制。通过将教育材料的创建形式化为以课程即代码范式为基础的结构化、多阶段管道，这种方法成功地超越了临时提示工程的短暂且容易出错的性质。实证验证表明，对任务的刻意分割，结合严格的上下文修剪以及使用精确的基于代码的工具（如 LaTeX 和 Python），可以有效地缓解大型语言模型有据可查的局限性，例如数学幻觉和格式不一致。This paper presented a robust and scalable AI-assisted architecture for instructional design, specifically tailored to the rigorous demands of STEM and engineering education. By formalizing the creation of educational materials into a structured, multi-phase pipeline grounded in the Curriculum as Code paradigm, this approach successfully transcends the ephemeral and error-prone nature of ad-hoc prompt engineering. The empirical validation demonstrated that the deliberate segmentation of tasks, combined with strict context pruning and the use of precise code-based tools like LaTeX and Python, effectively mitigates the well-documented limitations of Large Language Models, such as mathematical hallucinations and formatting inconsistencies.

这项工作的主要贡献之一是教师隐性教学知识的系统化。拟议的架构将个人教学实践和复杂的要求转化为明确的、可复制的计算规则，使机构能够减少教师的工作量，同时保持教学质量。随着主动学习方法越来越需要定制和情境化的教育资源，这种面向工程的框架可以提供结构化的内容开发方法。最终，所提出的方法支持高质量教育材料的可扩展生产，使教师能够将更多时间投入到最能从其专业知识中受益的活动中，例如促进学生学习和创新。One of the main contributions of this work is the systematization of the instructor’s tacit pedagogical knowledge. The proposed architecture translates individual teaching practices and complex requirements into explicit, replicable computational rules, allowing institutions to reduce faculty workload while preserving instructional quality. As active learning methodologies increasingly require customized and contextualized educational resources, engineering-oriented frameworks of this kind can provide a structured approach to content development. Ultimately, the proposed approach supports the scalable production of high-quality educational materials, allowing instructors to devote more time to activities that benefit most from their expertise, such as facilitating student learning and innovation.
