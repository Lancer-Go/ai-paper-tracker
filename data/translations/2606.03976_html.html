摘要Abstract

可以说，世界的表示包含有关特征的信息（例如，某物是蓝色的，某物是圆形），而且还包含有关哪些特征是同一对象的一部分的信息（例如，圆形是蓝色），我们将其称为绑定信息。任何能够理解具有多个对象的场景的系统都必须能够解决绑定问题：它需要知道哪些特征属于一起。然而，尽管研究表明视觉变换器（ViT）知道哪些补丁属于一起，但目前尚不清楚当前的深度学习模型是否学会展示绑定信息，即特征。我们可能认为没有太多的绑定信息，毕竟将特征错误地分配给错误的对象是基于 ViT 的架构的常见故障，尤其是在对象共享特征的场景中。在这里，我们用信息论方法形式化了绑定问题，并引入了一种探测方法来测量模型表示中的绑定信息。我们对 ViT 进行实验，测量架构不同组件的绑定，例如图像摘要令牌 [CLS] 或空间令牌。我们使用具有不同绑定挑战的数据集，例如特征共享、遮挡和自然特征，同时比较几个预训练的 ViT 的性能。总体而言，我们的研究表明结合是强大视觉识别和推理的关键要素。 [1]Representations of the world, arguably, contain information about features (e.g. something is blue, something is a circle) but also information about which features are part of the same object (e.g. the circle is blue), which we call binding information. Any system with the ability to understand scenes with multiple objects must be able to solve the binding problem: it needs to know which features belong together. However, despite work showing that Vision Transformers (ViTs) know which patches belong together, it is not known whether current deep learning models learn to exhibit binding information, i.e., for features. We may believe that there is not much binding information, after all misattributing features to wrong objects is a common failure of ViT-based architectures, especially in scenes with objects sharing features. Here we formalize the binding problem with an information-theoretic approach, and introduce a probing method to measure binding information in model representations. We perform experiments on ViTs, measuring binding from different components of the architecture, such as the image summary token [CLS] or the spatial tokens. We use datasets with different binding challenges, such as feature sharing, occlusion, and natural features, while comparing the performance of several pre-trained ViTs. Overall, our research demonstrates binding as a key ingredient to strong visual recognition and reasoning.[1]

一、简介1. Introduction

绑定是区分多对象场景中哪些特征属于哪些对象的能力。人类自然地将特征与对象绑定。这种能力是如此轻松，以至于我们很少将绑定本身视为一个计算问题。在视觉皮层中，不同的神经元群体被调整为特定的特征（例如颜色、方向、运动），但在多对象场景中，这种基于特征的编码本身并不能指定哪些特征属于同一对象的一部分。尽管如此，大脑以非凡的精度实现了结合，并且已经提出了一系列机制来解释这种能力（Treisman & Gelade，1980；von der Malsburg，1981；Zhang 等人，2020）（更多内容见 A 节），但这在很大程度上仍然是一个悬而未决的问题（Yu & Lau，2023）。Binding is the ability to tell which features belong to which objects in a multi-object scene. Humans naturally bind features to objects. This ability is so effortless that we rarely recognize binding as a computational problem in its own right. In the visual cortex, distinct neuronal populations are tuned to specific features (e.g., color, orientation, motion), but in multi-object scenes such feature-based encoding alone does not specify which features belong together as parts of the same object. Nevertheless, the brain achieves binding with extraordinary precision, and a range of mechanisms have been proposed to account for this capacity (Treisman & Gelade, 1980; von der Malsburg, 1981; Zhang et al., 2020) (more in Section A), but it remains largely an open problem (Yu & Lau, 2023).

绑定对于人工模型同样重要。组合学习是深度学习模型泛化和推理的标志（Uselis et al., 2025; Lake & Baroni, 2018; Hupkes et al., 2020; Wiedemer et al., 2023），它主要依赖于将组合学习的概念绑定到新组合中的能力（例如，在学习了动物、身体部位和运动的概念后，模型能否理解飞行的物体）企鹅？）（Greff 等人，2020）。Binding is just as equally important for artificial models. Compositional learning, a hallmark of generalization and reasoning for deep learning models (Uselis et al., 2025; Lake & Baroni, 2018; Hupkes et al., 2020; Wiedemer et al., 2023), relies crucially on the ability to bind the compositionally learned concepts into novel combinations (e.g., having learned the concepts of animals, body parts, and motion, can a model understand a flying penguin?) (Greff et al., 2020).

然而，人工视觉和视觉语言模型在绑定方面的表现明显比大脑差。在多目标场景中，特别是当目标杂乱和/或共享特征时，现代视觉和视觉语言模型正确识别目标及其相应特征的能力显着下降（Campbell et al., 2025；Zhang et al., 2024；Lewis et al., 2024；Yuksekgonul et al., 2023；Assouel et al., 2025b）。例如，（Campbell 等人，2025）表明，随着场景中共享特征的对象三元组数量的增加，视觉语言模型准确描述对象及其特征的能力单调下降（图 1），在对象计数、搜索和类比推理任务中观察到类似的趋势。另一个例子（Zhang et al., 2024）提示视觉语言模型描述 Raven 矩阵给定块中的对象（图 1）。相反，该模型组合了相邻块中的元素，将它们归因于同一对象。他们进一步表明，在传递网格之前将网格分割成单独的图像可以显着减少此类错误。人工失败However, artificial vision and vision-language models perform markedly worse at binding than the brain. A. 相关工作A. Related Work

绑定问题The Binding Problem绑定问题源于早期人工智能（Rosenblatt，1962），半个多世纪以来一直困扰着计算机科学家和认知神经科学家（Greff et al.，2020；Von der Malsburg，1999；Feldman，2013；Roskies，1999；Treisman，1996）。由于早期的人工视觉模型还处于起步阶段，缺乏复杂场景分解的能力，因此理解特征绑定的最初动力主要来自心理学和神经科学（Treisman，1999；von der Malsburg，1981）。尽管对于大脑中结合的确切神经机制尚未达成共识（Robertson，2003；Wolfe，2012；Di Lollo，2012；Scholte & de Haan，2025；Roelfsema & Serre，2025），但在识别其一些核心机制组件方面已经取得了重大进展（Treisman & Gelade，1980；Reynolds & Desimone， 1999；罗尔夫塞玛，2023；辛格和格雷，1995）。这些生物学见解启发了旨在明确解决绑定问题的架构，无论是通过时间同步（Miyato 等人，2024 年；Gopalakrishnan 等人，2024 年）还是基于注意力的机制（Salehi 等人，2024 年）。With its roots in early artificial intelligence (Rosenblatt, 1962), the binding problem has puzzled computer scientists and cognitive neuroscientists for over half a century (Greff et al., 2020; Von der Malsburg, 1999; Feldman, 2013; Roskies, 1999; Treisman, 1996). Since early artificial vision models were in their infancy and lacked the capacity for complex scene decomposition, the initial momentum for understanding feature binding came largely from psychology and neuroscience (Treisman, 1999; von der Malsburg, 1981). Although there is yet no consensus on the exact neural mechanisms of binding in the brain (Robertson, 2003; Wolfe, 2012; Di Lollo, 2012; Scholte & de Haan, 2025; Roelfsema & Serre, 2025), significant progress has been made in identifying some of its core mechanistic components (Treisman & Gelade, 1980; Reynolds & Desimone, 1999; Roelfsema, 2023; Singer & Gray, 1995). These biological insights have inspired architectures designed to explicitly address binding, whether through temporal synchrony (Miyato et al., 2024; Gopalakrishnan et al., 2024) or attention-based mechanisms (Salehi et al., 2024).

深度神经网络中的特征绑定Feature Binding in Deep Neural Networks随着深度学习在计算机视觉领域取得前所未有的成功（Krizhevsky 等人，2012；LeCun 等人，2015），绑定问题在机器学习社区中重新引起了人们的兴趣。早期的方法试图通过明确的以对象为中心的架构和训练偏差来解决绑定问题（Locatello et al., 2020; Puebla & Bowers, 2025; Assouel et al., 2025a; Aydemir et al., 2023），称为以对象为中心的学习（OCL）（Locatello et al., 2020; Greff et al., 2019）。 Slot Attention（Locatello 等人，2020）和 MONet（Burgess 等人，2019）等方法通过迭代细化将输入分解为排列不变的“槽”，每个槽代表一个对象。尽管这些模型擅长对空间相干区域进行分组（对象部分绑定），但它们可以说在特征绑定方面存在不足，通常难以将纹理等复杂属性与其各自的对象正确关联起来（Karazija et al., 2021）。此外，它们的一般适用性在很大程度上被自监督视觉变换器（ViTs）（Simeoni et al.´，2025；He et al.，2022；Zhou et al.，2021）和多模态模型（Radford et al.，2021；Girdhar et al.，2023；Rubinstein et al.，2025）的性能和可扩展性所超越。最先进的 ViT 现在在全局识别和密集预测任务（例如分割）方面表现出色（Simeoni 等人，2025），无需显式的架构组件或绑定训练过程。这促使越来越多的研究调查这些模型中结合能力自然出现的程度（Li et al., 2025），确定驱动其部分成功的潜在机制（Okawa et al., 2023; Seitzer et al., 2022; Wu et al., 2025），并提出弥补其局限性的方法（Seo et al., 2025; Hu et al., 2024; Koishigarina）等，2025）。Following the unprecedented success of deep learning in computer vision (Krizhevsky et al., 2012; LeCun et al., 2015), the binding problem has gained renewed interest within the machine learning community. Earlier approaches attempted to address binding through explicit object-centric architectural and training biases (Locatello et al., 2020; Puebla & Bowers, 2025; Assouel et al., 2025a; Aydemir et al., 2023), known as Object-Centric Learning (OCL) (Locatello et al., 2020; Greff et al., 2019). Approaches such as Slot Attention (Locatello et al., 2020) and MONet (Burgess et al., 2019) decompose inputs to permutation-invariant ”slots” via iterative refinement, each representing an object. Although these models excel at grouping spatially coherent regions (object-part binding), they arguably fall short in feature binding, often struggling to correctly associate complex attributes like texture with their respective objects (Karazija et al., 2021). Also, their general applicability is largely surpassed by the performance and scalability of selfsupervised Vision Transformers (ViTs) (Simeoni et al.´ , 2025; He et al., 2022; Zhou et al., 2021) and multi-modal models (Radford et al., 2021; Girdhar et al., 2023; Rubinstein et al., 2025). State-of-the-art ViTs now excel in both global recognition and dense prediction tasks (e.g., segmentation) (Simeoni et al.´ , 2025) without requiring explicit architectural components or training process for binding. This has prompted a growing body of research investigating the degree to which binding capabilities naturally emerge in these models (Li et al., 2025), identifying the underlying mechanisms that drive their partial success (Okawa et al., 2023; Seitzer et al., 2022; Wu et al., 2025), and proposing methods to remedy their limitations (Seo et al., 2025; Hu et al., 2024; Koishigarina et al., 2025).

探测绑定Probing for Binding对特征绑定的严格评估需要适当复杂的数据集和精确的量化指标。理想的数据集必须包括具有多个对象的场景，以挑战模型的绑定能力，同时提供将对象链接到其特定属性的真实注释。 MS-COCO（Lin 等人，2015）等标准基准和 CLEVR（Johnson 等人，2016）等合成环境传统上起到了这一作用，而最近的工作引入了专门的数据集来实现细粒度的组合理解（Liu 等人，2024）。在测量方面，现有方法分为两类。 “症状”方法通过高级下游任务的表现间接评估结合，例如视觉推理（Campbell 等人，2025）、文本图像检索错误（Yuksekgonul 等人，2023）和组合概括（Lewis 等人，2024；Pearson 等人，2025）。相反，“机械”方法旨在直接评估内部表征内的绑定，提供一种与任务无关的衡量信息如何在低级别组织和绑定的方法（Li et al., 2025）。A rigorous evaluation of feature binding requires both a suitably complex dataset and a precise metric for quantification. An ideal dataset must include scenes with multiple objects to challenge the model’s binding capability, while providing ground-truth annotations that link objects to their specific attributes. Standard benchmarks such as MS-COCO (Lin et al., 2015) and synthetic environments like CLEVR (Johnson et al., 2016) have traditionally served this role, while recent works have introduced specialized datasets to target fine-grained compositional understanding (Liu et al., 2024). In terms of measurement, existing approaches fall into two categories. The ”symptomatic” approaches assess binding indirectly via performance on high-level downstream tasks, such as visual reasoning (Campbell et al., 2025), text-image retrieval errors (Yuksekgonul et al., 2023) and compositional generalization (Lewis et al., 2024; Pearson et al., 2025). Conversely, ”mechanistic” approaches aim to evaluate binding directly within the internal representations, offering a task-agnostic measure of how information is organized and bound at the low level (Li et al., 2025).
