摘要Abstract

生成推理重排序器通过在重新排序候选列表之前发出一条思想链来实现很强的推荐准确性，但它们的推理速度很慢：自回归 (AR) 解码器为每个推理标记花费一次顺序前向传递，并且推理轨迹远远超过其产生的排名。 为了降低这一成本，块扩散语言模型通过几个去噪步骤并行解码许多位置，并且速度要快得多，但天真地将 AR 重排序器转换为 AR 重排序器会带来两个精度差距：(1)Generative reasoning re-rankers achieve strong recommendation accuracy by emitting a chain-of-thought before re-ordering a candidate list, but they are slow at inference: an autoregressive (AR) decoder spends one sequential forward pass per reasoning token, and the reasoning trace far exceeds the ranking it produces. To reduce this cost, block-diffusion language models decode many positions in parallel over a few denoising steps and are substantially faster, yet naively converting an AR re-ranker into one opens two accuracy gaps: (1) a结构性的structural间隙：答案位置并行去噪并独立评分，因此解码器会发出无效排名（重复、丢弃或超出设置的标识符），AR 通过从左到右的屏蔽来避免这些排名； (2) 一个gap: answer positions are denoised in parallel and scored independently, so the decoder emits invalid rankings (duplicated, dropped, or out-of-set identifiers) that AR avoids through left-to-right masking; and (2) a分布式的distributional差距：在固定教师轨迹上微调转换后的模型相对于推理时自身的解码来说是偏离策略的，从而留下了残余精度差距。为了在保持加速的同时缩小这两个差距，我们建议gap: fine-tuning the converted model on fixed teacher trajectories is off-policy relative to its own decoding at inference, leaving a residual accuracy gap. First,转换微调conversion fine-tuning(CFT) 采用 AR 初始化的扩散模型，将答案去噪为自身有效的排列，无需外部约束解码器。 接下来，(CFT) adapts the AR-initialized diffusion model to denoise the answer into a valid permutation on its own, without an external constrained decoder. 1 简介Introduction

生成推理重新排序器Generative reasoning re-rankers(liang2026generative)基于大型语言模型 (LLM) 构建built on large language models (LLMs)(radford2018improving; radford2019language)最近的研究表明，在提交排序之前，可以通过对简短候选列表进行显式推理来完善推荐管道的最后阶段。 推理重排序器将用户的购买历史记录与检索器生成的候选项目的预先排序列表一起获取，生成证明重新排序合理性的思路链，并发出候选项目的排列。 推理使这些模型变得准确：它基于项目语义和用户意图而不是单个学习分数来做出决策。 这也是它们昂贵的原因。自回归 (AR) 解码器对每个推理标记花费一次顺序前向传递，并且推理轨迹远远长于它最终产生的排名。在生产重新排名设置中，每次展示都会查询相同的模型，此顺序成本是绑定约束。have recently been shown to refine the final stage of recommendation pipelines by reasoning explicitly over a short candidate list before committing to an ordering. A reasoning re-ranker takes a user’s purchase history together with a pre-ranked list of candidate items produced by a retriever, generates a chain-of-thought that justifies a re-ordering, and emits a permutation of the candidates. The reasoning is what makes these models accurate: it grounds the decision in item semantics and user intent rather than a single learned score. It is also what makes them expensive. An autoregressive (AR) decoder spends one sequential forward pass per reasoning token, and the reasoning trace is far longer than the ranking it ultimately produces. In a production re-ranking setting, where the same model is queried for every impression, this sequential cost is the binding constraint.

块扩散语言模型Block-diffusion language models (arriola2025interpolating; wu2025fast)建议一条出路。 他们不是从左到右遍历序列，而是一次解码许多位置，并通过少量的去噪步骤对其进行细化，在每个步骤中提交最可信的位置。 这恰恰在 AR 最昂贵的地方（即长推理跨度）具有吸引力，并且它是巨大潜在加速的来源。 但加速并不是免费的。用扩散解码器替换 AR 解码器suggest a way out. Rather than walking the sequence left to right, they decode many positions at once and refine them over a small number of denoising steps, committing the most confident positions at each step. This is attractive precisely where AR is most expensive, i.e., the long reasoning span, and it is the source of a large potential speedup. But the speedup does not come for free. Replacing the AR decoder with a diffusion decoder排名准确性下降drops ranking accuracy，本文的中心问题是如何将重排序器转换为扩散解码器，同时放弃尽可能小的精度。 我们直接研究这种权衡：我们测量转换的准确性成本，并开发一种将其最小化的方法。我们将生成的系统称为, and the central question of this paper is how to convert the re-ranker to a diffusion decoder while giving up as little accuracy as possible. We study this trade-off directly: we measure the accuracy cost of the conversion and develop a recipe that minimizes it. We refer to the resulting system as扩散-GR2Diffusion-GR2，我们的自回归重排序器 GR2 的块扩散对应项。, the block-diffusion counterpart of our autoregressive re-ranker GR2.

起点是我们训练有素的 AR 重新排序器。 在GR2之后，它是通过对基于语义ID的项目的高质量推理轨迹进行监督微调（SFT），然后针对重新排名奖励进行强化学习（RL）而获得的； 生成的模型设置了我们在转换后尝试恢复的准确度标准。 我们从这个 AR 模型初始化一个块扩散模型，并要求它在并行解码下重现相同的推理和排名行为。The starting point is our trained AR re-ranker. Following GR2, it is obtained by supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on high-quality reasoning traces over semantic-ID-grounded items, followed by reinforcement learning (RL) against a re-ranking reward; the resulting model sets the accuracy bar we try to recover after conversion. We initialize a block-diffusion model from this AR model and ask it to reproduce the same reasoning-and-ranking behavior under parallel decoding.

转换的准确性成本有两个来源，Diffusion-GR2 依次解决每个来源。第一个来源是The accuracy cost of the conversion has two sources, and Diffusion-GR2 addresses each in turn. The first source is结构性的structural并专注于答案。 最终排名必须是所给出的候选人的准确排列。 AR 解码器隐式地强制执行这一点：它一次提交一个项目，并且可以屏蔽它已经使用过的项目。 扩散解码器并行预测答案位置并对它们进行独立评分，因此没有什么可以阻止它重复一项、省略一项或发出一个从来都不是候选的标识符。 在实践中，这种情况经常发生，足以影响真正的准确性。我们通过以下方式解决这种故障模式and concentrates at the answer. The final ranking must be a permutation of exactly the candidates that were given. An AR decoder enforces this implicitly: it commits to one item at a time and can mask the items it has already used. A diffusion decoder predicts the answer positions in parallel and scores them independently, so nothing prevents it from repeating an item, omitting one, or emitting an identifier that was never a candidate. In practice this happens often enough to cost real accuracy. We address this failure mode with转换微调conversion fine-tuning(CFT)：因为扩散模型是从 AR 重排序器初始化的，它可以免费生成有效的排列，因此在重排序数据上对其进行微调可以传递这种能力，并且转换后的模型可以学习将答案范围去噪为自己的有效排列，而无需外部约束解码器，从而恢复转换时损失的大部分准确性。(CFT): because the diffusion model is initialized from the AR re-ranker, which produces valid permutations for free, fine-tuning it on the re-ranking data transfers this competence, and the converted model learns to denoise the answer span into a valid permutation on its own without an external constrained decoder, recovering most of the accuracy lost at conversion.

RL 阶段应用最有效The RL stage is most effective applied在上面on top ofOPD。 OPD 提供了一个健康的、具有非退化轨迹的分布策略，从这个起点开始，RL 改进了排名指标，并比单独的 OPD 缩小了更多的剩余差距。因此，缩小差距的主要秘诀是OPD. 4 实验Experiments

我们围绕三个问题组织评估：We organize the evaluation around three questions:

  • •

  • •

    Q2：与自回归解码相比，块扩散解码器的推理加速效果如何？准确度如何？: What inference speedup does the block-diffusion decoder buy over autoregressive decoding, and at what accuracy?

  • •

    Q3：转换后的模型是否忠实地推理，或者使用降级的填料达到其排名？: Does the converted model reason faithfully, or reach its rankings with degraded filler?
