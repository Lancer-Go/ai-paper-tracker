摘要Abstract

在不过度拟合的情况下训练神经网络 (NN) 是很困难的；检测过度拟合也很困难。我们提出了一种新颖的随机矩阵理论方法，可以在不访问训练或测试数据的情况下检测深度学习模型中过度拟合的发生。对于每个模型层，我们按元素随机化每个权重矩阵，Training Neural Networks (NNs) without overfitting is difficult; detecting that overfitting is difficult as well. We present a novel Random Matrix Theory method that detects the onset of overfitting in deep learning models without access to train or test data. For each model layer, we randomize each weight matrix element-wise,𝐖→𝐖rand\mathbf{W}\rightarrow\mathbf{W}_{\mathrm{rand}}，将混洗矩阵的经验谱分布与 Marchenko-Pastur 分布进行拟合，并识别违反自平均的大异常值。我们将这些异常值称为相关陷阱。在过度拟合的开始阶段，我们将其称为长视野 grokking 中的“反 grokking”阶段，随着测试精度下降而训练精度保持较高，相关陷阱的数量和规模会形成并增长。陷阱可能是良性的，也可能损害泛化；我们提供了一种经验方法来区分它们，通过训练模型传递随机数据并评估输出 logits 的 JS 散度。我们的研究结果表明，反 Grokking 是一个额外的 Grokking 阶段，具有较高的训练精度和较低的测试精度，通过其相关陷阱在结构上与预 Grokking 不同。更广泛地说，我们发现一些基础规模的法学硕士表现出相同的相关陷阱，表明潜在有害的过度拟合。, fit the shuffled matrix’s empirical spectral distribution with a Marchenko-Pastur distribution, and identify large outliers that violate self-averaging. We call these outliers Correlation Traps. During the onset of overfitting, which we call the "anti-grokking” phase in long-horizon grokking, Correlation Traps form and grow in number and scale as test accuracy decreases while train accuracy remains high. Traps may be benign or may harm generalization; we provide an empirical approach to distinguish between them by passing random data through the trained model and evaluating the JS divergence of output logits. Our findings show that anti-grokking is an additional grokking phase with high train accuracy and decreasing test accuracy, structurally distinct from pre-grokking through its Correlation Traps. More broadly, we find that some foundation-scale LLMs exhibit the same Correlation Traps, indicating potentially harmful overfitting.

1 简介Introduction

开放权重模型越来越多地用作下游系统的基础，但通常不清楚检查点是否经过稳健训练或对其训练分布过度拟合。用户可以访问权重和模型卡，但不能访问训练数据、保留损失、优化器状态或长期检查点历史记录。因此，两个模型从外部看起来很相似，但它们的权重是编码有用的结构还是脆弱的、特定于数据的相关性，两者之间存在很大差异。这就引发了一个基本问题：我们是否可以直接从训练模型的权重中检测过度拟合的特征，而无需访问训练或任何测试数据？Open-weight models are increasingly used as foundations for downstream systems, but it is often unclear whether a checkpoint is robustly trained or overfit to its training distribution. Users may have access to the weights and a model card, but not to the training data, held-out losses, optimizer state, or long-horizon checkpoint history. As a result, two models can look similar from the outside while differing sharply in whether their weights encode useful structure or brittle, data-specific correlations. This motivates a basic question: can we detect signatures of overfitting directly from the weights of a trained model, without having access to the training or any test data?

因为这样的历史通常对于开放权重检查点是不可用的，所以我们首先研究一个设置，其中相关动态是可见的，并且通过长视野训练很容易引起过度拟合：Because such histories are usually unavailable for open-weight checkpoints, we first study a setting where the relevant dynamics are visible and overfitting can be readily induced with long-horizon training:格罗金grokking。在 grokking 中，训练精度达到近乎完美，而测试精度在许多优化步骤中保持接近机会，然后突然提高. In grokking, training accuracy reaches near perfection while test accuracy stays near chance for many optimization steps, before abruptly improving[22]。 Grokking 已在多种架构和任务中进行了研究，包括模块加法、计算机视觉模型和 GPT 式转换器等算法任务. Grokking has been studied across several architectures and tasks, including algorithmic tasks such as modular addition, computer vision models, and GPT-style transformers[17, 24]。我们将这种观点扩展到 grokking 之后的长视野，其中长视野训练可以使模型进入经典的过拟合阶段。我们称这种后概括制度为. We extend this view to the long-horizon after grokking, where long-horizon training can drive the model into a classical overfitting phase. We call this post-generalization regime反格罗金anti-grokking.

我们的设置让我们可以比较学习的三个阶段：预神探、神探和反神探。仅从训练和测试精度来看，预grokking和反grokking看起来似乎很相似：在这两种情况下，训练精度很高，而保留精度很差。但它们在结构上是不同的。 Pre-grokking 发生在模型找到泛化解之前；反格罗克（anti-grokking）是在找到这样的解决方案然后丢失之后发生的。在比较不同阶段时，这使得长视野探索成为研究有害过度拟合的绝佳案例。Our setup lets us compare three phases of learning: pre-grokking, grokking, and anti-grokking. Pre-grokking and anti-grokking can look deceptively similar from train and test accuracy alone: in both regimes, training accuracy is high while held-out accuracy is poor. But they are structurally different. Pre-grokking occurs before the model has found a generalizing solution; anti-grokking occurs after such a solution has been found and then lost. In comparing different phases, this makes long-horizon grokking an excellent case for studying harmful overfitting.

Grokking 是由 Power 等人提出的。Grokking was introduced by Power et al.[22]因为训练精度已经饱和后延迟泛化。随后的工作研究了算法和机械设置中的 grokking，包括模算术、MLP 和 Transformer 模型as delayed generalization after training accuracy has already saturated. Most of this literature focuses on the transition from memorization to generalization: the model first fits the training set, then later discovers a rule that generalizes. Our focus is the long-horizon regime after this transition, where a model that has already grokked can lose held-out performance (and overfit its training data) after extended training.

8 结论Conclusion

主要的教训是，摸索并不总是训练的结束。模型可以首先学习泛化规则，然后在持续优化下失去泛化能力。我们称之为后期故障模式The main lesson is that grokking is not always the end of training. A model can first learn a rule that generalizes, and then lose that generalization under continued optimization. We call this late-stage failure mode反格罗金anti-grokking：具体而言，泛化后，测试准确性会下降，而训练准确性仍然很高。在 MNIST MLP、模加法 (MA) 和 GPT2 式 grokking 实验中，这种转变的标志是异常值的出现: specifically, post-generalization, test accuracy degrades, while training accuracy remains high. Across MNIST MLP, Modular Addition (MA), and GPT2-style grokking experiments, this transition is marked by the appearance of outliers(λt​r​a​p)(\lambda_{trap})在一个或多个随机、分层权重矩阵的特征谱中。我们称这些异常值为in the eigenspectrum of one or more randomized, layer-wise weight matrices. We call these outliers相关陷阱Correlation Traps.

术语“相关陷阱”是可操作的。我们将这些异常值称为“相关陷阱”，因为它们识别出似乎“捕获”相关性的潜在结构，导致模型过度拟合其数据，降低模型的测试准确性，但（不一定）降低训练准确性。随着过度拟合变得越来越严重，更多的陷阱就会出现。此外，删除它们甚至会损害模型的测试（和训练）准确性，显然会消除模型已经学到的相关性。The term Correlation Trap is operational. We call these outliers Correlation Traps because they identify latent structures that appear to "trap" the correlations, causing the model to overfit its data, reducing the test accuracy of the model, but not (necessarily) the training accuracy. And as the overfitting gets worse, more traps appear. Moreover, removing them can even hurt the model’s test (and training) accuracy, apparently removing the correlations the model had learned.

相关陷阱揭示了两个表面相似的制度之间的结构差异。在pre-grokking和anti-grokking中，训练精度都很高，但测试精度很差。但权重谱不同。在预悟期间，陷阱数量保持在接近于零的水平。在成功的摸索过程中，它们也保持缺席或几乎缺席。它们只在反格罗克期间出现；因此，陷阱不仅仅检测记忆、低测试准确度或高训练准确度。他们检测 anti-grokk 过程中出现的过度拟合。Correlation Traps reveal a structural difference between two superficially similar regimes. In both pre-grokking and anti-grokking, training accuracy can be high while test accuracy is poor. But the weight spectra are different. During pre-grokking, trap counts remain near zero. During successful grokking, they also remain absent or nearly absent. They appear only during anti-grokking; thus, traps do not merely detect memorization, low test accuracy, or high training accuracy. They detect overfitting that arises during anti-grokking.

诊断很简单，只需重量即可。它不需要训练数据、测试数据、梯度、优化器状态或访问训练管道。它只需要一个检查点。对于每一层，我们按条目对权重矩阵进行洗牌，形成随机协方差谱，拟合 Marchenko–Pastur (MP) 块，并计算超出拟合 MP/TW 边缘的右边缘异常值。 MP/TW 法则提供了一个自平均基线。相关陷阱是一种违反此基线的分离光谱模式，可能是有害的，也可能是良性的。The diagnostic is simple and weight-only. It does not require training data, test data, gradients, optimizer state, or access to the training pipeline. It only requires a checkpoint. For each layer, we shuffle the weight matrix entry-wise, form the randomized covariance spectrum, fit a Marchenko–Pastur (MP) bulk, and count right-edge outliers beyond the fitted MP/TW edge. The MP/TW law provides a self-averaging baseline. A Correlation Trap is a separated spectral mode that violates this baseline, and may be harmful or benign.

自平均解释解释了为什么这些模式很重要。陷阱可以通过两种方式破坏浓度：几何定位和/或光谱浓度。在任何一种情况下，当陷阱与模型的泛化层特征分量显着重叠时，陷阱对齐的可观测量（例如测试误差）可能会变得非自平均。这给出了至少两种具体机制，通过这些机制，权重中的谱异常可以成为行为故障模式。The self-averaging interpretation explains why these modes matter. A trap can disrupt concentration in two ways: geometric localization and/or spectral concentration. In either case, trap-aligned observables like the test error can become non-self-averaging when the trap overlaps significantly with the model’s generalizing layer eigen-components. This gives at least two concrete mechanisms by which a spectral anomaly in the weights can become a behavioral failure mode.

我们可以使用无数据的经验检查（称为 JSD 陷阱消融测试）区分有害陷阱和良性陷阱。我们用随机向量替换陷阱，将探针输入传递给原始模型和消融模型，并测量其输出分布之间的 Jensen-Shannon 散度。大的 JSD 意味着移除陷阱会显着改变模型输出，即使对于随机输入数据也是如此，这表明对实际输入的影响。 如果更换改变了测试精度，则陷阱是有害的；如果影响很小或没有影响，则陷阱是良性的。We can distinguish harmful from benign traps using a data-free empirical check, called the JSD trap ablation test. We replace the trap with a random vector, pass probe inputs through the original and ablated models, and measure the Jensen-Shannon divergence between their output distributions. A large JSD means removing the trap significantly changes the model outputs even on random input data, indicative of the effect on real inputs. The trap is harmful if replacement changes the test accuracy, and benign if it has little-to-no effect.

MLP 案例研究展示了有害陷阱的机制。在反grokking中，映射的第一层陷阱方向在像素空间中变得类似原型，并且对陷阱的干预改变了混淆结构。该模型仍然正确地对有限训练集进行分类，但在受陷阱影响的测试示例上，其行为越来越依赖于展平向量幅度和原型统计数据，例如The MLP case study shows what a harmful trap can look like mechanistically. In anti-grokking, mapped first-layer trap directions become prototype-like in pixel space, and intervening on the trap changes the confusion structure. The model still classifies the finite training set correctly, but on trap-affected test examples its behavior increasingly depends on flattened-vector amplitude and prototype statistics, such as‖x‖2\|x\|_{2}，而不是相关的二维数字模式。这给出了一个具体示例，说明模型如何保持完全适合其训练数据，同时失去支持泛化的基于相关性的规则。, rather than the correlated two-dimensional digit pattern. This gives one concrete example of how a model can remain perfectly fit to its training data while losing the correlation-based rule that supported generalization.

相同的陷阱开始特征并非特定于图像 MLP。模块化加法提供了一种算法变压器设置，其中陷阱计数在 grokking 之前和期间保持接近零，并在 anti-grokking 期间上升。 GPT2 式的实验在 Transformer 语言模型架构中展示了相同的长期模式。总之，这些实验表明，相关陷阱在本文研究的模型系列和任务类型中重复出现：密集图像分类器、算法转换器和 GPT 式序列模型。The same trap-onset signature is not specific to image MLPs. Modular Addition provides an algorithmic transformer setting where trap counts remain near zero before and during grokking and rise during anti-grokking. The GPT2-style experiment shows the same long-horizon pattern in a transformer language-model architecture. Together, these experiments show that Correlation Traps recur across the model families and task types studied here: dense image classifiers, algorithmic transformers, and GPT-style sequence models.

前沿筛选实验指出了一个实际用例。一些大型开放式法学硕士检查点存在大量陷阱。这些配置文件本身并不能证明这些模型存在有害的过度拟合，但它们暗示了潜在的问题。对于基础模型训练，陷阱计数可以作为检查点级别的警告信号。对于微调，它们可能有助于识别基础模型层，其行为应在适应之前进行测试。The frontier-scale screening experiment points to a practical use case. Some large open-weight LLM checkpoints exhibit a large number of traps. These profiles do not by themselves prove harmful overfitting in those models, but they are suggestive of potential problems. For foundation-model training, trap counts could serve as a checkpoint-level warning signal. For fine-tuning, they may help identify base-model layers whose behavior should be tested before adaptation.

我们并不声称每种形式的过度拟合都一定会产生相关陷阱。这里的主张更加尖锐：在长视域摸索中，陷阱开始跟踪反摸索，而前摸索提供无陷阱的负控制。使用 JSD 诊断陷阱消融测试，可以识别给定模型的陷阱是有害的还是良性的。这些结果表明，某些形式的有害过度拟合会在看似训练有素的模型的层权重矩阵中留下可检测的特征。下一步是在训练和微调期间使用此诊断，并测试是否可以在不损害有用的学习结构的情况下抑制、删除或规范化有害的相关陷阱。We do not claim that every form of overfitting must produce Correlation Traps. The claim here is sharper: in long-horizon grokking, trap onset tracks anti-grokking while pre-grokking provides the trap-free negative control. Using the JSD diagnostic trap ablation test, traps can be identified as harmful or benign for a given model. These results suggest some forms of harmful overfitting leave detectable signatures in the layer weight matrices of seemingly well-trained models. The next step is to use this diagnostic during training and fine-tuning, and to test whether harmful Correlation Traps can be suppressed, removed, or regularized without damaging useful learned structure.

9 局限性Limitations

我们的受控证据来自三种长期摸索设置：MNIST MLP、模块化加法 (MA) 和 GPT2 式训练轨迹。这些实验清楚地揭示了前grokking、grokking和反grokking阶段，但它们并没有耗尽架构、优化器、数据集、损失或种子的空间。 GPT-OSS结果显示，大型开放权重模型中也存在相关陷阱，但这些结果仅是筛选证据；它们本身并不会形成有害行为。主要风险是过度解释：如果没有额外的基于探针和基于任务的评估，陷阱计数不应用作通用模型质量或安全评分。Our controlled evidence comes from three long-horizon grokking settings: an MNIST MLP, modular addition (MA), and a GPT2-style training trajectory. These experiments expose the pre-grokking, grokking, and anti-grokking phases clearly, but they do not exhaust the space of architectures, optimizers, datasets, losses, or seeds. The GPT-OSS results show that Correlation Traps also occur in large open-weight models, but those results are screening evidence only; they do not by themselves establish harmful behavior. The main risk is over-interpretation: trap counts should not be used as universal model-quality or safety scores without additional probe-based and task-based evaluations.

潜在的社会影响。Potential societal impacts.

有超过一百万个开源模型权重可供下载，但其中许多模型毫无疑问过度拟合了他们的训练数据。这项研究允许用户选择过度拟合特征较少且可能更适合所需任务的模型。 此外，随着对基础规模模型监管的呼声，相关陷阱可以为监管机构提供一种公正的方式来衡量模型安全性，并且无需访问专有的、安全的甚至任何可用的数据。There are over a million open-source model weights available to download, but many of them, however, have no doubt overfit their training data. This study lets users select for models that have fewer signatures of overfitting and potentially better suited to the desired tasks. Moreover, with the calls for regulation of foundation-scale models, Correlation Traps can provide regulatory bodies one unbiased way to gauge of model safety, and without needing access to proprietary, secure, or even any available data.
