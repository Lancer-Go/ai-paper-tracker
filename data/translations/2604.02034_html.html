摘要。Abstract.

保险申请流程通常依赖于冗长且标准化的调查问卷，难以捕捉个体差异。此外，保险公司必须盲目相信用户的回答，从而增加了欺诈的可能性。 ARQuest 框架引入了一种新的承保方法，通过使用大型语言模型 (LLM) 和替代数据源来创建个性化和自适应调查问卷。社交媒体图像分析、地理数据分类和检索增强生成（RAG）等技术用于提取有意义的用户见解并指导有针对性的后续问题。Insurance application processes often rely on lengthy and standardized questionnaires that struggle to capture individual differences. Moreover, insurers must blindly trust users’ responses, increasing the chances of fraud. The ARQuest framework introduces a new approach to underwriting by using Large Language Models (LLMs) and alternative data sources to create personalized and adaptive questionnaires. Techniques such as social media image analysis, geographic data categorization, and Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) are used to extract meaningful user insights and guide targeted follow-up questions.

集成到行业合作伙伴移动应用程序中的人寿保险系统通过两个实验进行了测试。虽然传统调查问卷在风险评估方面的准确性稍高，但由 GPT 模型支持的自适应版本需要的问题较少，并且因其更流畅和更具吸引力的体验而受到用户的青睐。A life insurance system integrated into an industry partner mobile app was tested in two experiments. While traditional questionnaires yielded slightly higher accuracy in risk assessment, adaptive versions powered by GPT models required fewer questions and were preferred by users for their more fluid and engaging experience.

ARQuest 在提高用户满意度和简化保险流程方面显示出巨大的潜力。随着进一步发展，这种方法在风险准确性方面可能会超越传统方法，并有助于推动保险行业的创新。ARQuest shows great potential to improve user satisfaction and streamline insurance processes. With further development, this approach may exceed traditional methods regarding risk accuracy and help drive innovation in the insurance industry.

1. 简介Introduction

保险承保——评估客户风险的过程——是盈利能力和长期业务可持续性的基石。传统方法通常依赖于冗长的、手动填写的调查问卷，容易出现人为错误、欺诈，并且无法捕捉个人的细微差别Insurance underwriting — the process of evaluating client risk — is a cornerstone of profitability and long-term business sustainability. Traditional methods, often relying on lengthy, manually filled questionnaires, are prone to human error, fraud, and fail to capture individual nuances(b1, )，导致低风险和高风险客户的保费不公平。, leading to unfair premiums for both low and high risk clients.

人工智能 (AI) 的指数级增长，加上来自数字平台和社交网络的大数据，提供了变革机遇The exponential growth of Artificial Intelligence (AI), coupled with Big Data from digital platforms and social networks, offers a transformative opportunity(b2, )。自然语言处理 (NLP) 和大型语言模型 (LLM) 等创新可以增强与客户的沟通，而先进的算法（神经网络、决策树）可以增强风险预测. Innovations like Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Large Language Models (LLMs) can enhance communication with customers, while advanced algorithms (neural networks, decision trees) can boost risk prediction(b3, )。然而，大多数保险调查问卷仍然是静态的，忽略了来自外部来源的有价值的背景信息。. However, most insurance questionnaires remain static, ignoring valuable contextual information from external sources.

这项工作引入了一种新颖的方法，该方法使用人工智能通过结合外部用户见解来动态生成个性化保险调查问卷，从而实现适合每个人的定制风险评估，与传统的一刀切形式形成鲜明对比。然而，将人工智能驱动的自适应问卷引入现实世界的保险环境会带来几个关键挑战：This work introduces a novel approach that uses AI to dynamically generate personalized insurance questionnaires by incorporating external user insights, enabling tailored risk assessment that adapts to each individual, contrasting with conventional one-size-fits-all forms. However, bringing AI-driven adaptive questionnaires into real-world insurance contexts introduces several key challenges:

  • •

    多线可扩展性：当前大多数模型都是针对单一保险领域进行优化的，因此很难适应人寿、健康和财产保险等多样化的产品线。Multiline scalability: Most current models are optimized for a single insurance domain, making it difficult to adapt to diverse product lines such as life, health, and property insurance.

  • •

    数据集成：外部用户洞察通常是非结构化的、依赖于上下文且嘈杂的，需要进行高级预处理以实现 AI 可解释性，同时还要满足严格的隐私和合规性要求Data integration: External user insights are often unstructured, context-dependent, and noisy, requiring advanced preprocessing for AI interpretability, while also meeting strict privacy and compliance requirements(b4, ; b5, ).

  • •

    公平性：不完善的承保模式可能会产生有偏见的报价，对某些客户群造成不成比例的影响，并可能损害信任Fairness: Imperfect underwriting models risk producing biased offers, disproportionately affecting certain customer segments and potentially undermining trust(b6, ).

本文通过设计、实施和评估 ARQuest（自适应风险提问）来解决这些挑战，ARQuest 是一种自适应问卷框架，利用法学硕士和检索增强生成 (RAG) 来个性化承保流程、整合异构来源并确保风险分析的公平性和透明度。这项工作是与高等教育机构和跨国专业服务公司合作的成果This paper addresses these challenges through the design, implementation, and evaluation of ARQuest (Adaptive Risk Questioning), an adaptive questionnaire framework that leverages LLMs and Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) to personalize underwriting flows, integrate heterogeneous sources, and ensure fairness and transparency in risk profiling. This work results from a collaboration with a higher education institution and a multinational professional services company(b7, )，确保科学相关性和行业适用性。, ensuring scientific relevance and industry applicability.

2. 最先进的State of the Art

当前系统中很大程度上忽视了保险调查问卷的个性化。风险预测任务具有一组与订阅旅程个性化相关的底层流程The personalization of insurance questionnaires is largely overlooked in current systems. Risk prediction tasks have an underlying set of processes that are relevant for the individualization of the subscription journey(b3, )，例如保险公司收集申请人数据的方式、用于分析风险的人工智能模型和技术，以及所有相关的道德问题。, such as the way insurers collect data about an applicant, the AI models and techniques employed to analyze risk, and all surrounding ethical concerns.

2.1. 数据整合Data Integration

随着大数据的大规模应用和数据驱动的机器学习模型的兴起With Big Data available on a larger scale and data-driven ML models on the rise(b2, )，保险公司不仅仅依靠表格和报告来了解个人的风险状况。社交媒体、电子健康记录、可穿戴设备和地理信息是增强这些系统的数据源, insurance companies do not just rely on forms and reports to understand the risk profile of an individual. Social media, electronic health records, wearable devices, and geographical information are data sources that enhance these systems(b8, )。 API 和网络抓取方法提取见解，然后通过转换过程将其转换为可管理的格式. APIs and web scraping methods extract insights, which then go through a transformation process that converts them to a manageable format(b9, )。 NLP 技术专注于处理数据中发现的非结构化自然语言. NLP techniques are focused on processing the unstructured natural language found in the data(b10, )，而图像可以通过 BLIP 等高级模型来解释, while images can be interpreted by advanced models like BLIP(b11, )，它生成文本描述。然而，社交媒体挖掘很容易受到用户生活方式解释方面的细微差别的影响, which generates textual descriptions. However, social media mining is susceptible to nuances regarding the interpretation of a user’s lifestyle(b10, ).

2.2. 个性化问答Personalized Q&A

法学硕士和变压器可以处理特定的数据集以生成个性化的问题和答案，这可以取代先入为主的传统保险调查问卷。微调和提示调整是伟大的现代技术，它们利用这些预先训练的通用语言模型，并将它们适应或上下文化到目标领域LLMs and transformers can process specific datasets to generate personalized questions and answers, which could substitute the preconceived traditional insurance questionnaires. Fine-tuning and prompt-tuning are great modern techniques that leverage these pre-trained general language models and either adapt or contextualize them to the target domain(b12, )。使用 GPT、T5 或 BERT 变体等知名模型，可以自动创建不同类型的问题，然后通过人工评估和适当的指标进行过滤. Using well-known models like GPT, T5, or BERT variants, different types of questions can be created automatically and then filtered via human evaluation and adequate metrics(b13, )。可以使用相似的策略来实现问答，再加上语义相似度来进行答案质量评估. Question answering can be achieved using similar strategies, coupled with semantic similarity for answer quality evaluation(b14, )。采用基于 RAG 的架构来减轻这些场景中的 LLM 幻觉，过滤所提供的相关内容. RAG-based architectures are adopted to mitigate LLM hallucinations in these scenarios, filtering the relevant content that is provided(b15, )，如图所示, as visible in Figure1.

了解法学硕士对其分析的信心程度也很有用。答案的置信度可以从生成的单词的内部概率中提取It is also useful to understand how confident an LLM is in its analysis. The confidence of an answer can be extracted from the internal probabilities of the words generated(b16, ).

2.3. 风险评估Risk Assessment

在风险评估方面，保险公司通常会实施各种机器学习算法（例如随机森林、XGBoost 等）Regarding risk assessment, insurers typically implement a variety of ML algorithms (e.g., random forest, XGBoost, etc.)(b17, )甚至是 Transformer，在解释用户风险方面优于传统模型and even transformers, which outperform traditional models at interpreting user risk(b18, )。这些模型分析年龄、体力活动、慢性疾病、事故历史和天气状况等变量，以推断个人的风险是否低或高，并执行欺诈检测等其他任务。例如，GPT-4V 是一个功能强大的模型，根据智能提示，可以处理不同类型输入的内容以捕获有趣的风险指标. The models analyze variables such as age, physical activity, chronic diseases, accident history, and weather conditions to infer if an individual is low or high risk, with additional tasks like fraud detection being executed as well. GPT-4V, for instance, is a powerful model that, following an intelligent prompt, can process the contents of different types of input to capture interesting risk indicators(b19, ).

2.4. 道德问题Ethical Concerns

由于保险对用户生计的影响，在集成机器学习模型时需要考虑道德方面。遵守 GDPR 等监管框架Due to the insurance implications for user livelihoods, ethical aspects need to be considered when integrating ML models. Compliance with regulatory frameworks like the GDPR(b20, )和美国建筑师协会and the AIA(b21, )应该是一个优先事项，因为保险应用程序本质上是一个高风险系统should be a priority, as insurance apps are inherently a high-risk system(b21, )。保护消费者数据的尝试包括数据加密和匿名化. Attempts at protecting consumer data include data encryption and anonymization(b10, )。此外，模型歧视被视为另一个重大挑战，因为来自少数群体的客户可能会受到有偏见的溢价归因的影响. In addition, model discrimination is presented as another vital challenge, as customers from minority groups may be affected by biased premium attribution(b6, )。重采样技术有助于转换数据集，使其能够代表总体. Resampling techniques aid in transforming a dataset so that it becomes representative of the population(b22, )。选择适当的评估基准和可解释性方法，例如SHAP. Selecting proper evaluation benchmarks and explainability methods, such as SHAP(b23, ), 石灰, LIME(b23, )，或基于提示的技术, or prompting-based techniques(b24, )尽可能将公平性和透明度纳入分析和决策过程也至关重要。, is also essential to integrate fairness and transparency into the analytical and decision-making process as much as possible.

3. AR探索ARQuest

ARQuest（自适应风险提问）框架是一种通过调整用户的问题来个性化保险调查问卷的解决方案，旨在改善订阅体验和更精确的风险评估。The ARQuest (Adaptive Risk Questioning) framework is presented as a solution to personalize insurance questionnaires by adjusting the questions to the users, aiming at an improved subscription experience and a more precise risk assessment.

框架，详细如图The framework, detailed in Figure2，分为四个独特的模块来完成不同的任务：, is divided into four unique modules for different tasks:

  1. (1)

    用户分析：User Profiling:申请人提供一些基本的个人信息，例如位置，用于检索相关地理风险指标；来自外部平台的数据也可以共享，所有这些信息都经过处理和汇总。the applicant provides some basic personal information, such as the location, which is used to retrieve relevant geographical risk indicators; data from external platforms can also be shared, with all this information being processed and aggregated.

  2. (2)

    响应预测：Response Forecasting:通过分析收集到的见解，法学硕士可以从预定义的列表中识别风险；这些风险都有分配的问题，法学硕士会提前预测相应的答案；用户可以选择查看并纠正这些预测。by analyzing the collected insights, an LLM identifies risks from a pre-defined list; these risks have questions assigned, and the LLM predicts the respective answers beforehand; optionally, the user can review and correct these predictions.

  3. (3)

    动态提问：Dynamic Questioning:有些风险无法从初始数据中检测出来，因此法学硕士（从剩余的）中选择下一个因素来询问用户；重复这个过程，用户不断提供答案，直到模型认为获得了足够的信息；简而言之，当剩下的因素都没有影响力或可能产生危险的反应时，法学硕士会被提示停止提问过程。some risks are not detectable from the initial data, so the LLM chooses the next factor (from the remaining) to question the user; this process is repeated, and the user continuously provides answers until the model considers enough information is acquired; briefly, the LLM is prompted to stop the questioning process when none of the remaining factors are both impactful or likely to yield a risky response.

  4. (4)

    风险评估：Risk Assessment:根据完成的调查问卷，使用特定模型或算法计算总体风险评分；还使用猜测和答案之间的相似性度量来识别法学硕士的预测被用户大幅纠正的情况；如果这种情况经常发生，要么是法学硕士在分析中表现不佳，要么是申请人故意误导了评估。based on the completed questionnaire, a global risk score is computed using a specific model or algorithm; cases where the LLM’s predictions are drastically corrected by the user are also identified using a similarity measure between guesses and answers; if this occurs frequently, either the LLM performed poorly in its analysis or the applicant intentionally misled the evaluation.

3.3. 问卷生成Questionnaire Generation

对于每个用户，数据集中所有可能问题的答案都是根据存储的个人信息和概率预先计算的。这些保存的答案充当“基本事实”，并在执行测试时自动填写调查问卷。例如，考虑一个有关家庭糖尿病的问题，如果用户的健康记录提到糖尿病，并且区域指标表明该疾病的患病率很高，则答案概率将倾向于肯定答案，因为家庭成员很可能患有这种疾病，而这种疾病具有遗传成分。For each user, answers to all possible questions from the dataset are pre-computed, resorting to the individual information stored and probabilities. These saved answers act as ”ground truths” and automate questionnaire filling upon the execution of tests. Considering a question addressing family diabetes, for instance, if the user’s health records mention diabetes and the regional indicators present a high prevalence of the disease, the answer probabilities will favor a positive answer, as it is likely that a family member suffers from that condition, which possesses a hereditary component to it.

3.5. 风险评分Risk Scoring

根据调查问卷内容确定申请人的总体风险评分的方法有多种。一方面，概率人工智能模型可以通过现实世界趋势的训练获得准确的结果，但由于其黑盒性质，存在可解释性问题，结果可能受到隐藏模式的影响。另一方面，确定性模型允许对所有变量进行完全控制，即使它增加了实现全局优化的难度。There are multiple ways to determine the overall risk score of an applicant based on the questionnaire content. On the one hand, a probabilistic AI model can achieve accurate results through training with real-world trends, but there are interpretability issues due to its black-box nature, with results potentially being influenced by hidden patterns. On the other hand, a deterministic model allows for total control over all variables, even if it increases the difficulty of reaching global optimization.

这里的首要任务是拥有一个易于分析的用于比较目的的可靠算法。因此，系统采用单调可加风险模型，其中风险从零开始，只能上升，并随着非最佳答案而增加。此外，相关因素之间的某些反应组合会增加风险的附加值。The priority here is to have a solid algorithm for comparison purposes that is easy to analyze. Therefore, the system resorts to a monotonic additive risk model, where risk starts at zero and can only go up, increasing with non-optimal answers. Moreover, some combinations of responses between related factors increase risk by an additional value.

4. 评价Evaluation

进行了两个主要实验来评估该解决方案。第一个使用综合申请人数据集将传统调查问卷与由 GPT-3.5 Turbo 和 GPT-4.1 支持的自适应版本进行比较。第二个依赖于一小部分真实用户的参与来收集用户体验见解和总体印象。Two major experiments were conducted to evaluate the solution. The first used the synthetic applicant dataset to compare traditional questionnaires with adaptive versions powered by GPT-3.5 Turbo and GPT-4.1. The second relied on the participation of a small sample of real users to collect user experience insights and overall impressions.

4.1. 用户综合评价Synthetic User Evaluation

85 名综合申请人中的每一位都进行了一次传统订阅和两次动态订阅，其中一个使用 GPT-3.5 Turbo 模型，另一个使用 GPT-4.1，以评估旧的法学硕士与更新、能力更强的法学硕士相比是否能够应对挑战。Each of the 85 synthetic applicants performed a single traditional subscription and two dynamic subscriptions, one using the GPT-3.5 Turbo model and another with GPT-4.1, to assess whether the older LLM is up to the challenge compared to the newer, more capable one.

此外，这些人工用户之前被分配了“真实”风险评分，该评分是根据风险数据集中所有可能问题的答案权重之和计算得出的，而不仅仅是传统或动态调查问卷中出现的问题。Furthermore, these artificial users are previously assigned with ”true” risk scores, calculated from the sum of the weights of the answers to all possible questions from the risk dataset, not just the ones that appear in the traditional or dynamic questionnaires.

4.3. 讨论Discussion

在提出方法论和分析之后，研究问题就可以得到正确的回答。After the presented methodologies and analysis, the research questions can be properly answered.

问题一：RQ1:

AI如何清晰生成个性化保险调查问卷？How can AI generate personalized insurance questionnaires in a clear way?
通过遵循 ARQuest 框架。简而言之，人工智能驱动的系统可以简单地遵循以下步骤：By following the ARQuest framework. Briefly, an AI-powered system can simply follow these steps:

  1. (1):

    分析有关申请人的见解Analyze insights about the applicant

  2. (2):

    从预先制定的清单中识别风险Identify risks from a pre-made list

  3. (3):

    提前预测这些答案，自动完成调查问卷。Predict those answers beforehand, automating questionnaire completion.

  4. (4):

    解决其他因素，一一质疑。Address additional factors, questioning them one by one.

这是一个简单的解决方案，客户和保险公司都很容易理解。This is a straightforward solution that is easily understandable by both customers and insurers.

问题2：RQ2:

整合外部用户对保险的见解时会出现哪些问题？What concerns arise when integrating external user insights for insurance?
主要是数据处理和道德问题。替代来源通常由非结构化数据组成，这些数据可能与上下文相关且不明确。必须仔细考虑转换过程背后的决策，以便更容易地解释法学硕士。该系统还应确保透明度，以与客户建立信任关系，并以保证公平决策的方式接触每个申请人。Mainly data processing and ethical concerns. Alternative sources are usually composed of unstructured data, which can be substantially contextual and ambiguous. The decisions behind the transformation processes must be thought of carefully to allow easier LLM interpretability. The system should also ensure transparency to build a trusting relationship with the customer and approach each applicant in a way that guarantees fair decision-making.

问题3：RQ3:

当前的人工智能模型在捕捉个体风险因素方面面临哪些限制？What limitations do current AI models face in capturing individual risk factors?
考虑到法学硕士在这种情况下的参与，提示长度和模型可解释性之间存在重大权衡。向模型提供数据集中所有问题的全部内容以供选择会减慢响应速度并增加发生幻觉的可能性。或者，简单地列出所有风险因素的名称以供选择会导致提示中的细微差别显着丧失。Considering the involvement of LLMs in this context, there is a major trade-off between prompt length and model interpretability. Providing the model with the entire content of all questions from the dataset to choose from slows down responses and increases the likelihood of hallucinations occurring. Alternatively, simply listing the names of all risk factors for selection causes a significant loss of nuance within the prompt.

问题四：RQ4:

模型置信度和可解释性如何影响人工智能风险评估？How do model confidence and explainability affect AI risk assessments?
通过提高人工智能代理对申请人和保险公司进行的风险分析的理解，从而提高可信度。预测的置信度对于识别高置信度预测被用户大幅改变的问题非常重要。这些解释提高了透明度，对于逐个案例分析后的校对风险检测至关重要。By improving comprehension of the risk analysis performed by the AI agent for both applicants and insurers, thereby boosting trustworthiness. The confidence level of the predictions is important to identify questions where a high-confidence prediction is drastically altered by the user. The explanations increase transparency and are crucial in proof-checking risk detection, following a case by case analysis.

问题5：RQ5:

个性化承保解决方案如何扩展到不同类型的保险？How can personalized underwriting solutions scale to different types of insurance?
ARQuest 框架演示了如何实现这一点。对于给定的保险产品线，系统需要一个包含以下组件的知识库：上下文相关的地理指标、一组高质量的风险和相关问题、根据提供的用户答案计算风险评分的模型，以及帮助检测模型猜测和用户更正之间差异的词嵌入。The ARQuest framework demonstrates how this can be achieved. For a given insurance line, the system needs a knowledge base with the following components: contextually relevant geographical indicators, a quality set of risks and associated questions, a model that calculates a risk score based on the provided user answers, and word embeddings that help detect discrepancies between model guesses and user corrections.

5. 结论Conclusion

这项研究提出了保险承保的个性化调查问卷，旨在改善跨多个领域的风险分析。它弥补了现有研究中的一个空白，即质疑过程经常被忽视，而且很少有解决方案能够无缝地适应保险行业。尽管越来越多地利用替代数据源，但风险评估经常被委托给能够捕获复杂模式的预先训练的语言模型。考虑到保险的敏感性，此类解决方案还必须确保透明度、公平性和监管合规性。This study advances personalized questionnaires for insurance underwriting, aiming to improve risk profiling across multiple domains. It addresses a gap in existing research, where the questioning process is often overlooked and few solutions adapt seamlessly across insurance sectors. While alternative data sources are increasingly leveraged, risk assessment is frequently delegated to pre-trained language models capable of capturing complex patterns. Given the sensitivity of insurance, such solutions must also ensure transparency, fairness, and regulatory compliance.

拟议的 ARQuest 框架通过整合外部用户见解来识别相关因素，从而减少手动输入，从而实现个性化风险评估。它动态地选择信息最丰富的问题​​来完成风险概况，并结合了检测模型推理和用户响应之间不一致的机制，从而增强了自动化的可靠性。The proposed ARQuest framework personalizes risk assessment by integrating external user insights to identify relevant factors, reducing manual input. It dynamically selects the most informative questions to complete the risk profile and incorporates mechanisms to detect inconsistencies between model reasoning and user responses, strengthening automation reliability.

通过两个实验实施和评估人寿保险原型：结合地理、健康、健身和社交媒体输入的综合档案，以及通过移动应用程序进行的真实用户测试。虽然传统问卷在检测某些风险因素方面略优于自适应版本，但用户始终更喜欢动态方法，因为它的效率、上下文相关性和改进的体验。A life insurance prototype was implemented and evaluated through two experiments: synthetic profiles combining geographic, health, fitness, and social media inputs, and real-user testing via a mobile application. While traditional questionnaires marginally outperformed the adaptive version in detecting certain risk factors, users consistently preferred the dynamic approach for its efficiency, contextual relevance, and improved experience.

采用 ARQuest 可为保险公司带来切实的运营和商业优势。自适应询问与外部数据集成相结合，缩短了申请和承保时间，降低了人工处理成本，并通过更顺畅、更个性化的流程提高了客户满意度。通过以更少的步骤捕获准确的数据，保险公司可以提高预测准确性、简化工作流程并完善定价策略，从而增强其竞争地位。Adopting ARQuest offers insurers tangible operational and commercial advantages. Adaptive questioning, coupled with external data integration, shortens application and underwriting times, reduces manual processing costs, and enhances customer satisfaction through a smoother, more personalized process. By capturing accurate data in fewer steps, insurers can improve predictive accuracy, streamline workflows, and refine pricing strategies, strengthening their competitive position.

ARQuest框架的未来增强将集中在四个主要方向。首先，采用具有更大上下文窗口和改进推理能力的下一代法学硕士预计将能够更准确地检测微妙和复杂的风险模式。其次，该框架应该在健康、财产和汽车等多个保险领域进行测试，以评估其对不同风险领域和不同监管要求的适应性。第三，在高质量、特定领域的数据集上微调底层法学硕士可以提高一致性和预测准确性，减少对单独即时工程的依赖。最后，集成能够与外部工具和数据源自主交互的代理人工智能功能将支持承保流程的完全端到端自动化，进一步减少运营开销并提高可扩展性。Future enhancements to the ARQuest framework will focus on four main directions. First, the adoption of next-generation LLMs with larger context windows and improved reasoning capabilities is expected to enable more accurate detection of subtle and complex risk patterns. Second, the framework should be tested across multiple insurance lines — such as health, property, and auto — to evaluate its adaptability to diverse risk domains and varying regulatory requirements. Third, fine-tuning the underlying LLM on high-quality, domain-specific datasets could improve both consistency and predictive accuracy, reducing reliance on prompt engineering alone. Finally, integrating agentic AI capabilities capable of autonomously interacting with external tools and data sources would support full end-to-end automation of the underwriting process, further reducing operational overhead and increasing scalability.
