1 简介Introduction

工具集成大语言模型 (LLM) 的最新进展使网络搜索成为信息查找代理的核心能力Recent advances in tool-integrated large language models (LLMs) have made web search a core capability of information-seeking agents(Yao et al., 2022; Nakano et al., 2021; Jin et al., 2025a; Li et al., 2026b)。这些代理可以迭代地搜索、浏览和推理外部源，将语言模型扩展到其参数中可用的知识之外。随着该领域朝着广泛、开放领域和长期任务发展. These agents can iteratively search, browse, and reason over external sources, extending language models beyond the knowledge available in their parameters. As the field moves toward broad, open-domain and long-horizon tasks(Wong et al., 2025; Zhu et al., 2026)，代理人必须收集大量事实，协调不同来源的证据，并产生具有可验证引用的结构化答案。, an agent must gather large collections of facts, reconcile evidence across sources, and produce a structured answer with verifiable citations.

然而，仅靠更强的搜索能力并不能使这个过程可靠。随着交互历史的增长，代理会忘记已经确定的内容和尚未解决的内容。证据被隐藏在上下文中，导致遗漏、冗余收集和相互矛盾的主张难以被发现。当搜索路径没有产生有用的信息时，单个代理可能会重复发出类似的搜索或继续探索相同的死胡同，浪费预算并降低最终答案的质量。简单地添加更多代理并不能消除这些问题：并行工作人员可能会重复工作，在预期字段上存在分歧，或者在系统等待最慢的任务时使执行槽空闲。Stronger search capability alone, however, does not make this process reliable. As interaction histories grow, agents lose track of what has been established and what remains unresolved. Evidence becomes buried in context, making omissions, redundant collection, and conflicting claims difficult to detect. When a search path yields no useful information, a single agent may repeatedly issue similar searches or continue exploring the same dead end, wasting its budget and degrading the final answer. Simply adding more agents does not eliminate these problems: parallel workers may duplicate effort, disagree on the intended fields, or leave execution slots idle while the system waits for the slowest task.

这些失败的出现是因为传统代理将计划、进展、证据和失败视为短暂的对话内容。These failures arise because conventional agents treat plans, progress, evidence, and failures as transient conversation content.搜索状态应由系统维护，而不是从交互历史记录中重复推断Search state should be maintained by the system rather than inferred repeatedly from interaction history。一个长期的搜索系统应该公开哪些实体和属性已被覆盖，保留每个值的来源，协调工作人员解决明显的差距，并在执行停滞时进行干预。它还应该跨会话保留有效的搜索和访问模式，而不是为每个任务重新发现它们。. A long-horizon search system should expose what entities and attributes have been covered, preserve the provenance of every value, coordinate workers around explicit gaps, and intervene when execution stalls. It should also retain effective search and access patterns across sessions instead of rediscovering them for every task.

6.1 代理信息寻求Agentic Information Seeking

工具集成的语言代理交织搜索、浏览、推理、证据收集和综合Tool-integrated language agents interleave search, browsing, reasoning, evidence collection, and synthesis(Nakano et al., 2021; Yao et al., 2022; Trivedi et al., 2023; Qin et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2025b; Jin et al., 2025a; Li et al., 2026b; Zheng et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025)。他们的能力通过搜索策略优化得到了提升. Their capabilities have advanced through search policy optimization(Qian and Liu, 2025; Luo et al., 2025)、任务分解, task decomposition(Chen et al., 2025b, 2026)，综合交互数据, synthetic interaction data(Tao et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025)，以及基于强化的后训练, and reinforcement-based post-training(Wang et al., 2025b; Jin et al., 2025a; Gao et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2025a; Zheng et al., 2025)。然而，进展通常仍然存在于不断增长的互动历史中，掩盖了既定的证据和未解决的问题。. Yet progress typically remains in growing interaction histories, obscuring established evidence and unresolved questions.

广泛的信息寻求也被表述为结构化或面向表格的完成Broad information seeking has also been formulated as structured or table-oriented completion(Tao et al., 2025; Wong et al., 2025; Zhu et al., 2026; Lan et al., 2026; Chen et al., 2026; Huang et al., 2026)。此类输出公开缺失的实体和属性并支持并行收集，但不捕获任务依赖性、相互冲突的观察、出处或失败的尝试。. 6.2 多代理编排系统Multi-Agent Orchestration Systems

多代理框架通过专门的角色、消息交换和结构化工作流程分解复杂的任务Multi-agent frameworks decompose complex tasks through specialized roles, message exchange, and structured workflows(Wu et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2025b; Fourney et al., 2024);分层方法将战略规划与专业执行脱钩; hierarchical approaches decouple strategic planning from specialized execution(Jin et al., 2025b)，而结构化执行系统区分依赖子任务和并行子任务并同时执行独立分支, while structured execution systems distinguish dependent from parallel sub-tasks and execute independent branches concurrently(Kim et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025)。然而，协调状态通常保留在对话、计划或一般任务分类账中，使得冗余收集、陈旧工作、覆盖范围差距和闲置容量在长期搜索过程中难以明确管理。. However, coordination state usually remains in conversations, plans, or general task ledgers, making redundant collection, stale work, coverage gaps, and idle capacity difficult to manage explicitly during long-horizon search.

代理内存和上下文管理的最新进展通过反射、内存存储、虚拟上下文、压缩或轨迹整合解决了相关挑战Recent advances in agent memory and context management address related challenges through reflection, memory stores, virtual context, compression, or trajectory consolidation(Park et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023; Zhong et al., 2024; Packer et al., 2023; Jiang et al., 2023; Ye et al., 2025)。相比之下，面向搜索的上下文管理 (SOCM) 将中间搜索状态具体化为前沿任务、证据图、覆盖图和故障记忆，并为每个代理提供特定于角色的预测。. In contrast, Search-Oriented Context Management (SOCM) externalizes intermediate search state as a Frontier Task, Evidence Graph, Coverage Map, and Failure Memory, with role-specific projections for each agent.

最近的调查将代理工具视为一流的基础设施层，管理执行、工具、上下文、状态、生命周期、可观察性和评估，而工程报告则强调可靠的长期代理的可检查环境、反馈循环和上下文管理Recent surveys treat the agent harness as a first-class infrastructure layer governing execution, tools, context, state, lifecycle, observability, and evaluation, while engineering reports emphasize inspectable environments, feedback loops, and context curation for reliable long-horizon agents(Li et al., 2026a; Meng et al., 2026; Lopopolo, 2026; Anthropic, 2025)。先前的系统通过环境中间件、消息驱动和容错运行时、操作系统抽象、高效的模型工具拦截和运行时约束来实例化该层的一部分. Prior systems instantiate parts of this layer through environment middleware, message-driven and fault-tolerant runtimes, operating-system abstractions, efficient model–tool interception, and runtime constraints(Gu et al., 2024; Gao et al., 2024; Mei et al., 2025; Abhyankar et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025a; Ning et al., 2026; Zhang et al., 2026)。的. The搜索操作系统SearchOS搜索工具中间件框架通过准备特定于角色的上下文、验证和奠定证据产生的观察结果以及监控重复、停滞和预算耗尽，将这些想法应用于长期信息搜索。Search Tool Middleware Harness applies these ideas to long-horizon information seeking by preparing role-specific context, validating and grounding evidence-producing observations, and monitoring repetition, stalls, and budget exhaustion.

7 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍In this work, we introduce搜索操作系统SearchOS，一个多代理框架，将长视野开放域搜索外部化为系统维护的状态。我们将请求制定为带有基础引用的关系模式补全，并使用面向搜索的上下文管理 (SOCM) 来组织覆盖范围差距、证据和中间搜索工件。管道并行编排可以解决未解决的差距，而我们的搜索工具中间件线束则准备上下文、地面观察并处理停顿和预算限制。分层技能库提供可重用的编排、搜索策略和源访问知识。, a multi-agent framework that externalizes long-horizon open-domain search as system-maintained state. We formulate requests as relational schema completion with grounded citations and use Search-Oriented Context Management (SOCM) to organize coverage gaps, evidence, and intermediate search artifacts. Pipeline-parallel orchestration dispatches unresolved gaps, while our Search Tool Middleware Harness prepares context, grounds observations, and handles stalls and budget limits. A hierarchical skill library supplies reusable orchestration, search-strategy, and source-access knowledge.
