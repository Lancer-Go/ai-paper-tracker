Tripwire：通过统计认证的安全神经元触发一致拒绝Tripwire: Triggering Aligned Refusal via Statistically Certified Safety Neurons

Wei Zhao    Zhe Li    Peixin Zhang    Jun Sun
摘要Abstract

神经元和路径级干预提供了保护大型语言模型 (LLM) 免受越狱攻击的最细粒度途径，但现有方法达不到这一承诺，即它们经常会严重损害模型的实用性。具体来说，其中一项工作是抑制有毒神经元以消除有害语义，但由于此类语义分布在整个网络中，阻塞每条路径都会迫使大量干预足迹。另一条研究重点是使用外部分类器识别安全神经元。虽然很有希望，但现有的方法受到神经元损害，而这对于模型的实用性也很重要。此外，这两种方法始终保持开启状态，因此即使不存在攻击，也会干扰每个良性请求。为了解决这些限制，我们提出Neuron- and path-level interventions offer the finest-grained route to defending large language models (LLMs) against jailbreak attacks, yet existing methods fall short of this promise, i.e., they often compromise model utility significantly. Specifically, one line of work suppresses toxic neurons to erase harmful semantics, but since such semantics are distributed across the network, blocking every pathway forces a large intervention footprint. An alternative line of research focus on identify safety neurons using external classifiers. While promising, the existing approaches suffer from compromising neurons that are important for the model utility as well. Moreover, both approaches remain always on and thus perturb every benign request even when no attack is present. To address these limitations, we present绊线TripWire，一种无需训练的防御，首先通过错误发现率控制下的每个神经元假设检验以及实用程序特异性过滤器来识别安全特定神经元。基于这种识别，触发式钳位将选定的神经元保持在有害条件平均激活状态，注入内部有害输入信号，触发在对齐过程中学到的拒绝行为。然后通过两种可证明等效的部署模式来实现钳位，即检测器门控推理时间干预和离线偏差补丁权重编辑。四个与安全相关的法学硕士和四个代表性攻击的广泛实验表明, a training-free defense that first identifies safety-specific neurons through per-neuron hypothesis tests under false-discovery-rate control together with a utility-specificity filter. Based on this identification, a trigger-style clamp holds the selected neurons at their harmful-conditional mean activations, injecting an internal harmful-input signal that triggers the refusal behavior learned during alignment. The clamp is then realized by two provably equivalent deployment modes, namely a detector-gated inference-time intervention and an offline bias-patch weight edit. Extensive experiments across four safety-aligned LLMs and four representative attacks demonstrate that绊线TripWire在MT-Bench上，平均攻击成功率最多降低至2.0%，而效用下降仅0.5%至5.3%，是所有防御中最小的。代码可在 https://anonymous.4open.science/r/Tripwire-65C4 获取。reduces the average attack success rate to at most 2.0% while incurring a utility drop of only 0.5% to 5.3% on MT-Bench, the smallest among all defenses. Despite these advances, existing defenses exhibit a persistent safety and utility trade-off: strengthening refusal behavior degrades performance on benign tasks. However, existing neuron-level methods fall short of this promise in practice for three reasons.

结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们诊断了为什么现有的神经元级越狱防御无法兑现细粒度保护的承诺：擦除式方法必须阻止分布在网络上的有害语义，基于分类器的归因无法将安全神经元与一般重要的神经元分开，并且即使不存在攻击，始终在线的干预也会扰乱良性请求。这些可修复的问题，而不是神经元粒度本身，才是观察到的效用成本的真正来源。In this work, we diagnose why existing neuron-level jailbreak defenses fail to deliver the promise of fine-grained protection: erasure-style methods must block harmful semantics distributed across the network, classifier-based attribution cannot separate safety neurons from generally important ones, and always-on interventions perturb benign requests even when no attack is present. These fixable problems, rather than the neuron granularity itself, are the true sources of the observed utility cost.
