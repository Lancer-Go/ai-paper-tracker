评估静态和演化数据解释方法的挑战Challenges in Evaluating Explanation Methods for Static and Evolving Data

Jerzy Stefanowski
摘要Abstract

本文解决了可解释人工智能（XAI）在评估不足方面的局限性。它们通过用于偏差检测和概念遗忘的 DetoxAI 图像识别系统进行说明。然后，提出了用于解释图像分类的方法的以人为本的评估示例。本文进一步探讨了使解释适应具有概念漂移的不断演变的数据流的方法。讨论了针对该问题采用反事实的经验。最后，它与跟踪数据、模型和解释的共同演化的挑战有关。This paper addresses the limitations of Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) with respect to insufficient evaluation. They are illustrated through the DetoxAI image recognition system for bias detection and concept unlearning. Then, an example of a human-grounded evaluation of methods for explaining image classification is presented. The paper further explores methods for adapting explanations to evolving data streams with concept drift. Experiences with adapting counterfactuals for this problem are discussed. Finally it is related to the challenges of tracking the co-evolution of data, models, and explanations.111这篇论文已被 J.Nalepa（编辑）Explainable AI in Space 接受发表。 IJCAI-ECAI 2026 不来梅 EASi 2026 研讨会论文集，Springer CCIS vol 3107 (2016)。This paper has been accepted for a publication in J.Nalepa (ed) Explainable AI in Space. Proceedings of EASi 2026 Workshop at IJCAI-ECAI 2026 Bremen, Springer CCIS vol 3107 (2016).

1 简介Introduction

近几十年来，人工智能系统的发展取得了许多成功，但也使人们越来越意识到风险和满足额外要求的必要性。其中许多涉及负责任或值得信赖的人工智能，其中系统透明度的需求以及解释其工作原理的能力变得很重要The development of Artificial Intelligence systems in recent decades has led to many successes, but also to a growing awareness of the risks and the need to meet additional requirements. Many of them concern Responsible or Trustworthy AI, where the need for transparency in the systems and the ability to explain how they work become important[14]。当前的机器学习（ML）系统通常基于越来越复杂的深度网络，这些网络对用户来说是不透明的，并且不提供此类解释。. The current Machine Learning (ML)systems are often based on more and more complex deep networks, which are opaque to users and do not provide such explanations.

这种可解释性的缺乏给许多领域的有效应用带来了障碍，特别是在涉及人的高风险决策的领域。特别是它涉及医疗应用，至少有以下原因This lack of explainability poses an obstacle to effective application in many fields, especially where high-stakes decisions involving people are made. In particular, it concerns medical applications, at least for the following reasons[23]:

  • •

    可解释性提高了临床信任：医生必须在高风险治疗之前验证输出背后的临床逻辑。Explainability improves clinical trust: physicians must verify the clinical logic behind an output before high-risk treatments.

  • •

    它可能会发现一些错误、数据集偏差和错误相关性，从而防止错误的自动诊断决策。It may discover some errors, dataset biases and false correlations, preventing wrong automatic diagnostic decisions.

  • •

    支持法律问责：医疗责任在于人；因此医生应该要求可解释的证据来证明他们的行为合理。It supports legal accountability: medical responsibility lies with humans; so physician should require interpretable evidence to justify their actions.

  • •

    它可以用于科学发现：可解释的模型可以突出新颖的模式，推进医学研究和教育。It could be used for scientific discovery: explainable models can highlight novel patterns, advancing medical research and education.

同样，由于关键任务验证和故障取证、极端领域适应或严格的监管合规性，解释对于关键系统（包括航空航天）极其重要。由于许多此类系统都是自主的，并且部署在高风险环境中，深度学习的“黑匣子”性质带来了严重的运营风险。例如，在关键任务航空航天应用中，例如航天器健康监测和异常检测，集成可解释性是保证操作信任和系统可用性的严格先决条件Similarly, explanations are extremely important for critical systems (including aerospace) due to mission-critical verification and failure forensics, extreme domain adaptation or strict regulatory compliance. As many such systems are autonomous and are deployed in high-stakes environments, the ’black-box’ nature of deep learning introduces severe operational risks. For instance, in mission-critical aerospace applications, such as spacecraft health monitoring and anomaly detection, integrating explainability is a strict prerequisite to guarantee operational trust and system usability[29].

上述论点为可解释人工智能（XAI）的开发和使用提供了动机The above arguments provide motivations for the development and use of Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI)[8]。不幸的是，在XAI中，现在有一种趋势是不断引入越来越多的新方法，而对其有用性的评估却太有限. Unfortunately, in XAI there is now a trend to constantly introduce more and more new methods, while the assessment of their usefulness is too limited[20]。此外，XAI 方法的比较研究数量太有限，研究措施的范围太窄。从某种意义上说，这类似于今年年初围绕监督分类方法的发展及其实际用途所面临的严峻形势，用大卫·汉德（David Hand）的名言来说，这更像是一种“进步的幻觉”. Moreover, the number of comparative studies of XAI methods is too limited and the spectrum of studied measures is too narrow. In a certain sense, this resembles the critical situation from the beginning of the year surrounding the development of supervised classification methods and their actual usefulness, which, to paraphrase David Hand’s famous text, is more of an “illusion of progress”[11]

我们认为，更多的研究注意力应该集中在实验评估上，包括定量（更自动化研究的措施）和定性（基于人类或应用程序）评估，以及与人类专家的适当互动。本次演讲将首先回顾当前评估当地解释的建议，并通过案例研究讨论如何通过设计适当的人类调查来更好地评估它们。In our opinion more research attention should be devoted to experimental evaluations, both quantitative (measures for more automatic studies) and qualitative (human or application grounded) ones and to the appropriate interaction with human experts. This talk will firstly review the current proposals for evaluating local explanations, and by case studies will discuss how to evaluate them better also with designing appropriate human surveys.

本次演讲中讨论的另一个主题包括使用 XAI 来解释数据流的变化，其中数据分布和模型随着时间的推移而演变 - 这通常被研究为概念漂移The other topic discussed in this talk includes using XAI for explaining changes in data streams, where data distributions and models evolve over time – which is commonly studied as concept drift[17]。回想一下，大多数标准 XAI 方法都是针对静态设置而设计的，其中模型经过一次训练，并为固定数据和模型生成解释. Recall that most of standard XAI methods are designed for static settings, where models are trained once and explanations are generated for fixed data and model[12]。将此类静态 XAI 应用于不断变化的数据会导致失败，因为随着时间的推移，解释可能会变得陈旧、不一致并产生误导。本次演讲将介绍演讲者最新的原创作品和经验，以及针对这种背景调整原型和反事实的经验，并更好地描述概念漂移的原因。最后，与这些方面的评估挑战有关。. Applying such static XAI to evolving data leads to failures, as explanations may become stale, inconsistent over time and misleading. This talk will present the speaker’s latest original works and experiences with adapting prototypes and counterfactuals for this context, and to better characterize causes of the concept drift. Finally, it is related to challenges of evaluation of such aspects.

2 评估 XAI 方法的困难Difficulties in Evaluating XAI Methods

鉴于 XAI 的提案范围广泛且任务定义不明确，评估这些解释具有挑战性Evaluating the explanations is challenging, given the wide range of numerous proposals and ill-defined tasks of XAI[20].

首先，XAI 文献产生了广泛的主要是事后技术，以通过许多方法范式来近似模型性能的不同方面。然而，它们在数据类型（表格、图像、文本、时间序列等）、提供的解释的表示形式、针对不同受众的关注点以及其他方面存在很大差异。正如论文中所讨论的Firstly, the XAI literature has produced a wide range of mainly post-hoc techniques to approximate different aspects of model performance with many methodological paradigms. However, they differ very much in terms of the types of data (tables, images, text, time series, etc.), the representations of explanations provided, its focus on different audiences, and other aspects. As discussed in the paper[20]尽管方法论存在很大多样性，但 XAI 方法并不容易应用，对任意设计选择高度敏感，并且在实践中经常被误用。一般来说，模型开发人员或领域专家比其他用户更能正确使用它们。, despite substantial methodological diversity, XAI approaches are not easy to apply, are highly sensitive to arbitrary design choices, and are often misused in practice. In general, they are used correctly by model developers or domain experts often than by other users.

XAI 的概念相当不明确；请参阅各种定义的概述The concept of XAI is rather ill-defined; see the overview of various definitions in[2]。在这里，我们遵循定义. Here, we follow the definition[8]: 可解释的人工智能探索和研究生成或补充人工智能模型的方法，使算法的内部逻辑和结果易于访问和解释，使人类能够理解这一过程Explainable-AI explores and investigates methods to produce or complement AI models to make accessible and interpretable the internal logic and the outcome of the algorithms, making such process understandable by humans。 所以，我们可以区分. So, we can distinguish between全局解释global explanations和and当地解释local explanations– 我们将进一步将其理解为试图确定黑盒 ML 模型针对特定实例做出决策背后的原因。接下来，我们将考虑本地解释。– which we will further understand as attempts to identify the reasons behind a black-box ML model’s decision for a specific instance. Further on, we will consider local explanations.

另一个困难的方面是解释是为人们准备的并且应该对他们有用。这也是不明确的，因为它很大程度上取决于接收者的类型，包括他们的领域知识、偏好和要解决的特定任务。正在关注Another difficult aspect is that explanations are intended for people and should be useful to them. This is also ambiguous, as it depends strongly on the type of recipient, including their domain knowledge, preferences, and the specific task being solved. Following[4]目前提出的XAI方法过多地考虑了AI系统开发人员的角度，而不是其他用户的角度。, currently proposed XAI methods take too much into account the perspective of the AI system developers, not other users.

此外，在评估这些建议的有效性时，人工评估并未得到充分利用。审查论文Furthermore, human evaluation is underutilized in assessing the effectiveness of these proposals. The review paper[16]引用了对最近发表的文章的研究，根据该研究，97％的评论作品指出这种解释为用户服务，但大多数没有通过任何用户研究对其进行评估。同样，其他作者回顾了 381 篇 XAI 论文，发现只有 5% 明确关注对 XAI 方法的更深入评估。论文cites research on recently published articles, according to which 97% of the reviewed works pointed out that such explanations serve users, but most of them did not evaluate them through any user study. Similarly, other authors reviewed 381 XAI papers and found that only 5% explicitly focused on a deeper evaluation of XAI methods. The paper[16]还指出了其他难题，例如：评估缺乏科学共识；缺乏多学科性（需要整合人机交互、认知科学、心理学）；缺乏标准化程序，缺乏良好的可视化和人机交互工具also point out other difficult issues such as: Lack of scientific consensus on evaluations; Lack of multi-disciplinarity (needs for integrating HCI, Cognitive Science, Psychology); Lack of standarized procedures and Lack of good tools for visualization and human interaction

3 两个案例：如何更好地评估Two Cases: How to Better Evaluate

我们将简要介绍两个互不相同的评估图像识别深度神经网络解释方法挑战的案例研究。第一个案例研究展示了 XAI 在自动化方法中的使用，而第二个案例则说明了以人为本的评估研究的挑战。We will briefly present two case studies of challenges in evaluating explanation methods for image recognition deep neural networks, which differ from one another. The first case study demonstrates the use of XAI in the context of automated methods, while the second illustrates the challenges of a human-grounded evaluation study.

3.1 DetoxAI：使用 XAI 提高公平性DetoxAI: Using XAI to Improve Fairness

第一个案例展示了使用 XAI 来检测数据和卷积神经网络 (CNN) 性能中的偏差，然后采取旨在有效提高系统性能的行动，其有效性可以使用选定的指标来衡量。The first case shows using XAI to detect bias in the data and in the performance of a convolutional neural network (CNN), followed by actions aimed at effectively improving the system’s performance, the effectiveness of which can be measured using selected metrics.

它致力于机器学习系统决策的公平性和偏见。在处理所谓的受保护属性（例如性别或种族）时，这变得尤其成问题，这些属性是高级特征（概念），仅在像素数据上运行而无需创建显式特征的机器学习模型无法访问这些特征。特别是图像神经网络可能会开发出内部表示，这些表示不仅编码有用的高级特征，还编码有害的偏差It is devoted to fairness and bias in decisions of ML systems. This becomes particularly problematic when dealing with so called protected attributes (such as gender or race), which are high-level features (concepts) that are inaccessible to ML models operating only on pixel data without explicit features creation. In particular image neural networks may develop internal representations that encode not only useful high-level features but also harmful biases[3].

In [28]我们考虑了几种不同的 CNN 模型（不同的 Resnet 架构）和人脸数据集，并观察了针对多种指标的预测，包括群体公平性。在 CelebA 数据集中，我们发现佩戴某些物品（例如领带）与人的性别高度相关。这意味着“佩戴领带特征”可以用作推断性别的代理特征，从而创造潜在的意想不到的歧视途径。如图所示。we have considered several different models of CNN (different Resnet architectures) and human face datasets and observed predictions with respect to several measures, including group fairness. In in the CelebA dataset, we discovered that wearing some items, such as a necktie, is highly correlated with the person gender. It means that the ’wearing necktie feature’ can be used as a proxy feature to infer gender, thus creating potential unintended pathways for discrimination. It is illustrated in Fig.1。此外，研究还发现，这些模型始终将戴领带的个人错误地归类为不微笑，这暴露了无意的偏见，并降低了所选公平措施的价值。. Moreover, it has observed that the models consistently misclassifies individuals wearing neckties as not smiling, revealing unintended bias and decreasing values of selected fairness measures.

第一个挑战是如何发现这种偏见。在 DetoXAI 系统中The first challenge was how to detect this bias. In the DetoXAI system[27]，我们使用基于显着性图的 XAI 方法（突出显示影响网络预测的输入图像区域） - 见图 1。, we used XAI methods based on Saliency Maps (which highlight regions of the input image that influence the network’s predictions) – see Fig.1。我们考虑了各种生成它们的方法，最终转向使用概念相关相关性传播（CRP）. We considered various methods for generating them, finally directed to using Concept-wise Relevance Propagation (CRP)[1]，其中相关性分数通过网络层向后传播，同时还允许我们识别哪些神经元（基于它们的分数）与不需要的概念（偏差）最密切相关。可以使用 中引入的显着性图的新指标更自动地执行其定量评估, where relevance scores are propagated backward through the network layers, while also allowing us to identify which neurons (based on their scores) are most closely related to the undesired concept (bias). Its quantitative evaluation can be performed more automatically using new metrics for saliency maps introduced in[28].

另一个挑战是决定如何消除这种不必要的偏见。由于修改训练数据集和从头开始重新训练网络的成本很高，因此我们决定使用概念取消学习方法。我们测试了几种最近的事后方法，例如基于 Savani 和Zhang、LEACE、AClarc 的方法以及事后阈值优化Another challenge is deciding how to eliminate this unwanted bias. Since modifying the training dataset and retraining the network from scratch is costly, we decided to use concept unlearning methods. We tested several recent post-hoc methods, such as those based on Savani and Zhang, LEACE, AClarc, and post-hoc threshold optimization[18].

实验结果描述于The results of the experiments described in[28]清楚地表明，尽管方法有所不同，但大多数方法都消除了网络对偏差的不良关注——这可以通过对显着性图的目视检查来定性地观察到，并可以定量地改进网络预测的选定组级公平性指标（例如 EqualizedOdds 和人口统计平等）。clearly showed that, although the methods differed, most of them led to the elimination of the network’s undesirable focus on biases—which can be observed qualitatively by a visual inspection of saliency maps and quantitatively in improvement of selected group-level fairness metrics (such as EqualizedOdds and Demographic Parity) for the network’s predictions.

反事实解释（简述Counterfactual explanations (briefly反事实counterfactuals）是黑盒模型预测的另一种事后解释) are another, type of post-hoc explanations for black-box model predictions[30]。给定输入实例及其黑盒分类器的相应预测，反事实指定从模型获得不同的、通常更有利的预测所需的特征值的最小变化。与其他试图回答“为什么做出这个决定”这个问题的流行解释不同，反事实直接解决这个问题：“需要改变什么才能使决定有所不同？”。它们提供对模型行为的可行见解，使个人能够理解金融、医疗保健等领域的自动化决策，并可能对自动化决策提出异议. We should conduct more fundamental research on new experimental standards. There are several open issues such as:

  • •

    目前的评估措施在很大程度上取决于解释的类型和任务——它们不能概括。Current evaluation measures depend very much on the type of explanation and the tasks - they do not generalize.

  • •

    它们中的大多数都是局部事后解释器的一种功能代理，并且由于它们仅近似模型的相当复杂和隐藏的行为 - 应该进行更多的比较研究。Most of them are a kind functional proxies for local post-hoc explainers and as they only approximate fairly complex and hidden behaviors of the models - more comparative studies should be undertaken.

  • •

    了解其局限性后，可以应用更广泛、更多样化的措施来评估 XAI 方法，也可以从多标准决策分析中获得灵感。Knowing their limitations, the wider and more diversified set of measures can be applied to evaluate XAI methods also with inspirations from the multi-criteria decision analysis.

  • •

    目前的研究主要涉及局部事后解释，而全球性的研究较少。The most current research covers mainly local post-hoc explanations, while the global ones are less studied.

  • •

    缺乏足够的基准数据集以及用于解释的地面实况注释信息；有必要对它们以及合成发电机进行更多的研究。There is lack of enough benchmark datasets with annotated information on the ground-truth for explanations; More work on them, also with synthetic generators, are necessary.

解释必须为人类服务。尽管基于应用和人性化的评估是最重要的，但它们在当前的 XAI 研究中却被忽视了。需要对人类研究进行更系统的工作，同时从实验心理学的经验以及设计和进行调查研究的现有框架或协议中汲取灵感。总体而言，XAI领域应开展更多的跨学科研究。Explanations must serve humans. Although application- and human- grounded evaluations are the most important, they are too neglected in current XAI research. More systemic work on human studies is needed – also taking inspirations from the experiences of experimental psychology and existing frameworks or protocols for designing and conducting survey research. In general, more interdisciplinary research should be carried in XAI field.

关于以人为本的研究的其他研究问题可能包括：如何组织与用户的互动会议？我们应该如何为用户做好准备？我们应该如何验证他们答案的正确性。我们是否应该进入 NLP 顺序对话，而不是简单的一步回答？我们如何才能更好地将因果关系（因果关系）和背景知识结合起来？还有一个更普遍的研究问题——这些解释真的能增加人类对预测和使用人工智能系统的信任吗？Other research questions on human grounded studies could cover: How to organize interaction sessions with users? How should we prepare users for them? How should we validate the correctness of their answers. Whether we should move into NLP sequential dialog, instead of simple one-step answers? How could we better incorporate cause-effect relationships (causality) and background knowledge? And a more general research question - Do the explanations really increase human’s trust in predictions and using AI systems?

此外，我们指出了解释动态环境中模型性能的困难挑战（例如随着概念漂移或持续学习而不断发展的数据流）。评估这些变化以及随着时间的推移而改变的解释仍然是 XAI 最具挑战性的方面之一。现有的评估措施侧重于静态设置并测量单个时间点的基本属性，而我们需要考虑较长时间动态的问题（以及数据、模型和解释变化之间可能的相互作用）。该领域本质上需要更深入的基础研究和有趣的应用问题来验证新提出的想法。Moreover, we pointed out the difficult challenges of explaining model performance in dynamic environments (such as evolving data streams with concept drifts or continual learning). Evaluating such changes, also for changing explanations over time, remains one of the most challenging aspects for XAI. Existing evaluation measure are focused on static setting and measure basic properties at single points in time, while we need to take into account issues of longer temporal dynamics (and also possible interactions between data, model and explanation changes). This area essentially requires more intensive fundamental research and interesting application problems to verify new proposed ideas.

我们希望空域探索、地球观测和医学的各个子领域——正如 EASi-EXPLIMED IJCAI-ECAI 研讨会所考虑的那样——可以为上述研究挑战提供灵感。We hope that air space exploration, earth observation, and various sub-fields of medicine - as considered in the EASi-EXPLIMED IJCAI-ECAI workshop - may provide inspirations to the aforementioned research challenges.
