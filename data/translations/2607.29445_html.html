用于对红外视觉语言模型进行定向语义攻击的 QR 结构热触发器QR-Structured Thermal Triggers for Targeted Semantic Attacks on Infrared Vision–Language Models

Xiang Chen, Yingying Zhao, Chao Li, Jiaju Han, Ben Zhang, Ang Li, Jiahuan Long, Yiwei Wei, Jiujiang Guo, Chengyin Hu
摘要Abstract

红外视觉语言模型 (IR-VLM) 将热感知从封闭集识别扩展到开放词汇分类、图像字幕和视觉问答 (VQA)，从而能够通过语言一致的表示来解释红外输入。然而，它们对结构化热扰动的鲁棒性和跨模态语义对齐的稳定性仍然没有得到充分研究。在本文中，我们介绍了 QR-Structured Thermal Triggers (QR-STT)，这是一种隐形、免训练的黑盒框架，用于针对 IR-VLM 进行有针对性的语义引导。 QR-STT 构建了一个低对比度热触发器，具有保留的 QR 功能区域和可优化的内部模块，其中每个模块都分配有冷、中性或热热状态。在这个可解释的模块空间内，QR-STT 联合优化模块拓扑和渲染参数，包括放置、缩放、旋转、强度、模糊和圆度，以使触发器适应红外图像，同时保持视觉隐形。具有贪婪模块翻转细化的三阶段无梯度搜索处理混合离散连续攻击空间。该目标促进目标对准，抑制源证据，并规范 QR 结构和视觉隐形，从而无需模型训练、数据中毒或梯度访问即可实现从源到目标的引导。跨多个 CLIP 式编码器的实验表明，QR-STT 可靠地将图像文本对齐重定向到攻击者选择的概念，同时保持视觉隐形。此外，针对分类进行优化的对抗性图像转移到字幕和 VQA 任务，表明 CLIP 端转向可以传播到生成级行为并引起目标一致的语义漂移。这些发现揭示了 QR 结构的热触发器作为语言驱动的红外感知的可解释的攻击面，并激发了针对结构化跨任务语义风险的鲁棒性评估。Infrared vision–language models (IR-VLMs) extend thermal perception from closed-set recognition to open-vocabulary classification, image captioning, and visual question answering (VQA), enabling infrared inputs to be interpreted through language-aligned representations. However, their robustness to structured thermal perturbations and the stability of cross-modal semantic alignment remain insufficiently studied. In this paper, we introduce QR-Structured Thermal Triggers (QR-STT), a stealthy, training-free black-box framework for targeted semantic steering against IR-VLMs. QR-STT constructs a low-contrast thermal trigger with preserved QR functional regions and optimizable internal modules, where each module is assigned a cold, neutral, or hot thermal state. Within this interpretable module space, QR-STT jointly optimizes module topology and rendering parameters, including placement, scale, rotation, intensity, blur, and roundness, to adapt the trigger to infrared imagery while maintaining visual stealth. A three-stage gradient-free search with greedy module-flip refinement handles the mixed discrete–continuous attack space. The objective promotes target alignment, suppresses source evidence, and regularizes QR structure and visual stealth, enabling source-to-target steering without model training, data poisoning, or gradient access. Experiments across multiple CLIP-style encoders show that QR-STT reliably redirects image–text alignment toward attacker-chosen concepts while maintaining visual stealth. Moreover, adversarial images optimized for classification transfer to captioning and VQA tasks, showing that CLIP-side steering can propagate to generation-level behavior and induce target-consistent semantic drift. These findings reveal QR-structured thermal triggers as an interpretable attack surface for language-driven infrared perception and motivate robustness evaluation against structured cross-task semantic risks.

简介Introduction

视觉语言模型 (VLM) 通过大规模跨模式预训练来调整视觉和语言表示，支持开放词汇分类、图像字幕和视觉问答 (VQA)Vision–language models (VLMs) align visual and linguistic representations through large-scale cross-modal pre-training, supporting open-vocabulary classification, image captioning, and visual question answering (VQA)(Radford et al. 2021; Cherti et al. 2023; Xu et al. 2024; Sun et al. 2023; Liu et al. 2023, 2024; Li et al. 2023; Dai et al. 2023)。最近的红外视觉语言模型（IR-VLM）将此功能扩展到热图像，使红外场景能够通过语言查询而不是固定的探测器类别来解释. Recent infrared vision–language models (IR-VLMs) extend this capability to thermal imagery, enabling infrared scenes to be interpreted through language queries rather than fixed detector categories(Jiang et al. 2024; Cao et al. 2025; Zhang et al. 2025b)。此类模型有望用于夜间驾驶、监视、搜救和恶劣天气感知，其中热传感在光照不足的情况下仍能提供信息。. Such models are promising for nighttime driving, surveillance, search-and-rescue, and adverse-weather perception, where thermal sensing remains informative under poor illumination.

从封闭集识别到语言一致感知的转变引入了明显的安全风险。传统的红外探测器在预定义的标签上运行，现有的攻击通常旨在抑制检测或引发一般错误。相反，IR-VLM 将视觉输入与共享嵌入空间中的一组广泛的文本概念进行比较。因此，紧凑的对抗性线索可能会增加与攻击者选择的概念的一致性，而无需完全删除原始的视觉证据。这种有针对性的语义引导比无针对性的降级更重要，因为攻击者控制了错误的方向。如图This transition from closed-set recognition to language-aligned perception introduces a distinct security risk. Conventional infrared detectors operate over predefined labels, and existing attacks typically aim to suppress detections or induce generic errors. IR-VLMs instead compare visual inputs with a broad set of textual concepts in a shared embedding space. A compact adversarial cue may therefore increase alignment with an attacker-chosen concept without fully removing the original visual evidence. This targeted semantic steering is more consequential than untargeted degradation because the attacker controls the direction of the error. As shown in Figure1，相同的红外输入可以重定向到狗、汽车或自行车等概念。, the same infrared input can be redirected toward concepts such as dog, car, or bicycle.

现有的稳健性研究并未完全描述这种威胁的特征。大多数 VLM 攻击都是在可见光谱中开发的，依赖于像素扰动、可转移的对抗样本、补丁、提示相关的漏洞、照明变换或一般的多模式攻击Existing robustness studies do not fully characterize this threat. Most VLM attacks are developed in the visible spectrum and rely on pixel perturbations, transferable adversarial examples, patches, prompt-related vulnerabilities, illumination transformations, or general multimodal attacks(Lu et al. 2023; Yin et al. 2023; Zhao et al. 2023; Zhang et al. 2025a; Xie et al. 2025; Liu et al. 2025; Nie et al. 2026; Hu et al. 2026; Guo et al. 2026)。红外对抗研究主要针对使用物理贴片、贴纸、网格、热/冷块或基于曲线的热模式的探测器和跟踪器. Infrared adversarial research has primarily targeted detectors and trackers using physical patches, stickers, grids, hot/cold blocks, or curve-based thermal patterns(Wei et al. 2023b; Zhu et al. 2024; Tiliwalidi et al. 2025; Wei et al. 2023a; Hu et al. 2024a, b; Jia et al. 2025)。这些方法主要追求漏检、误报或总体性能下降。紧凑的结构化载体是否可以有意操纵 IR-VLM 的开放词汇语义仍有待探索。. These methods mainly pursue missed detections, false positives, or general performance degradation. Whether a compact structured carrier can deliberately manipulate the open-vocabulary semantics of IR-VLMs remains underexplored.

我们研究 QR 码作为数字红外图像领域的结构化攻击载体。 QR 码自然地将固定功能区域与可编辑内部模块分开。查找器模式、计时模式、格式相关区域和安静区域保留了其全局组织，而其余模块定义了可以针对特定目标引导进行优化的离散拓扑。与不受约束的补丁不同，这种表示形式明确地将保留的支架与攻击变量分开，为编码对抗性线索提供了结构化且可解释的空间。We investigate QR codes as structured attack carriers in the digital infrared image domain. A QR code naturally separates fixed functional regions from editable internal modules. Finder patterns, timing patterns, format-related regions, and the quiet zone preserve its global organization, while the remaining modules define a discrete topology that can be optimized for target-specific steering. Unlike unconstrained patches, this representation explicitly separates the preserved scaffold from the attack variables, providing a structured and interpretable space for encoding adversarial cues.

直接的高对比度 QR 覆盖在视觉上会很明显，并且在方法上与普通的补丁插入类似。因此，我们为每个可编辑模块分配三种相对红外强度状态之一：冷、中性或热，对应于局部强度降低、响应不变和局部强度增加。触发进一步由触发尺度、扰动强度、活动模块比率和结构相似性控制。这会产生低对比度 QR 结构扰动，同时保留原生 QR 组织。我们的核心问题是 QR 码的功能支架和可编辑模块拓扑是否可以在显式视觉扭曲约束下共同支持有针对性的语义引导。A direct high-contrast QR overlay would be visually conspicuous and methodologically similar to ordinary patch insertion. We therefore assign each editable module one of three relative infrared intensity states: cold, neutral, or hot, corresponding to local intensity decreases, unchanged responses, and local intensity increases. The trigger is further controlled by trigger scale, perturbation intensity, active-module ratio, and structural similarity. This yields a low-contrast QR-structured perturbation while retaining the native QR organization. Our central question is whether the functional scaffold and editable module topology of a QR code can jointly support targeted semantic steering under explicit visual-distortion constraints.

基于这个公式，我们提出了 QR 结构热触发器（QR-STT），这是一种针对 IR-VLM 的免训练黑盒针对性攻击。给定干净的红外图像和攻击者指定的目标标签，QR-STT 联合优化触发器放置、缩放、旋转、渲染参数和内部模块状态。其目标是促进目标对齐、抑制源证据、规范 QR 拓扑并限制视觉失真。为了解决由此产生的混合离散连续搜索空间，我们开发了一种渐进式无梯度策略，包括粗区域搜索、模块拓扑搜索、红外渲染细化和贪婪模块翻转细化。Based on this formulation, we propose QR-Structured Thermal Triggers (QR-STT), a training-free black-box targeted attack for IR-VLMs. Given a clean infrared image and an attacker-specified target label, QR-STT jointly optimizes trigger placement, scale, rotation, rendering parameters, and internal module states. Its objective promotes target alignment, suppresses source evidence, regularizes QR topology, and limits visual distortion. To address the resulting mixed discrete–continuous search space, we develop a progressive gradient-free strategy comprising coarse zone search, module-topology search, infrared rendering refinement, and greedy module-flip refinement.

我们评估 QR-STT 在多个 CLIP 式编码器上的目标零样本分类，并进一步评估向图像字幕和 VQA 的传输。下游生成模型被排除在攻击优化之外。因此，其输出中目标一致的变化表明引起的对齐偏移超出了特定于任务的分类器并传播到生成级行为。We evaluate QR-STT on targeted zero-shot classification across multiple CLIP-style encoders and further assess transfer to image captioning and VQA. The downstream generative models are excluded from attack optimization. Consequently, target-consistent changes in their outputs indicate that the induced alignment shift extends beyond a task-specific classifier and propagates to generation-level behavior.

我们的贡献总结如下：Our contributions are summarized as follows:

Comparisons with structured infrared baselines and detailed ablations demonstrate the effectiveness, cross-task transferability, and interpretability of QR-STT.

Recent infrared and thermal VLM studies adapt these ideas to low-texture thermal imagery, where discriminative cues depend more on heat radiation, object silhouette, and sensor statistics than RGB color or fine-grained texture(Jiang et al. 2024; Cao et al. 2025; Moshtaghi et al. 2025; Zhang et al. 2025b)。这些模型使红外感知更易于语言访问，但也引入了开放词汇语义攻击面。. These models make infrared perception more language-accessible, but also introduce open-vocabulary semantic attack surfaces.

对抗性攻击下视觉语言一致性的鲁棒性。Robustness of Vision–Language Alignment under Adversarial Attacks.多模态对抗攻击通常利用 CLIP 式模型的共享图像文本表示，通过扰乱图像、引导跨模型传输或诱导下游标题/VQA 错误Multimodal adversarial attacks often exploit the shared image–text representation of CLIP-style models, either by perturbing the image, guiding transfer across models, or inducing downstream caption/VQA errors(Lu et al. 2023; Yin et al. 2023; Zhao et al. 2023; Zhang et al. 2025a; Xie et al. 2025; Liu et al. 2025; Nie et al. 2026; Hu et al. 2026; Guo et al. 2026)。因为指令调整的 VLM 经常继承来自类 CLIP 编码器的视觉特征. Because instruction-tuned VLMs frequently inherit visual features from CLIP-like encoders(Liu et al. 2023, 2024; Awadalla et al. 2023)，图像-文本对齐的变化可以传播到语言输出。我们的工作重点是红外场景中的这种传输，并询问隐形符号热载体是否可以将共享表示转向指定目标。, a shift in image–text alignment can propagate to language outputs. Our work focuses on this transfer in infrared scenarios and asks whether a stealthy symbolic thermal carrier can steer the shared representation toward a specified target.

结构红外扰动。Structured infrared perturbations.红外攻击通常将扰动限制为物理意义或低频模式，包括对抗性红外贴片、汽车贴纸、可穿戴热/冷块、网格模式以及样条或曲线载体Infrared attacks often constrain perturbations to physically meaningful or low-frequency patterns, including adversarial infrared patches, car stickers, wearable hot/cold blocks, grid patterns, and spline or curve carriers(Brown et al. 2017; Wei et al. 2023b; Zhu et al. 2024; Wei et al. 2023a; Hu et al. 2024a; Tiliwalidi et al. 2025; Hu et al. 2024b; Jia et al. 2025)。这些研究主要针对检测器，而 QR-STT 针对开放词汇 IR-VLM 语义。我们在相同的源到目标协议下采用代表性的结构化红外载体作为基线。. These studies mainly target detectors, whereas QR-STT targets open-vocabulary IR-VLM semantics. We adapt representative structured infrared carriers as baselines under the same source-to-target protocol.

符号视觉载体和QR结构。Symbolic visual carriers and QR structure.标准 QR 码具有固有的固定功能区域和可编辑的数据模块，使其成为研究结构化视觉扰动的非常有用的符号布局Standard QR codes have inherent fixed functional regions and editable data modules, making them a highly useful symbolic layout for studying structured visual perturbations(International Organization for Standardization 2015)。然而，直接粘贴类似 QR 的普通补丁在视觉上是显而易见的，并且在方法上接近于通用补丁攻击. QR-STT instead fully preserves QR functional regions and optimizes only the internal thermal modules under strict stealth constraints, testing whether symbolic module topology itself can serve as an interpretable attack space.

渐进式黑盒优化Progressive Black-Box Optimization

QR-STT 的搜索空间是混合离散连续的：触发器 几何和渲染参数是连续的，而模块 状态是离散的。联合优化所有变量创建 高维且不稳定的黑盒问题。因此我们采用 从粗略空间出发的渐进策略 配置到细粒度模块拓扑。The search space of QR-STT is mixed discrete–continuous: trigger geometry and rendering parameters are continuous, whereas the module states are discrete. Jointly optimizing all variables creates a high-dimensional and unstable black-box problem. We therefore adopt a progressive strategy that proceeds from coarse spatial configurations to fine-grained module topology.

在阶段At stagek∈{1,2,3}k\in\{1,2,3\}, QR-STT 优化阶段特定变量 设置, QR-STT optimizes a stage-specific variable setΩk\Omega_{k}:

θ(k)⁣⋆=arg⁡minθ∈Ωk⁡ℒQ​R−S​T​T​(AQR​(xi;θ),si,t).\theta^{(k)\star}=\arg\min_{\theta\in\Omega_{k}}\mathcal{L}_{QR-STT}\big(A_{\mathrm{QR}}(x_{i};\theta),s_{i},t\big). (15)

每个阶段的最佳解决方案都会初始化下一阶段。The best solution from each stage initializes the next stage.

第一阶段：粗略区域搜索。Stage 1: coarse zone search.我们将可编辑区域划分为粗略的空间区域，所有 每个区域中的模块共享放松状态。此阶段优化 触发位置、规模、旋转、强度和区域级别 冷/热倾向以确定有前途的位置和粗略 拓扑。We partition the editable region into coarse spatial zones, with all modules in each zone sharing a relaxed state. This stage optimizes trigger position, scale, rotation, intensity, and zone-level cold/hot tendencies to identify a promising placement and coarse topology.

第2阶段：模块拓扑搜索。Stage 2: module topology search.从最佳粗解开始，每个可编辑模块是 分配一个独立的松弛状态。然后优化器搜索 对于细粒度的特定于目标的拓扑，它提供了主要 触发器的语义能力。Starting from the best coarse solution, each editable module is assigned an independent relaxed state. The optimizer then searches for a fine-grained target-specific topology, which provides the main semantic capacity of the trigger.

第三阶段：渲染细化。Stage 3: rendering refinement.给定第 2 阶段获得的拓扑，我们细化强度、模糊、 圆度和小的几何修正。此阶段抑制 尖锐的伪影并改善目标对齐之间的权衡 和视觉相似性。Given the topology obtained in Stage 2, we refine intensity, blur, roundness, and small geometric corrections. This stage suppresses sharp artifacts and improves the trade-off between target alignment and visual similarity.

At iterationℓ\ell, 候选人, a candidateM~(ℓ)\widetilde{M}^{(\ell)}是通过改变一小部分生成的 冷、中性和热状态之间的可编辑模块。候选人 仅当它降低了总体目标时才被接受：is generated by changing a small subset of editable modules among cold, neutral, and hot states. The candidate is accepted only if it decreases the complete objective:

M(ℓ+1)={M~(ℓ),Δ​ℒ<0,M(ℓ),otherwise,M^{(\ell+1)}=\begin{cases}\widetilde{M}^{(\ell)},&\Delta\mathcal{L}<0,\\ M^{(\ell)},&\text{otherwise},\end{cases} (16)

哪里where

Δ​ℒ=ℒQ​R−S​T​T​(M~(ℓ))−ℒQ​R−S​T​T​(M(ℓ)).\Delta\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{QR-STT}\big(\widetilde{M}^{(\ell)}\big)-\mathcal{L}_{QR-STT}\big(M^{(\ell)}\big). (17)

当查询预算耗尽或没有查询预算时，细化终止 发现了进一步的改进。The refinement terminates when the query budget is exhausted or no further improvement is found.

总体而言，渐进策略将三个耦合决策分开： 触发器放置在哪里，哪个模块拓扑对目标进行编码 信号，以及如何呈现触发器。这种分解减少了 早期搜索复杂性并稳定细粒度拓扑 优化。Overall, the progressive strategy separates three coupled decisions: where the trigger is placed, which module topology encodes the target signal, and how the trigger is rendered. This decomposition reduces early-stage search complexity and stabilizes fine-grained topology optimization.

结论与讨论Conclusion and Discussion

我们提出了 QR-STT，这是一种无需训练的黑盒框架，用于红外 VLM 的目标语义控制。通过保留固定的 QR 支架并通过渐进式混合离散连续搜索优化可编辑热模块，QR-STT 实现了跨分类、字幕和 VQA 的强大攻击有效性和跨任务可转移性。消融研究验证了拓扑搜索、离散细化和面向隐形渲染的作用，将 QR 结构扰动建立为红外 VLM 的有效且可解释的攻击空间。We propose QR-STT, a training-free black-box framework for targeted semantic steering of infrared VLMs. By preserving the fixed QR scaffold and optimizing editable thermal modules through progressive mixed discrete–continuous search, QR-STT achieves strong attack effectiveness and cross-task transferability across classification, captioning, and VQA. Ablation studies validate the roles of topology search, discrete refinement, and stealth-oriented rendering, establishing QR-structured perturbations as an effective and interpretable attack space for infrared VLMs.
