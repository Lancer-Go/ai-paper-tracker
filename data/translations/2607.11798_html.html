StoryTeller：长篇音频描述的免培训叙事基础StoryTeller: Training-Free Narrative Grounding for Long-Form Audio Description

Seung Hyun Hahm    Minh T. Dinh    SouYoung Jin
摘要Abstract

长篇音频描述 (AD) 需要的不仅仅是描述可见的动作：它必须保留各个场景中的人物、事件、关系和故事背景，以便盲人和弱视 (BLV) 观众能够观看电影。现代视频语言模型（VLM）对于短片很有效，但它们通常独立地处理每个时刻，产生的描述会忽略角色是谁、事件为何重要以及当前场景如何与早期叙事背景联系起来。我们提出了 StoryTeller，这是一个用于故事感知长篇 AD 的免培训框架。 StoryTeller 不只依赖局部视觉线索，而是保留经过验证的叙事记忆，跨场景传递与故事相关的信息，使后续的描述保持连贯、基础和上下文信息丰富。仅给定原始视频和电影标题，StoryTeller 可以选择检索公共电影元数据来解析名称和故事上下文，同时仅接受视频通过语义过滤和 VLM 验证支持的事实。该方法不需要字幕、脚本、AD 转录本、对齐字幕、字符库、预先计算的面部身份或特定于任务的微调。为了评估生成的 AD 是否保留了叙述信息，我们引入了 StoryAD-QALong-form audio description (AD) requires more than describing visible actions: it must preserve characters, events, relationships, and story context across scenes so that blind and low-vision (BLV) audiences can follow a film. Modern video–language models (VLMs) are effective on short clips, but they often treat each moment independently, producing descriptions that miss who characters are, why events matter, and how the current scene connects to earlier narrative context. We propose StoryTeller, a training-free framework for story-aware long-form AD. Instead of relying only on local visual cues, StoryTeller maintains a verified narrative memory that carries forward story-relevant information across scenes, enabling later descriptions to remain coherent, grounded, and contextually informative. Given only raw video and a movie title, StoryTeller can optionally retrieve public movie metadata to resolve names and story context, while accepting only facts that are supported by the video through semantic filtering and VLM verification. The method requires no subtitles, scripts, AD transcripts, aligned captions, character banks, precomputed face identities, or task-specific fine-tuning. To evaluate whether generated AD preserves narrative information, we introduce StoryAD-QA111StoryAD-QA基准数据集和评估代码：StoryAD-QA benchmark dataset and evaluation code:
https://github.com/SEE-AI-Lab/ECCV2026_StoryTeller_StoryAD_QA，一个问答基准，测试语言模型是否可以仅使用生成的描述来回答故事上下文问题。标准 AD 基准和各种长格式视频的实验表明，与自动、基于 QA 和人工评估的强大基线相比，StoryTeller 持续提高了叙述连贯性、事实基础和故事理解。, a question-answering benchmark that tests whether a language model can answer story-context questions using only the generated descriptions. Experiments on standard AD benchmarks and diverse long-form videos show that StoryTeller consistently improves narrative coherence, factual grounding, and story comprehension over strong baselines in automatic, QA-based, and human evaluations.

1 简介Introduction

音频描述 (AD) 通过在对话和其他声音之间叙述重要的视觉信息，使盲人和低视力 (BLV) 观众能够访问视觉媒体。有效的广告不仅仅描述当前框架中的物体或动作：它识别在场人员，解释刚刚发生的事情，传达为什么某个时刻很重要，并将场景与更广泛的叙述联系起来。专业的广告作家能够提供这种叙事基础，因为他们观看电影并在脑海中记住整个故事。然而，制作高质量的 AD 仍然成本高昂且耗时，而且版权限制使得大型可公开分发的 AD 数据集难以获得。Audio description (AD) enables blind and low-vision (BLV) audiences to access visual media by narrating important visual information between dialogue and other sounds. Effective AD goes beyond describing objects or actions in the current frame: it identifies who is present, explains what has just happened, conveys why a moment matters, and connects the scene to the broader narrative. Professional AD writers are able to provide this narrative grounding because they watch the film and write with the whole story in mind. However, producing high-quality AD remains costly and time-intensive, and copyright restrictions make large publicly distributable AD datasets difficult to obtain.

视频语言模型 (VLM) 的最新进展Recent advances in video–language models (VLMs)[singh2025openai, li2026qwen3, wang2025internvl3, videollama2]大型语言模型激发了自动广告生成的研究。最先进的身份识别 AD 系统and large language models have motivated research on automatic AD generation. State-of-the-art identity-aware AD systems[autoad1, autoad2, raajesh2024micap]通常在 LSMDC 等精选电影数据集上训练为特定于任务的模型are typically trained as task-specific models on curated movie datasets such as LSMDC[rohrbach2017movie]和疯狂and MAD[Soldan_2022_CVPR]。这些管道包含了基于标记数据训练的人脸识别主干构建的身份感知组件。虽然在基准设置中有效，但这种对受版权保护的训练数据、策划的电影注释和专门的身份模块的依赖限制了它们的可扩展性、可重复性和对新电影的适用性。. These pipelines incorporate identity-aware components built from face-recognition backbones trained on labeled data. While effective in benchmark settings, this reliance on copyrighted training data, curated movie annotations, and specialized identity modules limits their scalability, reproducibility, and applicability to new films.

免培训管道，包括 AutoAD-ZeroTraining-free pipelines, including AutoAD-Zero[autoadzero]和 MM_Narratorand MM_Narrator[zhang2024mm]，最近作为监督 AD 生成的替代方案出现。这些方法消除了对特定任务模型训练的需要，但它们通常仍然依赖于 AD 生成之前准备的资源，包括字符库、对齐的字幕或现有的 AD 转录本。此外，它们通常以浅层或静态形式传播上下文，例如连接先前的描述或重用固定的身份特征。尽管这些策略可以提高附近剪辑的局部一致性，但它们并没有明确地模拟叙事信息如何随着时间的推移而演变：哪些角色、关系和事件应该保持显着，哪些细节应该随着电影的进展而消失。, have recently emerged as an alternative to supervised AD generation. These methods remove the need for task-specific model training, but they often still rely on resources prepared before AD generation, including character banks, aligned subtitles, or existing AD transcripts. Moreover, they typically propagate context in shallow or static forms, such as concatenating previous descriptions or reusing fixed identity features. Although these strategies can improve local consistency across nearby clips, they do not explicitly model how narrative information evolves over time: which characters, relationships, and events should remain salient, and which details should fade as the film progresses.

人类观众无法通过保留先前帧的完整列表来理解电影。相反，它们保持了故事的不断发展，其中中心人物、未解决的冲突和反复出现的关系仍然突出，而附带的视觉细节逐渐失去重要性。这种观点表明，长篇广告应该在电影中保留身份连续性、因果结构和不断发展的角色互动，而不依赖于字幕、脚本、广告转录、角色库或预先计算的面部身份等外部资源。Human viewers do not understand a film by retaining a complete list of previous frames. Instead, they maintain an evolving representation of the story, where central characters, unresolved conflicts, and recurring relationships remain salient, while incidental visual details gradually lose importance. This perspective suggests that long-form AD should preserve identity continuity, causal structure, and evolving character interactions across a film, without relying on external resources such as subtitles, scripts, AD transcripts, character banks, or precomputed face identities.

我们介绍We introduce讲故事的人StoryTeller，一种无需训练的长篇 AD 框架，在按时间顺序处理剪辑时保持明确的故事状态。给定一系列视频剪辑, a training-free framework for long-form AD that maintains an explicit story state as it processes clips in chronological order. Given a sequence of video clips{v1,…,vT}\{v_{1},\dots,v_{T}\}，系统保留两种形式的记忆：用于重复出现的角色的身份图和用于经过验证的故事事实的显着性加权记忆。每个新剪辑都通过三个步骤更新此状态：跟踪可见身份，使用可选的公共电影元数据提取候选事实，并在更新内存之前根据视频验证这些事实。然后，记忆会强化仍然相关的事实，并削弱不再重要的事实。, the system keeps two forms of memory: an identity graph for recurring characters and a salience-weighted memory for verified story facts. Each new clip updates this state through three steps: track visible identities, extract candidate facts with optional public movie metadata, and verify those facts against the video before updating memory. The memory then reinforces facts that remain relevant and decays facts that no longer matter.

StoryTeller 仅将原始视频和电影标题作为输入。该标题可用于检索公共 IMDb 情节摘要、角色列表和简短的角色描述。检索到的文本只是可能的名称或上下文的来源；它不会被复制到 AD 中、直接存储为内存或被视为基本事实。仅当当前剪辑支持并被语义过滤和 VLM 验证接受时，事实才会进入内存。StoryTeller takes as input only the raw video and a movie title. The title may be used to retrieve public IMDb plot summaries, character lists, and short character descriptions. This retrieved text is only a source of possible names or context; it is not copied into the AD, stored directly as memory, or treated as ground truth. A fact enters memory only if it is supported by the current clip and accepted by semantic filtering and VLM verification.

除了生成之外，我们还解决评估问题。标准指标，例如 BLEUBeyond generation, we also address evaluation. Standard metrics such as BLEU[papineni2002bleu], 苹果酒, CIDEr[vedantam2015cider]，和香料, and SPICE[anderson2016spice]测量生成的描述和参考标题之间的词汇重叠，但它们不评估听众是否可以仅根据 AD 重建叙述。为了解决这个限制，我们引入measure lexical overlap between generated descriptions and reference captions, but they do not evaluate whether a listener can reconstruct the narrative from the AD alone. To address this limitation, we introduceStoryAD-QA，一个叙述理解问答基准。对于每个场景，我们都会生成问题，其答案取决于当前剪辑和之前的叙述上下文。在评估过程中，语言模型仅接收生成的音频描述，并且必须在不访问视频的情况下回答这些问题。该协议直接衡量描述是否保留了故事理解所需的人物、事件和因果关系，而不是仅仅孤立地描述视觉细节。, a narrative-comprehension question-answering benchmark. For each scene, we generate questions whose answers depend on both the current clip and preceding narrative context. During evaluation, a language model receives only the generated audio descriptions and must answer these questions without access to the video. This protocol directly measures whether the description preserves characters, events, and causal relationships necessary for story understanding rather than merely describing visual details in isolation.

4 StoryAD-QA：评估叙事理解StoryAD-QA: Evaluating Narrative Comprehension

现有的字幕指标，例如 CIDErExisting captioning metrics such as CIDEr[vedantam2015cider], 香料, SPICE[anderson2016spice], 胭脂, ROUGE[linrouge], 和 BLEU, and BLEU[papineni2002bleu]测量生成的描述和参考描述之间的词汇或语义相似性。虽然可以有效地评估场景级别的字幕质量，但它们无法评估音频描述序列是否保留了理解故事所需的信息。叙事理解需要跟踪人物、关系、事件和场景中的因果发展，但广告本质上是简洁的，可能会省略重要的上下文。因此，高字幕分数并不一定表明对故事层面的理解很强。为了解决这一限制，我们引入了 StoryAD-QA，这是一种多项选择基准，用于评估生成的音频描述是否为下游叙事推理保留了足够的信息。 StoryAD-QA 不是将生成的描述与参考文本进行比较，而是衡量是否可以仅使用生成的 AD 来回答有关故事的问题。measure lexical or semantic similarity between generated and reference descriptions. While effective for evaluating caption quality at the scene level, they do not assess whether a sequence of audio descriptions preserves the information needed to understand a story. Narrative comprehension requires tracking characters, relationships, events, and causal developments across scenes, yet ADs are essentially concise and may omit important context. As a result, high captioning scores do not necessarily indicate strong story-level understanding. To address this limitation, we introduce StoryAD-QA, a multiple-choice benchmark that evaluates whether generated audio descriptions retain sufficient information for downstream narrative reasoning. Rather than comparing generated descriptions against reference text, StoryAD-QA measures whether questions about the story can be answered using only the generated AD.

5 实验Experiments

我们从三个维度评估 StoryTeller： (i) MAD-Eval 上的字幕质量，(ii) 使用拟议的 StoryAD-QA 基准进行叙事理解，以及 (iii) 通过消融研究各个管道组件的贡献。所有实验都是在没有特定任务训练、字幕、脚本、AD 转录本、字符库或预先计算的面部身份的情况下进行的。We evaluate StoryTeller along three dimensions: (i) captioning quality on MAD-Eval, (ii) narrative comprehension with the proposed StoryAD-QA benchmark, and (iii) the contribution of individual pipeline components through ablation studies. All experiments are conducted without task-specific training, subtitles, scripts, AD transcripts, character banks, or precomputed face identities.

6 结论Conclusion

我们推出了 StoryTeller，这是一种无需培训的长格式音频描述框架，它使用叙事记忆来捕捉跨场景不断变化的故事背景。通过维护身份图和结构化叙事记忆，系统可以生成连贯的描述，无需特定任务的培训或策划的电影资源；公共电影元数据可以暗示上下文，但只有视频支持的事实才能进入记忆。我们还引入了 StoryAD-QA，这是一个评估生成的 AD 是否支持叙事理解的基准。实验表明，叙事感知建模可以改善长视频描述中的上下文基础和故事理解。We introduced StoryTeller, a training-free framework for long-form audio description that uses narrative memory to capture evolving story context across scenes. By maintaining an identity graph and structured narrative memory, the system generates coherent descriptions without task-specific training or curated movie resources; public movie metadata can suggest context, but only video-supported facts enter memory. We also introduced StoryAD-QA, a benchmark for evaluating whether generated AD supports narrative understanding. Experiments show that narrative-aware modeling improves contextual grounding and story comprehension in long-form video description.
