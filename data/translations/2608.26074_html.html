摘要Abstract

车辆交互过程中意图的误解会导致反复出现的规划失败。我们研究了一个决策层，其中语言引导的意图模块读取结构化描述符，计算平滑的意图几何分歧分数，并在走廊包络线上游的承诺之前对计划的操作进行门控。在重放的越野出发和冻结、公开实施下的四个碰撞片段中，门控是修复计划的唯一层：在主要情况下，它在漂移开始后 72 毫秒但在走廊出口前 161 毫秒触发，在所有 10 次重放中保持走廊中的轨迹。第一次校准在 5.9 分钟内绘制了 9 个错误触发，每个触发都将不确定性评分为一半冲突；预先注册的重新设计将不确定性视为弃权，将其减少到每分钟 0.341。两个消融限制了模型的贡献：在部署的资格下，满分在五次失败中的四次中检测速度最快，在未否决规则的五次失败中检测到三次（一个周期为 000871；在不确定的一段时间内发生预发火灾为 000228，五个片段无法分类为信号或巧合；放弃置信项需要两次检测），而在相同误报率的域内轨道上，几何规则的检测量增加了三倍以上。证据支持门控机制；该模型所展示的作用是对这些故障的最快检测以及对几何规则的不确定性否决。Intent misinterpretation during vehicle interactions causes recurring planning failures. We study a decision layer in which a language-guided intent module reads structured descriptors, computes a smoothed intent-geometry divergence score, and gates the planned maneuver before commitment, upstream of a corridor envelope. On a replayed off-road departure and four crash clips under a frozen, disclosed implementation, gating is the only layer that repairs the plan: on the main case it fires 72 ms after the drift onset but 161 ms before the corridor exit, keeping the trajectory in the corridor in all ten replays. The first calibration draws nine false triggers in 5.9 minutes, each from scoring uncertainty as half a conflict; a preregistered redesign treating uncertainty as abstention cuts this to 0.341 per minute. Two ablations bound the model’s contribution: the full score detects fastest on four of five failures under the deployed eligibility, three of five against the unvetoed rule (000871 by one cycle; 000228 by a pre-onset fire on an uncertain stretch that five clips cannot classify as signal or coincidence; dropping the confidence term costs two detections), while on in-domain tracks at equal false positives the geometric rule more than triples its detection. The evidence supports the gating mechanism; the model’s demonstrated roles are the fastest detection on these failures and an uncertainty veto on the geometric rule.

I 简介Introduction

自动驾驶车辆通常会与周围交通进行交互，超越迎面驶来的车辆，协商并道，并对车道变换做出响应，但这些交互阶段仍然是灾难性决策失败的主要根源Autonomous vehicles routinely interact with surrounding traffic, passing oncoming vehicles, negotiating merges, and responding to lane changes, yet these interaction phases remain a primary source of catastrophic decision failures[1, 2]。此类失败通常归因于感知限制。感知确实会下降，包括在这里重播的事件中，当迎面而来的车辆清除时，车道估计会受到几帧的干扰。本文提出的问题是决策层在该窗口中应该做什么：计划者做出与交互阶段不一致的横向操作，将短暂的感知干扰转变为不可恢复的状态，而我们测试的信号是自我自己做出的操作与推断的交互语义之间的一致性. Such failures are usually attributed to perception limits. Perception does degrade, including in the incident replayed here, where lane estimates are disturbed for a few frames as the oncoming vehicle clears. The question this paper asks is what the decision layer should do in that window: a planner that commits a lateral maneuver inconsistent with the interaction phase turns a transient perception disturbance into an unrecoverable state, and the signal we test is the consistency between the ego’s own committed maneuver and the inferred interaction semantics[3, 4].

交互后阶段，即迎面驶来的车辆通过或并道完成后立即的时间间隔，尤其危险。在此窗口期间，自我规划者可能会尝试纠正感知到的横向偏移，而没有认识到纠正本身构成了与安全操作的意图背离。在真实事件重播部分The post-interaction phase, the interval immediately after an oncoming vehicle passes or a merge completes, is especially dangerous. During this window the ego planner may attempt to correct a perceived lateral offset without recognizing that the correction itself constitutes an intent divergence from safe operation. In the real incident replayed in SectionIV，可测量的漂移在迎面而来的车辆清除自我平面后 167 毫秒开始，并且车辆在 400 毫秒内离开道路走廊。如果漂移将车辆带到未铺砌的路肩上，轮胎与路面摩擦力的变化可能会使偏离升级为失控。, the measurable drift begins 167 ms after the oncoming vehicle clears the ego plane, and the vehicle leaves the road corridor within 400 ms. If the drift carries the vehicle onto an unpaved shoulder, the change in tire-road friction can escalate the departure into a loss of control.

现有的安全架构从后果方面解决了这个问题。控制屏障功能Existing safety architectures address this problem from the consequence side. Control barrier functions[5], 责任敏感的安全, responsibility-sensitive safety[6]，并且避免碰撞约束检测不安全状态并在做出决定后进行反应性干预。此外还需要的是预期：检测到自我车辆的预期操作偏离了安全交互语义，并修复计划而不是推翻它。, and collision-avoidance constraints detect unsafe states and intervene reactively, after the decision has been committed. What is needed in addition is anticipation: detecting that the ego vehicle’s intended maneuver is diverging from safe interaction semantics, and repairing the plan rather than overriding it.

我们研究的决策层包括（1）一个语言引导的多智能体意图预期模块，该模块推断意图假设，计算意图几何分歧分数，并触发机动门控和计划修复； (2) 提供场景上下文的单目感知堆栈和轻量级数字孪生； (3) 作为反应性后盾的意图调节安全包络线。本文报告了针对一项冻结实施的两轮实验。第一轮 (v1) 测量机制并诊断特定的评分缺陷。第二轮 (v2) 在实施前预先注册，重新设计不确定性评分并重新测量一切。该论文的贡献是：We study a decision layer comprising (1) a language-guided multi-agent intent anticipation module that infers intent hypotheses, computes an intent-geometry divergence score, and triggers maneuver gating and plan repair; (2) a monocular perception stack and a lightweight digital twin that provide scene context; and (3) an intent-conditioned safety envelope as a reactive backstop. This paper reports two experimental rounds against one frozen implementation. Round one (v1) measures the mechanism and diagnoses a specific scoring defect. Round two (v2), preregistered before implementation, redesigns the uncertainty scoring and re-measures everything. The contributions of the paper are:

七VII结论Conclusion

我们在冻结、完全公开的配置下端到端地测量了意图门控机制两次。第一轮支持它作为预防层：在五个真正的自我失败中，旗帜位于主案例上的走廊出口和四个剪辑上的漂移开始之前，而门控是修复计划的唯一层。第一轮也给它带来了代价：检测滞后于全视野走廊检查，名义驾驶每分钟产生 1.54 次错误触发，所有这些都是因为将模型不确定性评分为一半冲突。第二轮，在实施前预先注册，将单一评分选择重新设计为带有滞后的弃权语义：错误触发降至每分钟 0.341，并且保留了每个意图级别检测，而 v2 未命中的一个预先注册标准正是循环日志显示由评分缺陷制造的 v1 检测的位置：夸大误报的缺陷也可能夸大检测。然后，同一学科纠正了我们自己的界限：重放缺陷使五例消融报告的每个评分变体的发射周期相同，而纠正后的重放则颠倒了该读数。在五个真实故障中，满分在部署资格下的五个故障中检测到最快，在未否决规则的五个故障中检测到三个（一个周期为 000871；在不确定的一段时间内发生预发火灾为 000228；放弃置信项需要两次检测），并且模型的不确定否决权使名义集保持安静（四个无模型错误触发对两个）。如果线索在另一个代理中，则模型似乎会购买预期，67 与 45 相冲突，直到比较以相同的假阳性率进行，此时几何规则大致与其预期相符，并且其检测量增加了三倍以上。这是一个有规则标记的语料库和五次重播失败的狭隘结果；改变它的工作：语义运动学无法恢复的交互，以及结合两个线索而不是让一个线索代替另一个线索的分数。We measured an intent-gating mechanism end to end under frozen, fully disclosed configurations, twice. Round one supported it as a prevention layer: on five real ego failures the flag preceded the corridor exit on the main case and the drift onset on the four clips, and gating was the only layer that repaired the plan. Round one also priced it: detection lagged a full-horizon corridor check, and nominal driving drew 1.54 false triggers per minute, all from scoring model uncertainty as half a conflict. Round two, preregistered before implementation, redesigned that single scoring choice into abstention semantics with hysteresis: false triggers fell to 0.341 per minute and every intent-level detection was preserved, while the one preregistered criterion v2 misses is precisely where the cycle logs show a v1 detection manufactured by the scoring defect: a defect that inflates false positives can inflate detections too. The same discipline then corrected our own bound: a replay defect had made the five-case ablation report identical firing cycles for every scoring variant, and the corrected replay reverses that reading. On the five real failures the full score detects fastest on four of five under the deployed eligibility, three of five against the unvetoed rule (000871 by one cycle; 000228 by a pre-onset fire on an uncertain stretch; dropping the confidence term costs two detections), and the model’s uncertain veto keeps the nominal set quiet (four model-free false triggers against two). Where the cue is in the other agent instead, the model appears to buy anticipation, 67 conflicts against 45, until the comparison is held at equal false-positive rate, at which point the geometric rule roughly matches its anticipation and more than triples its detection. That is a narrow result on one rule-labelled corpus and five replayed failures; the work that would change it: interactions whose semantics kinematics cannot recover, and a score that combines the two cues rather than letting one stand in for the other.
