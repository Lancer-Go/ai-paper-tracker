1 简介Introduction

多智能体系统中的合作是一个基础研究领域，专注于提高系统效率Cooperation in multi-agent systems is a foundational research domain focused on enhancing system efficacy(Amirkhani and Barshooi, 2022; Yu et al., 2010; Du et al., 2023; Jiang and Lu, 2018; Guo et al., 2024; Han et al., 2024; Jaques et al., 2019)。同样，在人类社会中，合作行为已被证明对于解决历史和当代背景下的公共资源（CPR）困境是不可或缺的. Similarly, in human societies, cooperative behavior has proven indispensable for resolving Common Pool Resource (CPR) dilemmas across both historical and contemporary contexts(Gardner et al., 1990; Ostrom, 1990; Ostrom et al., 1994)。 CPR 被认为是可扣除资产，很难甚至不可能执行任何提取限制. CPRs are characterized as subtractable assets for which it is very difficult, or even impossible, to enforce any extraction limits(Ostrom, 1990)，这使得他们的解决方案变得非常重要。事实上，这些问题展现了连续社会困境（SSD）的许多特征, which makes their resolution non-trivial. Indeed these problems exhibit many of the features of sequential social dilemmas (SSDs)(Leibo et al., 2017)先前已投入精力探索合作、领导力和沟通在这些环境中所发挥的作用where prior effort has been invested in exploring the role played by cooperation, leadership, and communication in these settings(Slumbers et al., 2025; Jaques et al., 2019; Park et al., 2022; Piatti et al., 2024).

然而，这些工作并没有直接解决在团体可以通过选举进行集体决策并遵守明确的团体政策议程的情况下领导的影响。这些因素在人类社会中广泛存在，通过民众参与促进治理体系的良好运作，正如几部开创性的政治哲学著作中所指出的那样However, these works do not directly address the effects of leadership in cases where the group can exercise collective decision making through elections and subject to explicit group policy agendas. Such factors are widespread among human societies that promote well functioning systems of governance through popular participation as noted among several seminal works of political philosophy(Plato, 1994; Locke et al., 2003; Rousseau, 1964; Marx and Engels, 2019; Hayek, 1980; Dewey, 1916; Habermas, 1991)。这进一步体现在社会福利的改善和人类社会群体之间的合作性上. This is further reflected among improved social welfare and cooperativity among human social groups(Ostrom, 1990; Ostrom et al., 1994; Allen and Sanglier, 1981; Plowman et al., 2007)并且，在这项工作中，我们着手确定这些是否是促进多主体法学硕士系统中合作和群体健康的重要因素。and, in this work, we set out to determine whether these are important factors in promoting cooperation and group health in multi-agent LLM systems.

影响报告Impact Statement

我们试图了解自组织领导力在多主体法学硕士领域在一系列连续的社会困境中所扮演的角色。我们专注于一种可以在实践中广泛应用的相对通用的方法（计算资源网络、人力资源管理系统、自动驾驶汽车网络、工厂机器人等），因此值得注意的是，在现实世界系统中部署代理时，尤其是在涉及人类的情况下，应始终保持极度谨慎。如果要认真考虑部署具有自组织社交和领导结构的多代理系统，那么在沙盒环境中对特定用例进行广泛的测试至关重要。特别是，一些代理人可能会出于操纵治理流程、代理人之间相互勾结或做出强烈偏见和歧视性决策的动机。We have sought to understand the role that self-organized leadership plays in multi-agent LLM domains over a set of sequential social dilemmas. We have focused on a relatively general approach that could be applied broadly in practice (compute resource networks, human resource management systems, networks of autonomous vehicles, factory robotics etc.) so it is important to note that extreme caution should always be exercised when deploying agents in real-world systems, especially where humans are concerned. If any serious consideration is to be taken to deploy multi-agent systems with self-organized social and leadership structures it would be critical to perform extensive testing for the particular use case in a sand-boxed environment. In particular some agents might be motivated toward manipulation of governance processes, collusion among agents, or strongly biased and discriminatory decision-making.

根据所管理资源的波动性和价值，应分阶段部署经过良好测试的系统，并始终进行良好监控，并制定缓解计划，以防发生任何故障。这项工作是在通过公共 API 服务提供的预训练 LLM 模型子集上进行的，因此我们强烈建议在实际用例中可能考虑的任何新模型上复制我们的研究。此外，法学硕士的幻觉是一个众所周知的问题，任何部署都应该考虑到这一问题，我们要注意的是，法学硕士生成的任何决策都可能基于捏造的前提。然而，在安全部署的情况下，我们相信，与可以发挥多种社会价值观和信息的传统流程相比，传统的人类决策社区和完全合成的决策社区都可以实现潜在的丰富。Depending on the volatility and value of any resources under management, well tested systems should be deployed in a phased manner and always well monitored with mitigation plans in place in case of any failures. This work was carried out on subset of pretrained LLM models available through public API services and therefore we would strongly advise replicating our study on any new models that may be under consideration in a practical use case. Further, hallucinations of LLMs is a well known issue that should be accounted for alongside any deployment and we would caution that any LLM generated decision making could be predicated on fabricated premises. Given safe deployment however, we believe that both traditional human decision making communities and completely synthetic ones could realize a potential enrichment when compared to traditional processes where a diverse set of social values and information can be brought to bear.

完整的合作分类分类结构定义如下：The full cooperative taxonomic categorical structure is defined below:

基于控制：Control based:做出决定是为了获得或保持对游戏动态的更好控制。无论是掌控自己的命运，还是掌控他人的行为。Decision is made in order to gain or maintain better control of the game dynamics. Whether it be control of their own destiny or control of others’ behavior.

合作论证：Cooperative argument:决策的部分或全部合理性是基于促进与其他代理人更好的合作。Decision is partly or fully justified on the basis of fostering better cooperation with other agents.

避免报复/厌恶惩罚：Retaliation avoidance / Punishment aversion:做出决定是为了避免报复或担心未来遭到报复，无论在这种情况下报复是否合理。Decision is made to avoid retaliation or for fear of future retaliation, regardless of whether retaliation is justified or not in this case.

厌恶复杂性：Complexity aversion:决策部分或完全基于减少决策中增加复杂性的因素，相对而言，这种厌恶复杂性的偏好对未来收益的影响不太重要。Decision is partly or fully based on reducing the complexity-increasing factors in the decision making, with comparatively less importance given to the impact of this complexity-averse preference on future payoffs.

回报自满：Payoff complacency:通过声称他们的回报足够高，表明进一步最大化的愿望有限，决策部分或完全合理。Decision is partly or fully justified by claiming that their payoff is high enough as it is, showing limited desire to maximize further.

收益最大化：Payoff maximization:决策的部分或全部合理性在于相信该决策将允许未来的收益优化或增加。Decision is partly or fully justified by the belief that said decision will allow for future payoff optimization or increases.

声誉问题：Reputation concerns:完全或部分基于希望维持、改善或限制对个人声誉的损害而做出的决定。Decision fully or partly based in the hopes of maintaining, improving or limiting harm to one’s reputation.

风险规避：Risk aversion:该决定是合理的，因为代理人更愿意最大限度地减少不可预测结果的风险。在这种情况下，代理人做出的选择被认为会提供更大的确定性或更少的波动性，即使从理论上讲，另一个选择可能会提供更高的潜在回报。The decision is justified because the agent prefers to minimize exposure to unpredictable outcomes. In this case, the agent chooses makes the choice that is believed to offer more certainty or less volatility—even if, in theory, another option might offer higher potential payoffs.

道德考虑：Moral considerations:该决定部分（或全部）基于道德或公平问题。代理人可能会做出选择，因为它“感觉正确”或符合他们正义之举的信念——即使这种选择并不是严格意义上的回报最大化。这可能包括责任感或“道德立场”。The decision is partly (or fully) based on ethical or fairness concerns. An agent might make its choice because it ’feels right’ or aligns with their belief in doing what is just—even if that choice is not strictly payoff maximizing. This could include a sense of duty or making a ’moral stand.’

现状偏见或惰性：Status quo bias or inertia:该决定在维持当前状态或先前选择方面是合理的。代理人可能会坚持使用它，只是因为它很熟悉，或者因为变化感觉太多破坏，即使其他地方存在更高回报的潜力。The decision is justified on maintaining the current state or previous choices. An agent may stick with it simply because it is familiar or because change feels like too much disruption, even if the potential for higher payoffs exists elsewhere.

学习与实验：Learning and experimentation:该决定的动机是收集信息或测试新策略的愿望。代理可能会稍微调整他们的方法，作为“实验”游戏动态的一种方式，即使即时回报不是最高的。这里的目标是更多地了解其他人随着时间的推移如何反应。The decision is motivated by a desire to gather information or test new strategies. An agent might slightly adjust their approach as a way to ’experiment’ with the game dynamics, even if the immediate payoff isn’t the highest possible. The goal here is to learn more about how others respond over time.
