Chaotic dynamics arise across scientific and engineering domains, from fluid flows and climate systems to multi-agent systems, where reliable learning is highly desirable. Standard RL methods optimise expected returns through scalar value functions, implicitly averaging over diverging trajectories and entangling trajectory level instability with the learning objective. We show that under mild statistical stability assumptions, the return distribution evolves more regularly than individual trajectories when measured under the11-Wasserstein 度量，产生更平滑的分布贝尔曼目标。 通过将优化与这种测量级别结构结合起来，分布式强化学习可以提供更好的条件学习。 我们对混沌系统中分布方法的优势以及混沌下强化学习目标的几何结构提供了原则性的解释。-Wasserstein metric, yielding a smoother distributional Bellman objective. By aligning optimisation with this measure level structure, distributional RL provides better conditioned learning. We offer a principled explanation for the advantages of distributional methods in chaotic systems and the geometries of RL objectives under chaos.

1 简介Introduction

在强化学习（RL）的许多应用中，混沌本身经常被忽视，而是与随机性混为一谈，但这种框架掩盖了它的一些重要结构。 事实上，与随机噪声不同，混沌本质上通常是确定性的，由明确定义的底层动态规则引起，但由于无穷小的扰动随时间的放大而变得不可预测In many applications of Reinforcement Learning (RL), chaos as itself is oft ignored and is instead conflated with stochasticity, but this framing obscures some of its important structure. In fact, unlike stochastic noise, chaos is typically deterministic in nature, arising from well-defined underlying dynamical rules, yet unpredictable because of amplification of infinitesimal perturbations over time(Strogatz, 2024)。 复合误差导致多种性质不同的长期结果和一组可能的系统状态，形成复杂的自相似几何结构，而不是平滑的景观. The compounded errors lead to multiple qualitatively distinct long term outcomes and sets of possible system states that form intricate self-similar geometric structures rather than smooth landscapes(Mandelbrot, 2004)。 因此，平均回报（RL 中的标准假设）可能从根本上产生误导。 强化学习和混沌动力学的交叉点广泛且日益突出，涵盖多智能体系统. As a result, averaging returns, a standard assumption in RL, can be fundamentally misleading. The intersection of RL and chaotic dynamics is broad and increasingly prominent, spanning multi-agent systems(Sato et al., 2002; Rudd-Jones et al., 2026)、金融体系的稳定, the stabilisation of financial systems(Rigatos et al., 2019)，空气和水翼流中的湍流减少, turbulence reduction in aero- and hydrofoil flows(Montalà et al., 2025)、气候政策设计, climate policy design(Rudd-Jones et al., 2025b, a)和电网同步, and synchronisation of power grids(Eroglu et al., 2017; Halekotte et al., 2021)。 然而，混沌系统的特性使得许多传统的强化学习假设变得无效，包括价值景观的平滑性和稳定的梯度更新. However, the characteristics of chaotic systems render many traditional RL assumptions invalid, including smoothness in value landscapes and stable gradient updates(Boccaletti et al., 2000; Wang et al., 2023, 2024).

Under identical open-loop policies, one trajectory reaches a goal with high reward while the other diverges into a failure basin. Standard RL collapses these outcomes into a single scalar value, producing a misleading target that corresponds to no realisable behaviour. In chaotic systems, infinitesimal errors in state estimation or action selection amplify exponentially over time, even tiny differences in state can lead to vastly different future trajectories which undermines reliable long-term prediction and control. This “butterfly effect"(Lorenz, 1972)创建了一个敌对的优化环境，因为我们缺乏价值函数表示的状态平滑性，并且在尝试直接优化策略时可能会收到很大的回报方差。 传统方法通常会陷入困境，因为价值预测误差的界限迅速爆炸，使得长期规划不可避免地充满噪音creates a hostile optimisation landscape as we lack smoothness in state for value function representation and can receive a large variance of returns when trying to directly optimise policies. Traditional methods typically struggle because the bounds of value prediction error explode rapidly, rendering long-horizon planning inextricably noisy(Young and Pugeault, 2024).

In chaotic systems, due to the exponential divergence of trajectories, long-horizon returns become highly sensitive to initial conditions. Averaging over such trajectories produces irregular bootstrapped targets, leading to sharp curvature and severe gradient scaling issues in the optimisation landscape. 3.2 为什么混沌动力学下分布比轨迹更稳定Why Distributions are More Stable than Trajectories Under Chaotic Dynamics

耗散动力系统通常朝着吸引子演化，吸引子是相空间的不变子集Dissipative dynamical systems typically evolve toward an attractor, an invariant subset of the phase space(Strogatz, 2024)。 虽然简单系统允许不动点或极限环，但混沌系统通常具有奇怪的吸引子，它们表现出分形几何，因此具有非整数豪斯多夫维数. While simple systems admit fixed points or limit cycles, chaotic systems often possess strange attractors, which exhibit fractal geometry and thus a non-integer Hausdorff dimension(Ruelle, 1976)。 有趣的是，尽管轨迹呈指数发散，但奇异吸引子是相空间中限制轨迹的有界集合，在轨迹内它们表现出混沌行为。. Interestingly, although trajectories diverge exponentially, the strange attractor is a bounded set in phase space that confines trajectories, within which they exhibit chaotic behaviour.

6.3 讨论Discussion

尽管在 Logistic 和 Ikeda Map 中，智能体通过将系统稳定到固定点来逐步抑制混沌行为，但即使在 Double Gyre 和 ABC 流中，学习也会逐步规范所经历的动态。 随着政策更加一致，国事访问分布集中在更狭窄的吸引区域。 从这个意义上说，即使底层流程仍然混乱，学习也会引发某种形式的排序。 然而，由于对初始条件、噪声和扰动的敏感性，返回随机性仍然存在。 分布方法仍然有用，因为它们捕获了这种剩余的多模态，而不是将结果压缩为期望。Although in the Logistic and Ikeda Map the agent progressively suppresses chaotic behaviour by stabilising the system to a fixed point, even in the Double Gyre and ABC flow learning progressively regularises the experienced dynamics. As policies become more consistent, the state visitation distribution concentrates on narrower regions of the attractor. In this sense, learning induces a form of ordering even when the underlying flow remains chaotic. However, return stochasticity persists due to sensitivity to initial conditions, noise, and perturbations. Distributional methods remain useful because they capture this residual multi-modality instead of collapsing outcomes into an expectation.

7 结论Conclusion

贡献摘要。Summary of the Contributions.在这项工作中，我们研究了混沌动力学如何影响基于 Q 学习的 RL 目标。 虽然混沌会导致指数轨迹敏感性和非平滑值景观，但我们表明，在温和的统计稳定性假设下，收益分布可以在 Wasserstein 距离中保持 Lipschitz。 这种测量水平的规律性有助于解释为什么分布式强化学习在混沌环境中产生更好的条件优化。 根据经验，分布式方法可以减少损失变异性并稳定训练。 当混乱被抑制时，增益是适度的，但当强烈混合持续存在时，增益会更清晰。 我们并不声称具有普遍优越性，相反，分布式强化学习在引导目标不稳定的情况下为基于 Q 学习的方法提供了鲁棒性。In this work we investigated how chaotic dynamics impact Q-learning based RL objectives. While chaos causes exponential trajectory sensitivity and non-smooth value landscapes, we show that return distributions can remain Lipschitz in Wasserstein distance under mild statistical stability assumptions. This measure level regularity helps explain why distributional RL produces better conditioned optimisation in chaotic environments. Empirically, distributional methods reduce loss variability and stabilise training. Gains are modest when chaos is suppressed, but clearer when strong mixing persists. We do not claim universal superiority rather, distributional RL offers robustness in settings with unstable bootstrap targets for Q-learning based methodologies.

局限性。Limitations.我们的理论假设 Wasserstein 距离具有统计稳定性，但在分叉附近或完全确定性系统中可能会失败。 尽管分布界限可能会被打破，但对多模式收益进行建模仍然可以优于平均。 我们的实验侧重于离散动作；将分析扩展到连续控制仍然很重要。 最后，我们的结果强调优化稳定性而不是渐近最优性或样本效率改进。Our theory assumes statistical stability in Wasserstein distance, which may fail near bifurcations or in perfectly deterministic systems. Although distributional bounds may break down, modelling multi-modal returns can still outperform averaging. Our experiments focus on discrete actions; extending the analysis to continuous control remains important. Finally, our results emphasise optimisation stability rather than asymptotic optimality or sample efficiency improvements.

未来的工作。Future Work.未来的方向包括将理论扩展到连续动作或研究利用吸引子几何的表示学习。 例如，使用具有策略梯度方法的分布式方法将表明学习分布的好处是否会继续存在，也许安抚和学习回报分布的组合将是混沌系统的最佳方法。 分布式强化学习和策略梯度方法应用平滑，但仍然学习分形值函数，可以避免这一艰巨的表示任务吗？ 最后，最近的工作提出了更好地表征混沌动力学的替代措施Future directions include extending theory to continuous actions or studying representation learning with attractor geometry. For example, using distributional methods with policy gradient approaches would indicate whether the benefits of learning a distribution carry on over, perhaps the combination of mollification and learning a return distribution would be an optimal methodology for chaotic systems. Distributional RL and policy gradient methods apply smoothing, but still learn a fractal value function, can one avoid this tough representation task? Finally, recent work suggests alternative measures that better characterise chaotic dynamics(Finzi et al., 2026).
