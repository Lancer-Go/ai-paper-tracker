1 简介Introduction

代理已经从自然语言交互迅速发展到为人类用户提供工作产品。此类代理的一个主要承诺是能够协助员工完成日常办公套件生产力任务，特别是涉及文档、电子表格、演示文稿和 PDF 的任务。尽管这些任务很常见并且可能看起来是例行公事，但它们通常需要长期执行。在前沿大语言模型 (LLM) 的支持下，编码代理取得了显着的商业成功和广泛的影响。除了编码之外，人们普遍预计 LLM 代理的下一步是从“vibe 编码”到“vibe 工作”的转变Agents have evolved rapidly from natural-language interaction toward delivering work products for human users. A key promise of such agents is their ability to assist workers with everyday office-suite productivity tasks, particularly those involving documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and PDFs. Although these tasks are common and may appear routine, they often require long-horizon execution. Powered by frontier large language models (LLMs), coding agents have achieved notable commercial success and widespread impact. Beyond coding, it is widely anticipated that the next step for LLM agents is the shift from ``vibe coding'' to ``vibe working''chauhan2025vibeworking：使用LLM代理完成长期办公套件工作。这些能力可以大幅提高生产力并带来深远的社会和经济影响。然而，要实现这一潜力，需要制定基准来衡量客服人员是否真正有能力以可接受的成本执行此类办公套件工作流程。: using LLM agents to complete long-horizon office-suite work. Such capabilities could substantially improve productivity and carry profound societal and economic implications. Realizing this potential, however, requires benchmarks that measure whether agents are actually capable of carrying out such office-suite workflows at an acceptable cost.

OmegaUse-OfficeVal 的构建遵循三个设计原则。首先，基准应反映源自真实工作场所场景的真实办公生产力要求。这些任务应该是有意义的，同时保留明确的目标和具体的可交付成果，这些目标和具体的可交付成果通常可以由初级办公室职员、助理或实习生完成。他们的指令还应该反映用户如何在专业环境中自然地提出请求，而不是表达为低级操作的人为序列。其次，与 GDPVal 和 RLI 不同的是，GDPVal 和 RLI 旨在涵盖广泛的具有经济价值的工作类别，OmegaUse-OfficeVal 侧重于每项任务的经济价值。这种任务级别的视角可以直接比较人力成本和法学硕士推理成本。任务层面的经济基础还可以进行价值加权评估，补充基于平均任务绩效的传统分析。第三，我们只包括长期任务。虽然短期和简单的办公室任务也很重要，但我们特意选择具有挑战性的、长期的任务来探索LLM代理人的前沿能力和未来潜力。The construction of OmegaUse-OfficeVal is guided by three design principles. First, the benchmark should reflect authentic office productivity requirements originating from real workplace scenarios. The tasks should be meaningful while retaining clear objectives and concrete deliverables that could typically be completed by an entry-level office worker, assistant, or intern. Their instructions should also reflect how users naturally formulate requests in professional settings, rather than being expressed as artificial sequences of low-level operations. Second, unlike GDPVal and RLI, which aim to cover broad categories of economically valuable work, OmegaUse-OfficeVal focuses on the economic value of each individual task. This task-level perspective enables direct comparisons between human labor costs and LLM inference costs. Task-level economic grounding also enables value-weighted evaluation, complementing conventional analyses based on average task performance. Third, we include only long-horizon tasks. Although short and simple office tasks are also important, we deliberately select challenging, long-horizon tasks to probe the frontier capabilities and future potential of LLM agents.

由于这些原则，OmegaUse-OfficeVal 的一个显着特征是每项任务都与两个经济信号配对。人工劳动时间记录了人工注释者在没有法学硕士协助的情况下完成任务所需的时间，根据人工激励协议收集。任务价格代理估计完成任务的市场价格，使用来自从业者的明确价格信号（如果可用），否则使用基于一致性的专家估计聚合。为了使评估随着法学硕士的发展而具有可重复性和稳定性，我们避免了人工和法学硕士作为评委评分，而是采用基于代码的验证器。每项任务都通过细粒度的规则进行验证，奖励完成的要求并惩罚增加用户维修负担的意外损坏；这些标准被翻译成可执行代码，并通过迭代的人类代码差异解决过程进行校准。As a result of these principles, a distinctive feature of OmegaUse-OfficeVal is that every task is paired with two economic signals. Human labor time records the time required by human annotators to complete the task without LLM assistance, collected under a human incentive protocol. Task price proxy estimates the market price of completing the task, using explicit price signals from practitioners when available and a consistency-based aggregation of expert estimates otherwise. To make evaluation reproducible and stable as LLMs evolve, we avoid human and LLM-as-judge scoring and instead adopt code-based verifiers. Each task is verified through fine-grained rubrics that reward completed requirements and penalize unintended damage that increases the user's repair burden; these rubrics are translated into executable code and calibrated through an iterative human–code discrepancy resolution process.

4.3 基于代码的验证协议Code-based Verification Protocol

我们采用确定性的基于代码的验证协议来评估每个任务的完成质量。 OmegaUse-OfficeVal 评估最终输出文件，而不是强制执行固定的执行轨迹，其中多个有效的工作流程可以产生相同的可交付成果。 基于代码的验证的两种常见替代方法是人工评估和法学硕士法官评估。人工评估作为 RLI 和 GDPVal 中的主要评估机制，可以评估复杂且开放的工件，但其成本高昂、评估延迟高、评估者主观性和可重复性有限。这些因素使得很难在许多模型和代理配置中扩展评估。 另一种选择是使用前沿模型来判断生成的工件的质量，通常称为 LLM 作为判断评估。 对于基准建设，我们认为有两个问题特别成问题。首先，随着前沿法学硕士的快速提高，法官模型的选择变得不稳定。其次，如果未来评估中评委模型发生变化，之前报告的分数可能不再具有直接可比性。 为了缓解这些限制，OmegaUse-OfficeVal 使用基于代码的验证器作为主要评估机制。我们通过迭代过程构建验证协议。We adopt a deterministic code-based verification protocol to evaluate the completion quality of each task. OmegaUse-OfficeVal evaluates the final output files rather than enforcing a fixed execution trajector, where multiple valid workflows can lead to the same deliverable. Two common alternatives to code-based verification are human evaluation and LLM-as-judge evaluation. Human evaluation, used as the primary evaluation mechanism in RLI and GDPVal, can assess complex and open-ended artifacts, but it incurs substantial cost, high evaluation latency, evaluator subjectivity, and limited reproducibility. These factors make it difficult to scale evaluation across many models and agent configurations. Another alternative is to use a frontier model to judge the quality of generated artifacts, commonly referred to as LLM-as-judge evaluation. For benchmark construction, we view two issues as particularly problematic. First, as frontier LLMs rapidly improve, the choice of judge model becomes unstable. Second, if the judge model is changed in future evaluations, previously reported scores may no longer be directly comparable. To mitigate these limitations, OmegaUse-OfficeVal uses code-based verifiers as the primary evaluation mechanism. We build the verification protocol through an iterative process.

4.3.1 专家修订的标题生成。Rubric Generation with Expert Revision.

对于每项任务，我们首先在法学硕士的协助和专家修订下构建细粒度的评分标准。每个量规包含两个维度：For each task, we first construct fine-grained scoring rubrics with LLM assistance and expert revision. Each rubric contains two dimensions: a可用性评价标准usability rubric，它检查交付的工件是否有效、可打开、可编辑和实际可用，以及, which checks whether the delivered artifact is valid, openable, editable, and practically usable, and a任务完成量表task-completion rubric，奖励已完成的特定任务要求，并惩罚增加用户维修负担的意外更改。请注意，我们从接收文件的用户的角度评估工件，测量代理完成了多少有用的工作，以及交付的工件是否在最少的额外人工修复的情况下仍然保持实际可用。, which rewards completed task-specific requirements and penalizes unintended changes that increase the user's repair burden. Note that we evaluate the artifact from the perspective of a user who receives the file, measuring both how much useful work the agent has completed and whether the delivered artifact remains practically usable with minimal additional human repair.

4.3.2 验证码生成和人机代码差异解决。Verification Code Generation and Human-Code Discrepancy Resolution.

然后，我们在编码代理的帮助下将专家修订的标准转换为可执行的验证代码。生成的验证器由专家检查，通过LLM反思进行修改，并通过人类代码差异解决进一步校准，其中专家分数和验证器分数在单个标题项目的级别上进行比较。该过程产生可重复的工件级评估，同时与基于原始用户需求的专家判断保持紧密一致。我们迭代这个过程，直到基于代码的分数与专家判断充分一致。附录中提供了标题设计、验证器生成和差异解决过程的详细信息We then convert the expert-revised rubrics into executable verification code with the assistance of coding agents. The generated verifier is inspected by experts, revised through LLM reflection, and further calibrated through human-code discrepancy resolution, where expert scores and verifier scores are compared at the level of individual rubric items. This process yields reproducible artifact-level evaluation while remaining closely aligned with expert judgments based on the original user requirements. We iterate this process until the code-based scores are sufficiently aligned with expert judgment. Details of the rubric design, verifier generation, and discrepancy-resolution process are provided in AppendixD.

6 结论Conclusion

在本研究中，我们引入了OmegaUse-OfficeVal，并在基准上对LLM代理进行了实验评估。 OmegaUse-OfficeVal 包含 100 个任务，每个任务都与两个经济信号配对，即人类劳动时间和任务价格代理，并通过根据细粒度规则构建的基于代码的验证器进行验证。通过关注最终的办公工件，该基准支持对代理是否能够生成正确、可用且具有实际价值的可交付成果进行可重复的评估。我们的实验表明，当前的法学硕士代理人在办公套件工作流程方面取得了有意义的进步，但在交付质量方面仍然远远落后于人类基线。我们希望 OmegaUse-OfficeVal 及其发布的资产将成为一个严格的测试平台，用于跟踪可靠的“氛围工作”的进展。In this study, we introduced OmegaUse-OfficeVal and conducted an experimental evaluation of LLM agents on the benchmark. OmegaUse-OfficeVal comprises 100 tasks, each paired with two economic signals, namely human labor time and a task price proxy, and verified through code-based verifiers built from fine-grained rubrics. By focusing on final office artifacts, the benchmark supports reproducible evaluation of whether agents can produce deliverables that are correct, usable, and practically valuable. Our experiments show that current LLM agents make meaningful progress on office-suite workflows, yet still remain substantially behind the human baseline in deliverable quality. We hope that OmegaUse-OfficeVal, together with its released assets, will serve as a rigorous testbed for tracking progress toward reliable ``vibe working.''

First, we generate task-specific rubrics by combining LLM assistance with expert revision. Next, we use a coding agent to translate the rubrics into executable verification code. Finally, we conduct human-code discrepancy resolution to revise both the rubrics and the verifiers. We iterate this process until the code-based scores are sufficiently aligned with expert judgment.

D.1 专家修订的评分标准生成Rubric Generation with Expert Revision

我们构建细粒度的评分标准，并通过专家修订对其进行完善。我们首先使用法学硕士根据任务目标、输入文件和预期交付成果生成初始评估标准。然后，我们进行深入的专家修订，迭代提高评分标准的正确性、覆盖面和可评价性。每个任务评分标准由两个主要维度组成：可用性评分标准和任务完成评分标准。We construct fine-grained scoring rubrics and refine them through expert revision. We first use an LLM to generate an initial rubric based on the task objective, input files, and expected deliverable. We then conduct in-depth expert revision to iteratively improve the correctness, coverage, and evaluability of the rubric. Each task rubric consists of two main dimensions: a usability rubric and a task completion rubric.

D.2 验证码生成和反射。Verification Code Generation and Reflection.

专家完成评分细则修改后，我们将评分细则转换为验证码。根据任务类型和输出文件格式，代码验证器自动检查最终交付的工件是否满足标准并报告相应的分数。 代码验证器是在编码代理的帮助下生成的，并随后由专家进行修改。具体来说，编码代理首先根据上一节中描述的专家定义的规则生成初始验证码。然后，我们再次向编码代理提供评分细则和生成的验证器，促使法学硕士反思评分细则和验证码之间潜在的不匹配情况。根据这个反思输出，专家逐项检查代码逻辑，检查每条评分规则是否准确对应评分标准，是否存在遗漏、误报或漏报、实施偏差。对于无法通过代码可靠验证的主观项目，专家进一步修改了标题，将其表达为可交付成果的更具体、可观察和可执行的属性。经过反思和专家修改后，由编码代理重新生成验证码。此过程确保 OmegaUse-OfficeVal 中包含的任务不仅基于实际的用户请求，而且支持稳定且可重复的基于代码的自动评估。After experts complete rubric revision, we convert the scoring rubrics into verification code. Depending on the task type and output file format, the code verifier automatically checks whether the final delivered artifact satisfies the rubric and reports the corresponding score. The code verifier is generated with the assistance of coding agents and subsequently revised by experts. Specifically, a coding agent first generates initial verification code based on the expert-defined rubric described in the previous section. We then provide both the rubric and the generated verifier to the coding agent again, prompting the LLM to reflect on potential mismatches between the rubric and the verification code. Based on this reflection output, experts inspect the code logic item by item, checking whether each scoring rule accurately corresponds to the rubric and whether there are omissions, false positives or negatives, or implementation deviations. For subjective items that cannot be reliably verified by code, experts further revise the rubric to express them as more concrete, observable, and executable properties of the deliverable. After the reflection and expert revision, we generate the verification code again by the coding agent. This process ensures that tasks included in OmegaUse-OfficeVal are not only grounded in realistic user requests, but also support stable and reproducible code-based automated evaluation.

D.3 人类代码差异解决方案Human-Code Discrepancy Resolution

为了确保代码验证者的质量，我们在LLM反思和专家对代码逻辑进行检查后，进一步进行了人为代码差异解决过程。具体来说，对于每项任务，专家手动对代理生成的工件进行评分，并将其分数与基于代码的验证器生成的分数进行比较。专家首先根据评分标准检查LLM代理生成的最终交付成果，确定满足哪些标准项目，并分配相应的分数。然后，我们在相同的工件上运行代码验证程序以获得自动评分结果。To ensure the quality of the code verifiers, we further conduct a human-code discrepancy resolution process after LLM reflection and expert inspection of the code logic. Specifically, for each task, experts manually score agent-generated artifacts and compare their scores with the scores produced by the code-based verifier. Experts first inspect the final deliverable generated by an LLM agent according to the scoring rubric, determine which rubric items are satisfied, and assign the corresponding score. We then run the code verifier on the same artifacts to obtain the automated grading results.

我们在各个评估项目的层面上比较专家评分和验证者评分，并分析差异的来源。如果差异是由实现错误引起的，我们会修改验证者逻辑。如果它是由模糊的标题描述引起的，我们将返回到标题修订阶段。如果反映专家解读不一致，我们会进一步校准评分标准。如果是由于存在多种合理的方法来完成任务，我们将扩展验证者逻辑以涵盖不同但有效的方法。We compare expert scores and verifier scores at the level of individual rubric items and analyze the sources of discrepancy. If a discrepancy is caused by an implementation error, we revise the verifier logic. If it arises from an ambiguous rubric description, we return to the rubric revision stage. If it reflects inconsistent expert interpretation, we further calibrate the scoring standard. If it is due to the existence of multiple reasonable ways to complete the task, we extend the verifier logic to cover different but valid approaches.

通过人类代码差异分析、评分标准修正和验证者修订的循环，我们逐步提高自动评估和专家判断之间的一致性。此过程确保基于代码验证器的评估结果是可重复的，同时与基于原始用户需求的人类专家评估保持紧密一致。Through this cycle of human-code discrepancy analysis, rubric correction, and verifier revision, we progressively improve the consistency between automated evaluation and expert judgment. This process ensures that code verifier-based evaluation results are reproducible while remaining closely aligned with human expert assessments grounded in the original user requirements.
