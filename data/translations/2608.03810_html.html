摘要Abstract

大型语言模型通常描述社会显着目标——政治人物、国家、宗教、组织、历史事件和社会群体——将情感框架与事实内容一起编码：目标可能显得有利或有威胁、平静或冲突、强大或脆弱。现有的工作通过情绪、好感度和情感基准来捕捉这个空间的一部分，但没有一个结合目标导向的 VAD 归因、明确的评分者合同和通行证报告格式。我们引入了 VIBE，这是在效价-唤醒-支配（VAD）空间中以实体为中心的 LLM 输出情感分析的基准。其核心贡献是一个测量契约：VIBE将生成与外部评分分开，区分标量好感度、响应级别VAD和目标导向VAD，并通过情感护照报告概况。三个经验层支持该契约。 H1 显示标量好感度不包含唤醒度和主导度：效价研究结果经过交叉验证（Large language models routinely describe socially salient targets—political figures, countries, religions, organizations, historical events, and social groups—encoding affective framing alongside factual content: a target may appear favorable or threatening, calm or conflictual, powerful or vulnerable. Existing work captures parts of this space through sentiment, favorability, and emotion benchmarks, but none combines target-directed VAD attribution, an explicit scorer contract, and a passport reporting format. 1 简介Introduction

大语言模型 (LLM) 通过情感框架来解释社会世界，将目标呈现为安全或危险、强大或脆弱，而不仅仅是陈述事实。虽然之前的工作表明目标条件行为是可以测量的Large language models (LLMs) interpret the social world through affective framing—presenting targets as safe or dangerous, powerful, or vulnerable—rather than merely stating facts. While prior work shows that target-conditioned behavior is measurable(Buyl et al., 2026; Bang et al., 2024; Plaza-del-Arco et al., 2024; Hamborg and Donnay, 2021; Dufraisse et al., 2023)对评估协议高度敏感and highly sensitive to evaluation protocols(Röttger et al., 2024; Zheng et al., 2023; Zeng et al., 2024)、标准标量或分类指标忽略了关键的细微差别。例如，标量监视器同时通过了罗兴亚人（好感度）, standard scalar or categorical metrics miss crucial nuances. A scalar monitor, for instance, passes both Rohingya (favorability=0.93=0.93）和菲利普·莱纳德（) and Philipp Lenard (0.500.50），完全忽略了前者的极端低显性痛苦框架和后者的高唤醒隐藏消极框架。), entirely missing the extreme low-dominance suffering framing of the former and the high-arousal hidden-negative framing of the latter.

我们引入 VIBE，这是一个基于 VAD 的基准，用于对 LLM 输出进行以实体为中心的情感分析。其核心贡献是We introduce VIBE, a VAD-informed benchmark for entity-centered affective profiling of LLM outputs. Its core contribution is a计量合同measurement contract共同确定：(i) 测量对象（对指定目标的影响）、(ii) 评分者界面（具有明确评分者身份的连续 VAD）、(iii) 启发协议和 (iv) 报告边界。 VIBE 并未声称测量内部模型情绪，而是量化生成文本中的情感属性，并通过that jointly fixes: (i) the measured object (affect toward a named target), (ii) the scorer interface (continuous VAD with explicit scorer identity), (iii) the elicitation protocol, and (iv) the reporting boundary. Rather than claiming to measure internal model emotions, VIBE quantifies affective properties in generated text, reporting them via an情感护照Affective Passport.

维度影响和情感基准。Dimensional affect and emotion benchmarks.

VAD 提供了一个紧凑的连续情感空间：效价捕获评估方向，唤醒捕获激活、紧张、紧迫性或情绪强度，支配性捕获控制、代理、权力、脆弱性或依赖。这种表示基于情感理论和资源，例如环线模型、Warriner 规范、NRC VAD 和 EmoBankVAD provides a compact continuous affect space: valence captures evaluative direction, arousal captures activation, tension, urgency, or emotional intensity, and dominance captures control, agency, power, vulnerability, or dependence. This representation is grounded in affect theory and resources such as the circumplex model, Warriner norms, NRC VAD, and EmoBank(Russell, 1980; Warriner et al., 2013; Mohammad, 2018; Buechel and Hahn, 2017)。情感 NLP 数据集和 LLM 情感基准评估类别情感、强度、对话情感、情商、同理心和角色忠诚度. Emotion NLP datasets and LLM emotion benchmarks evaluate categorical emotions, intensity, dialogue emotion, emotional intelligence, empathy, and role fidelity(Demszky et al., 2020; Mohammad and Bravo-Marquez, 2017; Poria et al., 2019; Sabour et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2024; Feng et al., 2025)。 VIBE 的不同之处在于将目标表示本身作为测量对象：问题是生成的文本如何有效地表示目标，而不是模型是否可以执行情感推理。. VIBE differs by making the target representation itself the measurement object: the question is how generated text affectively represents a target, rather than whether a model can perform emotional reasoning.

稳健性、角色漂移和评估者依赖性。Robustness, persona drift, and evaluator dependence.

法学硕士的价值和意见评估对提示措辞、回复格式、语言、角色和角色很敏感Value and opinion evaluations of LLMs are sensitive to prompt wording, response format, language, role, and persona(Röttger et al., 2024; Moore et al., 2024; Faulborn et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2024; Hu and Collier, 2024)。快速鲁棒性框架和行为测试促进多条件评估，而不是单一模板声明. Prompt-robustness frameworks and behavioral testing motivate multi-condition evaluation rather than single-template claims(Mizrahi et al., 2024; Chatterjee et al., 2024; Ribeiro et al., 2020)。法学硕士作为法官的工作表明，评估者可能有用，但存在偏见、脆弱且需要校准. LLM-as-judge work shows that evaluators can be useful but biased, brittle, and in need of calibration(Zheng et al., 2023; Zeng et al., 2024; Petre-Vlad et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2024; Shi et al., 2025; Muhammad and others, 2025)。这些发现激发了 VIBE 设计的核心选择：记分器配置和协议漂移必须报告，而不是隐藏。. These findings motivate a central VIBE design choice: scorer configuration and protocol drift must be reported, not hidden.

测量和记录。Measurement and documentation.

道德考虑Ethical Considerations

VIBE 涵盖政治、宗教、地缘政治、历史和社会身份目标，因此护照可能被滥用为政治标签、模特排名或有关内部信仰的主张。报告应说明测量合同、不确定性、覆盖范围、评分者身份和解释边界；敏感目标输出应根据具体情况进行汇总、汇总、在需要时进行编辑，并在可能的情况下由人工进行审核。公共工件应优先考虑家庭级别的聚合、记录的评分者合同、编辑规则、预期用途声明和选定的示例，而不是每个目标的排行榜。VIBE covers political, religious, geopolitical, historical, and social-identity targets, so passports can be misused as political labels, model rankings, or claims about internal beliefs. Reports should state the measurement contract, uncertainty, coverage, scorer identity, and interpretation boundary; sensitive target outputs should be contextualized, aggregated, redacted when needed, and audited by humans where possible. Public artifacts should prioritize family-level aggregates, documented scorer contracts, redaction rules, intended-use statements, and selected examples rather than per-target leaderboards.
