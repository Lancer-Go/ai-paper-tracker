摘要ABSTRACT

了解人类活动如何随着时间的推移改变地球是现代遥感和地理空间情报的一个重要目标。重复的卫星观测可以揭示这些变化，但此类观测往往稀疏、采样不规则、受传感条件影响，并且难以解释为连贯的活动序列。重型施工是这一挑战的一个有用的例子，因为工地可能需要数月或数年的时间才能从土地清理到场地准备，再到积极施工，并最终完成。本文介绍了正在进行的基于多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 的地理空间-时间意义构建的工作，通过从情报高级研究项目活动 (IARPA) 基于空间的机器自动识别技术重型构造 (SMART-HC) 数据集导出 SMART-HC-VQA，这是一个基于 Sentinel-2 的视觉问答 (VQA) 数据集。 SMARTHC-VQA 将施工现场注释、施工类型标签、时间阶段标签、位置元数据和观察关系转换为自然语言问题和答案。该公式将重型施工监控重新定义为时间扩展的自动目标识别 (ATR) 和 VQA 问题，其中固定的地理空间站点被视为其属性和活动状态随时间变化的目标。当前数据集包括 21,837 个可访问的 Sentinel-2 图像芯片、65,511 个单图像 VQA 示例，以及通过我们新颖的图像成对组合增强方法生成的约 230 万个两图像时间比较示例。我们描述了用于检索 Sentinel-2 图像、解析历史图像参考、将大型卫星图块分割成以站点为中心的图像、保留 SMART-HC 注释的可追溯性以及分析站点大小、观测计数、时间覆盖、构建类型和阶段标签的分布的数据准备工作流程。我们还描述了基于 LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B 架构的已实现的多图像 MLLM 训练框架，该框架适合接受多个过时的图像输入并在元数据派生的 VQA 示例上进行训练。这项工作不是提出最终的模型评估结果或声称已完成的操作系统，而是为理解语言引导的遥感活动提供了可重复的基础，其目标不仅是检测变化，而且是解释正在发生的事情、它是如何进展的以及它可能会变成什么。Understanding how human activity changes the Earth over time is an important goal for modern remote sensing and geospatial intelligence. Repeated satellite observations can reveal these changes, but such observations are often sparse, irregularly sampled, affected by sensing conditions, and difficult to interpret as coherent activity sequences. Heavy construction is a useful example of this challenge because a site may progress from land clearing to site preparation, to active construction, and eventually to completion over months or years. This paper presents ongoing work toward multimodal large language model (MLLM)-based geospatial-temporal sensemaking by deriving SMART-HC-VQA, a Sentinel-2-based visual question answering (VQA) dataset from the Intelligence Advanced Research Projects Activity (IARPA) Space-Based Machine Automated Recognition Technique Heavy Construction (SMART-HC) dataset. SMARTHC-VQA converts construction-site annotations, construction-type labels, temporal phase labels, location metadata, and observation relationships into natural-language questions and answers. This formulation reframes heavy-construction monitoring as a temporally extended automatic target recognition (ATR) and VQA problem in which a fixed geospatial site is treated as a target whose attributes and activity state evolve over time. The current dataset includes 21,837 accessible Sentinel-2 image chips, 65,511 single-image VQA examples, and approximately 2.3 million two-image temporalcomparison examples generated through our novel Image-Pairwise Combinatorial Augmentation method. We describe the data preparation workflow for retrieving Sentinel-2 imagery, resolving historical image references, chipping large satellite tiles into site-centered images, preserving traceability to SMART-HC annotations, and analyzing distributions of site size, observation count, temporal coverage, construction type, and phase labels. We also describe an implemented multi-image MLLM training framework based on the LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B architecture, adapted to accept multiple dated image inputs and train on metadata-derived VQA examples. Rather than presenting final model evaluation results or claiming a finished operational system, this work provides a reproducible foundation for understanding language-guided remotesensing activity, where the objective is not only to detect change but also to explain what is happening, how it is progressing, and what it may become.

1.1 意义建构定义1.1 Sensemaking Definition

我们将意义建构定义为将多样化、不完整和异质的观察转化为可操作的理解的过程。在遥感中，这意味着超越像素级检测、对象分类或孤立变化检测，转向对活动、进展和重要性的解释。这种区别很重要，因为现代卫星、雷达、视频、跟踪和检测系统可以生成大量传感器数据。尽管如此，收集到的数据量本身并不能产生有意义的情报。对于 SMART-HC 分析 [1, 3]，地理空间-时间意义构建需要整合场地图像、元数据、施工类型标签和时间阶段观察，以推断施工活动的当前状态和可能的进展。因此，所需的输出不仅是类标签，而且是支持基于活动的智能的简明分析描述，例如确定站点是否正在准备、正在积极构建、已完成或可能与特定基础设施功能相关。通过这种方式，该任务与时间推理和意图识别密切相关：模型必须将多个观察结果解释为展开活动的证据，而不是独立的图像。We define sensemaking as the process of transforming diverse, incomplete, and heterogeneous observations into actionable understanding. In remote sensing, this means moving beyond pixel-level detection, object classification, or isolated change detection toward interpretation of activity, progression, and significance. This distinction is important because modern satellite, radar, video, tracking, and detection systems can generate large volumes of sensor data. Still, the volume of collected data does not, in itself, produce meaningful intelligence. For SMART-HC analysis [1, 3], geospatial-temporal sensemaking requires integrating site imagery, metadata, construction-type labels, and temporal phase observations to infer the current state and likely progression of construction activity. The desired output is therefore not only a class label, but a concise analytic description that supports activity-based intelligence, such as determining whether a site is being prepared, actively constructed, completed, or likely associated with a specific infrastructure function. In this way, the task is closely related to temporal reasoning and intent recognition: the model must interpret multiple observations as evidence of an unfolding activity rather than as independent images.

In the SMART-HC setting, this means treating a construction site as a fixed geospatial target whose visual characteristics evolve from land clearing and site preparation to active construction and eventual completion. The task is to predict site attributes, activity phase, temporal progression, and possible intent from one or more dated observations. Example language outputs may include “beginning construction of a large site,” “active construction of an industrial structure,” or “this completed building is likely a data center.” This formulation reframes construction monitoring as a temporally extended ATR and VQA problem in which the model must reason across sparse image sequences rather than classify isolated images.

1.3 贡献和范围1.3 Contributions and Scope

本文介绍了将 IARPA 基于空间的机器自动识别技术重型结构 (SMART-HC) 注释转换为语言监督的地理空间-时间数据集的持续努力，用于多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 研究。第一个贡献是 SMART-HC-VQA 的推导，这是一个基于 Sentinel-2 的视觉问答（VQA）数据集，其中站点级构造观察、构造类型标签、时间阶段标签、位置元数据和图像芯片被转换为自然语言问答三元组。第二个贡献是可重复的实施工作流程，用于从公共时空资产目录 (STAC) 源获取 Sentinel-2 图像 [2]，解析异构 SMART-HC 图像标识符，将大型卫星切片分割成以站点为中心的图像样本，并保留原始注释、下载图像和生成芯片之间的可追溯性。第三个贡献是可访问的 Sentinel-2 子集的数据集分析，包括施工现场芯片大小、每个站点的观察计数、时间覆盖范围、施工类型不平衡和时间阶段标签分布。第四个贡献是一种数据增强策略，该策略使用新颖的图像成对组合增强算法将有限的标记观察扩展到单图像 VQA 样本和大规模两图像时间比较示例。最后，本文提出了一种基于先前 LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B 自动目标识别（ATR）工作的专门多图像 MLLM 架构，适用于对多个过时的施工现场观察进行推理，并生成目标属性、时间状态和活动进展的自然语言预测。总之，这些贡献提供了一条从 SMART-HC 地理空间注释 [1, 3] 到基于 MLLM 的时间意义构建的实用路径，同时谦虚地避免了对操作性能的声明。This paper presents an ongoing effort to transform the IARPA Space-Based Machine Automated Recognition Technique Heavy Construction (SMART-HC) annotations into a language-supervised geospatial-temporal dataset for multimodal large language model (MLLM) research. The first contribution is the derivation of SMART-HC-VQA, a Sentinel-2-based visual question answering (VQA) dataset in which site-level construction observations, construction-type labels, temporal phase labels, location metadata, and image chips are converted into natural-language question-answer triplets. The second contribution is a reproducible implementation workflow for acquiring Sentinel-2 imagery [2] from public Spatio-Temporal Asset Catalog (STAC) sources, resolving heterogeneous SMART-HC imagery identifiers, chipping large satellite tiles into site-centered image samples, and preserving traceability between original annotations, downloaded imagery, and generated chips. The third contribution is a dataset analysis of the accessible Sentinel-2 subset, including construction-site chip sizes, observation counts per site, temporal coverage, construction-type imbalance, and temporal phase-label distributions. The fourth contribution is a data augmentation strategy that expands limited labeled observations into single-image VQA samples and large-scale two-image temporal-comparison examples using a novel Image-Pairwise Combinatorial Augmentation algorithm. Finally, this paper proposes a specialized multi-image MLLM architecture based on prior LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B automatic target recognition (ATR) work, adapted to reason over multiple dated constructionsite observations and generate natural-language predictions of target attributes, temporal state, and activity progression. Together, these contributions provide a practical path from SMART-HC geospatial annotations [1, 3] to MLLM-based temporal sensemaking, while humbly avoiding claims of operational performance.

2. 相关工作2. RELATED WORK

最近的研究已经开始通过几个互补的方向来解决时间遥感理解问题，包括时空电光（EO）和合成孔径雷达（SAR）基础模型、多时相地球观测对话系统、长期城市动态基准和双时态变化字幕多模态大语言模型（MLLM）。这些方法表明人们越来越有兴趣超越静态场景解释，转向能够表示变化、时间顺序和不断变化的地理空间背景的模型。然而，大多数现有工作强调表示学习、密集城市变化分析、配对图像变化描述或一般遥感对话。相对较少的工作集中在稀疏、不规则、站点级活动进展和阶段推理上，其中模型必须解释对同一地理空间目标的多个时间上分离的观察，并用自然语言表达该进展。Recent research has begun to address temporal remote-sensing understanding through several complementary directions, including spatio-temporal electro-optical (EO) and synthetic aperture radar (SAR) foundation models, multi-temporal Earth-observation dialogue systems, long-term urban-dynamics benchmarks, and bi-temporal change-captioning multimodal large language models (MLLMs). These methods demonstrate growing interest in moving beyond static scene interpretation toward models that can represent change, temporal order, and evolving geospatial context. However, most existing work emphasizes representation learning, dense urban change analysis, paired-image change description, or general remote-sensing dialogue. Comparatively less work has focused on sparse, irregular, site-level activity progression and phase reasoning, where a model must interpret multiple temporally separated observations of the same geospatial target and express that progression in natural language.

In contrast, SMART-HC sensemaking requires sparse, irregular, site-level temporal reasoning over activity phase progression [1, 3], which remains less explored.

2.2 时态地球基础模型2.2 Temporal Earth Foundation Models

时间感知地球观测基础模型为基于 MLLM 的遥感对话系统提供了相关工作的补充。 SkySense [7] 特别相关，因为它将遥感视为多模态时空表示学习问题，使用大规模光学和合成孔径雷达（SAR）图像序列来学习可转移的地球观测特征。 SkySense 并不关注语言生成，而是强调跨传感器、地理位置和下游任务的强大时空编码，展示了时间上下文对于通用遥感解释的价值。 OlmoEarth [8] 通过将地球观测数据构建为固有的空间、多模态和序列，类似地推进了这一基础模型方向。其稳定的潜像建模方法旨在从大量多模态地球观测数据（包括时间结构化观测数据）中学习可重用的表示。这些方法表明时间结构对于遥感模型的基础变得越来越重要[7, 8]。然而，它们主要解决表征学习和下游适应，而不是语言介导的活动解释。相比之下，我们的研究使用生成 MLLM 来推理稀疏的站点级构造观察，其中时间阶段进展可以直接用自然语言表达。Temporally aware Earth-observation foundation models provide a complementary line of related work to MLLM-based remote-sensing dialogue systems. SkySense [7] is particularly relevant because it treats remote sensing as a multimodal spatio-temporal representation-learning problem, using large-scale optical and synthetic aperture radar (SAR) image sequences to learn transferable Earth-observation features. Rather than focusing on language generation, SkySense emphasizes robust spatio-temporal encoding across sensors, geographies, and downstream tasks, demonstrating the value of temporal context for general-purpose remote-sensing interpretation. OlmoEarth [8] similarly advances this foundationmodel direction by framing Earth-observation data as inherently spatial, multimodal, and sequential. Its stable latent image modeling approach is designed to learn reusable representations from large volumes of multimodal Earth-observation data, including temporally structured observations. These methods show that temporal structure is becoming increasingly central to the foundation of remote-sensing models [7, 8]. However, they primarily address representation learning and downstream adaptation rather than language-mediated explanation of activity. In contrast, our research uses a generative MLLM to reason over sparse site-level construction observations, where temporal phase progression may be expressed directly in natural language.

2.3 遥感VLM和MLLM2.3 Remote Sensing VLM and MLLM

越来越多的工作将视觉语言模型（VLM）和多模态大语言模型（MLLM）应用于遥感图像理解。 2023 年 11 月，GeoChat [9] 首次引入了具有图像级和区域级推理、视觉接地、字幕、视觉问答 (VQA)、场景分类和语言引导对象定位的接地遥感对话。 RS-LLaVA [10] 类似地将 LLaVA 架构适应遥感图像，以使用低秩自适应和遥感特定指令数据进行联合字幕和 VQA。 EarthGPT [11] 通过大规模指令调整统一光学、合成孔径雷达（SAR）和红外解释任务，将这一方向扩展为通用的多传感器遥感 MLLM。与此同时，LHRS-Bot [12] 结合了自愿提供的地理信息和遥感特定的对齐策略，以提高地理空间理解。与此同时，DOFA [13] 代表了一种补充的基础模型方法，强调跨异构模态的传感器灵活的地球观测表示学习，而不是语言生成。最近的系统将遥感 MLLM 扩展到知识基础和规模：RSRAG [14] 检索外部地理空间和世界知识以改进字幕、分类和 VQA，而 GeoLLaVA-8K [15] 通过令牌修剪和令牌选择机制解决超高分辨率遥感解释。总的来说，这些方法确立了将 VLM 和 MLLM 应用于遥感图像的可行性 [9-15]。然而，大多数关注静态图像理解、基础、检索、大场景感知或多传感器解释，而不是固定地理空间站点上的稀疏时间活动进展。A growing body of work adapts vision-language models (VLMs) and multimodal large language models (MLLMs) to remote-sensing image understanding. In November 2023, GeoChat [9] first introduced grounded remote-sensing dialogue with image-level and region-level reasoning, visual grounding, captioning, visual question answering (VQA), scene classification, and language-guided object localization. RS-LLaVA [10] similarly adapts the LLaVA architecture to remote-sensing imagery for joint captioning and VQA using low-rank adaptation and remote-sensing-specific instruction data. EarthGPT [11] expands this direction into a universal multi-sensor remote-sensing MLLM by unifying optical, synthetic aperture radar (SAR), and infrared interpretation tasks through large-scale instruction tuning. At the same time, LHRS-Bot [12] incorporates volunteered geographic information and remote-sensing-specific alignment strategies to improve geospatial understanding. In parallel, DOFA [13] represents a complementary foundation-model approach, emphasizing sensor-flexible Earth-observation representation learning across heterogeneous modalities rather than language generation. More recent systems extend remote-sensing MLLMs toward knowledge grounding and scale: RSRAG [14] retrieves external geospatial and world knowledge to improve captioning, classification, and VQA, while GeoLLaVA-8K [15] addresses ultra-high-resolution remote-sensing interpretation through token-pruning and tokenselection mechanisms. Collectively, these methods establish the feasibility of adapting VLMs and MLLMs to remotesensing imagery [9–15]. However, most focus on static image understanding, grounding, retrieval, large-scene perception, or multi-sensor interpretation rather than sparse temporal activity progression over fixed geospatial sites.

For electro-optical maritime ATR, Lan et al. [16] introduce an efficient prompt-tuning strategy for large VLMs using hierarchical, multi-granularity prompts and remote-sensing ship priors to improve few-shot and unseen-category fine-grained ship classification. IFShip [17] extends this ship-recognition direction by constructing a domain-knowledge-enhanced chain-of-thought prompt generation mechanism and an instruction-following dataset for fine-grained ship classification, enabling both class prediction and natural-language reasoning. CARP [18] addresses robustness under degraded sensing conditions by developing cloud-adaptive prompting for ship classification under cloud occlusion. For broader remote-sensing object detection, VLPRSDet [19] incorporates vision-language representations into an object detector for language-conditioned recognition. At the same time, LLaMAUnidetector [20] decouples class-agnostic localization from MLLM-based category recognition to support open-

VectorLLM [21] extends this trend from object detection to structured geospatial target representation by generating vectorized building contours from remote sensing imagery, making it especially relevant to understanding constructed infrastructure. However, most remain focused on single-image recognition or detection rather than temporal activity progression.

2.4.1 SAR ATR

合成孔径雷达 (SAR) 和 SAR/光电混合 ATR 研究也开始纳入语言监督和多模态推理。王等人。 [22]提出了一种零镜头SAR目标识别框架，该框架使用视觉语言模型从光学遥感图像中提取语义信息，使用生成扩散模型来支持三维目标建模和SAR模拟，并使用域适应来缩小模拟和测量的SAR图像之间的差距。 SARVLM [23]，包括其 SARCLIP 对比组件，构建大型 SAR 图像文本语料库，并使用对比语言图像对齐训练 SAR 特定视觉语言模型，支持语义检索和零样本 SAR 目标识别。 Popeye [24] 是一种相关的多源船舶检测框架，它使用统一的视觉语言模型来支持跨异构遥感图像的船舶检测。总的来说，这些方法表明 VLM 以及较小程度上的 MLLM 开始通过提示调整、指令调整、EO 到 SAR 语义传输、SAR 特定图像文本预训练、开放词汇识别和多源目标检测来支持 ATR [20, 22–24]。然而，它们主要解决单观察目标识别、检索或定位问题，而目前的工作则侧重于多观察时间意义建构。Synthetic aperture radar (SAR) and SAR/electro-optical hybrid ATR research has also begun to incorporate languagesupervised and multimodal reasoning. Wang et al. [22] propose a zero-shot SAR target recognition framework that uses a visual-language model to extract semantic information from optical remote-sensing imagery, a generative diffusion model to support three-dimensional target modeling and SAR simulation, and domain adaptation to reduce the gap between simulated and measured SAR imagery. SARVLM [23], including its SARCLIP contrastive component, constructs large SAR image-text corpora and trains SAR-specific vision-language models using contrastive language-image alignment, supporting semantic retrieval and zero-shot SAR target recognition. Popeye [24] is relevant as a multi-source ship detection framework that uses a unified visual-language model to support ship detection across heterogeneous remote-sensing imagery. Collectively, these methods indicate that VLMs and, to a lesser extent, MLLMs are beginning to support ATR through prompt tuning, instruction tuning, EO-to-SAR semantic transfer, SAR-specific image-text pretraining, openvocabulary recognition, and multi-source target detection [20, 22–24]. However, they primarily address single-observation target recognition, retrieval, or localization, whereas the present work focuses on multi-observation temporal sensemaking.

2.4.2 基于 MLLM 的 SAR ATR2.4.2 MLLM-Based SAR ATR

我们之前的工作将自动目标识别（ATR）重新定义为语言介导的目标理解问题，而不仅仅是封闭集图像分类任务。在我们最初的研究[26]中，利用移动和静止目标获取和识别（MSTAR）数据集[25]，合成孔径雷达（SAR）车辆图像芯片被视为ATR目标，其类别标签和目标特征可以转换为自然语言字幕和视觉问答（VQA）式监督。 LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B 视觉语言模型 [27] 使用量化和基于低秩适应 (LoRA) 的参数高效微调 (PEFT) 进行调整，以从有限的 SAR 图像中预测车辆类型和描述性目标属性。这表明多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 可以从稀疏标记数据中学习细粒度 SAR 目标语义，同时也暴露了涉及标记化、过度拟合、幻觉生成以及更丰富的 VQA 增强的需求等实际挑战。Our prior work reframed automatic target recognition (ATR) as a language-mediated target-understanding problem rather than only a closed-set image-classification task. In our initial study [26] utilizing the Moving and Stationary Target Acquisition and Recognition (MSTAR) dataset [25], synthetic aperture radar (SAR) vehicle image chips were treated as ATR targets whose class labels and target characteristics could be converted into natural-language captions and visual question answering (VQA)-style supervision. A LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B vision-language model [27] was adapted using quantization and Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)-based parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) to predict vehicle type and descriptive target attributes from limited SAR imagery. This demonstrated that multimodal large language models (MLLMs) can learn fine-grained SAR target semantics from sparse labeled data, while also exposing practical challenges involving tokenization, overfitting, hallucinated generations, and the need for richer VQA augmentation.

后续的 SAR-RAG 工作 [28] 通过将 MSTAR 图像芯片和元数据嵌入到矢量数据库中，扩展了这一公式，从而能够检索语义相似的 SAR 样本，作为 VQA、目标识别、解释生成和定量物理特性估计的基础上下文。这使得评估超越了分类准确性，包括描述性目标质量和车辆重量、尺寸和武器系统装备的回归式估计。这些研究共同展示了从 SAR 目标分类到基于 MLLM 的目标表征的进展，其中检测到的对象被表示为具有可预测的定性和定量属性的语言监督实体。目前的 SMART-HC 工作将这种面向 VQA 的 MLLM 范式从以车辆为中心的 SAR ATR 转移到地理空间施工现场目标，其中自然语言标签描述了目标属性和时间活动状态。The follow-on SAR-RAG work [28] extended this formulation by embedding MSTAR image chips and metadata into a vector database, enabling retrieval of semantically similar SAR exemplars as grounded context for VQA, target recognition, explanation generation, and quantitative physical-property estimation. This enabled evaluation beyond categorical accuracy, including descriptive target qualities and regression-style estimates of vehicle weight, dimensions, and weapon-system armament. Together, these studies show progression from SAR target classification to MLLM-based target characterization, in which detected objects are represented as language-supervised entities with predictable qualitative and quantitative attributes. The present SMART-HC work transfers this VQA-oriented MLLM paradigm from vehicle-centered SAR ATR to geospatial construction-site targets, where natural-language labels describe both target attributes and temporal activity state.

2.5 相关数据集和任务2.5 Related Datasets and Tasks

最近的 SAR 视觉语言数据集表明，遥感图像理解正在从封闭集分类转向指令跟踪、字幕、检测和视觉问答 (VQA)。 SARLANG-1M [29] 引入了 SAR 图文学习的大规模基准，拥有超过一百万个 SAR 图文对、细粒度语义描述、土地覆盖类别、物体标签和多任务问答对。 SARChatBench-2M [30]同样为SAR解释开发了一个大型多模态对话基准，包括图像文本对、目标注释、视觉理解任务和物体检测任务。这些数据集具有相关性，因为它们展示了如何将遥感注释转换为适合训练 VLM 和 MLLM 的语言监督示例。然而，它们主要是为 SAR 图像解释而设计的，而不是稀疏地理时空构建意义构建。在目前的工作中，我们将类似的语言监督原理应用于 SMART-HC 数据集，将施工现场元数据、时间阶段标签和 Sentinel-2 观察结果转换为 VQA 示例，以进行现场级活动推理。Recent SAR vision-language datasets indicate that remote-sensing image understanding is moving from closed-set classification toward instruction-following, captioning, detection, and visual question answering (VQA). SARLANG-1M [29] introduces a large-scale benchmark for SAR image-text learning, with more than one million SAR image-text pairs, fine-grained semantic descriptions, land-cover categories, object labels, and multitask question-answer pairs. SARChatBench-2M [30] similarly develops a large multimodal dialogue benchmark for SAR interpretation, including image-text pairs, target annotations, visual understanding tasks, and object detection tasks. These datasets are relevant because they show how remote-sensing annotations can be converted into language-supervised examples suitable for training VLMs and MLLMs. However, they are primarily designed for SAR image interpretation rather than sparse geospatial-temporal construction sensemaking. In the present work, we apply a similar language-supervision principle to the SMART-HC dataset, converting construction-site metadata, temporal phase labels, and Sentinel-2 observations into VQA examples for site-level activity reasoning.

上述相关工作表明，遥感数据集可以转换为用于字幕、对话和视觉问答（VQA）的语言监督资源。在这项工作中，我们将该原则应用于 IARPA SMART Heavy Construction (SMART-HC) 数据集 [1, 3]，该数据集为施工活动提供结构化的地理空间-时间注释，而不是传统的单图像标签。我们的目标不是创建新的建筑标签或模拟额外的图像，而是将现有的 SMART-HC 注释转换为可重现的 MLLM 就绪数据集。本节介绍源注释、可用图像、Sentinel-2 访问工作流程、以站点为中心的图像芯片生成以及用于派生 SMART-HC-VQA 的 VQA 转换过程。The preceding related work shows that remote-sensing datasets can be transformed into language-supervised resources for captioning, dialogue, and visual question answering (VQA). 3.1 IARPA SMART 重型建筑数据集3.1 IARPA SMART Heavy Construction Dataset

创建 IARPA SMART 重型建筑 (SMART-HC) 数据集 [1, 3] 是为了支持通过卫星图像对全球范围内大规模人类活动进行检测、分类和监测的研究。公共 SMARTHC 存储库 [31] 将数据集描述为重型施工活动的时空注释的集合，旨在用于广域搜索、活动分类和活动预测中的算法开发和评估。与仅定位离散对象的数据集不同，SMART-HC 将重型建筑定义为大型建筑物和相关基础设施的建设。场地边界旨在包括与施工相关的活动的全部空间范围，包括土地整备、配套基础设施、通道、停车场、临时区域和其他相关设施，而不仅仅是最终的建筑占地面积。这使得 SMART-HC 对于地理空间时间意义构建特别有用，因为它的注释不仅描述了建筑工地存在的位置，还描述了其空间边界和活动状态如何随时间演变。The IARPA SMART Heavy Construction (SMART-HC) dataset [1, 3] was created to support research on global-scale detection, classification, and monitoring of large-scale anthropogenic activity from satellite imagery. The public SMARTHC repository [31] describes the dataset as a collection of spatio-temporal annotations for heavy construction activity, intended for algorithm development and evaluation in broad-area search, activity classification, and activity prediction. Unlike datasets that localize only discrete objects, SMART-HC defines heavy construction as the construction of largescale buildings and associated infrastructure. Site boundaries are intended to include the full spatial extent of constructionrelated activity, including land preparation, supporting infrastructure, access roads, parking lots, staging areas, and other associated facilities, rather than only final building footprints. This makes SMART-HC especially useful for geospatialtemporal sensemaking because its annotations describe not only where a construction site exists, but also how its spatial boundary and activity state evolve over time.

SMART-HC 将注释组织到区域、站点和观察中。区域是一个大的地理区域，定义注释和处理的空间和时间范围，并且可以包含任意数量的建筑工地。场地是一个较小的地理空间区域，对于重型建筑数据集来说大于 8,000 平方米，代表活动的基本单位。观察是一张过时的图像或一组图像，捕捉离散时间某个地点的状态，其中一天用作最精细的时间单位。这些概念在 GeoJSON 区域和站点模型中进行编码：区域模型定义可搜索的时空范围，而站点模型使用过时的观测值、多边形和阶段标签来表示随时间变化的活动。由于施工活动可以扩展、收缩或呈现多个同时状态，因此场地边界可能会因观察而变化，并且当同一场地的不同部分处于不同施工阶段时，可以使用子场地多边形。SMART-HC organizes annotations into regions, sites, and observations. A region is a large geographic area that defines the spatial and temporal bounds for annotation and processing, and may contain any number of construction sites. A site is a smaller geospatial area, larger than 8,000 m² for the Heavy Construction dataset, that represents the fundamental unit of activity. An observation is a dated image or set of images capturing the state of a site at a discrete time, with one day used as the finest temporal unit. These concepts are encoded in GeoJSON region and site models: region models define searchable spatial-temporal extents, while site models represent activity over time using dated observations, polygons, and phase labels. Because construction activity can expand, contract, or exhibit multiple simultaneous states, site boundaries may vary across observations, and sub-site polygons may be used when different portions of the same site are in different construction phases.

该注释结构提供了将 SMART-HC 转换为语言监督数据集所需的信息。区域和站点模型提供地理背景；多边形定义施工现场目标边界；观察日期确定时间顺序；建筑类型标签描述站点功能；时间阶段标签描述活动状态。这些字段自然支持 VQA 和指令遵循示例，包括有关施工类型或阶段的单图像问题、有关变化和进展的配对图像问题，以及要求模型总结场地从无活动到场地准备、主动施工和施工后的发展情况的多图像提示。因此，在这项工作中，SMART-HC 不仅被视为活动检测基准 [1, 3]，而且被视为训练 MLLM 的结构化监督来源，以推理其属性和活动状态在稀疏卫星观测中演变的固定施工目标。This annotation structure provides the information needed to convert SMART-HC into a language-supervised dataset. Region and site models provide geographic context; polygons define construction-site target boundaries; observation dates establish temporal order; construction-type labels describe site function; and temporal phase labels describe activity state. These fields naturally support VQA and instruction-following examples, including single-image questions about construction type or phase, paired-image questions about change and progression, and multi-image prompts asking a model to summarize a site’s development from No Activity through Site Preparation, Active Construction, and Post Construction. In this work, SMART-HC is therefore treated not only as an activity-detection benchmark [1, 3] but as a structured source of supervision for training MLLMs to reason about fixed construction targets whose attributes and activity states evolve across sparse satellite observations.

3.1.1 构造类型和阶段注释3.1.1 Construction Type and Phase Annotations

SMART-HC 提供了两个对于地理空间-时间视觉问答特别有用的标签族：构造类型标签和时间相位标签。建筑类型标签描述了重型建筑工地的明显功能类别，包括商业、工业、中型住宅、重型住宅、其他、未知和未标记记录。商业示例可以包括购物中心、医院、体育场、办公室、酒店、存储设施和其他面向公众的设施。工业示例可能包括工厂、发电厂、仓库、配送中心、制造场地以及运输或物流基础设施。住宅类别将中密度低层住宅与较大的高层住宅建筑区分开来。同时，“其他”类捕获了不完全适合主要类别的重型建筑工地。尽管构造类型预测不是核心 SMART-HC 评估任务 [1]，但这些标签对于基于 MLLM 的意义构建很有价值，因为它们将构造观察结果转换为语义上有意义的目标属性。站点类型有助于了解新兴基础设施、活动可能发生的原因以及预期的最终站点功能。构造类型标签不平衡，许多记录标记为“未标记”。然而，带标签的子集仍然支持直接的 VQA 提示，例如“显示了哪种类型的施工现场？”或“此站点是商业、工业还是住宅？”，即使真实答案是“未知”。 SMART-HC 还定义了负面、排除和忽视的类别，包括轻型住宅建筑、独立道路或停车场、运动场、高尔夫球场、太阳能场、自然灾害变化、未继续建设的拆除以及小于 8,000 平方米的活动。这些类别提供了有用的对比监督，因为并非每个可见的变化、清理的地块或类似场地的多边形都应该被解释为积极的重型建设。如果有额外的开发，这些负面的、被排除的和被忽略的标签可能会有用。SMART-HC provides two label families that are especially useful for geospatial-temporal visual question answering: construction-type labels and temporal phase labels. Construction-type labels describe the apparent functional category of a positive heavy-construction site, including Commercial, Industrial, Medium Residential, Heavy Residential, Other, Unknown, and Unlabeled records. Commercial examples may include malls, hospitals, stadiums, offices, hotels, storage facilities, and other public-facing facilities. Industrial examples may include factories, power plants, warehouses, distribution centers, manufacturing sites, and shipping or logistics infrastructure. The residential categories distinguish medium-density low-rise housing from larger high-rise residential construction. At the same time, the Other class captures heavy-construction sites that do not fit cleanly into the primary categories. Although construction-type prediction was not the central SMART-HC evaluation task [1], these labels are valuable for MLLM-based sensemaking because they convert construction observations into semantically meaningful target attributes. Site type helps contextualize the emerging infrastructure, the reasons the activity may be occurring, and the expected final site function. The construction-type labels are imbalanced, with many records marked Unlabeled. However, the labeled subset still supports direct VQA prompts such as “What type of construction site is shown?” or “Is this site commercial, industrial, or residential?”, even when the ground-truth answer is Unknown. SMART-HC also defines negative, excluded, and ignored categories, including light residential construction, standalone roads or parking lots, sports fields, golf courses, solar fields, natural-disaster change, demolition without continued construction, and activities smaller than 8,000 m². These categories provide useful contrastive supervision because not every visible change, cleared lot, or site-like polygon should be interpreted as positive heavy construction. These negative, excluded, and ignored labels might be usable given additional development.

时间阶段标签是推理中最重要的 SMART-HC 注释，因为它们将每个施工现场从静态目标转换为不断变化的活动序列。 SMART-HC定义了五个时间标签： 无活动，代表构建前基线；场地准备，包括土地清理、平整、地面整形和相关准备工作；主动施工，代表建筑物、地基、中间结构或配套基础设施的可见施工；施工后，表示工地或子工地边界内的施工活动明显完成；和未知，表示无法根据可用图像或支持信息可靠地确定相位的观测结果。由于这些标签被分配给过时的场地或子场地面，因此每个施工位置都可以表示为随时间变化的空间边界和活动状态的序列。这使得 VQA 任务能够询问活动是否已开始、施工是否正在进行、站点是否已在阶段之间过渡以及是否已达到最终状态。聚合的 SMART-HC 记录包含 60,437 个时间阶段标签，主要是 30,676 个主动构建观察； Sentinel-2 子集包含 26,587 个时间标签，其中包括 13,820 个主动构建观测值。尽管阶段标签不平衡，但它们为阶段分类、图像对比较、活动进展描述和未来状态推理提供直接监督。构造类型、否定/排除和时间相位注释将 SMART-HC 从检测范围扩展到更丰富的目标理解，使其成为地理空间-时间 VQA 语言监督的结构化源。The temporal phase labels are the most important SMART-HC annotations for reasoning because they convert each construction site from a static target into an evolving activity sequence. SMART-HC defines five temporal labels: No Activity, representing the pre-construction baseline; Site Preparation, representing land clearing, grading, ground shaping, and related preparation; Active Construction, representing visible construction of buildings, foundations, intermediate structures, or supporting infrastructure; Post Construction, representing apparent completion of construction activity within the site or sub-site boundary; and Unknown, representing observations where the phase cannot be reliably determined from available imagery or supporting information. Because these labels are assigned to dated site or sub-site polygons, each construction location can be represented as a sequence of spatial boundaries and activity states over time. This enables VQA tasks that ask whether activity has begun, whether construction is progressing, whether a site has transitioned between phases, and whether it has reached an end state. The aggregated SMART-HC records contain 60,437 temporalphase labels, dominated by 30,676 Active Construction observations; the Sentinel-2 subset contains 26,587 temporal labels, including 13,820 Active Construction observations. Although the phase labels are imbalanced, they provide direct supervision for phase classification, image-pair comparison, activity-progression description, and future-state reasoning. Together, the construction-type, negative/excluded, and temporal phase annotations extend SMART-HC beyond detection into richer target understanding, making it a structured source of language supervision for geospatial-temporal VQA.

3.1.2 卫星图像来源及获取3.1.2 Satellite Imagery Sources and Access

SMART-HC 注释与多源卫星成像生态系统相关，而不是与单图像产品相关。主要注释源包括 Landsat-8、Sentinel-2 和 Maxar WorldView 图像，而 Planet Labs 图像用作补充程序源，但不随发布的注释数据集一起分发。这种多传感器设计反映了 SMART-HC 结合互补的空间、光谱和时间特征的目标：Landsat-8 以较粗的空间分辨率提供广泛的多光谱覆盖，Sentinel-2 提供可公开访问的具有中等空间分辨率和频繁重访的多光谱图像 [2]，WorldView 提供非常高分辨率的商业图像，而 Planet 图像提供高时间节奏但公共可用性有限。原则上，这种多样性支持跨异构传感条件的稳健地理空间-时间活动分析。然而，在实践中，可用的图像子集受到许可、目录访问、历史元数据一致性和再现性的限制。The SMART-HC annotations are associated with a multi-source satellite-imaging ecosystem rather than a single-image product. The primary annotated sources include Landsat-8, Sentinel-2, and Maxar WorldView imagery, while Planet Labs imagery was used as a supplemental program source but is not distributed with the released annotation dataset. This multisensor design reflects the SMART-HC objective of combining complementary spatial, spectral, and temporal characteristics: Landsat-8 provides broad multispectral coverage at coarser spatial resolution, Sentinel-2 provides openly accessible multispectral imagery with moderate spatial resolution and frequent revisit [2], WorldView provides very-highresolution commercial imagery, and Planet imagery offers high temporal cadence but limited public availability. In principle, this diversity supports robust geospatial-temporal activity analysis across heterogeneous sensing conditions. In practice, however, the usable imagery subset is constrained by licensing, catalog access, historical metadata consistency, and reproducibility.

聚合的 SMART-HC 注释清单包含 119,453 个目标观测值、13,254 个源图像图块和 32,433 个建筑工地。 “无源”类别包含 62,556 个目标观测值和 31,278 个建筑工地，但没有关联的源图像图块，使得这些记录无法作为 MLLM 培训的直接视觉输入。图像来源之间的公众获取也参差不齐。 Sentinel-2 Level-2A 是用于可重复数据集构建的最实用的开源，而某些 Landsat-8 和 Sentinel-2 Level-1C 产品可能需要请求者付费的云访问。 WorldView 和 Planet 图像通常需要单独的商业许可，这限制了它们在公开可复制工作流程中的立即使用。此外，一些早期的 SMART-HC 图像引用早于当前的时空资产目录 (STAC) 工作流程，并且可能难以在现代公共目录中解析。因此，尽管 SMARTHC 是为多源地理空间-时间分析而设计的，但这项工作优先考虑了 Sentinel-2 图像，因为它为构建 SMART-HC 衍生的 VQA 数据集提供了公共可访问性、时间覆盖范围、再现性和规模的最佳平衡。The aggregated SMART-HC annotation inventory contains 119,453 target observations, 13,254 source image tiles, and 32,433 construction sites. The “No Source” category contains 62,556 target observations and 31,278 construction sites, but no associated source image tiles, making those records unavailable as direct visual inputs for MLLM training. Public access is also uneven among imagery sources. Sentinel-2 Level-2A is the most practical open source for reproducible dataset construction, while some Landsat-8 and Sentinel-2 Level-1C products may require requester-pays cloud access. WorldView and Planet imagery generally require separate commercial licensing, limiting their immediate use in an openly reproducible workflow. In addition, some early SMART-HC image references predate the current Spatio-Temporal Asset Catalog (STAC) workflows and may be difficult to resolve in modern public catalogs. Consequently, although SMARTHC was designed for multi-source geospatial-temporal analysis, this work prioritizes Sentinel-2 imagery because it offers the best balance of public accessibility, temporal coverage, reproducibility, and scale for constructing a SMART-HCderived VQA dataset.

3.2 衍生数据集：SMART-HC-VQA3.2 Derived Dataset: SMART-HC-VQA

SMART-HC 数据集提供了一个机会，可以将先前基于多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 的自动目标识别 (ATR) 工作从以车辆为中心的识别扩展到对固定基础设施的地理空间-时间理解。在这种情况下，建筑工地被视为 ATR 目标，因为它是一个感兴趣的局部物体或活动，其身份、属性、状态和演变必须从遥感观测中推断出来。该任务不是识别单个车辆芯片或静态物体作物，而是描述跨时间的固定地理空间站点的特征。因此，我们推导出 SMART-HC-VQA，这是一个由 SMART-HC 注释构建的基于 Sentinel-2 的视觉问答 (VQA) 数据集。该数据集将构造类型标签、时间阶段标签、地理元数据、站点观测和观测关系转换为自然语言问答对。由此产生的公式允许 MLLM 回答有关目标属性的问题，例如站点是否是商业、工业或住宅，以及临时活动状态，例如无活动、场地准备、施工中或施工后。在此过程中，SMART-HC-VQA 将重型施工监测重新定义为临时扩展的 ATR 和 VQA 问题，其中目标是施工现场，其可观测状态在稀疏卫星观测中发生变化。The SMART-HC dataset provides an opportunity to extend prior multimodal large language model (MLLM)-based automatic target recognition (ATR) work from vehicle-centered recognition to geospatial-temporal understanding of stationary infrastructure. In this setting, a construction site is treated as an ATR target because it is a localized object or activity of interest whose identity, attributes, state, and evolution must be inferred from remote-sensing observations. Rather than recognizing a single vehicle chip or static object crop, the task is to characterize a fixed geospatial site across time. We therefore derive SMART-HC-VQA, a Sentinel-2-based visual question answering (VQA) dataset constructed from SMART-HC annotations. This dataset converts construction-type labels, temporal phase labels, geographic metadata, site observations, and observation relationships into natural-language question-answer pairs. The resulting formulation allows an MLLM to answer questions about target attributes, such as whether a site is commercial, industrial, or residential, and temporal activity states, such as No Activity, Site Preparation, Active Construction, or Post Construction. In doing so, SMART-HC-VQA reframes heavy-construction monitoring as a temporally extended ATR and VQA problem in which the target is a construction site whose observable state changes across sparse satellite observations.

3.2.1 Sentinel-2图像下载3.2.1 Sentinel-2 Imagery Download

Sentinel-2 图像是从哥白尼数据空间生态系统获取的，该生态系统是欧洲航天局 (ESA) 运营的欧盟哥白尼计划的一部分 [2]。 Sentinel-2 星座提供多光谱地球观测图像，具有 13 个光谱带、高达 10 m 的空间分辨率、290 km 的测绘带，以及使用两颗卫星星座进行大约五天的重访。如果可用，我们使用 Level-2A 产品，它提供适合分析的大气校正表面反射率图像。Sentinel-2 imagery was acquired from the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem, part of the European Union Copernicus program operated by the European Space Agency (ESA) [2]. The Sentinel-2 constellation provides multispectral Earthobservation imagery with 13 spectral bands, up to 10 m spatial resolution, a 290 km swath, and approximately five-day revisit using the two-satellite constellation. When available, we use Level-2A products, which provide atmospherically corrected surface-reflectance imagery suitable for analysis.

尽管 Sentinel-2 数据是公开可用的，但检索与 SMART-HC 注释相关的图像需要自定义下载工作流程。 SMART-HC 使用多种不一致的格式记录参考 Sentinel-2 图像，包括 EarthSearch 标识符、ESA 产品名称、仅时间戳条目、紧凑图块/日期字符串以及结合国家、地区、站点、图块、日期、处理级别和资产信息的 SMART 特定标签。因此，下载器将每个引用标准化为可搜索字段，例如卫星平台、图块、日期、处理级别、站点几何形状和首选图像资产。然后，它使用越来越灵活的策略搜索 EarthSearch 时空资产目录 (STAC)，从精确的项目查找开始，然后扩展到平铺/日期搜索、回退元数据、基于几何的搜索、更宽的时间窗口以及需要时具有 1C 级回退的 2A 级。Although Sentinel-2 data is publicly available, retrieving the imagery associated with SMART-HC annotations required a custom download workflow. The SMART-HC records reference Sentinel-2 imagery using several inconsistent formats, including EarthSearch identifiers, ESA product names, timestamp-only entries, compact tile/date strings, and SMARTspecific labels that combine country, region, site, tile, date, processing level, and asset information. The downloader therefore normalizes each reference into searchable fields such as satellite platform, tile, date, processing level, site geometry, and preferred image asset. It then searches the EarthSearch Spatio-Temporal Asset Catalog (STAC) using increasingly flexible strategies, beginning with exact item lookup and then expanding to tile/date searches, fallback metadata, geometry-based searches, wider time windows, and Level-2A with Level-1C fallback when needed.

需要额外的工程来使大规模检索可靠且可重复。一些站点几何形状很小，可能无法可靠地与整个 Sentinel-2 场景足迹相交，一些目录记录对视觉图像资产使用不同的名称，并且一些较旧的参考在当前公共目录中不再可用。因此，工作流程使用元数据一致性和时间邻近性对候选场景进行排名，重试失败的下载，避免重复下载，分配确定性文件名，并记录从每个 SMART-HC 记录到已解析的 Sentinel-2 图像和保存的文件的可追溯性。使用此工作流程，数十万个搜索排列被发送到 EarthSearch STAC 目录，从而生成超过 1 TB 的 Sentinel-2 图像，用于下游碎片和质量评估。Additional engineering was required to make large-scale retrieval reliable and reproducible. Some site geometries are small and may not reliably intersect the full Sentinel-2 scene footprint, some catalog records use different names for visual image assets, and some older references are no longer available in current public catalogs. The workflow therefore ranks candidate scenes using metadata agreement and temporal proximity, retries failed downloads, avoids duplicate downloads, assigns deterministic filenames, and records traceability from each SMART-HC record to the resolved Sentinel-2 image and saved file. Using this workflow, hundreds of thousands of search permutations were issued to the EarthSearch STAC catalog, resulting in more than 1 TB of Sentinel-2 imagery for downstream chipping and quality assessment.

3.2.2 施工现场目标切削3.2.2 Construction Site Target Chipping

下载 Sentinel-2 源图像后，每个大型卫星图块都被转换为较小的以站点为中心的图像芯片，用于视觉问答。此步骤是必要的，因为单个 Sentinel-2 图块覆盖了很大的地理区域，并且可能包含在不同时间观察到的许多 SMART-HC 施工现场。碎石管道使用每个场地的多边形几何形状来定位施工目标，然后提取以场地边界为中心的方形图像。该芯片并没有严格掩盖多边形，而是保留了周围的环境，包括道路、集结区、附近的建筑物、裸露地面和支持基础设施，这可能有助于解释施工类型和阶段。After Sentinel-2 source imagery was downloaded, each large satellite tile was converted into smaller site-centered image chips for visual question answering. This step was necessary because a single Sentinel-2 tile covers a large geographic area and may contain many SMART-HC construction sites observed at different times. The chipping pipeline uses each site’s polygon geometry to locate the construction target, then extracts a square image centered on the site boundary. Rather than tightly masking the polygon, the chip preserves surrounding context, including roads, staging areas, nearby buildings, bare ground, and supporting infrastructure, which may be useful for interpreting construction type and phase.

在切割过程中必须解决几个实际的地理空间问题。 SMART-HC 站点几何形状以多边形或多多边形坐标的形式提供，有时以需要解析和验证的表格格式存储。由于纬度和经度坐标不对应于相等的地面距离，因此管道在定义芯片范围之前将每个场地几何图形转换为局部投影坐标系。然后，源栅格被高效读取，重新采样到一致的 10 m 地面采样距离，并写入北上图像芯片。如果请求的芯片超出了可用的 Sentinel-2 块，则会填充缺失的区域以维持输出大小和空间范围。Several practical geospatial issues had to be addressed during chipping. SMART-HC site geometries are provided as polygon or multi-polygon coordinates, sometimes stored in tabular formats that require parsing and validation. Because latitude and longitude coordinates do not correspond to equal ground distances, the pipeline converts each site geometry into a local projected coordinate system before defining the chip extent. The source raster is then read efficiently, resampled to a consistent 10 m ground sampling distance, and written as a north-up image chip. If the requested chip exceeds the available Sentinel-2 tile, the missing region is padded to maintain the output size and spatial extent.

生成的文件名对关键元数据进行编码，包括站点标识符、采集时间戳、构造类型标签和时间阶段标签。该管道还记录诊断信息，包括芯片中心、空间边界、像素尺寸、地面样本距离、站点跨度以及是否需要填充。这些设计选择使切片过程具有可重复性和可审计性，同时保留下游 MLLM 训练所需的空间环境。生成的芯片可作为 SMART-HC-VQA 的视觉输入，其中每个图像都可以与有关构造类型、时间阶段、位置或随时间变化的问题配对。The generated filenames encode key metadata, including site identifier, acquisition timestamp, construction-type label, and temporal phase label. The pipeline also records diagnostic information, including chip center, spatial bounds, pixel dimensions, ground sample distance, site span, and whether padding was required. These design choices make the chipping process reproducible and auditable while preserving the spatial context needed for downstream MLLM training. The resulting chips serve as visual inputs to SMART-HC-VQA, where each image can be paired with questions about construction type, temporal phase, location, or change over time.

3.2.3 站点数据分析3.2.3 Site Data Analysis

由此产生的 SMART-HC-VQA Sentinel-2 图像芯片数据集在施工现场级别不均匀，这对时间学习和 MLLM 训练效率都有重要影响。大多数网站生产的芯片相对较小，图像尺寸集中在 50 × 50 像素左右。只有少数站点超过 100 × 100 像素，少数站点超过 200 × 200 像素，很少有异常值超过 400 × 400 像素。这种长尾尺寸分布带来了两个相互竞争的挑战：非常小的芯片可能缺乏足够的视觉上下文来进行可靠的解释，而大芯片需要更多的视觉标记，并且可能在训练期间产生 GPU 内存峰值。观察计数同样不平衡。许多建筑工地只有一个或几个可用的观测值，而较小的子集包含数十个观测值，而罕见的工地包含超过 100 个甚至 250 个时间图像芯片。时间覆盖范围也差异很大，许多地点仅在一天内观察到，而其他地点则跨越长达五年的多年时间。因此，数据集包含稀疏、不规则的卫星观测历史，而不是密集的类似视频的序列。尽管这种稀疏性，许多站点仍包含多个时间阶段标签。信息最丰富的示例包括施工的几个阶段，通常是无活动、场地准备、施工中和施工后。仅具有一个观察到的阶段的站点提供的变化证据有限，而具有三个或更多观察到的阶段的站点对于学习时间进程特别有价值。总体而言，该数据集支持地理空间-时间意义建构，但其分布揭示了实际挑战：大多数站点都很小，许多时间序列稀疏，最有用的多阶段构建历史集中在可用位置的子集中。The resulting SMART-HC-VQA Sentinel-2 image-chip dataset is uneven at the construction-site level, which has important implications for both temporal learning and MLLM training efficiency. Most sites produce relatively small chips, with image dimensions concentrated around 50 × 50 pixels. Only a small number of sites exceed 100 × 100 pixels, a few exceed 200 × 200 pixels, and rare outliers exceed 400 × 400 pixels. This long-tailed size distribution creates two competing challenges: very small chips may lack sufficient visual context for reliable interpretation, while large chips require many more visual tokens and may produce GPU memory spikes during training. Observation counts are similarly imbalanced. Many construction sites have only one or a few usable observations, while a smaller subset contains tens of observations, and rare sites contain more than 100 or even 250 temporal image chips. Temporal coverage also varies widely, with many sites observed on only a single day and others spanning multi-year periods of up to five years. As a result, the dataset comprises sparse, irregular satellite observation histories rather than dense video-like sequences. Despite this sparsity, many sites contain multiple temporal phase labels. The most informative examples include several phases of construction, typically No Activity, Site Preparation, Active Construction, and Post Construction. Sites with only one observed phase provide limited evidence of change, while sites with three or more observed phases are especially valuable for learning temporal progression. Overall, the dataset supports geospatial-temporal sensemaking, but its distribution reveals practical challenges: most sites are small, many temporal sequences are sparse, and the most useful multi-phase construction histories are concentrated in a subset of available locations.

3.2.4 生成 VQA 三元组3.2.4 Generating VQA Triplets

为了将 SMART-HC 注释转换为视觉问答 (VQA) 数据，每个标记的站点观察都表示为图像-问题-答案三元组。该图像是以建筑工地为中心的 Sentinel-2 芯片，问题询问该工地的已知属性，答案是来自 SMART-HC 元数据的相应地面实况值。施工类型标签支持诸如“显示什么类型的施工现场？”之类的问题，时间阶段标签支持诸如“可见施工的哪个阶段？”之类的问题，位置元数据支持有关与现场相关的国家或地区的问题。我们使用具有已知值的元数据字段定义一组有限的可靠问题类型，确保目标答案保持确定性且可直接评估。 21,837 个 Sentinel-2 施工现场芯片中的每一个都与多个元数据衍生的 VQA 任务配对，包括施工类型、时间阶段和地理位置，生成 65,511 个单图像 VQA 样本。为了提高语言多样性，大型语言模型 (LLM) 可以生成每个问题的释义版本，同时保留相同的真实答案。这增加了自然语言提示的多样性，而不会在输出标签中引入歧义。通过这种方式，SMART-HC 施工类型、时间阶段、位置和相关元数据被转换为单图像 VQA 监督，以了解场地属性和施工活动状态。To convert SMART-HC annotations into visual question answering (VQA) data, each labeled site observation is represented as an image-question-answer triplet. The image is the Sentinel-2 chip centered on a construction site, the question asks about a known property of that site, and the answer is the corresponding ground-truth value from the SMART-HC metadata. Construction-type labels support questions such as “What type of construction site is shown?”, temporal phase labels support questions such as “What phase of construction is visible?”, and location metadata supports questions about the country or region associated with the site. We define a finite set of reliable question types using metadata fields with known values, ensuring that target answers remain deterministic and directly evaluable. Each of the 21,837 Sentinel-2 construction-site chips is paired with multiple metadata-derived VQA tasks, including construction type, temporal phase, and geographic location, producing 65,511 single-image VQA samples. To improve language diversity, large language models (LLMs) can generate paraphrased versions of each question while preserving the same groundtruth answer. This increases the variety of natural-language prompts without introducing ambiguity into the output labels. In this way, SMART-HC construction types, temporal phases, locations, and related metadata are transformed into singleimage VQA supervision to learn both site attributes and construction activity states.

3.3 图像成对组合增强3.3 Image-Pairwise Combinatorial Augmentation

为了超越单图像 VQA，我们引入了图像成对组合增强，它使用来自同一施工现场的多个观察结果来生成两个图像时间比较示例。在此设置中，多图像 MLLM 接收来自同一地点的两个过时的芯片，并被要求对它们进行比较，识别该地点是否发生变化，确定相对时间顺序，或对两个观察之间的构造状态转换进行分类。由于具有多个观测值的站点可以以许多有效的组合进行配对，因此受监督示例的数量会组合增长，而无需新的图像、模拟数据或手动注释的标签。在本研究中，成对扩展将数据集从 65,511 个单图像 VQA 示例增加到大约 230 万个双图像时间比较示例。这种增强将稀疏的卫星观测转化为大量有监督的时间推理任务，使未来的模型能够学习诸如“无活动”与“场地准备”、“场地准备”与“主动施工”、“主动施工”与“施工后”或无明显相变等关系。To extend beyond single-image VQA, we introduce Image-Pairwise Combinatorial Augmentation, which uses multiple observations from the same construction site to generate two-image temporal comparison examples. In this setting, a multiimage MLLM receives two dated chips from the same site and is asked to compare them, identify whether the site changed, determine relative temporal order, or classify the construction-state transition between the two observations. Because a site with multiple observations can be paired in many valid combinations, the number of supervised examples grows combinatorially without requiring new imagery, simulated data, or manually annotated labels. In this study, pairwise expansion increases the dataset from 65,511 single-image VQA examples to approximately 2.3 million two-image temporal comparison examples. This augmentation transforms sparse satellite observations into a large set of supervised temporal reasoning tasks, enabling future models to learn relationships such as No Activity to Site Preparation, Site Preparation to Active Construction, Active Construction to Post Construction, or no apparent phase change.

4. 传销培训4. MLLM TRAINING

这项工作实现了一个多图像多模态大语言模型（MLLM）训练框架，用于 SMART-HC 施工现场观测的地理空间时间意义构建。该框架将我们之前的合成孔径雷达 (SAR) 工作中的 LLaVA-NeXT Mistral7B 自动目标识别 (ATR) 管道改编为 Sentinel-2 SMART-HC-VQA 数据集，其中每个建筑工地都被视为固定的地理空间目标，其属性和活动状态随着时间的推移而变化。当前的实现支持有关构造类型、时间阶段和位置的单图像问题，以及有关变化、时间顺序和阶段进展的两图像问题。本节不是提出新的基础模型，而是描述如何修改现有的 MLLM 架构以接受多个过时的图像输入并训练元数据派生的视觉问答 (VQA) 示例。This work implements a multi-image multimodal large language model (MLLM) training framework for geospatialtemporal sensemaking of SMART-HC construction-site observations. The framework adapts the LLaVA-NeXT Mistral7B automatic target recognition (ATR) pipeline from our prior synthetic aperture radar (SAR) work to the Sentinel-2 SMART-HC-VQA dataset, where each construction site is treated as a fixed geospatial target whose attributes and activity state evolve over time. The current implementation supports single-image questions about construction type, temporal phase, and location, as well as two-image questions about change, temporal ordering, and phase progression. Rather than proposing a new foundation model, this section describes how an existing MLLM architecture was modified to accept multiple dated image inputs and train on metadata-derived visual question answering (VQA) examples.

4.1 基线 MLLM 架构4.1 Baseline MLLM Architecture

该训练框架建立在我们之前基于 MLLM 的 SAR ATR 工作中使用的 LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B 架构的基础上 [26-28]。该模型遵循 LLaVA 风格的设计，结合了预训练语言模型、视觉编码器和用于图像条件语言生成的多模态投影层。自然语言问题由 Mistral-7B 语言模型进行标记和处理。同时，每个输入图像均由对比语言图像预训练 (CLIP) 视觉变换器 (ViT)-Large Patch-14 336 像素视觉编码器进行编码。 LLaVANeXT 投影仪将生成的视觉嵌入映射到语言模型嵌入空间，并在其中插入到提示上下文中。然后，解码器根据问题和视觉内容共同生成答案。The training framework builds on the LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B architecture used in our prior MLLM-based SAR ATR work [26–28]. This model follows a LLaVA-style design that combines a pretrained language model, a vision encoder, and a multimodal projection layer for image-conditioned language generation. A natural-language question is tokenized and processed by the Mistral-7B language model. At the same time, each input image is encoded by a Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training (CLIP) Vision Transformer (ViT)-Large Patch-14 336-pixel vision encoder. The LLaVANeXT projector maps the resulting visual embeddings into the language-model embedding space, where they are inserted into the prompt context. The decoder then generates an answer conditioned jointly on the question and the visual content.

该基线适用于 SMART-HC-VQA，因为它已经适用于使用有限标记图像的遥感 ATR 任务。在我们之前的 MSTAR 工作中，LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B 通过量化和参数高效微调进行训练，而不是完整的模型重新训练。具体来说，4 位量化模型权重、16 位大脑浮点计算和量化低阶适应 (QLoRA) 减少了内存需求，同时保留了预训练模型的语言和视觉推理能力。对于目前的工作，我们保留了这个基线架构，但修改了输入结构和训练数据，以便模型可以处理 Sentinel-2 施工现场芯片并回答有关施工属性、时间阶段和活动进展的问题。This baseline is appropriate for SMART-HC-VQA because it has already been adapted to remote-sensing ATR tasks using limited labeled imagery. In our prior MSTAR work, LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B was trained with quantization and parameter-efficient fine-tuning rather than full model retraining. Specifically, 4-bit quantized model weights, 16-bit brain floating point computation, and Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation (QLoRA) reduced memory requirements while preserving the pretrained model’s language and visual reasoning capabilities. For the present work, we retain this baseline architecture but modify the input structure and training data so that the model can process Sentinel-2 construction-site chips and answer questions about construction attributes, temporal phases, and activity progression.

4.2 多图像输入适配4.2 Multi-Image Input Adaptation

SMART-HC-VQA 的核心架构变化是支持来自同一施工现场的多个过时观测。在基线单图像 VQA 设置中，模型接收单个图像芯片和单个文本提示。然而，对于地理空间-时间意义构建，该模型必须比较同一地点的两个或多个 Sentinel-2 观测结果，并生成反映视觉证据和时间背景的答案。为了支持这一点，每个 Sentinel2 芯片均由共享 CLIP 视觉编码器和 LLaVA-NeXT 投影仪独立编码。生成的视觉标记序列作为单独的图像片段插入到语言模型上下文中。The central architectural change for SMART-HC-VQA is support for multiple dated observations from the same construction site. In the baseline single-image VQA setting, the model receives a single image chip and a single text prompt. For geospatial-temporal sensemaking, however, the model must compare two or more Sentinel-2 observations of the same site and generate an answer that reflects both visual evidence and temporal context. To support this, each Sentinel2 chip is encoded independently by the shared CLIP vision encoder and LLaVA-NeXT projector. The resulting visualtoken sequences are inserted into the language-model context as separate image segments.

每个图像片段都与明确的文本标记或元数据配对，因此语言模型可以区分观察结果。例如，提示可以将输入标识为“图像 1”和“图像 2”，包括采集日期，或指定图像是否按时间顺序排列。这些标记很重要，因为解码器本质上并不知道两个图像令牌块对应于不同的时间，除非该关系在提示中明确编码或在训练期间学习。因此，在当前的实现中，时间推理是通过有序图像放置来引发的，Each image segment is paired with explicit text markers or metadata so the language model can distinguish observations. For example, prompts may identify inputs as “Image 1” and “Image 2,” include acquisition dates, or specify whether the images are in chronological order. These markers are important because the decoder does not inherently know that two image-token blocks correspond to different times unless that relationship is explicitly encoded in the prompt or learned during training. In the current implementation, temporal reasoning is therefore induced through ordered image placement,

日期感知提示和来自 SMART-HC 阶段标签的监督答案，而不是通过单独的时间转换器或视频编码器。date-aware prompts, and supervised answers derived from SMART-HC phase labels, rather than through a separate temporal transformer or video encoder.

这种设计与稀疏卫星观测非常匹配。 SMART-HC 图像序列不是密集视频；它们是站点状态的不规则间隔的快照。因此，当前的训练实现强调将两个图像比较作为第一个实用的时间学习任务。稍后可以通过添加额外的过时图像片段、选择关键观察结果或从站点级内存中检索先前的示例来表示更长的站点历史。This design is well matched to sparse satellite observations. SMART-HC image sequences are not dense videos; they are irregularly spaced snapshots of the site state. The current training implementation, therefore, emphasizes two-image comparison as the first practical temporal learning task. Longer site histories may later be represented by adding additional dated image segments, selecting key observations, or retrieving prior examples from site-level memory.

4.3 VQA任务格式4.3 VQA Task Formatting

训练示例被格式化为遵循指令的视觉问答 (VQA) 样本，并具有从 SMART-HC 元数据派生的确定性答案。在单图像设置中，每个示例都包含一个 Sentinel-2 施工现场芯片、一个自然语言问题以及施工类型、时间阶段、位置或其他已知现场属性的规范答案。例如，提示可能会问“显示什么类型的建筑工地？”或“此图像中可以看到哪个施工阶段？”答案映射到固定标签集，例如商业、工业、场地准备、施工中、施工后或未知。Training examples are formatted as instruction-following visual question answering (VQA) samples with deterministic answers derived from SMART-HC metadata. In the single-image setting, each example contains one Sentinel-2 construction-site chip, a natural-language question, and a canonical answer for construction type, temporal phase, location, or another known site attribute. For example, a prompt may ask, “What type of construction site is shown?” or “What phase of construction is visible in this image?” with answers mapped to fixed label sets such as Commercial, Industrial, Site Preparation, Active Construction, Post Construction, or Unknown.

在多图像设置中，每个示例都包含来自同一建筑工地的两个带日期的观察结果。该问题要求模型比较图像，识别时间顺序，确定是否发生有意义的变化，或对观察之间的相变进行分类。代表性答案标签包括无明显相变、无活动到现场准备、现场准备到主动施工以及主动施工到施工后。在这个阶段，这种受控的 VQA 格式比不受限制的叙述性字幕更受青睐，因为生成的响应可以标准化为已知标签并可重复评分。问题释义可用于增加语言多样性，但目标答案保持固定以保持精确评估。In the multi-image setting, each example contains two dated observations from the same construction site. The question asks the model to compare the images, identify temporal order, determine whether meaningful change occurred, or classify the phase transition between observations. Representative answer labels include no apparent phase change, No Activity to Site Preparation, Site Preparation to Active Construction, and Active Construction to Post Construction. This controlled VQA format is intentionally preferred over unrestricted narrative captioning at this stage because generated responses can be normalized to known labels and scored reproducibly. Question paraphrasing may be used to increase linguistic diversity, but the target answers remain fixed to preserve precise evaluation.

4.4 参数高效微调4.4 Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning

微调完整的多模态大语言模型的计算成本很高，因为训练必须存储模型参数、梯度、优化器状态和中间激活。在 SMART-HC-VQA 设置中，此成本会增加，因为每个附加图像都会向语言模型上下文添加视觉标记。因此，多图像示例需要仔细控制上下文长度和图形处理单元 (GPU) 内存使用情况。因此，当前的训练实施优先考虑单图像和两图像示例，然后再扩展到更长的站点历史记录。Fine-tuning a full multimodal large language model is computationally expensive because training must store model parameters, gradients, optimizer states, and intermediate activations. This cost increases in the SMART-HC-VQA setting because each additional image adds visual tokens to the language model context. As a result, multi-image examples require careful control of context length and graphics processing unit (GPU) memory usage. The current training implementation, therefore, prioritizes single-image and two-image examples before extending to longer site histories.

我们遵循之前的 LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B 自动目标识别工作中使用的参数高效微调方法。预训练的语言模型和视觉编码器在很大程度上仍处于冻结状态，而少量可训练的适配器参数是通过量化低阶适应（QLoRA）引入的。在基线配置中，模型权重采用 4 位量化加载，计算使用 16 位大脑浮点精度，并将低秩适应 (LoRA) 模块插入到选定的语言模型注意层中。这使得模型能够适应 SMART-HC 术语、Sentinel-2 视觉外观、构造类型标签、时间阶段标签和相变模式，而无需重新训练完整的基础模型。We follow the parameter-efficient fine-tuning approach used in our prior LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B automatic target recognition work. The pretrained language model and vision encoder remain largely frozen, while a small number of trainable adapter parameters are introduced through Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation (QLoRA). In the baseline configuration, model weights are loaded with 4-bit quantization, computation uses 16-bit brain floating point precision, and Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) modules are inserted into selected language-model attention layers. This allows the model to adapt to SMART-HC terminology, Sentinel-2 visual appearance, construction-type labels, temporal phase labels, and phase-transition patterns without retraining the full foundation model.

之前的 SAR ATR 实现使用 LoRA 等级 8、LoRA 缩放 16、5% 的退出率以及融合 AdamW 优化作为实际的起始设置。这些值保留作为 SMART-HC-VQA 的初始超参数，但并不假定它们是最佳的。未来的训练实验应该评估适配器放置、LoRA 等级、按图像计数或芯片大小进行批处理以及视觉标记减少方法，以提高稳定性并减少多图像微调期间的内存峰值。The prior SAR ATR implementation used a LoRA rank of 8, LoRA scaling of 16, a 5% dropout rate, and fused AdamW optimization as practical starting settings. These values are retained as initial hyperparameters for SMART-HC-VQA, but they are not assumed to be optimal. Future training experiments should evaluate adapter placement, LoRA rank, batching by image count or chip size, and visual-token reduction methods to improve stability and reduce memory spikes during multi-image fine-tuning.

4.5 培训与考核计划4.5 Training and Evaluation Plan

训练被组织为从更简单的单图像任务到更困难的时间比较任务的分阶段进展。在第一阶段，模型在单图像 SMART-HC-VQA 示例上进行训练，以了解 Sentinel-2 建筑工地的外观和元数据衍生标签，包括建筑类型、时间阶段、位置和其他工地属性。在第二阶段，模型在来自同一施工现场的双图像示例上进行训练，其中必须比较过时的观察结果，确定时间顺序，确定是否发生了有意义的变化，并对相变进行分类，例如从“无活动”到“场地准备”或“场地准备”到“活动施工”。具有较长观察历史的站点稍后可能支持短有序序列示例，但成对比较是当前框架中的主要时间训练任务。Training is organized as a staged progression from simpler single-image tasks to more difficult temporal comparison tasks. In the first stage, the model is trained on single-image SMART-HC-VQA examples to learn the appearance of Sentinel-2 construction sites and metadata-derived labels, including construction type, temporal phase, location, and other site attributes. In the second stage, the model is trained on two-image examples from the same construction site, where it must compare dated observations, determine temporal order, identify whether meaningful change occurred, and classify phase transitions, such as No Activity to Site Preparation or Site Preparation to Active Construction. Sites with longer observation histories may later support short ordered-sequence examples, but pairwise comparison is the primary temporal training task in the current framework.

评估将类似地按任务类型进行组织。将生成的文本映射到规范标签后，可以将单图像构建类型和时间阶段问题评估为分类分类。可以使用时间顺序准确性和转换分类准确性来评估成对示例，并将响应归一化为标签，例如无明显变化、无活动到场地准备、场地准备到活动施工、活动施工到施工后或未知过渡。由于本文提出的是早期框架而不是最终的表现结果，因此评估计划强调在开放式叙事意义构建之前受控的答案格式和可重复的标签匹配。这种保守的策略使 SMART-HC-VQA 首先充当自动目标识别和 VQA 基准，然后支持更丰富的活动摘要、定量站点测量、检索增强推理和面向意图的地理空间分析。Evaluation will similarly be organized by task type. Single-image construction-type and temporal-phase questions can be evaluated as categorical classification after mapping generated text to canonical labels. Pairwise examples can be evaluated using temporal-order accuracy and transition-classification accuracy, with responses normalized to labels such as no apparent change, No Activity to Site Preparation, Site Preparation to Active Construction, Active Construction to Post Construction, or Unknown transition. Because this paper presents an early-stage framework rather than final performance results, the evaluation plan emphasizes controlled answer formats and reproducible label matching before open-ended narrative sensemaking. This conservative strategy allows SMART-HC-VQA to first function as an automatic target recognition and VQA benchmark, while later supporting richer activity summaries, quantitative site measurements, retrieval-augmented reasoning, and intent-oriented geospatial analysis.

5. 未来的工作5. FUTURE WORK

未来的工作将从三个方向扩展 SMART-HC-VQA：更丰富的传感、更丰富的监督和更高效的多模态模型架构。首先，通过合并来自 Sentinel-1 [32] 和 Capella Space 和 Umbra 等商业 SAR 提供商的合成孔径雷达 (SAR) 观测数据，数据集可以扩展到 Sentinel-2 光学图像之外。 SAR 图像提供全天候、白天/夜间传感，可帮助填补因云层覆盖、季节性影响或稀疏光学收集造成的时间间隙。将 Sentinel-2 光电图像与 SAR 观测相结合，将使任务从单传感器时间视觉问答 (VQA) 转向多传感器地理空间感知，其中模型必须对互补的空间、光谱、时间和雷达散射证据进行推理。其他地理空间图层，包括土地利用/土地覆盖 (LULC) 产品、交通网络、分区信息、植被指数和基础设施地图，也可以为场地功能和周围活动提供弱上下文标签。Future work will extend SMART-HC-VQA in three directions: richer sensing, richer supervision, and more efficient multimodal model architectures. First, the dataset can be expanded beyond Sentinel-2 optical imagery by incorporating synthetic aperture radar (SAR) observations from Sentinel-1 [32] and commercial SAR providers such as Capella Space and Umbra. SAR imagery provides all-weather, day/night sensing that may help fill temporal gaps caused by cloud cover, seasonal effects, or sparse optical collection. Combining Sentinel-2 electro-optical imagery with SAR observations would move the task from single-sensor temporal visual question answering (VQA) toward multi-sensor geospatial sensemaking, where a model must reason over complementary spatial, spectral, temporal, and radar-scattering evidence. Additional geospatial layers, including Land Use/Land Cover (LULC) products, transportation networks, zoning information, vegetation indices, and infrastructure maps, could also provide weak contextual labels for site function and surrounding activity.

其次，未来的工作应该将预测目标从分类标签扩展到定量测量和更高层次的活动描述。当前的 SMART-HC-VQA 公式侧重于构造类型、时间阶段、位置和相变。然而，SMART-HC 场地多边形还支持定量问题，包括施工场地面积、面积随时间的变化、阶段之间的经过时间和表观扩张率。这个方向将把 SAR-RAG [28] 中探索的回归式 VQA 方法从车辆测量扩展到固定基础设施目标。更广泛地说，建筑工地应被视为不断变化的地理空间目标，具有可观察的属性，包括大小、形状、周围土地利用、完工程度和时间状态。长期目标不仅是将站点归类为“主动建设”，还要推断正在发生的活动、进展情况、可以解释的背景因素以及未来可能的状态。Second, future work should expand the prediction targets from categorical labels to quantitative measurements and higherlevel activity descriptions. The current SMART-HC-VQA formulation focuses on construction type, temporal phase, location, and phase transitions. However, SMART-HC site polygons also support quantitative questions, including construction-site area, area change over time, elapsed time between phases, and apparent expansion rate. This direction would extend the regression-style VQA approach explored in SAR-RAG [28] from vehicle measurements to fixed infrastructure targets. More broadly, construction sites should be treated as evolving geospatial targets, with observable attributes including size, shape, surrounding land use, degree of completion, and temporal state. The long-term objective is not only to classify a site as Active Construction, but to infer what activity is occurring, how it is progressing, what contextual factors may explain it, and what future state is likely.

第三，未来的实验应该评估更新的多模态基础模型作为 LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B 基线的替代品，特别是在保持计算实用性的同时改进图像推理的模型。 Gemma 3 [33] 是相关的，因为它包括轻量级多模态变体，支持长上下文，并引入了内存高效的架构更改，这可能有利于多图像遥感 VQA。 LLaMA 3.2 [34] 也很重要，因为它的 11B 视觉模型支持图像推理、字幕和视觉基础，同时比更大的前沿规模系统更实用；其较小的纯文本变体还可以支持元数据推理、问题生成或检索增强管道。 Qwen3 [35] 提供了强大的小型密集语言模型，当与视觉编码器和投影仪配合使用时，可以充当高效的语言侧主干。最近的 DeepSeek 条件记忆和 V4 版本 [36, 37] 提出了另一个有希望的方向：将外部记忆或查找式上下文与动态推理分开。这种记忆增强方法可以存储站点历史、先前的观察结果、地理背景和已知的施工示例，而无需将所有信息强加到模型权重中。Third, future experiments should evaluate newer multimodal foundation models as alternatives to the LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B baseline, especially models that improve image reasoning while remaining computationally practical. Gemma 3 [33] is relevant because it includes lightweight multimodal variants, supports long context, and introduces memoryefficient architectural changes that may benefit multi-image remote-sensing VQA. LLaMA 3.2 [34] is also relevant because its 11B vision model supports image reasoning, captioning, and visual grounding while remaining more practical than larger frontier-scale systems; its smaller text-only variants may also support metadata reasoning, question generation, or retrieval-augmented pipelines. Qwen3 [35] provides strong small dense language models that could serve as efficient language-side backbones when paired with a vision encoder and projector. Recent DeepSeek conditional-memory and V4 releases [36, 37] suggest another promising direction: separating external memory or lookup-style context from dynamic reasoning. Such memory-augmented approaches could store site histories, prior observations, geographic context, and known construction examples without forcing all information into model weights.

因此，最有希望的近期路径是将高效的多模式主干与更好的时态数据组织相结合，而不是简单地扩展到更大的模型。未来的研究应该使用相同的单图像和图像成对 VQA 分割，将 LLaVA-NeXT Mistral7B 与更小或更新的多模态替代方案进行比较。长期工作应研究检索增强站点记忆、Sentinel-1/Sentinel-2 SAR/EO 融合、定量站点回归和短序图像序列。这些扩展支持更广泛的目标，即从施工阶段识别转向地理空间目标的时间推理和意图识别，包括更复杂的应用，例如基础设施扩展、海上活动监控以及其含义共同取决于时间、位置、活动状态和环境条件的其他场景。The most promising near-term path is therefore to combine efficient multimodal backbones with better temporal data organization, rather than simply scaling to a much larger model. Future studies should compare LLaVA-NeXT Mistral7B against smaller or newer multimodal alternatives using identical single-image and image-pairwise VQA splits. Longerterm work should investigate retrieval-augmented site memory, Sentinel-1/Sentinel-2 SAR/EO fusion, quantitative site regression, and short ordered image sequences. These extensions support the broader objective of moving from construction-phase recognition toward temporal reasoning and intent recognition over geospatial targets, including more complex applications such as infrastructure expansion, maritime activity monitoring, and other scenarios whose meaning depends jointly on time, location, activity state, and environmental conditions.

6. 结论6. CONCLUSIONS

本文介绍了使用多模态大语言模型对遥感活动观测进行地理空间-时间意义构建的正在进行的工作。我们没有将 IARPA SMART Heavy Construction 数据集仅视为大范围搜索或阶段分类的基准，而是将其重新解释为视觉问答的语言监督的结构化源。在这个公式中，建筑工地被视为固定的地理空间目标，其身份、属性和活动状态随着稀疏的卫星观测而变化。这将重型施工监控重新定义为一个临时扩展的自动目标识别和 VQA 问题，其目标不仅是对图像进行分类，而且是描述现场发生的情况、活动的进展情况以及观察到的发展可能意味着什么。This paper presents ongoing work toward geospatial-temporal sensemaking of remote-sensing activity observations using multimodal large language models. Rather than treating the IARPA SMART Heavy Construction dataset only as a benchmark for broad-area search or phase classification, we reinterpret it as a structured source of language supervision for visual question answering. In this formulation, a construction site is treated as a fixed geospatial target whose identity, attributes, and activity state evolve across sparse satellite observations. This reframes heavy-construction monitoring as a temporally extended automatic target recognition and VQA problem, where the objective is not only to classify an image, but to describe what is happening at a site, how the activity is progressing, and what the observed development may imply.

这项工作的一个核心贡献是从 SMART 重型建筑注释和 Sentinel-2 图像中推导出 SMART-HC-VQA。我们将构造类型标签、时间阶段标签、位置元数据、站点观察和图像关系转换为适合 MLLM 训练的自然语言问答示例。当前导出的数据集包括 21,837 个可访问的 Sentinel-2 施工现场图像芯片、65,511 个单图像 VQA 示例，以及通过图像配对组合增强创建的约 230 万个两图像时间比较示例。这种扩展增加了有监督的时间比较示例的数量，而不需要新的模拟、合成图像或不支持的标签。A central contribution of this work is the derivation of SMART-HC-VQA from SMART Heavy Construction annotations and Sentinel-2 imagery. We convert construction-type labels, temporal phase labels, location metadata, site observations, and image relationships into natural-language question-answer examples suitable for MLLM training. The current derived dataset includes 21,837 accessible Sentinel-2 construction-site image chips, 65,511 single-image VQA examples, and approximately 2.3 million two-image temporal comparison examples created through Image-Pairwise Combinatorial Augmentation. This expansion increases the number of supervised temporal comparison examples without requiring new simulations, synthetic imagery, or unsupported labels.

这项工作还强调了使公共地理空间时间注释可用于 MLLM 研究所需的实际工程。我们描述了一个工作流程，用于解析异构 Sentinel-2 源标识符、从公共 STAC 目录中检索图像、将大型卫星切片分割成以站点为中心的图像、保留周围的空间上下文以及维护原始 SMART-HC 记录和生成的图像芯片之间的可追溯性。由此产生的数据集分析表明，SMART-HC-VQA 很有价值，但本质上是不平衡的：许多站点很小，许多只包含少量观察，时间覆盖稀疏且不规则，并且构建类型和阶段标签不平衡。这些属性反映了基于卫星的活动理解的真正挑战，并激发了数据增强和多图像建模。This work also highlights the practical engineering required to make public geospatial-temporal annotations usable for MLLM research. We describe a workflow for resolving heterogeneous Sentinel-2 source identifiers, retrieving imagery from public STAC catalogs, chipping large satellite tiles into site-centered images, preserving surrounding spatial context, and maintaining traceability between original SMART-HC records and generated image chips. The resulting dataset analysis shows that SMART-HC-VQA is valuable but naturally uneven: many sites are small, many contain only a few observations, temporal coverage is sparse and irregular, and construction-type and phase labels are imbalanced. These properties reflect the real challenges of satellite-based activity understanding and motivate both data augmentation and multi-image modeling.

最后，我们描述了一个基于 LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B 实现的多图像 MLLM 训练框架。该框架扩展了先前的 SAR ATR 管道，允许将多个过时的 Sentinel-2 图像芯片作为单独的视觉输入插入到语言模型上下文中，并使用提示标记和元数据来区分观察结果。当前系统旨在训练单图像和两图像 SMART-HC-VQA 示例，支持有关构造类型、时间相位、时间顺序和相变的问题。尽管本文没有提供最终的模型评估结果，但它定义了数据集构建的可重复工作流程、受控 VQA 任务制定、增强策略和实际训练方向。这些贡献共同为语言引导的地理空间-时间意义构建奠定了基础，未来将扩展到 SAR/EO 融合、检索增强站点记忆、定量站点测量以及对地球表面人类活动的更广泛时间推理。Finally, we describe an implemented multi-image MLLM training framework based on LLaVA-NeXT Mistral-7B. The framework extends a prior SAR ATR pipeline by allowing multiple dated Sentinel-2 image chips to be inserted into the language-model context as separate visual inputs, with prompt markers and metadata used to distinguish observations. The current system is designed to train on single-image and two-image SMART-HC-VQA examples, supporting questions about construction type, temporal phase, temporal order, and phase transition. Although this paper does not present final model evaluation results, it defines a reproducible workflow for dataset construction, a controlled VQA task formulation, an augmentation strategy, and a practical training direction. Together, these contributions establish a foundation for language-guided geospatial-temporal sensemaking, with future extensions toward SAR/EO fusion, retrieval-augmented site memory, quantitative site measurement, and broader temporal reasoning over human activity on the Earth’s surface.
