5 讨论Discussion

旋转作为表现维度。Rotation as an expressive dimension.

我们的结果表明旋转流形是一个结构化的表达通道而不仅仅是一个位置支架。 就像代数中的虚轴一样，可学习的旋转维度提供了一个正交空间来编码 单独的嵌入空间不可见的关系。桌子Our results suggest the rotation manifold is a structured expressive channel rather than a mere positional scaffold. Much like the imaginary axis in algebra, a learnable rotation dimension provides an orthogonal space to encode relationships invisible to the embedding space alone. Table3证实了这一点：在注入时间时 将特征引入嵌入是无效或有害的，通过旋转流形路由它们会产生一致的增益。 这将旋转识别为一种本质上不同的表示模式，表明了一个统一的代数结构，其中 语义（嵌入）和动态（旋转）维度提供非冗余信息。confirms this: while injecting temporal features into embeddings is ineffective or detrimental, routing them through the rotation manifold yields consistent gains. This identifies rotation as a qualitatively distinct representation mode, suggesting a unified algebraic structure where semantic (embedding) and dynamic (rotation) dimensions provide non-redundant information.

6.1 更广泛的影响声明Broader Impact Statement

所提出的 SIREN-RoPE 框架引入了一种更具表现力的编码方法 Transformer 架构中的时间和语义信号。虽然主要是 表征学习及其应用的根本改进——特别是 在大规模推荐和排名系统中——具有重要的社交意义 影响。The proposed SIREN-RoPE framework introduces a more expressive method for encoding temporal and semantic signals in Transformer architectures. While primarily a fundamental improvement in representation learning, its applications—particularly in large-scale recommendation and ranking systems—carry significant social implications.

积极影响。Positive impacts.

通过使模型能够更准确地捕获复杂的周期性和用户行为 模式，SIREN-RoPE 可以带来更相关、更高效的内容发现。 统一的框架可以减少对特定功能的手动操作的需求 工程，有可能降低开发复杂序列的障碍 模型。By enabling models to more accurately capture complex periodicities and user behavior patterns, SIREN-RoPE can lead to more relevant and efficient content discovery. The unified framework may reduce the need for manual, feature-specific engineering, potentially lowering the barrier to developing sophisticated sequential models.

潜在的负面影响。Potential negative impacts.

与排名和推荐方面的任何进步一样，模型表达能力也得到了提高 可能会无意中强化过滤气泡或算法偏差，如果底层 语义信号包含历史偏见。As with any advancement in ranking and recommendation, improved model expressivity could inadvertently reinforce filter bubbles or algorithmic biases if the underlying semantic signals contain historical prejudices.

6.2 局限性Limitations

外推约束。Extrapolation constraints.

SIREN-RoPE 的“内容感知”性质可能受到分布的限制 训练期间看到的时间和语义特征。概括为 分布外的时间间隔或全新的语义类别仍然是一个 需要进一步实证研究的开放问题。The “content-aware” nature of SIREN-RoPE may be constrained by the distribution of temporal and semantic features seen during training. Generalization to out-of-distribution time intervals or entirely new semantic categories remains an open question requiring further empirical study.
