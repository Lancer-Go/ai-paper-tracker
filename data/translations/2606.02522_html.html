瞬间视频：诊断视频 MLLM 对瞬间视觉事件的时间保真度Moment-Video: Diagnosing Temporal Fidelity of Video MLLMs on Momentary Visual Events

摘要Abstract

视频多模态大语言模型（MLLM）在一般和长格式视频理解方面取得了快速进展，但它们保存简短答案关键视觉证据的能力仍未得到充分探索。许多实际问题是由瞬时视觉事件决定的：可能仅持续几帧的局部动作或状态转换。这些证据可以通过稀疏帧采样来跳过，通过视觉标记压缩来抑制，或者通过粗时间聚合来稀释，从而导致语言端推理无法可靠地恢复的故障。我们介绍Video multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have made rapid progress on general and long-form video understanding, yet their ability to preserve brief answer-critical visual evidence remains underexplored. Many practical questions are determined by momentary visual events: localized actions or state transitions that may last only a few frames. Such evidence can be skipped by sparse frame sampling, suppressed by visual-token compression, or diluted by coarse temporal aggregation, causing failures that language-side reasoning cannot reliably recover. We introduce瞬间视频Moment-Video，通过瞬时视觉事件理解诊断视频 MLLM 时间保真度的基准。每个问题都基于局部的、视觉可观察的和采样敏感的事件，要求模型注意、计数、描述或推理瞬时证据，而不是依赖于持久对象、全局场景上下文或语言先验。, a benchmark for diagnosing the temporal fidelity of video MLLMs through momentary visual event understanding. Each question is grounded in a localized, visually observable, and sampling-sensitive event, requiring models to notice, count, describe, or reason about transient evidence rather than rely on persistent objects, global scene context, or language priors.瞬间视频Moment-Video包含 7 个领域和 25 个细粒度子类别的 1,000 个经过人工验证的视频 QA 对，涵盖四种任务类型：时间发生、时间计数、动作描述和时间推理。 我们评估了 33 个专有和开源 MLLMcontains 1,000 human-verified video-QA pairs across 7 domains and 25 fine-grained subcategories, covering four task types: Temporal Occurrence, Temporal Counting, Action Description, and Temporal Reasoning. We evaluate 33 proprietary and open-source MLLMs on瞬间视频Moment-Video。性能最好的模型 Seed-2.0-Pro 的总体准确率仅为 39.6%，而大多数开源模型仍低于 25%，这表明瞬时视觉事件理解方面存在巨大差距。诊断分析表明，更密集的帧采样改进了一些模型，但并没有消除瓶颈，而且更长的视频带来了更强的时间定位挑战。这些发现表明，当前的视频 MLLM 仍然缺乏捕捉、保存和使用简短但决定性的视觉证据的时间忠实表示。. The best-performing model, Seed-2.0-Pro, achieves only 39.6% overall accuracy, while most open-source models remain below 25%, revealing a substantial gap in momentary visual event understanding. Diagnostic analyses show that denser frame sampling improves some models but does not eliminate the bottleneck, and longer videos introduce stronger temporal-localization challenges. These findings suggest that current video MLLMs still lack temporally faithful representations for capturing, preserving, and using brief but decisive visual evidence.

1 简介Introduction

视频多模态大语言模型（MLLM）最近在动态视觉理解方面取得了实质性进展。 通过帧采样、时间位置编码、视觉标记压缩和多模态指令调整将图像语言模型扩展到视频，最新的系统在视频问答、动作识别、时间推理和长视频理解方面取得了强大的性能Video multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have recently made substantial progress in dynamic visual understanding. By extending image-language models to videos through frame sampling, temporal positional encoding, visual-token compression, and multimodal instruction tuning, recent systems achieve strong performance in video question answering, action recognition, temporal reasoning, and long-video comprehension[1, 4, 42, 11]。 最近的全模态和面向推理的模型通过视听输入、思维链推理、强化学习和工具增强推理进一步增强了视频理解. More recent omni-modal and reasoning-oriented models further enhance video understanding with audio-visual inputs, chain-of-thought reasoning, reinforcement learning, and tool-augmented inference[6, 2, 5, 12, 13, 14]。 尽管取得了这些进步，但短暂但决定性的视觉事件通常仍未被观察到，揭示了模型中的差距. Despite these advances, brief but decisive visual events often remain unobserved, revealing a gap in model时间保真度temporal fidelity.

许多现实世界的视频问题取决于Many real-world video questions depend on瞬时视觉事件momentary visual events：在短时间内（有时只有几帧）可视觉观察到的动作或状态转换。 示例包括行人短暂踏入道路: visually observable actions or state transitions over a short duration, sometimes only a few frames. Examples include a pedestrian briefly stepping into the road[33]，表现出瞬时异常运动的机器, a machine exhibiting momentary abnormal motion[34]、命令后闪烁的 GUI 元素或游戏角色执行决定结果的短攻击。 尽管这些事件只占视频的一小部分，但它们往往对答案具有决定性作用。 如果关键帧在稀疏采样期间被跳过、被视觉标记压缩削弱或被粗时间聚合稀释，则仅靠语言端推理无法可靠地恢复丢失的证据。 这一限制凸显了模型保存的必要性, a GUI element flashing after a command, or a game character performing a short attack that determines the outcome. Although these events occupy only a brief portion of the video, they are often decisive for the answer. If the critical frames are skipped during sparse sampling, weakened by visual-token compression, or diluted by coarse temporal aggregation, language-side reasoning alone cannot reliably recover the missing evidence. This limitation highlights the need for models to preserve时间保真度temporal fidelity：捕捉、保留和推理短暂的、关键答案的视觉证据的能力。: the ability to capture, retain, and reason over short-lived, answer-critical visual evidence.

现有的视频基准测试很少隔离此功能。 通用、长视频和推理导向的基准测试Existing video benchmarks rarely isolate this capability. General-purpose, long-video, and reasoning-oriented benchmarks[15, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 30, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28]评估场景理解、时间聚合或持久对象。 以运动为中心的基准测试assess scene understanding, temporal aggregation, or persistent objects. Motion-centric benchmarks[18, 19]评估运动感知或描述，但他们的问题通常不集中于简短的、采样敏感的事件。 因此，目前尚不清楚当前的 MLLM 是否能够可靠地检测、计数、描述和推理瞬态、瞬间的视觉事件。evaluate motion perception or description, but their questions generally do not focus on brief, sampling-sensitive events. Thus, it remains unclear whether current MLLMs can reliably detect, count, describe, and reason about transient, momentary visual events.

为了解决这个差距，我们引入To address this gap, we introduce瞬间视频Moment-Video，通过瞬时视觉事件理解诊断时间保真度的基准。 它包含 7 个领域和 25 个细粒度子类别的 1,000 个经过人工验证的视频 QA 对，涵盖现实世界和虚拟场景，包括 AI 生成的内容、GUI 录制、自然场景、工业环境、游戏、人类活动和动物。 每个问题都基于局部瞬时事件，确保模型依赖于事件证据而不是全局背景或外部知识。 我们注意到一小部分样本涉及瞬态文本或意图驱动的线索（例如 GUI 界面或动物意图），只有在视觉上可观察到且对答案至关重要的情况下才会包含这些线索。, a benchmark for diagnosing temporal fidelity via momentary visual event understanding. It contains 1,000 human-verified video-QA pairs across 7 domains and 25 fine-grained subcategories, covering real-world and virtual scenarios including AI-generated content, GUI recordings, natural scenes, industrial environments, games, human activities, and animals. Each question is grounded in a localized momentary event, ensuring models rely on event evidence rather than global context or external knowledge. We note that a small subset of samples involve transient textual or intention-driven cues (e.g., GUI interfaces or animal intent), which are included only if they are visually observable and answer-critical.

问题在Questions in瞬间视频Moment-Video被组织成四种互补的任务类型。are organized into four complementary task types.时间发生 (TO)Temporal Occurrence (TO)评估模型是否可以注意到发生了短暂事件或状态转换。evaluates whether a model can notice that a brief event or state transition happens.时间计数 (TC)Temporal Counting (TC)评估模型是否可以跟踪和计算瞬态操作、对象更改或重复事件发生。evaluates whether a model can track and count transient actions, object changes, or repeated event occurrences.动作描述（AD）Action Description (AD)评估模型是否可以表征瞬时事件的动态，例如其方向、轨迹、目标、交互或状态变化。evaluates whether a model can characterize the dynamics of a momentary event, such as its direction, trajectory, target, interaction, or state change.时间推理 (TR)Temporal Reasoning (TR)评估模型是否可以整合事件前状态、瞬时事件和事件后状态来推断事件的后果。这些任务类型共同形成了对时间保真度的渐进评估：从捕获简短事件的存在，到保留其动态细节，再到将其用于状态变化推理。evaluates whether a model can integrate the pre-event state, the momentary event, and the post-event state to infer the event’s consequence. Together, these task types form a progressive evaluation of temporal fidelity: from capturing the existence of a brief event, to preserving its dynamic details, to using it for state-change reasoning.

Task-wise, TO and AD are easier than TC and TR, highlighting challenges in counting and reasoning over transient events. 视频 MLLM 的进步。Advancements in Video MLLMs.最近的视频 MLLM 已从简单的基于帧的图像语言模型扩展发展为具有时间编码、动态视觉处理、视觉令牌压缩和大规模多模式指令调整的功能更强大的系统Recent video MLLMs have evolved from simple frame-based extensions of image-language models to more capable systems with temporal encoding, dynamic visual processing, visual-token compression, and large-scale multimodal instruction tuning[1, 4, 42, 11]。这些模型通常将采样帧或视频剪辑转换为按时间顺序排列的视觉标记，并将它们与 LLM 对齐，以实现视频问答、动作识别、时间推理和长视频理解。同时，全模态和推理导向的模型通过视听输入、思维式推理、强化学习和工具增强推理进一步扩展视频理解. While these advances improve broad video comprehension, most systems are primarily evaluated on persistent visual content, coarse temporal structure, or long-context reasoning. Their ability to retain and reason over brief localized visual evidence remains insufficiently studied.

The final benchmark contains 1,000 videos paired with 1,000 human-verified question-answer pairs, spanning 7 high-level domains and 25 fine-grained subcategories. 3.1 是什么让事件变得短暂？What Makes an Event Momentary?

我们定义一个We define a瞬间视觉事件momentary visual event作为视觉上可观察到的动作或状态转换，其决定性证据位于较短的时间窗口内。 这样的事件不仅仅是一个快速的运动模式；这是视频中对答案至关重要的更改。 模型必须在正确的时刻感知到这种变化，才能正确回答相关问题。 为了在数据集构建期间实施此定义，我们施加以下标准：as a visually observable action or state transition whose decisive evidence is localized within a short temporal window. Such an event is not merely a fast motion pattern; it is an answer-critical change in the video. A model must perceive this change at the right moment in order to answer the associated question correctly. To operationalize this definition during dataset construction, we impose the following criteria:

1.本地化证据。1. Localized evidence.回答问题的决定性证据必须集中在视频中的特定时刻，而不是分布在整个剪辑中。例如，答案可能取决于球改变方向、物体掉落、GUI 元素出现或动物突然移动的瞬间。The decisive evidence for answering the question must be concentrated around a specific moment in the video, rather than being distributed across the entire clip. For example, the answer may depend on the instant when a ball changes direction, an object falls, a GUI element appears, or an animal suddenly moves.

2.短暂的视觉变化。2. Transient visual change.目标事件必须涉及临时动作、交互或状态转换。可以从持久对象属性、静态场景布局或视频整体主题回答的问题被排除在外。此要求确保基准评估动态视觉证据而不是静态图像理解。The target event must involve a temporary action, interaction, or state transition. Questions that can be answered from a persistent object attribute, a static scene layout, or the overall topic of the video are excluded. This requirement ensures that the benchmark evaluates dynamic visual evidence rather than static image understanding.

3、采样灵敏度。3. Sampling sensitivity.在稀疏帧采样下，该事件应该有很大的机会被错过。该标准反映了视频 MLLM 的一个常见限制：当仅采样几个帧或视觉标记被严重压缩时，短暂的证据在到达语言模型之前可能会被跳过、削弱或稀释。The event should have a non-trivial chance of being missed under sparse frame sampling. This criterion reflects a common limitation of video MLLMs: when only a few frames are sampled or when visual tokens are heavily compressed, short-lived evidence may be skipped, weakened, or diluted before it reaches the language model.

4.可评估性。4. Evaluability.每个问题都必须有明确的答案。当参考答案在语义上收敛并且可以可靠地判断时，我们使用开放式问题。当答案难以用自由形式的语言标准化时，我们使用多项选择格式，并手动设计干扰项，这些干扰项在视觉上合理且语义上接近正确答案。Each question must have a well-defined answer. We use open-ended questions when the reference answer is semantically convergent and can be judged reliably. When the answer is difficult to standardize in free-form language, we use a multiple-choice format with manually designed distractors that are visually plausible and semantically close to the correct answer.

这些标准共同区分Together, these criteria distinguish瞬间视频Moment-Video来自评估通用运动识别或通用视频级语义的基准。 关键的构造限制不仅仅在于视频包含运动，而且答案取决于简短的、局部的、基于视觉的证据。from benchmarks that evaluate generic motion recognition or general video-level semantics. The key construction constraint is not simply that the video contains motion, but that the answer depends on brief, localized, visually grounded evidence.

3.2 数据集构建Dataset Construction

我们构建We construct瞬间视频Moment-Video通过由领域分类设计、视频收集、以事件为中心的时间基础和人类专家注释组成的多阶段管道。through a multi-stage pipeline consisting of domain taxonomy design, video collection, event-centric temporal grounding and human-expert annotation.

域分类。Domain taxonomy.为了确保广泛覆盖瞬时视觉事件，我们构建了一个包含 7 个高级领域的分层分类法：AIGC、GUI、自然、工业、游戏、人类和动物。这些领域涵盖了不同的视觉风格、运动模式、场景布局和事件类型。每个域都分为细粒度的子类别。例如，游戏领域包括FPS游戏、MOBA游戏、格斗游戏、音乐游戏；人类领域包括球类运动、非球类运动和日常生活活动；动物领域包括鸟类、哺乳动物、爬行动物、两栖动物、鱼类和无脊椎动物。这种分类法使我们能够评估模型是否可以概括自然视频、屏幕录制、合成视频和复杂交互环境中的瞬时事件理解。类别分布如图所示。To ensure broad coverage of momentary visual events, we build a hierarchical taxonomy with 7 high-level domains: AIGC, GUI, Nature, Industry, Games, Human, and Animals. These domains cover diverse visual styles, motion patterns, scene layouts, and event types. Each domain is divided into fine-grained subcategories. For example, the Games domain includes FPS games, MOBA games, combat games, and music games; the Human domain includes ball sports, non-ball sports, and daily-life activities; and the Animals domain includes birds, mammals, reptiles, amphibians, fish, and invertebrates. This taxonomy allows us to evaluate whether models can generalize momentary event understanding across natural videos, screen recordings, synthetic videos, and complex interactive environments. 视频合集。Video collection.我们从公共在线资源获取候选人视频We source candidate videos from public online sources[45, 46, 47, 48, 10, 9, 7, 8, 3]以及自己收集的录音，每份录音都需要至少包含一个视觉上清晰的瞬间事件。我们过滤掉低质量的视频（例如，严重模糊、低分辨率、严重遮挡）以及依赖音频、字幕或外部上下文进行理解的视频。 此外，我们删除了可以从持久的文本提示或元数据中轻松推断出答案的样本。必要时，不相关的视觉文本会被裁剪或屏蔽，以减轻基于 OCR 的快捷方式。 最终数据集涵盖自然和合成领域，包括现实世界的镜头、屏幕录制、游戏玩法和人工智能生成的视频。附录中提供了更多详细信息and self-collected recordings, each required to feature at least one visually clear momentary event. We filter out low-quality videos (e.g., severe blur, low resolution, heavy occlusion) and those relying on audio, subtitles, or external context for comprehension. Furthermore, we remove samples where the answer can be trivially inferred from persistent textual cues or metadata. When necessary, irrelevant visual text is cropped or masked to mitigate OCR-based shortcuts. The final dataset encompasses natural and synthetic domains, including real-world footage, screen recordings, gameplay, and AI-generated videos. Further details are provided in Appendix1.

以事件为中心的时间基础。Event-centric temporal grounding.对于每个候选视频，注释者在编写问题之前首先识别目标瞬时事件。这一步至关重要：基准是从关键答案事件到问题构建的，而不是从通用剪辑到广泛的问题。由于许多目标事件都很简短并且缺乏可靠的帧级边界，因此我们使用弱时间基础而不是需要密集的开始和结束时间戳。注释器记录大致的关键时刻、涉及的对象或代理、事件描述、适用的事件计数以及相关的事件前和事件后状态。此信息用于注释和质量控制，但不会在测试期间提供给评估模型。 当视频包含多个简短事件时，注释者会选择一个目标事件并围绕它编写问题。这避免了模型应该关注什么的含糊不清。如果事件无法本地化，如果竞争事件产生不同的答案，或者问题在没有关键时刻的情况下可以回答，则样本将被拒绝或修改。For each candidate video, annotators first identify the target momentary event before writing the question. This step is essential: the benchmark is constructed from answer-critical events to questions, rather than from generic clips to broad questions. Because many target events are brief and lack reliable frame-level boundaries, we use weak temporal grounding instead of requiring dense start and end timestamps. Annotators record the approximate critical moment, involved objects or agents, the event description, the event count when applicable, and relevant pre-event and post-event states. This information is used for annotation and quality control, but is not provided to evaluated models during testing. When a video contains multiple brief events, annotators choose one target event and write the question around it. This avoids ambiguity about what the model should attend to. A sample is rejected or revised if the event cannot be localized, if competing events yield different answers, or if the question is answerable without the key moment.

人类专家注释。Human-expert annotation.根据本地化的目标事件，注释者为每个视频制定问题和参考答案。每个样本都标有域、子类别、任务类型和答案格式。所有样本均手动构建并交叉检查，以确保清晰、可靠的问答对。对于开放式问题，参考答案是简洁的语义描述，而不是详尽的视频标题。对于多项选择题，注释者会在正确选项附近创建视觉上合理的干扰项，避免那些因常识、选项长度或语言先验而可移除的干扰项。我们进一步对基准子集进行人类可解决性研究，以验证问题是可回答的且明确的。附录中提供了详细的注释指南、数据统计、质量控制程序和人工验证结果Based on localized target events, annotators formulate a question and reference answer for each video. Each sample is labeled with a domain, subcategory, task type, and answer format. All samples are manually constructed and cross-checked to ensure clear and robust Q&A pairs. For open-ended questions, the reference answer is a concise semantic description rather than an exhaustive video caption. For multiple-choice questions, annotators create visually plausible distractors close to the correct option, avoiding those removable by common sense, option length, or language priors. We further conduct a human solvability study on a benchmark subset to verify that questions are answerable and unambiguous. Detailed annotation guidelines, data statistics, quality-control procedures, and human validation results are provided in Appendix5.

3.3 任务分类Task Taxonomy

我们将问题分为四种任务类型：时间发生、时间计数、动作描述和时间推理，如图 1 所示。We organizes questions into four task types: Temporal Occurrence, Temporal Counting, Action Description, and Temporal Reasoning, as shown in Fig.3。 这些类型将时间保真度分解为逐渐丰富的功能：注意短暂事件、量化瞬态变化、表征事件动态以及对事件引起的状态变化进行推理。统计数据见附录. These types decompose temporal fidelity into progressively richer capabilities: noticing brief events, quantifying transient changes, characterizing event dynamics, and reasoning over event-induced state changes. Statistics are shown in Appendix3.

时间发生 (TO)。Temporal Occurrence (TO).时间发生评估模型识别瞬时事件或状态转换的能力。此类问题探讨短暂视觉事件是否存在、不存在或立即发生变化。典型示例包括检测坠落物体、突然的动物运动、操作后 GUI 更新或意外的物理异常。该任务测试时间保真度的最基本要求：模型必须注意到关键时刻。Temporal Occurrence assesses a model’s ability to identify momentary events or state transitions. Questions in this category probe the presence, absence, or immediate change of a transient visual event. Typical examples include detecting a falling object, a sudden animal movement, a post-operation GUI update, or an unexpected physical anomaly. This task tests the most basic requirement of temporal fidelity: the model must notice the critical moment.

时间计数（TC）。Temporal Counting (TC).时间计数评估模型对简短视觉序列中瞬态对象、动作或事件发生进行计数的能力。与静态对象计数不同，此任务需要跟踪随时间的动态变化，同时防止遗漏或冗余计数。典型的查询涉及量化关键时间范围内的特定操作、状态变化或运动。值得注意的是，该任务对稀疏采样高度敏感，因为即使遗漏单个简短事件也可能从根本上改变最终预测。Temporal Counting assesses a model’s ability to count transient objects, actions, or event occurrences within a brief visual sequence. Distinct from static object counting, this task necessitates tracking dynamic changes over time while preventing omissions or redundant counts. Typical queries involve quantifying specific actions, state changes, or motions within a critical timeframe. Notably, this task is highly sensitive to sparse sampling, since the omission of even a single brief event can fundamentally alter the final prediction.

动作描述（AD）。Action Description (AD).动作描述评估模型是否可以表征瞬时事件的动态。模型不仅要确定事件发生，还必须描述事件如何展开，包括其方向、轨迹、目标、交互、运动模式或状态转换。例如，一个问题可能会询问物体在撞击后向哪个方向移动，哪个玩家执行动作，两个代理之间发生什么交互，或者短暂操作后界面状态发生什么变化。此任务测试模型在视频编码和视觉令牌压缩后是否保留足够的动态细节。Action Description evaluates whether a model can characterize the dynamics of a momentary event. Rather than only determining that an event occurs, the model must describe how it unfolds, including its direction, trajectory, target, interaction, motion pattern, or state transition. For example, a question may ask which direction an object moves after impact, which player performs an action, what interaction happens between two agents, or what interface state changes after a brief operation. This task tests whether the model preserves sufficient dynamic details after video encoding and visual-token compression.

时间推理（TR）。Temporal Reasoning (TR).时间推理评估模型是否可以整合事件前状态、瞬时事件和事件后状态来推断结果。此类问题需要的不仅仅是事件识别。模型必须比较事件前后的状态，跟踪事件如何改变场景，并推断结果条件。例如，在观察到最初存在的几个对象以及一些快速离开、下落或改变状态之后，模型可能需要推断还有多少对象、场景达到什么状态或者为什么发生后续变化。Temporal Reasoning evaluates whether a model can integrate the pre-event state, momentary event, and post-event state to infer a consequence. Questions in this category require more than event recognition. The model must compare states before and after the event, track how the event changes the scene, and infer the resulting condition. For example, after observing several objects initially present and some quickly leaving, falling, or changing state, the model may need to infer how many remain, what state the scene reaches, or why a subsequent change occurs.

瞬间视频Moment-Video包含多项选择题和开放式问题。 每个问题都会根据其答案格式转换为二进制正确性标签。总体基准分数计算为所有 1,000 个样本的平均准确度。contains both multiple-choice and open-ended questions.
