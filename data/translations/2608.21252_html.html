简介Introduction

回答长文档问题仍然是大型语言模型 (LLM) 的基本挑战。在许多现实世界的应用中，回答问题所需的信息很少包含在单个段落中。金融分析师可能需要比较多份报告的价值，法律研究人员可能需要对许多判断的证据进行分类或汇总，科学家可能需要追踪论文集中的引文、方法或发现。这些任务要求系统不仅要查找相关证据，还要保存实体身份、文档结构以及分散信息之间的关系。Answering questions over long documents remains a fundamental challenge for large language models (LLMs). In many real-world applications, the information required to answer a question is rarely contained within a single passage. Financial analysts may need to compare values across multiple reports, legal researchers may need to classify or aggregate evidence from many judgments, and scientists may need to trace citations, methods, or findings across collections of papers. These tasks require systems not only to locate relevant evidence, but also to preserve entity identity, document structure, and relationships among dispersed pieces of information.

法学硕士的最新进展使得长文档问答变得越来越可行。一个突出的方向是扩展上下文窗口，允许模型直接消耗更长的输入Recent advances in LLMs have made long-document question answering increasingly feasible. One prominent direction is to extend the context window, allowing models to consume longer inputs directly(1; 12)。然而，仅较大的上下文窗口并不能保证可靠的证据利用：模型对相关信息的位置仍然敏感，表现出众所周知的“迷失在中间”现象. However, larger context windows alone do not guarantee reliable evidence utilization: models remain sensitive to the position of relevant information, exhibiting the well-known “lost in the middle” phenomenon(28)。此外，最近的长上下文基准测试表明，即使是前沿模型也仍然难以解决需要跨长文档进行证据聚合和多跳推理而不是本地化信息查找的问题. Moreover, recent long-context benchmarks demonstrate that even frontier models continue to struggle with questions requiring evidence aggregation and multi-hop reasoning across long documents rather than localized information lookup(36; 4; 3)。另一个方向是检索增强生成（RAG），在生成之前检索外部证据. Another direction is retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), which retrieves external evidence before generation(25; 15)。虽然 RAG 大大减少了 LLM 处理的文本量，但它却将负担转移给了检索者，因为答案质量最终受到其检索到的证据的限制。. While RAG substantially reduces the amount of text processed by the LLM, it shifts the burden to the retriever, since answer quality is ultimately limited by the evidence it retrieves.

大多数现有的 RAG 系统将文档视为固定长度块的集合，并使用词汇或密集相似性来检索它们。虽然简单且可扩展，但固定大小的分块通常与长文档的语义组织不一致。块边界可以将实体与其支持证据分开，将表行与其标题分离，或者将多个不相关的主题合并到同一检索单元中。因此，检索可能会返回与查询局部相似但不足以回答它的段落，同时忽略与问题几乎没有词汇相似性的语义必要证据。Most existing RAG systems treat documents as collections of fixed-length chunks and retrieve them using lexical or dense similarity. Although simple and scalable, fixed-size chunking is often poorly aligned with the semantic organization of long documents. Chunk boundaries may separate entities from their supporting evidence, detach table rows from their headers, or merge multiple unrelated topics into the same retrieval unit. Consequently, retrieval may return passages that are locally similar to the query but insufficient for answering it, while overlooking semantically essential evidence that shares little lexical similarity with the question.

我们的主要观察是检索单元应该按语义定义，而不是机械定义。 EnSI-RAG 不是对任意块建立索引，而是将文档表示为Our key observation is that retrieval units should be defined semantically rather than mechanically. Instead of indexing arbitrary chunks, EnSI-RAG represents documents as以实体为中心的段落entity-centered passages，其中每个段落主要关注一个实体以及描述该实体的本地上下文。这些段落保留了语义连贯的证据，同时减少了语义碎片和污染，为检索提供了更可靠的基础。然而，仅语义连贯的段落是不够的。为了针对任意未来问题有效地检索它们，它们还必须组织成独立于查询的表示，以公开其底层语义结构。, where each passage focuses primarily on one entity together with the local context describing that entity. Such passages preserve semantically coherent evidence while reducing both semantic fragmentation and contamination, providing a more reliable foundation for retrieval. However, semantically coherent passages alone are insufficient. To retrieve them efficiently for arbitrary future questions, they must also be organized into a query-independent representation that exposes their underlying semantic structure.

基于这一观察，我们建议Based on this observation, we propose恩斯拉格EnSI-RAG（实体结构索引检索增强生成），一个独立于查询的长文档问答框架。 EnSI-RAG 首先通过构建以实体为中心的段落并提取描述实体、其语义类型及其关联属性、关系和方面的结构化记录来预处理文档集合。这些记录被组织成一个实体结构索引，充当一组指向原始段落的检索句柄。在查询时，索引引导检索到支持的段落，而法学硕士仍然负责整合检索到的证据并综合最终答案。与在标准化数据库上执行推理的完全结构化方法不同，EnSI-RAG 仅使用结构来改进证据本地化；推理仍以原始文档文本为基础。(Entity-Structure-Indexed Retrieval-Augmented Generation), a query-independent framework for long-document question answering. EnSI-RAG first preprocesses a document collection by constructing entity-centered passages and extracting structured records describing the entities, their semantic types, and their associated properties, relations, and aspects. These records are organized into an entity-structure index that serves as a set of retrieval handles pointing back to the original passages. At query time, the index guides retrieval toward the supporting passages, while the LLM remains responsible for integrating the retrieved evidence and synthesizing the final answer. Unlike fully structured approaches that perform reasoning over normalized databases, EnSI-RAG uses structure only to improve evidence localization; reasoning remains grounded in the original document text.

Loong evaluates heterogeneous multi-document reasoning across financial, legal, and academic domains, while Oolong emphasizes large-scale information aggregation over long inputs. Across these different document structures and reasoning requirements, EnSI-RAG applies a unified retrieval framework while allowing domain-specific passage construction. Experimental results demonstrate that this design achieves competitive or superior performance compared with strong retrieval baselines. Our contributions are threefold. First, we introduce an entity-centered passage representation that defines retrieval units according to semantic coherence rather than fixed-length segmentation. Second, we propose a query-independent entity-structure index that enables passages to be retrieved through entities and their associated semantic information while preserving the original documents as evidence. Third, we develop a unified retrieval framework that combines structured evidence localization with LLM-based semantic synthesis, providing an effective middle ground between conventional chunk-based RAG and fully structured question answering systems.

结论Conclusion

我们提出了 EnSI-RAG，一种用于长文档问答的实体结构索引检索增强生成框架。 EnSI-RAG 不是将文档索引为任意固定大小的块，而是构造语义包含的段落并构建以下形式的独立于查询的索引We presented EnSI-RAG, an entity-structure-indexed retrieval-augmented generation framework for long-document question answering. Instead of indexing documents as arbitrary fixed-size chunks, EnSI-RAG constructs semantically contained passages and builds a query-independent index of the form[entity][entity_type][property∣relation∣aspect:value]→{psg_id}[\textit{entity}][\textit{entity\_type}][\textit{property}\mid\textit{relation}\mid\textit{aspect}:\textit{value}]\rightarrow\{\textit{psg\_id}\}。该索引提供了从未来问题到原始支持段落集的结构化访问路径，改进了证据本地化，同时保留了法学硕士作为最终语义整合者的角色。在金融、法律、学术和聚合型 QA 设置中，EnSI-RAG 旨在在非结构化的基于块的 RAG 和完全结构化的数据库式推理之间提供灵活的中间立场。更广泛地说，我们的结果表明，长文档 QA 受益于将证据本地化与答案合成分开：索引引导系统找到相关段落，而法学硕士将这些段落整合到最终答案中。. This index provides structured access paths from future questions to sets of original supporting passages, improving evidence localization while preserving the LLM’s role as the final semantic integrator. Across financial, legal, academic, and aggregation-heavy QA settings, EnSI-RAG is designed to provide a flexible middle ground between unstructured chunk-based RAG and fully structured database-style reasoning. More broadly, our results suggest that long-document QA benefits from separating evidence localization from answer synthesis: the index guides the system toward relevant passages, and the LLM integrates those passages into the final answer.
