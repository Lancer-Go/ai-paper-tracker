  2. 2.

  3. 3.

2 AgentDS 基准和竞争The AgentDS Benchmark and Competition

2.1 设计理念Design Philosophy

AgentDS 建立在三个核心原则之上：AgentDS is built on three core principles:

1. 特定领域的复杂性。1. Domain-specific complexity.我们的设计方式是强大的性能需要特定领域的洞察力。通用方法最多只能产生基线结果；竞争绩效要求了解每种情况下哪些功能很重要以及哪些处理步骤是适当的。这种设计选择有意测试代理是否可以应用真正的领域推理。We design in the way that strong performance requires domain-specific insights. Generic methods yield baseline results at best; competitive performance demands understanding what features matter in each context and what processing steps are appropriate. This design choice deliberately tests whether agents can apply genuine domain reasoning.

2.多式联运。2. Multimodal integration.现实世界的数据科学很少涉及单个表格数据集。因此，AgentDS 不仅提供包含预测目标的主要表格数据集，还提供其他数据模式，例如图像（例如产品照片或车辆状况图像）、文本（例如客户评论或临床记录）和结构化文件（例如 JSON、PDF 或链接到主数据集的其他 CSV 文件）。这种设计引入了特定于领域的复杂性，更贴近地反映了现实世界的数据科学挑战。Real-world data science rarely involves a single tabular dataset. AgentDS therefore provides not only a primary tabular dataset containing the prediction target, but also additional data modalities such as images (e.g., product photos or vehicle condition images), text (e.g., customer reviews or clinical notes), and structured files (e.g., JSON, PDFs, or additional CSV files linked to the main dataset). This design introduces domain-specific complexity that more closely reflects real-world data science challenges.

3.现实世界的合理性。3. Real-world plausibility.虽然我们的数据是综合的，但生成过程忠实地反映了实际行业数据中发现的真实关系。每个领域的数据集都包含从业者遇到的现实约束和相关性。我们查阅领域文献，包括学术论文、行业报告和从业者博客，以确保我们的数据反映真实的模式并且不与既定的领域知识相矛盾。While our data is synthesized, the generation process faithfully mirrors genuine relationships found in actual industry data. Each domain’s datasets incorporate realistic constraints and correlations that practitioners encounter. We consult the domain literature, including academic papers, industry reports, and practitioner blogs, to ensure that our data reflect authentic patterns and do not contradict established domain knowledge.

2.3 数据管理流程Data Curation Process

创建既现实、具有挑战性又信息丰富的数据集需要采用系统方法。我们的管理流程涉及四个阶段，如下所述。Creating datasets that are simultaneously realistic, challenging, and informative requires a systematic approach. Our curation pipeline involves four stages as described below.

第一阶段：领域研究。Stage 1: Domain research.对于每个领域，我们都会确定数据科学提供价值的关键问题、常见的特征和数据类型、特定领域的工具和特征工程实践，以及预测变量和结果之间的合理关系。这项研究将我们的数据集生成建立在真实的领域知识的基础上，确保解决我们的挑战能够反映解决实际的行业问题。For each domain, we identify critical problems where data science provides value, the types of features and data commonly encountered, domain-specific tools and feature engineering practices, and plausible relationships between predictors and outcomes. This research grounds our dataset generation in authentic domain knowledge, ensuring that solving our challenges mirrors solving real industry problems.

第二阶段：数据生成。Stage 2: Data generation.我们使用精心设计的数据生成过程来合成数据，这些过程尊重第一阶段确定的领域约束。重要的是，生成过程确保强大的预测性能需要特定于领域的推理，而不是纯粹的通用建模管道。为了实现这一目标，我们将影响预测目标的某些潜在变量转换为其他数据模态（例如图像），以便从这些模态中有效提取特征需要特定领域的见解。因此，每个挑战数据集都包含一个主要表格数据集，其中包含预测目标以及编码补充信息的附加数据模式。我们迭代测试基线方法（例如，仅将 XGBoost 应用于表格数据），以验证它们相对于适当利用具有特定领域见解的附加模式的方法表现不佳。中提供了说明此过程的示例We synthesize data using carefully designed data-generating processes that respect the domain constraints identified in Stage 1. Importantly, the generation procedure ensures that strong predictive performance requires domain-specific reasoning rather than purely generic modeling pipelines. To achieve this, we transform certain latent variables that influence the prediction target into additional data modalities (e.g., images), so that effective feature extraction from these modalities requires domain-specific insights. As a result, each challenge dataset consists of a primary tabular dataset containing the prediction target together with additional data modalities that encode complementary information. We iteratively test baseline approaches (e.g., applying XGBoost to the tabular data alone) to verify that they underperform relative to methods that appropriately leverage the additional modalities with domain-specific insights. An example illustrating this process is provided inLuo et al. [2025a]，具有合成财产保险数据集，其中关键的潜在变量嵌入在屋顶图像中。, with a synthetic property insurance dataset where crucial latent variables were embedded in roof images.

第三阶段：性能界限和难度校准。Stage 3: Performance bounds and difficulty calibration.因为我们控制数据生成过程，所以我们可以通过评估在完全了解数据生成机制的情况下可达到的分数来确定性能的理论上限。这使我们能够校准挑战难度并区分可能的参与者方法中的基本限制和差距。Because we control the data generation process, we can determine the theoretical upper bound on performance by evaluating the score achievable under perfect knowledge of the data-generating mechanism. This allows us to calibrate challenge difficulty and distinguish between fundamental limits and gaps in possible participant approaches.

We validate that domain experts find the challenges realistic and that the documented information is sufficient (though not prescriptive) for informed approaches. Finally, the data is prepared per domain, meaning that all challenges within the same domain are organized together as a single package.

3.3 人机协作优于单独协作Human-AI Collaboration Outperforms Either Alone

AgentDS 竞赛中的高性能方法有效地将人类战略判断与人工智能计算支持结合起来。这种合作有多种形式：High-performing approaches in AgentDS competition effectively combine human strategic judgment with AI computational support. This collaboration takes several forms:

人工智能负责加速，人类负责方向。AI for acceleration, humans for direction.成功的方法使用人工智能代理来处理日常任务，例如数据加载、初始探索性分析、样板代码生成，而人类保留对战略决策的控制：要设计哪些功能、要比较哪些模型、如何解释结果。这种分工可以发挥各自的优势。Successful approaches use AI agents to handle routine tasks, such as data loading, initial exploratory analysis, boilerplate code generation, while humans retain control over strategic decisions: which features to engineer, which models to compare, how to interpret results. This division of labor leverages the strengths of each.

迭代的人类-人工智能反馈循环。Iterative human-AI feedback loops.有效的协作不是将人工智能视为完全自主，而是涉及紧密的反馈循环：人类提出方法，人工智能快速实施它们，人类评估结果并完善假设。重要的是，这些循环始终是人类发起的。参与者描述了人类判断结果何时不令人满意的工作流程，诊断可能的原因，并为人工智能制定下一个指令。代理加速迭代，但每个循环的方向是由人类推理决定的。Rather than treating AI as fully autonomous, effective collaboration engages tight feedback loops: humans propose approaches, AI implements them rapidly, and humans evaluate results and refine hypotheses. Importantly, these loops are consistently human-initiated. Participants described workflows in which humans judged when results were unsatisfactory, diagnosed the likely cause, and framed the next instruction to the AI. The agent accelerates iteration, but the direction of each cycle is determined by human reasoning.

互补，而非替代。Complementarity, not replacement.人类-人工智能团队通过互补性取得卓越表现：人类提供领域基础、因果推理和错误纠正；人工智能提供计算能力、快速原型设计和详尽的搜索。两者单独发挥的效果都无法与它们的综合效果相媲美。Human-AI teams excel through complementarity: humans provide domain grounding, causal reasoning, and error correction; AI provides computational power, rapid prototyping, and exhaustive search. Neither alone matches their combined effectiveness.

这些发现与越来越多的人类与人工智能协作研究产生共鸣These findings resonate with a growing body of research on human-AI collaborationLai et al. [2021], Inkpen et al. [2023], Cao et al. [2023], Revilla et al. The central insight is that collaboration quality, meaning how effectively human judgment and AI capabilities are integrated, is as important as the capabilities of either alone. When human-AI collaboration is thoughtfully designed, the resulting partnership can outperform either humans or AI acting alone.

4 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

AgentDS 代表了在特定领域的数据科学中严格评估人工智能和人类与人工智能协作的第一步，但有几个局限性值得讨论：AgentDS represents an initial step toward rigorous evaluation of AI and human-AI collaboration in domain-specific data science, but several limitations warrant discussion:

综合数据。Synthetic data.虽然我们的数据生成过程反映了现实世界的关系，但它无法捕捉真实行业数据集的全部混乱、模糊性和噪音。未来的迭代可能会在可行的情况下纳入真实（匿名）数据集。While our data generation process mirrors real-world relationships, it cannot capture the full messiness, ambiguity, and noise of genuine industry datasets. Future iterations may incorporate real (anonymized) datasets where feasible.

参与池有限。Limited participation pool.我们的首届竞赛吸引了有价值的参与，但更广泛、更多样化的参与将加强研究成果。我们的目标是在未来的版本中扩大覆盖范围。Our inaugural competition drew valuable participation, but larger and more diverse engagement would strengthen findings. We aim to expand outreach in future editions.

域的范围。Scope of domains.六个领域虽然各不相同，但并没有穷尽应用数据科学的前景。未来的工作可以扩展到其他领域（例如能源或其他金融领域）以测试我们研究结果的普遍性。Six domains, while diverse, do not exhaust the landscape of applied data science. Future work can expand to additional domains (e.g., energy or other areas of finance) to test the generalization of our findings.

不断发展的人工智能能力。Evolving AI capabilities.人工智能系统快速改进。我们当前竞争的结果可能无法反映未来的能力。 AgentDS 被设计为一个持续的基准；随着代理系统的进步，我们将继续跟踪性能。AI systems improve rapidly. Findings from our current competition may not reflect future capabilities. AgentDS is designed as an ongoing benchmark; we will continue tracking performance as agentic systems advance.

合作的观察分析。Observational analysis of collaboration.我们对人类与人工智能协作的分析依赖于参与者报告、代码提交、 和工作流程的定性检查。虽然这些来源提供了丰富的见解 团队如何与AI工具交互，竞赛设置不允许受控 合作策略的实验。未来的工作可以设计对照研究 系统地改变自主程度、激励策略或人类监督 量化哪些协作模式可以产生最佳结果。Our analysis of human-AI collaboration relies on participant reports, code submissions, and qualitative inspection of workflows. While these sources provide rich insight into how teams interacted with AI tools, the competition setting does not allow controlled experiments on collaboration strategies. Future work could design controlled studies that systematically vary the degree of autonomy, prompting strategies, or human oversight to quantify which collaboration patterns produce the best outcomes.

5 结论Conclusion

AgentDS 引入了在现实条件下研究特定领域数据科学的基准和竞赛。该基准测试包含跨越 6 个领域的 17 项挑战，每项挑战的设计目的都是为了实现强大的性能，需要领域知识、多模式推理和深思熟虑的建模决策，而不是通用的机器学习管道。通过将受控数据生成框架与开放竞争环境相结合，AgentDS 提供了一个系统环境，用于评估自主 AI 代理和特定领域数据科学的人机协作。AgentDS introduces a benchmark and competition for studying domain-specific data science under realistic conditions. The benchmark comprises 17 challenges across six domains, each designed so that strong performance requires domain knowledge, multimodal reasoning, and thoughtful modeling decisions rather than generic machine learning pipelines. By combining a controlled data generation framework with an open competition setting, AgentDS provides a systematic environment for evaluating both autonomous AI agents and human–AI collaboration for domain-specific data science.

我们的结果揭示了三个一致的发现。首先，当前的代理人工智能系统难以进行特定领域的推理，特别是当必须合并多模态信号和上下文知识时。其次，人类专业知识仍然至关重要：参与者反复展示了诊断建模失败、通过特征设计和特定领域规则注入领域知识以及做出有关模型泛化的战略决策的能力。第三，最成功的解决方案来自人类与人工智能的协作，人类指导问题解决过程，而人工智能则加速编码、实验和迭代。Our results reveal three consistent findings. First, current agentic AI systems struggle with domain-specific reasoning, particularly when multimodal signals and contextual knowledge must be incorporated. Second, human expertise remains essential: participants repeatedly demonstrated the ability to diagnose modeling failures, inject domain knowledge through feature design and domain-specific rules, and make strategic decisions about model generalization. Third, the most successful solutions emerge from human–AI collaboration, where humans guide the problem-solving process while AI accelerates coding, experimentation, and iteration.

这些发现表明，数据科学中人工智能的未来可能不在于完全自主的自动化，而在于有效的人机协作。因此，进步不仅取决于模型能力的提高，还取决于设计能够更好地支持人类推理、领域知识整合和迭代问题解决的人工智能。 AgentDS 为研究这些动态和开发增强而不是取代人类专业知识的人工智能奠定了基础。These findings suggest that the future of AI in data science may not lie in fully autonomous automation, but in effective human–AI collaboration. Progress therefore depends not only on improving model capabilities, but also on designing AI that better support human reasoning, domain knowledge integration, and iterative problem solving. AgentDS provides a foundation for studying these dynamics and for developing AI that augment, rather than replace, human expertise.
