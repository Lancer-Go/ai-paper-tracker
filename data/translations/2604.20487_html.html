1 简介Introduction

法学硕士在推理、语言理解和知识回忆方面表现出强大的能力。然而，他们的知识主要存储在大规模预训练期间学习的参数权重中，如果不进行昂贵的再训练或微调，就很难更新或扩展LLMs demonstrate strong capabilities in reasoning, language understanding, and knowledge recall. However, their knowledge is predominantly stored in parametric weights learned during large-scale pretraining, making it difficult to update or extend without costly retraining or fine-tuning[26, 6, 24]。这种限制激发了越来越多的非参数知识增强工作。. This limitation has motivated a growing body of work on non-parametric knowledge augmentation.

RAG是这个方向上最广泛采用的范例，从外部语料库中检索相关文档并将其附加到外部知识条件生成的提示中RAG is the most widely adopted paradigm in this direction, retrieving relevant documents from external corpora and appending them to the prompt to condition generation on external knowledge[18, 11, 14]。虽然在实践中有效，但 RAG 从根本上来说是通过. While effective in practice, RAG fundamentally operates through语境扩展context expansion：外部知识作为输入序列中的附加标记引入。: external knowledge is introduced as additional tokens in the input sequence.

这种设计导致了固有的限制。检索到的证据必须与序列中的所有其他标记竞争注意力，包括格式化标记、话语结构和不相关的上下文。因此，检索到的知识的影响是间接且不稳定的，常常导致推理中的利用不足或不一致This design leads to an inherent limitation. Retrieved evidence must compete for attention with all other tokens in the sequence, including formatting tokens, discourse structure, and irrelevant context. As a result, the influence of retrieved knowledge is indirect and unstable, often leading to underutilization or inconsistency in reasoning[4, 34, 2]。至关重要的是，这个问题不仅仅是经验性的，而且是结构性的：外部知识是在令牌级别注入的，而模型的推理过程是由对内部记忆表示的关注来控制的。. Crucially, this issue is not merely empirical but structural: external knowledge is injected at the token level, whereas the model’s inference process is governed by attention over internal memory representations.

从 Transformer 架构的角度来看，注意力可以理解为可微的联想记忆的一种形式。在推理过程中，查询向量通过计算与关键向量的相似度来检索信息，并通过 softmax 加权竞争过程聚合相应的值向量From the perspective of Transformer architectures, attention can be understood as a form of differentiable associative memory. During inference, query vectors retrieve information by computing similarity with key vectors, and the corresponding value vectors are aggregated through a softmax-weighted competition process[32]。这种解释与更广泛的工作相一致，将注意力机制构建为. This interpretation aligns with a broader line of work that frames attention mechanisms as内容寻址存储系统content-addressable memory systems，其中键值对构成通过基于相似性的检索访问的动态内存存储, where key–value pairs constitute a dynamic memory store accessed via similarity-based retrieval[29, 36, 9]。从这个角度来看，生成是通过内存条目的竞争性仲裁过程产生的，而不是纯粹的顺序令牌处理。因此，标记概率不仅取决于语言上下文，还取决于记忆痕迹之间向量空间竞争的结果。. In this perspective, generation emerges from a competitive arbitration process over memory entries rather than purely sequential token processing. Token probabilities are therefore determined not only by linguistic context, but by the outcome of a vector-space competition among memory traces.

这种观点揭示了 RAG 式系统的根本不匹配。参数知识已经作为键值记忆嵌入到模型中，并直接参与注意力竞争。相比之下，检索到的文档作为原始标记引入，必须首先将其编码为内部表示，然后才能影响注意力。因此，外部知识在结构上仍然处于劣势：它存在于上下文中，但没有集成到控制推理的记忆基底中。This perspective reveals a fundamental mismatch in RAG-style systems. Parametric knowledge is already embedded as key–value memory within the model and participates directly in attention competition. In contrast, retrieved documents are introduced as raw tokens, which must first be encoded into internal representations before they can influence attention. Consequently, external knowledge remains structurally disadvantaged: it is present in the context but not integrated into the memory substrate that governs inference.

2.1 检索增强生成及其局限性Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Its Limitations

RAG 通过检索外部文档并根据检索到的上下文生成条件来扩展法学硕士RAG extends LLMs by retrieving external documents and conditioning generation on the retrieved context[18, 10, 14, 28]。事实证明，这种范式可以有效地利用大型外部语料库来提高事实准确性和知识覆盖率。. This paradigm has proven effective for improving factual accuracy and knowledge coverage by leveraging large external corpora.

然而，RAG 从根本上来说是通过However, RAG fundamentally operates through上下文级别的增强context-level augmentation：检索到的信息作为原始标记附加到输入序列中。因此，外部知识必须先编码到内部表征中，然后才能影响注意力，并在位置和上下文偏差下与其他标记竞争。这种设计引入了结构性限制，特别是在长上下文和多跳场景中，相关证据可能被稀释、支离破碎或不一致地利用: retrieved information is appended as raw tokens to the input sequence. As a result, external knowledge must be encoded into internal representations before it can influence attention, and competes with other tokens under positional and contextual biases. 6 结论Conclusion

这项工作引入了知识胶囊和外部键值注入（KVI）框架作为将外部知识集成到大型语言模型中的新范例。与通过上下文扩展进行操作的基于检索的方法相比，我们的方法使知识能够作为结构化记忆直接参与 Transformer 的注意力机制。This work introduces Knowledge Capsules and the External Key–Value Injection (KVI) framework as a new paradigm for integrating external knowledge into large language models. In contrast to retrieval-based methods that operate through context expansion, our approach enables knowledge to participate directly in the Transformer’s attention mechanism as structured memory.

通过将知识表示为规范化的关系胶囊并将其编译为注意力兼容的键值表示，KVI 将知识集成从令牌级调节转变为记忆级交互。这种设计允许外部检索的知识以与参数存储器相同的水平影响生成，从而提高可控性和推理稳定性。By representing knowledge as normalized relational capsules and compiling them into attention-compatible key–value representations, KVI shifts knowledge integration from token-level conditioning to memory-level interaction. This design allows externally retrieved knowledge to influence generation at the same level as parametric memory, improving both controllability and reasoning stability.

多个 QA 基准的实证结果表明，KVI 始终优于 RAG 和 GraphRAG，在多跳和长上下文推理任务中表现尤其强劲。这些改进凸显了将外部知识与模型的内部推理机制结合起来的重要性，而不是将其视为辅助上下文。Empirical results across multiple QA benchmarks demonstrate that KVI consistently outperforms both RAG and GraphRAG, with particularly strong gains in multi-hop and long-context reasoning tasks. These improvements highlight the importance of aligning external knowledge with the model’s internal inference mechanism rather than treating it as auxiliary context.

总的来说，这项工作表明法学硕士的有效知识增强应该超越上下文级别的检索，转向有原则的记忆级别整合。我们希望知识胶囊和 KVI 框架为未来非参数记忆系统和以注意力为中心的知识整合的研究奠定基础。Overall, this work suggests that effective knowledge augmentation in LLMs should move beyond context-level retrieval toward principled memory-level integration. We hope that Knowledge Capsules and the KVI framework provide a foundation for future research on nonparametric memory systems and attention-centric knowledge integration.

7 局限性Limitations

尽管所提出的框架展示了强大的实证性能，但仍然存在一些局限性。Although the proposed framework demonstrates strong empirical performance, several limitations remain.

首先，KVI的有效性取决于知识提取的质量。提取的三元组中的错误或不一致可能会传播到 KV 内存中并影响下游推理。尽管证据基础提供了可追溯性，但提高提取的稳健性仍然是一个重要的方向。First, the effectiveness of KVI depends on the quality of knowledge extraction. Errors or inconsistencies in the extracted triples may propagate into the KV memory and affect downstream reasoning. Although evidence grounding provides traceability, improving extraction robustness remains an important direction.

其次，当前系统依赖于实体检测和图形引导检索。当查询缺乏明确的实体锚点或需要更抽象的推理时，检索质量可能会下降。扩展框架以更好地处理隐式实体和开放域查询是未来工作的一个潜在领域。Second, the current system relies on entity detection and graph-guided retrieval. When queries lack clear entity anchors or require more abstract reasoning, retrieval quality may degrade. Extending the framework to better handle implicit entities and open-domain queries is a potential area for future work.

第三，KV注入在推理过程中引入了额外的内存开销。尽管与原始文本相比，注入的表示形式很紧凑，但扩展到非常大的知识图可能需要更有效的选择和压缩策略。Third, KV injection introduces additional memory overhead during inference. Although the injected representations are compact compared to raw text, scaling to very large knowledge graphs may require more efficient selection and compression strategies.

最后，本评估主要侧重于具有结构化答案的 QA 基准（例如，以实体为中心或基于 ID 的设置）。虽然这可以实现受控和可重复的评估，但需要对开放式自由文本任务进行进一步验证，以充分评估泛化能力。Finally, the present evaluation focuses primarily on QA benchmarks with structured answers (e.g., entity-centric or ID-based settings). While this enables controlled and reproducible evaluation, further validation on open-ended free-text tasks is needed to fully assess generalization.

解决这些限制可以进一步增强大型语言模型中内存级知识集成的鲁棒性、可扩展性和普遍适用性。Addressing these limitations may further enhance the robustness, scalability, and general applicability of memory-level knowledge integration in large language models.
