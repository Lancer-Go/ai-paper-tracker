用于样式标题文本转语音for Style-Captioned Text-to-Speech

Nityanand    Hamees    Wasim    Apoorv    Sameer    Akshat    Sudarshan 1 Smallest.ai nityanandmathur@gmail.com
摘要Abstract

风格字幕文本转语音系统使用自然语言来控制语音特征，但单个单词如何影响声音输出仍不清楚。理解这一点对于诊断故障模式和提高表达 TTS 的可控性至关重要。我们提出了语音扩散模型的交叉注意力归因，首次将 DAAM 框架应用于语音领域，并将其应用于 CapSpeech-TTS。我们的方法跨 25 层和 24 个 ODE 步骤提取每个代币的热图。我们分析了 3,600 个（样式标题、文本转录）组合，其中包含 120 个样式标题，每个组合调节了 30 个文本转录的生成，揭示了标题标记如何塑造波形。结果显示：（1）风格标记比内容/功能标记具有更低的时间方差，证实了全局调节； (2)风格注意力与F0和能量相关； (3) 早期阶段和深层的风格调节峰值； (4)注意力熵在第 17 层达到最小值，与风格重要性峰值同时出现，表明在风格最关键的阶段网络选择性最大。这是首次研究自然语言如何影响语音扩散模型中的交叉注意力。Style-captioned text-to-speech systems use natural language to control voice characteristics, but how individual words influence acoustic output remains unclear. Understanding this is critical for diagnosing failure modes and improving controllability in expressive TTS. We propose cross-attention attribution for speech diffusion models, adapting the DAAM framework to the speech domain for the first time, and apply it to CapSpeech-TTS. Our method extracts per-token heatmaps across 25 layers and 24 ODE steps. We analyze 3,600 (style caption, text transcript) combinations comprising 120 style captions conditioning the generation of 30 text transcripts each, revealing how caption tokens shape waveforms. Results show: (1) style tokens have lower temporal variance than content/function tokens, confirming global conditioning; (2) style attention correlates with F0 and energy; (3) style conditioning peaks in early steps and deep layers; (4) attention entropy reaches its minimum at layer 17, co-occurring with the style importance peak, indicating maximal network selectivity at the most style-critical stage. This is the first study of how natural language influences cross-attention in speech diffusion models.

1 简介Introduction

现代文本转语音 (TTS) 系统已经超越了固定的说话人嵌入Modern text-to-speech (TTS) systems have moved beyond fixed speaker embeddings[dehak2011front]朝着自然语言调节的方向发展，其中自由格式的字幕（例如“平静、低沉的声音慢慢地说”）控制生成的语音风格toward natural language conditioning, where free-form captions such as ``a calm, deep voice speaking slowly'' control the style of generated speech[wang2024capspeech, le2024voicebox, tan2024naturalspeech3]。这种范式建立在生成口语建模的进步之上. This paradigm, building on advances in generative spoken language modeling[lakhotia2021generative]，提供了强大的表达能力，但引入了一个基本的可解释性问题：, offers powerful expressivity but introduces a fundamental interpretability question:样式标题中的各个单词如何影响合成音频？how do individual words in a style caption influence the synthesized audio?

5 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusion

我们的三个实验集中在一个统一的画面上：样式标题 TTS 将交叉注意力实现为Our three experiments converge on a unified picture: style-captioned TTS implements cross-attention as a分层全局调节通道hierarchical global conditioning channel。风格标记在时间上均匀地分配注意力（. Style tokens distribute attention uniformly across time (9.2×9.2\times方差低于功能标记，lower variance than function tokens,d=−1.16d={-}1.16），与语义连贯模式中的声学特征相关（“大声”), correlate with acoustic features in semantically coherent patterns (``loud''↔\leftrightarrow能源，energy,r=+0.64r=+0.64），并遵循从粗到细的时间表，其中早期 ODE 步骤建立全局结构，深层变压器层逐步细化声学细节。这构成了第一个定量证据，表明流量匹配 TTS 中的交叉注意力作为一种全局调制机制发挥作用，与它在自回归 TTS 中发挥的时间对齐作用根本不同), and follow a coarse-to-fine schedule where early ODE steps establish global structure and deep transformer layers progressively refine acoustic details. This constitutes the first quantitative evidence that cross-attention in flow-matching TTS functions as a global modulation mechanism, fundamentally distinct from the temporal alignment role it plays in autoregressive TTS[ren2021fastspeech2].

每个单词的分析揭示了一个清晰的The per-word analysis reveals a clear分工division of labor：声学基础词（“大声”、“鼻音”）部分定位于其效果最强的区域，而抽象描述符（“愉快”、“深沉”）保持均匀分布。早期的 ODE 步骤建立粗略的全局结构，更深的层细化声学细节，并且注意力在关键层 (17-18) 缩小以集中于信息标记 - 在单一机制内实现高效的全局风格调节和细粒度的顺序控制。: acoustically grounded words (``loud'', ``nasal'') partially localize to regions where their effect is strongest, while abstract descriptors (``cheerful'', ``deep'') remain uniformly distributed. Early ODE steps establish coarse global structure, deeper layers refine acoustic details, and attention narrows at critical layers (17–18) to focus on informative tokens—enabling both efficient global style conditioning and fine-grained sequential control within a single mechanism.

局限性。Limitations.我们的分析仅限于一种模型 (CapSpeech) 和超过 30 个样式单词的合成提示。未来的工作应扩展到：(1) 其他基于流量匹配和扩散的 TTS 架构，(2) 用户研究中自然发生的提示，(3) 通过注意力编辑进行因果干预Our analysis is limited to one model (CapSpeech) and synthetic prompts over 30 style words. Future work should extend to: (1) other flow-matching and diffusion-based TTS architectures, (2) naturally occurring prompts from user studies, (3) causal intervention via attention editing[hertz2023p2p]，(4) 每头分析以识别专门的风格编码头，(5) 与基线注意力模式进行比较以量化学习结构。, (4) per-head analysis to identify specialized style-encoding heads, and (5) comparison with baseline attention patterns to quantify learned structure.
