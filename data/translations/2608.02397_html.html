摘要Abstract

我们对十一种音频分类方法进行了基准测试——五种任务感知封闭集法学硕士 （四个 Gemini 模型加上开放式 Kimi-Audio-7B-Instruct），四个固定词汇 标记器（YAMNet、PANN、Whisper-AT、SSLAM）、零样本音频文本模型 (CLAP) 以及 一个基于音频的法学硕士（BAT）——关于封闭集声源识别任务 2,242 个剪辑，涵盖 23 个细粒度类别（11 个类别）。由于这些方法 他们如何接受任务以及如何对产出进行评分有根本的不同，我们 将它们分为四个评估层，而不是一个排行榜，报告宏观 每层的精确率、召回率、F1 和假阴性率。最佳模型 (Gemini-3.1-Pro-Preview)达到85.6%的类别级和56.7%的细粒度F1； Kimi-Audio 的规模具有竞争力 (67.5%/32.9%)，但未能回答 1.6% 的问题 样品。 SSLAM 和 CLAP 匹配或超过最佳闭集模型 类别级别未看到候选列表，但在类别级别上落后 细粒度级别。分析 8,968 个 Gemini 模型的思路 响应，我们发现响应长度并不能预测准确性，一个明显的 “整体判断胜过详细分析”效应可以更好地解释为 困难令人困惑，92-100% 的人自信地说出错误答案 时间。我们报告了所有 11 个类别的完整的每类混淆矩阵和指标 方法，识别大多数精度损失背后的结构误差模式 粒度之间，并为选择这些方法提供实用指导 家庭。We benchmark eleven audio classification methods — five task-aware closed-set LLMs (four Gemini models plus open-weight Kimi-Audio-7B-Instruct), four fixed-vocabulary taggers (YAMNet, PANNs, Whisper-AT, SSLAM), a zero-shot audio-text model (CLAP), and an audio-grounded LLM (BAT) — on a closed-set sound-source identification task over 2,242 clips spanning 23 fine-grained classes (11 categories). Since these methods differ fundamentally in how they receive the task and how outputs are scored, we group them into four evaluation tiers rather than one leaderboard, reporting macro Precision, Recall, F1, and false-negative rate per tier. The best model (Gemini-3.1-Pro-Preview) reaches 85.6% category-level and 56.7% fine-grained F1; Kimi-Audio is competitive for its size (67.5%/32.9%) but fails to answer 1.6% of samples. SSLAM and CLAP match or exceed the best closed-set model at the category level without seeing the candidate list, but fall behind at the fine-grained level. Analyzing the Gemini models’ chain-of-thought across 8,968 responses, we find response length does not predict accuracy, an apparent "holistic judgment beats detailed analysis" effect is better explained as a difficulty confound, and wrong answers are stated confidently 92–100% of the time. We report full per-class confusion matrices and metrics for all eleven methods, identify the structural error modes behind most of the accuracy loss between granularities, and give practical guidance for choosing among these method families.
