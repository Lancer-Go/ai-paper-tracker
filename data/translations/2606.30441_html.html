1 简介Introduction

多智能体系统 (MAS) 的形式化验证需要能够捕获多个智能体如何随着时间的推移交互、竞争和合作的逻辑形式Formal verification of Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) requires logical formalisms capable of capturing how multiple agents interact, compete, and cooperate over time[30, 33, 23]。在这种情况下，诸如交替时间时序逻辑（ATL）之类的策略逻辑. In this setting, strategic logics such as Alternating-time Temporal Logic (ATL⋆) [1]提供一个自然的框架来指定代理联盟可以执行的内容，而不管其他人的行为如何。这使得该逻辑特别适合安全关键和对抗性场景，即使存在不可控或竞争实体，保证也必须成立。然而，编写正确的 ATLprovide a natural framework for specifying what coalitions of agents can enforce, regardless of the behavior of others. This makes the logic particularly suitable for safety-critical and adversarial scenarios, where guarantees must hold even in the presence of uncontrollable or competing entities. Yet, writing correct ATL⋆规范需要形式方法、时序逻辑和博弈论语义方面的大量专业知识，这使得非专业从业者难以构建战略规范。即使对于 ATL（ATL 的普通片段）也是如此specifications requires substantial expertise in formal methods, temporal logic, and game-theoretic semantics, making the construction of strategic specifications difficult to non-expert practitioners. This remains true even for ATL, the vanilla fragment of ATL⋆。困难的一个主要来源是，需求通常用自然语言表达，这本质上是不明确的，既因为其解释取决于上下文，也因为相同的表面形式可能允许不同的结构解读. A major source of difficulty is that requirements are typically expressed in natural language, which is inherently ambiguous, both because its interpretation depends on context and because the same surface form may admit different structural readings[9, 31]。然而，在形式验证中，不能容忍歧义：验证管道需要具有精确语法和明确语义的格式良好的公式。更一般地说，语言表达和逻辑形式之间的差距长期以来引发了这样一种观点，即句子的表面语法可能无法使其底层的形式结构变得明确. In formal verification, however, ambiguity cannot be tolerated: verification pipelines require well-formed formulas with precise syntax and unambiguous semantics. More generally, the gap between linguistic expression and logical form has long motivated the view that the surface grammar of a sentence may fail to make its underlying formal structure explicit[14, 28, 29]。在战略推理的背景下，这种不匹配变得尤其重要，因为模糊性可能会影响联盟归因、时间范围以及对替代战略结果的预期解释. In the context of strategic reasoning, this mismatch becomes especially critical, since ambiguities may affect coalition attribution, temporal scope, and the intended interpretation of alternative strategic outcomes[20]。当自然语言需求由大型语言模型 (LLM) 处理时，这些问题会进一步放大。除了继承自然语言固有的歧义性之外，法学硕士的输入也可能不明确或包含不相关的任务相关细节，这两者都可能导致同一请求出现多种看似合理的形式化。这在战略环境中尤其成问题，即使解释上的微小差异也可能产生逻辑上不同的 ATL 或 ATL. These issues are further amplified when natural-language requirements are processed by Large Language Models (LLMs). Besides inheriting the intrinsic ambiguity of natural language, LLM inputs may also be under-specified or contain irrelevant task-related details, both of which can lead to multiple plausible formalizations of the same request. This is particularly problematic in the strategic setting, where even small differences in interpretation may produce logically distinct ATL or ATL⋆具有显着不同验证结果的公式。formulas with substantially different verification outcomes.

法学硕士的最新进展引起了人们对将自然语言需求自动翻译为正式规范的兴趣。然而，现有的工作主要集中在单主体或纯粹的时间形式主义上，例如线性时间逻辑（LTL）Recent advances in LLMs have generated growing interest in the automatic translation of natural-language requirements into formal specifications. Existing work, however, has focused predominantly on single-agent or purely temporal formalisms, such as Linear Temporal Logic (LTL)[25], 信号时序逻辑 (STL), Signal Temporal Logic (STL)[21]，和计算树逻辑（CTL）, and Computation Tree Logic (CTL)[6]。相比之下，金管局的战略布局在很大程度上仍未被探索。据我们所知，目前还没有经过培训的法学硕士，也没有任何公开可用的数据集，专门用于支持将自然语言要求翻译为 ATL 或 ATL. By contrast, the strategic setting of MAS remains largely unexplored. To the best of our knowledge, there is currently no established trained LLM, nor any publicly available dataset, specifically designed to support the translation of natural-language requirements into ATL or ATL⋆规格。specifications.

自然语言歧义。Natural Language Ambiguities.

语言学形式语法和语义提供了定义明确的形式化，用于利用图论和逻辑分析来识别自然语言歧义Linguistics Formal Syntax and Semantics offer well defined formalizations for identifying natural language ambiguity drawing on graph theory and logical analysis[5]。我们专注于翻译中的句法歧义，因为它们可以被可靠地检测和验证。由于术语或表达的含义的细微差异而产生的语义歧义提出了更大的挑战。它们出现在数据集中，但确定正确的解释并非易事。因此，句法歧义的准确度指标比语义歧义的准确度指标更可靠（更多详细信息，请参阅评估部分）。在这里，我们解决了我们工作中所涉及的含糊之处。前两种类型是右错位和左错位，当一个成分（无论是论元还是附属词）出现在子句边界之外（无论是在其之前还是之后）时，就会发生这种情况。右错位的例子是“玩家们，他们最终可以确保游戏结束”，而“自动售货机，玛丽可以确保它能工作”则说明了左错位。. We focus on syntactic ambiguities in translation, as they can be reliably detected and verified. Semantic ambiguities arising from subtle differences in the meaning of terms or expressions present a greater challenge. They appear in the dataset but determining the correct interpretation is non trivial. Consequently, accuracy metrics for syntactic ambiguities are more reliable than those for semantic ones (see the Evaluation section for more details). Here we address the ambiguities covered in our work. The first two types are right and left dislocation, which occurs when a constituent, wether an argument or adjunct appears outside the clause boundaries, either preceding or following it. Right dislocation is illustrated by "They can eventually ensure the game ends, the players", while "The vending machine, Mary can ensure it will work" exemplifies left dislocation.
