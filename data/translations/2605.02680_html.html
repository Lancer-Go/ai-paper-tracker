摘要Abstract

我们记录了 1980 年至 2019 年间负收益的增加：无论是在上市公司还是在更广泛的美国公司中，报告亏损的公司百分比都在长期增加，而且上市公司每年持续亏损的情况也在长期增加。这种增长是伴随着销售和收益分配的扩大以及公司支出从生产成本和传统投资转向销售一般和管理费用的重组而发生的。我们通过异质公司参与的模型来合理化这些现象We document the rise of negative earnings between 1980 and 2019: a secular increase in the percent of firms reporting losses, both among public firms and in the broader universe of US corporations, and a secular increase in the persistence of losses year-to-year among public firms. This rise has occurred alongside a spreading of the sales and earnings distribution and a recomposition of firm spending away from production costs and traditional investment and towards sales general and administrative expenses. We rationalize these phenomena with a model of heterogenous firms engaging in供需转移投资supply and demand shifting investment。我们的模型包括一个. Our model includes a需求规模弹性scale elasticity of demand确定集约需求边际（每个客户的需求）和扩展需求边际（客户数量）之间的关系。我们能够定量地匹配报告损失的增长，并定性地匹配（1）损失持续性的增加，（2）销售和收益分配的扩散，以及（3）公司支出的重组，将此参数作为稳态均衡变化的单一驱动因素。与报告损失增加相关的规模弹性的上升具有重要的总体影响：在我们的模型中，它通过将劳动力从商品和资本生产中重新分配以及将需求从生产性企业中重新分配出来，使GDP降低了-9.1%。determining the relationship between the intensive margin of demand (demand per customer) and the extensive margin of demand (number of customers). We are able to quantitatively match the rise in reported losses and qualitatively match (1) the increased persistence of losses, (2) the spreading of the sales and earning distribution and (3) the recomposition of firm spending with this parameter as the single driver of changes across steady state equilibria. The rise in the scale elasticity associated with the increase in reported losses has non-trivial aggregate implications: in our model it lowers GDP by -9.1% by reallocating labor away from goods and capital production and reallocating demand away from productive firms.

简介Introduction

大量经济学实证文献的出现记录了属于一个共同主题的事实：美国企业今天的行为与五十年前不同。我们的目标是在这组事实中添加A large empirical literature in economics has emerged documenting facts that fall under a common theme: US businesses behave differently today than they did fifty years ago. We aim to add to this set of facts the负收益上升rise of negative earnings：如图: as shown in Figure1报告显示，当年亏损的上市公司比例从1980年的10.81%上升到2019年的28.04%，而这些公司的平均负盈利“咒语”，即一家公司连续没有盈利的年数，从1.93上升到4.08。, the percentage of public firms reporting losses in a given year has risen from 10.81% in 1980 to 28.04% in 2019, while the average negative earnings “spell” among such firms, the number of consecutive years a firm with no earnings has had no earnings, rose from 1.93 to 4.08.

这种上升在两个方面令人费解。首先，同期上市公司的盈利总体上升：按实际价值计算，1980 年至 2019 年间，平均（中位数）盈利增长了 3.83（3.75）倍。其次，同期所有公司和盈利为负的公司的可变成本 (COGS) 和实物投资 (CapEx) 在销售额中所占的份额有所下降。如果企业整体盈利增加，生产成本和传统前瞻性投资的收入减少，那么为什么还有这么多企业亏损呢？This rise is puzzling on two fronts. First, over the same period public firms’ earnings rose overall: in real terms, mean (median) earnings rose by a factor of 3.83 (3.75) between 1980 and 2019. Second, over the same period variable costs (COGS) and physical investment (CapEx) fell as a share of sales, for all firms and for firms with negative earnings. If firms are making more overall and spending less of their revenue on production costs and traditional forward-looking investment, then why are so many losing money?
