1 简介Introduction

全球约有 4.3 亿人（占全球人口的 5% 以上）无法使用口语，他们患有残疾性听力损失，世界卫生组织预计，到 2050 年，这一数字将超过 7 亿Spoken language is inaccessible to the approximately 430 million people worldwide, over 5% of the global population, who live with disabling hearing loss, a figure the World Health Organization projects will exceed 700 million by 2050Organization [2023]。在孟加拉国，听力障碍患病率接近 9.6%，几乎是全球平均水平的两倍，估计有 2.5 至 300 万人每天依赖孟加拉手语 (BdSL). In Bangladesh the prevalence of hearing impairment is nearly 9.6%, almost double the global average, and an estimated 2.5 to 3 million people rely on Bangla Sign Language (BdSL) dailyTarafder et al. [2015], Hadiuzzaman et al. [2024]。他们在教育、医疗保健、就业和社会参与方面的障碍不是来自残疾本身，而是来自缺乏沟通桥梁。经过认证的 BdSL 口译员数量很少，而且集中在城市中心，因此对于大多数聋哑人和听力障碍 (DHH) 人群来说，在需要沟通时无法找到口译员。因此，在人们已经拥有的硬件上运行的自动识别系统并不是一种便利，而是一种原本不存在的服务的替代品。. Their barriers to education, healthcare, employment, and social participation follow not from the disability itself but from the absence of communication bridges. Certified BdSL interpreters are few and concentrated in urban centres, so for most of the deaf and hard-of-hearing (DHH) population an interpreter is not available when communication is needed. An automatic recognition system running on the hardware people already own is therefore not a convenience but a substitute for a service that does not otherwise exist.

手语是一种完整的、有规则的视觉手势语言，有自己的音韵、形态、语法和词汇，并不在国家或地区之间普遍共享Sign language is a complete, rule-governed visual-gestural language with its own phonology, morphology, grammar, and lexicon, and is not universally shared across nations or regionsValli and Lucas [2000], Sutton-Spence and Woll [1999]。 BdSL 于 2000 年得到残疾发展中心的正式认可，是孟加拉国 DHH 社区的主要沟通方式. BdSL, formally recognized in 2000 by the Centre for Disability in Development, is the primary means of communication for the DHH community in BangladeshKabir et al. [2025];它由 38 个手势组成，代表孟加拉字母表中的 51 个字母，有单手和双手两种形式; it comprises 38 hand signs representing the 51 letters of the Bangla alphabet, in both one-handed and two-handed formsNihal et al. [2021]。字母级或手指拼写识别是问题的切入点：它支撑姓名和外来词拼写、课堂教学和交互式学习工具，并提供视觉前端，以便后续构建单词和句子级系统. Alphabet-level, or fingerspelling, recognition is the entry point to the problem: it underpins name and loanword spelling, classroom instruction, and interactive learning tools, and supplies the visual front end on which later word- and sentence-level systems are builtRastgoo et al. [2021].

从视觉上看，该任务比 38 路标签空间所暗示的更难。多个 BdSL 标志仅在单个手指的弯曲或手掌方向的微小变化方面有所不同，因此图像空间中的类间距离可以小于由签名者、视点或照明的变化引起的类内距离；不同手语者的肤色、手的大小和发音习惯都有很大差异。区分性证据占据一个小的、可变形的、自遮挡的区域，而其余的像素、衣服、家具、教室墙壁与类别无关，并且通常比手本身的对比度更高。因此，在实验室外工作的识别器必须从资源匮乏的手语实际上可以提供的适度标记数据中学习在哪里寻找以及寻找什么。Visually the task is harder than its 38-way label space suggests. Several BdSL signs differ only in the flexion of a single finger or a small change of palm orientation, so the between-class distance in image space can be smaller than the within-class distance induced by a change of signer, viewpoint, or lighting; skin tone, hand size, and articulation habits all vary strongly across signers. The discriminative evidence occupies a small, deformable, self-occluding region, while the remaining pixels, clothing, furniture, classroom walls, are class-irrelevant and often higher in contrast than the hand itself. A recognizer that works outside the laboratory must therefore learn where to look as well as what to look for, from the modest labelled data that low-resource sign languages can realistically provide.

These three lines, efficient design, attention, and verifiable explanation, are individually well developed but are rarely brought together in the BdSL literature.

可部署性是决定其中任何内容是否到达用户的属性。辅助 BdSL 识别器最有可能在中端 Android 手机、教室平板电脑或低成本嵌入式板上离线运行，与周围的应用程序共享内存和电池。每帧执行数亿次乘法累加运算的 20M 参数主干网在该约束下不太适合，无论其基准精度有多高：每个参数、每个乘法累加运算和每兆字节的精度与精度本身一样重要，并且应该在目标硬件上进行测量，而不是仅从参数计数中推断出来。Deployability is the property that decides whether any of this reaches a user. An assistive BdSL recognizer will most plausibly run offline on a mid-range Android phone, a classroom tablet, or a low-cost embedded board, sharing memory and battery with the application around it. A 20M-parameter backbone executing hundreds of millions of multiply–accumulate operations per frame is a poor fit under that constraint, however strong its benchmark accuracy: accuracy per parameter, per multiply–accumulate operation, and per megabyte matters as much as accuracy itself, and should be measured on the target hardware rather than inferred from parameter counts alone.

It therefore often remains unclear whether reported accuracies reflect architectural merit, dataset idiosyncrasies, or evaluation leakage.

2.2 需要真实且经过专家验证的数据Need for Realistic and Expert-Verified Data

识别器对数据的收集方式高度敏感：根据少数志愿者的姿势训练的模型可以在相同设置的测试中得分很高，但在新环境中的真实用户中却会失败。专家验证之所以重要，有一个特定的原因：手指形状或手的方向的微小变化会改变符号的含义，并且不每天签名的志愿者可能会生成接近但不是规范形式的表格，然后将其作为标签噪声进入训练集。因此，一个现实的基准需要真实的签名者、多样化的捕获条件、专家验证以及一种不会将一个签名者的图像放置在两个分区中的协议。Recognizers are highly sensitive to how the data are collected: a model trained on posed gestures from a few volunteers can score well on a test split from the same setting and fail on real users in new environments. Expert verification matters for a specific reason: small changes in finger shape or hand orientation change a sign’s meaning, and a volunteer who does not sign daily may produce a form close to, but not, the canonical one, which then enters the training set as label noise.
