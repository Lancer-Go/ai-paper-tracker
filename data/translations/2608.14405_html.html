1 简介1 Introduction

艺术风格是专业视觉创作的灵魂。对于插画家和概念艺术家等数字艺术家来说，风格不仅是一种可识别的视觉签名，也是一种通过反复出现的视觉和概念选择来表达艺术身份 [1]、情感意图 [34] 和创造性思维的方式 [56, 68]。因此，在艺术家的创作实践和长期发展中，风格探索是一个迭代的过程，在这个过程中，他们研究参考文献，尝试技术和风格方向，反思自己的创作决策，并吸收同行的反馈[30,38,44,68]。Art style is the soul of professional visual creation. For digital artists like illustrators and concept artists, style functions not only as a recognizable visual signature, but also a way of expressing artistic identity [1], emotional intent [34], and creative thinking through recurring visual and conceptual choices [56, 68]. In artists’ creative practice and long-term development, style exploration is therefore an iterative process in which they study references, experiment with techniques and stylistic directions, reflect on their creative decisions, and incorporate feedback from peers [30, 38, 44, 68].

在数字时代，扩大参考资料访问范围并实现更快风格实验的工具加速了风格探索过程。例如，数字绘画工具支持画笔、调色板和渲染技术的高效迭代[5]，而在线平台则让艺术家接触到更广泛的视觉材料和创意影响[82]。生成式人工智能 (GenAI) 通过实现快速构思 [16, 82, 84]、图像细化 [64, 81] 和探索视觉替代方案 [75, 82] 进一步扩展了这些可能性。然而，先前的研究主要集中在支持构思或提高图像生成的速度和质量，而支持风格探索的设计空间仍未得到充分探索。In the digital era, the style exploration process has been accelerated by tools that expand access to references and enable faster style experimentation. For instance, digital painting tools support efficient iteration with brushes, palettes, and rendering techniques [5], while online platforms expose artists to a much broader range of visual material and creative influences [82]. Generative AI (GenAI) further extends these possibilities by enabling rapid ideation [16, 82, 84], image refinement [64, 81], and exploration of visual alternatives [75, 82]. However, prior research has focused on supporting ideation or improving the speed and quality of image generation, while the design space for supporting style exploration remains underexplored.

在许多人工智能研究中，风格被操作为一组可以在图像之间复制、转移或再现的美学特征[65]。这种观点与艺术家在实践中体验风格的方式不同：风格不是作为一种固定的视觉属性，而是作为创造性工作的一个不断发展和所处的方面[52]，需要大量的时间、反思和迭代探索。当前的 GenAI 工具还倾向于优先考虑风格输出而不是艺术家的解释和决策，从而降低了他们的控制感和作者身份 [41, 45]。因此，虽然有许多基于 GenAI 的创造力支持工具，但现有的 AI 支持对于风格探索的价值有限。In much of AI research, style is operationalized as a set of aesthetic features that can be copied, transferred, or reproduced across images [65]. This view differs from how artists experience style in practice: not as a fixed visual property, but as an evolving and situated aspect of creative work [52] that requires substantial time, reflection, and iterative exploration. Current GenAI tools also tend to prioritize stylistic outputs over artists’ interpretation and decisionmaking, reducing their sense of control and authorship [41, 45]. As a result, while there are many GenAI-based tools for creativity support, existing AI support offers limited value for style exploration.

2.1 风格探索中的过程很重要2.1 Process Matters in Style Exploration

对于数字艺术家来说，风格既是可识别的签名，也是创意身份的标志 [62, 67]。它允许观众和客户通过调色板、纹理、笔画和构图模式等重复属性来识别艺术家的作品，从而促进识别[61, 79]。风格体现了艺术家的思维过程、个人表达、影响和交流意图[31,33,46]。此外，风格还具有社会维度，将艺术家与文化路线和社区联系起来，同时在专业背景下区分他们[26, 71]。基于这些特征，本研究定义For digital artists, style serves as both a recognizable signature and a marker of creative identity [62, 67]. It facilitates recognition by allowing audiences and clients to identify an artist’s work through recurring attributes such as color palettes, textures, strokes, and compositional patterns [61, 79]. Style embodies an artist’s thinking process, personal expression, influences, and communicative intent [31, 33, 46]. Furthermore, style also has a social dimension, connecting artists with cultural lines and communities while distinguishing them in professional contexts [26, 71]. Based on these characteristics, this study defines风格style作为艺术家的视觉、概念和社会签名。as the visual, conceptual, and social signature of an artist.

艺术家探索风格的过程被定义为一个连续的、反复出现的循环，其中艺术家参与参考作品、工具和创作媒介之间的动态对话[32]。这是一个非线性的纵向过程，通常跨越数年，在此期间艺术家在实际的艺术创作中不断调整自己的风格。这个探索过程涉及对先前作品的分析和对反馈的反思以迭代风格[41,52,65]。The process of artists exploring style is defined as a continuous, recurring cycle, in which artists engage in a dynamic dialog between reference work, the tools and creative medium [32]. It is a non-linear longitudinal process that often spans years, during which artists continuously adapt their style in the actual making of the art. This exploratory process involves the analysis of prior works and the reflection on feedback to iterate the style [41, 52, 65].

在数字艺术领域，现有的研究集中在以艺术品为中心的艺术品制作过程上，该过程通常侧重于创作特定作品的工作流程[76, 80]。然而，艺术家探索新风格的数字支持仍未得到充分审查。我们的工作通过实证检验数字艺术家在风格探索方面的实践来解决这一差距，将创造力支持工具的重点从艺术品生产转移到风格演变的过程。Within the field of digital arts, existing studies center on the artifact-centric process of artwork production, which often focuses on the workflow of creating a specific work [76, 80]. However, the digital support available to artists to explore new styles remains under-examined. Our work addresses this gap by empirically examining digital artists’ practices in style exploration, shifting the focus of creativity-support tools from artifact production to the process of style evolution.

2.2 基于 GenAI 的工具中的隐形过程和以结果为中心的风格迁移2.2 The Invisible Process and Outcome-centered Style Transfer in GenAI-based Tools

计算研究在很大程度上将风格视为一组可转移的视觉属性，定义为一组可计算再现的因素，例如颜色和纹理[39,52,66]。将风格视为一组可量化的视觉特征，为风格迁移研究奠定了计算基础。Computational research has largely treated style as a transferable set of visual attributes, defined as a set of computationally reproducible factors, such as color and texture [39, 52, 66]. Treating style as a quantifiable set of visual features lays the computational foundation for style transfer research.

从概念上讲，风格迁移涉及从参考图像中提取风格成分并将其应用于其他图像。早期的里程碑，例如赫兹曼的图像类比 [37]，将风格迁移定义为从配对示例中学习转换。接下来是 Gatys 等人的神经风格 [29]，它使用 CNN 来分解内容和风格，以及 Johnson 等人的用于实时性能的前馈网络 [43]。随后的进展包括用于配对和不配对翻译的基于 GAN 的模型，以及使文本到样式生成广泛可用的扩散模型，其中 ControlNet 在改变样式的同时保留结构保真度 [86]。最近的技术通过从单个参考图像和提示生成风格化结果来强调轻量级个性化[53]，或者通过将风格参考与单独的内容或结构化输入相结合以进行更精细的控制[6]。然而，这些计算方法主要将风格构建为输出的再现，忽略了人类与这些人工智能注入的系统交互时所涉及的潜在创造性过程。Conceptually, style transfer involves extracting stylistic components from a reference image and applying them to other images. Early milestones such as Hertzman’s Image Analogies [37], framed style transfer as learning transformations from paired examples. This is followed by Gatys et al.’s Neural Style [29], which disentangles content and style using CNNs, and Johnson et al.’s feed-forward networks for real-time performance [43]. Subsequent advances included GAN-based models for paired and unpaired translation, and diffusion models that made text-to-style generation broadly accessible, with ControlNet preserving structure fidelity while altering style [86]. More recent techniques emphasize lightweight personalization by generating stylized results from a single reference image and prompt [53], or by combining a style reference with a separate content or structured input for finer control [6]. However, these computational approaches frame style primarily as reproduction of output, ignoring the potential creative process involved when humans interact with these AI-infused systems.

这种以输出为中心的取向可能会隐藏艺术家的真实意图，并忽视艺术家为重新评估风格选择而进行的反思实践。特别是当 GenAI 工具强加了强大的、内置的美学方向时，它们会覆盖原始表达，导致不同用户的输出变得越来越标准化。独特视觉识别的丧失随后可能导致风格同质化[4,24,54]。This output-centric orientation can hide an artist’s true intention and neglect the reflective practice that artists engage in to reassess stylistic choices. Particularly when GenAI tools impose a strong, built-in aesthetic direction, they override original expression causing the outputs of diverse users to become increasingly standardized. This loss of unique visual identity can subsequently lead to homogenization of style [4, 24, 54].

此外，大多数数字创意支持系统将风格简化为可复制的视觉特征，针对保真度进行优化，而不是支持艺术实践[39, 42]。波尔奎特等人。进一步批评当前的风格转移工具为艺术家提供的价值很少，并且有削弱他们的代理权的风险 [51, 65]。 GenAI 系统经常抽象出明确的提示，将高级概念转化为不透明的中间表示，使艺术家更难理解为什么会产生特定图像，最终剥夺艺术家的艺术控制和代理权 [9]。Furthermore, most digital creativity-support systems reduce style to reproducible visual features, optimized for fidelity rather than supporting artistic practice [39, 42]. Porquet et al. further critique that current style transfer tools provide little value to artists and risk diminishing their agency [51, 65]. GenAI systems often abstract away explicit prompting, translating high-level concepts into opaque intermediate representations that make it harder for artists to understand why specific images are produced, ultimately depriving artists of artistic control and agency [9].

AER框架反映了艺术家对于风格探索的实际实践，包括学习参考、实验和寻求反馈。我们期望这种三部分模式可以扩展到其他艺术领域：例如，在音乐中，音乐家分析影响（分析），测试主题（实验），并分享批评草稿（恢复）[27, 36]。类似的结构也出现在写作 [22, 72]、产品设计 [10] 和其他创意领域。创造力支持系统不应该将推理分解为端到端自动化，而应该围绕可解释的元素、用户驱动的重组和嵌入式反射来构建。从这个角度来看，嵌入 AER 的 GenAI 从自动化工具转变为支撑探索和创造性增长的基础设施。我们鼓励进一步研究 AER 框架如何支持数字艺术以外领域的风格探索。AER framework reflects artists’ actual practices for style exploration, including studying references, experimenting, and seeking feedback. Similar structures appear in writing [22, 72], product design [10], and other creative fields. Rather than collapsing reasoning into end-to-end automation, creativity-support systems should be structured around interpretable elements, userdriven recombination, and embedded reflection. Viewed this way, AER-embedded GenAI shifts from an automation tool to an infrastructure that scaffolds exploration and creative growth. We encourage further research to examine how the AER framework supports style exploration in fields beyond digital arts.
