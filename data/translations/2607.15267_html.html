Prior work on poisoning pretraining data has largely exploited established data sources such as Wikipedia, which do not represent the large scale and heterogeneity typical of pretraining corpora, and has ignored the interaction between poisoned data and data curation pipelines. We demonstrate that poisoning attacks on pretraining data are feasible beyond this limited setting through an existing web-scale content injection mechanism: public discussion interfaces. Additionally, to measure whether malicious content is included after web crawling and data curation, we introduce半条命HalfLife，一种新颖的分析方法，用于估计基于网络爬行的 LM 训练数据中包含的对抗性内容。我们使用, a novel analysis for estimating adversarial content inclusion in web-crawl based LM training data. Our analysis demonstrates the importance of estimating whether poison injections are included in pretraining data, and establishes third-party webpage content as a possible vector for attacking language model pretraining.

2 第三方网页内容是中毒媒介Third-Party Web Content as a Poisoning Vector

即使对手对 LM 训练数据管道的了解和影响力极小，仍然可能通过大规模瞄准异构网络源来影响最终的训练数据集。我们发现了一种新的攻击媒介，即第三方内容注入，它允许对手通过现有的网络基础设施将自己的恶意文本包含在网络源中。Even with minimal knowledge and influence over the LM training data pipeline, an adversary may still influence final training datasets by targeting heterogeneous web sources at a large scale. We identify a new attack vector, third-party content injection, that allows an adversary to include their own malicious text in web sources via the existing infrastructure of the web.

2.1 动机Motivation

我们考虑对手可以将内容注入网页的途径，这些网页随后会被抓取并作为网络规模预训练语料库的一部分使用。具体来说，我们调查公共讨论界面，攻击者可以使用工具在公共基础设施上自动发布帖子或评论，从而使攻击者控制的文本直接集成到网页内容中。 为了实现这一目标，我们假设对手使用 Selenium 等浏览器自动化工具部署自动评论基础设施，以大规模分发恶意内容。We consider avenues by which an adversary can inject content into webpages that are later crawled and consumed as part of web-scale pretraining corpora. Specifically, we investigate public discussion interfaces, where the adversary can use tooling for automating posts or comments on public infrastructure to cause attacker-controlled text to be integrated directly into webpage content. To achieve this, we consider an adversary who deploys automated commenting infrastructure using browser automation tools such as Selenium to distribute malicious content at scale.

我们的分析灵感来自社会信息活动和宣传，其中大规模分发目标内容旨在影响下游读者。在我们的设置中，这些下游“读者”不是人类，而是收集和使用这些数据的爬虫（最终是语言模型）。 我们的设置类似于社交僵尸网络中先前的威胁模型We take inspiration in our analysis from social information campaigns and propaganda, where mass distribution of targeted content is intended to influence downstream readers. In our setting, rather than humans, these downstream “readers” are crawlers (and eventually LMs) that collect and use this data. Our setting is analogous to prior threat models in social botnets(Zhang et al., 2016)其中单个机器人管理员能够控制和扩展机器人群，但只能通过公共接口与平台交互。 因此，信息活动和中毒媒介之间的实际方法有相似之处（“内容如何广泛传播？”）。然而，毒物向量的有效分布可能会针对不同的站点，并包含针对 LM 和爬虫而不是人类的不同内容。in which a single botmaster is capable of controlling and scaling bot swarms, but can only interact with platforms through public interfaces. Thus, there is a similarity in practical approach between information campaigns and the poisoning vector (“how can content be distributed widely?”). However, effective distribution of the poison vector may target different sites and contain different content to target LMs and crawlers rather than humans.

2.2 威胁模型Threat model

在本节中，我们定义威胁模型，该模型描述了我们为所考虑的攻击者假设的能力和目标。 我们的威胁模型假设对手对训练数据收集和包含的了解和影响最少。因此，对手对下游数据的影响是间接的和概率性的，因为他们无法控制最终训练数据集中包含哪些特定页面。In this section, we define our threat model, which describes the capabilities and goals we assume for the attacker we consider. Our threat model assumes minimal adversary knowledge and influence over training data collection and inclusion. As a result, the adversary’s influence on downstream data is indirect and probabilistic since they cannot control which specific pages are included in the final training dataset.

对手能力。Adversary capabilities.

我们将具有与网络交互能力的对手视为标准消费者和/或内容开发者。 我们不假设对手知道或控制在数据收集期间抓取哪些特定页面。 重要的是，对手无法直接访问他们试图影响的模型的训练管道或数据集（例如在We consider an adversary with the ability to interact with the web as a standard consumer and/or content developer. We do not assume that the adversary knows or controls what specific pages are crawled during data collection. Importantly, the adversary does not have direct access to the training pipeline or datasets of models they are attempting to influence (such as is assumed inZhang et al., 2024）。这种访问权限的缺乏包括训练代码、基础设施、数据和模型权重。我们假设对手有能力运行公开发布的工具来进行预训练数据过滤，作为开发其注入内容的一部分。). This lack of access includes training code, infrastructure, data, and model weights. We assume that the adversary has the ability to run publicly released tools for pretraining-data filtering as part of developing their injected content.

对手的目标。Adversary goals.

我们主要考虑两大类对抗意图。首先，我们考虑一个对手，其目标是通过将未来的语言模型视为接触用户的代理来影响用户。通过毒害模型的训练数据，攻击者的目的是使模型生成特定的主张、叙述或建议。我们考虑的第二个对抗目标是降低模型质量。 在这种情况下，对手的目标是未来模型本身。此设置中的潜在攻击包括越狱模型以产生有害输出或诱导模型对某些短语做出无意义的响应，以阻止用户收到有关这些主题的有用响应。We primarily consider two broad classes of adversarial intent. First we consider an adversary that aims to influence users by treating future language models as a proxy to reach their users. By poisoning a model’s training data, the adversary aims to cause the model to generate specific claims, narratives, or recommendations. The second adversarial aim we consider is degrading model quality. In this setting, the adversary’s target is the future model itself. Potential attacks in this setting include jailbreaking the model to produce harmful outputs or inducing a model to respond with nonsense to certain phrases to prevent users from receiving useful responses on those topics.

我们的分析有几个局限性。我们使用 Common Crawl 作为 AI 训练管道的爬行行为的代理，但主要 AI 实验室的生产爬虫可能在范围、频率和过滤方面有所不同。 我们的评论注入分析基于对静态 HTML 中评论的检测，如果网站针对抓取页面中不可见的僵尸网络采用服务器端保护措施，则可能会高估真实的注入面。 我们不会在真实网站上进行实时注入实验，这可能会损害用户或系统，而是在沙盒环境中验证攻击。 最后，我们在§中的包含估计Our analysis has several limitations. We use Common Crawl as a proxy for the crawling behavior of AI training pipelines, but production crawlers at major AI labs may differ in scope, frequency, and filtering. Our comment injection analysis is based on detection of comments in static HTML, which may overestimate the true injection surface if sites employ server-side protections against botnets not visible in the scraped page.
