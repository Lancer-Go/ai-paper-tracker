摘要Abstract

本文研究了利用纯文本数据来改进语音识别的有效方法，重点关注有助于更快识别的编码器主导模型。我们提供了集成纯文本数据的技术的全面比较，包括模态匹配和动态下采样以在编码器内达到文本级表示。我们在 LibriSpeech 语料库上的实验表明，具有较小解码器的较大编码器可以等于或超过具有较大解码器的架构的性能。我们证明了简单的配置（例如随机持续时间模型）通常比复杂的替代方案更有效，从而显着简化了训练流程。所有代码和配方均公开可用。This paper investigates efficient methods for utilizing textonly data to improve speech recognition, focusing on encoderdominated models that facilitate faster recognition. We provide a comprehensive comparison of techniques to integrate textonly data, including modality matching and dynamic downsampling to reach text-level representations within the encoder. Our experiments on the LibriSpeech corpus show that a larger encoder with a smaller decoder can equal or surpass the performance of architectures with larger decoders. We demonstrate that simple configurations, such as random duration models, are often more effective than complex alternatives, significantly simplifying the training pipeline. All code and recipes are made publicly available.

一、简介1. Introduction

除了配对的音频-文本数据之外，如何最好地利用大量纯文本数据来改进 ASR 仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。标准方法是在纯文本数据上训练单独的语言模型 (LM)，然后在解码过程中使用浅层融合来利用 LM。另一种方法是使用文本转语音 (TTS) 将纯文本数据转换为合成语音，然后与配对的音频文本数据一起进行训练，该方法已被证明非常有效 [1-3]。It remains an open question how to best utilize the large amount of text-only data for improving ASR, in addition to the paired audio-text data. A standard approach is to train a separate language model (LM) on the text-only data, and then use shallow fusion to utilize the LM during decoding. Using text-tospeech (TTS) to convert the text-only data into synthetic speech and then train on that together with the paired audio-text data is another approach, which has been shown to be very effective [1–3].

2.相关工作2. Related Work

很多工作都是在解码器中利用预先训练的语言模型 (LM)，甚至是大型 LM (LLM)。 (L)LM 被扩展以集成编码器，通常通过预先考虑适应 LM 维度的编码器输出（称为 prefixLM [9]），然后对配对的音频文本数据进行微调。A lot of work is on utilizing a pre-trained language model (LM) in the decoder, or even a large LM (LLM). The (L)LM is extended to integrate the encoder, usually by prepending the encoder output adapted into the LM dimension, known as prefixLM [9], and then finetuned on paired audio-text data.

编码器中的文本利用也以各种方式进行了探索，例如模态匹配 [4,5,10–12]、直接利用文本数据作为编码器的输入 [6–8, 13–17] 或 TTS [1–3,18]。Text-utilization in encoder has also been explored in various ways, such as modality matching [4,5,10–12], direct utilization of the text data as input to the encoder [6–8, 13–17] or TTS [1–3,18].

还探索了通过 CTC 压缩在编码器中间对文本级表示进行下采样 [19-21]。Downsampling to text-level representation in the middle of the encoder via CTC compression has also been explored [19– 21].

3.1.伪语音编码器3.1. Pseudo-speech-encoder

伪语音编码器是一个将文本输入转换为伪语音表示的模块，然后将其输入到类文本编码器中，以允许文本数据也可以在编码器中使用。这部分仅用于使用不配对的纯文本数据进行训练，在识别过程中不使用。The pseudo-speech-encoder is a module that converts text input into pseudo-speech representation, which is then fed into the text-like encoder, to allow the text data to be utilized in the encoder as well. This part is only used for training with unpaired text-only data, and is not used during recognition.

它从嵌入层开始，或者在文本标记之间插入空白，然后嵌入。接下来是可选的文本编码器。之后，我们要么有可训练的持续时间模型，要么有不可训练的纯随机持续时间模型。最后，我们有一个线性层将维度转换为与类文本编码器维度相同的维度。It starts with an embedding layer, or with interleaving blanks between the text tokens and then embedding. An optional text encoder follows. After that, we have either a trainable duration model, or a non-trainable purely random duration model. Finally, we have a linear layer to convert the dimension into the same as the text-like encoder dimension.

4. 培训4. Training

4.1.配对的音频文本数据4.1. Paired audio-text data

我们使用典型的监督训练，在解码器输出上使用交叉熵损失，在编码器输出上使用 CTC 损失。We use the typical supervised training with cross-entropy loss on the decoder output, and CTC loss on the encoder output.

通过使用语音编码器输出和文本编码器输出之间的 CTC 强制对齐，使用配对的音频文本数据来训练伪语音编码器中的可训练持续时间模型。Trainable duration models in the pseudo-speech-encoder are trained with the paired audio-text data, by using the CTC forced alignment between the speech encoder output and the text encoder output.

4.2.未配对的纯文本数据4.2. Unpaired text-only data

伪语音编码器。Pseudo-speech-encoder.数据被输入伪语音编码器以获得伪语音表示，然后将其输入类文本编码器。类文本编码器和解码器上的所有损失与配对音频文本数据训练中的相同，即解码器输出上相同的交叉熵损失，以及类文本编码器输出上相同的 CTC 损失。The data is fed into the pseudospeech-encoder to get the pseudo-speech representation, which is then fed into the text-like encoder. All the losses on top of the text-like encoder and decoder are the same as in the paired audio-text data training, i.e., the same cross-entropy loss on the decoder output, and the same CTC loss on the text-like encoder output.

语音合成。TTS.作为一个极端的变体，我们还使用 TTS 模型将文本输入转换为合成语音，然后将合成语音输入语音编码器。在这种情况下，我们根本没有伪语音编码器。As an extreme variant, we also use a TTS model to convert the text input into synthetic speech, and then feed the synthetic speech into the speech encoder. In this case, we do not have the pseudo-speech-encoder at all.

4.3.伪语音编码器训练变体。4.3. Pseudo-speech-encoder training variants.

我们有两种变体：We have two variants:

模态匹配。Modality matching.伪语音编码器以与语音编码器模态匹配的方式进行训练：我们使用 CTC 强制对齐将持续时间精确地应用于文本输入，以便伪语音表示长度与语音编码器输出长度匹配。然后我们使用简单的 L2 损失将伪语音表示与语音编码器输出进行匹配。在不成对的纯文本数据训练中，我们停止从类文本编码器和解码器到伪语音编码器的梯度，以便伪语音编码器仅使用模态匹配损失用成对的音频文本数据进行训练。The pseudo-speech-encoder is trained in a modality matching way with the speech encoder: We use a CTC forced alignment to apply the durations on the text input exactly such that the pseudo-speech representation length matches the speech encoder output length. Then we use simple L2 loss to match the pseudo-speech representation with the speech encoder output. In the unpaired text-only data training, we stop the gradient from the text-like encoder and decoder to the pseudo-speech-encoder, so that the pseudo-speech-encoder is only trained with the paired audio-text data using the modality matching loss.

直接。Direct.我们不会用任何模态匹配损失来训练伪语音编码器。在配对音频文本数据训练中，伪语音编码器根本没有经过训练（持续时间模型除外，如果它是可训练的）。在不成对的纯文本数据训练中，我们允许梯度反向传播到伪语音编码器，以便它与类文本编码器和解码器联合训练。We don’t train the pseudo-speech-encoder with any modality matching loss. In the paired audio-text data training, the pseudo-speech-encoder is not trained at all (except for the duration model if it is trainable). In the unpaired text-only data training, we allow the gradient to backpropagate to the pseudospeech-encoder, so that it is trained jointly with the text-like encoder and decoder.

4.4.批处理和数据比率4.4. Batching and data ratios

我们分三种情况：We have three cases:

仅配对的音频文本数据。Only paired audio-text data.这就是典型的监督训练。在这种情况下，我们根本不使用未配对的纯文本数据。我们不使用梯度累积。This is the typical supervised training. We use no unpaired text-only data at all in this case. We use no gradient accumulation.

通过 TTS 使用未配对的纯文本数据。Unpaired text-only data utilized via TTS.未配对的纯文本数据由 TTS 模型离线转换为合成语音并存储为音频文件。然后训练与配对音频文本数据训练相同，只是我们有更多的数据。在训练期间，我们将配对的音频文本数据和未配对的纯文本数据（TTS 转换后）保持大约 1:1 的比例，即我们看到配对的音频文本数据和来自未配对的纯文本数据的合成语音的小时数相同。我们不使用梯度累积。The unpaired text-only data is converted into synthetic speech by the TTS model offline and stored as audio files. Then the training is the same as the paired audio-text data training, except that we have more data. We keep an approximate ratio of 1:1 for the paired audio-text data and the unpaired text-only data (after TTS conversion) during the training, i.e. we see the same amount of hours of paired audio-text data and the synthetic speech from the unpaired text-only data. We don’t use gradient accumulation.

通过伪语音编码器使用未配对的纯文本数据。Unpaired text-only data utilized via pseudo-speech-encoder.出于效率原因，我们总是让一批仅使用配对的音频文本数据，另一批仅使用未配对的纯文本数据。我们对两个批次都使用梯度累积，以便每个更新步骤都基于两种类型的数据。我们改变训练中使用的不配对的纯文本数据的总量，以及配对的音频文本数据和不配对的纯文本数据之间的比率。这有效地改变了两个批次的批次大小。For efficiency reasons, we always have one batch using only paired audio-text data, and the other batch using only the unpaired text-only data. We use gradient accumulation over both batches such that every update step is based on both types of data. We vary the total amount of unpaired text-only data used in the training, and the ratio between the paired audio-text data and the unpaired text-only data. This effectively varies the batch sizes of the two batches.

7. 结论和未来的工作7. Conclusions & Future Work

我们已经证明，当编码器中使用纯文本数据时，编码器主导的模型对于语音识别非常有效。我们的结果表明，较大的编码器与较小的解码器配对所提供的性能等于或优于具有较大解码器的模型（表 1）。在编码器主导的模型中，编码器端训练的文本利用至关重要。所提出的伪语音编码器能够集成大规模文本数据，而无需 TTS 的高计算成本。此外，我们发现简单的伪语音编码器配置（例如使用随机持续时间模型的配置）非常有效，并且可以胜过更复杂的可训练版本。然而，这种简单的文本利用方法与利用 TTS 模型合成语音的模型的性能之间仍然存在很大差距。 TTS 模型可以看作是伪语音编码器模型的变体，我们希望在未来的工作中研究两种方法之间的个体差异。We have shown that encoder-dominated models are highly effective for speech recognition when text-only data is utilized within the encoder. Our results indicate that a larger encoder paired with a small decoder provides performance that is equal to or better than models with larger decoders (Table 1). In encoder-dominated models, text-utilization on the encoder-side training is essential. The proposed pseudo-speech-encoder enables the integration of large-scale text data without the high computational cost of TTS. Furthermore, we found that simple pseudo-speech-encoder configurations, such as those using random duration models, are surprisingly effective and can outperform more complex, trainable versions. However, there is still a significant gap between this simple approach of text-utilization and the performance of models utilizing synthetic speech from a TTS model. The TTS model can be seen as a variant of the pseudo-speech-encoder model, and we want to investigate the individual differences between both approaches in future work.
