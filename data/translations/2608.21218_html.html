摘要Abstract

预测性政治问答 (QA)，例如预测政治参与者将如何投票，超出了事实查找的范围。 外部政治资源提供了丰富的历史证据，但很少包含答案本身。 现有的法学硕士增强方法，包括基于参与者概况的模拟和知识图证据注入，改善了政治推理，但在很大程度上将外部资源视为基于知识的证据，从而使与预测相关的信号模型不足。 我们为预测性政治问答确定了两个互补信号：捕捉特定问题偏好的参与者立场，以及捕捉政治参与者之间间接依赖关系的高阶结构信号。 我们提出 PSL，一种双视图框架，可将半结构化政治记录转换为法学硕士的面向推理的证据。 PSL 从语义视图中与问题相关的参与者记录中提取立场信号，并从向量视图中的参与者交互图中学习结构感知的参与者表示。 在三个现实世界数据集和多个法学硕士中，PSL 始终优于基线，消融证实了立场和结构信号的互补增益。Predictive political question answering (QA), such as predicting how a political actor will vote, goes beyond factual lookup. External political resources offer rich historical evidence, but rarely contain the answer itself. Existing LLM augmentation methods, including actor-profile-based simulation and knowledge graph evidence injection, improve political reasoning but largely treat external resources as knowledge-based evidence, leaving prediction-relevant signals under-modeled. We identify two complementary signals for predictive political QA: actor stances that capture issue-specific preferences, and high-order structure signals that capture indirect dependencies among political actors. We propose PSL, a dual-view framework that converts semi-structured political records into inference-oriented evidence for LLMs. PSL extracts stance signals from question-relevant actor records in a semantic view, and learns structure-aware actor representations from an actor interaction graph in a vector view. Across three real-world datasets and multiple LLMs, PSL consistently outperforms baselines, with ablations confirming the complementary gains of stance and structure signals.

1 简介Introduction

Large language models (LLMs) provide a powerful foundation for political QA, but their knowledge is often incomplete and unreliable in specialized, long-tail settings37。 因此，利用外部政治资源增强法学硕士是一个自然的方向. Augmenting LLMs with external political resources is therefore a natural direction9。 政治领域包含丰富的外部数据，包括社会背景信息. However, many political QA tasks are not simple factual queries but predictive questions, such as predicting how a political actor will vote or respond to a future event49; 14。 对于这些问题，外部资源提供了有价值的记录，但很少直接包含答案，因为相关行为尚未发生，现有记录仅作为间接事实。 因此，预测性政治问答不能仅通过直接事实检索来解决。. Therefore, predictive political QA cannot be solved by direct factual retrieval alone.

Political actors are connected not only by direct ties but also through higher-order relational patterns. 在这项工作中，我们提出了 PSL，这是一种双视图框架，它使用半结构化政治数据来增强法学硕士的预测性政治 QA。 PSL 提取并集成两种互补类型的信号：i）来自语义视图的演员姿态信号和 ii）来自向量视图的高阶结构信号。 具体来说，PSL 从丰富的记录中为每个政治参与者构建 JSON 格式的参与者档案，并通过共享记录链接参与者以构建交互图。 在语义视图中，PSL 从配置文件中检索与问题相关的记录，并推断可扩展的立场信号以捕获参与者对特定问题的偏好。 在向量视图中，PSL 在交互图上传播信息以获得编码高阶邻域信号的参与者表示。 然后，PSL 将参与者表示与问题焦点嵌入共同嵌入，生成相互细化的向量，并通过轻量级微调为 LLM 配备结构感知推理。 最后，PSL 将语义视图中的立场与向量视图中的协作表示相结合，并将它们注入到 LLM 中，为预测性政治 QA 提供更强的推理证据。In this work, we propose PSL, a dual-view framework that uses semi-structured political data to enhance LLMs for predictive political QA. PSL extracts and integrates two complementary types of signals: i) actor stance signals from a semantic view and ii) high-order structure signals from a vector view. Specifically, PSL constructs an actor profile in JSON format for each political actor from abundant records, and links actors via shared records to build an interaction graph. In the semantic view, PSL retrieves question-relevant records from profiles and infers extensible stance signals to capture an actor’s preference on a specific issue. In the vector view, PSL propagates information over the interaction graph to obtain actor representations that encode high-order neighborhood signals. PSL then co-embeds the actor representation with the question-focus embedding, producing mutually refined vectors, and equips the LLM with structure-aware reasoning through lightweight fine-tuning. Finally, PSL integrates stances from the semantic view with collaborative representations from the vector view and injects them into the LLM, providing stronger inferential evidence for predictive political QA.

这项工作做出了三个贡献。 首先，我们在半结构化政治记录中识别出两个互补信号：行为者立场和高阶结构信号。前者捕捉行动者对特定问题的偏好，而后者则表征共同政治行为所揭示的群体影响力。 其次，我们提出了 PSL，这是针对这两个信号的双视图框架：语义视图提取演员姿态信号，矢量视图建模高阶结构信号，共同增强法学硕士的预测推理。 第三，我们对不同法学硕士的三个真实数据集进行了全面的实验。结果表明 PSL 显着优于所有基线方法。 消融验证了语义立场证据和矢量化结构信号的互补优势。This work makes three contributions. First, we identify two complementary signals in semi-structured political records: actor stances and high-order structure signals. The former capture actors’ preferences on specific issues, whereas the latter characterize group influence revealed by shared political behavior. Second, we propose PSL, a dual-view framework for these two signals: a semantic view extracts actor-stance signals, and a vector view models high-order structure signals, jointly enhancing LLMs’ predictive reasoning. Third, we conduct comprehensive experiments on three real-world datasets across different LLMs. The results demonstrate that PSL significantly outperforms all the baseline methods. Ablations verify the complementary benefits of semantic stance evidence and vectorized structure signals.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出 PSL，一个将半结构化政治记录转化为面向推理的证据以进行预测性政治 QA 的框架。 PSL 使文本语义和图结构特征能够共同加强 LLM 推理。 这种基于半结构化数据的双视图设计提供了更加面向预测的证据表示。而且，与KG三元组相比，PSL更好地保留了上下文细节；与文本轮廓模拟相比，它更全面地模拟了参与者之间的结构关系。 对多个真实世界数据集和 LLM 的实验表明，PSL 始终优于现有的增强方法。更广泛地说，我们的结果提供了初步证据，证明基于知识的证据之外的推理导向证据对于预测性政治质量保证是有效的。我们将发布代码和模型检查点以方便进一步的研究。We propose PSL, a framework that transforms semi-structured political records into inference-oriented evidence for predictive political QA. PSL enables textual semantics and graph-structural features to jointly strengthen LLM reasoning. This dual-view design, grounded in semi-structured data, provides a more prediction-oriented evidence representation. Moreover, compared with KG triples, PSL better preserves contextual detail; compared with text-profile simulation, it more fully models structural relations among actors. Experiments on multiple real-world datasets and LLMs show that PSL consistently outperforms existing augmentation methods. More broadly, our results provide initial evidence that inference-oriented evidence beyond knowledge-based evidence is effective for predictive political QA. We will release the code and model checkpoints to facilitate further research.

局限性Limitations

数据范围。Data scope.本文在美国政治环境中验证了 PSL，使用立法投票、法案信息和外交事件等公共记录来构建半结构化档案和交互图。这一设置为测试 PSL 的核心设计提供了明确的实验基础：从常见的政治记录中提取行动者立场信号和高阶结构信号，以增强预测性政治 QA。 然而，数据范围仍有扩大的空间。 一方面，未来的工作可以将 PSL 应用于更多的国家、政治体系和语言环境，以评估其在政治环境中的普遍性。 另一方面，现有记录也可以扩展到更丰富的来源，例如共同赞助、委员会活动、公开声明、竞选捐款和政策文本。这些扩展不需要改变 PSL 的基本设计，但可以为立场提取和结构建模提供更全面的行为证据。This paper validates PSL in U.S. political settings, using public records such as legislative votes, bill information, and diplomatic events to construct semi-structured profiles and an interaction graph. This setting provides a clear experimental basis for testing PSL’s core design: extracting actor stance signals and high-order structure signals from commonly available political records to enhance predictive political QA. However, there remains room to broaden the data scope. On the one hand, future work could apply PSL to more countries, political systems, and language contexts to assess its generalizability across political environments. On the other hand, existing records could also be extended to richer sources, such as co-sponsorship, committee activities, public statements, campaign donations, and policy texts. These extensions do not require changes to PSL’s basic design, but could provide more comprehensive behavioral evidence for stance extraction and structure modeling.

信号融合。Signal fusion.本文设计了两个互补信号来增强预测性政治质量保证。实验和消融研究表明，这些信号从不同角度为法学硕士提供了有效的推理证据。然而，不同的问题可能不同程度地依赖这两个信号。有些问题可能更多地取决于行为者在相关问题上的历史立场，而另一些问题可能更多地依赖于政治行为者之间的群体层面的行为模式。未来的工作可以探索自适应融合机制，根据问题、参与者或政治环境动态调整两个信号的权重，从而实现跨预测任务的证据需求的更细粒度的建模。This paper designs two complementary signals to enhance predictive political QA. Experiments and ablation studies show that these signals provide effective reasoning evidence for LLMs from different perspectives. However, different questions may rely on the two signals to different degrees. Some questions may depend more on an actor’s historical stance on related issues, whereas others may rely more on group-level behavioral patterns among political actors. Future work could explore adaptive fusion mechanisms that dynamically adjust the weights of the two signals according to the question, actor, or political setting, enabling finer-grained modeling of evidence needs across predictive tasks.
