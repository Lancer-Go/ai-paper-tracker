摘要。Abstract.

微架构设计空间探索面临着广阔的搜索空间和昂贵的 PPA 评估，只为设计决策留下了很少的模拟预算。现有方法在不考虑微架构依赖性的情况下执行盲目搜索，并且无法有效地从迭代搜索中学习，导致评估浪费和帕累托收敛性较弱。在本文中，我们建议Microarchitecture design space exploration suffers from expansive search spaces and expensive PPA evaluation, leaving only a small simulation budget for design decision-making. Existing methods perform blind search without considering microarchitectural dependencies and fail to learn from the iterative search effectively, leading to wasted evaluations and weak Pareto convergence. In this paper, we propose微进化MicroEvo，一个知识引导框架，将现成的法学硕士与蒙特卡洛树搜索 (MCTS) 结合起来，以实现多目标微架构优化。 MicroEvo 结合了 LLM 驱动的进化算子、平衡 Pareto 贡献和多样性的 Pareto 感知树策略、提取和重用优化见解的主动知识积累机制，以及适应在线搜索行为的状态感知指令。实验表明，MicroEvo 将 Pareto 前沿质量提高了多达, a knowledge-guided framework that couples off-the-shelf LLMs with Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) for multi-objective microarchitecture optimization. MicroEvo combines LLM-driven evolutionary operators, a Pareto-aware tree policy that balances Pareto contribution and diversity, an active knowledge accumulation mechanism that extracts and reuses optimization insights, and state-aware directives that adapt the search behavior online. 1. 简介Introduction

新兴应用越来越多地推动硬件设计转向特定于工作负载的微架构Emerging applications are increasingly driving hardware design toward workload-specific microarchitectures(Liu et al., 2019; Halpern et al., 2016; Grayson et al., 2020)。在这种趋势下，设计人员需要在广阔的空间中搜索可行的微架构配置，以确定所需的性能、功耗和面积 (PPA) 权衡。然而，现代计算机体系结构的设计空间通常是高维和离散的，这使得设计空间探索（DSE）特别具有挑战性。微架构参数对 PPA 的影响是高度非线性的，即使很小的参数变化也会改变底层的数据流逻辑. Under this trend, designers need to search over a broad space of feasible microarchitecture configurations to identify desirable performance, power, and area (PPA) trade-offs. However, the design space of modern computer architecture is typically high-dimensional and discrete, making design space exploration (DSE) particularly challenging. The impact of microarchitecture parameters on PPA is highly nonlinear, and even small parameter changes can alter the underlying dataflow logic(Bai et al., 2023)。此外，PPA评估往往周期较长，无法及时反馈以调整优化决策. Moreover, PPA evaluation often has a long turnaround time, preventing timely feedback to adjust optimization decisions(Zhao et al., 2025)。因此，在实际处理器开发的紧张日程下，必须尽可能有效地利用稀缺的评估机会来接近帕累托前沿。. As a result, under the tight schedules of practical processor development, scarce evaluation opportunities must be used as efficiently as possible to approach the Pareto front.

为了解决这个昂贵的多目标优化（MOO）问题，工业实践通常依赖建筑师的先验知识来直接提出候选设计To address this expensive multi-objective optimization (MOO) problem, industrial practice often relies on the prior knowledge of architects to directly propose candidate designs(Naffziger et al., 2021)，但这种方法不可避免地受到个人偏见和有限专业知识的限制。早期的方法尝试使用分析模型加速 PPA 估计, but such approaches are inevitably constrained by individual bias and limited expertise. Earlier methods attempt to accelerate PPA estimation using analytical models(Karkhanis and Smith, 2007; Sun et al., 2011)。然而，它们需要大量的建模工作，并且随着微架构的发展，可移植性较差。最近的研究提出了基于机器学习的代理模型来预测工具评估，这降低了建模难度和评估成本. However, they require substantial modeling effort and suffer from poor transferability as microarchitectures evolve. Recent studies propose machine learning-based surrogate models to predict tool evaluations, which reduces both modeling difficulty and evaluation cost(Bai et al., 2021; Wang et al., 2023a; Xue et al., 2025; Yu et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023b)。尽管这些方法显着提高了搜索效率，但在微架构 DSE 的小样本范围内绝对 PPA 预测的精度有限，仍然会引入与真实情况的偏差，这可能导致结果不理想. Although these methods have significantly improved search efficiency, the limited accuracy of absolute PPA prediction in the small-sample regime of microarchitecture DSE still introduces deviations from ground truth, which can lead to suboptimal results(Zhai and Cai, 2023)。因此，现有方法仍然不足以满足现代微体系结构 DSE 的采样效率和帕累托覆盖要求。. Consequently, existing methods remain insufficient for the sampling-efficiency and Pareto-coverage requirements of modern microarchitecture DSE.

5. 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了 MicroEvo，一种用于微架构 DSE 的知识引导 LLM-MCTS 框架。通过集成Pareto感知树搜索、经验积累和状态感知扩展，MicroEvo在Pareto质量和采样效率方面优于现有方法，并且在复杂的工业级核心下保持实用性。这些结果表明，LLM 在微架构多目标优化中的价值在于将评估反馈转化为更有效的探索决策。This paper presents MicroEvo, a knowledge-guided LLM-MCTS framework for microarchitecture DSE. By integrating Pareto-aware tree search, experience accumulation, and state-aware expansion, MicroEvo outperforms prior methods in Pareto quality and sampling efficiency, and remains practical under a complex industrial-grade core. These results suggest that the value of LLMs in microarchitecture multi-objective optimization lies in turning evaluation feedback into more effective exploration decisions.
