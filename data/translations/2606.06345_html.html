使用 TRIBE v2 数据增强增强大脑到图像的解码Boosting Brain-to-Image Decoding with TRIBE v2 Data Augmentation

Yohann Benchetrit    Marlène Careil    Simon Dahan    Hubert Banville    Stéphane d’Ascoli    Jean-Rémi King Meta AI ybenchetrit@meta.com
(2026 年 6 月 4 日June 4, 2026)
摘要Abstract

大脑解码受到标记神经数据可用性的限制，并且在低数据情况下仍然具有挑战性。为了解决这个问题，我们研究是否以及何时可以通过使用预训练的 fMRI 对刺激反应的模型生成的合成数据来增强小型 fMRI 数据集来增强大脑解码。我们使用 TRIBE v2，这是一种大型编码模型，经过超过 1000 小时的 fMRI 对视频、音频和语言响应的预训练。对于每个数据集，我们评估系统网格，显示图像解码器的性能如何随着用于训练的合成数据量的变化而变化。我们的结果基于两个数据集（7T fMRI 自然场景数据集和 3T fMRI BOLD5000），显示与仅在真实数据上训练的解码器相比，Top-10 图像检索精度提高了 68%。重要的是，达到给定图像解码性能所需的增强数据的比例需要根据数据源进行调整。 令人惊讶的是，专门针对合成 fMRI 进行训练的图像解码器在某些设置中的表现可以高于预期，这表明 TRIBE v2 可以支持零样本脑到图像解码。 这些结果共同表明，功能磁共振成像对视觉、声音和语言响应的大规模模型如何为提高图像解码的数据效率提供基础。Brain decoding is limited by the availability of labeled neural data, and remains challenging in low-data regimes. To address this issue, we investigate whether and when brain decoding can be boosted by augmenting small fMRI datasets with synthetic data generated by a pretrained model of fMRI responses to stimuli. We use TRIBE v2, a large encoding model pretrained on more than 1000 hours of fMRI responses to video, audio and language. For each dataset, we evaluate systematic grids that show how the performance of image decoders varies with the amount of synthetic data used for training. Our results, based on two datasets (the 7T fMRI Natural Scenes Dataset and 3T fMRI BOLD5000), show up to 68% improvement in Top-10 image-retrieval accuracy compared to decoders trained only on real data. Importantly, the proportion of augmented data required to reach a given image decoding performance needs to be adjusted depending on the data source. Surprisingly, image decoders trained exclusively on synthetic fMRI can perform above chance in some settings, suggesting that TRIBE v2 can support zero-shot brain-to-image decoding. Together, these results show how large-scale models of the fMRI responses to sight, sound and language may provide a foundation to improve the data efficiency for image decoding.

1 简介Introduction

由于功能磁共振成像与强大的预训练视觉表示和生成模型的结合，大脑到图像的解码取得了快速进展Brain-to-image decoding has progressed rapidly thanks to the pairing of fMRI with powerful pretrained visual representations and generative models(scotti2024mindeye2; ozcelik2023brain; chen2023seeing; benchetrit2024brain; shen2019end)。 然而，其实际影响仍然受到大脑成像数据数量（和质量）的限制： 高性能解码器通常需要来自同一个人的数千个刺激响应对，这些刺激响应对是通过多次扫描会话收集的. Yet its practical reach remains restricted by the amount (and quality) of brain imaging data: High-performing decoders typically require thousands of stimulus-response pairs from the same individual, collected over many scanning sessions(banville2025scaling)。 例如，用于 fMRI 到图像重建的最流行的数据集之一是 自然场景数据集 (NSD)，其中包含每个受试者 30-40 小时的 7T fMRI. For example, one of the most popular datasets for fMRI-to-image reconstruction is the Natural Scenes Dataset (NSD), which contains 30-40 hours of 7T fMRI per subject(allen2022massive)。相比之下，大多数神经科学研究规模要小得多、噪音更大且异质性强。. In comparison, most neuroscience studies are far smaller, noisier, and heterogeneous e.g.(chang2019bold5000; shen2019end; hebart2023things)。 这种差异造成了一个主要瓶颈：现代解码方法在数据体系中最强，而很少有实验室有能力收集。. This discrepancy creates a main bottleneck: modern decoding methods are strongest precisely in the data regime that few laboratories can afford to collect.

数据增强是机器学习中对数据稀缺的标准反应，但功能磁共振成像提出了一个不同寻常的挑战。 裁剪、翻转或颜色抖动等经典增强技术不适用于神经记录，因为神经数据本质上与受试者的固定生物结构相关，而不是与 2D 图像网格的流体、平移不变结构相关。此外，“无模型”替代方案，例如噪声注入或 MixCo 等试验混合策略Data augmentation is the standard response to data scarcity in machine learning, but fMRI presents an unusual challenge. Classical augmentations such as cropping, flipping, or color jitter do not apply trivially to neural recordings, because neural data is inherently tied to the fixed biological architecture of the subject rather than the fluid, translation-invariant structure of a 2D image grid. In addition, ‘model-free’ alternatives like noise injection or trial-mixing strategies like MixCo(scotti2024mindeye; scotti2024mindeye2)可以规范解码器并提高性能，但它们对已测量的神经反应进行操作，因此不会为新的外部刺激提供响应。can regularize a decoder and improve performance, but they operate on neural responses that have already been measured and therefore do not provide responses for new external stimuli.

在这里，我们研究了一种替代方案——基于模型Here, we investigate an alternative – model-based和and刺激条件 - 数据增强：我们利用预训练编码 模型来预测大脑将如何响应新的刺激，然后使用这些预测的响应作为逆问题（即解码）的附加训练数据。stimulus-conditioned – data augmentation: we leverage a pretrained encoding model to predict how the brain would respond to new stimuli, then use those predicted responses as additional training data for the inverse problem i.e., decoding.

我们使用 TRIBE v2 在 fMRI 到图像解码上对这种方法进行基准测试We benchmark this approach on fMRI-to-image decoding using TRIBE v2(dascoli2026tribe)，一种最先进的基础模型，经过训练可以预测对视频、音频和语言的功能磁共振成像反应。尽管 TRIBE v2 没有专门针对静态图像刺激进行训练，但它的视觉路径可以通过将图像视为静态短视频来应用于图像。我们使用 TRIBE v2 合成新图像的 fMRI 响应，在真实（即测量的）和合成 fMRI 的受控混合物上训练 fMRI 到图像解码器，并评估一组保留的真实 fMRI 数据的检索性能。, a state-of-the-art foundation model trained to predict fMRI responses to video, audio, and language. Although TRIBE v2 was not trained specifically on static image stimuli, its visual pathway can be applied to images by treating them as short static videos. We use TRIBE v2 to synthesize fMRI responses for novel images, train fMRI-to-image decoders on controlled mixtures of real (i.e., measured) and synthetic fMRI, and evaluate retrieval performance on a held-out set of real fMRI data.

超越调查Beyond investigating是否whether合成功能磁共振成像有助于图像解码，我们这里的目标是解决一个更实际的问题：synthetic fMRI helps image decoding, we here aim to resolve a more practical question:在什么条件和数据机制下，功能磁共振成像到图像解码器可以利用合成数据？under what conditions and data regimes can an fMRI-to-image decoder leverage synthetic data?

通过改变两个量——真实 fMRI 的分数和相对于真实子集添加的合成的 TRIBE 生成的 fMRI 的数量——我们表明，使用合成 fMRI 数据可以显着改善中低数据状态下的图像解码，但过多的合成数据可能会饱和甚至损害性能。我们发现最佳的真实合成比在不同的数据集和解码器中是不同的。By varying two quantities – the fraction of real fMRI and the amount of synthetic, TRIBE-generated fMRI added relative to that real subset – we show that using synthetic fMRI data can substantially improve image decoding in low-to-medium data regimes, but excessive synthetic data can saturate or even hurt performance. We find that the best real-to-synthetic ratio differs across datasets and decoders.

  2. 2.

In low-data regimes, adding TRIBE-synthetic responses improves Top-10 image-retrieval accuracy by up to 68%. We further show that the same augmentation strategy can improve an fMRI-to-image reconstruction model(careil2025dynadiff).

2 相关工作Related Work

大脑解码。Brain decoding.

大脑解码的目的是从神经活动中推断感知或想象的内容。 早期的功能磁共振成像工作使用线性和贝叶斯模型解码简单的视觉特征和自然图像识别Brain decoding aims to infer perceived or imagined content from neural activity. Early fMRI work decoded simple visual features and natural image identity with linear and Bayesian models(kamitani2005decoding; kay2008identifying; naselaris2009bayesian)。 最近的系统使用预先训练的视觉表示和扩散模型来检索或重建来自大脑活动的图像， 包括MindEye. Recent systems use pretrained visual representations and diffusion models to retrieve or reconstruct images from brain activity, including MindEye(scotti2024mindeye)、MindEye2, MindEye2(scotti2024mindeye2), 脑扩散器, Brain-Diffuser(ozcelik2023brain)、MindVis, MindVis(chen2023seeing), 和 DynaDiff, and DynaDiff(careil2025dynadiff)。 正如之前的工作所示，这些进步使数据瓶颈更加明显：强大的解码器通常需要许多特定于主题的样本。 MindEye2 通过多主体预训练解决这一挑战，我们探索了一种补充方法：使用图像条件 fMRI 合成来扩展解码器的训练集，而无需获取额外的真实 fMRI。. As shown in this prior work, these advances make the data bottleneck more visible: strong decoders usually require many subject-specific samples. Where MindEye2 addresses this challenge through multi-subject pretraining, we explore a complementary approach: using image-conditioned fMRI synthesis to expand the decoder’s training set without acquiring additional real fMRI.

功能磁共振成像编码模型。fMRI encoding models.

编码模型预测来自刺激的神经反应，长期以来一直充当计算神经科学和机器学习之间的桥梁Encoding models predict neural responses from stimuli and have long served as a bridge between computational neuroscience and machine learning(kay2008identifying; naselaris2011encoding; st2023brain)。 现代编码模型使用深层视觉特征，可以预测整个视觉皮层的反应. Modern encoding models use deep visual features and can predict responses across the visual cortex(st2023brain; conwell2023can)。 TRIBE v2 将这一研究范围扩展到大规模、三模态模型，该模型经过 720 名受试者的 1,000 多个小时的 fMRI 训练. TRIBE v2 extends this line of research to a large-scale, tri-modal model trained on over 1,000 hours of fMRI from 720 subjects(dascoli2026tribe)。 重要的是，我们使用 TRIBE v2 不是作为正向模型，而是作为逆向任务（即解码）的合成 fMRI 响应生成器。. Importantly, we use TRIBE v2 not as a forward model, but as a generator of synthetic fMRI responses for the inverse task, i.e., decoding.

用于神经成像和机器学习的合成数据。Synthetic data for neuroimaging and machine learning.

合成数据增强提高了图像分类、医学成像和语言建模中的数据效率Synthetic data augmentation improves data efficiency in image classification, medical imaging, and language modeling(azizi2023synthetic; he2022synthetic; fernandez2022can; scotti2024mindeye; scotti2024mindeye2)。 在功能磁共振成像中，增强通常依赖于噪声注入、试验操作或针对目标数据训练的生成模型. In fMRI, augmentation typically relies on noise injection, trial manipulation, or generative models trained on the target data(nguyen2023blends; wang2023learning)。 我们的设置有所不同：合成样本以外部刺激为条件，并由预先训练的大脑模型生成，而无需在目标解码数据集上安装新的功能磁共振成像生成器。. Our setting is different: the synthetic samples are conditioned on external stimuli and generated by a pretrained brain model without fitting a new fMRI generator on the target decoding dataset.

5 讨论Discussion

合成功能磁共振成像增强仅在特定数据范围内有帮助。Synthetic fMRI augmentation only helps in specific data-regimes.

我们的结果表明 TRIBE v2Our results show that TRIBE v2(dascoli2026tribe)当功能磁共振成像数据稀缺时，增强可以将大脑到图像的解码提高高达 68%。正如预期的那样，这种收益既取决于可用数据的数量，也取决于所使用的合成数据的比例；超过某一点，解码性能就会饱和，甚至会随着更多合成数据的添加而恶化。实际意义是直接的：在扫描时间预算有限的情况下，应仔细调整合成数据与真实数据的比率。augmentation can improve brain-to-image decoding by up to 68% gain when fMRI data is scarce. As expected, such gains depend both on the amount of available data and on the proportion of synthetic data used; beyond a certain point, decoding performance saturates and can even deteriorate as more synthetic data are added. The practical implication is direct: with a limited scan-time budget, the synthetic-to-real data ratio should be carefully tuned.

分布外预训练。Out-of-distribution pretraining.

值得注意的是，这种方法确实有效：TRIBE v2It is quite remarkable that this approach works at all: TRIBE v2(dascoli2026tribe)既没有接受针对静态图像（或静态图像嵌入）的大脑反应的训练，也没有接受解码的训练。它的视觉通路是根据大脑对长电影的反应以及音频和语言进行训练的。在这里，我们简单地将静态图像转换为短片。因此，目前的结果表明，TRIBE 捕获了从自然多模态刺激转移到更受控制的快速串行视觉图像呈现的视觉反应模式。同时，这种不匹配也可以解释为什么增强收益饱和以及为什么必须校准最佳合成与真实比率。is neither trained on brain responses to static images (or static image embedding) nor is it trained for decoding. Its visual pathway is trained on brain responses to long movies, jointly with audio and language. Here, we simply converted the static images into short movie clips. Consequently, the present results suggest that TRIBE captures patterns of visual responses that transfer from naturalistic multimodal stimulation to a more controlled rapid-serial-visual-presentation of images. At the same time, this mismatch may also explain why augmentation benefits saturate and why the optimal synthetic-to-real ratio must be calibrated.

为什么增强可以超过全真实参考？Why can augmentation exceed the full-real reference?

某些配置超出了 100% 真实参考值。 这不应被理解为合成功能磁共振成像比真实功能磁共振成像包含更多特定于主题的信息。 一个更合理的解释是，TRIBE v2 增加了刺激多样性和群体水平的视觉反应结构，从而规范了 fMRI 到嵌入的映射，特别是当真实训练集较小或特殊时。 由于增强池从目标数据集中排除了图像，并且评估始终在真实的 fMRI 上进行，因此这些增益不是由与评估刺激重叠或通过对合成数据进行测试而引起的。Some configurations exceed the 100% real reference. This should not be read as synthetic fMRI containing more subject-specific information than real fMRI. A more plausible explanation is that TRIBE v2 adds stimulus diversity and population-level visual response structure that regularize the fMRI-to-embedding mapping, especially when the real training set is small or idiosyncratic. Because the augmentation pool excludes images from the target dataset and evaluation is always on real fMRI, these gains are not caused by overlap with evaluation stimuli or by testing on synthetic data.

与主体无关的增强有其局限性。Subject-agnostic augmentation has limits.

在这里，TRIBE v2 预测平均受试者大脑反应（即没有目标受试者适应）。 这使得增强可以广泛部署，但这也意味着综合反应缺乏个体特异性的解剖结构和反应特质。 纯合成参考抓住了这一限制：高概率解码是可能的，但最强的结果仍然需要来自目标受试者的真实功能磁共振成像。 未来的工作可以将操作网格协议与特定主题的 TRIBE 适应、学习的合成数据加权或多主题解码器预训练相结合Here, TRIBE v2 predicts an average-subject brain response (i.e. there is no target-subject adaptation). This makes the augmentation broadly deployable, but it also means synthetic responses lack individual-specific anatomy and response idiosyncrasies. The synthetic-only reference captures this limitation: above-chance decoding is possible, but the strongest results still require real fMRI from the target subjects. Future work could combine the operating grid protocol with subject-specific TRIBE adaptation, learned synthetic-data weighting, or multi-subject decoder pretraining(scotti2024mindeye2).

局限性。Limitations.

第一First，尽管我们在两个数据集上复制了我们的发现，但后者的受试者数量很少。因此，扩展到其他数据集（包括其他模式）仍有待进一步评估。鉴于 TRIBE v2 是多模式的，我们的方法不需要进行重大改变。, although we replicate our findings on two datasets, the latter have a small number of subjects. Extension to additional datasets, including other modalities thus remains to be further evaluated. Given TRIBE v2 is multimodal, our approach should not require major changes.第二Second，主要任务侧重于从 DINOv2-small 嵌入中检索图像, the primary task focuses on image retrieval from a DINOv2-small embedding(oquab2023dinov2);图像重建仅通过第 2 节中的 DynaDiff 实验进行初步评估; image reconstruction is evaluated only preliminarily through the DynaDiff experiments in Section4.6。这种选择受到以下事实的限制：大多数可用的功能磁共振成像到图像模型（例如. This choice was constrained by the fact that most available fMRI-to-image models (e.g.scotti2024mindeye; scotti2024mindeye2）基于与时间无关的 fMRI beta 图，而 TRIBE v2 生成动态 fMRI 信号。将目前的测试扩展到其他 fMRI 到图像模型非常重要，例如，通过与 NSD 匹配的预处理从 TRIBE v2 导出 beta 图) are based on time-independent fMRI beta maps, while TRIBE v2 generates dynamic fMRI signals. It will be important to extend the present tests to other fMRI-to-image models, e.g., by deriving beta maps from TRIBE v2 with preprocessing matched to NSD(allen2022massive)和大胆5000and BOLD5000(chang2019bold5000).

更广泛的影响。Broader impact.

如果可靠，基于模型的功能磁共振成像增强可以使图像解码研究减少对非常大的扫描时间预算的依赖，因此更容易跨实验室和人群进行研究。 同时，结果不应被解释为在没有任何真实数据的情况下即可进行解码：最佳性能仍然依赖于特定于受试者的真实 fMRI 响应。If reliable, model-based fMRI augmentation could make image-decoding research less dependent on very large scan-time budgets and therefore easier to study across laboratories and populations. At the same time, the results should not be interpreted as enabling decoding without any real data: the best performance still relies on subject-specific real fMRI responses.

6 结论Conclusion

合成功能磁共振成像增强可以减少对大量扫描时间预算的依赖，从而标志着向更容易获得的神经成像的转变。虽然 TRIBE v2 证明自然主义的多模态预训练可以有效地规范数据稀缺情况下的解码，但该领域的未来在于平衡这些一般人群水平的先验与特定主题的细微差别。最终，校准合成多样性和真实神经信号之间的协同作用对于构建有效的大脑活动解码器至关重要。Synthetic fMRI augmentation could mark a shift toward more accessible neuroimaging by reducing the reliance on massive scan-time budgets. While TRIBE v2 demonstrates that naturalistic, multimodal pretraining can effectively regularize decoding in data-scarce regimes, the future of the field lies in balancing these general population-level priors with subject-specific nuances. Ultimately, calibrating the synergy between synthetic diversity and real neural signals will be essential for building efficient decoders of brain activity.
