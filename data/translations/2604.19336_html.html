摘要Abstract

在线联合学习（OFL）已成为一种流行的框架，可在不损害客户隐私的情况下对连续数据流进行去中心化决策。然而，标准 OFL 中假设的对手模型通常排除了并行化的任何潜在好处。此外，它未能充分捕获 OFL 问题中统计变化的不同来源。在本文中，我们通过集成随机扩展对手（SEA）来扩展 OFL 范式。在此框架下，随着时间的推移，损失函数在各个客户之间保持固定。然而，攻击者每次都会动态且独立地选择每个客户端的数据分布。我们建议Online federated learning (OFL) has emerged as a popular framework for decentralized decision-making over continuous data streams without compromising client privacy. However, the adversary model assumed in standard OFL typically precludes any potential benefits of parallelization. Further, it fails to adequately capture the different sources of statistical variation in OFL problems. In this paper, we extend the OFL paradigm by integrating a stochastically extended adversary (SEA). Under this framework, the loss function remains fixed across clients over time. However, the adversary dynamically and independently selects the data distribution for each client at each time. We propose the联邦东南亚协会FedSEA算法来解决这个问题，该算法在客户端利用在线随机梯度下降，并通过服务器进行定期全局聚合。我们在一段时间内对全球网络遗憾设定了界限algorithm to solve this problem, which utilizes online stochastic gradient descent at the clients, along with periodic global aggregation via the server. 1 简介Introduction

在线学习。Online Learning.

随着大数据的快速增长，当数据不断到达和演变时，传统的批量学习范式面临着巨大的局限性。在批量学习中，主要目标是使用静态数据集学习模型，该模型可以很好地推广到未见过的数据。然而，金融市场预测、自动驾驶汽车和推荐系统等现实世界的应用With the rapid growth of big data, traditional batch learning paradigms face significant limitations when data arrives and evolves continuously. In batch learning, the primary aim is to learn a model using a static dataset that generalizes well to unseen data. However, real-world applications such as financial market forecasting, autonomous vehicles, and recommendation systems(hazan2023introductiononlineconvexoptimization)通常会获得连续的数据流，并且底层分布会随着时间的推移而变化often get continuous streams of data, and the underlying distribution shifts over time(conceptdrift)。在线学习（OL）范式解决了动态环境中的学习问题。在线学习器不是从头开始重新训练模型，而是随着新数据的到来逐步更新其模型. The online learning (OL) paradigm addresses this problem of learning in dynamic environments. Instead of retraining the model from scratch, an online learner updates its model incrementally as new data arrives(hazan2023introductiononlineconvexoptimization; orabona2025modernintroductiononlinelearning)。与批量学习不同，OL 的目标是生成一系列模型，最大限度地减少学习者的累积遗憾，定义为学习者的累积损失与具有系统动力学先验知识的理想化比较器的累积损失之间的差异. Unlike batch learning, the goal in OL is to produce a sequence of models that minimizes the cumulative regret of the learner, defined as the difference between the cumulative loss of the learner and that of an idealized comparator with prior knowledge of the system dynamics(OL_survey).

向联邦学习的转变。The Shift to Federated Learning.

标准 OL 框架假设传入的数据流可以在中央服务器上收集。随着数据生成迅速转移到分散的边缘设备The standard OL frameworks assume that the incoming data streams can be collected at a centralized server. As data generation rapidly shifts to decentralized edge devices(Edgecomputingsurvey)，集中化带来了重大挑战，例如增加的通信开销和严重的隐私问题。 联合学习 (FL) 通过确保原始数据永远不会离开客户端设备来缓解这些问题, centralization introduces significant challenges such as increased communication overhead and severe privacy concerns. Federated learning (FL) mitigates these issues by ensuring that the raw data never leaves the client devices(FL_McMahana)。客户端使用本地数据来训练模型并间歇性地与服务器通信，服务器又定期将这些本地更新聚合到全局模型中. The clients use their local data to train models and communicate with the server intermittently, which in turn periodically aggregates these local updates into a global model(FL_McMahana)。通过分布式训练过程，FL 通过利用本地计算资源确保数据隐私并减少通信开销. By distributing the training process, FL ensures data privacy and reduces communication overhead by leveraging local computational resources(FL_monograpg_kairouz)。 FL 已成功应用于医疗保健、物联网 (IoT) 和分散式无线通信网络等各个领域. FL has been successfully used in various sectors like healthcare, Internet of Things (IoT), and decentralized wireless communication networks(FL_overview_strategies)。然而，FL 的现有工作主要集中在批量学习问题上. However, the existing work in FL primarily focuses on batch learning problems(FL_McMahana; SCAFFOLD; fedprox; khaled_tighter_theory_of_LSGD; stich2019localsgdconvergesfast).

为了克服全梯度反馈的局限性并获得并行化带来的线性加速的好处，我们需要更现实的假设。随机扩展对手 (SEA) 框架最初由 Sachs 等人提出。To overcome the limitations of full gradient feedback and capture the benefits of linear speedup resulting from parallelization, we need more realistic assumptions. The Stochastically Extended Adversary (SEA) framework, initially introduced by Sachs et al.(sachs_SEA)提供了一个强大的替代方案。在 SEA 设置中，学习者和对手在每个时间步同时进行动作：学习者做出决策，对手选择数据分布。然后双方观察从所选分布中抽取的随机样本，学习者根据该样本遭受损失provides a robust alternative. In the SEA setting, the learner and the adversary make simultaneous moves at each time step: the learner plays a decision, and the adversary selects a data distribution. Both parties then observe a random sample drawn from the chosen distribution, and the learner incurs a loss based on this sample(sachs_SEA; Chen_SEA_OMD)。 SEA 框架优雅地弥合了随机优化（对手被限制为单一固定分布）和对抗性在线优化（对手选择 Dirac delta 分布）之间的差距。. The SEA framework gracefully bridges the gap between stochastic optimization (adversary is restricted to a single fixed distribution) and adversarial online optimization (adversary selects Dirac delta distributions).
