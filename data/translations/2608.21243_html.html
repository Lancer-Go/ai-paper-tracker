在顺序推荐中采用知识图进行行为去噪Adapting Knowledge Graphs for Behavior Denoising in Sequential Recommendation

Zichun Jin    Zihan Zhou    Yinan Liu    Bin Wang    Xiaochun Yang
摘要Abstract

顺序推荐根据用户的交互历史记录预测下一个项目，但并非每次交互都提供同样的信息。真实日志将持久偏好与临时需求、探索和偶然行为结合起来，因此某些交互可能会扭曲历史表示或提供不可靠的监督。现有的去噪方法主要根据共现、顺序或模型预测来判断这种相互作用，而没有来自项目之间关系的明确证据。知识图（KG）提供了这一证据，但项目流行度、图度、覆盖不均匀和广泛共享的实体可能会夸大连通性和偏差可靠性估计。 在这里我们介绍Sequential recommendation predicts the next item from a user’s interaction history, but not every interaction is equally informative. Real logs combine persistent preferences with temporary needs, exploration, and incidental behavior, so some interactions can distort history representations or provide unreliable supervision. Existing denoising methods judge such interactions mainly from co-occurrence, order, or model predictions, without explicit evidence from relations between items. Knowledge graphs (KGs) offer this evidence, but item popularity, graph degree, uneven coverage, and widely shared entities can inflate connectivity and bias reliability estimates. Here we present适配KGAdaptedKG，它为每个训练示例导出校准的 KG 证据，而无需向推荐模型添加图形表示。它首先将观察到的上下文与结构匹配的替代方案进行比较，以识别异常突出的关系路径，并使用它们构建本地知识图谱视图。然后，它将每个交互与结构匹配的参考项进行比较，以校准其在该视图中的支持。由此产生的保留系数门控历史表征并重新衡量目标损失。所有特定于样本的分数都是使用训练离线计算的 相互作用和固定的KG，所以主干保持不变，没有 推理时需要KG访问。实验显示了标准顺序推荐器和多个行为去噪顺序推荐器的增益。, which derives calibrated KG evidence for each training example without adding graph representations to the recommendation model. It first compares the observed context with structurally matched alternatives to identify relational paths that are unusually prominent and uses them to build a local KG view. It then compares each interaction with structurally matched reference items to calibrate its support within that view. The resulting retention coefficients gate historical representations and reweight target losses. All sample-specific scores are computed offline using training interactions and a fixed KG, so the backbone remains unchanged and no KG access is required at inference. Experiments show gains with a standard sequential recommender and multiple behavior-denoising sequential recommenders.

1 简介Introduction

顺序推荐依赖于用户的交互历史记录 预测下一个项目Sequential recommendation relies on a user’s interaction history to predict the next itemKang and McAuley 2018; Quadrana et al. 2019，然而这段历史中的相互作用并没有 提供同样可靠的用户偏好证据, yet the interactions in this history do not provide equally reliable evidence of user preferenceSun et al. 2021。真实世界的日志 反映长期偏好、短期需求的混合体， 探索和偶然行为，使个体互动 对于序列建模的价值不均匀. Real-world logs reflect a mixture of long-term preferences, short-term needs, exploration, and incidental behavior, making individual interactions unevenly valuable for sequence modelingChen et al. 2022。相互作用较弱 与同一序列中的其他相互作用相关联的可能都 扰乱历史表述并提供不稳定的监督 当用作训练目标时. An interaction weakly associated with other interactions in the same sequence may both disrupt historical representations and provide unstable supervision when used as a training targetSun et al. 2021。现有的去噪方法主要依靠 基于共现、顺序或模型预测来识别此类 互动. Existing denoising methods mainly rely on co-occurrence, order, or model predictions to identify such interactionsLin et al. 2023; Sun et al. 2023; Zhang et al. 2024，但缺乏明确的项目间关系的证据 做出这样的判断。, but lack evidence from explicit inter-item relations for making this judgment.

知识图对连接项目的实体和关系进行编码 因此提供了填补这一空白的潜在方法Knowledge graphs encode entities and relations connecting items and therefore offer a potential means of filling this gapHogan et al. 2022; Guo et al. 2022。不过，比较有钱 图中的路径连通性并不一定意味着更相关 项目之间的关系. However, richer path connectivity in the graph does not necessarily imply more relevant relations between itemsShomer et al. 2023。人气较高的物品，KG较高 学位，或者更广泛的覆盖范围自然会接纳更多的路径，而 许多项目共享的实体也可以引发广泛的联系。使用 这些连接可能直接导致可靠性估计偏向 紧密相连的项目和常见的关系模式。路径是否为当前交互提供独特的支持 因此，必须根据结构上可比较的参考进行评估。. Items with higher popularity, higher KG degree, or more extensive coverage naturally admit more paths, while entities shared by many items can also induce broad connections. Using these connections directly may bias reliability estimation toward richly connected items and common relational patterns. Whether a path provides distinctive support for the current interaction must therefore be assessed against structurally comparable references.

基于这个想法，我们提出Based on this idea, we propose适配KGAdaptedKG。而不是注射 KG表示进入推荐主干，它使用两个 具有不同执行角色的结构匹配阶段 依次。这些阶段将全局 KG 中的连接转换为 个体行为示例的 KG 一致性证据并得出 保守的保留系数来调制训练信号。在 第一阶段，具有相似结构的替代上下文 属性表征关系的背景分布 路径。该方法保留并加权更重要的关系模式 相对于匹配背景在当前上下文中突出， 形成特定于示例的本地 KG 视图。固定了这个观点后， 第二阶段使用具有相似结构属性的参考项 校准这些关系模式为 评估互动。所得保留系数门 历史交互表示和重新加权目标损失， 分别。所有 KG 适应和样本特定评分均离线进行 使用训练交互和固定 KG，而不改变 主干架构或在推理时需要 KG 访问。实验表明. Rather than injecting KG representations into the recommendation backbone, it uses two structural-matching stages with distinct roles that are executed sequentially. These stages convert connections in the global KG into KG-consistency evidence for individual behavioral examples and derive conservative retention coefficients to modulate training signals. In the first stage, alternative contexts with similar structural attributes characterize the background distribution of relational paths. The method retains and weights relational patterns that are more prominent in the current context relative to the matched background, forming an example-specific local KG view. With this view fixed, the second stage uses reference items with similar structural attributes to calibrate the support that these relational patterns provide for the evaluated interaction. The resulting retention coefficients gate historical interaction representations and reweight target losses, respectively. All KG adaptation and sample-specific scoring are performed offline using training interactions and a fixed KG, without changing the backbone architecture or requiring KG access at inference. Experiments show that适配KGAdaptedKG应用于时会产生改进 标准顺序推荐模型和多重推荐模型 行为去噪模型。yields improvements when applied to a standard sequential recommendation model and multiple behavior-denoising models.

3 方法论Methodology

原始 KG 连接性无法在示例之间直接进行比较。包含流行或高度项目的上下文可能包含许多共同路径，而易于连接的候选者可能会出于同样的原因获得高度支持。Raw KG connectivity is not directly comparable across examples. A context containing popular or high-degree items may contain many common paths, while an easily connected candidate may receive high support for the same reason.适配KGAdaptedKG按顺序处理这些效果：匹配空上下文选择从可比上下文中脱颖而出的路径；固定这些路径后，匹配的参考将支持转换为交互级别的保留系数。handles these effects in sequence: matched-null contexts select paths that stand out from comparable contexts; with those paths fixed, matched references convert support into an interaction-level retention coefficient.
