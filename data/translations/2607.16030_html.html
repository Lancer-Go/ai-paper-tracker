摘要Abstract

量子连续学习旨在使量子学习模型能够获取顺序任务，同时保留以前学到的知识。 然而，在非平稳任务分布下训练时，变分量子分类器（VQC）仍然容易遭受灾难性遗忘。 在这项工作中，我们提出了量子弹性权重合并（QEWC），这是一种基于量子费希尔信息（QFI）的正则化框架，用于减轻量子连续学习中的遗忘。 与基于经典 Fisher 信息 (CFI) 的传统弹性权重合并（CFI）通过测量相关的输出统计定义参数重要性相比，QEWC 使用 QFI 来量化参数化量子态的内在敏感性。 该公式提供了信息几何视角，其中参数重要性由量子态流形对参数变化的局部响应决定。 我们使用在顺序二元分类任务（包括经典图像分类任务和量子相位分类任务）上训练的 VQC 来评估 QEWC。 我们的模拟表明，不规则的顺序训练会导致严重的灾难性遗忘，而基于 CFI 的 EWC 和基于 QFI 的 QEWC 都大大提高了对先前学习任务的保留。 进一步的机理分析表明，这两种方法产生了不同的正则化几何结构。 基于 CFI 的惩罚更有选择性地作用于测量敏感参数方向，而基于 QFI 的惩罚对参数空间施加更密集的状态几何约束。 这种区别导致了顺序训练期间不同的稳定性-可塑性行为。 在去极化噪声下，CFI 值受到测量统计数据退化的强烈抑制，而 QFI 保留了与噪声参数化量子态相关的更稳定的灵敏度结构。 这些结果将 QEWC 确立为一个物理驱动的框架，用于通过底层量子态的几何结构来研究和减轻量子连续学习中的遗忘。Quantum continual learning seeks to enable quantum learning models to acquire sequential tasks while retaining previously learned knowledge. However, variational quantum classifiers (VQCs) remain vulnerable to catastrophic forgetting when trained under nonstationary task distributions. In this work, we propose quantum elastic weight consolidation (QEWC), a quantum Fisher information (QFI)-informed regularization framework for mitigating forgetting in quantum continual learning. In contrast to conventional elastic weight consolidation based on classical Fisher information (CFI), which defines parameter importance through measurement-dependent output statistics, QEWC uses the QFI to quantify the intrinsic sensitivity of the parameterized quantum state. This formulation provides an information-geometric perspective in which parameter importance is determined by the local response of the quantum state manifold to parameter variations. We evaluate QEWC using VQCs trained on sequential binary classification tasks, including classical image-classification tasks and a quantum phase-classification task. Our simulations show that unregularized sequential training leads to severe catastrophic forgetting, whereas both CFI-based EWC and QFI-based QEWC substantially improve the retention of previously learned tasks. Further mechanistic analyses reveal that the two approaches induce distinct regularization geometries. The CFI-based penalty acts more selectively on measurement-sensitive parameter directions, whereas the QFI-based penalty imposes a denser state-geometric constraint over the parameter space. This distinction leads to different stability–plasticity behaviors during sequential training. Under depolarizing noise, the CFI values are strongly suppressed by degraded measurement statistics, while the QFI retains a more stable sensitivity structure associated with the noisy parameterized quantum state. These results establish QEWC as a physically motivated framework for studying and mitigating forgetting in quantum continual learning through the geometry of the underlying quantum state.

边界是针对完整任务平均 QFI 矩阵规定的。在数值实验中，我们使用其对角线近似来提高计算效率。该对角代理保留了参数方面的敏感性，同时忽略了不同参数方向之间的相关性。因此，它应该被视为完整 QFI 度量的实用坐标近似，而不是完全坐标不变的几何惩罚。The bounds are stated for the full task-averaged QFI matrix. In the numerical experiments, we use its diagonal approximation for computational efficiency. This diagonal surrogate retains parameter-wise sensitivities while neglecting correlations between different parameter directions. It should therefore be viewed as a practical coordinate-wise approximation to the full QFI metric, rather than as a fully coordinate-invariant geometric penalty.

6 EWC 和 QEWC 框架的机制分析Mechanistic Analysis of the EWC and the QEWC frameworks

我们进一步分析了 EWC 和 QEWC 框架如何减轻灾难性遗忘，我们使用三种互补的诊断来分析量子分类器的内部优化动态。这些诊断包括 Fisher 信息 (FI) 值在参数空间中的分布、按 Fisher 重要性分组的平均参数位移以及输出分布的统计可分离性。它们共同提供了一个多尺度视图，将正则化几何与参数级动态连接起来，并最终连接到输出级任务行为。We further analyze how the EWC and QEWC frameworks mitigate catastrophic forgetting, we analyze the internal optimization dynamics of the quantum classifier using three complementary diagnostics. These diagnostics consist of the distribution of Fisher information (FI) values across parameter space, the average parameter displacement grouped by Fisher importance, and the statistical separability of the output distributions. Together, they provide a multi-scale view that connects the regularization geometry to parameter-level dynamics and, ultimately, to output-level task behavior.

FI 分布首先确定哪些参数方向受到惩罚以及正则化器对它们的加权强度。然而，仅 FI 值并不能确定这些惩罚是否在训练期间有效地约束优化器。 因此，我们分析按 Fisher 重要性分组的参数位移，以测试强加的正则化几何是否会导致参数更新的差异抑制。最后，由于受约束的参数运动不一定保证保留特定于任务的表示，因此我们检查输出分布以确定所得的动态是否维持或降低每个任务的类可分离性。总而言之，这些诊断阐明了 FI 指标的选择如何塑造优化轨迹，从而影响顺序任务性能。The FI distribution first identifies which parameter directions are penalized and how strongly they are weighted by the regularizer. However, the FI values alone do not determine whether these penalties effectively constrain the optimizer during training. We therefore analyze parameter displacements grouped by Fisher importance to test whether the imposed regularization geometry leads to differential suppression of parameter updates. Finally, because constrained parameter motion does not necessarily guarantee preservation of task-specific representations, we examine the output distributions to determine whether the resulting dynamics maintain or degrade class separability for each task. Taken together, these diagnostics clarify how the choice of FI metric shapes the optimization trajectory and consequently affects sequential task performance.

8 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们介绍了 QEWC，一种基于 QFI 的正则化框架，用于减轻量子连续学习中的灾难性遗忘。 为了进行比较研究，我们考虑了三种训练策略，包括非正则化的 VQC 基线、基于 CFI 的 EWC 框架和提出的基于 QFI 的 QEWC 框架。 我们将这些方法应用于顺序二元分类任务，并评估它们在无噪声和噪声模拟设置中的性能。 通过一系列的比较分析，我们不仅证明了基于正则化的方法可以大大减轻 VQC 中的灾难性遗忘，而且还表明 QFI 通过将参数重要性的概念从依赖于测量的输出统计转移到参数化量子态的内在几何结构，为量子连续学习提供了独特的视角。In this work, we introduce QEWC, a QFI-informed regularization framework for mitigating catastrophic forgetting in quantum continual learning. For comparative studies, we consider three training strategies, including an unregularized VQC baseline, the CFI-based EWC framework, and the proposed QFI-based QEWC framework. We apply these methods to sequential binary classification tasks and evaluate their performance in both noiseless and noisy simulation settings. Through a series of comparative analyses, we not only demonstrate that regularization-based approaches can substantially mitigate catastrophic forgetting in VQC, but also show that the QFI provides a distinct perspective on quantum continual learning by shifting the notion of parameter importance from measurement-dependent output statistics to the intrinsic geometry of parameterized quantum states.

值得注意的是，量子模型中的持续学习提出了一个独特的挑战，因为参数重要性不仅是经典输出分布的属性，而且还与参数化电路生成的基础量子态的几何形状有关。 传统的 EWC 框架依赖于 CFI，CFI 明确依赖于所选的测量统计数据，因此提供了依赖于测量的灵敏度特征。 为了解决这个限制，我们用 QFI 代替 CFI 正则化度量，QFI 量化相邻量子态的局部可区分性，并提供与测量无关的状态敏感性描述。 我们进一步提供了局部理论论证，表明 QFI 加权参数位移控制分类器生成的量子态的漂移，并通过固定的读出可观测量和 Softmax 映射控制输出概率的漂移和先前任务的交叉熵损失。Notably, continual learning in quantum models presents a distinct challenge because parameter importance is not merely a property of the classical output distribution, but is also tied to the geometry of the underlying quantum state generated by the parameterized circuit. The conventional EWC framework relies on the CFI, which depends explicitly on the chosen measurement statistics and therefore provides a measurement-dependent characterization of sensitivity. To address this limitation, we replace the CFI regularization metric with the QFI, which quantifies the local distinguishability of neighboring quantum states and provides a measurement-independent description of state sensitivity. We further provide a local theoretical justification showing that the QFI-weighted parameter displacement controls the drift of the quantum states generated by the classifier and, through fixed readout observables followed by the softmax map, the drift of the output probabilities and the previous-task cross-entropy loss.

此外，我们的机制分析表明，EWC 和 QEWC 框架通过性质不同的参数级动态来减轻遗忘。 EWC 使用的 CFI 识别测量敏感参数的相对稀疏的子集，而 QEWC 使用的 QFI 对参数空间施加更密集的状态几何约束。 通过分析 FI 分布、参数位移和输出分布可分离性，我们表明这些不同的正则化几何结构会在顺序训练期间导致不同的稳定性-可塑性行为。 这表明 QEWC 不仅仅是传统 EWC 的直接量子模拟，而且是一种状态几何方法，用于理解量子模型如何保留先前学习的任务表示。Furthermore, our mechanistic analysis reveals that the EWC and QEWC frameworks mitigate forgetting through qualitatively different parameter-level dynamics. The CFI used by EWC identifies a comparatively sparse subset of measurement-sensitive parameters, whereas the QFI used by QEWC imposes a denser state-geometric constraint over the parameter space. By analyzing FI distributions, parameter displacements, and output distribution separability, we show that these distinct regularization geometries lead to distinct stability–plasticity behaviors during sequential training. This indicates that QEWC is not merely a direct quantum analogue of conventional EWC, but rather a state-geometric approach to understanding how quantum models preserve previously learned task representations.

总之，我们的结果表明，QFI 为量子模型中的持续学习提供了物理激励和几何意义的度量。 通过底层量子态的局部敏感性来表征参数重要性，QEWC 为传统的基于 CFI 的合并提供了补充视角，该合并与测量引起的输出统计相关。 我们的结果进一步表明，这种状态几何观点对于分析噪声量子动力学下的遗忘特别有用，其中依赖于测量的 FI 可以被强烈抑制，同时 QFI 保留更稳定的灵敏度结构。 同时，QEWC 的实际有效性取决于多种实现选择，包括 QFI 的估计、对角线近似的使用、噪声模型以及控制稳定性-塑性平衡的正则化强度。 这些观察表明，量子模型中的遗忘不仅应该通过输出级性能来研究，还应该通过参数化量子态流形的几何结构来研究。In summary, our results demonstrate that QFI provides a physically motivated and geometrically meaningful metric for continual learning in quantum model. By characterizing parameter importance through the local sensitivity of the underlying quantum states, QEWC offers a complementary perspective to conventional CFI-based consolidation, which is tied to measurement-induced output statistics. Our results further indicate that this state-geometric viewpoint is particularly useful for analyzing forgetting under noisy quantum dynamics, where measurement-dependent FI can be strongly suppressed while the QFI retains a more stable sensitivity structure. At the same time, the practical effectiveness of QEWC depends on several implementation choices, including the estimation of the QFI, the use of diagonal approximations, the noise model, and the regularization strength controlling the stability–plasticity balance. These observations suggest that forgetting in quantum models should be studied not only through output-level performance, but also through the geometry of the parameterized quantum state manifold.

附录 附录 B。Appendix Appendix B.量子费舍尔矩阵的对角线近似Diagonal Approximation of the Quantum Fisher Matrix

在我们的主要数值模拟中，我们在 QEWC 框架内采用 QFI 矩阵的对角线近似。 完整的QFI矩阵表征了不同参数方向之间的相关性；然而，其计算和内存需求随着可训练参数数量的增加而迅速增加。 对于深度变分量子电路来说，这个问题变得尤其严重，其中电路参数的数量随着量子位数量和变分层数量的增加而增加。 因此，从实际的角度来看，我们通过仅保留其对角线条目来近似 QFI 矩阵。In our main numerical simulations, we employ a diagonal approximation of the QFI matrix within the QEWC framework. The full QFI matrix characterizes correlations among different parameter directions; however, its computational and memory requirements increase rapidly with the number of trainable parameters. This issue becomes particularly severe for deep variational quantum circuits, where the number of circuit parameters grows with both the number of qubits and the number of variational layers. Therefore, from a practical standpoint, we approximate the QFI matrix by retaining only its diagonal entries.

在对角 QFI 实现中，我们通过评估任务平均 QFI 矩阵并仅保留其对角条目用于 QEWC 惩罚来构建参数重要性权重。 所得的对角正则化器保留了与每个单独参数方向相关的局部灵敏度，同时忽略了不同参数之间的非对角相关性。 这种近似减少了表示和应用完整 QFI 矩阵的计算和存储成本，同时保留对角二次正则化项所需的参数重要性信息。In the diagonal-QFI implementation, we construct the parameter-wise importance weights by evaluating the task-averaged QFI matrix and retaining only its diagonal entries for use in the QEWC penalty. The resulting diagonal regularizer preserves the local sensitivity associated with each individual parameter direction, while neglecting off-diagonal correlations between different parameters. This approximation reduces the computational and storage costs of representing and applying the full QFI matrix, while retaining the parameter-wise importance information required by the diagonal quadratic regularization term.
