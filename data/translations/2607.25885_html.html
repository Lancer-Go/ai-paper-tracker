基于机器学习的非常规油田气举优化工作流程A Machine-Learning-Based Gas Lift Optimization Workflow for Unconventional Fields

苗莎*Sha(Sasha) Miao*1, 亚历山德拉·文代蒂, Alexandra Vendetti2, 洛根·斯马特, Logan Smart2, 贡塔·乔姆恰勒姆, Gunta Chomchalerm1, 杨晨, Yang Chen1克里斯托弗·弗雷泽, Christopher Frazier2达斯汀·哈拉尔森, Dustin Haralson2, 杰里米·索伦森, Jeremy Sorenson2, 小马, Xiao Ma1, 孙华飞, Huafei Sun1, 亚伦·辛恩, Aaron Shinn2, 郑海宁, Haining Zheng1, 吴晓辉, Xiao-Hui Wu1, 徐鹏, Peng Xu1, 1. 埃克森美孚技术与工程公司, 2. 埃克森美孚上游公司, 1. 摘要Abstract

在本文中，我们提出了一种使用机器学习（ML）的自动化数据驱动工作流程，用于非常规领域的气举优化。该工作流程集成了准确预测气举性能曲线的机器学习模型和贝叶斯优化框架，以在设施容量的约束下求解最佳注气速率。 ML 模型利用历史生产时间序列数据，无需井下仪表或多速率试井。我们在巴肯 5 个井场的 30 口井上试行了这一工作流程，平均产量提高了 5% 以上。随着试点的成功，我们现在已在巴肯的 200 多口气举和柱塞辅助气举 (PAGL) 井中全面部署了这一工作流程。此外，本文提出的基于机器学习的气举优化工作流程对于由于成本或设施限制而无法获得/不可行井下数据或多速率测试的其他资产来说是一种有效且经济的解决方案。In this paper, we present an automated data-driven workflow using Machine Learning (ML) for gas lift optimization in unconventional fields. This workflow integrates a ML model that accurately forecasts the Gas Lift Performance Curve, and a Bayesian Optimization Framework to solve for the optimal gas injection rates under the constraints of facility capacity. The ML model leverages the historical production time series data without requiring downhole gauges or multi-rate well tests. We piloted this workflow on 30 wells across 5 well pads in Bakken and obtained >5% production uplift on average. With the success of the pilot, we have now fully-deployed this workflow in Bakken across 200+ gas lift and plunger-assisted gas lift (PAGL) wells. Moreover, the ML-based gas lift optimization workflow presented in this paper is an effective and economic solution for other assets where downhole data or multi-rate testing are not available/feasible due to cost or facility constraints.

简介Introduction

对于非常规油田，由于油藏压力迅速下降，自然自流井生产几个月后，产油率通常会急剧下降。然后需要不同种类的人工举升方法来继续生产。气举（GL）是非常规领域最经济、最常用的人工举升方法之一。在气举作业期间，气体通过阀门从环空注入油管。气体与流体混合降低了混合物密度，从而降低了静水压力梯度，降低了流动的井底压力，增加了压力下降，从而最终增加了石油流量（Brown 1967）。当 GL 井处于半稳定状态时，其生产率与注气量之间的关系通常遵循一条称为“气举性能曲线”的钟形曲线（图 1）。在相对较高的注气速率下，摩擦压力损失开始与静水压头的减小相竞争，导致生产率降低。此外，气举性能曲线随着时间的推移而演变并趋于成熟。因此，重点业务之一For unconventional fields, oil rates typically drop sharply after a few months of production in naturally flowing wells due to the rapid decline of the reservoir pressure. Different kinds of artificial lift methods are then required to continue the production. Gas Lift (GL) is one of the most economic and commonly used artificial lift methods in unconventional fields. During a gas-lift operation, gas is injected into the tubing through a valve from the annulus. The gas mixing with the fluid reduces the mixture density, which decreases the hydrostatic pressure gradient, lowers the flowing bottom hole pressure, increases the pressure draw down and thus ultimately increases the oil flow rate (Brown 1967). When a GL well is in a semi-steady state, its production rate versus gas injection rate typically follows a bell-shaped curve called “Gas Lift Performance Curve” (Figure 1). At a relatively high gas injection rate, the frictional pressure loss starts to compete with the hydrostatic head reduction, resulting in lower production rate. Moreover, the Gas Lift Performance Curve evolves over time as well matures. Therefore, one of the key business

基于物理和数据驱动的方法是解决气举优化问题的两种典型方法（Looi et al., 2023; Borden et al., 2016,Physics-based and data-driven approaches are the two typical types of methods solving for Gas Lift Optimization problem (Looi et al., 2023, Borden et al., 2016,Masud 等人，2023；Gambaretto 和 Rashid，2023；Maut 和 Prakash，2023）。Masud et al., 2023, Gambaretto & Rashid 2023, Maut & Prakash, 2023).前一种方法通常需要过多的成本/精力才能获得动态物理特性的良好估计并确保整个油井生命周期的模型得到良好校准。此外，基于物理的方法通常计算成本昂贵，并且可能涉及不适合非常规领域的假设。这些都对将基于物理的方法扩展到非常规油田的数百口井提出了挑战。 另一方面，数据驱动的方法更容易在整个现场规模上部署和维护。然而，可能需要井下仪表提供实时流动的井底压力数据或多速率测试，以在短时间内干扰注气速率，从而建立气举性能曲线。由于设施/成本限制，井下数据或多速率试井在许多非常规资产中可能不可用/不可行。因此，许多现有的数据驱动解决方案无法采用。The former approach usually requires excessive cost/efforts to obtain good estimates of dynamic physical properties and to ensure well-calibrated models throughout wells’ lifecycle. Moreover, physics-based approach is usually computationally expensive and may involve assumptions that are not suitable for unconventional fields. These pose challenges to scale up the physicsbased methods to hundreds of wells in unconventional fields. Data-driven approach, on the other hand, is easier to deploy and sustain on a full field scale. However, it may require downhole gauges to provide real-time flowing bottom hole pressure data or multi-rate testing to perturb on gas injection rates in a short time window to establish the Gas Lift Performance Curve. Due to the facility/cost constraints, downhole data, or multi-rate well tests may not be available/feasible in many unconventional assets. Thus, many existing data-driven solutions cannot be adopted.

具体来说，由于腐蚀环境和极端天气条件，本文研究的巴肯资产仪表稀疏，没有井下仪表，压缩机可靠性问题也很严重。巴肯使用垫压缩，每周可行的气体更换有限。此外，气体速率调整通常会造成压缩机可靠性问题，导致整个垫多天的生产损失。因此，我们提出了一种新颖的数据驱动工作流程，不需要井下数据或气体速率扰动测试。我们建立了一个机器学习模型，根据历史生产数据进行训练，以每周预测气举性能曲线。将该机器学习预测器与贝叶斯优化方法相结合，工作流程可以预测每口井的每周最佳注气速率，以最大限度地提高总垫产量，同时不超过压缩机容量。有了最佳的产气量和潜在的抬升量，巴肯的人工举升团队就可以从风险缓解的角度，更科学、更高效地决定何时调整压缩机。Specifically, due to the corrosive environment and extreme weather conditions, the Bakken asset studied in this paper is sparsely instrumented having no downhole gauges and significant compressor reliability issues. Pad compression is used in Bakken with limited feasible gas changes per week. Furthermore, gas rate adjustment can often create compressor reliability issues, resulting in multiple days of production loss for the whole pad. Therefore, we propose a novel data-driven workflow that does not require downhole data or gas-rate-perturbation tests. We build a machine learning model trained on historical production data to forecast Gas Lift Performance Curves on a weekly basis. Combining this ML forecaster with Bayesian optimization method, the workflow predicts the weekly optimal gas injection rate for every well to maximize the total pad production while not exceeding the compressor capacity. With the optimal gas rate and the potential uplift, the artificial lift team in Bakken can make decisions on when to adjust compressor from risk mitigation perspective in a more scientific and efficient way.

  • 平滑/下采样数据。Smooth/Down-sample Data.

我们应用中值下采样器将每日时间序列数据聚合到每周数据，以进一步平滑数据以消除异常值。通常，最佳注气速率不会每周发生显着变化。此外，巴肯的压缩机每周仅允许 1-2 次气体流量变化。权衡数据平滑度和数据规模，我们选择一周作为下采样时间窗口。We apply median down sampler to aggregate the daily time series data to weekly data to further smooth the data removing outliers. Usually, the optimal gas injection rate would not change dramatically from week to week. In addition, the compressors in Bakken only allow 1-2 gas rate changes per week. Balancing between data smoothness and data size, we choose one week as the down-sample time window.

  • 估算数据。Impute Data.

前向填充插值方案用于估算丢失的时间特征以防止数据泄漏。对于静态井特征（例如，横向长度、面积、总垂直深度……），平均值和众数插补分别用于数值和分类特征。Forward-filling interpolation scheme is used to impute missing temporal features to prevent data leakage. For static well features (e.g., lateral length, area, total vertical depth…), mean and mode imputations are used for numerical and categorical features, respectively.

一旦我们有了机器学习预测器，我们就可以通过循环不同的注气速率来预测下周每口井的气举性能曲线。我们对机器学习预测器进行了健全性检查，以确保结果符合工程师的经验。通常，我们预计气举性能曲线每周不会发生巨大变化。给定井的无约束最佳注气速率（对应于气举性能曲线中的最大生产率）应按月而不是按周变化。我们在图 10 中展示了两个示例。可以看出，A 井和 B 井的气举性能曲线随时间变化缓慢。它们相应的无约束最佳注气量按预期按月变化。Once we have a ML forecaster, we can forecast every well’s Gas Lift Performance Curve for the next week by looping through different gas injection rates. We performed sanity check of the ML forecaster to make sure the results are physical following engineers’ experience. Typically, we would not expect the Gas Lift Performance Curve varying dramatically from week to week. The unconstrained optimal gas injection rate (corresponding to the maximum production rate in Gas Lift Performance Curve) of a given well should vary on a monthly scale instead of weekly scale. We show two examples in Figure 10. As can be seen, the gas lift performance curves vary slowly over time for both well A and well B. Their corresponding unconstraint optimal gas injection rates change on a monthly scale as expected.

(2)贝叶斯优化(2) Bayesian Optimization

如果没有设施限制，最佳注气速率是微不足道的。气举性能曲线中对应最大生产率的正是注气量。然而，巴肯正在进行垫压缩。每台压缩机都有其自己的最大和最小所需容量。我们进一步将该问题表述为约束优化问题（见图 10）。If there are no facility constraints, the optimal gas injection rate is trivial. It is just the gas injection rate that corresponds to the maximum production rate in the Gas Lift Performance Curve. However, Bakken is on pad compression. Each compressor has its own maximum and minimum required capacity. We further formulate this problem as a constrained optimization problem (see Figure 10).

  • 井与压缩机的映射关系Wells-to-compressor mapping relationship

  • ML 预测器预测的气举性能曲线Gas Lift Performance Curves forecasted from ML forecaster

一旦约束优化问题被表述出来，您就可以根据您的喜好选择任何优化器。由于问题的规模，我们应用贝叶斯优化方法，通过利用过去的评估来指导未来的选择，智能地探索参数空间。这可以显着缩小搜索范围并以更有效的方式解决优化问题。Once the constrained optimization problem is formulated, you can choose any optimizer based on your preference. Due to the scale of the problem, we apply a Bayesian optimization method which intelligently explores the parameter space by leveraging past evaluations to guide future selections. This can significantly narrow down the search range and solve the optimization problem in a much more efficient way.

(3)部署(3) Deployment

我们通过云端将上述工作流程部署到Bakken。底层架构由两个持续集成持续部署 (CICD) 管道组成（见图 10）。We deployed the above workflow to Bakken through cloud. The underlying architecture consists of two continuous integration continuous deployment (CICD) pipelines (see Figure 10).

图 112. 气举优化工作流程的部署架构Figure 112. Deployment Architecture of the Gas Lift Optimization Workflow

  • 预测管道Prediction Pipeline

预测管道每周运行一次。它有两个目的。一方面，它加载预先训练的 ML 模型来预测所有井的气举性能曲线，并考虑下周的压缩机容量来求解受约束的最佳注气速率。结果被推送到最终用户的仪表板。另一方面，它通过将上周的预测与真实的测量结果进行比较来监控机器学习预测器的表现。如果连续几周观察到较大偏差，则会提醒开发人员进行故障排除。The prediction pipeline is running on a weekly basis. It serves two purposes. On one hand, it loads a pre-trained ML model to forecast all the wells’ Gas Lift Performance Curves and solves for the constrained optimal gas injection rates considering the compressor capacity for the next week. The results are pushed to a dashboard to the end users. On the other hand, it monitors the ML forecaster’s performance by comparing last week’s prediction against the true measurements. If large deviation has been observed for several weeks in a row, developers will be alerted to do troubleshooting.

  • 培训渠道Training Pipeline

培训管道每月运行一次。预测管道中加载的预训练 ML 模型是通过该管道生成的。随着越来越多的井转换为 GL 井以及更多的 GL 井转换为 PAGL 井，数据分布可能会随着时间的推移而发生变化。为了捕捉数据变化趋势，我们每月使用最新数据重新训练机器学习模型。生成的新 ML 模型将在两个模型都未见过的相同保留测试集上与现有旧模型进行比较。如果新模型具有更好的性能（例如 RMSE），那么它将自动升级。否则，旧模型仍然是最新的，可以继续运行下个月。Training pipeline is running monthly. The pre-trained ML model loaded in prediction pipeline is generated through this pipeline. As more wells converted to GL wells and more GL wells converted to PAGL wells, the data distribution would probably shift over time. To capture the data shifting trend, we re-train the ML model every month with the latest data. The new ML model generated will be compared against the existing old model on the same hold-out test set that both models haven’t seen. If the new model has better performance (such as RMSE), then it would be automatically promoted. Otherwise, the old model is still up-to-date and can continue running for the next month.

讨论Discussion

在我们的例子中，大多数井都有专用的分离器来记录日产量。然而，此工作流程可以轻松扩展到具有每日分配产量或相对频繁的试井的资产。In our case, most of the wells have dedicated separators to record daily production rate. However, this workflow can be easily extended to assets where wells have daily allocated production rate or relatively frequent well tests.

结论Conclusions

我们展示了使用机器学习在非常规领域进行气举优化的自动化数据驱动工作流程。自 2023 年 3 月以来，该工作流程已在巴肯 200 多口 GL 和 PAGL 井上成功部署。自部署以来，至少有 50 多口井使用该工作流程进行了优化，并获得了 >5% 的平均产量提升。而且，该工作流程已成为 Bakken 工程师的日常监控和优化工具，以提高他们的生产力和效率。这种基于机器学习的气举优化工作流程易于扩展和维护，无需井下仪表或多气体速率测试。对于具有成本或设施限制的资产来说，这是一种有效且经济的解决方案。We demonstrate an automated data-driven workflow using ML for gas lift optimization in unconventional fields. This workflow has been successfully deployed to Bakken on 200+ GL and PAGL wells since March in 2023. Since deployment, 50+ wells have been at least optimized once using this workflow and >5% averaged production uplift has been obtained. Moreover, this workflow has become Bakken engineers routine surveillance and optimization tool to increase their productivity and efficiency. This ML-based gas lift optimization workflow is easy to scale up and maintain without requiring downhole gauges or multi-gas-rate testing. It is an effective and economic solution for assets that have cost or facility constraints.
