摘要Abstract

由于模拟器、机器人实施例和任务目标的多样性，视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型的后训练至关重要。现有的计算服务，无论是直接加速器租赁还是批量工作负载提交，通常都会向单个租户分配一组独占的 GPU 和 CPU 资源。虽然这种模式最大限度地提高了客户端的灵活性，但它给用户带来了基础设施适应的负担，而且固定的卡时计费模型使短期或突发的工作负载对租户来说既昂贵又对服务提供商来说效率低下。为了应对这些挑战，我们提出The post-training of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models is essential due to the diversity of simulators, robot embodiments, and task objectives. Existing compute services, whether offered as direct accelerator rental or batch-workload submission, typically allocate an exclusive set of GPU and CPU resources to a single tenant. While this paradigm maximizes client flexibility, it burdens users with infrastructure adaptation, and the fixed card-hour accounting model renders short or bursty workloads both expensive for tenants and inefficient for the service provider. To address these challenges, we present欢乐联通JoyNexus，用于多租户 VLA 监督微调、强化学习和评估的统一服务。 JoyNexus 将训练模型服务、推理模型服务和环境服务解耦，每个服务都通过 API 访问，并由具有租户特定插槽的常驻共享基础模型提供支持。租户可以直接调用高级语义 API 进行训练、部署和评估，或者使用较低级别的 API 及其分配的端点来编写自定义算法。多个租户同时提交工作负载；它们的动作模块、优化器、部署记录和策略版本保持隔离，服务由全局训练队列和推理队列调度。为了进一步提高多租户训练效率，JoyNexus 引入了异构 VLA 数据模式的组批处理，这些数据模式共享兼容的面向模型的前缀，从而实现对分组样本的单个共享骨干前向传递。最后，我们通过工作负载模拟和现实场景中的组批处理管道来评估 JoyNexus。结果表明，与隔离的单租户执行相比，JoyNexus 通过共享资源上的跨租户调度减少了 GPU 总时间并提高了服务利用率。, a unified service for multi-tenant VLA supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, and evaluation. JoyNexus decouples the Training Model Service, Inference Model Service, and Environment Service, each accessed through APIs and backed by resident shared base models with tenant-specific slots. Tenants can directly invoke high-level semantic APIs for training, rollout, and evaluation, or compose custom algorithms using lower-level APIs and their assigned endpoints. Multiple tenants submit workloads concurrently; their action modules, optimizers, rollout records, and policy versions remain isolated, and the service is scheduled by the global Training Queue and Inference Queue. To further improve multi-tenant training efficiency, JoyNexus introduces group batching for heterogeneous VLA data schemas that share a compatible model-facing prefix, enabling a single shared backbone forward pass over grouped samples. Finally, we evaluate JoyNexus through workload simulation and a group-batching pipeline in a realistic embodied scenario. Results show that, compared with isolated single-tenant execution, JoyNexus reduces aggregate GPU time and improves service utilization via cross-tenant scheduling on shared resources.

1 简介Introduction

视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型为将基础模型的多模态理解能力与具体代理和系统集成提供了一条有前途的途径。 RT-2等型号Vision-language-action (VLA) models offer a promising pathway toward integrating the multimodal comprehension capabilities of foundation models with embodied agents and systems. Models such as RT-2[zitkovich2023rt], 开放VLA, OpenVLA[kim2024openvla], π0\pi_{0} [black2024pi0]、GR00T, and GR00T[bjorck2025gr00t]证明预训练的视觉语言表示可以有效地适应机器人操纵和控制任务。 虽然大规模的预训练赋予了这些模型通用的能力，但由于不同任务中数据模式和机器人实施例的多样性，后期训练仍然是必要的。 此外，不同难度的任务需要不同的训练后范式。 典型的开发工作流程可能涉及对标记数据进行监督微调 (SFT)demonstrate that pretrained vision-language representations can be effectively adapted to robotic manipulation and control tasks. While large-scale pretraining endows these models with general capabilities, post-training remains necessary due to the diversity of data schemas and robot embodiments across different tasks. Moreover, tasks of varying difficulty demand different post-training paradigms. A typical development workflow may involve supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on labeled data[kim2024openvla, kim2025fine, octo]，模拟器上的评估, evaluation on simulators[gu2023maniskill2, liu2023libero, mees2022calvin, james2020rlbench]，带有模拟器的在线和离线强化学习（RL）, online and offline reinforcement learning (RL) with simulators[li2025simplevla, vlarft, vlac, sun2026rl]以及数据管道编排, and data pipeline orchestration[ramos2021rlds, openxembodiment, cadene2024lerobot]等阶段。, among other stages.

对于云服务提供商而言，现有范例要么向租户租用专用计算资源For cloud service providers, existing paradigms either rent dedicated compute resources to tenants[armbrust2009above]或在共享集群上执行用户提交的工作负载or execute user-submitted workloads on shared clusters[verma2015large, lab2026mint]。 这两种方法都授予租户对执行的完全控制权，但要求他们管理复杂的基础设施依赖性，这对于涉及异构模型和模拟器环境的 VLA 训练尤其具有挑战性。 此外，由于 VLA 模型的规模通常适中，租户设计的分布式训练可能会导致加速器利用率较低。 因此，固定卡时定价对于小型、迭代和突发性 VLA 工作负载来说效率低下，在这些工作负载中，GPU 在部署、数据加载、评估或环境同步期间可能保持空闲状态。. Both approaches grant tenants full control over execution but require them to manage complex infrastructure dependencies, which is particularly challenging for VLA training involving heterogeneous model and simulator environments. Moreover, as VLA models are often moderate in scale, tenant-designed distributed training can lead to poor accelerator utilization. Therefore, fixed card-hour pricing is inefficient for small, iterative, and bursty VLA workloads, where GPUs may remain idle during rollout, data loading, evaluation, or environment synchronization.

Tinker 式系统表明，模型后训练可以通过编程 API 公开，同时抽象出大部分分布式执行基础Tinker-style systems have shown that model post-training can be exposed through programmatic APIs while abstracting away much of the distributed execution substrate[tml2026tinker]。 最近，代理强化学习系统探索了推出即服务和训练服务分离. More recently, agentic RL systems have explored rollout-as-a-service and training-service separation[zhang2026prorl, zhu2026opentinker]，并且托管的法学硕士培训基础设施为大规模培训和服务提供了类似的抽象, and managed LLM training infrastructures provide analogous abstractions for training and serving at scale[lab2026mint, relax2026]。 这些工作的根本创新在于将租户从底层基础设施的担忧中解放出来，让他们可以专注于算法开发。 通过公开可组合的服务 API（租户可从中构建自己的工作负载和管道），提供商可以更细粒度地调度共享资源。 然而，当前的框架主要针对语言模型后训练，而 VLA 特定的强化学习和评估仍未得到充分探索。. The fundamental innovation of these works lies in freeing tenants from low-level infrastructure concerns so they can focus on algorithm development. By exposing composable service APIs from which tenants construct their own workloads and pipelines, providers can schedule shared resources at finer granularity. Nevertheless, current frameworks mainly target language-model post-training, while VLA-specific RL and evaluation remain underexplored.

2.1 针对基础模型的修补式服务Tinker-style Services for Foundation Models

Tinker 式系统通过编程 API 公开模型后训练，同时向用户隐藏大部分分布式执行基础。 Tinker 允许用户针对服务原语表达微调逻辑，而不是手动管理工作器放置、分布式模型初始化和工件移动Tinker-style systems expose model post-training through programmatic APIs while hiding much of the distributed execution substrate from users. Tinker lets users express fine-tuning logic against service primitives rather than manually managing worker placement, distributed model initialization, and artifact movement[tml2026tinker, knight2025tinker]。关键思想不是远程执行，而是关注点分离：用户编写学习程序，而服务拥有资源分配、模型驻留、调度、同步和持久性。. The key idea is not remote execution, but a separation of concerns: users compose learning programs, while the service owns resource allocation, model residency, scheduling, synchronization, and persistence.

最近的系统将此服务视图扩展到语言模型后训练。开放修补程序Recent systems extend this service view to language-model post-training. OpenTinker[zhu2026opentinker]研究关注代理强化学习中的分离并建立在 veRL 的基础上studies concern separation in agentic reinforcement learning and builds on the veRL[sheng2024hybridflow]RLHF 基础设施线路。 ProRL代理line of RLHF infrastructure. To our knowledge, JoyNexus is the first Tinker-style system designed for a complete VLA-specific post-training ecosystem, including SFT, RL, rollout, evaluation, parameter export, and inference synchronization.

Ray provides a general distributed execution substrate for task and actor workloads, and RLlib builds distributed RL abstractions on top of it[moritz2018ray, rllib]。这些系统专注于扩展一个或多个训练工作负载的计算和内存； JoyNexus 解决了它们之上的补充层，即租户工作负载如何被接纳、路由、隔离以及连接到驻留模型和环境服务。. LobRA jointly fine-tunes multiple LoRA adapters over heterogeneous tenant data while sharing the base model[lin2025lobra]。在服务层，Clipper引入了自适应批处理以实现低延迟预测，AlpaServe研究跨多个模型的统计复用，vLLM和Sarathi提高了大模型推理的内存和调度效率. At the serving layer, Clipper introduced adaptive batching for low-latency prediction, AlpaServe studies statistical multiplexing across multiple models, and vLLM and Sarathi improve the memory and scheduling efficiency of large-model inference[clipper, alpaserve, vllm, sarathi]。 Punica 和 S-LoRA 跨共享一个驻留基础的 LoRA 适配器进行批量推理，而 dLoRA 跨服务副本动态编排请求和适配器. 3.2 SFT、RL 和评估工作流程SFT, RL, and Evaluation Workflows

VLA 后训练通常在监督微调、强化学习和评估之间交替进行。如图所示VLA post-training commonly alternates among supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, and evaluation. As abstracted in Figure2这些工作流程在数据来源和模型参数是否更新方面有所不同，但它们重用了许多相同的系统组件：数据访问、模型推理、训练更新、参数导出和工件存储。这种共享结构是 JoyNexus 使用的主要工作流程级抽象。, these workflows differ in where data comes from and whether model parameters are updated, but they reuse many of the same system components: data access, model inference, training updates, parameter export, and artifact storage. This shared structure is the main workflow-level abstraction used by JoyNexus.

监督微调。Supervised fine-tuning.

在SFT中，租户提供演示记录的离线数据集。每个记录通常包含观察结果、语言指令、可选的机器人状态和目标动作。训练过程从数据集中对受监督批次进行采样，运行 VLA 模型，计算动作预测损失，并更新租户的可训练模块。经过固定数量的训练步骤后，系统会导出一个参数工件，例如适配器检查点，稍后可以加载该参数工件以进行推理或评估。In SFT, a tenant provides an offline dataset of demonstration records. Each record typically contains observations, a language instruction, an optional robot state, and target actions. The training process samples supervised batches from the dataset, runs the VLA model, computes an action prediction loss, and updates the tenant’s trainable modules. After a fixed number of training steps, the system exports a parameter artifact, such as an adapter checkpoint, that can later be loaded for inference or evaluation.

强化学习。Reinforcement learning.

在强化学习中，数据源是在线或模拟环境，而不是固定的演示数据集。当前策略接收观察结果，通过推理路径预测操作，并将这些操作发送回环境。由此产生的转换、奖励、终止信号和策略元数据被写入为部署记录。然后，训练作业会使用这些部署记录，执行 RL 更新，导出更新的租户参数，并同步未来部署使用的服务策略。因此，与 SFT 相比，RL 增加了推理、环境交互、rollout 存储、训练和参数同步之间的闭环。In RL, the data source is an online or simulated environment rather than a fixed demonstration dataset. The current policy receives observations, predicts actions through the inference path, and sends those actions back to the environment. The resulting transitions, rewards, termination signals, and policy metadata are written as rollout records. A training job then consumes those rollout records, performs an RL update, exports the updated tenant parameters, and synchronizes the serving policy used by future rollouts. Compared with SFT, RL therefore adds a closed loop among inference, environment interaction, rollout storage, training, and parameter synchronization.

评价。Evaluation.

评估衡量的是固定政策而不改变其参数。评估工作负载加载选定的适配器或策略修订、绑定环境或数据集、运行推理并记录指标。这可以被视为推出过程的一部分，因此可以重用相同的推理模型服务、环境服务和工件存储作为训练。Evaluation measures a fixed policy without changing its parameters. An evaluation workload loads a chosen adapter or policy revision, binds an environment or dataset, runs inference, and records metrics. This can be considered as part of the rollout process, and can therefore reuse the same Inference Model Service, Environment Service, and artifact store as training.

4.1.3 系统功能System Functionality

除了模型执行和环境交互之外，JoyNexus 还为长期运行的多租户训练后工作负载提供了必要的运行时功能。 我们重点介绍三个关键功能：集中监控、故障隔离和弹性扩展。Beyond model execution and environment interaction, JoyNexus provides essential runtime capabilities for long-running multi-tenant post-training workloads. We highlight three key functionalities: centralized monitoring, fault isolation, and elastic scaling.

监控。Monitoring.

JoyNexus 采用集中式监控架构，将指标收集与各个工作人员分开。 运行时服务不是将跟踪逻辑嵌入到每个训练或部署组件中，而是异步地将指标事件发送到专用指标服务，例如 ClearMLJoyNexus adopts a centralized monitoring architecture that separates metric collection from individual workers. Instead of embedding tracking logic into each training or rollout component, runtime services asynchronously emit metric events to a dedicated Metrics Service, such as ClearML[clearml]或万德贝or WandB[wandb].

这些事件包含三类信息。 主服务使用控制信号（包括作业状态、队列深度、正在进行的请求和策略陈旧性）来进行准入控制和资源管理。 学习信号（例如训练损失、部署回报、评估分数和策略版本）被转发到实验跟踪系统以进行面向用户的分析。 系统信号（包括服务运行状况、执行延迟、权重同步开销和加速器利用率）使操作员能够诊断性能瓶颈。同时，检查点和工作负载清单独立存储在每个租户的输出目录中，确保即使在临时运行时状态发布后，实验工件仍然可重现和可审计。These events contain three categories of information. Control signals, including job states, queue depth, in-flight requests, and policy staleness, are consumed by the Master Service for admission control and resource management. Learning signals, such as training losses, rollout returns, evaluation scores, and policy versions, are forwarded to experiment tracking systems for user-facing analysis. System signals, including service health, execution latency, weight synchronization overhead, and accelerator utilization, enable operators to diagnose performance bottlenecks. Meanwhile, checkpoints and workload manifests are stored independently in each tenant’s output directory, ensuring that experiment artifacts remain reproducible and auditable even after temporary runtime states are released.

故障隔离和部分重启。Fault isolation and partial restart.

为了支持可靠的长时间运行的工作负载，JoyNexus 通过专用的运行状况管理器引入了服务级故障隔离。 健康管理器通过心跳和明确的错误报告持续监控常驻服务。To support reliable long-running workloads, JoyNexus introduces service-level fault isolation through a dedicated Health Manager. The Health Manager continuously monitors resident services through heartbeats and explicit error reports.

当发生局部故障时，控制器会执行就地重新启动，而不是重新启动整个工作负载。 具体来说，只有失败的角色被终止并在现有资源分配内重新部署，其执行状态在恢复服务之前从记录的进度恢复。 其他组件和租户工作负载继续执行而不会中断。 仅当故障影响共享协调组件或超出本地恢复预算时，才会触发全局重启。 这种设计将故障限制在尽可能小的范围内，并提高了多租户环境中的系统稳健性。When a localized failure occurs, the controller performs an in-place restart instead of restarting the entire workload. Specifically, only the failed role is terminated and redeployed within the existing resource allocation, with its execution state restored from the recorded progress before resuming service. Other components and tenant workloads continue execution without interruption. A global restart is triggered only when failures affect shared coordination components or exceed the local recovery budget. This design confines failures to the smallest possible scope and improves system robustness in multi-tenant environments.

弹性缩放。Elastic scaling.

通过将推理和训练解耦为独立服务，JoyNexus 可以在不中断优化过程的情况下动态扩展部署容量。By decoupling inference and training into independent services, JoyNexus enables dynamic scaling of rollout capacity without interrupting the optimization process.

缩放操作是异步执行的。 横向扩展期间，启动新的rollout引擎，与当前租户策略权重同步，并逐渐纳入服务池。 在缩减期间，引擎首先耗尽未完成的请求，然后再释放分配的资源。 并发扩展操作被序列化，以保持服务一致性并在部分故障下实现安全回滚。Scaling operations are performed asynchronously. During scale-out, new rollout engines are launched, synchronized with the current tenant policy weights, and gradually admitted into the serving pool. During scale-in, engines first drain outstanding requests before releasing allocated resources. Concurrent scaling operations are serialized to maintain serving consistency and enable safe rollback under partial failures.

这种弹性对于多租户 VLA 训练尤其重要，其中环境交互工作负载可能因任务而异。 JoyNexus 可以在推理密集阶段动态分配额外的部署资源，并在工作负载完成后回收空闲加速器，而无需修改租户端训练逻辑。This elasticity is particularly important for multi-tenant VLA training, where environment interaction workloads can vary significantly across tasks. JoyNexus can dynamically allocate additional rollout resources during inference-intensive phases and reclaim idle accelerators after workloads complete, without modifying tenant-side training logic.

4.3 多租户组批处理Multi-Tenant Group Batching

JoyNexus 的核心调度挑战是协调不同租户同时发出的训练和推理请求。The central scheduling challenge in JoyNexus is to coordinate training and inference requests issued concurrently by different tenants.

培训要求。Training requests.

An RL training job typically contains all trajectories collected in one rollout, whereas an SFT training job contains a batch of demonstrations produced by its dataloader; the same abstraction also accommodates other offline-data workloads, such as offline RL. By default, the Training Scheduler leases ready training jobs in FIFO order. 离线工作负载可以直接从常驻数据集中获取样本，而在线强化学习必须等待环境交互。 如果没有准入控制，容易获得的离线数据可能会比生成在线轨迹更快地占用训练队列。 为了防止离线工作负载挤占在线训练作业，JoyNexus 限制了每个租户任务策略同时接纳的训练作业数量。 即使整个离线数据集立即可用，这些运行中的限制也会限制相关的排队样本数量。Offline workloads can obtain samples directly from a resident dataset, whereas online RL must wait for environment interaction. To prevent offline workloads from crowding out online training jobs, JoyNexus limits the number of concurrently admitted training jobs for each tenant–task policy. These in-flight limits bound the associated number of queued samples even when an entire offline dataset is immediately available.

6 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了 JoyNexus，一种用于 VLA 后期训练的多租户服务架构。 JoyNexus 通过常驻共享模型和环境服务将 SFT、RL、部署和评估转变为租户私有工作负载，因此用户可以在提供者管理会话、路线、操作模块状态、工件和策略修订的同时表达学习意图。当前的原型通过基于 RLinf 的客户端工作流、主服务管理的租户状态、训练队列和推理队列、驻留模型服务、推出和参数工件以及统一服务 API 来实现这一抽象。混合工作负载实验表明，事件驱动的多租户执行减少了 GPU 总时间，同时提高了训练和推理利用率。分组批处理实验进一步表明，兼容的租户可以共享冻结的主干转发，同时保持操作模块、损失、优化器和检查点分离。This paper presented JoyNexus, a multi-tenant service architecture for VLA post-training. JoyNexus turns SFT, RL, rollout, and evaluation into tenant-private workloads over resident shared model and environment services, so users can express learning intent while the provider manages sessions, routes, action-module state, artifacts, and policy revisions. The current prototype grounds this abstraction with RLinf-based client workflows, Master Service-managed tenant state, the Training Queue and Inference Queue, resident model services, rollout and parameter artifacts, and unified service APIs. The mixed-workload experiment shows that event-driven multi-tenant execution reduces aggregate GPU time while improving both training and inference utilization. The group-batching experiments further show that compatible tenants can share a frozen backbone forward while keeping action modules, losses, optimizers, and checkpoints separate.

动态资源调整。Dynamic Resource Adjustment.

虽然 JoyNexus 支持动态添加和删除模型服务，但租户工作负载在执行过程中仍然很大程度上依赖于固定路由和资源分配。 更具适应性的设计可以利用运行时信号来动态调整服务路由和资源分配，而不改变用户可见的算法语义。 此类调度策略可以响应队列压力、模拟器延迟和租户优先级，从而提高利用率并减少等待时间。While JoyNexus supports dynamic addition and removal of model services, tenant workloads remain largely tied to fixed routes and resource assignments during execution. A more adaptive design could leverage runtime signals to dynamically adjust service routing and resource allocation without changing user-visible algorithm semantics. Such scheduling policies can respond to queue pressure, simulator latency, and tenant priority, improving utilization and reducing waiting time.

定价和产品化。Pricing and Productization.

除了系统效率之外，JoyNexus 还需要适当的定价和优先机制来进行实际部署。 一个关键的挑战是设计平衡租户成本、平台利用率和服务保障的收费策略。 未来的工作将探索基于使用情况和优先级感知的定价方案，该方案考虑了资源消耗、工作负载特征和延迟要求。Beyond system efficiency, JoyNexus requires appropriate pricing and priority mechanisms for practical deployment. A key challenge is designing charging strategies that balance tenant cost, platform utilization, and service guarantees. Future work will explore usage-based and priority-aware pricing schemes that account for resource consumption, workload characteristics, and latency requirements.

用户灵活性和隔离性。User Flexibility and Isolation.

JoyNexus 的目标是让用户专注于 VLA 算法设计而不是基础设施组装，但 VLA 工作负载需要多个迁移路径。有些用户可能运行平台提供的模拟器，有些用户可能上传Docker环境，有些用户可能暴露外部环境API。算法层提出了类似的问题：用户可能需要覆盖数据处理、奖励、推出逻辑或训练代码。更大的灵活性提高了采用率，但也带来了安全性、正确性和效率风险。未来的版本应该为用户提供的代码和环境定义安全扩展点、沙箱规则、攻击检测和性能契约。对于异构 VLA SFT，同样的原则适用于数据模式：规范化可以实现共享，但前提是掩码、动作维度、相机插槽和状态约定保留每个租户所需的语义。JoyNexus aims to let users focus on VLA algorithm design rather than infrastructure assembly, but VLA workloads require several migration paths. Some users may run a simulator provided by the platform, others may upload a Docker environment, and others may expose an external environment API. The algorithm layer raises a similar question: users may need to override data processing, rewards, rollout logic, or training code. More flexibility increases adoption but also introduces security, correctness, and efficiency risks. Future versions should define safe extension points, sandboxing rules, attack detection, and performance contracts for user-provided code and environments. For heterogeneous VLA SFT, the same principle applies to data schemas: canonicalization can enable sharing, but only when masks, action dimensions, camera slots, and state conventions preserve the semantics required by each tenant.

服务感知算法设计。Service-Aware Algorithm Design.

服务底层还开启了算法机会。通过租户许可和隐私保护，平台可以检测用户之间的相关数据集、任务或操作模式，并将它们用于数据增强、传输或更好的初始化。当前的训练队列和推理队列有意使用简单的类似 FIFO 的语义以及兼容性检查。更丰富的调度程序可以根据观察到的模式、批量大小、截止日期和资源压力形成动态训练组，从而提高利用率和学习吞吐量。总的来说，我们认为 JoyNexus 是迈向可编程、多租户 VLA 培训后服务的宝贵一步。The service substrate also opens algorithmic opportunities. With tenant permission and privacy safeguards, the platform may be able to detect related datasets, tasks, or action schemas across users and use them for data augmentation, transfer, or better initialization. The current Training Queue and Inference Queue intentionally use straightforward FIFO-like semantics with compatibility checks. Richer schedulers could form dynamic training groups based on observed schemas, batch sizes, deadlines, and resource pressure, improving both utilization and learning throughput. Overall, we view JoyNexus as a valuable step toward programmable, multi-tenant VLA post-training services.

可选的 actor-forward 阶段读取 rollout 分区，评估常驻 actor 下的行为动作，并附加学习目标所需的对数概率和值字段。 然后，优势阶段通过训练队列的两步元数据/数据接口获取批次，计算特定于算法的优势和回报，并根据相同的样本元数据写回派生字段。 具体的估计器可以是基于 PPO、GRPO 或基于收益的；它不会改变调度协议。The optional actor-forward stage reads the rollout partition, evaluates the behavior actions under the resident actor, and attaches the log-probability and value fields needed by the learning objective. The advantage stage then obtains batches through the Training Queue’s two-step metadata/data interface, computes algorithm-specific advantages and returns, and writes the derived fields back against the same sample metadata. The concrete estimator may be PPO-, GRPO-, or return-based; it does not change the scheduling protocol.

async def advantage_stage():
    job = training_scheduler.lease_stage(
        state="needs_advantages", consumer_role="actor",
        domain="vla", leased_state="computing_advantages")
    if job is None:
        return
    try:
        for batch_size in global_batch_plan(job):
            meta = await training_queue.async_get_meta(
                data_fields=advantage_input_fields(),
                batch_size=batch_size,
                partition_id=job["partition_id"],
                task_name="compute_advantages_and_returns")
            samples = await training_queue.async_get_data(meta)
            derived = compute_advantages_and_returns(samples)
            await training_queue.async_put(data=derived, metadata=meta)
        training_scheduler.mark(job["job_id"], "ready")
    except Exception as error:
        training_scheduler.fail(job["job_id"], reason(error))
        raise

这些阶段租约仅适用于控制元数据。 推出样本保留在原始分区中，派生张量与样本元数据相关联，而不是复制到训练调度程序中。 因此，演员前锋和优势工作者可以独立部署或省略，而无需更改演员的最终输入合同。These stage leases apply only to control metadata. The rollout samples remain in the original partition, and derived tensors are associated with the sample metadata rather than copied into the Training Scheduler. Consequently, actor-forward and advantage workers can be independently deployed or omitted without changing the actor’s final input contract.
