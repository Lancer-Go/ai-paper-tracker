Falkor-IRAC：印度司法人工智能中经过验证的法律推理的图约束生成Falkor-IRAC: Graph-Constrained Generation for Verified Legal Reasoning in Indian Judicial AI

乔伊·博斯Joy Bose

独立研究员，印度班加罗尔Independent Researcher, Bengaluru, India

摘要Abstract

法律推理不是语义相似性搜索。法院判决编码受约束的符号推理：先例传播、程序状态转换和受法规约束的推理。这些是基于向量的检索增强生成 (RAG) 无法忠实表示的属性。幻觉的先例、过时的法规引用和不受支持的推理链仍然是基于法学硕士的法律人工智能的持续失败模式，对印度等案件量大的司法管辖区诉诸司法产生了真正的后果。本文介绍了 Falkor-IRAC，这是一种印度法律人工智能的图约束生成框架，其生成基于 IRAC（问题、规则、分析、结论）知识图的结构化推理。印度最高法院和高等法院的判决被吸收为 IRAC 节点结构，其中包含程序状态转换、先例关系和法定参考，存储在 FalkorDB 中以进行低延迟代理遍历。在推理时，只有当可以通过图形追踪有效的支持路径时，LLM 生成的答案才会被接受，这是由称为验证代理的可证伪性预言机执行的检查。该系统还将检测教义冲突作为一流的输出，而不是默默地解决它们。 Falkor-IRAC 使用图本机指标进行评估：引文接地准确性、路径有效性率、幻觉先例率和冲突检测率。这些指标被认为比 BLEU 和 ROUGE 更适合法律推理评估。在包含 51 项最高法院判决的概念验证语料库中，验证代理正确验证了已完成查询的引文，并正确拒绝了伪造的引文。针对纯矢量 RAG 基线的评估留待未来工作，GPU 加速推理也是如此，以解决 CPU 硬件上当前的超时率。Legal reasoning is not semantic similarity search. A court judgment encodes constrained symbolic reasoning: precedent propagation, procedural state transitions, and statute-bound inference. These are properties that vector-based retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) cannot faithfully represent. Hallucinated precedents, outdated statute citations, and unsupported reasoning chains remain persistent failure modes in LLM-based legal AI, with real consequences for access to justice in high-caseload jurisdictions such as India. This paper presents Falkor-IRAC, a graph-constrained generation framework for Indian legal AI that grounds generation in structured reasoning over an IRAC (Issue, Rule, Analysis, Conclusion) knowledge graph. Judgments from the Supreme Court and High Courts of India are ingested as IRAC node structures enriched with procedural state transitions, precedent relationships, and statutory references, stored in FalkorDB for low-latency agentic traversal. At inference time, LLM-generated answers are accepted only if a valid supporting path can be traced through the graph, a check performed by a falsifiability oracle called the Verifier Agent. The system also detects doctrinal conflicts as a first-class output rather than silently resolving them. Falkor-IRAC is evaluated using graph-native metrics: citation grounding accuracy, path validity rate, hallucinated precedent rate, and conflict detection rate. These metrics are argued to be more appropriate for legal reasoning evaluation than BLEU and ROUGE. On a proof-ofconcept corpus of 51 Supreme Court judgments, the Verifier Agent correctly validated citations on completed queries and correctly rejected fabricated citations. Evaluation against vector-only RAG baselines is left for future work, as is GPU-accelerated inference to address current timeout rates on CPU hardware.

一、简介1. Introduction

法院是伪装成散文的图形遍历引擎。最高法院的判决不是检索意义上的文件；而是一份文件。它是一种遍历记录，是法官遍历问题、规则、先例和法律规定的图表的证据，并得出可追溯到沿途每个节点的结论。Courts are graph traversal engines disguised as prose. A Supreme Court judgment is not a document in the retrieval sense; it is a traversal record, evidence that a judge walked a path through a graph of issues, rules, precedents, and statutory provisions and arrived at a conclusion traceable back to every node along the way.

法律人工智能中的主流方法将这种遍历记录视为检索问题。判断文本被分块，嵌入到向量空间中，并在查询到达时通过语义相似性进行检索。这就是检索增强生成（RAG），它已经在许多领域产生了有用的系统。对于法律来说，它在三个结构性方面失败了。The dominant approach in legal AI treats this traversal record as a retrieval problem. Judgment text is chunked, embedded in a vector space, and retrieved by semantic similarity when a query arrives. This is Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and it has produced useful systems in many domains. For law, it fails in three structural ways.

首先，语义相似性并不具有法律关联性。具有里程碑意义的先例可能使用与法庭审理的案件完全不同的词汇；它的相关性是结构性的，而不是词汇性的。其次，幻觉先例是一种职业责任。不存在的听起来合理的引文并不是质量缺陷；而是质量问题。这是一个可以直接伤害依赖它的人的缺陷。第三，法律推理是有状态的。查询嵌入在程序过程中，该过程的当前状态决定附加哪些权利、运行哪些时间限制以及管辖哪些先例。 RAG 没有这种状态的模型。First, semantic similarity is not legal relevance. A landmark precedent may use entirely different vocabulary from the case before the court; its relevance is structural, not lexical. Second, hallucinated precedents are a professional liability. A plausible-sounding citation that does not exist is not a quality failure; it is a defect that can directly harm the person relying on it. Third, legal reasoning is stateful. A query is embedded in a procedural process whose current state determines which rights attach, which time limits run, and which precedents govern. RAG has no model of this state.

2.1 印度法律NLP2.1 Indian Legal NLP

印度法律文件语料库（ILDC，Malik 等人，2021）为预测和解释任务提供了第一个大规模带注释的最高法院判决语料库，并且仍然是印度法律 NLP 的标准基准。 InLegalBERT 是一种针对印度法律文本进行预训练的 BERT 模型，在 HuggingFace 上的下载量超过 180 万次。 NyayaAnumana（Law-AI Lab，IIT Kharagpur）是印度最大的法律判决预测数据集。 INLegalLlama 通过对印度法律文件的持续预训练扩展了 LLaMA，在判断预测方面实现了约 90% 的 F1 分数。 IndicLegalQA (2025) 为印度司法环境提供了问答数据集，MILPaC 在平行法律语料库中涵盖了英语和九种印度语言。The Indian Legal Documents Corpus (ILDC, Malik et al. 2021) provided the first large-scale annotated corpus of Supreme Court judgments for prediction and explanation tasks and remains the standard benchmark for Indian legal NLP. InLegalBERT, a BERT model pre-trained on Indian legal text, has over 1.8 million downloads on HuggingFace. NyayaAnumana (Law-AI Lab, IIT Kharagpur) is the largest Indian legal judgment prediction dataset. INLegalLlama extends LLaMA through continual pre-training on Indian legal documents, achieving approximately 90% F1-score on judgment prediction. IndicLegalQA (2025) provides a question-answering dataset for the Indian judicial context, and MILPaC covers English and nine Indic languages in a parallel legal corpus.

这些系统都没有强制对生成的推理链进行接地验证。他们预测或总结判断；他们不检查自己提出的推理是否可以追溯到真实的先例。 Falkor-IRAC 直接解决了这一差距。None of these systems enforce grounded verification of generated reasoning chains. They predict or summarise judgments; they do not check whether the reasoning they produce is traceable to real precedents. Falkor-IRAC addresses this gap directly.

法律人工智能领域的先前知识图谱工作主要集中在引文网络：通过 CITES 关系连接的案例节点。这在结构上是浅薄的。它记录了一个案例引用了另一个案例，但没有说明其主张是什么，具有什么权威，或者受到什么后续处理。 COLIEE、ECHR 数据集和 LegalBench 的工作推进了法律 NLP 基准测试，但在非印度司法管辖区且没有程序建模。先前的印度法律知识图谱不包括作为一流边缘的冲突型关系（CONFLICTS_WITH、RESOLVED_BY、NARROWED_BY），也不包括与教义推理一起的程序事件链。 Falkor-IRAC 是第一个包含两者的印度法律知识图谱。Prior knowledge graph work in legal AI has largely focused on citation networks: Case nodes connected by CITES relationships. 2.3 RAG 及其在法律推理中的局限性2.3 RAG and Its Limits in Legal Reasoning

标准 RAG（Lewis 等人，2020）通过矢量相似性和检索上下文的条件生成来检索文档块。 GraphRAG（Microsoft，2024）通过在生成之前遍历实体和关系图来改进检索。 GraphRAG 和 Falkor-IRAC 之间的区别是架构上的：GraphRAG 使用图来改进模型在检索时看到的内容； Falkor-IRAC 使用该图来限制模型在生成时允许声明的内容。区别在于更好的图书馆和具有否决权的外部审查员。Standard RAG (Lewis et al., 2020) retrieves document chunks by vector similarity and conditions generation on the retrieved context. GraphRAG (Microsoft, 2024) improves retrieval by traversing a graph of entities and relationships before generating. The distinction between GraphRAG and Falkor-IRAC is architectural: GraphRAG uses the graph to improve what the model sees at retrieval time; Falkor-IRAC uses the graph to constrain what the model is permitted to claim at generation time. The difference is between a better library and an external examiner with veto power.

2.4 法律人工智能的幻觉2.4 Hallucination in Legal AI

Han（2026）调查了值得信赖的法律推理，并将基础性和可验证性确定为两个主要的开放性问题。宋等人。 （2026）提出知识图辅助法学硕士后培训以减少法律幻觉，重点是微调而不是推理时间限制。 Karna（2026）提出了印度法律文本的混合 RAG-LLaMA 框架。 Falkor-IRAC 与这三者的不同之处在于，在推理时运行时具有硬图路径约束，不需要除基础 LLM 之外的额外培训。Han (2026) surveys trustworthy legal reasoning and identifies grounding and verifiability as the two primary open problems. Song et al. (2026) propose knowledge graph-assisted LLM posttraining to reduce legal hallucination, focusing on fine-tuning rather than inference-time constraint. Karna (2026) proposes a hybrid RAG-LLaMA framework for Indian legal text. Falkor-IRAC differs from all three in operating at inference time with hard graph-path constraints, requiring no additional training beyond the base LLM.

2.5 验证和约束生成2.5 Verified and Constrained Generation

宪法人工智能（Anthropic，2022）通过批评和修订应用软规范约束。 Self-RAG（Asai 等人，2023）引入了检索和反思标记，实现选择性检索和自我批评。两者都使用软约束：模型被引导至合规性，但不会硬阻止违规。 Falkor-IRAC Verifier 使用硬约束：图中路径不存在是否决权，而不是惩罚。在法律背景下，看似合理但无法验证的答案比没有答案更糟糕，这就是为什么硬约束是适当的设计。Constitutional AI (Anthropic, 2022) applies soft normative constraints through critique and revision. Self-RAG (Asai et al., 2023) introduces retrieval and reflection tokens enabling selective retrieval and self-critique. Both use soft constraints: the model is guided toward compliance but not hard-blocked on violation. The Falkor-IRAC Verifier uses a hard constraint: path nonexistence in the graph is a veto, not a penalty. In legal contexts, a plausible but unverifiable answer is worse than no answer, which is why the hard constraint is the appropriate design.

3. Falkor-IRAC 框架3. The Falkor-IRAC Framework

3.0 作为约束图遍历的法律推理3.0 Legal Reasoning as Constrained Graph Traversal

在描述系统之前，准确地陈述理论框架是有用的。法律推理在这里被视为有向图上的约束路径遍历问题，而不是语料库上的语义相似性搜索问题。Before describing the system, it is useful to state the theoretical framing precisely. Legal reasoning is treated here as a problem of constrained path traversal over a directed graph, not as a problem of semantic similarity search over a corpus.

令 G = (V, E) 为有向图，其中 V 是法律实体（案件、法规、章节、问题、规则、程序事件）的集合，E 是它们之间的类型关系的集合（CITES、APPLIES_RULE、TRIGGERS、CONFLICTS_WITH 等）。Let G = (V, E) be a directed graph where V is the set of legal entities (cases, statutes, sections, issues, rules, procedural events) and E is the set of typed relationships between them (CITES, APPLIES_RULE, TRIGGERS, CONFLICTS_WITH, and so on).

4

合法主张 c 是以下形式的命题：“结论 X 是根据先例 P 应用于事实 F 的规则 R 得出的。” c 的支持路径是 G 中的一系列节点和边 P_c = (v_1, e_1, v_2, e_2, ..., v_n)，使得该路径将 c 的每个元素置于 G 中的节点或关系中。A legal claim c is a proposition of the form: "conclusion X follows from rule R applied to facts F under precedent P." A support path for c is a sequence of nodes and edges P_c = (v_1, e_1, v_2, e_2, ..., v_n) in G such that the path grounds every element of c in a node or relationship in G.

有效性定义为：Validity is then defined as:

Valid(c) = 1  iff  there exists P_c in G such that P_c |= c

其中 |= 表示路径 P_c 在模式约束下为声明 c 提供了足够的基础（正确的关系类型、没有 OVERRULED 节点、没有 STALE 法规）。Where |= denotes that the path P_c provides sufficient grounding for claim c under the schema constraints (correct relationship types, no OVERRULED nodes, no STALE statutes).

根据这个定义，可以直接得出两个关键的评估指标。一组生成的声明 C 的幻觉率 H 为：From this definition, two key evaluation metrics follow directly. The hallucination rate H over a set of generated claims C is:

H = |C_invalid| / |C_total|

其中 C_invalid 是不存在有效支持路径的声明子集。一组生成的答案 A 的路径有效性 (PVR) 为：Where C_invalid is the subset of claims for which no valid support path exists. The path validity rate (PVR) over a set of generated answers A is:

PVR = |A_verified| / |A_generated|

其中 A_verified 是每个声明的引文都有有效支持路径的答案子集。Where A_verified is the subset of answers for which every claimed citation has a valid support path.

这一框架阐明了为什么硬否决是适当的设计。在生成概率观下，没有支持路径的主张并不是低置信度主张；这是一个完全没有法律依据的主张。将其视为软惩罚将允许无根据的声明出现在输出中，并降低置信度分数。硬否决将消除它们。This framing clarifies why hard veto is the appropriate design. Under the probabilistic view of generation, a claim without a support path is not a low-confidence claim; it is a claim with no grounding in the legal graph at all. Treating it as a soft penalty would allow ungrounded claims to appear in output with reduced confidence scores. The hard veto eliminates them.

一个重要的范围限定：此处定义的有效性与摄取的图 G 相关。缺失路径意味着该声明不能以 G 为基础，而不是说它没有法律依据。该系统并未声称已针对所有印度判例法进行了验证，仅针对所摄取的语料库进行了验证。这种区别在系统输出中得到明确体现，并在第 7 节中进一步讨论。One important scope qualification: validity as defined here is relative to the ingested graph G. A missing path means the claim cannot be grounded in G, not that it has no basis in law. The system makes no claim to have verified against all of Indian case law, only against the ingested corpus. This distinction is made explicit in the system output and discussed further in Section 7.

Given the proposed answer and the citation chain it claims to rely on, it queries FalkorDB to check path existence. For each cited case, it verifies existence in the graph, checks for subsequent overruling, and checks for conflict relationships with other cited cases. For each cited statute, it checks repeal status. The Verifier returns one of four outputs: VALID, INVALID, CONFLICT, or STALE.

当 INVALID 或 STALE 时，LLM 会被提示进行修改。修订提示包括拒绝原因，并指示模型仅引用经过验证的来源，或者在没有经过验证的答案时放弃。在两次失败的修改尝试后，系统将放弃而不是返回未经验证的答案。On INVALID or STALE, the LLM is prompted to revise. 3.4 冲突检测3.4 Conflict Detection

印度法院中的教义冲突以三种形式出现：协调法官分歧（两个相同规模的法官对同一法律条款得出相反的结论）、按错误引用（案件引用先例而没有注意到它被推翻）以及基于事实的区别应用得如此广泛，以至于实际上与最初的判决发生了冲突。 Falkor-IRAC 通过类型化 CONFLICTS_WITH 关系对这三种关系进行建模。Doctrinal conflict in Indian courts arises in three forms: coordinate bench disagreements (two benches of the same size reach opposite conclusions on the same statutory provision), per incuriam citations (a case cites a precedent without noticing it was overruled), and fact-based distinctions applied so broadly they effectively conflict with the original holding. Falkor-IRAC models all three through typed CONFLICTS_WITH relationships.

3.6 FalkorDB 作为推理基础设施3.6 FalkorDB as Reasoning Infrastructure

选择 FalkorDB 是出于与代理法律推理相关的三个原因。亚毫秒 Cypher 查询执行可实现每次推理所需的多个顺序图查询，而不会产生可察觉的延迟。本机混合向量加图支持允许未来将语义相似性检索与图遍历集成，而无需更改架构。 Redis 兼容协议与包括 LangGraph 在内的 Python 代理框架完美集成。FalkorDB is chosen for three reasons relevant to agentic legal reasoning. Sub-millisecond Cypher query execution enables the multiple sequential graph queries required per inference without perceptible latency. Native hybrid vector-plus-graph support allows future integration of semantic similarity retrieval alongside graph traversal without architectural changes. The Redis-compatible protocol integrates cleanly with Python agentic frameworks including LangGraph.

FalkorDB 是基础设施，而不是新奇事物。本文的贡献在于验证架构和模式。 FalkorDB 是使实时代理遍历在交互式法律查询所需的延迟下变得实用的基础。FalkorDB is infrastructure, not novelty. The contribution of this paper is the verification architecture and the schema. FalkorDB is the substrate that makes real-time agentic traversal practical at the latencies required for interactive legal queries.

6. 讨论6. Discussion

6.1 作为可证伪性预言机的验证者6.1 The Verifier as Falsifiability Oracle

验证者代理的作用与之前关于约束生成的工作不同。宪法人工智能和 Self-RAG 使用软约束：模型受到指导或惩罚，但不会因违规而被阻止。 Falkor-IRAC Verifier 使用硬约束：如果图中不存在引用路径，则答案将被否决。这种设计适用于法律环境，因为看似合理但无法验证的答案并不是正确答案的降级形式。它是一种不同类型的输出，可以对依赖它的人造成直接伤害。The Verifier Agent has a different role from prior work on constrained generation. Constitutional AI and Self-RAG use soft constraints: the model is guided or penalised but not blocked on violation. The Falkor-IRAC Verifier uses a hard constraint: if a citation path does not exist in the graph, the answer is vetoed. This design is appropriate for legal contexts because a plausible but unverifiable answer is not a degraded form of a correct answer. It is a different kind of output, one that can cause direct harm to someone relying on it.

弃权机制是这一设计的诚实推论。当两次修改尝试后没有得到验证的答案时，系统会这样说。对于法律援助从业者来说，明确声明无法核实索赔比自信的幻觉更有用。The abstention mechanism is the honest corollary of this design. When no verified answer is available after two revision attempts, the system says so. For a legal aid practitioner, an explicit statement that a claim cannot be verified is more useful than a confident hallucination.

实际限制是图形覆盖范围。验证者只能根据图中的内容进行伪造。未报告的命令、地区法院的判决以及尚未采纳的近期判决不属于核查范围。这在系统输出中得到承认，并在评估结果中清楚地注明。The practical limitation is graph coverage. The Verifier can only falsify against what is in the graph. Unreported orders, district court decisions, and recent judgments not yet ingested fall outside the verification envelope. This is acknowledged in the system output and noted clearly in evaluation results.

6.3 程序推理6.3 Procedural Reasoning

该模式的程序层（通过 TRIGGERS 和 PRECEDES 关系连接的 ProceduralEvent 节点）是以前的法律人工智能工作中最缺席的组件，也是对普通诉讼当事人最有价值的组件。面临保释拒绝的人主要不需要宪法判例摘要。他们需要知道从当前的程序立场来看，他们的选择是什么，适用的时间限制是什么，以及什么变化的情况可以证明重新申请是合理的。这是一个状态机问题，而不是一个检索问题。The procedural layer of the schema (ProceduralEvent nodes connected by TRIGGERS and PRECEDES relationships) is the component most absent from prior legal AI work and most valuable to ordinary litigants. A person facing a bail denial does not primarily need a summary of constitutional jurisprudence. They need to know what their options are from their current procedural position, what time limits apply, and what changed circumstances would justify a fresh application. This is a state machine question, not a retrieval question.

当前的实现模型是从个人判断中提取的程序序列。更丰富的方法将直接从 CrPC 和 BNSS 构建规范的程序图，可以根据该图验证案例特定的序列。这是程序层未来工作的主要方向。The current implementation models procedural sequences extracted from individual judgments. A richer approach would construct a normative procedural graph directly from the CrPC and BNSS, against which case-specific sequences can be validated. This is the primary direction for future work on the procedural layer.

6.4 超越法律的普遍性6.4 Generalisability Beyond Law

Falkor-IRAC 的架构（应用于概率语言生成的符号图约束）并不特定于法律。任何推理必须可追溯、可审计和诚实地对待不确定性的领域都是候选者：合规性监控、专利分析、电信监管、临床指南和治理系统都具有共同的结构属性，即它们的治理文件是规则、例外和先例的图表，而不是一堆文本。The architecture of Falkor-IRAC, symbolic graph constraints applied to probabilistic language generation, is not specific to law. Any domain where reasoning must be traceable, auditable, and honest about uncertainty is a candidate: compliance monitoring, patent analysis, telecom regulation, clinical guidelines, and governance systems all share the structural property that their governing documents are graphs of rules, exceptions, and precedents rather than bags of text.

法律领域对于此类系统来说是一个要求很高的测试用例，因为风险很高，结构明确，并且故障模式很好理解。法院写下他们的推理。法规已编号。引用了先例。图表已经在文本中了；使其明确并用它来限制生成就是贡献。The legal domain is a demanding test case for this class of system because the stakes are high, the structure is explicit, and the failure modes are well understood. Courts write down their reasoning. Statutes are numbered. Precedents are cited. The graph is already there in the text; making it explicit and using it to constrain generation is the contribution.

7. 局限性和未来的工作7. Limitations and Future Work

这项工作有一些局限性。在本节中，我们将详细讨论它们。This work has a few limitations. In this section we discuss them in detail.

图的覆盖范围是有限的。验证者只能针对摄取的判断进行伪造。 ILDC 语料库中未报告的命令、地区法院判决和法庭判决不在验证范围之内。随着语料库覆盖范围的扩大，验证保障加强；在当前的 v0.1 实现中，它应该被理解为研究原型而不是生产系统。Graph coverage is finite. The Verifier can only falsify against ingested judgments. Unreported orders, district court decisions, and tribunal judgments not in the ILDC corpus are outside the verification envelope. As corpus coverage grows, the verification guarantee strengthens; in the current v0.1 implementation it should be understood as a research prototype rather than a production system.

LLM 辅助的 IRAC 提取会引入噪音。提取质量取决于所使用的模型。这里报告的实验使用了一个小型的本地模型来实现可访问性和可重复性；生产质量提取需要更大的模型（7B 参数或以上）。比较不同模型大小的提取质量的注释质量实验是未来工作的明确方向。LLM-assisted IRAC extraction introduces noise. Extraction quality depends on the model used. The experiments reported here use a small local model for accessibility and reproducibility; production-quality extraction requires a larger model (7B parameters or above). Annotation quality experiments comparing extraction quality across model sizes are a clear direction for future work.

印度语言层（通过 Bhashini API 的印地语和孟加拉语语音接口）在架构和架构中进行了设计，但尚未评估。这是当前路线图中的 v0.4 范围。这项工作的诉诸司法的动机最终需要这一层；它是使技术贡献对不阅读英语法律散文的普通诉讼当事人有用的组成部分。The Indic language layer (Hindi and Bengali voice interface via the Bhashini API) is designed in the schema and architecture but not yet evaluated. 理论权重解析被建模为元数据，但尚未作为评分函数进行操作。当前系统检测两个案件是否存在冲突以及冲突是否已由更高的法官解决；它不计算优先权重分数。这是未来工作的主要理论扩展。Doctrinal weight resolution is modelled as metadata but not yet operationalised as a scoring function. 八、结论8. Conclusion

本文提出了 Falkor-IRAC，这是一种印度法律人工智能的概念验证图约束生成框架。核心架构的贡献是将生成与验证分离：Falkor-IRAC 不是使用知识图来改进语言模型检索的内容，而是使用它来限制语言模型允许声明的内容。验证器代理强制要求每个生成的答案在返回之前必须得到 FalkorDB 法律知识图中有效推理路径的支持。This paper has presented Falkor-IRAC, a proof-of-concept graph-constrained generation framework for Indian legal AI. The central architectural contribution is the separation of generation from verification: rather than using a knowledge graph to improve what a language model retrieves, Falkor-IRAC uses it to constrain what a language model is permitted to claim. The Verifier Agent enforces that every generated answer must be backed by a valid reasoning path in a FalkorDB legal knowledge graph before it is returned.

做出了三项具体贡献。首先，用于印度法律推理的 IRAC 知识图模式，通过程序状态转换和冲突型关系扩展了先前的引文网络方法。其次，冲突检测机制将教义分歧作为一流的输出，而不是默默地解决它们，这是一种诚实的设计，对诉诸司法有直接影响。第三，一个图原生评估套件，用于测量引文基础、路径有效性、幻觉率和冲突检测准确性，建议作为词汇指标不足的法律推理基准的标准。Three specific contributions have been made. First, an IRAC knowledge graph schema for Indian legal reasoning that extends prior citation-network approaches with procedural state transitions and conflict-typed relationships. Second, a conflict detection mechanism that surfaces doctrinal disagreements as a first-class output rather than silently resolving them, an honest design with direct implications for access to justice. Third, a graph-native evaluation suite that measures citation grounding, path validity, hallucination rate, and conflict detection accuracy, proposed as a standard for legal reasoning benchmarks where lexical metrics are inadequate.

基本论点是，法律推理在结构上更接近于约束图遍历，而不是模糊相似性搜索，并且应该相应地设计法律人工智能系统。法院是伪装成散文的图形遍历引擎。使该图变得明确并用它来约束生成是值得信赖的合法人工智能的正确方向。The underlying argument is that legal reasoning is structurally closer to constrained graph traversal than to fuzzy similarity search, and that AI systems for law should be designed accordingly. Courts are graph traversal engines disguised as prose. Making that graph explicit and using it to constrain generation is the right direction for trustworthy legal AI.
