1 简介Introduction

仅基于图像的重建算法Image-only based reconstruction algorithms[20, 21, 53]已经取得了令人瞩目的进步，但他们仍然难以在高速运动和曝光不足的极端场景下恢复退化的图像。 最近，仿生事件传感器已成为图像重建的强大工具。与以固定时间间隔捕获整个图像帧的传统相机不同，事件相机以微秒级时间分辨率和高动态范围（高达 120 dB）异步记录每像素强度变化（事件）。这种精细的粒度和极低的延迟使它们非常适合解决由运动和照明变化引起的退化问题。have shown impressive progress, but they still struggle to restore degraded images under extreme scenes with high-speed motion and insufficient exposure. Recently, the bio-inspired event sensor has emerged as a powerful tool for image reconstruction. Unlike traditional cameras that capture entire image frames at fixed time intervals, event cameras asynchronously record per-pixel intensity changes (events) with microsecond-level temporal resolution and a high dynamic range (up to 120 dB). This fine granularity and extremely low latency make them exceptionally well-suited for addressing degradation problems caused by motion and lighting variations.

2 相关工作Related Work

2.1 事件引导图像重建Event-guided Image Reconstruction

利用事件流的高动态范围和微秒级时间分辨率，最近的研究越来越多地利用事件来指导高质量图像重建。 早期工作Leveraging the high dynamic range and microsecond-level temporal resolution of event streams, recent research has increasingly utilized events to guide high-quality image reconstruction. Early work[46]首先引入事件来通过提出执行端到端去噪和去模糊的稀疏学习框架来协助图像重建。 此后，无数的方法first introduced events to assist image reconstruction by proposing a sparse learning framework that performs end-to-end denoising and deblurring. Since then, numerous approaches[17, 18, 42]开发了先进的基于 CNN 和 ViT 的网络，以整合全局和局部尺度的视觉和时间知识，实现更准确、更稳健的图像去模糊。 同样，有几种方法have developed advanced CNN- and ViT-based networks to integrate visual and temporal knowledge across both global and local scales, achieving more accurate and robust image deblurring. Similarly, several methods[30, 55, 51]引入了各种事件表示策略和注意力融合模块来对齐具有不同动态范围的信号，从而实现更好的 HDR 图像恢复。 然而，这些重建技术通常依赖于密集的注意力机制来聚合特征，而没有充分利用事件流固有的空间稀疏性。这种限制常常导致互补信息的次优融合和不必要的高计算复杂性。have introduced various event representation strategies and attention fusion modules to align signals with different dynamic ranges, enabling better HDR image restoration. However, these reconstruction techniques typically rely on dense attention mechanisms to aggregate features without fully exploiting the inherent spatial sparsity of event streams. This limitation often leads to the suboptimal fusion of complementary information and unnecessarily high computational complexity.

2.2 视觉状态空间模型Visual State Space Models

视觉状态空间模型（SSM）最近显示出跨各种任务的远程序列建模的强大潜力。 顾等人。Visual State Space Models (SSMs) have recently shown strong potential for long-range sequence modeling across various tasks. Gu et al.[11]和伊斯兰教等人。and Islam et al.[15]提出了结构化状态空间序列 (S4) 模型作为 CNN 或 Transformer 的有效替代方案，用于建模远程依赖性。 在此基础上，最近的研究proposed the Structured State-Space Sequence (S4) model as an efficient alternative to CNNs or Transformers for modeling long-range dependencies. Building on this, recent studies[1, 29, 27, 52]设计了 2D SSM 来处理视觉数据，用可扩展的基于 SSM 的骨干网取代传统的注意力机制，以生成高质量的图像。 特别是，Mamba 通过引入选择性机制和硬件感知的高效设计对 S4 进行了改进。 顺应这一趋势，一些研究人员have designed 2D SSMs to process visual data, supplanting traditional attention mechanisms with scalable SSM-based backbones to generate high-quality images. In particular, Mamba improves upon S4 by introducing a selective mechanism and a hardware-aware efficient design. Following this trend, several researchers[13, 36, 14, 9]探索了 SSM 在低级计算机视觉中的应用，通常结合卷积和通道注意力来增强标准 Mamba 架构的表示能力。 尽管取得了这些进步，Mamba 在捕获跨模式交互以进行事件引导图像重建方面的能力在很大程度上仍未得到探索。have explored the application of SSMs in low-level computer vision, often incorporating convolutions and channel attention to enhance the representation capabilities of standard Mamba architectures. Despite these advances, the power of Mamba in capturing cross-modal interactions for event-guided image reconstruction remains largely unexplored.

2.3 稀疏表示Sparse Representation

最近的研究Recent studies[50, 25, 43, 4]研究了事件数据的稀疏特征，将局部注意力操作引入 CNN 主干。通过限制对局部窗口大小的关注，这些方法利用稀疏连接表示而不是完全密集连接，从而显着降低计算成本。 由于局部注意力模块仅生成相邻元素之间的权重，因此它们的计算复杂度与空间分辨率成线性比例。利用现有的基于 Transformer 的方法have investigated the sparse characteristics of event data, introducing local attention operations into CNN backbones. By restricting attention to local window sizes, these methods utilize sparse connection representations rather than full dense connections, thereby significantly reducing computational costs. Because local attention modules only generate weights between adjacent elements, their computational complexity scales linearly with spatial resolution. Leveraging this, existing Transformer-based methods[59, 48, 47, 6]引入了局部归纳偏差来强制稀疏性，允许稀疏区域的标记与全局特征有效地交互。 与这些现有的表示方法不同，我们实现了一种简单而有效的 top-k 稀疏注意力机制，该机制近似事件流的全局稀疏属性，实现高效的特征表示和跨模态融合。have introduced local inductive biases to enforce sparsity, allowing tokens from sparse areas to interact with global features efficiently. Unlike these existing representation methods, we implement a simple yet effective top-k sparse attention mechanism that approximates the global sparse properties of event streams, achieving highly efficient feature representation and cross-modal fusion.

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，受到事件流的空间稀疏性和时间连续性的启发，我们提出了一种用于事件引导图像重建的高效视觉状态空间模型，称为 EmambaIR。我们的框架利用 TSAM 和 GSSM 来实现高质量的跨模态融合重建。 具体来说，TSAM在top-k稀疏注意力的指导下动态控制特征稀疏性并选择性地融合跨模态信息。 随后，GSSM 采用非线性门控单元来增强普通线性复杂度 SSM 的时间表示能力，从而大大减少通常与高分辨率重建相关的计算开销。 大量实验表明，我们的 EmambaIR 在多个任务中的性能优于最先进的方法，同时在内存消耗和计算成本方面保持显着优势。 虽然这项工作主要关注图像重建任务，但将这种事件引导的高效架构扩展到视频重建任务（例如视频去模糊、视频 HDR 和视频帧插值）仍然是未来研究的一个有前途的方向。In this work, inspired by the spatial sparsity and temporal continuity of event streams, we proposed an efficient visual state-space model for event-guided image reconstruction, dubbed EmambaIR. Our framework utilizes TSAM and GSSM to achieve high-quality cross-modal fusion reconstruction. Specifically, TSAM dynamically controls feature sparsity and selectively fuses cross-modal information under the guidance of top-k sparse attention. Subsequently, GSSM employs a nonlinear gated unit to enhance the temporal representation capabilities of vanilla linear-complexity SSMs, thereby drastically reducing the computational overhead typically associated with high-resolution reconstruction. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our EmambaIR outperforms state-of-the-art methods across multiple tasks while maintaining significant advantages in both memory consumption and computational cost. While this work primarily focuses on image reconstruction tasks, extending this event-guided efficient architecture to video reconstruction tasks—such as video deblurring, video HDR, and video frame interpolation—remains a promising direction for future research.
