1 简介Introduction

1.1 目标The goal

超人的国际象棋引擎已经存在了二十多年，但对于大多数棋手来说，它们作为训练伙伴实际上毫无用处。 当一位评级为 1500 的玩家询问 Stockfish 为什么他们的举动是错误的时，引擎会建议一条包含安静的车动作的线路，其目的只有在十二步之后才变得清晰——大师级别以下的人都无法想象这一序列，更不用说执行了。 超人游戏与人类理解之间的差距并不是一个特征；而是一个特征。这是一个障碍。Superhuman chess engines have existed for over two decades, yet for most players they are practically useless as training partners. When a 1500-rated player asks Stockfish why their move was wrong, the engine suggests a line involving a quiet rook maneuver whose purpose only becomes clear twelve moves later—a sequence no human below master level could conceive, let alone execute. The gap between superhuman play and human understanding is not a feature; it is a barrier.

玩家需要的是一个What players need is a相关的relatable对手：一个像人类一样玩的引擎，略高于他们的水平——整个游戏风格一致，偶尔会出现反映真实人类倾向的错误，但绝不会因为纯粹的位置混乱而出现错误。 我们的目标不是最大化比赛强度，而是最大化opponent: an engine that plays like a human slightly above their level—stylistically coherent across an entire game, capable of occasional inaccuracies that mirror real human tendencies, yet never blundering material through sheer positional confusion. The goal is not to maximize playing strength but to maximize人性化human-likeness达到目标技能水平。at a target skill level.

前期工作已取得重大进展。 玛雅和玛雅-2Prior work has made significant progress. Maia and Maia-2(McIlroy-Young et al., 2020; Tang et al., 2024)通过在不同技能水平的船上位置进行训练，以令人印象深刻的准确性预测人类的动作。 然而，这些模型在单盘状态（FEN）上运行，无法访问游戏历史记录——它们无法区分通过激进策略达到的相同位置与通过安静机动达到的位置，因此无法在整个游戏中保持风格的一致性。 另一方面，尝试将通用法学硕士重新用于国际象棋predict human moves with impressive accuracy by training on board positions at various skill levels. 1.2 为什么这很难：跟踪与决策的矛盾Why this is hard: the tracking–decision contradiction

我们的模型是一个标准的仅解码器 Transformer，其输入是 UCI 表示法中的一系列移动；它通过下一个标记预测来预测下一步行动。 至关重要的是，它永远看不到董事会。 每个棋子的位置都必须从走棋历史中隐式重建——从不断增长的代数标记序列推断出的 64 方棋盘状态。Our model is a standard decoder-only Transformer whose input is a sequence of moves in UCI notation; it predicts the next move via next-token prediction. Crucially, it never sees the board. Every piece’s location must be implicitly reconstructed from the move history—a 64-square board state inferred from a growing sequence of algebraic tokens.

这对模型提出了两个同时的要求：This places two simultaneous demands on the model:

状态跟踪（State tracking (TT).

该模型必须保持当前棋盘位置的准确内部表示——哪些棋子占据哪些方格、已捕获什么、是否保留易位权。 跟踪失败表现为非法移动：尝试移动模型认为不正确的棋子。The model must maintain an accurate internal representation of the current board position—which pieces occupy which squares, what has been captured, whether castling rights remain. Tracking failure manifests as illegal moves: attempts to move pieces that are not where the model believes them to be.

决策质量（Decision quality (QQ).

给定准确的棋盘表示，模型必须选择与人类水平的位置和战术理解一致的动作。 我们通过 centipawn 损失 (CPL) 和高评级子集的移动预测准确性来衡量决策质量。Given an accurate board representation, the model must select moves consistent with human-level positional and tactical understanding. We measure decision quality through centipawn loss (CPL) and move-prediction accuracy on high-rated subsets.
