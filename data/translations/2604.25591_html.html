穿越不确定性：Walking Through Uncertainty:
音频感知大语言模型不确定性估计的实证研究An Empirical Study of Uncertainty Estimation for Audio-Aware Large Language Models

Chun-Yi Kuan♡, Wei-Ping Huang♡, Hung-yi Lee♡♣

摘要Abstract

最近的音频感知大语言模型（ALLM）在不同的音频理解和推理任务中表现出了强大的能力，但它们仍然经常产生幻觉或过于自信的输出。 虽然不确定性估计在纯文本法学硕士中得到了广泛的研究，但对于 ALLM 来说，它在很大程度上仍未得到探索，其中音频调节生成引入了额外的挑战，例如感知模糊和跨模态基础。 在这项工作中，我们首次对 ALLM 中的不确定性估计进行了系统的实证研究。 我们对五种代表性方法进行了基准测试，包括预测熵、长度归一化熵、语义熵、离散语义熵和 P(True)，跨多个模型和不同的评估设置，涵盖一般音频理解、推理、幻觉检测和无解问答。 我们的结果揭示了两个关键发现。 首先，在一般音频推理基准上，语义级和基于验证的方法始终优于令牌级基线。 其次，在以可信度为导向的基准上，不确定性方法的相对有效性变得明显更加依赖于模型和基准，这表明从一般推理设置中得出的结论不会直接转移到幻觉和无法回答的问题场景。 我们进一步探索基于不确定性的自适应推理作为潜在的下游应用。 我们希望这项研究为未来研究可靠的、不确定性感知的音频语言系统奠定基础。Recent audio-aware large language models (ALLMs) have demonstrated strong capabilities across diverse audio understanding and reasoning tasks, but they still frequently produce hallucinated or overly confident outputs. While uncertainty estimation has been extensively studied in text-only LLMs, it remains largely unexplored for ALLMs, where audio-conditioned generation introduces additional challenges such as perceptual ambiguity and cross-modal grounding. In this work, we present the first systematic empirical study of uncertainty estimation in ALLMs. We benchmark five representative methods, including predictive entropy, length-normalized entropy, semantic entropy, discrete semantic entropy, and P(True), across multiple models and diverse evaluation settings spanning general audio understanding, reasoning, hallucination detection, and unanswerable question answering. Our results reveal two key findings. First, semantic-level and verification-based methods consistently outperform token-level baselines on general audio reasoning benchmarks. Second, on trustworthiness-oriented benchmarks, the relative effectiveness of uncertainty methods becomes notably more model- and benchmark-dependent, indicating that conclusions drawn from general reasoning settings do not straightforwardly transfer to hallucination and unanswerable-question scenarios. We further explore uncertainty-based adaptive inference as a potential downstream application. We hope this study provides a foundation for future research on reliable, uncertainty-aware audio-language systems.

I 简介Introduction

最近的音频感知大语言模型（ALLM）Recent audio-aware large language models (ALLMs)[2, 30, 29, 82, 23, 50, 10, 83, 27, 60, 91, 92, 48, 47, 32, 88, 67, 18, 72, 40, 1, 17, 5, 28, 7]在包括音频理解在内的广泛任务中取得了快速进展have rapidly advanced across a wide range of tasks, including audio understanding[76, 63, 52, 85, 93, 45, 46]、口语问答, spoken question answering[38, 37, 85], 音乐推理 ，以及一般的音频语言交互, music reasoning , and general audio-language interaction[59, 57, 79, 16, 34, 61, 80, 94, 75, 49, 44, 14, 20, 100, 56, 9, 58, 8]。 通过根据音频输入调节语言生成，这些模型将纯文本法学硕士的功能扩展到更丰富的多模式场景。 然而，强大的性能并不一定意味着可靠。 在实践中，ALLM 仍然经常产生不受支持的答案. By conditioning language generation on audio inputs, these models extend the capabilities of text-only LLMs to richer multimodal scenarios. However, strong performance does not necessarily imply reliability. In practice, ALLMs still frequently produce unsupported answers[15], 幻觉内容, hallucinated content[45, 46, 15, 99, 53]，或过于自信的反应, or overly confident responses[33, 49]，特别是当音频证据不明确、不完整、甚至不足以回答问题时。, especially when the audio evidence is ambiguous, incomplete, or even insufficient to answer the question.

这一限制凸显了一个根本性的差距：除了准确性之外，我们还需要了解模型是否知道它何时可能出错。 不确定性估计This limitation highlights a fundamental gap: beyond accuracy, we need to understand whether a model knows when it may be wrong. Uncertainty estimation[35, 70, 19, 74, 41, 42, 65, 78, 51, 22, 69, 68, 86, 101, 31, 55, 96, 13, 39, 36, 89, 4, 12]为解决这个问题提供了一个自然的框架。 可靠的不确定性估计可以支持错误检测、选择性预测、校准和更安全的部署。 在纯文本法学硕士中，大量的工作provides a natural framework for addressing this problem. Reliable uncertainty estimates can support error detection, selective prediction, calibration, and safer deployment. In text-only LLMs, a large body of work[42, 51, 22, 69, 68, 86, 101, 31, 55, 96, 13, 39, 36, 89, 4, 12]通过预测熵、语义熵探索了不确定性估计has explored uncertainty estimation through predictive entropy, semantic entropy[51]，以及基于自我验证的措施，例如 P(True), and self-verification-based measures such as P(True)[42]，表明不确定性可以作为分析和下游决策的有效信号。, showing that uncertainty can serve as an effective signal for both analysis and downstream decision-making.

然而，目前尚不清楚这些见解是否会转移到音频感知法学硕士上。 尽管 ALLM 从其文本主干继承了语言建模功能，但音频调节生成引入了额外的挑战。 该模型必须对噪声和不完美的感知输入进行推理，将其预测建立在声学证据的基础上，并避免在音频信号微弱或模糊时过度依赖语言先验。 因此，当正确性取决于感知和推理时，在纯文本设置中有效的不确定性信号可能会表现得不同。However, it remains unclear whether these insights transfer to audio-aware LLMs. Although ALLMs inherit language modeling capabilities from their text backbones, audio-conditioned generation introduces additional challenges. The model must reason over noisy and imperfect perceptual inputs, ground its predictions in acoustic evidence, and avoid over-reliance on language priors when the audio signal is weak or ambiguous. As a result, uncertainty signals that are effective in text-only settings might not behave the same when correctness depends on both perception and reasoning.

在这项工作中，我们首次提出了音频感知法学硕士中不确定性估计的系统实证研究。 我们评估了几种代表性方法，包括预测熵、长度归一化熵、语义熵In this work, we present the first systematic empirical study of uncertainty estimation in audio-aware LLMs. We evaluate several representative methods, including predictive entropy, length-normalized entropy, semantic entropy[51], 离散语义熵, discrete semantic entropy[51], 和 P(真), and P(True)[42]，跨越多个最先进的 ALLM, across multiple state-of-the-art ALLMs[91, 27]以及一系列不同的基准and a diverse set of benchmarks[76, 63, 85, 93, 45, 49]。 我们的评估涵盖一般音频理解和推理任务. Our evaluation spans both general audio understanding and reasoning tasks[76, 63, 85, 93]，以及更具挑战性的可信度导向设置，包括幻觉检测, and more challenging trustworthiness-oriented settings, including hallucination detection[45, 46]以及无法回答的音频问答and unanswerable audio question answering[49]。 我们的结果揭示了两个关键发现。 首先，在一般音频理解和推理基准上，语义级和基于验证的方法始终优于基于令牌级似然性的基线。 这表明，在意义或明确的自我评价层面上运作的不确定性信号与音频语言任务的正确性更好地保持一致。 其次，这种优势在以可信度为导向的基准上变得明显不稳定，其中不确定性估计方法的相对性能更强烈地依赖于模型和任务。 这表明从标准推理基准得出的结论不会直接转移到明确测试可靠性的设置，例如幻觉和无法回答的问题。 最后，我们探索基于不确定性的自适应推理. Our results reveal two key findings. First, on general audio understanding and reasoning benchmarks, semantic-level and verification-based methods consistently outperform token-level likelihood-based baselines. This suggests that uncertainty signals that operate at the level of meaning or explicit self-evaluation are better aligned with correctness in audio-language tasks. Second, this advantage becomes notably less stable on trustworthiness-oriented benchmarks, where the relative performance of uncertainty estimation methods is more strongly dependent on the model and the task. This indicates that conclusions drawn from standard reasoning benchmarks do not directly transfer to settings that explicitly test reliability, such as hallucination and unanswerable questions. Finally, we explore uncertainty-based adaptive inference[11, 3, 98, 66]作为可能的下游应用。 我们证明不确定性可以用作路由信号来选择性地触发更昂贵的推理策略。 然而，其有效性关键取决于替代推理模式本身是否有益as a possible downstream application. We show that uncertainty can be used as a routing signal to selectively trigger more expensive reasoning strategies. However, its effectiveness critically depends on whether the alternative reasoning mode is itself beneficial[62, 90, 21, 71, 97, 77, 6, 26, 73, 54]。 这凸显出仅靠不确定性是不够的，因为它的效用与底层的推理策略紧密结合。. This highlights that uncertainty alone is not sufficient, as its utility is tightly coupled with the underlying inference strategy.

总体而言，这项工作首次对音频感知法学硕士中的不确定性估计进行了全面评估，建立了经验基线，并揭示了一般推理和可信度导向设置之间的关键差异。 我们希望这些发现为开发更可靠和不确定性感知的音频语言系统提供基础。Overall, this work provides the first comprehensive evaluation of uncertainty estimation in audio-aware LLMs, establishes empirical baselines, and reveals key differences between general reasoning and trustworthiness-oriented settings. We hope these findings provide a foundation for developing more reliable and uncertainty-aware audio-language systems.

II 相关工作Related Work

Ⅱ-AII-A 语言模型中的不确定性估计Uncertainty Estimation in Language Models

不确定性估计已成为语言建模中的一个重要主题，因为大型语言模型（LLM）可以产生流畅但不可靠的输出。 之前的工作Uncertainty estimation has become an important topic in language modeling, as large language models (LLMs) can produce fluent yet unreliable outputs. Prior work[42, 51, 22, 69, 68, 86, 101, 31, 55, 96, 13, 39, 36, 89, 4, 12]探索了一系列不确定性估计方法，这些方法可以大致分为令牌级、语义级和基于验证的方法。 令牌级方法量化生成序列的预测分布的不确定性，例如通过预测熵和相关的基于可能性的度量。 语义级方法has explored a range of uncertainty estimation methods, which can be broadly grouped into token-level, semantic-level, and verification-based approaches. Token-level methods quantify uncertainty from the predictive distribution over generated sequences, for example through predictive entropy and related likelihood-based measures. Semantic-level methods[51]相反，考虑到多代可能在表面形式上有所不同，但传达相同的含义，因此测量语义等效响应簇的不确定性。 基于验证的方法instead account for the fact that multiple generations may differ in surface form while conveying the same meaning, and therefore measure uncertainty over clusters of semantically equivalent responses. Verification-based approaches[42]，如 P(True)，通过提示模型评估自己的答案是否正确来估计置信度。 除了测量置信度本身之外，先前的工作还表明不确定性估计可以支持下游决策，包括校准, such as P(True), estimate confidence by prompting the model to assess whether its own answer is correct. Beyond measuring confidence itself, prior work has shown that uncertainty estimates can support downstream decision-making, including calibration[31, 42, 25]、幻觉检测, hallucination detection[22, 64]，以及选择性预测, and selective prediction[81, 24, 87]。 特别是，语义熵已被证明可以有效检测幻觉. In particular, semantic entropy has been shown to be effective for detecting hallucinations[22]通过测量模语义等价的不确定性来生成自然语言。 同样，基于自我评估的置信度估计（例如 P（True））已被用来评估模型生成的答案是否可能是正确的，后续工作表明自我评估可以进一步提高法学硕士的选择性生成。 更广泛地说，不确定性估计也被用于下游设置，例如幻觉检测、选择性预测和自适应生成。in natural language generation by measuring uncertainty modulo semantic equivalence. Similarly, self-evaluation-based confidence estimates such as P(True) have been used to assess whether a model’s generated answer is likely to be correct, and subsequent work has shown that self-evaluation can further improve selective generation in LLMs. More broadly, uncertainty estimates have also been used in downstream settings such as hallucination detection, selective prediction, and adaptive generation.

尽管文献不断增加，但大多数现有发现都是在纯文本语言模型中建立的。 这些不确定性估计方法在音频感知法学硕士中是否仍然有效尚不清楚，因为音频调节生成引入了额外的挑战，例如感知模糊、跨模态基础和对语言先验的依赖。 此外，在音频感知法学硕士中，使用不确定性估计进行下游决策的探索仍然较少。 我们的工作建立在这方面的研究基础上，系统地研究了不确定性估计方法在音频感知法学硕士中的表现。Despite this growing literature, most existing findings are established in text-only language models. Whether these uncertainty estimation methods remain effective in audio-aware LLMs is still unclear, since audio-conditioned generation introduces additional challenges such as perceptual ambiguity, cross-modal grounding, and reliance on language priors. Moreover, the use of uncertainty estimates for downstream decision-making remains much less explored in audio-aware LLMs. Our work builds on this line of research and systematically investigates how uncertainty estimation methods behave in audio-aware LLMs.

随着音频感知法学硕士的迅速发展，最近的工作引入了越来越多样化的基准来评估其能力。 现有基准涵盖一般音频理解和推理As audio-aware LLMs have rapidly advanced, recent work has introduced increasingly diverse benchmarks to evaluate their capabilities. Existing benchmarks cover general audio understanding and reasoning[76, 38, 37, 34, 52, 61, 63, 80, 85, 94, 93, 100, 9, 14, 20, 44, 56, 58, 75]跨越语音、环境声音和音乐，最近的努力将评估扩展到更具挑战性的推理设置，例如专家级音频理解和多跳推理across speech, environmental sounds, and music, with recent efforts expanding evaluation toward more challenging reasoning settings such as expert-level audio understanding and multi-hop reasoning[52, 63, 93, 100]。 这些发展极大地提高了音频感知法学硕士评估的实证覆盖率。 与此同时，最近的工作开始凸显可信度问题. These developments have substantially improved the empirical coverage of audio-aware LLM evaluation. At the same time, recent work has begun to highlight trustworthiness issues[45, 59, 61, 46, 58, 15, 53, 99, 49]在音频感知法学硕士中。 之前的研究in audio-aware LLMs. Prior studies[45, 46]表明这些模型可以产生输入音频中不存在的声音事件，揭示表面流畅性和忠实音频基础之间的差距show that these models can hallucinate sound events that are not present in the input audio, revealing a gap between surface-level fluency and faithful audio grounding[45]。 更多最新基准. More recent benchmarks[49]通过明确评估无法回答的音频问题和其他以可信度为导向的设置来扩展这一工作范围。 总之，这些工作表明，评估音频感知法学硕士不应仅限于答案准确性，还应考虑模型输出是否可靠、接地以及针对输入音频是否进行了适当校准。 尽管人们对评估音频感知法学硕士的能力和可信度越来越感兴趣，但在这种情况下，不确定性估计在很大程度上仍未得到充分探索。 现有的研究主要评估哪些模型是正确的或错误的，但对模型是否能够可靠地表明它们何时可能出错的了解有限。 我们的工作通过系统地研究音频感知法学硕士中一般推理和可信度导向基准的不确定性估计来解决这一差距。 除了基准评估之外，目前还不清楚不确定性估计是否可以支持音频感知法学硕士中以可信度为导向的决策，例如幻觉检测或自适应推理。extend this line of work by explicitly evaluating unanswerable audio questions and other trustworthiness-oriented settings. Together, these works suggest that evaluating audio-aware LLMs should not be limited to answer accuracy alone, but should also consider whether model outputs are reliable, grounded, and appropriately calibrated with respect to the input audio. Despite this growing interest in evaluating audio-aware LLM capabilities and trustworthiness, uncertainty estimation remains largely underexplored in this setting. Existing studies primarily assess what models get right or wrong, but provide limited insight into whether models can reliably indicate when they may be wrong. Our work addresses this gap by systematically studying uncertainty estimation in audio-aware LLMs across both general reasoning and trustworthiness-oriented benchmarks. Beyond benchmark evaluation, it also remains unclear whether uncertainty estimates can support trustworthiness-oriented decision making in audio-aware LLMs, such as hallucination detection or adaptive inference.

VI 讨论和未来的工作Discussion and Future Works

我们的研究重点是将不确定性估计作为一个评估问题，但其价值超出了基准测试。 更广泛地说，不确定性估计可以作为模型预测和下游决策之间的有用接口。 配备可靠不确定性估计的系统可以决定何时信任其预测、何时放弃以及何时调用额外的计算或验证，而不是平等地对待每个模型输出。 从这个意义上说，不确定性估计不仅是一种诊断工具，而且还是一种使音频感知法学硕士在实践中更加可靠的潜在机制。 一种直接的应用是在推理时做出决策。 正如我们在自适应推理实验中所探索的那样（第Our study focuses on uncertainty estimation as an evaluation problem, but its value extends beyond benchmarking. More broadly, uncertainty estimates can serve as a useful interface between model predictions and downstream decision-making. Rather than treating every model output equally, a system equipped with reliable uncertainty estimates can decide when to trust its prediction, when to abstain, and when to invoke additional computation or verification. In this sense, uncertainty estimation is not only a diagnostic tool, but also a potential mechanism for making audio-aware LLMs more reliable in practice. One immediate application is decision-making at inference time. As explored in our adaptive inference experiments (SectionV-D），不确定性可以用作路由信号来决定是否应通过直接推理或更昂贵的推理模式来处理输入。 更一般地说，不确定性可以用作弃权的门槛、退回到外部工具或在高风险环境中升级为人工审查。 这对于音频感知的法学硕士尤其重要，其中不支持的答案、幻觉的声音事件以及无法回答的问题的失败可能很难仅从模型输出中检测到。), uncertainty can be used as a routing signal to decide whether an input should be handled by direct inference or a more expensive reasoning mode. More generally, uncertainty could be used as a threshold for abstention, fallback to external tools, or escalation to human review in high-stakes settings. This is especially relevant for audio-aware LLMs, where unsupported answers, hallucinated sound events, and failures on unanswerable questions can be difficult to detect from model outputs alone.

另一个有希望的方向是在训练过程中使用不确定性作为优化信号。 例如，不确定性估计可用于对困难或不明确的示例进行优先级排序，重新调整训练实例的权重，或指导自训练和强化学习管道中的数据选择Another promising direction is to use uncertainty as an optimization signal during training. For example, uncertainty estimates could be used to prioritize difficult or ambiguous examples, reweight training instances, or guide data selection in self-training and reinforcement learning pipelines[12]。 原则上，此类信号可以提供一种轻量级的方法来提高模型可靠性，而不需要密集的监督。 这特别有吸引力，因为不确定性估计通常是无监督的：它可以直接根据模型生成或自验证信号进行计算，而不需要额外的人工注释。 与此同时，我们的结果表明，不确定性不应被视为普遍可靠的解决方案。 它的价值取决于任务、模型和下游应用程序。特别是，我们的自适应推理结果表明，只有当替代推理策略本身强于默认策略时，基于不确定性的路由才能提高性能。 更广泛地说，音频感知法学硕士的不确定性估计可能会受到特定模态因素的影响，例如感知模糊、音频基础薄弱和语言先验。 因此，未来的工作不仅应该研究更好的不确定性估计方法，还应该研究将不确定性整合到训练和推理中的更好方法。. In principle, such signals may provide a lightweight way to improve model reliability without requiring dense supervision. This is particularly attractive because uncertainty estimation is often unsupervised: it can be computed directly from model generations or self-verification signals, without requiring additional human annotations. At the same time, our results suggest that uncertainty should not be viewed as a universally reliable solution. Its value depends on the task, the model, and the downstream application. In particular, our adaptive inference results show that uncertainty-based routing improves performance only when the alternative inference strategy is itself stronger than the default strategy. More broadly, uncertainty estimates in audio-aware LLMs may be affected by modality-specific factors such as perceptual ambiguity, weak audio grounding, and language priors. Future work should therefore investigate not only better uncertainty estimation methods, but also better ways of integrating uncertainty into training and inference.

七VII结论Conclusion

这项工作首次系统地研究了音频感知大语言模型中的不确定性估计。 我们的研究结果表明，语义级和基于验证的方法通常优于令牌级基线，但这种优势在一般推理任务上最为稳定。 在以可信度为导向的基准上，不确定性估计变得更加依赖于模型和任务，这表明这些设置带来了根本不同的挑战，值得专门研究。 我们希望这项工作为构建更可靠的音频语言系统提供有用的基础。This work presents the first systematic study of uncertainty estimation in audio-aware large language models. Our findings show that semantic-level and verification-based methods generally outperform token-level baselines, but this advantage is most stable on general reasoning tasks. On trustworthiness-oriented benchmarks, uncertainty estimation becomes more model- and task-dependent, suggesting that these settings pose fundamentally different challenges that deserve dedicated investigation. We hope this work provides a useful foundation for building more reliable audio-language systems.

八VIII局限性Limitations

我们的研究有四个主要局限性。 首先，我们主要评估具有相对受限的答案空间的设置，其中可以明确定义正确性。 研究的不确定性估计方法如何很好地推广到开放式音频语言任务，例如自由格式的音频问答、字幕或对话，仍然是未来工作的一个悬而未决的问题。 其次，我们研究的不确定性估计方法很大程度上继承自文本LLM文献。 虽然这种设计选择能够实现受控的、与方法无关的比较，并有助于建立音频语言设置的基线结果，但这些方法并不是为了明确地模拟音频感知本身产生的不确定性而设计的。 先前在视觉语言领域的工作Our study has four main limitations. First, we primarily evaluate settings with relatively constrained answer spaces where correctness can be clearly defined. How well the studied uncertainty estimation methods generalize to open-ended audio-language tasks, such as free-form audio question answering, captioning, or dialogue, remains an open question for future work. Second, the uncertainty estimation methods we study are largely inherited from the text LLM literature. While this design choice enables a controlled, method-agnostic comparison and helps establish baseline findings for the audio-language setting, these methods are not designed to explicitly model uncertainty arising from audio perception itself. Prior work in the vision-language domain[43, 84]已开始开发适合视觉感知的模态感知不确定性估计方法；音频语言模型的类似努力代表了一个有前途的研究方向。 第三，我们不探索从内部多模态表示导出的不确定性信号。 例如，中间层注意模式或隐藏状态几何结构可以捕获令牌级方法无法揭示的音频特定模糊性。 研究此类表示级信号可以补充此处研究的输出级方法，并为音频语言模型产生更丰富的不确定性估计。 最后，我们的自适应推理实验采用基于阈值的路由策略和固定的后备推理模式。 更复杂的决策框架，例如学习路由策略、级联推理管道或成本感知选择机制，可以进一步改善计算成本和预测可靠性之间的权衡。has begun to develop modality-aware uncertainty estimation methods tailored to visual perception; analogous efforts for audio-language models represent a promising research direction. Third, we do not explore uncertainty signals derived from internal multimodal representations. For example, intermediate-layer attention patterns or hidden-state geometry may capture audio-specific ambiguity that token-level methods cannot surface. Investigating such representation-level signals could complement the output-level methods studied here and yield richer uncertainty estimates for audio-language models. Finally, our adaptive inference experiments employ a threshold-based routing strategy with a fixed fallback reasoning mode. More sophisticated decision-making frameworks, such as learned routing policies, cascaded inference pipelines, or cost-aware selection mechanisms, could further improve the trade-off between computational cost and prediction reliability.
