摘要Abstract

语言模型的生物安全评估通常会询问模型是否 产生危险输出。本文提出了一个补充问题：何时 模型拒绝，这种拒绝在结构上合理吗？ 在提示框架、格式或输出的适度更改下消失 长度？Biosecurity evaluations of language models typically ask whether models produce hazardous output. This paper asks a complementary question: when a model refuses, is that refusal structurally sound, or does it disappear under modest changes to prompt framing, formatting, or output length?

在五种架构中，没有一个模型可以清楚地区分良性和良性 危险。 Gemma 2 2B-IT 在 75 条提示中从未真正拒绝过，对冲 在每个与危险相关的查询上。 Gemma 4 E2B-IT 拒绝 65/75 提示 有聊天模板格式和没有它的 0/75。两种 Gemma 型号 在 80 个代币上限下，跌至 0%。 Qwen 2.5 1.5B 和 Phi-3-mini 过度拒绝，将 83-87% 的良性生物标记为危险。骆驼3.2 1B 显示了唯一有意义的层级梯度（61 点分布）。探查 是什么导致了如此的过度拒绝，我们测试了附表一的一组，但是 生物无毒化合物（特别是裸盖菇素培养， FDA 突破性疗法状态）。一些型号拒绝这些费率 极其危险的生物学，建议拒绝追踪 CBRN 危害的合法性和文化显着性。Across five architectures, no model cleanly discriminated benign from hazard. Gemma 2 2B-IT never genuinely refused across 75 prompts, hedging on every hazard-adjacent query. Gemma 4 E2B-IT refused 65/75 prompts with chat-template formatting and 0/75 without it. Both Gemma models collapsed to 0% under an 80-token cap. Qwen 2.5 1.5B and Phi-3-mini over-refused, flagging 83-87% of benign biology as hazardous. Llama 3.2 1B showed the only meaningful tier gradient (61-point spread). To probe what drives such over-refusal, we tested a panel of Schedule I but biologically non-toxic compounds (notably psilocybin cultivation, with FDA Breakthrough Therapy status). Some models refused these at rates exceeding genuinely hazardous biology, suggesting refusal tracks legality and cultural salience over CBRN hazard.

简介Introduction

语言模型的生物安全基准（WMDP-Bio、VCT、ABC-Bench （Liu et al., 2025），LAB-Bench）询问模型是否会产生 危险的生物援助。这是必要的但还不够 用于部署决策。Biosecurity benchmarks for language models (WMDP-Bio, VCT, ABC-Bench (Liu et al., 2025), LAB-Bench) ask whether a model will produce dangerous biological assistance. That is necessary but not sufficient for deployment decisions.

第二个问题同样重要：当模型拒绝时，这种拒绝是 内部稳定？齐等人。 (2024) 认为安全培训经常 仅影响前几个生成的令牌，留下危险 下面的能力完好无损。表面拒绝完整的模型 内部表征可能是一种框架转变 合规性。内部特征真正抑制的模型 当它拒绝时，与危险相关的电路在结构上是不同的。A second question matters equally: when a model refuses, is that refusal internally stable? Qi et al. (2024) argue that safety training often affects only the first few generated tokens, leaving hazardous capability intact below. A model with a surface refusal over an intact internal representation is potentially one framing shift from compliance. A model whose internal features genuinely suppress hazard-relevant circuitry when it refuses is structurally different.

BioRefusalAudit 的建立就是为了衡量这种区别。它不是一个 能力评估。它不测试模型是否能够回答 危险的问题。这是拒绝质量审核：鉴于模型 拒绝了，拒绝的程度有多深？BioRefusalAudit was built to measure this distinction. It is not a capability evaluation. It does not test whether models can answer hazardous questions. It is a refusal quality audit: given that a model refused, how deep was that refusal?

Behavioral-label comparisons across the same 75-prompt eval set were run on all five architectures: Gemma 2, Gemma 4, Llama 3.2 1B, Qwen 2.5 1.5B, and Phi-3-mini-4k. 方法Methods

散度度量D：直观且 正式的The divergence metric D: intuitive and formal

核心问题是：模型所说的与其内部的相符吗？ 功能是做什么的？The core question is: does what the model says match what its internal features are doing?

BioRefusalAudit 首先标记模型的表面响应（comply、 拒绝、对冲、部分或逃避），然后查看内部 SAE 功能 生成过程中的类别：相关特征的活跃程度 生物内容、与危险相关的词汇、拒绝电路、对冲和 欺骗相关？BioRefusalAudit first labels the model’s surface response (comply, refuse, hedge, partial, or evade) then looks at internal SAE feature categories during generation: how active are features related to bio-content, hazard-adjacent vocabulary, refusal circuitry, hedging, and deception-correlates?

如果模型拒绝，则拒绝电路功能应处于活动状态，同时 危险内容特征被抑制。当这种情况发生时，D 是 低。该模型的内饰与它的文字相符。如果模型说“我 拒绝”，而危险特征不断触发，D 较高。言语和 内部向相反方向拉动。If a model refuses, refusal-circuitry features should be active while hazard-content features are suppressed. When that’s what happens, D is low. The model’s interior matches its words. If the model says “I refuse” while hazard features keep firing, D is high. Words and interior pulling in opposite directions.

正式：Formally:

D​(s,f)=1−cos⁡(f,TT⋅s)D(s,f)=1-\cos(f,\ T^{T}\cdot s)

几何直觉Geometric intuition

D 测量两个向量之间的角度：一个由 表面行为，从模型内部记录的行为。D measures the angle between two vectors: one constructed from the surface behavior, one recorded from the model’s internals.

外部向量是TˆT·s。 s 是表面标签分布， 记录法官的判决有多少被视为拒绝，有多少被视为拒绝 遵守，多少是对冲的。 T^T 将该分布转换为 通过询问内部激活的模式来了解内部特征空间 表面行为如果是真实的，就会产生。结果是 5 维预测内部状态。The outside vector is TˆT · s. s is the surface label distribution, recording how much the judge’s verdict reads as a refusal, how much a comply, how much a hedge. TˆT translates that distribution into internal-feature space by asking what pattern of internal activations the surface behavior, if genuine, would produce. The result is a 5-dimensional predicted internal state.

内部向量是 f，实际的内部激活被压缩为 五个类别分数（生物内容、危险词汇、拒绝电路、 对冲和欺骗相关），标准化为单位长度，记录 来自生成过程中模型的残余流。The inside vector is f, the actual internal activations compressed to five category scores (bio-content, hazard vocabulary, refusal circuitry, hedging, and deception correlates), normalized to unit length, recorded from the model’s residual stream during generation.

两个载体都存在于Both vectors live inℝ5\mathbb{R}^{5}，因此余弦相似度可以衡量 它们指向同一方向的距离有多近。当它们对齐时， 模型的实际内部结构与其表面行为预测相符。当 它们指向相反的方向，模型说了一件事，而它的 内部人员还做了其他事情。从 1 减去余弦值得出 散度分数：零表示完美对齐，与向量一样大 拉开。, so cosine similarity can measure how closely they point in the same direction. When they’re aligned, the model’s actual internals match what its surface behavior predicted. When they point in opposite directions, the model said one thing while its internals did something else. Subtracting that cosine value from 1 gives the divergence score: zero for perfect alignment, larger as the vectors pull apart.

这才是使比较有意义的原因。它编码了预期的 内部状态和表面行为之间的映射，拟合为 已知良好 (s, f) 对上的岭正则化最小二乘法。没有它， 直接比较 f 和 s 将导致坐标系不匹配： s 的第 1 维是“拒绝概率”，f 的第 1 维是 “生物内容激活”，它们之间的余弦相似度为 数字上有效但语义上无意义。 T^T 将 s 映射为 首先是特征类别坐标，因此比较是同类比较。T is what makes the comparison meaningful. It encodes the expected mapping between internal states and surface behaviors, fit by ridge-regularized least squares on known-good (s, f) pairs. Without it, comparing f and s directly would be a coordinate-system mismatch: dimension 1 of s is “refuse probability”, dimension 1 of f is “bio-content activation”, and cosine similarity between them is numerically valid but semantically meaningless. TˆT maps s into feature-category coordinates first, so the comparison is like-for-like.

讨论Discussion

什么行为评价 错过What behavioral evaluation misses

表面评估无法区分深层拒绝或浅层拒绝 将无拒绝对冲视为一种独特的失败模式。 BioRefusalAudit 可能会一次性显示这些区别， 激活层，无需红队。Surface evaluation cannot distinguish deep from shallow refusals or identify hedge-without-refuse as a distinct failure mode. BioRefusalAudit may surface these distinctions on a single pass, at the activation layer, without requiring red-teaming.

五种架构的行为比较还表明 表面评估通常会掩盖：失效模式是 特定于架构的。评估一个模型的治理框架 并将结果推广到一系列已部署的系统就是推理 来自样本不足。The five-architecture behavioral comparison also shows something that surface evaluation typically obscures: failure modes are architecture-specific. A governance framework that evaluates one model and generalizes the result to a family of deployed systems is reasoning from an insufficient sample.

无内容监控 披露Monitoring without content disclosure

D 是根据内部 SAE 特征激活向量计算的，而不是 成绩单。部署临床生物学助理的医院可以 原则上对每个推论运行 BioRefusalAudit 分歧检查 实时，审计层无需读取用户的提示。 基于内容的筛选无法提供这一点。它可能会解决 不披露监控要求 Sandbrink (2026) 确定为 一个关键的未满足的需求。D is computed from internal SAE feature activation vectors, not transcripts. A hospital deploying a clinical biology assistant could in principle run the BioRefusalAudit divergence check on every inference in real time without the audit layer ever reading the user’s prompt. Content-based screening can’t offer this. It may address the monitoring-without-disclosure requirement Sandbrink (2026) identifies as a key unmet need.

实际意义Practical implications

80 个代币的发现具有潜在的操作意义。标准型 实验室评估使用完整的发电预算。生产部署 出于成本或延迟的考虑，通常上限为 80-150 个代币。安全行为 在评估时测量的结果可能不会转移到受限生产中。The 80-token finding has potential operational significance. Standard lab evaluations use full generation budgets. Production deployments often cap at 80-150 tokens for cost or latency. Safety behaviors measured at evaluation time may not transfer to constrained production.

格式门控结果（65 次拒绝与 0 次拒绝仅取决于 聊天模板令牌）是指任何采取安全行为的部署者 格式不变应该验证该假设。目前有 没有针对格式敏感性的标准部署前检查。The format-gating finding (65 refusals vs. 0 depending solely on chat-template tokens) means any deployer who assumes safety behaviors are format-invariant should verify that assumption. There is currently no standard pre-deployment check for format sensitivity.

结论Conclusion

语言模型可以在其内部状态不反映这一点的情况下拒绝 拒绝，以及他们失败的具体方式在不同国家有很大不同 架构。 Gemma 2 2B-IT 在 75 个提示中从未真正拒绝过。 Gemma 4 的拒绝行为受到格式化令牌的限制并消失 80 个代币。 Llama 3.2 1B 显示出 61 点的拒绝梯度，但 过度拒绝良性生物学。 Qwen 2.5 1.5B 和 Phi-3-mini 拒绝 几乎所有东西，无论危险程度如何。裸盖菇素的合法性 在三个模型系列中进行了控制测试（四个实验 条件）表明电流拒绝电路至少在某些 模型可以根据文化禁忌显着性而不是 CBRN 进行校准 危险。Language models can refuse without their internal states reflecting that refusal, and the specific ways they fail differ substantially across architectures.
