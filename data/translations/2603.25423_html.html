摘要。Abstract.

微视频的兴起重塑了错误信息的传播方式，扩大了其传播速度、影响范围以及对公众信任的影响。现有的基准通常侧重于单一欺骗类型，忽视了涉及多模式操作、人工智能生成的内容、认知偏差和脱离上下文的重用的现实世界案例的多样性。与此同时，大多数检测模型缺乏细粒度的归因，限制了可解释性和实用性。为了解决这些差距，我们引入The rise of micro-videos has reshaped how misinformation spreads, amplifying its speed, reach, and impact on public trust. Existing benchmarks typically focus on a single deception type, overlooking the diversity of real-world cases that involve multimodal manipulation, AI-generated content, cognitive bias, and out-of-context reuse. Meanwhile, most detection models lack fine-grained attribution, limiting interpretability and practical utility. To address these gaps, we introduce野生假长凳WildFakeBench，一个包含超过 10,000 个真实世界微视频的大规模基准，涵盖不同的错误信息类型和来源，每个视频都带有专家定义的归因标签。在此基础上，我们开发, a large-scale benchmark of over 10,000 real-world micro-videos covering diverse misinformation types and sources, each annotated with expert-defined attribution labels. Building on this foundation, we develop假代理FakeAgent，一个受德尔菲启发的多主体推理框架，它将多模态理解与外部证据相结合，以进行基于归因的分析。 FakeAgent 联合分析内容并检索证据，以识别操纵行为、识别认知和人工智能生成的模式，并检测断章取义的错误信息。大量实验表明，FakeAgent 在所有错误信息类型上始终优于现有的 MLLM，而 WildFakeBench 为推进可解释的微视频错误信息检测提供了一个现实且具有挑战性的测试平台。, a Delphi-inspired multi-agent reasoning framework that integrates multimodal understanding with external evidence for attribution-grounded analysis. FakeAgent jointly analyzes content and retrieved evidence to identify manipulation, recognize cognitive and AI-generated patterns, and detect out-of-context misinformation. 1. 简介Introduction

微视频重新定义了错误信息在网上的传播方式，成为新闻消费和公共话语的主要媒介Micro-videos have redefined how misinformation spreads online, becoming a major medium for news consumption and public discourse(Walker and Matsa, 2021)。这些平台在实现开放参与的同时，也加速了欺骗性内容的传播，对信息生态系统的公众信任提出了新的挑战。与基于文本的错误信息相比. While these platforms enable open participation, they also accelerate the circulation of deceptive content, posing new challenges to public trust in the information ecosystem. Compared with text-based misinformation(Zhang et al., 2024)、欺骗性微视频涉及, deceptive micro-videos involve欺骗形式多样且相互交织diverse and intertwined forms of deception，包括多模式操纵, including multimodal manipulation(Wang et al., 2025c; Liu et al., 2023a), AI生成的内容, AI-generated content(Batra et al., 2025; Gao et al., 2024; Liu et al., ), 认知偏差利用, cognitive bias exploitation(Chen et al., 2025a; Wang et al., 2024a)以及断章取义地重复使用真实的镜头, and out-of-context reuse of authentic footage(Xu et al., 2025b; Guo et al., 2024; Luo et al., 2021)。高级创作者利用这些变化来误导观众，而不会留下明显的不一致之处。. Advanced creators exploit these variations to mislead viewers without leaving obvious inconsistencies.

尽管多模态错误信息检测取得稳步进展Despite steady progress in multimodal misinformation detection(Choi and Ko, 2021; Shang et al., 2021; Qi et al., 2023a; Bu et al., 2024; Zeng et al., 2025b; Zong et al., 2024; Tong et al., 2024; Lu et al., 2025; Tong et al., 2025)，仍然存在两个主要差距。 一、现有, two major gaps remain. First, existing基准benchmarks仅限于特定的欺骗类型，例如 AIGC 或视觉操纵，因此无法捕捉现实世界错误信息的复杂性和混合性。 二、新兴are confined to specific deception types, such as AIGC or visual manipulation, and therefore fail to capture the complex and hybrid nature of real-world misinformation. Second, emerging基于推理的方法reasoning-based methods，特别是那些使用多模式大语言模型（MLLM）的, particularly those using multimodal large language models (MLLMs)(Hong et al., 2025; Tran, 2025; Niu et al., 2025; Xu et al., 2025b)，可以生成自然语言解释，但经常产生幻觉并且缺乏对外部证据的可验证归因，限制了事实检查者和从业者的可靠性。, can generate natural-language explanations but often hallucinate and lack verifiable attribution to external evidence, limiting their reliability for fact-checkers and practitioners.

为了弥合这些数据和推理差距，我们提出了两项​​互补的贡献，共同推进对现实世界微视频错误信息的可靠、基于证据的检测。 我们首先介绍一下To bridge these data and reasoning gaps, we present two complementary contributions that together advance reliable, evidence-based detection of real-world micro-video misinformation. We first introduce野生假长凳WildFakeBench，一个包含超过 10,000 个现实世界微视频的大规模基准，捕获了多样化且相互交织的欺骗策略。它提供了一个统一的测试平台，用于分析操纵、认知偏差、人工智能生成和上下文扭曲等错误信息的感知和推理方面。 在此资源之上，我们进一步开发, a large-scale benchmark of over 10,000 real-world micro-videos that capture diverse and intertwined deceptive strategies. It provides a unified testbed for analyzing both perceptual and reasoning aspects of misinformation across manipulation, cognitive bias, AI generation, and contextual distortion. On top of this resource, we further develop假代理FakeAgent，一个多主体推理框架，通过基于归因的分析来检测和解释错误信息。通过联合检查多模态内容和外部证据，它生成透明的推理链，从而增强可解释性和可靠性。这些贡献共同为研究和减少野外错误信息奠定了统一的基础。, a multi-agent reasoning framework that detects and explains misinformation through attribution-grounded analysis. By jointly examining multimodal content and external evidence, it generates transparent reasoning chains that enhance both interpretability and reliability. Together, these contributions establish a unified foundation for studying and mitigating misinformation in the wild.

Instead of opaque binary predictions, FakeAgent jointly analyzes multimodal content and open-world knowledge to (1) detect manipulations across modalities, (2) distinguish cognitive-bias and AI-generated semantics, and (3) retrieve and attribute supporting evidence for out-of-context misinformation. By integrating multi-view knowledge reasoning with explicit attribution, FakeAgent improves both the accuracy and interpretability of misinformation detection, paving the way for more transparent and reliable multimodal reasoning frameworks.

为了确保注释的可信度以及与经过验证的来源的一致性，我们策划了引用经过事实核查的事件的微视频To ensure the credibility of annotations and consistency with verified sources, we curated micro-videos referencing fact-checked events from政治事实PolitiFact和and the中国互联网联合辟谣平台China Internet Joint Rumor-Refuting Platform，两个国家认可的错误信息核实机构。该数据集涵盖微博、抖音、快手、YouTube、Instagram、哔哩哔哩六大平台，时间范围为2017年至2025年。, two nationally recognized authorities in misinformation verification.
