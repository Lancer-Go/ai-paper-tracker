几何感知框架增强了整个人脑动力学的无创映射A geometry aware framework enhances noninvasive mapping of whole human brain dynamics

摘要Abstract

无创电生理学缺乏准确重建全脑时空动力学同时结合个体皮质几何形状的方法，使得当前的脑电图和脑磁图源成像受到简单化或生物学上不可信的先验的限制。在这里，我们展示了嵌入患者特定的几何基础函数（GBF），即源自每个个体皮质表面的本征模式，提供了强大的解剖学约束，可以解决逆问题并提高重建保真度。该方法允许将源重建为神经动力学几何组织的线性组合。我们通过元源基准、任务诱发数据、静息状态网络、颅内刺激和癫痫数据来验证 GBF。结果表明，GBF 具有较高的定位精度，并捕获与解剖路径一致的快速时空动态。这些发现表明，自发的和诱发的全脑活动都可以通过数百种几何模式来描述，从而提供神经源的紧凑而准确的表示。通过将皮质几何学与电生理动力学联系起来，GBF 为科学和临床应用提供了多功能源成像工具。Non invasive electrophysiology lacks methods that accurately reconstruct whole brain spatiotemporal dynamics while incorporating individual cortical geometry, leaving current electroencephalography and magnetoencephalography source imaging limited by simplistic or biologically implausible priors. Here, we show that embedding patient-specific Geometric Basis Function (GBF), eigenmodes derived from each individual’s cortical surface, provides a powerful anatomic constraint that resolves the inverse problem and improves reconstruction fidelity. The method allows reconstruction of the sources as linear combinations of geometric organization of neural dynamics. We validate GBF across the Meta-Source Benchmark, task-evoked data, resting-state networks, intracranial stimulation, and epilepsy data. The results demonstrate that GBF yields high localization accuracy and captures fast spatiotemporal dynamics consistent with anatomical pathways. These findings suggest that both spontaneous and evoked whole-brain activity can be described by hundreds of geometric modes, providing a compact yet accurate representation of neural sources. By linking cortical geometry to electrophysiological dynamics, GBF offers a versatile source imaging tool for both scientific and clinical applications.

1 简介Introduction

脑电波是神经活动的核心特征，反映了不同大脑区域的同步振荡Brain waves are a core feature of neural activity, reflecting synchronized oscillations across different brain regions[baillet2017magnetoencephalography, vidaurre2018spontaneous, adamantidis2019oscillating]。这些波支撑着广泛的功能，从感觉处理和运动控制到复杂的认知任务. These waves underpin a wide range of functions, from sensory processing and motor control to complex cognitive tasks[shine2016dynamics, vogels2005neural]。通过将大脑活动组织成大规模网络，协调的脑电波支持注意力等基本过程. By organizing brain activity into large-scale networks, coordinated brain waves support essential processes such as attention[gaillard2022neural]、记忆巩固, memory consolidation[staresina2024coupled], 推理, reasoning[xu2023interacting]，和意识, and consciousness[raut2021global]。因此，理解脑电波动力学是神经科学的基础。鉴于脑电波的高时间动态. Understanding brain wave dynamics is thus fundamental to neuroscience. Given the high temporal dynamics of brain waves[timofeev2020spatio, liang2021cortex]，捕获它们需要具有高空间和时间分辨率的全脑成像。 这种能力对于理解大脑如何跨网络协调复杂的功能至关重要, capturing them requires whole-brain imaging with both high spatial and temporal resolution. This capability is essential for understanding how the brain coordinates complex functions across networks[edelman2015eeg, weiner2023propofol, coito2016directed]以及识别癫痫等疾病的异常动态and for identifying abnormal dynamics in conditions like epilepsy[coito2016directed, sun2022deep, cao2022virtual]。这种成像技术的进步可以改变我们绘制大规模静息态网络、查明任务相关区域和描绘病理网络的能力。因此，迫切需要一种能够以高时间和空间精度揭示全脑电波的技术。实现这一目标将大大增强我们对大脑动力学的理解，并改善临床诊断和针对性治疗。. Such imaging advances could transform our ability to map large-scale resting-state networks, pinpoint task-related areas, and delineate pathological networks. Therefore, there is a pressing need for a technique that can reveal whole-brain waves with both high temporal and spatial accuracy. Achieving this will substantially enhance our understanding of brain dynamics and improve clinical diagnostics and targeted treatments.

揭示快速变化的脑电波需要一种理想的功能成像技术，该技术具有两个关键特征：高时间分辨率和全脑覆盖。这些特征对于准确跟踪脑电波在大规模网络中的快速传播至关重要。 虽然功能磁共振成像 (fMRI) 提供出色的空间分辨率和全脑覆盖，但它是神经活动的间接测量，缺乏捕获快速电生理信号所需的时间分辨率Unraveling fast-changing brain waves requires an ideal functional imaging technique with two key characteristics: high temporal resolution and whole-brain coverage. These features are critical for accurately tracking the rapid propagation of brain waves across large-scale networks. While functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) offers excellent spatial resolution and whole-brain coverage, it is an indirect measure of neural activity and lacks the temporal resolution needed to capture fast electrophysiological signals[he2008multimodal, katwal2013measuring, hall2014relationship]。同时，侵入性电生理记录，例如皮质电图（ECoG）. Meanwhile, invasive electrophysiological recordings, such as electrocorticography (ECoG)[buzsaki2012origin]和立体脑电图（SEEG）and stereoelectroencephalography (SEEG)[jaber2024spatial]，提供很高的时间精度，但只能捕获局部区域，而使大脑的大部分区域未被观察到。这一差距给全脑电生理动力学成像带来了全面覆盖和高时空分辨率的重大挑战。脑电图（EEG）和脑磁图（MEG）等非侵入性技术提供高时间分辨率和全脑覆盖，使它们成为研究脑电波大规模通信的有前途的工具。 然而，传感器空间中 EEG/MEG 的空间分辨率不可避免地受到可用传感器数量和密度的限制, offer high temporal precision but can only capture localized areas, leaving much of the brain unobserved. This gap creates a major challenge in imaging whole-brain electrophysiological dynamics with both comprehensive coverage and high spatiotemporal resolution. Non-invasive techniques like electroencephalography (EEG) and magnetoencephalography (MEG) offer both high temporal resolution and whole-brain coverage, positioning them as promising tools for studying the large-scale communications of brain waves. However, the spatial resolution of EEG/MEG in sensor space is inevitably limited by the number and density of available sensors[wens2023exploring]。这强调了 EEG/MEG 源成像 (ESI) 的重要性，其旨在通过使用计算模型重建全脑源来提高空间分辨率. This underlines the importance of EEG/MEG source imaging (ESI), which aims to improve spatial resolution by using computational modeling to reconstruct whole-brain sources[liu2018detecting, sohrabpour2020noninvasive, sun2022deep]。 由于多个源配置可以解释相同的传感器信号，因此 ESI 是一个不适定问题. As multiple source configurations can explain the same sensor signals, ESI is an ill-posed problem[he2018electrophysiological]。解决这个问题需要结合有关神经源的先验信息. Addressing this issue requires incorporating prior information about neural sources[zhang2025artificial]。然而许多方法依赖于简单化或非生物学先验. Yet many methods rely on simplistic or non-biological priors[haufe2011large, petrov2012harmony, chowdhury2013meg]，限制了源重建的准确性和可解释性。因此，生物学先验和源建模的进步对于捕获脑电波的复杂性和改善全脑动力学的重建至关重要。, limiting the accuracy and interpretability of the source reconstructions. Thus, advances in biologically informed priors and source modeling are crucial for capturing the complexity of brain waves and improving the reconstruction of whole-brain dynamics.

脑电波由大脑的结构组织决定，包括皮质表面的几何形状和连接模式Brain waves are shaped by the brain’s structural organization, including the geometry of the cortical surface and connectivity patterns[pang2023geometric, zilles2013development, koller2024human, markello2022neuromaps]。神经活动的传播受到皮质折叠的限制，皮质折叠定义了大脑的表面几何形状. The propagation of neural activity is constrained by cortical folding, which defines the brain’s surface geometry[zilles2013development]，以及互补的结构和组织特征，例如白质连接和全脑功能梯度, and by complementary structural and organizational features such as white matter connections and whole-brain functional gradients[koller2024human, markello2022neuromaps]。最近的研究表明，通过功能磁共振成像观察到的全脑动力学可以通过几何基函数（GBF）有效地描述，即在从结构磁共振成像获得的每个个体的皮层表面上计算的拉普拉斯-贝尔特拉米本征模式. Recent studies have shown that whole-brain dynamics observed with fMRI can be effectively described by geometric basis functions (GBFs)—the Laplace–Beltrami eigenmodes computed on each individual’s cortical surface obtained from structural MRI[pang2023geometric, gabay2017cortical, cabral2023intrinsic, atasoy2018harmonic]。值得注意的是，据报道，在 32,000 个 fMRI 顶点中观察到的全脑活动可以通过线性组合仅用 200 个本征模来有效表示. Remarkably, it has been reported that the whole-brain activity, observed in 32,000 fMRI vertices, can be efficiently represented by merely 200 eigenmodes through a linear combination[pang2023geometric]。这一发现凸显了使用少量参与者特定的几何模式来表示大规模大脑动力学的潜力。鉴于 EEG 和 MEG 信号主要反映突触后皮层电流. This finding highlights the potential for using a small number of participant-specific geometric modes to represent large-scale brain dynamics. Given that EEG and MEG signals predominantly reflect postsynaptic cortical currents[gross2019magnetoencephalography, thio2023relative]，将这些相同的几何基础合并为 ESI 的空间先验，提供了一种增强重建准确性和神经生理学有效性的原则性方法。, incorporating these same geometric bases as spatial priors for ESI offers a principled approach to enhance reconstruction accuracy and neurophysiological validity.

在这里，我们介绍 GBF 框架，这是一种用于 EEG/MEG 源成像的几何约束方法，可根据每个大脑的内在皮质几何结构重建神经活动（图 1）。Here, we introduce the GBF framework, a geometry-constrained approach for EEG/MEG source imaging that reconstructs neural activity in alignment with each brain’s intrinsic cortical geometry (Fig.1）。 与依赖于简单先验的传统 ESI 方法不同，GBF 直接集成源自 MRI 的个性化皮质结构，为源成像提供解剖学基础的约束。 我们通过一系列分析来验证 GBF，包括综合基准、基于任务的脑电图、功能连接 (FC) 重建、颅内刺激和临床癫痫定位。 这些分析共同证明，将几何结构嵌入到源成像中可以实现大规模神经动力学的生物可解释和空间精确重建，将皮质解剖学与认知和临床环境中的功能活动联系起来。). Unlike conventional ESI methods that rely on simplistic priors, GBF directly integrates individualized cortical structure derived from MRI, providing anatomically grounded constraints for source imaging. We validate GBF through a series of analyses, including synthetic benchmarks, task-based EEG, functional connectivity (FC) reconstruction, intracranial stimulation, and clinical epilepsy localization. These analyses collectively demonstrate that embedding geometric structure into source imaging enables biologically interpretable and spatially precise reconstruction of large-scale neural dynamics, linking cortical anatomy with functional activity in both cognitive and clinical contexts.

Each term represents a specific functional theme, encapsulating brain regions associated with particular cognitive processes or behaviors. The value associated with each term represents the degree to which a statistical image reflects activation related to a given cognitive function or experimental condition as identified in Neurosynth’s large-scale text-mining analysis of neuroimaging studies. These functional terms are derived from a large-scale database of cognitive neuroscience studies, creating a semantic link between brain activity and cognitive function. For example, a term like ”visual processing” may encompass brain regions activated during tasks involving visual stimuli.

从这个带注释的集合中，我们构建了一个图像术语矩阵，该矩阵反映了大脑活动模式（由统计图像表示）与其相应的认知主题（由 Neurosynth 术语表示）之间的关系。然后我们应用主成分分析（PCA）来降低维度并提取最相关的特征。From this annotated set, we constructed an image-term matrix that reflects the relationship between brain activity patterns (as represented by the statistical images) and their corresponding cognitive themes (as represented by the Neurosynth terms). Then we applied Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to reduce the dimensionality and extract the most relevant features.

在 PCA 的每次迭代中，使用 Nilearn 库从图像项矩阵中提取 70 个空间分量。这个过程重复了 200 次迭代，总共产生了 14,000 个空间分量。然后，根据由相关功能项确定的共享主题，使用 K 最近邻 (KNN) 聚类算法将这些组件聚类成 200 个独特的空间地图。这种聚类过程反映了大脑活动模式中共享的认知和功能主题，提供了认知任务中常见的大规模大脑过程的表示。In each iteration of the PCA, 70 spatial components were extracted from the image-term matrix using the Nilearn library. This process was repeated in 200 iterations, yielding a total of 14,000 spatial components. These components were then clustered into 200 unique spatial maps using a K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) clustering algorithm, based on their shared topics, determined by the associated functional terms. This clustering process reflects shared cognitive and functional themes across brain activity patterns, providing a representation of large-scale brain processes commonly observed in cognitive tasks.

随后使用 Neuromaps 将聚类空间图转换为 fsaverage 表面空间The clustered spatial maps were subsequently transformed into the fsaverage surface space using Neuromaps[markello2022neuromaps, wu2018accurate]。这些最终的空间地图现在位于表面空间中，充当基准中的源空间地图。为了生成脑电图传感器数据，我们使用特定于参与者的头部模型进行正向建模。每个基于表面的源图都通过边界元法 (BEM) 正演模型投影到 EEG 头皮地形。为了模拟真实条件，我们添加了两种类型的噪声：(1) 受控信噪比 (SNR) 下的高斯白噪声，以及 (2) 从经验 EEG 协方差矩阵中采样的真实噪声（详细信息请参阅补充说明 1）。生成的数据集包含 200 个源-EEG 对，具有已知的真实源位置和功能描述符。. Each surface-based source map was projected to EEG scalp topographies via the boundary element method (BEM) forward model. To simulate realistic conditions, we added two types of noise: (1) Gaussian white noise at controlled signal-to-noise ratios (SNR), and (2) realistic noise sampled from empirical EEG covariance matrices (see Supplementary Note 1 for details). The resulting dataset comprises 200 source-EEG pairs with known ground-truth source locations and functional descriptors.
