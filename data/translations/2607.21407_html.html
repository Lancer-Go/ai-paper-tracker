1 简介Introduction

代理模型长期以来一直是计算物理学的主力，当直接数值模拟太慢而无法大规模探索或优化问题时，代理模型可以替代第一原理求解器Surrogate models have long been a workhorse of computational physics, standing in for first-principles solvers when direct numerical simulation is too slow to explore or optimize a problem at scale[42, 33, 23, 9]。它们在模拟最困难的地方最有用：多尺度物理、僵硬的控制方程以及大参数扫描或实时推理的需要。但这也是代孕妈妈最有可能失败的地方。仅当代理重现其行为最尖锐、接近阈值、分叉和陡峭空间梯度的系统时，代理才值得信赖；这些正是最难学习的区域。. They are most useful precisely where simulation is hardest: multiscale physics, stiff governing equations, and the need for large parameter scans or real-time inference. But this is also where a surrogate is most likely to fail. A surrogate is only trustworthy if it reproduces the system where its behavior is sharpest, near thresholds, bifurcations, and steep spatial gradients; these are exactly the regions that are hardest to learn.

刮掉层（SOL）等离子体是一个教科书案例。在 SOL 中，等离子体-中性和等离子体-材料相互作用是由动力学过程、详细的原子和分子速率以及作用于短时空尺度的鞘层物理决定的。然而，对于偏滤器设计、场景优化和反馈控制而言重要的量，即目标热通量、密度分布和辐射图，存在于整个边界的宏观尺度上，并在几毫秒到几秒内演变。预测性地桥接这些尺度对于当前和未来的设备至关重要，但它在计算上仍然具有挑战性Scrape-off-layer (SOL) plasma is a textbook case. In the SOL, plasma–neutral and plasma–material interactions are set by kinetic processes, detailed atomic and molecular rates, and sheath physics acting on short spatial and temporal scales. Yet the quantities that matter for divertor design, scenario optimization, and feedback control, namely the target heat fluxes, the density profiles, and the radiation patterns, live at the macroscopic scale of the whole boundary and evolve over milliseconds to seconds. Predictively bridging these scales is essential for current and future devices, yet it remains computationally challenging[34, 27].

对于 SOL 物理，最先进的工具是 SOLPS-ITER，它将二维 Braginskii 流体求解器 (B2.5) 与三角容器网格上的动力学蒙特卡罗中性输运模型 (EIRENE) 结合起来For SOL physics, the state-of-the-art tool is SOLPS-ITER, which couples a two-dimensional Braginskii fluid solver (B2.5) to a kinetic Monte Carlo neutral transport model (EIRENE) on a triangulated vessel mesh[28]。该多物理场代码已广泛用于设计和解释现有设备上的实验，以及评估未来机器的偏滤器概念。它的计算成本也很高。每次运行都解决一个僵硬的非线性定点问题，其挂钟时间通常超过几个小时，其收敛对初始化高度敏感，并且当扫描广泛的参数集或进入分离状态时，其故障率急剧上升。由于这些原因，对输入功率、气体喷流和传输系数的详尽扫描在计算上是令人望而却步的，并且求解器不能直接放置在优化循环或实时工作流程中。. This multiphysics code has been used extensively to design and interpret experiments on existing devices and to evaluate divertor concepts for future machines. It is also computationally expensive. Each run solves a stiff nonlinear fixed-point problem whose wall-clock time often exceeds several hours, whose convergence is highly sensitive to initialization, and whose failure rate climbs sharply when one scans broad parameter sets or enters detached regimes. For these reasons, exhaustive sweeps over input power, gas puff, and transport coefficients are computationally prohibitive, and the solver cannot be placed directly inside an optimization loop or a real-time workflow.

边缘等离子体的数据驱动替代物受到越来越多的关注，它们使用经过高保真模拟训练的简化模型或神经网络来跨参数空间进行插值Data-driven surrogates for edge plasmas have received growing attention, using reduced models or neural networks trained on high-fidelity simulations to interpolate across parameter space[14]。这些替代物已经恢复了基本特征，包括分离、目标通量和轮廓形状。然而，仍然存在两个限制。大多数仅限于少数固定位置的一维剖面，并且它们很少强制前向和反向映射之间的任何物理一致性。结果，预测到达时没有内置方法来判断它是否可信，而这正是在尖锐的、样本不足的情况下所需要的，在这种情况下，替代品最有可能是错误的。. These surrogates already recover the essential features, including detachment, target fluxes, and profile shapes. Two limitations persist, however. Most are confined to one-dimensional profiles at a handful of fixed locations, and they rarely enforce any physical consistency between the forward and inverse mappings. As a result, a prediction arrives with no built-in way to tell whether it can be trusted, which is exactly what one needs in the sharp, undersampled regimes where the surrogate is most likely to be wrong.

6 讨论Discussion

我们的框架建立在托卡马克边缘等离子体替代建模的流形和循环一致性范例的基础上。阿尼鲁德等人。Our framework builds on the manifold-and-cycle-consistency paradigm for surrogate modeling of tokamak edge plasmas. Anirudh et al.[3]使用学习低维潜在表示的 Wasserstein 自动编码器强制流形一致性；相反，我们使用条件 U-Net，其跳跃连接保留了精细的空间细节。两者都有一个核心见解，即循环一致性正则化逆模型，并且在两者中，重构的输出都被限制在学习的物理流形上。托卡马克设置也增加了其自身的困难：网格几何形状不规则，这导致了上述掩模的产生，并且场上有数百个尖锐的回收前沿，这些前沿不允许光滑的低维结构。enforce manifold consistency with a Wasserstein autoencoder that learns a low-dimensional latent representation; we instead use a conditional U-Net whose skip connections preserve fine spatial detail.
