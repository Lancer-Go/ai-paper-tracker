摘要Abstract

从研究文章中准确提取细致入微的上下文数据既费力又费时。在这里，我们研究了基于浏览器的前沿大型语言模型 (LLM) 的性能，以提取高度上下文化的信息。我们演示了四种逐步升级的工作流程，1）给定专家策划的提示和研究文章，大多数前沿法学硕士在数据提取方面表现良好，但在解释科学背景和细微差别方面可能会遇到困难，2）给定简单的说明，法学硕士可以编写自己的提示，几乎与专家编写的提示一样有效，3）研究文献的自主发现很困难，代理商要么错过或产生幻觉参考文献，4）法学硕士可以根据与紧密匹配的已发布指南创建新数据集人类专家法官，但仍然需要有人参与。这些发现共同定义了一个可审计的分工，其中专家指定证据标准，模型交叉检查重复提取，研究人员解决有争议的案例，为在不放弃专家监督的情况下扩大科学数据管理提供了一条实用途径。Accurately extracting nuanced, contextualized data from research articles is laborious and time intensive. Here, we investigate the performance of frontier, browser-based large language models (LLMs) to extract highly contextualized information. We demonstrate four escalating workflows, 1) given an expert curated prompt and research articles, most frontier LLMs perform well at data extraction, however can struggle with interpreting scientific context and nuance, 2) given simple instructions, LLMs can author their own prompts which were almost as eNective as expert-written prompts, 3) autonomous discovery of research literature was diNicult, agents either missed or hallucinated references, and 4) LLMs can create new datasets from published guidelines that closely match human-expert judges, but still require a human-in-the-loop. Together, these findings define an auditable division of labour in which experts specify the evidence standard, models cross-check repeated extractions and researchers resolve disputed cases, providing a practical route to scaling scientific data curation without relinquishing expert oversight.

结论Conclusion

这项研究表明，可靠的法学硕士辅助文献管理不仅取决于模型智能，还取决于及时的工程、精心策划的资源和人机交互验证。我们表明，当给定固定的文章集和专家提示时，当前的前沿模型可以恢复 90% 以上的真实值和实验条件。数值 aNinity 常数的准确度接近 99%。主要挑战是确定应该和不应该提取数值的细微差别和背景。重复运行还表明，单次运行可能会歪曲模型性能。This study demonstrates that reliable LLM-assisted literature curation depends not only on model intelligence, but also on prompt engineering, well curated sources and humanin-the-loop validation. We show that the current frontier models recovered more than 90% of ground-truth values and experimental conditions when given a fixed article set and an expert prompt. Numerical aNinity constants approached 99% accuracy. The principal challenge was establishing the nuance and context under which numerical values should and shouldn’t be extracted. Repeated runs also showed that a single run can misrepresent model performance.

我们进一步表明，法学硕士可以根据知情的用户简介生成有效的主提示。尽管这些提示与专家编写的提示的性能不符，但它们可以为迭代用户细化提供强有力的起点。更大的自主权暴露出更实质性的限制。当需要深入研究代理来定位和解释源文献时，即使是最强大的系统平均也只能恢复 53.7% 的专家策划的主要参考文献，并且两个系统生成了幻觉引用。We further show that LLMs can generate eNective master prompts from an informed-user brief. Although these prompts did not match the performance of the expert-written prompt, they could provide a strong starting point for iterative user-refinement. Greater autonomy exposed a more substantial constraint. When deep-research agents were required to locate and interpret the source literature, even the strongest system recovered only 53.7% of the expert-curated primary references on average, and two systems generated hallucinated citations.

一个核心发现是模型共识是法学硕士输出质量控制的重要组成部分。将重复的跨模型评估应用于不相关的类器官报告数据集，不仅揭示了模型之间的分歧，而且还揭示了用于建立参考标准的人类评审员之间的分歧。如果审稿人同意，模型共识会准确再现他们的评估；如果模型存在分歧，这些案例可以提交专家审查。跨模型的收敛还有助于暴露原始心脏参考数据集中的错误，表明共识可以审核提取的数据和用于评估它们的标准。A central finding is that model consensus is an important part of LLM-output quality control. Applying repeated and cross-model assessments to an unrelated organoidreporting dataset revealed disagreement not only between models, but also between the human reviewers used to establish the reference standard. Where reviewers agreed, model consensus closely reproduced their assessments; where models disagreed, those cases could be directed to expert review. Convergence across models also helped expose errors in the original cardiac reference dataset, showing that consensus can audit both extracted data and the standards used to evaluate them.

这些发现共同为可重复的法学硕士辅助文献管理建立了一个实用框架。专家明确问题、来源和证据标准；模型跨系统执行重复提取；人工审查侧重于有争议的、相关的或高影响力的案件。这种分工提供了一条透明且可审计的途径来构建高质量的科学数据集，同时保留人工验证、反馈和科学判断。Together, these findings establish a practical framework for reproducible LLM-assisted literature curation. Experts define the question, sources and evidence standard; models perform repeated extractions across systems; and human review focuses on contested, contextual or high-impact cases. This division of labour provides a transparent and auditable route to constructing high-quality scientific datasets while retaining human verification, feedback and scientific judgement.
