    网格路由GridRoute (Li et al., 2025a)通过要求模型返回从起点 A 到终点 B 的有效相邻网格坐标序列来评估寻路，避开障碍物且不进行对角线移动。assesses pathfinding by asking the model to return a valid sequence of adjacent grid coordinates from a starting point A to an end point B, avoiding obstacles and without diagonal movements.

  4. d)

    PPNL (Aghzal et al., 2025)将寻路任务扩展到多目标设置，其中模型必须通过中间点的无序列表才能产生正确的解决方案，这使得它比 GridRoute 更困难。extends the pathfinding task to a multi-objective setting, where the model must pass through an unordered list of intermediate points to produce a correct solution, making it more difficult than GridRoute.

5. 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了This paper presents地理基准法学硕士GeoBenchLLM，一个包含十二个数据集的通用基准，用于评估大型语言模型在不同认知水平的地理相关任务中的能力。我们的基准测试包含八项任务，包括, a general benchmark containing twelve datasets for assessing the abilities of large language models in geo-related tasks at different cognitive levels. Our benchmark contains eight tasks ranging from坐标预测Coordinates Prediction to 寻路Pathfinding。我们确保使基准尽可能可供未来的研究使用。我们还引入了新的指标来评估一代背景下的开放性问题。最后，我们提出了由 Qwen3 系列的带思考和不带思考的小型模型以及从 20B 到 120B 参数的较大模型组成的基线。我们发现参数差距可以通过思维来缩小，特别是对于. We made sure to make the benchmark as accessible as possible to future research. We also introduced new metrics to evaluate open questions in a generation context. Finally, we present baselines consisting of small models from the Qwen3 family with and without thinking, as well as larger models ranging from 20B to 120B parameters. We found that the parameter gap could be closed using thinking, especially for推理Reasoning和and应用Application任务。tasks.
