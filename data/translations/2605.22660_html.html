道德语义在机器翻译中幸存：Moral Semantics Survive Machine Translation:
来自道德基础语料库的跨语言证据Cross-Lingual Evidence from Moral Foundations Corpora

Maciej Skórski 0000-0003-2997-7539 maciej.skorski@gmail.com University of LuxembourgLuxembourg
摘要。Abstract.

道德语言是微妙且随文化而变化的，因此 很难忠实地跨语言翻译。惯用语 表达方式、俚语和文化参考引入了难以避免的翻译 文物。然而自动化的道德价值观 分类取决于特定于语言的注释语料库 几乎只以英语存在。Moral language is subtle and culturally variable, making it difficult to translate faithfully across languages. Idiomatic expressions, slang, and cultural references introduce hard-to-avoid translation artifacts. Yet automated moral values classification depends on language-specific annotated corpora that exist almost exclusively in English.

我们研究基于法学硕士的翻译是否可以弥补这一点 间隙，以波兰语作为测试用例。使用We investigate whether LLM-based translation can bridge this gap, taking Polish as a test case. Using∼\sim50k 道德注释 来自不同主题的社交媒体帖子，我们应用 有原则的四方法验证管道：LaBSE 跨语言 嵌入相似性、中心核对齐（CKA）、LLM-as-judge 评估和深度学习分类器奇偶测试。我们表明 尽管在处理俚语、粗俗语和粗话方面存在缺陷 文化负载的表达方式，直接翻译保留微妙 道德线索足以被跨语言机器收获 学习——平均余弦相似度为 0.86，AUC 差距为 所有基础的 0.01–0.02 在语言模型的微调下进一步接近。50k morally-annotated social media posts from a diverse range of topics, we apply a principled four-method validation pipeline: LaBSE cross-lingual embedding similarity, Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA), LLM-as-judge evaluation, and deep learning classifier parity tests. We show that despite shortcomings in handling slang, vulgarity, and culturally-loaded expressions, direct translation preserves subtle moral cues well enough to be harvested by cross-lingual machine learning — with mean cosine similarity of 0.86 and AUC gaps of 0.01–0.02 across all foundations closing further under fine-tuning of language models.

这些结果表明机器翻译是一种实用的 语言道德价值观研究的经济有效途径 目前该领域资源不足。我们演示这一点 波兰语作为斯拉夫语族的代表语言，预计 泛化到相关语言。These results demonstrate that machine translation is a practical and cost-effective path to moral values research in languages currently under-resourced in this domain. We demonstrate this for Polish as a representative Slavic language, with expected generalisation to related languages.

1. 简介Introduction

道德语言是微妙的。讽刺将其颠倒过来。文化习语模糊不清 它。寄存器移位会稀释它。跨语言大规模研究 和文化，需要一个原则框架。道德基础 理论（MFT）Moral language is subtle. Irony inverts it. Cultural idiom obscures it. Register shifts dilute it. Studying it at scale, across languages and cultures, requires a principled framework. Moral Foundations Theory (MFT)(Haidt and Joseph, 2004; Graham et al., 2013)提供 正是如此：五个道德维度的跨文化分类法——provides exactly that: a cross-cultural taxonomy of five moral dimensions —照顾/伤害care/harm, 公平/作弊fairness/cheating, 忠诚/背叛loyalty/betrayal, 权威/颠覆authority/subversion, 和, and神圣/堕落sanctity/degradation——扎根于数十年 跨文化道德心理学研究。— grounded in decades of cross-cultural moral psychology research.

5. 讨论Discussion

总体而言。Overall.来自四种独立验证方法的收敛证据 支持一个也许令人惊讶的结论：基于 LLM 的 ENThe convergent evidence from four independent validation methods supports a perhaps surprising conclusion: LLM-based EN→\toPL 翻译将道德语义内容保留在足够的水平 用于下游分类。道德语言——带有讽刺意味， 习语和文化敏感性——事实证明更稳健 翻译比研究法学硕士的局限性可能 建议PL translation preserves moral-semantic content at a level sufficient for downstream classification. Moral language — with its irony, idiom, and cultural sensitivity — proves more robust to translation than research on LLM limitations might suggest(Nicholas and Bhatia, 2023).

权威差距。The authority gap.AUC 与权威的差距虽小但在统计上可检测到 (0.014) 是一个值得研究的例外。我们将此归因于 翻译错误但真正的跨文化差异如何 波兰语话语中表达了权威——一项观察 符合跨文化MFT 文学The small but statistically detectable AUC gap on authority (0.014) is the one exception worth examining. We attribute this not to translation error but to genuine cross-cultural divergence in how authority is expressed in Polish discourse — an observation consistent with the cross-cultural MFT literature(Graham et al., 2013; Skorski and Landowska, 2025b), 其中 权威规范显示出最大的跨国差异 对域变化的最高敏感性。未来的工作可以验证 这是通过使用本地注释器重新注释波兰语样本来实现的。, where authority norms show the highest cross-national variance and the highest sensitivity to domain variation. Future work could verify this by re-annotating a Polish sample with native annotators.

更难的语料库。Harder corpora.相对于 MFTC 分数较低（CKA 0.804，平均评判 8.9/10） MFRC（CKA 0.833，平均评判 9.2/10）归因于 AAVE 重 Twitter 内容，翻译在哪里 必然涉及释义。即便如此，分类器奇偶性确认翻译后的数据仍然可用于训练。The lower MFTC scores (CKA 0.804, mean judge 8.9/10) relative to MFRC (CKA 0.833, mean judge 9.2/10) are attributable to AAVE-heavy Twitter content, where translation necessarily involves paraphrase. Even so, classifier parity confirms the translated data remains usable for training.

实用的可达性。Practical accessibility.管道可访问：The pipeline is accessible:∼\sim50k 条帖子翻译为 大约 200 美元，使语料库扩展到更多 无需大量注释即可使用的语言 预算。50k posts translate for approximately 200 USD, making corpus extension to additional languages feasible without large annotation budgets.

推广到其他斯拉夫语言。Generalisation to other Slavic languages.波兰语是形态最复杂的语言之一 斯拉夫语族，有七个语法格和丰富的屈折变化 形态学。神经跨语言迁移研究表明 语系内直接的形态相关性 促进知识转移Polish is among the most morphologically complex languages in the Slavic family, with seven grammatical cases and rich inflectional morphology. Research on neural cross-lingual transfer shows that morphological relatedness within a language family directly facilitates knowledge transfer(Kann et al., 2017)，建议 我们对波兰语的结果提供了一个合理的下限 更广泛的斯拉夫家族。, suggesting that our results for Polish provide a reasonable lower bound for the broader Slavic family.

局限性。Limitations.标签继承自英文注释 没有用波兰语重新注释，这妨碍了测量 跨文化标签的转变。该管道已在 Reddit 上进行了验证，并且 Twitter 的话语风格；域名转移到新闻或议会 尽管主题的多样性，语料库可能需要重新评估 涵盖——日常道德话语、政治辩论、社会 运动和自然灾害——表明合理的鲁棒性 跨越道德语言使用的共同领域。最后提示一下 工程是在没有波兰人参与的情况下进行的 演讲者——道德话语模式的潜在盲点 特定于波兰文化背景的一次性示例未涵盖。Labels are inherited from English annotations without re-annotation in Polish, which precludes measuring cross-cultural label shift. The pipeline was validated on Reddit and Twitter discourse styles; domain transfer to news or parliamentary corpora may require re-evaluation, though the diversity of topics covered — everyday moral discourse, political debate, social movements, and natural disasters — suggests reasonable robustness across common domains of moral language use. Finally, prompt engineering was conducted without involvement of native Polish speakers — a potential blind spot for moral discourse patterns specific to Polish cultural context not captured by the one-shot examples.

6. 结论Conclusion

我们提出了一条经过验证的途径来扩展英国道德价值观 通过法学硕士翻译将语料库翻译成波兰语。跨多元化测试 我们发现主题范围和 MFT 子语料库 翻译很好地保留了微妙的道德暗示 跨语言机器学习来收获它们——AUC 差距 在 0.01-0.02 范围内，通过微调几乎接近。道德 语义在机器翻译中幸存下来，开启了一个实用且 波兰语道德价值观研究的成本效益途径 延伸，更广泛的斯拉夫语系。We present a validated pipeline for extending English moral values corpora to Polish via LLM translation. Testing across a diverse range of topics and MFT subcorpora, we find that translation preserves subtle moral cues well enough for cross-lingual machine learning to harvest them — with AUC gaps in the range 0.01–0.02, nearly closed by fine-tuning. Moral semantics survive machine translation, opening a practical and cost-effective path for moral values research in Polish and, by extension, the broader Slavic family.
