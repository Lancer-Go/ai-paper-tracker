5 结论和未来的工作Conclusion and Future Work

我们推出了 JoyAI-LLM Flash，这是一种最先进的中型指令语言模型，拥有 30 亿个激活参数和 480 亿个总参数。 JoyAI-LLM Flash 使用 Muon 优化器对 20 万亿个文本标记进行预训练，然后在不同环境中进行大规模监督微调 (SFT)、直接偏好优化 (DPO) 和强化学习 (RL)。 JoyAI-LLM Flash 在前沿知识、推理、编码任务和代理能力方面均取得了强劲的表现。展望未来，我们的目标是通过整合持续学习和持久记忆来扩展模型的范例，使法学硕士能够随着时间的推移动态地适应和保留知识。We present JoyAI-LLM Flash, a state-of-the-art medium-sized instruct language model with 3 billion activated parameters and 48 billion total parameters. JoyAI-LLM Flash was pretrained on 20 trillion text tokens using Muon optimizer, followed by large-scale supervised fine-tuning (SFT), direct preference optimization (DPO), and reinforcement learning (RL) across diverse environments. JoyAI-LLM Flash achieves strong performance across frontier knowledge, reasoning, coding tasks, and agentic capabilities. Moving forward, we aim to extend the model’s paradigm by integrating continual learning and persistent memory, enabling the LLM to dynamically adapt and retain knowledge over time.
