KEditVis：用于知识编辑的可视化分析系统KEditVis: A Visual Analytics System for Knowledge Editing
大型语言模型of Large Language Models

Zhenning Chen    Hanbei Zhan    Yanwei Huang    Xin Wu    Dazhen Deng    Di Weng    and Yingcai Wu
摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在回答事实问题方面表现出卓越的能力，但有时会提供错误的答案。为了解决这个问题，知识编辑技术已经成为纠正法学硕士事实信息的有效方法。然而，典型的知识编辑工作流程很难确定用于编辑的最佳模型层集，并且依赖于提供不足指导的摘要指标。这种透明度的缺乏阻碍了最佳编辑策略的有效比较和识别。在本文中，我们提出了 KEditVis，这是一种新颖的可视化分析系统，旨在帮助用户通过交互式可视化更深入地了解知识编辑，改善编辑结果，并为知识编辑算法的未来发展发现有价值的见解。通过KEditVis，用户可以选择合适的图层作为编辑目标，探索编辑无效的原因，进行更有针对性、更有效的编辑。我们的评估（包括使用场景、专家访谈和用户研究）验证了系统的有效性和可用性。Large Language Models (LLMs) demonstrate exceptional capabilities in factual question answering, yet they sometimes provide incorrect responses. To address this issue, knowledge editing techniques have emerged as effective methods for correcting factual information in LLMs. However, typical knowledge editing workflows struggle with identifying the optimal set of model layers for editing and rely on summary indicators that provide insufficient guidance. This lack of transparency hinders effective comparison and identification of optimal editing strategies. In this paper, we present KEditVis, a novel visual analytics system designed to assist users in gaining a deeper understanding of knowledge editing through interactive visualizations, improving editing outcomes, and discovering valuable insights for the future development of knowledge editing algorithms. With KEditVis, users can select appropriate layers as the editing target, explore the reasons behind ineffective edits, and perform more targeted and effective edits. Our evaluation, including usage scenarios, expert interviews, and a user study, validates the effectiveness and usability of the system.

我们的目标是帮助LLM从业者更精准、更有效地进行知识编辑，并探索有利于知识编辑技术发展的相关见解。在在线会议期间，我们与领域专家进行了头脑风暴会议和原型讨论。这个迭代过程使我们能够完善和确定可视化系统的关键用户需求。最终我们总结出以下六点用户需求。Our goal is to help LLM practitioners perform knowledge editing more precisely and effectively, as well as to explore relevant insights that can benefit the development of knowledge editing techniques. Users need efficient ways to create facts and comprehensive prompts across multiple categories to evaluate edit effectiveness and potential knowledge interference. R2：选择适当的模型层进行编辑。R2: Selection of appropriate model layers for editing.在典型的工作流程中，自动执行知识编辑方法无法定位最合适的层，从而导致结果不佳。 系统应提取并可视化与预期编辑相关的关键模型数据，以实现明智的图层选择。用户应该能够分析和权衡权衡，选择适当的层，以及通过比较编辑前后的可视化来了解模型的变化。In typical workflows, automatically executing knowledge editing methods fails to target the most suitable layers, leading to poor results. The system should extract and visualize key model data relevant to the intended edit for informed layer selections. R3：不同编辑策略的比较分析。R3: Comparative analysis across editing strategies.由于编辑过程缺乏透明度，比较编辑策略和理解不良结果变得困难。系统应支持不同编辑方案的比较分析。 用户应该能够分析编辑策略及其相应结果之间的差异，获得关于为什么某些方法有效而其他方法产生次优结果的实际见解。Due to the lack of transparency in the editing process, it becomes difficult to compare editing strategies and understand poor outcomes. Users should be able to perform multiple edits on a model in succession, with each edit building on previous results. For providing a more understandable approach for users, the experts suggested analyzing from the perspective of data input-output and whether shifts have occurred.

When comparing pre- and post-editing ranking charts, they observed that successful edits maintained consistency in where the target token initially formed and its general trajectory. This pattern could potentially indicate the toxicity of edits. Through comparative visualizations, they also found minimal cosine similarity changes in edited layers but significant changes in subsequent layers, which P2 and P7 attributed to greater involvement of related tokens.

6 讨论Discussion

影响Implications。 首先，我们基于分层的方法揭示了促进知识编辑研究的隐藏模式。与仅关注算法和总体指标的传统方法不同，我们的交互式方法使用户能够通过序列分析、比较分析和个案检查轻松了解编辑影响、识别问题并设计有效的策略。 其次，我们的交互式可视化可以帮助用户分析和选择编辑图层，识别不完美的结果并执行有效的编辑。自动化方法有局限性——由最小余弦相似度值自动识别的范围并不总是最佳的，并且目标标记有时不会出现在排名图表中。 这些限制可以通过人机参与的干预来解决，实现上下文判断，引入领域专业知识，并允许超越自动化指标的细致决策。 第三，虽然之前的研究. First, our layer-based approach reveals hidden patterns that advance knowledge editing research. Unlike traditional approaches focusing only on algorithms and overall metrics, our interactive method enables users to easily understand editing impacts, identify issues, and design effective strategies through sequence analysis, comparative analysis, and case-by-case examination. Second, our interactive visualization helps users analyze and select editing layers, identify imperfect results, and perform effective edits. Automated methods have limitations – the range automatically identified by minimum cosine similarity values is not always optimal, and target tokens sometimes do not appear in ranking charts. These limitations can be addressed through human-in-the-loop intervention, enabling contextual judgment, introducing domain expertise, and allowing nuanced decision-making beyond automated metrics. Third, while previous research[hong2024interpretability, huang-etal-2024-commonsense]虽然证明了跨数据集的余弦相似度和标记投影方法的有效性，但我们的工作表明，在某些情况下依赖单一选择方法可能不可靠。这一发现建议针对不同的情况结合各种选择方法。demonstrated the effectiveness of cosine similarity and token projection approaches across datasets, our work revealed that relying on single selection methods can be unreliable for certain instances. This finding suggests combining various selection methods for different instances.

普遍性Generalizability。 我们的可视化分析方法是可推广的，并扩展到不同的编辑方法和法学硕士，我们的贡献独立于特定的编辑技术或法学硕士。 我们的系统支持不同的知识编辑后端. Our visual analytics approach is generalizable and extends to different editing methods and LLMs, with our contributions independent of specific editing techniques or LLMs. Our system supports different knowledge editing backends[wang-etal-2024-easyedit]。本地修改方法（目前主流）可能在算法细节上有所不同，但都遵循“定位然后编辑”策略并与我们的系统保持兼容。 我们的系统适用于经典的基于 Transformer 的 LLM，例如 GPT 系列。 知识编辑方法本质上支持不同的法学硕士，因为它们在大多数法学硕士共享的基本架构组件上运行，而不依赖于特定的实现. Local modification methods – currently mainstream – may differ in algorithmic details but all follow the locate-then-edit strategy and remain compatible with our system. Our system is applicable to classical Transformer-based LLMs, such as the GPT family. Knowledge editing methods inherently support different LLMs because they operate on the fundamental architectural components shared by most LLMs, without depending on specific implementations[fang2025alphaedit, meng2022mass]。 层选择方法还利用了不同LLM之间的共性，支持各种LLM架构. Layer selection methods also utilize the commonalities between different LLMs, supporting various LLM architectures[hong2024interpretability, huang-etal-2024-commonsense]。 此外，我们的方法可以扩展到知识编辑之外，例如模型蒸馏。通过视觉比较，我们可以确定哪些层包含应优先保留在精炼模型中的关键知识。. In addition, our approach can extend beyond knowledge editing, such as model distillation. Through visual comparison, we can determine which layers contain critical knowledge that should be prioritized for retention in distilled models.

局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work。 首先，由于人类的参与，很难衡量 KEditVis 和基线在我们的用户研究中是否投入了完全相同的努力，因为目前没有方法来衡量人类和机器是否投入了同等的努力。未来可以进行更精细的定量比较，进一步提高严谨性。 其次，随着编辑的事实和模型层数量的增加，可能需要尝试更多的方案，从而导致更高的计算成本和用户工作量。未来的工作可以探索有效的方案推荐方法。 第三，未来的工作可以比较法学硕士的不同编辑方法，以确定最佳方法并探索神经元级编辑以实现更精细的控制。. First, due to human involvement, it is difficult to measure whether KEditVis and the baselines invest completely equivalent efforts in our user study, as there is currently no approach to measure whether humans and machines invest equal efforts. More refined quantitative comparisons can be conducted to further enhance rigor in the future. Second, as the number of edited facts and model layers increases, more schemes may need to be attempted, leading to higher computational costs and user efforts. Future work could explore efficient scheme recommendation methods. Third, future work could compare different editing methods across LLMs to identify optimal approaches and explore neuron-level editing for finer control.

7 结论Conclusion

本文介绍了 KEditVis，这是一种用于 LLM 知识编辑的新型可视化分析系统，可帮助用户定位适当的模型层、识别次优编辑的原因并有效改善结果。 我们的评估包括使用场景、专家访谈和用户研究，证明了其有效性并揭示了有价值的见解。 随着知识编辑已成为一个活跃的研究领域，这项工作通过交互式方法解决了当前工作流程的限制，有助于知识编辑方法的进步。This paper presents KEditVis, a novel visual analytics system for LLM knowledge editing that helps users target appropriate model layers, identify reasons for suboptimal edits, and improve outcomes effectively. Comprising usage scenarios, expert interviews, and a user study, our evaluation demonstrates its effectiveness and reveals valuable insights. As knowledge editing has become an active research area, this work addresses current workflow limitations through an interactive approach, contributing to the advancement of knowledge editing methodologies.
