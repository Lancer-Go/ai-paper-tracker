2.1 人类社会的集体智慧Collective Intelligence in Human Society

集体智慧被广泛地定义为一种分布式智慧，当群体实时协调以调动和整合分散的知识时就会出现这种形式。Collective intelligence is broadly defined as a form of distributed intelligence that emerges when groups coordinate in real time to mobilize and integrate dispersed knowledge(Woolley et al., 2010a)。早就研究过了 作为人类社会的一个基本现象。首先，集体智慧是. It has long been studied as a fundamental phenomenon of human societies. First, collective intelligence is可测量的measurable: Woolley et al. (2010b)证明跨不同任务的群体表现可以通过单个潜在因素来预测，并且该因素更多地取决于社会敏感性和互动结构而不是个人能力。第二，集体智慧demonstrated that group performance across diverse tasks can be predicted by a single latent factor, and that this factor depends more on social sensitivity and interaction structure than on individual ability. Second, collective intelligence is可分解的decomposable: Malone et al. 2.2 从个体代理人到代理人协会From Individual Agents to Agent Societies

最近的工作已逐步将基于法学硕士的系统从个体自主代理扩展到网络社会Recent work has progressively scaled LLM-based systems from individual autonomous agents to networked societies(Piao et al., 2025; Piatti et al., 2024)，将焦点从孤立的决策转移到持续的多主体交互中出现的集体行为。这一轨迹分三个阶段展开。, shifting the focus from isolated decision-making to collective behavior emerging from sustained multi-agent interaction. This trajectory unfolds in three stages.

第一阶段为个体智能体配备自主能力：推理-行动循环The first stage equipped individual agents with autonomous capabilities: reasoning-acting loops(Yao et al., 2022)、自我完善机制, self-improvement mechanisms(Shinn et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2025b; Wang et al., 2025)，以及大规模工具的使用, and large-scale tool usage(Qin et al., 2023; Patil et al., 2024)在开放式环境中。第二阶段引入多智能体系统（MAS），多个智能体之间结构化交互，提高任务性能 通过协调讨论、基于角色的协作和工作流程编排in open-ended environments. The second stage introduced multi-agent system (MAS), structured interaction among multiple agents, improving task performance through coordinated discussion, role-based collaboration, and workflow orchestration(Li et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024; Guan et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024a; Zhu et al., 2024; Campedelli et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024b; Fontana et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2025a)。同时，还部署了多代理系统来模拟金融市场等复杂环境. The implicit promise driving this trajectory is compelling: if human societies produce collective intelligence through open interaction, scaling agent populations should yield the same. Yet this assumption remains entirely untested; existing evaluations focus on scale, individual behavior quality, or simulation realism, but none directly measure whether agent interactions produce outcomes beyond what individuals achieve alone.

5 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们首次对大规模自主人工智能代理社会中的集体智慧进行了实证评估。使用 MoltBook 和建议的In this work, we present the first empirical evaluation of collective intelligence in a large-scale autonomous AI agent society. Using MoltBook and the proposed\我们的\ours，我们跨三个层次探究集体行为：联合推理、信息合成和基本交互。 我们的实验表明，集体智慧不会自发出现。代理社会在复杂的推理任务上无法超越个体前沿模型，很少综合分布式信息，甚至经常无法完成琐碎的协调任务。进一步的分析表明，主要的瓶颈是代理之间的交互极其稀疏和浅层：大多数帖子没有收到回复，并且许多回复都是通用的或与对话上下文不一致。 这些发现表明, we probe collective behavior across three tiers: joint reasoning, information synthesis, and basic interaction. Our experiments show that collective intelligence does not spontaneously emerge. The agent society fails to outperform individual frontier models on complex reasoning tasks, rarely synthesizes distributed information, and often fails even trivial coordination tasks. Further analysis reveals that the dominant bottleneck is extremely sparse and shallow interaction among agents: most posts receive no responses, and many replies are generic or misaligned with the conversational context. These findings suggest that仅靠规模不足以发挥集体智慧scale alone is insufficient for collective intelligence。未来的智能体社会可能需要促进持续互动、共享对话环境和协调信息交换的机制。. Future agent societies will likely require mechanisms that promote sustained interaction, shared conversational context, and coordinated information exchange.
