1 简介Introduction

脑电图 (EEG) 是临床环境中测量大脑活动最容易获得、时间分辨且经济有效的方式。尽管有这些优势，基于脑电图的生物标志物在中枢神经系统 (CNS) 疾病的临床试验中仍未得到充分利用，其中 II/III 期失败率超过 85%，每种批准药物的平均成本为 26 亿美元Electroencephalography (EEG) is the most accessible, temporally resolved, and cost-effective modality for measuring brain activity in clinical settings. Despite these advantages, EEG-based biomarkers remain underutilized in clinical trials for central nervous system (CNS) disorders, where Phase II/III failure rates exceed 85% and the average cost per approved drug is $2.6 billion[1, 2]。主要障碍是难以从脑电图记录中提取可在患者、记录设置和临床条件之间传输的通用特征. A primary barrier is the difficulty of extracting generalizable features from EEG recordings that transfer across patients, recording setups, and clinical conditions[3].

基础模型，即通过自我监督学习在大量未标记语料库上进行预训练的大型神经网络，已经改变了自然语言处理Foundation models, large neural networks pretrained on massive unlabeled corpora via self-supervised learning, have transformed natural language processing[4]和计算机视觉and computer vision[5]通过学习可转移的表示，以最少的标记数据泛化下游任务。 2024-2025 年，这种范式扩展到脑电图，多个小组发布了预训练的脑电图基础模型，能够在脑机接口 (BCI) 和临床任务上进行跨受试者传输by learning transferable representations that generalize across downstream tasks with minimal labeled data. In 2024–2025, this paradigm reached EEG, with several groups releasing pretrained EEG foundation models capable of cross-subject transfer on brain–computer interface (BCI) and clinical tasks[6, 7, 8, 9, 10].

然而，该领域目前缺乏标准化评估协议下这些模型的系统比较。已发布的基准通常评估与预训练数据具有相同分布的数据集上的模型，使用不同的评估协议（微调与线性探测、受试者内与跨受试者），并且很少同时测试多个临床任务。一个关键的差距仍然存在：虽然基础模型在保留受试者上显示出强大的冷冻探针性能However, the field currently lacks systematic comparisons of these models under standardized evaluation protocols. Published benchmarks typically evaluate models on datasets from the same distribution as pretraining data, use varying evaluation protocols (fine-tuning vs. linear probing, within-subject vs. cross-subject), and rarely test on multiple clinical tasks simultaneously. Leave-One-Subject-Out (LOSO) cross-validation is the strictest protocol, ensuring the model has never seen any data from the test subject during training. Most published foundation model benchmarks use within-dataset evaluation, potentially inflating performance when pretraining and evaluation datasets share distributions (e.g., both from the TUH corpus). 4.8 统计分析Statistical Analysis

不确定性是在每次评估所使用的受试者或倍数水平上报告的。重复的种子不被视为额外的独立折叠。对于阿尔茨海默病，报告的区间是近似平均值加或减两个标准误差区间，而不是引导区间，并且推断基于配对差异或受试者级别聚合，而不是将一个模型的区间范围与另一个模型的平均值进行比较。Uncertainty is reported at the subject or fold level used by each evaluation. Repeated seeds are not treated as additional independent folds. For Alzheimer’s disease, the reported interval is an approximate mean-plus-or-minus-two-standard-errors interval rather than a bootstrap interval, and inference is based on paired differences or subject-level aggregation rather than comparing one model’s interval bound with another model’s mean.

5 讨论Discussion

5.1 解读Interpretation

结果不支持基于模型规模、通道数或适应数据量的单一因果解释：架构、目标、语料库暴露、任务、蒙太奇和评估协议一起变化。因此，韩国的结果被视为一项外部队列压力测试，而不是复制的全球人口效应。数据集对探针建立易于解码的数据集成员资格，但无法将记录位点与设备、预处理、群体或临床组成分开。有针对性的控制措施回答了不同的问题，并且仅在其假设成立的情况下应用。The results do not support a single causal account based on model scale, channel count, or adaptation data volume: architecture, objective, corpus exposure, task, montage, and evaluation protocol vary together. The Korean result is therefore framed as one external-cohort stress test, not a replicated global population effect. The dataset-pair probes establish readily decodable dataset membership but cannot separate recording site from device, preprocessing, population, or clinical composition. The targeted controls answer different questions and are applied only where their assumptions hold.

5.2 局限性Limitations

  1. 1.

    基准是异构的：任务、群组、蒙太奇、语料库暴露和分割结构不同，因此跨任务模式是描述性的，而不是因果规模或通道密度效应。The benchmark is heterogeneous: tasks, cohorts, montages, corpus exposure, and split structures differ, so cross-task patterns are descriptive rather than causal scale or channel-density effects.

  2. 2.

    CAUEEG 主要交叉验证是记录级别的，因为公共患者标识符不可用。患者不相交的保留分割被报告为敏感性界限，但训练中的重复记录仍然未量化。CAUEEG primary cross-validation is recording-level because public patient identifiers are unavailable. The patient-disjoint held-out split is reported as a sensitivity bound, but repeat recordings within training remain unquantified.

  3. 3.

    数据集身份探测将站点与硬件、预处理、总体、诊断组成和数据集历史混淆。他们仅建立可解码的数据集成员资格。The dataset-identity probes confound site with hardware, preprocessing, population, diagnosis composition, and dataset history. They establish decodable dataset membership only.

  4. 4.

    CHB-MIT 使用具有近似位置坐标的双极导数，并且每历元归一化消除了绝对幅度。幅度感知经典比较器和双极坐标灵敏度仍有待评估。CHB-MIT uses bipolar derivations with approximate positional coordinates, and per-epoch normalization removes absolute amplitude. An amplitude-aware classical comparator and bipolar-coordinate sensitivity remain to be evaluated.

  5. 5.

    BENDR 和 LaBraM 具有特定于模型的采样、带宽、通道填充和位置映射不匹配；他们的冻结结果不应被解释为预训练目标的干净测试。BENDR and LaBraM have model-specific sampling, bandwidth, channel-padding, and position-mapping mismatches; their frozen results should not be interpreted as clean tests of pretraining objective.

  6. 6.

    统计比较受到少量独立受试者或折叠、重叠交叉验证训练集和多重探索性分析的限制。强调效果估计和有针对性的控制而不是验证意义。Statistical comparisons are limited by small numbers of independent subjects or folds, overlapping cross-validation training sets, and multiple exploratory analyses. Effect estimates and targeted controls are emphasized over confirmatory significance.

5.3 对临床脑电图人工智能的影响Implications for Clinical EEG AI

结果支持评估工作流程而不是部署声明。对于任何拟议的临床收益，评估单位应与预期的临床决策相匹配；应审核受试者泄漏和训练前暴露；应加强经典和非线性比较器；和控制应选择具体的替代解释。 CHB-MIT 结果支持 REVE 在测试方案下的跨受试者发作期与发作间期可分离性，但未建立样本精确的发病检测或广泛的临床转移。 TUAB是域内的，受试者聚集后阿尔茨海默病表现并不优越，韩国队列是单一的外部压力测试。The results support an evaluation workflow rather than a deployment claim. For any proposed clinical gain, the evaluation unit should match the intended clinical decision; subject leakage and pretraining exposure should be audited; classical and nonlinear comparators should be strengthened; and controls should be selected for the specific alternative explanation. The CHB-MIT result supports cross-subject ictal-versus-interictal separability for REVE under the tested protocol, but does not establish sample-precise onset detection or broad clinical transfer. TUAB is in-domain, Alzheimer performance is not superior after subject aggregation, and the Korean cohort is a single external stress test.

6 结论Conclusion

我们对五项临床任务中的六种脑电图基础模型进行了基准测试，包括一个外部韩国队列和用于数据集身份探测的其他数据集。对选定的 REVE 结果进行了有针对性的控制。支持三个结论：We benchmark six EEG foundation models across five clinical tasks, including one external Korean cohort and additional datasets used for dataset-identity probes. Targeted controls were applied to selected REVE findings. Three conclusions are supported:

  • •

    数据集转移需要谨慎解释。Dataset shift requires cautious interpretation.《冰雪奇缘》REVE 借鉴了韩国痴呆症评估的经典特征，包括患者与患者不相交的敏感性划分。数据集成员资格在测试对之间是高度可解码的，但这种设计无法将记录站点与数据集之间的其他差异隔离开来。Frozen REVE trails classical features on the Korean dementia evaluation, including on the patient-disjoint sensitivity split. Dataset membership is highly decodable across the tested pairs, but this design cannot isolate recording site from the other differences between datasets.

  • •

    控件回答不同的问题。Controls answer distinct questions.随机初始化测试预训练权重，标签排列测试探测能力，加扰标签适应测试标签依赖性，PCA 与 GRP 测试投影方法。在阿尔茨海默病方面，随着受试者聚集和更强的比较器，时代水平的积分优势消失； GRP-versus-PCA 不测试预训练。Random initialization tests pretrained weights, label permutation tests probe capacity, scrambled-label adaptation tests label dependence, and PCA-versus-GRP tests the projection method. On Alzheimer’s disease, the epoch-level point advantage disappears with subject aggregation and stronger comparators; GRP-versus-PCA does not test pretraining.

  • •

    最清晰的对照阳性范围很窄。The clearest controlled positive is narrow.REVE 将保留的 CHB-MIT 患者的发作期与同一会话的发作间期分开，并且优于匹配的随机初始化编码器和原始随机特征控制。这并没有建立样本精确的发病检测、广泛的临床转移或每个基础模型的优越性。REVE separates ictal from same-session interictal epochs in held-out CHB-MIT patients and outperforms the matched random-initialized encoder and raw-random-feature control. This does not establish sample-precise onset detection, broad clinical transfer, or superiority of every foundation model.

实际意义是方法论上的：临床脑电图基准应将评估单位与临床目标相匹配，披露训练前暴露和预处理异常，在标识符允许的情况下使用受试者不相交的分割，并根据正在测试的声明选择对照。The practical implication is methodological: clinical EEG benchmarks should match the evaluation unit to the clinical target, disclose pretraining exposure and preprocessing exceptions, use subject-disjoint splits where identifiers permit, and choose controls according to the claim being tested.
