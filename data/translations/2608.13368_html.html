通过损失引导多专家 GAN 进行手语视频合成（技术报告）Sign Language Video Synthesis via Loss-Guided Multi-Expert GANs (Technical Report)

Nong Dingzhan Affiliation: Glassbox AI    REN Zhihao Affiliation: Glassbox AI    Li Ziqi Affiliation: Glassbox AI    Tim Lo Thanks: Corresponding author: Tim Lo, admin@xai.hk Affiliation: nongdingzhan@gmail.com, renzhihao0919@gmail.com, ziq93812@gmail.com, tim.lo@xai.hk Affiliation: Glassbox AI
摘要Abstract

这份初步技术报告提出了一个手语视频合成框架，使用损失引导的多专家生成对抗网络 (GAN) 来增强听力障碍人士的沟通。三个专门的鉴别器（全局鉴别器、手鉴别器和头部鉴别器）各自引导生成器中相应的专家分支走向不同的视觉区域，从而实现隐式特征专业化，而不会造成显式多样性损失。为了稳定这个多判别器系统，该系统的早期训练否则会表现出混沌动态，我们引入了联合损失共识机制，该机制将每个判别器以 10% 的权重调整为整体平均值。每个分支进一步采用具有可学习的 AdaptiveFeatureFusion 的双通道卷积变换器设计，平衡卷积的稳定性与窗口自注意力的细节。生成器使用交替三模式调度（鉴别器、整体生成、分支专业生成）进行训练。在具有删除简单和重复样本的过滤测试集的自定义 156GB 数据集上，我们的 0.2B 参数变体实现了 29.8 PSNR，1.3B 参数变体实现了 30.7 PSNR，推理 VRAM 占用空间分别为 1.5GB 和 8GB，支持在消费级硬件上部署。由于单个 GPU 上的训练周期为 2-3 个月，完整的消融研究仍在进行中。该系统在2025香港前沿科技峰会上进行了展示。This preliminary technical report presents a framework for sign language video synthesis using a loss-guided multi-expert Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) to enhance communication for individuals with hearing impairments. Three specialized discriminators—global, hand, and head—each guide a corresponding expert branch in the generator toward a distinct visual region, enabling implicit feature specialization without explicit diversity losses. To stabilize this multi-discriminator system, whose early-phase training otherwise exhibits chaotic dynamics, we introduce a United Loss consensus mechanism that regularizes each discriminator toward the ensemble average at a 10% weight. Each branch further adopts a dual-pathway convolutional-transformer design with learnable AdaptiveFeatureFusion, balancing the stability of convolutions against the detail of windowed self-attention. The generator is trained using an alternating three-mode schedule (discriminator, holistic generation, branch-specialized generation). On a custom 156GB dataset with a filtered test set that removes easy and repetitive samples, our 0.2B-parameter variant achieves 29.8 PSNR and the 1.3B-parameter variant achieves 30.7 PSNR, with inference VRAM footprints of 1.5 GB and 8 GB respectively, enabling deployment on consumer-grade hardware. Full ablation studies remain ongoing due to the 2–3 month training cycle on a single GPU. The system was showcased at the 2025 Hong Kong Frontier Technology Summit.

1 简介Introduction

手语视频合成对于改善听力障碍人士的沟通能力至关重要，需要生成具有精确手势和面部表情的高保真视频。虽然生成对抗网络 (GAN) 具有先进的图像和视频合成功能，但传统的单鉴别器架构难以捕捉手语视频等复杂场景中的细粒度细节。这种限制通常会导致不切实际的输出，特别是对于复杂的手部和面部运动。Sign language video synthesis is critical for improving communication accessibility for individuals with hearing impairments, requiring high-fidelity video generation with precise hand gestures and facial expressions. While Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) have advanced image and video synthesis, traditional single-discriminator architectures struggle to capture fine-grained details in complex scenes like sign language videos. This limitation often results in unrealistic outputs, particularly for intricate hand and face movements.

为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了一个多判别器 GAN (MD-GAN) 框架，其中包含用于全局一致性、手势和面部表情的专门判别器。这种方法确保了对特定区域特征的详细监督。此外，我们引入了一个多并行 U-Net 生成器，其中三个并行编码器-解码器分支专注于不同的视觉区域（全身、手和面部），每个分支由相应的判别器驱动，以鼓励隐式特征专业化。To address these challenges, we propose a Multi-Discriminator GAN (MD-GAN) framework with specialized discriminators for global coherence, hand gestures, and facial expressions. This approach ensures detailed supervision of region-specific features. Additionally, we introduce a Multi parallel U-Net generator, where three parallel encoder-decoder branches focus on distinct visual regions (full body, hands, and face), with each branch driven by its corresponding discriminator to encourage implicit feature specialization.

由于优化目标相互冲突，使用多个判别器可能会导致训练不稳定。为了缓解这个问题，我们提出了一个联合损失函数，它在鉴别器之间建立了软共识约束。每个判别器的损失以 10% 的权重与整体平均值混合，惩罚与群体的极端偏差，同时保留个体的专业化。这种机制促进了稳定的收敛，而不会牺牲特定区域反馈的好处。The use of multiple discriminators can introduce training instability due to conflicting optimization objectives. To mitigate this, we propose a United Loss function that establishes a soft consensus constraint across discriminators. Each discriminator’s loss is blended with the ensemble average at a 10% weight, penalizing extreme deviation from the group while preserving individual specialization. This mechanism promotes stable convergence without sacrificing the benefit of region-specific feedback.

为了确保真实的输出，我们采用自适应实例归一化（AdaIN）来融合每个编码器层的骨架结构和风格图像外观。我们的模型专为消费级硬件而设计：所有变体都在单个 GPU 上进行训练，并在 1.5–8 GB VRAM 内进行推理。To ensure realistic outputs, we incorporate Adaptive Instance Normalization (AdaIN) to fuse skeleton structure with style image appearance at each encoder layer. Our model is designed for consumer-grade hardware: all variants train on a single GPU and infer within 1.5–8 GB VRAM.

我们的贡献包括：（1）一个损失引导的多专家框架，其中手部、面部和全局区域的专门判别器各自驱动相应的生成器分支； （2）具有双路径架构的多并行U-Net框架——每个分支通过可学习权重（AdaptiveFeatureFusion）融合的并行卷积和变压器路径独立处理特征，从而实现稳定的、特定于区域的特征提取； (3) 用于联邦判别器共识的联合损失函数； (4) 交替的三模式训练计划，将整体生成与分支专业优化解耦。我们在明确针对具有挑战性的案例的过滤测试集上报告了三个模型规模的 PSNR 结果。Our contributions include: (1) a loss-guided multi-expert framework in which specialized discriminators for hand, face, and global regions each drive a corresponding generator branch; (2) a Multi parallel U-Net framework with a dual-pathway architecture—each branch independently processes features through parallel convolutional and transformer pathways fused via learnable weights (AdaptiveFeatureFusion), enabling stable, region-specific feature extraction; (3) a United Loss function for federated discriminator consensus; and (4) an alternating three-mode training schedule that decouples holistic generation from branch-specialized optimization. We report PSNR results across three model scales on a filtered test set that explicitly targets challenging cases.

2.4 多重判别器Multi Discriminators

在传统的 GAN 训练中，生成器往往过度关注真实数据分布的部分模式（例如，重复生成相似的样本）而忽略多样性，导致输出缺乏真实数据的高复杂性。然而，现有的经典解决方案面临局限性：GMAN 和 D2GAN 等方法独立训练判别器，没有协作专业化，通常会产生冗余反馈。In traditional GAN training, the generator tends to overly focus on partial modes of the real data distribution (e.g., repeatedly generating similar samples) while neglecting diversity, resulting in outputs that lack the high complexity of real data. However, existing classic solutions face limitations: methods like GMAN and D2GAN train discriminators independently without collaborative specialization, often yielding redundant feedback.

5 结论Conclusion

5.1 贡献总结Summary of Contributions

我们提出了一种用于手语视频合成的损失引导多专家 GAN 框架，其特点是：（1）具有 Haar 小波预处理的三个专门鉴别器（全局、手、面部）； (2) 多并行 U-Net 生成器，其中三个独立的编码器-解码器分支专门研究不同的视觉区域，由相应的判别器驱动； (3) 联合损失共识机制，通过将个体损失与整体平均值进行 10% 的软混合来稳定多判别器训练； (4) 具有 AdaptiveFeatureFusion 的双通道卷积变换器架构——一种可学习的混合机制，在每个编码器块融合并行卷积和 Swin Transformer 流，确保跨分支的特征分歧； （5）整合三个空间尺度的Local-Global Merged Attention机制； (6) 具有独立的每分支优化器的交替三模式训练计划（判别器/整体/分支专用）。在针对具有挑战性的案例的过滤测试集上，我们的架构在整个模型规模上实现了 29.8–30.7 PSNR，并且推理可部署在消费级硬件上。We have presented a loss-guided multi-expert GAN framework for sign language video synthesis featuring: (1) three specialized discriminators (global, hand, face) with Haar wavelet preprocessing; (2) a Multi parallel U-Net generator where three independent encoder-decoder branches specialize on distinct visual regions, driven by corresponding discriminators; (3) a United Loss consensus mechanism that stabilizes multi-discriminator training through a 10% soft blending of individual losses with the ensemble average; (4) a dual-pathway convolutional-transformer architecture with AdaptiveFeatureFusion—a learnable blending mechanism that fuses parallel convolutional and Swin Transformer streams at each encoder block, ensuring feature divergence across branches; (5) a Local-Global Merged Attention mechanism integrating three spatial scales; and (6) an alternating three-mode training schedule (discriminator / holistic / branch-specialized) with independent per-branch optimizers. On a filtered test set targeting challenging cases, our architecture achieves 29.8–30.7 PSNR across model scales, with inference deployable on consumer-grade hardware.

5.2 局限性Limitations

这项工作有一些限制，我们希望在未来的迭代中解决这些限制：This work has several limitations that we aim to address in future iterations:

培训费用：Training Cost:在单个消费级 GPU 上进行完整训练需要 2-3 个月，这妨碍了对各个架构组件（联合损失、双路径架构、交替训练模式、多尺度注意力）的全面消融研究。每次消融都需要一个完整的再培训周期，使得系统性的组件分析在当前资源限制下不可行。Full training requires 2–3 months on a single consumer GPU, which has precluded comprehensive ablation studies of individual architectural components (United Loss, dual-pathway architecture, alternating training modes, multi-scale attention). Each ablation would require a full retraining cycle, making systematic component-wise analysis infeasible under current resource constraints.

消融间隙：Ablation Gap:United Loss 对训练稳定性和最终 PSNR 的贡献在当前的 v4 架构上并未被孤立。虽然观察证据——非线性收敛动力学、维持分支专业化以及无需外部正则化的训练稳定性——与假设的共识机制一致，但严格的因果归因需要受控实验。The contribution of United Loss to training stability and final PSNR has not been isolated on the current v4 architecture. While observational evidence—non-linear convergence dynamics, maintained branch specialization, and training stability without external regularization—is consistent with the hypothesized consensus mechanism, rigorous causal attribution requires controlled experiments.

扩展瓶颈：Scaling Bottleneck:当前的架构在训练和推理时激活所有三个分支。扩展到更专业的分支（例如，单独的左手和右手专家、面部子区域专家或多个特定领域专家）将导致计算和 VRAM 成本线性增加。这种可扩展性限制直接推动了我们正在进行的标签路由专家选择工作，其中预先训练的路由器在生成开始之前仅激活相关的专家分支，无论专家数量有多少，都保持有限的推理成本。The current architecture activates all three branches at both training and inference time. Extending to more specialized branches (e.g., separate left-hand and right-hand experts, facial sub-region experts, or multiple domain-specific experts) would incur linear cost increases in both compute and VRAM. This scalability limitation directly motivates our ongoing work on label-routed expert selection, where a pre-trained router activates only the relevant expert branches before generation begins, maintaining bounded inference cost regardless of the number of experts.

指标限制：Metric Limitations:我们目前仅报告 PSNR。已计划使用感知指标（FID、LPIPS）、动作准确性和人工评估进行综合评估，但尚未完成。 PSNR 和手语生成中感知质量之间的关系（其中手和面部的像素级精度尤为重要）值得进一步研究。We currently report only PSNR. Comprehensive evaluation with perceptual metrics (FID, LPIPS), action accuracy, and human evaluation is planned but not yet completed. The relationship between PSNR and perceptual quality in sign language generation—where pixel-level accuracy on hands and faces matters disproportionately—warrants further investigation.

5.4 未来的工作Future Work

除了完成计划的消融研究之外，我们的主要研究方向是将损失引导的专家专业化范式（在 GAN 框架中得到验证）扩展到扩散模型。我们正在进行的提案，Beyond completing the planned ablation studies, our primary research direction is extending the loss-guided expert specialization paradigm—validated here in the GAN framework—to diffusion models. Our ongoing proposal,与标签路由专家一起进行损耗引导的 MoE 扩散Loss-Guided MoE Diffusion with Label-Routed Experts，旨在用预先训练的标签路由器替换推理时的完整分支激活，该路由器在生成开始之前仅选择相关的专家分支。这将保留多专家架构的质量收益，同时保持推理成本有限，直接解决当前工作中发现的扩展瓶颈。, aims to replace full branch activation at inference with a pre-trained label router that selects only relevant expert branches before generation begins. This would preserve the quality gains of multi-expert architectures while keeping inference cost bounded, directly addressing the scaling bottleneck identified in the current work.
