摘要Abstract

在生物系统中，神经回路通过定向的、短延迟的交互进行计算，其效果在多个时间尺度和行为环境中展开。我们解决了从不同刺激下采样的神经群体活动中推断这些局部的、滞后特定的相互作用的问题，而不假设潜在动态的参数形式。我们的方法通过估计连续活动快照（即大脑状态）的联合窗口分数并通过跨块分数产品将这些分数转换为校准的定向边缘测试来利用去噪分数模型。关键的见解是这些产品恢复了非线性动力学下大脑状态之间转换图的雅可比行列式。为了清楚地分离滞后特定效应，我们引入了以中间时间点为条件的最小多块窗口，避免了成对分析中固有的遗漏滞后偏差。由此产生的方法，In biological systems, neural circuits compute through directed, short-latency interactions whose effects unfold across multiple time scales and behavioral contexts. We address the problem of inferring these local, lag-specific interactions from sampled neural population activity under varying stimuli, without assuming a parametric form for the underlying dynamics. Our approach leverages denoising score models by estimating joint-window scores over consecutive activity snapshots (i.e., brain states) and converting these scores into calibrated, directed edge tests via cross-block score products. The key insight is that these products recover the Jacobian of the transition map between brain states under nonlinear dynamics. 6 讨论Discussion

我们研究了在非线性动力学下从群体记录中恢复定向的、滞后特定的神经相互作用的问题，而不假设参数转换模型。我们的中心主张是，联合窗口分布的局部几何形状可以系统地转换为统计上可检验的电路假设。分数块时间图（SBTG）通过将联合窗口分数几何图形转换为带符号的、滞后解析的边缘测试来实例化该理论，将高维活动轨迹与可解释的陈述联系起来We studied the problem of recovering directed, lag-specific neural interactions from population recordings under nonlinear dynamics, without assuming a parametric transition model. 我们使用三个互补指标评估我们的模型：AUROC（接收器操作特征曲线下的面积）、AUPRC（精确回忆曲线下的面积）和最大 F1 分数。这些指标的选择是由于连接组的极度稀疏（We evaluate our model using three complementary metrics: AUROC (Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic Curve), AUPRC (Area Under the Precision-Recall Curve), and the Max F1 Score. AUROC（排名质量）：AUROC (Ranking Quality):测量随机选择的真实边缘排名高于随机选择的非边缘的概率。该指标有效地量化了模型捕获系统底层生物物理的程度，与阈值的选择无关。高 AUROC 表明学习到的耦合分数为真实的生物连接分配了统计上更高的值。Measures the probability that a randomly chosen true edge is ranked higher than a randomly chosen non-edge. This metric effectively quantifies how well the model captures the underlying biological physics of the system, independent of the choice of threshold. A high AUROC indicates that the learned coupling scores assign statistically higher values to true biological connections.

AUPRC（可靠性）：AUPRC (Reliability):衡量所有阈值的精确度和召回率之间的权衡，特别关注正类（边缘）。在连接组等稀疏数据集中，由于存在大量真阴性，AUROC 可能过于乐观。 AUPRC 提供了更严格的衡量排名靠前的预测的可靠性的方法，对误报的惩罚更加严厉。Measures the trade-off between precision and recall across all thresholds, focusing specifically on the positive class (edges). AUPRC provides a stricter measure of how reliable the top-ranked predictions are, penalizing false positives more heavily.
