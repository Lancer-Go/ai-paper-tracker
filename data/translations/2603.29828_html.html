1 简介Introduction

科学人工智能 (AI4Science) 的最新进展极大地扩展了我们生成科学假设和探索生物学、化学和材料科学领域广阔候选空间的能力。 AlphaFold 等突破Recent advances in AI for Science (AI4Science) have dramatically expanded our ability to generate scientific hypotheses and explore vast candidate spaces in biology, chemistry, and materials science. Breakthroughs such as AlphaFold[AlphaFold2021], AlphaFold 3, AlphaFold 3[AlphaFold3_2024]，以及以 GNoME 为代表的大规模材料发现, and large-scale materials discovery exemplified by GNoME[GNoME2023]已经证明机器智能可以以前所未有的规模捕获复杂的物理和生物约束。然而，科学发现的实验方面并没有以同样的速度取得进展。在实践中，计算假设的验证仍然依赖于劳动密集型的实验室工作流程，导致快速计算之间的差距不断扩大have demonstrated that machine intelligence can capture complex physical and biological constraints at unprecedented scale. Yet the experimental side of scientific discovery has not progressed at the same pace. In practice, the validation of computational hypotheses still depends on labor-intensive laboratory workflows, creating a widening gap between rapid计算机模拟in silico发现和缓慢的湿实验室验证。discovery and slow wet-lab verification.

这一差距的主要瓶颈在于科学表征。高精度表征对于将实验结果与材料结构、化学状态和功能特性联系起来至关重要，但它仍然是实验室自动化标准化程度最低的阶段之一A major bottleneck in this gap lies in scientific characterization. High-precision characterization is essential for connecting experimental outcomes to material structures, chemical states, and functional properties, but it remains one of the least standardized stages of laboratory automation[SDLReview2025]。与机器人合成和反应优化相比. Compared with robotic synthesis and reaction optimization[AIDrivenAutonomousLab2025, MobileRoboticProcessChemist]，在实际实验室中，表征很难实现自动化，因为它依赖于异构仪器、供应商特定的软件和专家驱动的解释。在许多实际设置中，关键的操作知识不是通过可编程接口公开的，而是嵌入到图形控制软件、未记录的交互程序以及经验丰富的操作员的默示启发中。因此，高精度表征仍然是实际实验室自主化的最困难障碍之一。, characterization is substantially harder to automate in real laboratories because it depends on heterogeneous instruments, vendor-specific software, and expert-driven interpretation. In many practical settings, critical operational knowledge is not exposed through programmable interfaces, but instead embedded in graphical control software, undocumented interaction procedures, and the tacit heuristics of experienced operators. As a result, high-precision characterization remains one of the most difficult barriers to practical laboratory autonomy.

这个挑战不仅仅是硬件集成问题，也是软件交互问题。现有的自主实验室系统在机器人合成、反应优化和闭环实验方面取得了令人瞩目的进展This challenge is not merely a hardware integration problem, but also a software interaction problem. Existing autonomous laboratory systems have achieved impressive progress in robotic synthesis, reaction optimization, and closed-loop experimentation[SDLReview2025]，包括综合材料平台, including integrated materials platforms[ALab2023, AutonomousLabNovelMaterials, CederAIInAction2026]、移动实验室代理, mobile laboratory agents[MobileRoboticChemist2020]和工作流编排堆栈, and workflow orchestration stacks[AlabOS2024, UniLabOS2025]。然而，大多数当前方法仍然依赖于预集成的设备 API、自定义中间件或紧密设计的硬件软件堆栈，这种模式也反映在程序化科学代理中. However, most current approaches still rely on pre-integrated device APIs, custom middleware, or tightly engineered hardware-software stacks, a pattern also reflected in programmatic scientific agents[Coscientist2023, ChemCrow2024]。此类设计在专门构建的自动化设置中是有效的，但在现实世界的实验室中部署起来要困难得多，这些实验室充满了异构的、特定于供应商的、通常是缺乏可编程接口的闭源仪器。特别是对于表征而言，许多关键决策是通过与光谱、显微镜图像、参数面板和实时仪器反馈的视觉交互来做出的。这些属性使 GUI 本机交互成为比纯 API 抽象更自然的基础，可用于实际表征自动化。. Such designs are effective in purpose-built automation settings, but are considerably harder to deploy in real-world laboratories populated by heterogeneous, vendor-specific, and often closed-source instruments that lack programmable interfaces. For characterization in particular, many key decisions are made through visual interaction with spectra, microscopy images, parameter panels, and real-time instrument feedback. These properties make GUI-native interaction a more natural foundation than API-only abstraction for practical characterization automation.

在这项工作中，我们认为科学仪器应该被视为专门的人工智能代理，科学软件是其一流的操作环境。 不要假设实验室智能必须专门构建在预定义的 API 之上，或者专有接口始终可以包含在命令行工具模式中In this work, we argue that scientific instruments should be regarded as specialized embodied AI agents, for which scientific software serves as the first-class operating environment. Owl-AuraID adapts the agent-runtime paradigm of modern coding agents to scientific characterization, providing a conversation-first interface for non-programmer scientific users while retaining workspace access, command execution, and autonomous tool use under the hood. 在此基础上，Owl-AuraID 将物理样品处理与机器人、基于 GUI 的仪器控制和科学数据解释连接到端到端表征工作流程中。该系统支持广泛的精确表征场景，涵盖多模态光谱分析、多尺度显微成像、元素测绘、晶体结构分析和相关数据处理任务。这样，Owl-AuraID 不仅仅是一个科学软件的 GUI 自动化系统，而且是一个以技能为中心的科学代理平台，将具体执行和数字分析连接在一个统一且可扩展​​的循环中。Built on this foundation, Owl-AuraID connects physical sample handling with robots, GUI-based instrument control, and scientific data interpretation into an end-to-end characterization workflow. 尽管取得了这些进步，大多数当前的 SDL 系统都依赖于一定程度的预先设计的集成，例如标准化设备 API、定制中间件或以自动化为中心的实验室重新设计。虽然这些假设在专用环境中可行，但在现实世界的表征实验室中却站不住脚。在这里，仪器仍然高度异构，供应商软件主要是闭源的，操作控制经常依赖于与遗留界面的手动视觉交互。与合成或液体处理工作流程不同，表征的特点是缺乏软件标准化，并且严重依赖专有的、以人为中心的控制程序。因此，表征仍然是实现真正的实验室自主化过程中最分散、最具挑战性的瓶颈之一。Despite these advances, most current SDL systems rely on a degree of pre-engineered integration—such as standardized device APIs, bespoke middleware, or automation-centric laboratory redesigns. Third, characterization is intrinsically visual: operators make decisions based on live images, spectral previews, parameter panels, reconstruction interfaces, and real-time feedback displayed in the GUI. A pure CLI abstraction cannot fully preserve this visual analytical context or the tacit decision-making process associated with it.

由于这些原因，GUI 原生交互不仅是缺少 API 的后备方案，而且是真实实验室中异构科学仪器的自然操作范例。 我们的工作建立在自主实验室、基于法学硕士的科学代理和 GUI 自动化方面的最新进展的基础上，同时解决了先前系统未完全覆盖的差距：使人工智能代理能够直接操作现实世界的科学表征软件，并在统一的工作流程中连接物理执行、仪器软件控制和科学数据分析。For these reasons, GUI-native interaction is not merely a fallback for missing APIs, but a natural operating paradigm for heterogeneous scientific instrumentation in real laboratories. Our work builds on recent progress in autonomous laboratories, LLM-based scientific agents, and GUI automation, while addressing a gap not fully covered by prior systems: enabling AI agents to directly operate real-world scientific characterization software and to connect physical execution, instrument software control, and scientific data analysis within a unified workflow.

总体而言，先前的工作在现实世界的科学表征方面尚未充分解决三个空白： (1) 大多数自主实验室系统依赖于预先集成的 API 或固定的自动化堆栈； (2) 大多数法学硕士科学代理人认为相关工具已经可以通过编程方式访问；和 (3) 大多数 GUI 代理在通用软件环境中进行评估，而不是在专有的、安全敏感的科学仪器中进行评估。 我们的工作通过 GUI 本机科学代理框架解决了这些差距，该框架集成了跨异构表征工作流程的具体执行、仪器软件操作和科学数据分析。Overall, prior work leaves three gaps insufficiently addressed for real-world scientific characterization: (1) most autonomous laboratory systems rely on pre-integrated APIs or fixed automation stacks; (2) most LLM scientific agents assume that relevant tools are already programmatically accessible; and (3) most GUI agents are evaluated in general-purpose software environments rather than proprietary, safety-sensitive scientific instrumentation. Our work addresses these gaps through a GUI-native scientific agent framework that integrates embodied execution, instrument software operation, and scientific data analysis across heterogeneous characterization workflows.

6 讨论Discussion

在这项工作中，我们表明，异构现实世界实验室中的自主科学表征可以被表述为 GUI 本机计算机使用问题，而不仅仅是 API 或 CLI 介导的自动化任务。通过将对话优先的代理运行时与可重用的 Type-1 GUI 操作技能和 Type-2 分析脚本技能相结合，AuraID 将物理样品处理、专有仪器软件操作、工作空间计算和科学数据解释连接到统一的表征工作流程中。这些结果表明，科学软件本身可以作为实验室智能的实用操作环境，特别是在特定于供应商的界面、闭源控制堆栈和基于视觉的决策仍然是日常实践的核心的环境中。In this work, we show that autonomous scientific characterization in heterogeneous real-world laboratories can be formulated as a GUI-native computer-use problem, rather than only as an API- or CLI-mediated automation task. By combining a conversation-first agent runtime with reusable Type-1 GUI operational skills and Type-2 analytical script skills, AuraID connects physical sample handling, proprietary instrument software operation, workspace computation, and scientific data interpretation within a unified characterization workflow. These results suggest that scientific software itself can serve as a practical operating environment for laboratory intelligence, particularly in settings where vendor-specific interfaces, closed-source control stacks, and visually grounded decision-making remain central to routine practice.

AuraID 通过将实验室智能扩展到以 GUI 为中心的科学表征的尚未开发的领域，在自主研究领域拥有独特的地位。 虽然传统的自动驾驶实验室通常依赖于预先设计的 API 和固定的自动化堆栈（将其范围限制为标准化合成或液体处理），但 AuraID 的 GUI 原生范例绕过了对稳定编程端点的要求。 通过直接导航现代仪器固有的专有接口和闭源控制堆栈，该框架能够将不同的模式快速统一到一个单一的、有凝聚力的分析连续体中。 这种多仪器集成允许同时询问结构、元素和功能特性。 最终，通过捕获复杂材料挑战所需的多维描述符，AuraID 将异构表征工作流程从孤立的手动任务转变为用于科学发现的整体自动化引擎。AuraID owns a distinct position within the landscape of autonomous research by extending laboratory intelligence into the underexplored domain of GUI-centric scientific characterization. While conventional self-driving laboratories often rely on pre-engineered APIs and fixed automation stacks—limiting their scope to standardized synthesis or liquid-handling—AuraID’s GUI-native paradigm bypasses the requirement for stable programmatic endpoints. By directly navigating the proprietary interfaces and closed-source control stacks inherent to modern instrumentation, this framework enables the rapid unification of diverse modalities into a single, cohesive analytical continuum. This multi-instrument integration allows for the simultaneous interrogation of structural, elemental, and functional properties. Ultimately, by capturing the multi-dimensional descriptors required for complex materials challenges, AuraID transforms heterogeneous characterization workflows from isolated manual tasks into a holistic, automated engine for scientific discovery.

除了自动化之外，AuraID 还可作为高质量科学数据采集的关键引擎。 “可重用技能”架构确保实验程序以专家级精度执行。 这种一致性很重要，因为通过系统地将手动实验室程序转换为结构化的机器可读数据集，我们为下一代科学基础模型提供了必要的高质量科学数据。 随着这些模型的发展，通过 GUI 原生代理生成的高保真数据将使人工智能不仅能够执行实验，还能对实验物理和化学产生更深入、跨领域的理解。Beyond automation, AuraID serves as a critical engine for high-quality scientific data acquisition. The “reusable skill” architecture ensures that experimental procedures are executed with expert-level precision. This consistency is important because, by systematically converting manual laboratory procedures into structured, machine-readable datasets, we provide the essential high-quality scientific data for the next generation of Scientific Foundation Models. As these models evolve, the high-fidelity data generated through GUI-native agents will allow AI to not only execute experiments but to develop a deeper, cross-domain understanding of experimental physics and chemistry.

虽然 AuraID 展示了对多种模式的强大覆盖范围，但仍然存在一些前沿问题。 当前对技能获取的专家演示的依赖表明需要自我监督的技能归纳，其中代理通过好奇心驱动的探索或通过“阅读”技术手册来学习使用新软件。 此外，虽然我们已经弥合了软件差距，但跨仪器对敏感样品的物理处理仍然需要复杂的机器人协调。While AuraID demonstrates robust coverage across diverse modalities, several frontiers remain. Furthermore, while we have bridged the software gap, the physical handling of sensitive samples across instruments still requires sophisticated robotic coordination.

By capturing and digitizing the tacit knowledge of human experts, we are building a persistent, composable capability that transcends individual experiments. As we integrate more instruments and refine the quality of the resulting data, these agents will eventually move from being mere operators to becoming autonomous co-scientists, capable of closing the loop between hypothesis generation and complex physical validation. This trajectory offers a practical and scalable path toward the fully autonomous, AI-driven laboratories of the future.
