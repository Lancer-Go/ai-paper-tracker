耳语感知法学硕士：用于鲁棒耳语语音识别的自我监督不确定性学习Whisper-Aware LLM: Self-Supervised Uncertainty Learning for Robust Whispered Speech Recognition

Gaopeng    Zhenyu    Zheng    Yinfeng    Haitao 1Qwen Business Unit of Alibaba, China {longque.xgp, wzy497957, jeremy.xz, xiayinfeng.xyf, timmy.yht}@alibaba-inc.com
摘要Abstract

耳语语音的信号模糊性促使 ASR 系统走向两种相反的故障模式：无法捕获耳语语音或幻觉噪声转录。本文介绍了 Whisper-Aware LLM，这是一个教授音频 LLM 感知这种不确定性并做出反应的框架。我们的模型通过有针对性的自我监督任务学习量化声学信号的物理缺陷，从而形成内在的自我意识。然后，这种学习到的不确定性通过一种新颖的置信融合解码机制进行操作，该机制为 LLM 解码器提供高级指令和帧级注意力调制。我们的实验证实了这种方法的有效性。该模型在耳语语音方面树立了新的最先进水平，AISHELL6-Whisper 的 CER 相对降低了 17%。同时，它直接解决了可靠性权衡问题，幻觉率从 25% 以上下降到 4.5%。The signal ambiguity of whispered speech drives ASR systems toward two opposing failure modes: failing to capture whispered speech or hallucinatory transcription of noise. This paper introduces the Whisper-Aware LLM, a framework that teaches an Audio-LLM to perceive and react to this uncertainty. Our model develops an intrinsic self-awareness by learning to quantify the physical deficiencies of acoustic signals through targeted self-supervised tasks. This learned uncertainty is then operationalized via a novel Confidence-Fused Decoding mechanism, which provides both high-level instructions and frame-level attention modulation to the LLM decoder. Our experiments confirm the effectiveness of this approach. The model sets a new state-of-the-art on whispered speech with a 17% relative CER reduction on AISHELL6-Whisper. At the same time, it directly addresses the reliability trade-off, with hallucination rates dropping from over 25% to 4.5%.

1 简介Introduction

耳语语音是一种没有声带振动的发声模式，对自动语音识别 (ASR) 系统提出了严峻且长期的挑战。耳语的声学表现，其特征是缺乏基频 (F0) 和谐波结构，并且具有类似噪声的质量Whispered speech, a mode of phonation without vocal fold vibration, presents a formidable and long-standing challenge for Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) systems. The acoustic manifestation of whispering characterized by the absence of a fundamental frequency (F0) and harmonic structure, and a noise-like quality[lin2023improving, li2024aishell6]造成了传统 ASR 模型难以解决的根本模糊性。这种模糊性会导致准确性与可靠性之间的权衡。一方面，主要训练正常语音的模型在遇到耳语时表现出感知失败，导致高错误率。另一方面，提高模型捕获这些微弱线索的灵敏度反而会增加其产生幻觉的可能性，从而错误地转录环境噪声中的语音。creates a fundamental ambiguity that conventional ASR models struggle to resolve. This ambiguity gives rise to a debilitating accuracy-reliability trade-off. On one hand, models trained primarily on normal speech exhibit perceptual failure when encountering whispers, leading to high error rates. On the other hand, increasing a model's sensitivity to capture these faint cues paradoxically heightens its susceptibility to hallucination, where it erroneously transcribes speech from ambient noise.

现有的研究试图通过两个主要途径来减轻这种权衡。第一种是以数据为中心的方法，专注于增强，例如生成“伪耳语”数据Existing research has attempted to mitigate this trade-off via two principal avenues. The first is a data-centric approach focusing on augmentation, such as generating ``pseudo-whisper'' data[lin2023improving, gudepu2020whisper]。这些方法虽然有益，但受到合成和真实耳语语音之间不可避免的分布差距的限制。第二条途径是以模型为中心的静态适应，例如 Li 等人提出的投影层。. While beneficial, these methods are limited by the inevitable distributional gap between synthetic and authentic whispered speech. The second avenue is model-centric static adaptation, exemplified by the projection layer proposed by Li et al.[li2024aishell6]。虽然是一种改进，但这种“一刀切”的静态解决方案无法动态调整以适应广泛的耳语变化。. While an improvement, such ``one-size-fits-all'' static solutions fail to dynamically adjust to the vast spectrum of whisper variability.

至关重要的是，这些流行的范例主要解决低声讲话的声学症状，而不是其根本原因：信号不确定性的根本增加。 Lin 等人的一项关键声学研究。Crucially, these prevailing paradigms primarily address the acoustic symptoms of whispered speech, rather than its underlying cause: a fundamental increase in signal uncertainty. A pivotal acoustic study by Lin et al.[lin2023improving]实验证实，声门信息的缺乏是降低识别性能的最关键因素。这一关键发现提出了一个新的方向：一种更稳健的方法是首先教会模型量化信号本身的内在不确定性，而不是强迫模型根据高度不确定的信号做出高置信度的预测。experimentally confirmed that the lack of glottal information is the single most critical factor degrading recognition performance. This key finding suggests a new direction: instead of forcing the model to make high-confidence predictions from highly uncertain signals, a more robust approach is to first teach the model to quantify the signal's intrinsic uncertainty itself.

本文就是建立在这一原则之上的。我们引入了 Whisper-Aware LLM，这是一个旨在赋予本地音频 LLM 固有的信号不确定性自我意识的框架。我们假设模型可以通过训练来识别定义低声讲话的物理缺陷，从而学会以自我监督的方式感知这种不确定性。为此，我们设计了两个创新的自监督任务：F0轮廓预测和掩蔽频谱重建。然后通过我们的置信融合解码机制对量化的不确定性进行操作。为了确保稳定的学习，我们引入了严格的三阶段培训策略。我们的贡献是：This paper is built upon this principle. We introduce the Whisper-Aware LLM, a framework designed to endow a native Audio-LLM with an intrinsic self-awareness of signal uncertainty. We posit that a model can learn to perceive this uncertainty in a self-supervised manner by being trained to identify the very physical deficiencies that define whispered speech. To this end, we design two innovative self-supervised tasks: F0 contour prediction and masked spectrum reconstruction. The quantified uncertainty is then operationalized through our Confidence-Fused Decoding mechanism. To ensure stable learning, we introduce a disciplined three-stage training strategy. As shown in Table4，标准微调模型表现出较高的幻觉率 (HR)。例如，即使像 S7 和 S5 这样的强基线也会错误地为超过 25% 的文件生成文本，最高比率达到 29.3% (S6)。这凸显了他们在面对模糊信号时“想象”言语的倾向。与此形成鲜明对比的是，我们的 Whisper-Aware LLM 的 HR 仅为 4.5%，大幅降低。这一显着的改进表明，通过学习感知信号的不确定性，我们的模型可以有效地学习何时不说话，从而大大降低了从非语音音频生成虚假文本的风险。这种减少主要归因于全局指令机制，它指导解码器，正如我们的消融研究中量化的那样。, standard fine-tuned models exhibit a high Hallucination Rate (HR). 4 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们介绍了 Whisper-Aware LLM，这是一个旨在处理耳语语音固有的不确定性的框架。我们的方法不仅仅是匹配模式，而是教导模型首先通过有针对性的自我监督任务来感知信号质量。然后，我们的置信融合解码机制会使用这种学习到的意识来指导法学硕士，使其能够选择性地信任其收到的声学证据。我们的实验证实了这一策略的成功。该模型在耳语语音基准上取得了最先进的结果，并大大减少了幻觉，同时仍然是一个高效的通用 ASR 系统。In this paper, we introduced the Whisper-Aware LLM, a framework designed to handle the uncertainty inherent in whispered speech. Instead of just matching patterns, our approach teaches the model to first perceive signal quality through targeted self-supervised tasks. This learned awareness is then used by our Confidence-Fused Decoding mechanism to guide the LLM, allowing it to selectively trust the acoustic evidence it receives. Our experiments confirm the success of this strategy. The model achieves state-of-the-art results on whispered speech benchmarks and drastically reduces hallucinations, all while remaining a highly effective general-purpose ASR system.
