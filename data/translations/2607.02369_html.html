全球模型：文化人工智能的文学工具World Wide Models: Literary Tools for Cultural AI

Nina Beguš University of California, Berkeley nbegus@berkeley.edu
摘要Abstract

法学硕士上演了一种大规模、自动化和单语的新形式的文化接触。文学学科始终通过文学比较阅读、叙事学和诗歌分析、批判理论、世界文学和翻译来应对文化斗争。这些工具现在已经成为构建具有文化素养的人工智能不可或缺的一部分。本文开发了一个分层框架，以实现更细致的文本模型和对人工智能的多元解释，强调文学与人工智能发展的自然交叉，将批判理论中当前的辩论与结构单语主义联系起来，并提出世界文学方法的新应用，通过宏观结构、流通和不可译性来解决全球人工智能文本性问题。LLMs stage a new form of cultural encounter that is massive, automated, and monolingual. Literary disciplines have always negotiated cultural struggles with comparative reading of literature, narratological and poetic analysis, critical theory, world literature, and translation. These tools have now become indispensable for building culturally literate AI. The essay develops a layered framework toward more nuanced textual models and pluralistic interpretations of AI, emphasizing the natural intersections of literature and AI development, connecting current debates in critical theory with structural monolingualism, and suggesting a new application of world literature approaches to address global AI textuality through macrostructure, circulation, and untranslatability.

计算机科学的人性化Humanitization of Computer Science

纵观该领域的历史，语言、视觉和游戏一直是人工智能系统的试验场。 2010 年代，自然语言处理 (NLP) 迅速发展，最终出现了大型语言模型 (LLM)，ImageNet 数据集、AlexNet 卷积神经网络 (CNN) 以及生成图像的生成对抗网络 (GAN) 的发明改变了计算机视觉；计算机在危险边缘游戏中击败了人类！然后去。无论好坏，2020 年代的作家、艺术家、学者和学生正在使用这些不断变化的人工智能工具，并通过使用为其发展做出贡献。创意领域是艺术和人文学科的核心，但文学研究、电影和媒体研究、修辞学、历史、STS 和许多其他相关领域在很大程度上被排除在这些最先进产品的开发之外。是时候改变这一点了；不仅因为影响我们所有人的技术不应该只掌握在工程师手中，还因为人工智能模型应该更加以人为中心，朝着多元化的价值观发展，而人文学科则有助于这一发展。Language, vision, and games have been proving grounds for AI systems throughout the history of the field. In the 2010s, natural language processing (NLP) advanced rapidly, culminating in large language models (LLMs), computer vision was transformed by the ImageNet dataset, the AlexNet convolutional neural network (CNN), and the invention of generative adversarial networks (GANs) that generated images; and computers defeated humans in the games of Jeopardy! and Go. For better or for worse, writers, artists, scholars, and students of the 2020s are using these ever-changing artificial intelligence tools and contributing to their development simply through use. Creative domains are central to the arts and humanities, and yet literary studies, film and media studies, rhetoric, history, STS, and many other related fields were largely excluded from the development of these state-of-the-art products. It is time to change this; not only because the technology that affects us all should not be only in the hands of engineers, but also because AI models should be more human-centered and steered toward diverse values, with the humanities contributing to this development.

在战后计算机科学的初期，当人工智能被制定为一个研究课题时，计算领域是高度跨学科的，无论是理论还是实践，不仅包括电气工程、数学和物理，还包括语言学、哲学、逻辑学。人工智能被认为是认知科学的一个工程分支，它很快就从认知科学中脱颖而出，成为一个独立的领域，并受到塑造二十世纪末科学的定量转向的进一步推动。在 20 世纪 90 年代，当人文学科被认为与人工智能的实际进步不相容时，计算机科学家出身的人文学科教授 Philip Agre 有先见之明地认为，人工智能作为研究对象拥有“底层的哲学”In the nascent, post-war years of computer science, when AI was formulated as a topic of study, the computing field was highly interdisciplinary, both theoretical and practical, comprising not only electrical engineering, mathematics, and physics, but also linguistics, philosophy, logic. AI was framed as an engineering arm of cognitive science, from which it quickly distinguished itself as a separate field, and was further driven by the quantitative turn that shaped the late twentieth-century sciences. In the 1990s, when humanities were seen as incompatible with the practical progress of AI, the computer scientist-turned-humanities professor Philip Agre presciently argued AI as an object of study holds “philosophy underneath”[agre_soul_1995, 2]，其研究始终涵盖哲学和技术层面。虽然他的主张对于当今的人文学科来说似乎是显而易见的，但我们尚未制定一个持久的框架来将其付诸实践。, its research always encompassing both philosophical and technical dimensions. While his claims seem obvious to the humanities today, we have yet to develop a lasting framework that brings them into action.

尽管 Agre 在跨学科开始蓬勃发展时提出了自己的呼吁，但他的论点直到 2020 年代才获得关注，因为计算方面的突破清楚地表明人工智能迫切需要社会技术视角。在 2010 年代，计算跨学科是跨学术和企业研究的社会科学追求。在计算机科学中，跨学科和跨学科性已成为必然，催生了人工智能伦理、以人为中心的设计和人机交互等新的子领域，随后人工智能政策和安全成为技术监管的核心。虽然这类工作具有很高的可操作性，但它没有触及技术的哲学基础及其产生的许多规范断裂：人工智能时代的文本、作者身份、知识产权、语言、创造力和文化如何变化？Although Agre was making his appeals at the time when interdisciplinarity started to flourish, his argument gained traction only in the 2020s, after computational breakthroughs made it clear that a sociotechnical lens to AI is urgently required. In the 2010s, computational interdisciplinarity was a social science pursuit across academic and corporate research. In computer science, interdisciplinarity and transdisciplinarity have since become a must, resulting in new subfields of AI ethics, human-centered design, and human-computer interaction, followed by AI policy and safety as central to technology regulation. While this kind of work is highly actionable, it does not reach philosophical fundamentals of the technology and the many normative ruptures it yields: how do text, authorship, intellectual property, language, creativity, and culture change in the age of AI?

人工智能生成的文化文物可以反馈到文化中With AI generating cultural artifacts that feed back into culture[brinkmann_machine_2023]，人文学科需要做出回应并改进其现有学科，以保持相关性和方法上的灵活性。新学科和重点领域应运而生，由前沿问题引领，最终需要建立新的概念框架或科学方法, the humanities need to respond and revamp their existing disciplines to remain relevant and methodologically agile. New disciplines and focus areas are born out of need, led by cutting-edge questions that eventually require the establishment of a new conceptual framework or scientific method[weber_objectivity_1949, 68]偶尔也会出现新的科学范式and occasionally a new scientific paradigm[kuhn_structure_1970, 92]。虽然科学与人文学科之间的相互作用同样鼓舞人心，但技术发明，特别是信息技术领域的发明，往往会给人文学科带来巨大压力，要求其重新评估其方法。人工智能需要一个新的概念框架，但不是全新的方法。 2020 年代初期出现的新概念框架范围广泛，从已经建立的文化分析、STS 和数字人文领域扩展到人工智能，到批判性人工智能、人工人文和文化人工智能等新阐明的框架。这些方法中的每一种都对计算，特别是人工智能，利用更广泛、更跨学科的工具、问题和方法进行了更有针对性的干预。. While the interplay between sciences and humanities is equally inspiring from both sides, technological inventions, particularly those in information technologies, tend to place acute pressure on the humanities to reevaluate their approaches. AI requests a new conceptual framework but not entirely new methods. New conceptual frameworks that have emerged in the first part of the 2020s range from the already established fields of Cultural Analytics, STS, and Digital Humanities expanding into AI to newly articulated frameworks of Critical AI, Artificial Humanities, and Cultural AI. Each of these approaches presents a more targeted intervention to computing and particularly AI in utilizing a wider and more interdisciplinary arsenal of tools, questions, and methods.

人文学科的职责范围因此扩大了。最低限度可行的道德回应意味着对与我们共享世界的其他人、我们之前和之后的人负责。在这个最低限度的道德框架下，一个有效的回应可能是不关心，这并不等于对人工智能放大的数字领域问题故意无知。然而，今天的人文主义者却不再处于这个位置。许多人都感受到了人工智能侵占他们工作的压力，尤其是在课堂上。他们注意到人工智能中长期存在的偏见，以及其中心化和单一文化的发展。如今，英语系的任务是培养 STEM 学生的人文学科，并开发课程来解决数据和计算的社会层面。大学和其他机构正在重组其研究结构，组建新的中心和部门，并与工业合作伙伴建立联系——与此同时，人文学科仍然受到迫切需要，但再次受到攻击The scope of the humanities’ responsibilities has thus expanded. A minimally viable ethical response stands in responsibility to others who share the world with us, those coming before us and after us. A valid response under this minimal ethics framework could be to not care, which does not equate to willful ignorance on the digital sphere issues amplified by AI. However, humanists are not in this position today. Many feel the pressure of AI colonizing their work, particularly in classrooms. They note the perpetuation of biases in AI, along with its centralized and monocultural development. English departments are nowadays tasked with training STEM students in humanities, with curricula developed to address the social dimensions of data and computing. Universities and other institutions are re-organizing their research structures, forming new centers and departments, and connecting with industrial partners—all while the humanities are acutely needed but again under attack[vadde_inside_2024, 557-–58]。人文学科处于解决人工智能社会影响的最前沿，但尚未被纳入其创造中；在这一点上，这是一个被接受的论点，在我的书中得到了进一步的发展. The humanities are at the forefront of addressing AI’s societal repercussions but have yet to be included in its creation; at this point, this is an accepted argument, developed further in my book人工人文Artificial Humanities [begus_artificial_2025]及相关工作and related work[kommers_computational_2025]，并得到施密特科学和图灵研究所等机构的支持。开发协作场所和成功的跨学科实践是我们最紧迫的任务，需要探索、实验和时间。, and supported institutionally by Schmidt Sciences and the Turing Institute, among others. Developing collaborative venues and successful interdisciplinary practices is our most pressing task that demands exploration, experimentation, and time.

我对现实危机的诊断是，我们正在经历计算机科学的人性化，这需要将人文方法、概念和价值观融入人工智能的创造、应用和评估中。虽然“人性化”一词可能会让人想起将机器拟人化或使它们与人类需求保持一致的想法，但我将这个术语用作计算内部和外部正在发生的更深刻、按时间顺序更新的转变。这种人性化是对人工智能技术定性特征的回应，必须在理论上和实践上解决这些问题，最好与技术的定量方法结合起来。人工智能引发了哲学辩论，其中许多辩论仍然肤浅，正是因为缺乏人文主义者。人工智能技术主要由营利性部门主导，因此错过了商业用途以外的潜在用途。赋予该技术应有的哲学尊严并认真对待其代理形式可以塑造人工智能的创造、应用和最终的政策。My diagnosis of what feels like a crisis on the ground is that we are undergoing a humanitization of computer science, which requires an integration of humanistic approaches, concepts, and values into the creation, application, and assessment of AI. While the term humanitization might recall the idea of anthropomorphizing machines or aligning them with human needs, I use this term as a much deeper, and chronologically newer, shift that is taking place within and without computing. This humanitization is a response to qualitative characteristics of AI technologies that have to be addressed both theoretically and practically, ideally jointly with the quantitative approaches to the technology. AI has stirred philosophical debates, many of which remain shallow precisely because of the lack of humanists at the table. Led largely by the for-profit sector, AI technologies are missing out on potential uses beyond commercial purposes. Giving the technology its due philosophical dignity and taking its forms of agency seriously could shape AI creation, application, and ultimately policy.

计算机科学的人性化是一项艰巨的任务，在许多方面比最近的经济学人性化要求更高。The humanitization of computer science is a colossal task, in many ways more demanding than the recent humanitization of economics.111经济学也经历了类似的发展，从亚当·斯密的道德哲学开始，不断受到人文学科思想的影响。随着时间的推移，它变得更加技术化，并使用数学方法，特别是计量经济学，在二十世纪使用统计模型和形式证明。二十一世纪，经济学再次变得更加人性化，产生了行为经济学、发展经济学、生态经济学、经济史和政策等子领域。Economics went through analogous development, starting in moral philosophy with Adam Smith and being continuously shaped with ideas from the humanities. Over time, it became more technical and used mathematical approaches, particularly econometrics, using statistical modeling and formal proofs in the twentieth century. In the twenty-first century, economics became more humanized again, resulting in subfields such as behavioral economics, development economics, ecological economics, economic history and policy.它是一个更大现象的一部分，在一个多世纪的不同目标之后，科学和人文学科走到了一起，提供了综合见解，否则这些见解将在高度细分的专业探究中丢失。It is a part of a larger phenomenon where sciences and humanities come together after over a century of divergent objectives, affording synthetic insights that would otherwise be lost in hypersegmented, specialized inquiry.

人工智能的本质是文学AI is Literary Underneath

文化人工智能是实现这种人性化的一种方式，如果不包含文学研究的见解就不可能成功。文学研究为人工智能文化成分的诊断、评估、理论化和实验提供了经过测试的新颖框架。 文化人工智能不是一个研究对象，而是一个需要一系列新颖方法的复杂问题。人工智能将文本从一种文化转移到另一种文化，是一种全新的文化相遇形式，其规模自动化，在文本历史上是前所未有的。文本和文化实践的条件——什么故事、比喻、形式、语言和价值观作为默认值传播，以及如何传播——需要文学研究的专业知识。文学学科已经通过文学比较阅读、叙事学和诗歌分析、批判理论、世界文学和翻译来应对文化斗争。由于人工智能是一种文本优先的技术，我建议解决文化人工智能的挑战，重点关注语言的产生。Cultural AI is a way into this humanitization and cannot succeed without including insights from literary studies. Literary studies provide tested and novel frameworks for diagnosing, evaluating, theorizing, and experimenting with AI’s cultural components. Cultural AI is not an object of study, it is a complex problem requiring a series of novel approaches. AI’s mediation of text from one culture to another is an entirely new form of cultural encounter, at an automated scale yet unprecedented in the history of text. The conditions of textual and cultural practice—what stories, tropes, forms, languages, and values circulate as defaults, and how—require the expertise of literary studies. Literary disciplines have already negotiated cultural struggles with comparative reading of literature, narratological and poetic analysis, critical theory, world literature, and translation. Since AI was set up as a text-first technology, I suggest addressing cultural AI’s challenges with a focus on the production of language.

文学研究具有独特的应对能力，因为它们已经在自己的文本材料上开发了理论、方法和诊断工具，现在可以将这些工具转移到人工智能的研究中。特别是针对法学硕士的技术和社会技术研究已经开始解决人工智能发展的一些文化方面的问题，例如算法单一文化的危险Literary studies are uniquely positioned to respond since they have developed theories, methodologies, and diagnostic tools on their own textual materials that can now be transferred to the study of AI. Technical and sociotechnical research on LLMs in particular has already begun to address some of the cultural aspects of AI development, such as dangers of algorithmic monoculture[kleinberg_algorithmic_2021, bommasani_picking_2022]以及为模型更加多元化所做的努力and efforts toward more pluralistic alignment for models[yong_state_2025, bhatt_extrinsic_2024]。与此同时，文学和文化研究将这项技术扎根于其历史根源，探索其叙事和创造能力，并研究了解释它的神话和叙事模板。. Meanwhile, literary and cultural studies have grounded the technology in its historical roots, explored its narrative and creative abilities, and examined the myths and narrative templates through which it is explained.

从制度上来说，工程学和文学研究是截然分开的。毕竟，机构是建立在将人类、自然和技术归类为完全独立的知识领域的哲学前提之上的。尽管如此，自 20 世纪 40 年代末人工智能诞生以来，文学想象力和人工智能计算就一直交织在一起，现在在法学硕士和其他神经网络模型中找到了新的交集。Institutionally, engineering and literary studies could hardly be more separated. After all, institutions are built on philosophical premises of categorizing the human, nature, and technology as entirely separate domains of knowledge. Still, literary imagination and AI computation have been interwoven since AI’s origins in the late 1940s and have now found a new intersection in LLMs and other neural network models.

下面，我提出了一个分层框架，将文学方法直接映射到人工智能的文化挑战上。Below, I propose a layered framework that maps literary methods directly onto the cultural challenges of AI.

第一层：文学形式和实践。Layer 1: Literary Forms and Practices.第一层涉及人工智能的灵感、实验、评价、阐释和解释中已经使用的文学形式和实践。我认为，从技术诞生至今，文学形式一直被用于技术的理论和实践发展。我聚焦于阿兰·图灵推测的智能机器以及他的人工智能研究议程的文学哲学影响，并改变了我们对基准作为高科技测试和机器发展北极星的看法。The first layer concerns the literary forms and practices already used in AI’s inspiration, experimentation, evaluation, interpretation, and explanation. I argue that literary forms have been used for the theoretical and practical development of the technology from its beginnings until today. I zoom in on Alan Turing’s speculated intelligent machine and the literary-philosophical repercussions of his AI research agenda and revamp our view of benchmarks as highly technical tests and north stars of machinic developments.

第二层：批判理论。Layer 2: Critical Theory.第二层是对其研究对象的元反思。我专注于将法学硕士与语言和认知联系起来的批判理论研究，揭示其技术设计中嵌入的认识论假设。我揭示了复兴的结构主义与后结构主义辩论如何引导我们走向文化人工智能的核心问题，我称之为The second layer is metareflective toward its object of study. I focus on critical theory studies that relate LLMs to language and cognition, exposing epistemological assumptions embedded in their technical design. I expose how the revived structuralism vs. poststructuralism debates lead us to the core problem of cultural AI, which I call结构性单语主义structural monolingualism：人工智能输出及其架构中文化和语言霸权的编码。我将展示如何在理论和实践中批判性地对待旗舰模型。: the encoding of cultural and linguistic hegemony in AI’s outputs and its architecture. I show how flagship models can be addressed critically, both in theory and in practice.

第三层：世界文学。Layer 3: World Literature.第三层是对不发达的探究领域的建议，从世界文学理论的角度解决人工智能不平衡的全球动态：1）帕斯卡尔·卡萨诺瓦（Pascale Casanova）提出的弗朗哥·莫雷蒂（Franco Moretti）的文本宏观结构方法，揭示了中心和外围的动态； 2）大卫·达姆罗什（David Damrosch）对流传文本及其新建立的联系的务实乐观态度； 3）艾米丽·阿普特（Emily Apter）基于不可译性的批评。The third layer is a suggestion toward an underdeveloped area of inquiry, addressing AI’s uneven global dynamics through perspectives from world literature theory: 1) Franco Moretti’s macro-structural approach to text, exposing the dynamics of centers and peripheries, introduced by Pascale Casanova; 2) David Damrosch’s pragmatic optimism about circulating texts and their newly established connections; and 3) Emily Apter’s criticism grounded in untranslatability.

每一层都让人工智能文化复杂性的不同部分变得可见。这些层根据它们与 AI 的关系按此顺序排列。第一层已经嵌入到技术发展中，第二层是响应技术发展而出现的，最后一层开启了与世界文学及其更广泛的学术界进行新对话的可能性。文学研究无疑可以提供更多东西，但我们才刚刚开始研究实际的人工智能技术，而不是几十年前我们全神贯注的想象技术。Each layer makes visible a different part of AI’s cultural complexity. The layers are arranged in this order according to their relation to AI. The first layer has been embedded into technological development, the second layer arose in response to it, and the final layer opens the possibility of a new dialogue with world literature and its wider scholarly communities. Literary studies undoubtedly have more to offer, yet we are only beginning to grapple with the actual AI technology rather than the imagined one that preoccupied us in the decades prior.

第一层：文学形式和实践Layer 1: Literary Forms and Practices

Looking at critical theory in Layer 2 through the lens of AI monolingualism, and using world literature approaches in Layer 3 to address the plurality of cultures clashing with AI products, the comparative and critical-theoretical framework provides the methodological spine to the rest of this essay’s layered argument.

第二层：人工智能的批判理论和结构单语主义Layer 2: Critical Theory and Structural Monolingualism of AI

人工智能以新的角度重新引发了文学领域的重大争论，重新开启了文学批评理论的基本问题：什么是文本？机器中介考虑了哪些文化表达形式？什么才算是人性化、原创性、创造性？什么才算认知？作为与人工智能的对抗，批判理论揭示了人工智能设计中嵌入的认识论假设。复兴的结构主义-后结构主义和文化认知辩论直接将我们引向我所说的结构单语主义：在人工智能的输出及其架构中编码文化和语言霸权。AI has revived major debates in the literary field with a new angle, reopening fundamental questions of literary critical theory: What is text? What forms of cultural expression are accounted for in machine mediation? What counts as human, original, creative? What counts as cognition? As a confrontation with AI, critical theory unveils the epistemological assumptions embedded in AI design. The revived structuralism-poststructuralism and culture-cognition debates lead us directly to what I call structural monolingualism: the encoding of cultural and linguistic hegemony in AI’s outputs and in its architectures.

结构主义与后结构主义的分歧尤其因为法学硕士而重新出现。二十世纪的结构主义著作可能被解读为对法学硕士的有先见之明的评论，泰德·安德伍德称之为“理论的经验胜利”The structuralism vs. poststructuralism divide was reanimated particularly because of LLMs. Twentieth-century structuralist writings may read as a prescient comment on LLMs, in what Ted Underwood called “the empirical triumph of theory”[underwood_empirical_2023]。这种联系是基于这样的认识：人工智能既可操作语言，又可将语言具体化。尽管结构主义具有相对稳定的结构来控制意义的产生，但它在情境语言和文化互动的细微差别方面仍然失败了——这与未能记录更细粒度的数据及其当地特殊性的大数据方法没有什么不同。相比之下，后结构主义批评认为法学硕士不稳定且根植于意识形态，通过表现而非稳定的知识产生意义，强调形式、声音和权力关系。争议在于，一方面，法学硕士似乎通过其数字内部性的关系制定了一种新形式的超结构主义；另一方面，他们的产出夸大了偏见，绕过了意义的产生，并产生了没有意图、背景或责任的言论。. This connection is grounded in the recognition that AI both operationalizes language and, in doing so, externalizes it. With its relatively stable structures governing the production of meaning, structuralism nonetheless fails at situated language and the nuances of cultural interaction—not unlike the big data approaches that fail to register finer-grained data and their local particularities. The poststructuralist critique, by contrast, sees LLMs as unstable and ideologically embedded, producing meaning through performance rather than stable knowledge, with an emphasis on form, voice, and power relations. The dispute is that, on the one hand, LLMs seem to enact a new form of hyperstructuralism made technical through the relations of their numerical interiority; while, on the other hand, their outputs dramatize bias, bypass the production of meaning, and produce utterances without intent, context, or accountability.

Weatherby argues that while generated text is cultural, the binary between cognition and culture in this framework is a false dichotomy: “Culture is the symptom of cognitivism”[weatherby_language_2025, 20]。人工智能输出的明显文化层并不是认知模型的表面附加物，而是作为形式化的认知的新兴特征。按照这种观点，文学和技术、文化和计算并不是可分离的模式，而是同一认识机制的纠缠表达。因此，批判理论将人工智能重新定义为一个场所，在这里它们的张力汇聚在一起，并且必须超越二元立场来锻造新的文化理论。. The apparent cultural layers of AI output are not superficial add-ons to cognitive modeling, but emergent features of cognition as formalization. In this view, the literary and the technical, the cultural and the computational, are not separable modes but entangled expressions of the same epistemic machinery. Critical theory therefore reframes AI as a site where their tensions converge and where new cultural theory must be forged beyond binary positions.

除了技术限制之外，人工智能最终是一系列人类决策和交互。每种文化都会向这些系统灌输不同的价值观，而且很明显，这不仅仅是技术人员的任务。Apart from technical constraints, AI is ultimately a series of human decisions and interactions. Each culture instills different values in these systems, and it has become clear that this is not solely a task for technologists.

The model’s inner embeddings, necessary to convert text into numbers to be operationalized by the model, thus become numerical representations of politics, shaping narrative outputs as truth. 这种意识形态的扁平化是综合单语制中更深层次的结构性问题的征兆。在为当代法学硕士提供支持的变压器架构中，文本数据被转换为数字标记。这些架构在形式上与数据无关，将文本视为数据、像素视为数据、任何其他输入视为数据。他们的标记化策略将单词、子词、标点符号和其他文本单元减少为标记，这意味着单词不是作为整个单词嵌入的，而是作为投影到模型潜在空间中的高维向量中的数字单元嵌入的。标记化是一种必要的工程解决方法，从离散单元（单词）到这些模型生成连续表示（向量）所需的专门的、较小的数字离散单元（标记）。向量根据训练数据模式中的相关性进行映射。因此，标记化将不均匀的语言材料转换为模型可以进行数学处理的专用离散单元。这个过程并没有完全消除语境，但它有效地将单词从其语言、历史、政治和美学语境中抽象为统计关系。一行诗、一份官僚备忘录或一首神圣的圣歌进入模型时具有截然不同的社会和文化功能，但都被呈现为针对预测而优化的可通约数据。This ideological flattening takes place as a symptom of a deeper structural problem in synthetic monolingualism. In the transformer architecture that powers contemporary LLMs, data as text is converted into numerical tokens. These architectures are formally data-agnostic in the sense of treating text as data, pixels as data, any other input as data. Their tokenization strategies reduce words, subwords, punctuation, and other textual units into tokens, meaning that words are not embedded as whole words but as numerical units that are projected into high-dimensional vectors in the model’s latent space. Tokenization was a necessary engineering workaround from discrete units (words) to specialized, smaller, numerical discrete units (tokens) that these models require in order to produce continuous representations (vectors). Vectors are mapped according to their relationality in the training data patterns. Tokenization thus converts the uneven material of language into specialized discrete units that models can process mathematically. This process does not erase context altogether, but it effectively abstracts words from their linguistic, historical, political, and aesthetic contexts into statistical relations. A line of poetry, a bureaucratic memo, or a sacred chant enter the model with radically different social and cultural functions, yet are all rendered as commensurable data optimized for prediction.

在某种程度上，代币化是一种已经在全球范围内使用的机械世界语。作为训练数据，任何语言都被分割成标记，并且可以作为其他语言出现在生成的输出中。这不是我们所知道的翻译行为：生成的输出被重新组装并从标记的公分母模拟为最可能的输出。法学硕士的翻译不是关于含义的对等，而是关于跨语言的标记相关性。这个问题不能像目前所做的那样通过简单地添加更多语言或更大的数据集来解决，因为它既涉及技术又涉及文化。除非我们从头开始以不同的方式设计模型，否则占主导地位的文化观点可能仍然是法学硕士的结构引力。Tokenization is, in a way, a machinic Esperanto that has reached global use. As training data, any language is fragmented into tokens and can emerge in the generated output as some other language. This is not an act of translation as we know it: the generated output is reassembled and simulated from the common denominator of tokens into the most probable output. Translation in LLMs is not about equivalence of meaning but about token correlation across languages. This problem cannot be fixed by simply adding more languages or bigger datasets, as it is currently being done, because it is both technical and cultural. Dominant cultural views are likely to remain a structural gravity of LLMs unless we design the models differently from scratch.

As Raja[lahiani_beyond_2025]以阿拉伯语 Mu'allaqāt 诗歌的人工翻译为例表明，由于这种诗歌形式中嵌入文化的非语言交流，误译很常见。 Lahiani 提出了注释工具、文化注释和多模式翻译方法。即使在数量较少的语言社区中，也仍然存在不可翻译的内容。它们捕捉到了无法全球化或可能拒绝旅行的独特地点、幽默和情感。不可译性在阿普特的作品中被视为一个机会：进入一个不同的思想和认知、语言和形式世界的节点。在进行快速拨号时，法学硕士还可以了解习语的局部性、文本的情境性、有意义的不对称性和歧义性。数字文本和模拟文本都具有足够的可塑性来适应它，各自以自己的方式。shows in the example of human translations of Arabic Mu’allaqāt poetry, mistranslations are common because of the culturally embedded nonverbal communication in this poetic form. Lahiani proposes annotation tools, cultural notes, and multimodal translational approaches. Even within numerically small language communities untranslatables remain. They capture the distinct place, humor, sentiment that are not globalizable or might refuse to travel. Untranslatability is framed as an opportunity in Apter’s work: a node into a different world of thought and cognition, language and form. While performing an imperial speed-dial, LLMs could also lead into the idiom’s locality, text’s situatedness, meaningful asymmetries and ambiguities. The digital and the analog text are malleable enough to accommodate it, each in their own way.

Moretti、Damrosch 和 Apter 相互合作或相互对抗，提供了一种关键实践，而全球文化范围内的人工智能发展尚未自行发展。总而言之，他们强烈主张具有文化素养的人工智能需要的不仅仅是扩展的数据集或多语言基准。人工智能需要一种哲学和设计，能够指出结构性不平等，为跨文化能力和丰富性设定标准，并为抵制翻译的空间保持开放。这些都是值得捍卫的价值观，这就是比较文学在最好的情况下已经所做的事情，也是它现在可以为人工智能提供的东西，作为全球范围内文化的方法论途径。Moretti, Damrosch, and Apter, working with and against each other, offer a critical practice that AI development on a culturally global scale has yet to develop for itself. Taken together, they make a strong claim that culturally literate AI requires more than expanded datasets or multilingual benchmarks. AI requires a philosophy and design that can name structural inequalities, set standards for cross-cultural competency and enrichment, and hold open the spaces that resist translation. These are the values worth defending, and this is what comparative literature, at its best, already does and what it can now offer to AI as a methodological approach to cultures on a global scale.

文学和人工智能似乎处于两个极端。一个高度人文性，另一个高度技术性；一个是人类主观性的表达，另一个是机械统计；一个提出问题，另一个回答；一个人抵制总结，另一个人则靠总结而茁壮成长。人工智能创造了个性化的确定性，而文学则提供了一种反实践，凸显了人类在世界上的复杂性。Literature and AI seem to stand at opposite ends of the spectrum. One highly humanistic, the other highly technical; one an expression of human subjectivity, the other of mechanical statistics; one asks questions, the other answers; one resists summary, the other thrives on it. Where AI creates personalized certainty, literature offers a counter-practice, foregrounding the complexity of being human in the world.[goethe_conversations_2004]’s 世界文学Weltliteratur呼吁各国相互纠正。 200 年后，人工智能可能会呼吁人类和机器之间进行关系谈判，以责任感和敏感性为指导，开启语言的新维度，为新的文化空间带来一种形成世界的力量，而不是全球化的力量。was a call toward mutual correction among nations. Two hundred years later, AI might be a call toward a relational negotiation among humans and machines, guided by accountability and sensibility that opens new dimensions of language, a world-forming rather than globalizing force for new cultural spaces.
