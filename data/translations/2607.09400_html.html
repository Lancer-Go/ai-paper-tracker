电动汽车设备上自适应电池电量预测On-Device Adaptive Battery Power Prediction for Electric Vehicles
††谢谢：thanks:这项研究由德国联邦研究、技术和空间部 (BMFTR) 资助，作为 KI4BoardNet 项目的一部分，拨款号为 16ME0778。This research was funded by the German Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR) as part of the project KI4BoardNet under Grant 16ME0778.

Avik Bhatnagar, Anton Paule, Tobias Schuermann, Sebastian Reiter, Oliver Bringmann
摘要Abstract

电动汽车 (EV) 中的自适应电源管理需要准确的功率预测。尽管深度学习模型对于该领域的时间序列预测非常有效，但当接触到分布与训练数据不同的数据时，它们的性能很容易下降。我们引入了一种新颖的方法，可以在资源有限的电动汽车系统中进行设备上学习，从而不断使预训练的电池预测模型适应新的、未见过的数据。我们利用现有的预训练模型，将其转换为适应性强的版本，保留初始训练中的关键超参数知识。我们全面研究在线和离线模型适应策略。我们的结果表明，各种模型和时间范围内的预测性能都有显着提高，通过在线和离线适应技术，平均绝对误差分别降低了 7.49% 和 14.88%。这项研究强调了设备自适应的巨大好处，与现实电动汽车场景中未适应的模型部署相比，可以增强电池电量预测。Adaptive power management in Electric Vehicles (EVs) requires accurate power prediction. Although deep learning models have emerged as highly effective for time-series forecasting in this domain, their performance is prone to degradation when exposed to data with distributions different from the training data. We introduce a novel approach that enables on-device learning in resource-constrained EV systems to continuously adapt pretrained battery prediction models to new, unseen data. We leverage existing pretrained models by transforming them into adaptable versions that retain critical hyperparameter knowledge from their initial training. We comprehensively investigate both online and offline model adaptation strategies. Our results demonstrate significant improvements in forecasting performance across various models and time horizons, achieving mean absolute error reductions of up to 7.49% and 14.88% with online and offline adaptation techniques, respectively. This study highlights the substantial benefit of on-device adaptation, resulting in enhanced battery power predictions than unadapted model deployments in real-world EV scenarios.

I 简介Introduction

时间序列信号在日常生活中无处不在，是定期记录的数据。示例包括金融市场数据、环境测量和医疗传感器数据。分析历史值以预测未来趋势为主动规划、运营优化和风险管理提供了显着的好处。深度学习的进步使其技术在时间序列预测应用中得到普及Time-series signals, which are ubiquitous in daily life, are data recorded at regular intervals. Examples include financial market data, environmental measurements, and medical sensor data. Analyzing historical values to predict future trends offers significant benefits for proactive planning, operational optimization, and risk management. Advancements in deep learning have popularized its techniques for time-series forecasting applications[1]。与传统统计方法相比，多层感知器 (MLP)、卷积神经网络 (CNN) 和递归神经网络 (RNN) 等架构在未来值预测方面表现出卓越的准确性. Architectures such as Multi-layer Perceptrons (MLP), Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN) demonstrate superior accuracy in future value prediction compared to conventional statistical methods[2]。这些神经网络隐式地从输入信号中学习时间特征，并根据先前的时间步长预测单个或多个未来的时间步长. These neural networks implicitly learn temporal features from input signals and predict single or multiple future time steps from the previous time steps[3].

深度学习技术也已成功应用于电动汽车 (EV) 的时间序列预测，包括预测充电需求Deep learning techniques have also been successfully applied in time series forecasting for Electric Vehicles (EVs), including predicting charging demand[4, 5, 6]，估计电池充电状态（SOC）和剩余容量, estimating the battery State of Charge (SOC) and residual capacity[7, 8]。我们的研究将深度学习的应用扩展到瞬时电池电量预测。虽然现有研究涵盖从几分钟到几个月的预测粒度，但我们使用深度学习模型预测了非常短期（1-3 秒）的未来电池功耗值。这种主动预测可以支持电池管理系统（BMS）运行，优化车辆电池供电，并通过负载管理方案防止突然的功率浪涌或峰值下降. Our study extends the application of deep learning to the instantaneous battery power prediction. While existing studies cover forecasting granularities from minutes to months, we predicted future battery power consumption values for a very short-term horizon (1–3 seconds) using deep-learning models. This proactive prediction can support Battery Management System (BMS) operations, optimize the vehicle battery power supply, and prevent sudden power surge or drop peaks through load management schemes[9].

然而，由于数据分布的动态变化，实际的电池电量预测面临着挑战。环境因素（例如天气和海拔）和用户行为显着影响电动汽车的电力需求However, practical battery power prediction faces challenges due to dynamically changing data distributions. Environmental factors (e.g., weather and elevation) and user behavior significantly influence the electrical power demand of EVs[10]。驾驶员行为，特别是驾驶风格，直接影响推进和辅助系统的整体电力需求。剧烈加速会增加电池电流需求，而快速制动会减少再生制动能量回收。此外，电气悬架系统也会受到影响，因为激进的操作需要更频繁的调整，这会消耗额外的能量。此外，气候控制、加热座椅和手机充电器等功能也会增加能源消耗，并提供平衡能源需求的方法。鉴于环境因素和用户行为对电动汽车电力需求的重大影响，我们的方法采用了能够适应这些动态条件的深度学习模型。通过在资源有限的车载嵌入式系统上实施实时推理和持续学习，与基于云的解决方案相比，我们的方法提供了增强的数据保护并降低了基础设施成本。. Driver actions, particularly driving style, directly impact the overall electrical power requirements for the propulsion and auxiliary systems. Aggressive acceleration increases battery current demand, while rapid braking reduces regenerative braking energy recovery. Furthermore, electrical suspension system is affected, as aggressive maneuvers require more frequent adjustments, which consume additional energy. In addition, features such as climate control, heated seats, and phone chargers contribute to energy consumption and offer a means of balancing energy demand. Given the significant influence of environmental factors and user behavior on EVs’ electrical power demand, our approach employs deep-learning models capable of adapting to these dynamic conditions. By implementing real-time inference and continuous learning on resource-constrained in-vehicle embedded systems, our method offers enhanced data protection and reduced infrastructure costs compared to cloud-based solutions.

近年来，针对时间序列预测模型的持续学习的研究工作显着增加，现有研究主要采用基于重放记忆的技术来存储关键的学习模式。这使得模型能够不断适应最新的数据，而不会忘记以前获得的知识，从而最大限度地减少预测误差。例如，FSNetIn recent years, there has been a notable increase in research efforts towards continual learning for time-series forecasting models, with existing research predominantly employing replay memory-based techniques to store crucial learned patterns. This enables the models to continuously adapt to recent data without forgetting previously acquired knowledge, thereby minimizing forecasting errors. For instance, FSNet[11]通过使用适配器和内存单元增强模型主干中的每一层，用新数据进行自我更新。固体updates itself with new data by augmenting each layer in the model backbone with adapters and memory units. SOLID[12]使用测试样本以及上下文相似的训练样本来更新模型的预测层。另一种方法，OneNetuses test samples alongside contextually similar training samples to update the model’s prediction layer. Another approach, OneNet[13]，采用预测网络集成，通过更新集成权重和模型参数来适应。然而，与单一预测模型相比，OneNet 较高的计算需求使其不太适合边缘预测系统。, employs an ensemble of forecasting networks that adapt by updating both the ensemble weights and the model parameters. However, compared with a single-forecasting model, OneNet’s higher computational demands make it less suitable for edge forecasting systems.

这些现有框架不是为嵌入式系统设计的，也没有考虑这些系统固有的资源限制。例如，当重要模式存储在内存中时，基于内存的技术可能会导致大量内存占用。此外，对于较短的预测范围，由于在资源受限的设备中观察到的计算延迟，多个数据点（当前的和存储的）的实时模型参数更新是不可行的。因此，本研究的目的不是依赖专门的模型架构来进行适应，而是旨在通过设备端学习来增强现有模型架构的能力，以持续适应新数据。本文做出以下贡献：These existing frameworks are not designed for embedded systems and do not consider the resource constraints inherent to these systems. Memory-based techniques, for instance, can lead to a substantial memory footprint when important patterns are stored in memory. Furthermore, for short prediction horizons, real-time model parameter updates on multiple data points (both current and stored) are unviable because of the computational latency observed in resource-constrained devices. Therefore, rather than relying on specialized model architectures for adaptation, this study aims to empower existing model architectures with on-device learning for continuous adaptation to new data. 据我们所知，这项研究首次在资源受限的边缘设备上启用基于深度学习的时间序列预测模型，并具有设备上的学习功能，使模型能够适应不断变化的电动汽车功率预测的运行条件。所演示的方法也可以无缝转移到其他时间序列预测领域。To the best of our knowledge, this study is the first to enable deep learning-based time-series forecasting models with on-device learning capabilities on resource-constrained edge devices, allowing the models to adapt to changing operating conditions for electric vehicle power predictions. Ⅱ-AII-A 最先进的电池电量预测模型State-of-the-Art Models for Battery Power Prediction

我们使用了 NIXTLA NeuralForecast 库We employed the NIXTLA NeuralForecast library[14]用于时间序列预测，利用其各种最先进模型的实现。我们利用历史外生特征和过去的电池电量值进行电池电量预测，以生成单变量预测。我们训练深度学习模型来隐式地从这些多个输入时间序列中学习并预测输出功率。考虑的模型包括基于多层感知器 (MLP) 的架构，例如具有外生变量的神经基础扩展分析 (N-BEATSx)for time-series forecasting, leveraging its implementation of various state-of-the-art models. We utilized historical exogenous features and past battery power values for battery power forecasting to generate univariate forecasts. The models considered included Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) based architectures, such as Neural Basis Expansion Analysis with exogenous variables (N-BEATSx)[15]，深度非参数时间序列预测器（DeepNPTS）, Deep Non-Parametric Time Series forecaster (DeepNPTS)[16]，时间序列密集编码器（TiDE）, Time-series Dense Encoder (TiDE)[17]，以及用于时间序列预测的神经分层插值 (N-HiTS), and Neural Hierarchical Interpolation for Time Series Forecasting (N-HiTS)[18]。此外，我们评估了一维卷积神经网络双向时序卷积网络（BiTCN）. 数据集：Dataset:我们利用了慕尼黑工业大学汽车技术研究所的实际驾驶周期数据集中的电池和加热数据We utilized the Battery and Heating Data in Real Driving Cycles Dataset from the Technical University of Munich, Institute of Automotive Technology[27]。该数据集包含使用 BMW i3 进行的 72 次真实驾驶行程（. For online continuous model adaptation, we selected a learning rate equal to 0.1 times the training learning rate for each model and prediction horizon. Lower learning rate is a common practice for fine-tuning models with limited data, ensuring conservative updates, and balancing learning from new data points while retaining prior prediction knowledge. This strategy allows for on-the-fly model updates, where performance improves as a trip progresses compared to unadapted models. Alternatively, an offline update strategy is used to adapt the model to recent trip data after trip completion to improve the performance of upcoming trips. This involves iterating through the trip data multiple times and updating the model with the same batch gradient settings (learning rate and batch size) used in the initial training for 100 epochs. In this batch learning setting, the gradients are averaged over the entire batch, preventing overfitting to a single observation, unlike online adaptation. Consequently, the original learning rate is appropriate here, whereas it would lead to suboptimal adaptation and degraded long-term performance in the online case. Offline adaptation is also computationally more intensive than online adaptation because it processes data for multiple epochs, whereas online adaptation processes data only once.
