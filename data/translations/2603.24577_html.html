摘要Abstract

可变形软组织的精确 3D 重建对于手术机器人感知至关重要。然而，低纹理表面、镜面高光和仪器遮挡通常会破坏几何连续性，这对现有的固定拓扑方法提出了挑战。为了解决这个问题，我们建议Accurate 3D reconstruction of deformable soft tissues is essential for surgical robotic perception. However, low-texture surfaces, specular highlights, and instrument occlusions often fragment geometric continuity, posing a challenge for existing fixed-topology approaches. To address this, we propose内镜VGGTEndoVGGT，一个以几何为中心的框架，配备了, a geometry-centric framework equipped with a变形感知图注意力（DeGAT）Deformation-aware Graph Attention (DeGAT)模块。 DeGAT 不是使用静态空间邻域，而是动态构建特征空间语义图来捕获相干组织区域之间的远程相关性。这使得结构线索能够在遮挡之间稳健传播，增强全局一致性并改善非刚性变形恢复。 SCARED 上的大量实验表明，我们的方法显着提高了保真度，与之前最先进的技术相比，PSNR 提高了 24.6%，SSIM 提高了 9.1%。至关重要的是，EndoVGGT 对看不见的 SCARED 和 EndoNeRF 域表现出强大的零样本跨数据集泛化能力，证实 DeGAT 能够学习与域无关的几何先验。这些结果凸显了动态特征空间建模对于一致的手术 3D 重建的功效。module. Rather than using static spatial neighborhoods, DeGAT dynamically constructs feature-space semantic graphs to capture long-range correlations among coherent tissue regions. This enables robust propagation of structural cues across occlusions, enforcing global consistency and improving non-rigid deformation recovery. Extensive experiments on SCARED show that our method significantly improves fidelity, increasing PSNR by 24.6% and SSIM by 9.1% over prior state-of-the-art. Crucially, EndoVGGT exhibits strong zero-shot cross-dataset generalization to the unseen SCARED and EndoNeRF domains, confirming that DeGAT learns domain-agnostic geometric priors. These results highlight the efficacy of dynamic feature-space modeling for consistent surgical 3D reconstruction.

1 简介Introduction

内窥镜手术场景的三维重建是现代手术实践的基本组成部分，包括机器人辅助手术Three-dimensional reconstruction of endoscopic surgical scenes is a fundamental component of modern surgical practice, including robot-assisted surgery[28, 27]和计算机辅助手术培训and computer-assisted surgical training[23]。通过提供准确的深度信息，系统增强了导航能力. By providing accurate depth information, the systems enhance navigation[13]和腹腔镜训练and laparoscopic training[5]，从而提高下游手术任务的感知精度。, thereby increasing perception precision for downstream surgical tasks.

大型重建模型 (LRM) 是基于变压器的前馈网络，在不同的多场景数据集上进行训练，以使用 VGGT 学习与场景无关的几何先验Large Reconstruction Models (LRMs) are transformer-based feed-forward networks trained on diverse multi-scene datasets to learn scene-agnostic geometric priors, with VGGT[24]作为基于几何的模型的代表性示例。 尽管 LRM 在自然场景中取得了成功，但其在手术环境中的扩展从根本上受到域转移的限制。现有的 LRM 主要在假设静态几何和稳定照明的严格的、以对象为中心的数据集上进行训练。相比之下，手术场景具有固有的非刚性、软组织变形和动态器械遮挡的特点as a representative example of geometry-grounded models. Although successful in natural scenes, LRMs’ extension to surgical settings is fundamentally limited by domain shift. Existing LRMs are predominantly trained on rigid, object-centric datasets assuming static geometry and stable illumination. In contrast, surgical scenes feature intrinsic non-rigidity, soft-tissue deformation, and dynamic instrument occlusion[5, 10]。因此，直接部署通用域模型会产生伪影，包括破坏的组织拓扑和深度误差。与此同时，最近基于 NeRF 或高斯分布的手术方法依赖于每个场景的优化，这需要对每个新病例进行重复拟合，限制了它们在不同手术中的大规模推广。. Consequently, direct deployment of general-domain models yields artifacts, including disrupted tissue topology and depth errors. In parallel, recent surgical approaches based on NeRF or Gaussian Splatting rely on per-scene optimization, which requires repeated fitting for each new case, limiting their large-scale generalization across diverse procedures.
