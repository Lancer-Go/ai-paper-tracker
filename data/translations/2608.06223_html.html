TS-RAG：用于时间序列预测的检索增强生成TS-RAG: Retrieval Augmented Generation for Time Series Forecasting

Yixiong Xiao xiaoyixiong@baidu.com Baidu, Inc.BeijingChina , Congxi Xiao xiaocongxi@baidu.com Baidu, Inc.BeijingChina , Shuangli Li Baidu, Inc.BeijingChina and Jingbo Zhou jingbozhou@baidu.com Baidu, Inc.BeijingChina
摘要。Abstract.

虽然深度学习模型，特别是基于 Transformer 的架构，在时间序列预测方面表现出了令人印象深刻的性能，但检索增强生成（RAG）在该领域的应用仍然有限。由于 RAG 已被证明可以通过合并相关外部信息来有效增强大型语言模型的能力，因此检索相似的时间序列序列作为参考也可能会提高时间序列预测任务的准确性。然而，大多数时间序列模型都受到有限的训练数据、较小的参数规模以及缺乏大型语言模型中广泛的生成能力的限制。就像在语言模型中所做的那样，简单地将参考序列连接到提示中可能不会产生预期的结果。为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了一种新方法 TS-RAG，它利用 RAG 来提高预测性能。该框架引入了专门设计的参考标记，可以有效地将输入序列中的信息与检索到的相似序列中的信息融合起来，从而能够更稳健地捕获复杂的时间动态。实验结果表明，TS-RAG 在多个现实世界的预测基准中实现了一致的最先进性能。While deep learning models, particularly transformer-based architectures, have shown impressive performance in time series forecasting, the application of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) in this domain remains limited. Since RAG has proven effective in enhancing the capabilities of large language models by incorporating relevant external information, retrieving similar time series sequences as references might also improve accuracy in time series forecasting tasks. However, most time series models are constrained by limited training data, smaller parameter scales, and a lack of the extensive generative capabilities found in large language models. Simply concatenating reference sequences into the prompt, as done in language models, may not yield the expected results. To address these challenges, we propose a novel approach, TS-RAG, which leverages RAG to enhance forecasting performance. The framework introduces specially designed reference tokens to effectively fuse information from the input sequence with that from retrieved similar sequences, enabling a more robust capture of complex temporal dynamics. Experimental results demonstrate that TS-RAG achieves consistent state-of-the-art performance across several real-world forecasting benchmarks.

1. 简介Introduction

时间序列预测在众多现实应用中发挥着至关重要的作用，包括金融、医疗保健、能源和气候科学。根据历史数据准确预测未来价值的能力对于跨这些领域的决策过程至关重要。近年来，深度学习模型，特别是基于 Transformer 的架构，由于能够对远程依赖关系进行建模并捕获复杂的时间模式，因此在时间序列预测方面表现出了卓越的性能Time series forecasting plays a crucial role in numerous real-world applications, including finance, healthcare, energy, and climate science. The ability to accurately predict future values based on historical data is essential for decision-making processes across these domains. In recent years, deep learning models, particularly transformer-based architectures, have demonstrated remarkable performance in time series forecasting due to their ability to model long-range dependencies and capture complex temporal patterns(Vaswani et al., 2017)。这些模型擅长通过使用自注意力机制来处理顺序数据，这些机制使它们能够专注于输入序列的相关部分，甚至在很长的时间范围内也是如此。尽管取得了这些进步，大多数现有模型主要侧重于增强模型训练以提高准确性，例如结合多尺度混合. These models excel at handling sequential data by using self-attention mechanisms that allow them to focus on relevant parts of the input sequence, even over long time horizons. Despite these advances, most existing models primarily focus on enhancing model training to improve accuracy, such as incorporating multi-scale mixing(Zhou and Xu, 2021)和时间成像技术and time imaging techniques(Li et al., 2020)。这些方法旨在捕获时间序列内不同时间尺度上的复杂关系和依赖关系。然而，关于如何有效利用检索增强机制来提高预测准确性的研究相对较少。. These approaches aim to capture the intricate relationships and dependencies across different time scales within the time series. However, research into how to effectively leverage retrieval-augmented mechanisms to enhance forecasting accuracy is relatively scarce.

检索增强生成（RAG）已成为自然语言处理（NLP）中的有效范例，它通过在推理过程中检索和合并相关外部信息来增强大型语言模型。这种方法已被证明可以提高法学硕士的准确性和泛化性，特别是在知识密集型任务中，通过使模型能够访问其训练数据之外的外部信息源。 RAG 利用检索系统从大型语料库中提取相关数据的能力，确保模型的输出包含相关示例，从而提高其稳健性和灵活性。在时间序列预测的背景下，时间序列数据通常表现出高度复杂的模式，例如季节性、趋势和波动性。 RAG 可以通过检索与当前输入共享关键特征的相似历史序列来提高性能，使模型能够更加关注这些相似序列中的复杂模式，从而提高预测准确性。从这个角度来看，将 RAG 从 NLP 扩展到时间序列预测是一种很有前途的方法。Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) has emerged as an effective paradigm in natural language processing (NLP), where it enhances large language models by retrieving and incorporating relevant external information during inference. This approach has been shown to improve the accuracy and generalization of LLMs, particularly in knowledge-intensive tasks, by enabling the model to access external sources of information beyond its training data. RAG leverages the ability of retrieval systems to pull in pertinent data from large corpora, ensuring that the model’s outputs are informed by relevant examples, improving its robustness and flexibility. In the context of time series forecasting, time series data often exhibits highly intricate patterns, such as seasonality, trends, and volatility. RAG can improve performance by retrieving similar historical sequences that share key characteristics with the current input, allowing the model to pay closer attention to the intricate patterns in these similar sequences, thereby improving prediction accuracy. From this perspective, extending RAG from NLP to time series forecasting thus presents a promising approach.

然而，将 RAG 纳入时间序列模型面临着一些挑战。虽然直接的适应可能涉及检索相似的历史序列并将它们与输入序列连接起来，就像在大型语言模型（LLM）中所做的那样，但这种方法在时间序列预测中可能不会直接有效，因为它依赖于大型语言模型的泛化能力。尽管在开发基础时间序列模型方面做出了一些努力，但大多数仍然受到模型规模较小和训练数据不足的限制，缺乏法学硕士的生成能力。因此，简单地将检索到的序列附加到输入中并不能促进有效的信息融合，因为时间序列模型通常缺乏法学硕士的广泛生成能力，并且需要专门的方法来有意义地集成外部信息。另一个关键挑战是相似性检索的效率，特别是在使用动态时间规整 (DTW) 等传统方法时。这些方法的计算成本可能很高，需要大量时间从大型数据集中识别相似序列。因此，即使检索提高了预测性能，推理所需的时间也可能令人望而却步，限制了此类模型的可扩展性和实时适用性。However, incorporating RAG into time series models faces several challenges. While a straightforward adaptation might involve retrieving similar historical sequences and concatenating them with the input sequence, as is done in large language models (LLMs), this approach may not be directly effective in time series forecasting because it relies on the generalization ability of large language models. Despite some efforts to develop foundational time series models, most are still limited by smaller model sizes and insufficient training data, lacking the generative capabilities of LLMs. As a result, simply appending retrieved sequences to the input does not facilitate effective information fusion, since time series models generally lack the extensive generative power of LLMs and require specialized methods to integrate external information meaningfully. Another key challenge is the efficiency of similarity retrieval, particularly when using traditional methods like Dynamic Time Warping (DTW). These methods can be computationally expensive, requiring substantial time to identify similar sequences from large datasets. As a result, even if retrieval improves forecasting performance, the time required for inference can be prohibitive, limiting the scalability and real-time applicability of such models.

为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了 TS-RAG，这是一种利用检索增强生成进行时间序列预测的新颖框架。 TS-RAG 引入了专门设计的参考标记，可以有效地集成来自输入序列和检索到的相似序列的信息。这种机制不仅有助于融合历史数据中复杂的时间依赖性，而且还确保外部信息被有意义地合并。此外，通过采用矢量搜索技术进行高效的相似性检索，TS-RAG 显着减少了与动态时间规整 (DTW) 等传统方法相关的计算负担。因此，TS-RAG 可以在不影响预测准确性的情况下实现更快的推理时间，从而增强跨不同预测任务的泛化能力。To address these challenges, we propose TS-RAG, a novel framework that leverages retrieval-augmented generation for time series forecasting. TS-RAG introduces specially designed reference tokens that effectively integrate information from both the input sequence and retrieved similar sequences. This mechanism not only helps fuse complex temporal dependencies from historical data but also ensures that external information is incorporated meaningfully. Moreover, by employing vector search techniques for efficient similarity retrieval, TS-RAG significantly reduces the computational burden associated with traditional methods like Dynamic Time Warping (DTW). As a result, TS-RAG enables faster inference times without compromising on predictive accuracy, enhancing generalization across diverse forecasting tasks.

我们在多个现实世界时间序列预测基准上评估 TS-RAG，证明它始终优于现有的最先进方法。我们的结果强调了检索增强生成通过使模型能够以有意义和结构化的方式利用外部信息来推进时间序列预测的潜力。这项工作有助于弥合基于检索的增强技术和时间序列预测之间的差距，为通过额外的参考提高预测性能开辟新途径。We evaluate TS-RAG on multiple real-world time series forecasting benchmarks, demonstrating that it consistently outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods. Our results highlight the potential of retrieval-augmented generation in advancing time series forecasting by enabling models to leverage external information in a meaningful and structured way. This work contributes to bridging the gap between retrieval-based augmentation techniques and time series forecasting, opening new avenues for improving predictive performance with additional references.

2. 相关工作Related Work

2.1. 时间序列预测Time Series Forecasting

深度学习已广泛应用于时间序列预测，主要利用 CNN 和 RNN。卷积神经网络 (CNN) 使用卷积滤波器提取局部时间特征，使其能够有效捕获时间序列数据中的短期依赖性Deep learning has been widely applied in time series forecasting, primarily utilizing CNNs and RNNs. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) extract localized temporal features using convolutional filters, making them effective in capturing short-term dependencies in time series data(Bai et al., 2018; Borovykh et al., 2017)。循环神经网络 (RNN) 及其变体，例如长短期记忆 (LSTM) 网络和门控循环单元 (GRU)，由于能够捕获长期依赖性而被广泛用于序列建模. Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and their variants, such as Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks and Gated Recurrent Units (GRUs), have been extensively used for sequence modeling due to their ability to capture long-term dependencies(Hewamalage et al., 2021)。然而，基于 RNN 的模型存在梯度消失和顺序计算效率低下等局限性，使得它们对于长期预测任务的可扩展性较差。. However, RNN-based models suffer from limitations such as vanishing gradients and sequential computation inefficiencies, making them less scalable for long-horizon forecasting tasks.

基于 Transformer 的架构的引入The introduction of Transformer-based architectures(Vaswani et al., 2017)通过提供一种无需顺序处理即可捕获远程依赖性的机制，彻底改变了时间序列预测。与 RNN 不同，Transformers 使用自注意力机制来对任意时间步长的依赖关系进行建模，从而显着提高效率和可扩展性。专门为时间序列预测开发了几种 Transformer 变体。日志传输revolutionized time series forecasting by providing a mechanism for capturing long-range dependencies without the need for sequential processing. Unlike RNNs, Transformers use self-attention mechanisms to model dependencies across arbitrary time steps, significantly improving efficiency and scalability. Several Transformer variants have been developed specifically for time series forecasting. LogTrans(Li et al., 2019)引入卷积自注意力来提高效率。告密者introduced convolutional self-attention to improve efficiency. Informer(Zhou et al., 2021)提出了 probSparse 自注意力机制，在保持预测准确性的同时降低了计算复杂度。自动成型机proposed the probSparse self-attention mechanism, reducing computational complexity while preserving predictive accuracy. Autoformer(Wu et al., 2021)结合了季节性趋势分解，使模型对非平稳模式更加稳健。美联储前任incorporated seasonal-trend decomposition, making the model more robust to non-stationary patterns. FedFormer(Zhou et al., 2022)通过使用频域注意力来改进长期预测，扩展了这一概念。尽管有效，基于 Transformer 的模型仍然面临可扩展性挑战，特别是在处理大规模数据集和高维多元预测方面。最近的研究表明，即使是简单的线性模型也能取得有竞争力的结果extended this concept by using frequency-domain attention to improve long-range forecasting. Despite their effectiveness, Transformer-based models still face scalability challenges, particularly in handling large-scale datasets and high-dimensional multivariate forecasting. Recent studies suggest that even simple linear models can achieve competitive results(Zeng et al., 2023)，导致轻量级架构的发展，例如基于 MLP 的预测模型, leading to the development of lightweight architectures such as MLP-based forecasting models(Chen et al., 2023a; Das et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024a)。这些模型使用时间序列分解技术和多周期性分析来有效提取时间特征，同时减少计算开销。. These models use time series decomposition techniques and multi-periodicity analysis to efficiently extract temporal features while reducing computational overhead.

最近出现的时间序列基础模型（TSFM）进一步推进了时间序列预测。与在特定领域数据集上训练的传统模型不同，TSFM 利用大规模、多领域预训练来实现零样本预测能力。时间GPT-1The recent emergence of Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs) has further advanced time series forecasting. Unlike traditional models trained on domain-specific datasets, TSFMs leverage large-scale, multi-domain pretraining to achieve zero-shot forecasting capabilities. TimeGPT-1(Garza and Mergenthaler-Canseco, 2023)是第一个闭源 TSFM，展示了大型预训练模型用于一般时间序列预测的潜力。预测PFNwas the first closed-source TSFM, demonstrating the potential of large pre-trained models for general time series prediction. ForecastPFN(Dooley et al., 2024)通过对合成时间序列数据进行预训练，实现了强大的零样本预测性能。时代调频achieved strong zero-shot forecasting performance by pretraining on synthetic time series data. TimesFM(Das et al., 2024)引入了基于补丁的解码器架构，提高了长期预测的计算效率。为了进一步提高泛化能力，最近的工作重点关注多源训练数据集。莫伊莱introduced patch-based decoder architectures, enhancing computational efficiency for long-horizon forecasting. To further improve generalization, recent works have focused on multi-source training datasets. MOIRAI(Woo et al., 2024)构建了 LOTSA（一个大型开源时间序列数据集），并训练了一个基于掩码编码器的基础模型，该模型实现了与全镜头模型竞争或优越的性能。微型时间混合器 (TTM)constructed LOTSA, a large open-source time series dataset, and trained a masked encoder-based foundation model that achieved competitive or superior performance to full-shot models. Tiny Time Mixers (TTMs)(Ekambaram et al., 2024)采用轻量级混合架构，展现出强大的零样本预测能力。尽管 TSFM 显着提高了跨领域的泛化能力，但它们仍然严重依赖在预训练期间学到的隐性知识，限制了它们适应罕见、非平稳或以前未见过的时间序列模式的能力。为了应对这一挑战，最近的研究引入了检索增强方法，该方法明确地将外部知识纳入预测过程。adopted a lightweight mixing-based architecture, demonstrating strong zero-shot forecasting capabilities. Although TSFMs significantly improve generalization across domains, they still rely heavily on implicit knowledge learned during pretraining, limiting their ability to adapt to rare, non-stationary, or previously unseen time series patterns. To address this challenge, recent research has introduced retrieval-augmented methods, which explicitly incorporate external knowledge into the forecasting process.

2.2. 检索增强生成 (RAG)Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

检索增强生成（RAG）作为一种通过在推理过程中检索外部知识来增强模型能力的方法，在自然语言处理（NLP）中得到了广泛的探索Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) has been widely explored in natural language processing (NLP) as a method to enhance model capabilities by retrieving external knowledge during inference(Lewis et al., 2020; Guu et al., 2020)。传统的大语言模型（LLM）经常遭受知识切断和幻觉的困扰，因为它们仅依赖于参数记忆。 RAG 通过从大规模语料库中动态检索相关信息来缓解这些限制，确保更准确且与上下文相关的响应. Traditional large language models (LLMs) often suffer from knowledge cutoffs and hallucinations, as they rely solely on parametric memory. RAG mitigates these limitations by dynamically retrieving relevant information from large-scale corpora, ensuring more accurate and contextually relevant responses(Karpukhin et al., 2020)。在文本生成中，RAG已应用于问答、摘要和事实验证等知识基础任务，显着提高了零样本和少样本性能。此外，在对话系统中，RAG 通过检索用户特定信息或相关对话历史记录来实现更加个性化和上下文感知的响应. In text generation, RAG has been applied to knowledge-grounded tasks such as question answering, summarization, and fact verification, significantly improving zero-shot and few-shot performance. Furthermore, in dialogue systems, RAG enables more personalized and context-aware responses by retrieving user-specific information or relevant conversational history(Huang et al., 2023)。与完全参数化模型相比，检索增强方法允许更加模块化和可解释的知识集成，减少持续微调的需要，同时提高跨不同领域的适应性。. Compared to fully parametric models, retrieval-augmented approaches allow for more modular and interpretable knowledge integration, reducing the need for continual fine-tuning while improving adaptability across different domains.

除了 NLP 之外，检索增强技术已成功应用于其他领域，包括计算机视觉和结构化数据建模。在图像生成中，基于检索的模型通过从外部图像数据库中选择相关的视觉特征、风格参考或对象组合来提高质量Beyond NLP, retrieval-augmented techniques have been successfully applied in other domains, including computer vision and structured data modeling. In image generation, retrieval-based models enhance quality by selecting relevant visual features, style references, or object compositions from external image databases(Chen et al., 2023b)。与纯粹的生成方法相比，这可以实现更详细、更连贯的视觉输出。在结构化数据任务中，基于检索的学习已在各种环境中进行了探索，例如最近邻检索. This allows for more detailed and coherent visual outputs compared to purely generative approaches. In structured data tasks, retrieval-based learning has been explored in various contexts, such as nearest-neighbor retrieval(Zhang et al., 2016)，基于内核的检索, kernel-based retrieval(Nader et al., 2022)，以及基于原型的学习, and prototype-based learning(Arik and Pfister, 2020)。这些技术表明，集成外部信息可以提高金融、医疗保健和推荐系统等领域的预测准确性和稳健性。最近，人们提出了用于表格数据预测的检索增强深度学习方法，其中模型利用类似注意力的检索机制来选择相关历史模式并提高预测准确性. These techniques demonstrate that integrating external information improves prediction accuracy and robustness in domains like finance, healthcare, and recommendation systems. More recently, retrieval-augmented deep learning methods have been proposed for tabular data forecasting, where models leverage attention-like retrieval mechanisms to select relevant historical patterns and enhance prediction accuracy(Gorishniy et al., 2024).

尽管检索增强预测在自然语言处理和结构化数据应用方面取得了成功，但在时间序列研究中仍然没有得到充分探索。传统的预测模型假设所有必要的信息都编码在模型权重内，从而限制了对看不见的分布的适应性。早期基于检索的预测方法，例如 ReTimeDespite its success in NLP and structured data applications, retrieval-augmented forecasting remains underexplored in time series research. Traditional forecasting models assume that all necessary information is encoded within model weights, limiting adaptability to unseen distributions. Early retrieval-based forecasting methods, such as ReTime(Jing et al., 2022)，引入了关系检索机制来改进不完整序列的预测和插补。最近，RAFT（时间序列检索增强预测）, introduced relational retrieval mechanisms to improve forecasting and imputation for incomplete sequences. More recently, RAFT (Retrieval-Augmented Forecasting of Time-Series)(Tire et al., 2024)提出了一个显式检索模块，该模块从训练数据集中选择与输入序列相似的历史模式。通过将检索到的模式与学习到的表示相结合，RAFT 提高了预测准确性，同时保持了模型效率。与仅依靠自注意力来隐式捕获时间依赖性的基于 Transformer 的方法不同，RAFT 显式检索并集成相关的过去序列，从而提高了对不同时间序列分布的泛化能力。proposed an explicit retrieval module that selects historical patterns similar to the input sequence from the training dataset. By incorporating retrieved patterns alongside learned representations, RAFT enhances forecasting accuracy while maintaining model efficiency. Unlike Transformer-based approaches that rely solely on self-attention to implicitly capture temporal dependencies, RAFT explicitly retrieves and integrates relevant past sequences, improving generalization to diverse time series distributions.

Although TCN-based retrieval is slightly slower than ED retrieval (0.004s vs. 0.002s), it is still significantly faster than DTW-based retrieval (14.27s), making it a more practical choice for real-time applications.

5. 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们引入了 TS-RAG，一种用于时间序列预测的新型检索增强生成（RAG）框架，它有效地将检索的历史序列集成到预测过程中。我们的方法通过参考标记合并外部信息，解决了现有深度学习模型的主要局限性，促进复杂时间依赖性的更结构化融合。通过利用高效的向量搜索技术而不是动态时间规整 (DTW) 等计算成本较高的方法，TS-RAG 可以在保持高预测精度的同时实现更快的推理。我们对多个现实世界时间序列预测基准的广泛评估表明，TS-RAG 始终优于最先进的模型，突显了其在常见和罕见事件场景中增强预测性能的潜力。与仅依赖于内部特征提取的基于标准 Transformer 的架构不同，我们的框架检索并利用相关的外部序列，使模型能够做出更明智的预测，特别是在训练数据稀缺或缺乏多样性的情况下。这项工作弥合了检索增强方法和时间序列预测之间的差距，展示了自然语言处理 (NLP) 的想法如何有效地适应时间序列领域。In this work, we introduced TS-RAG, a novel retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework for time series forecasting, which effectively integrates retrieved historical sequences into the forecasting process. Our approach addresses key limitations of existing deep learning models by incorporating external information through reference tokens, facilitating a more structured fusion of complex temporal dependencies. By leveraging efficient vector search techniques instead of computationally expensive methods like Dynamic Time Warping (DTW), TS-RAG achieves faster inference while maintaining high predictive accuracy.Our extensive evaluation on multiple real-world time series forecasting benchmarks demonstrates that TS-RAG consistently outperforms state-of-the-art models, highlighting its potential to enhance forecasting performance in both common and rare-event scenarios. Unlike standard transformer-based architectures that rely solely on internal feature extraction, our framework retrieves and utilizes relevant external sequences, allowing the model to make better-informed predictions, particularly when training data is scarce or lacks diversity.This work bridges the gap between retrieval-augmented methods and time series forecasting, showcasing how ideas from natural language processing (NLP) can be effectively adapted to the time series domain.
