在阴谋不断展开的背景下，人工智能揭穿真相的成功令人惊讶 因为——与历史事件的阴谋论（例如 9/11、COVID-19）不同——有 没有大量的纠正证据可供借鉴。我们的实验不仅证明 人工智能模型能够减少人们对正在展开的阴谋的信念，但也揭示了它是如何 这样做。人工智能的策略因危机事件而异：当可用信息很少时 关于枪手及其动机（第一次特朗普暗杀企图），人工智能相对更了解 可能会强调依赖可靠信息来源和维护信息的重要性 认知上的谦逊，并且相对不太可能依赖提供事实和证据（因此 缺乏事实和证据）。相反，即使在事后立即了解更多信息 关于枪手及其动机（柯克刺杀），人工智能很大程度上依赖于此 信息，采用与揭穿经典时类似的策略 阴谋。The success of AI debunking in the context of unfolding conspiracies is surprising because—unlike for conspiracy theories about historical events (e.g., 9/11, COVID-19)—there is no large body of corrective evidence to draw upon. Our experiments not only demonstrate that the AI model was able to reduce beliefs in unfolding conspiracies, but also shed light on how it does so. The AI’s strategy varied across crisis events: when little information was available about the shooter and his motives (first Trump assassination attempt), the AI was relatively more likely to emphasize the importance of relying on credible sources of information and maintaining epistemic humility, and relatively less likely to rely on providing facts and evidence (as such facts and evidence were lacking). 人工智能能够破坏有关正在发生的事件的阴谋论的另一个原因可能是 参与者的信念（尽管在许多情况下充满信心）并不 完全成型。正如新生的阴谋论让怀疑论者无法进行深入研究的反驳一样， 任何支持阴谋发生的积极主张的证据都可能类似 贫困，因此（相对）容易受到批判性思维和认知的诉求的影响 谦虚。Another reason why the AI was able to undermine conspiracy theories about unfolding events may have been that participants’ beliefs (despite being confidently held in many cases) were not fully formed. Just as nascent conspiracy theories deprive skeptics of well-researched rebuttals, any evidence supporting the positive claim that a conspiracy has occurred may be similarly impoverished, and thus (relatively) easily swayed with appeals to critical thinking and epistemic humility.
