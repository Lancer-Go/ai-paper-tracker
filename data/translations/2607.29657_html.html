摘要Abstract

故障检测和诊断 (FDD) 技术对于提高 HVAC 系统可靠性、能源效率和维护效率至关重要。然而，在建筑物中有效部署 FDD 解决方案需要结构化的领域知识，这些知识可以桥接异构数据源、不同的设备类型和不同的诊断输出。 FDD领域内有限的数据可解释性和互操作性导致了碎片化的信息孤岛，阻碍了FDD和相关应用的实施，例如基于数字孪生的FDD框架和人工智能（AI）驱动的维护决策系统。本文提出了FDD本体（FDDON），这是一种模块化和可扩展的本体，用于形式化地表示变风量（VAV）HVAC系统组件、故障类型、症状状态、故障影响和相关属性。 FDD-ON 集成了 HVAC 系统 FDD 语义，以提供故障和症状属性的全面表示，并由明确定义的控制词汇表支持。此外，FDD-ON 还提供全面的故障、症状和影响库，以捕获 VAV HVAC 系统中的各种运行异常及其后果。通过明确的原因-故障-症状-影响关系，FDD-ON 可作为机器可解释的基础，用于查询诊断知识、映射异构 FDD 输出以及开发可互操作的 FDD 相关应用程序。 FDD-ON 使用公开的 VAV HVAC 系统数据集进行评估，并通过 FDD 开发应用程序进行演示。结果表明，FDD-ON 为跨各种应用推进可扩展、透明和可互操作的 FDD 解决方案提供了基础语义框架。Fault detection and diagnosis (FDD) technology is essential for improving HVAC system reliability, energy efficiency, and maintenance effectiveness. However, effective deployment of FDD solutions in buildings requires structured domain knowledge that can bridge heterogeneous data sources, diverse equipment types, and varied diagnostic outputs. Limited data interpretability and interoperability within the FDD domain have led to fragmented information silos, hindering the implementation of FDD and related applications, such as the digital twin-enabled FDD frameworks and artificial intelligence (AI)-driven maintenance decision-making systems. This paper presents an FDD Ontology (FDDON), a modular and extensible ontology to formally represent variable air volume (VAV) HVAC system components, fault types, symptom statuses, fault impacts and associated attributes. FDD-ON integrates HVAC system FDD semantics to provide comprehensive representations of fault and symptom attributes, supported by the well-defined controlled vocabulary. Additionally, FDD-ON offers comprehensive fault, symptom, and impact libraries to capture a broad spectrum of operational abnormalities and their consequences in VAV HVAC systems. Through explicit contributing cause-fault-symptom-impact relations, FDD-ON serves as a machine-interpretable basis for querying diagnostic knowledge, mapping heterogeneous FDD outputs, and developing interoperable FDD-related applications. FDD-ON is evaluated using publicly available VAV HVAC system datasets and demonstrated through FDD development applications. Results indicate that FDD-ON provides a foundational semantic framework for advancing scalable, transparent, and interoperable FDD solutions across various applications.

For example, it is estimated that the global HVAC maintenance cost was approximately $78.5 billion in 2023 and is projected to exceed $116 billion by 2030, driven by increasing demands for energy efficiency and the adoption of digital maintenance technologies [2]. Numerous faults and malfunctions occurring in HVAC systems can significantly increase energy consumption and maintenance costs [3]. For instance, studies have estimated that 15%–30% of energy in commercial buildings is wasted due to faulty, poorly maintained, degraded, or improperly controlled HVAC equipment [4–7]. Consequently, fault detection and diagnostics (FDD) technologies have become essential for improving system reliability, enhancing operational performance, reducing energy waste and supporting efficient maintenance practices. Extensive research efforts have focused on developing advanced FDD algorithms with improved fault classification accuracy, lower false alarm rates, greater scalability, and enhanced capability to address the scarcity of labelled fault data [8–12].

尽管在改进FDD算法方面做出了大量努力，但相对较少的研究涉及知识模型的开发，知识模型可以增强FDD相关解决方案的开发、评估和集成，例如基于数字孪生（DT）的FDD部署、语义FDD推理框架和人工智能驱动的维护决策支持系统。Despite substantial efforts on improving FDD algorithms, relatively few studies have addressed the development of knowledge models, which can enhance the development, evaluation, and integration of FDD-related solutions, such as a digital twin (DT)-based FDD deployment, semantic FDD inference frameworks, and AI-driven maintenance decision-support systems.

缺乏此类框架带来了阻碍的三个关键挑战The absence of such frameworks presents three key challenges that impede

(1)缺乏对故障（或特定部件故障）、症状和影响的统一定义。在许多FDD算法开发中，FDD的过程是通过症状的表现来识别特定组件的异常或故障[13,14]。然而，也有报道称，在FDD解决方案中，各种症状甚至故障影响都被归类为故障[15]。例如，在 FDD 工具中，空气处理装置 (AHU) 中的异常送风流量被归类为故障，而不是潜在故障的症状。此外，热水或冷冻水消耗量的增加被视为基于结果的故障，而不是故障影响[15]。这造成了故障定义的模糊性，从而对建立稳健的故障推断机制和对 FDD 方法进行有意义的评估提出了挑战。(1) Lack of unified definitions of faults (or specific component malfunction), symptoms and impacts. In many FDD algorithm development, the process of FDD is to identify abnormalities or malfunctions in specific components through the manifestations of symptoms [13,14]. However, it is also reported that in FDD solutions, various symptoms and even fault impacts are classified into faults [15]. For example, in an FDD tool, an abnormal supply air flow rate in an air handling unit (AHU) is classified as a fault rather than a symptom of an underlying fault. Furthermore, an increased hot or chilled water consumption is considered as an outcome-based fault instead of a fault impact [15]. This creates ambiguity in fault definitions and hence poses challenges for establishing robust fault inference mechanisms and conducting meaningful assessments of FDD approaches.

(2) 缺乏对与故障和症状相关的属性的充分说明。这些属性描述了故障机制、症状特征以及故障、症状和影响之间的因果关系。因此，属性信息对FDD算法的开发和验证、后续的维护活动具有重要的支撑作用。例如，在大多数 HVAC FDD 算法中，症状模式仅由偏差水平表示（例如，AHU 中的送风温度高于/低于基线或设定点），以指示某些类型的故障（例如，冷却盘管阀卡住，或加热盘管阀卡住）。然而，其他症状模式，例如温度缓慢响应或振荡，表明其他类型的故障（例如冷却盘管水侧结垢或冷却盘管阀门振荡）经常被忽视。(2) Lack of sufficient illustration of attributes associated with faults and symptoms. The attributes describe fault mechanisms, symptom characteristics, as well as the cause-and-effect relations among faults, symptoms and impacts. Therefore, attribute information significantly supports FDD algorithm development and validation, subsequent maintenance activities. For example, in most HVAC FDD algorithms, symptom patterns are only represented by the deviation levels (e.g., supply air temperature in an AHU is higher/lower than the baseline or the setpoint) to indicate certain types of faults (e.g., cooling coil valve is stuck, or heating coil valve is stuck). However, other symptom patterns such as temperature slow response or oscillation, which indicate other types of faults (e.g., cooling coil water-side fouling or cooling coil valve is hunting) are often neglected.

(3)FDD工具生成的异构数据输出的互操作性有限，对FDD结果的有效解释和利用造成重大挑战[16]。现有FDD和系统维护工具的输出由预定义代码、缩写和自然语言描述的混合组成，导致与故障观察、症状表现、诊断结论和故障原因评估相关的大量非结构化和格式不一致的信息。这种异构性极大地阻碍了 FDD 相关数据的自动化处理、集成和分析，从而限制了将原始诊断输出转换为可操作见解的能力，以评估系统运行性能、支持维护决策以及通过有效的纠正措施促进故障管理循环的闭合。(3) The heterogeneous data outputs generated by FDD tools have limited interoperability, causing a significant challenge for the efficient interpretation and utilization of FDD results [16]. The outputs of existing FDD and system maintenance tools consist of a mix of predefined codes, abbreviations and natural language descriptions, resulting in a high volume of unstructured and inconsistently formatted information related to fault observations, symptom manifestations, diagnostic conclusions, and fault cause assessments. Such heterogeneity dramatically hinders the automated processing, integration and analysis of FDD related data, thereby restricting the ability to transform raw diagnostic outputs into actionable insights for evaluating system operational performance, supporting maintenance decision-making, and facilitating the closure of the fault management loop through effective corrective actions.

为了应对这些挑战，研究人员越来越多地转向本体论，它使用正式、明确的概念模型来支持语义推理、知识整合和可解释的诊断。本体提供了领域知识的结构化和机器可解释的表示，这对于将数据驱动和人工智能技术集成到暖通空调系统控制和运维中特别有价值。在HVAC系统FDD的背景下，本体可以明确地对HVAC设备、组件、传感器、运行状态、故障、症状以及它们之间的因果关系进行建模。To address these challenges, researchers have increasingly turned to ontologies, which use formal, explicit conceptual models — to support semantic reasoning, knowledge integration, and explainable diagnostics. Ontologies provide a structured and machine-interpretable representation of domain knowledge, which is particularly valuable for integrating data-driven and AI technologies into HVAC system control and O&M. In the context of HVAC system FDD, ontologies can explicitly model HVAC equipment, components, sensors, operating states, faults, symptoms, and the cause-and-effect relationships among them.

因此，它们提供了异构数据源的一致解释，并有助于描述不同系统配置中的故障行为和发生机制。此外，本体促进语义互操作性并支持可解释的推理。这使得诊断方法能够将基于规则的逻辑、基于物理的关系和元数据驱动的方法结合在一个统一的框架内。这些功能使本体成为开发可互操作、可解释和可扩展的 FDD 生态系统的基础技术，极大地支持整个故障管理生命周期，从 FDD 开发、部署和实施到维护规划和纠正措施。As such, they provide consistent interpretation of heterogeneous data sources and facilitate the characterization of fault behaviors and occurrence mechanisms in different system configurations. Moreover, ontologies promote semantic interoperability and support explainable reasoning. This allows diagnostic approaches to combine rule-based logic, physics-informed relationships, and metadata-driven methods within a unified framework. These capabilities make ontologies as a foundational technology for developing interoperable, explainable, and scalable FDD ecosystems, which significantly support the entire fault management lifecycle, from FDD development, deployment and implementation to maintenance planning and corrective actions.

在本研究中，我们开发了FDD本体（FDD-ON），它为各种类型的故障和症状以及与故障和症状相关的属性的分类创建了标准化结构。 FDD-ON 将各种术语和词汇中的故障和症状的表示统一为关系本体，允许对故障概念之间的关系进行推断和推理，并针对注释 HVAC 故障进行了优化。In this study, we develop an FDD ontology (FDD-ON), which creates a standardized structure for the classification of various types of faults and symptoms, as well as attributes associated with faults and symptoms. The FDD-ON unifies the representation of faults and symptoms among varied terminologies and vocabulary, into a relational ontology that permits inference and reasoning of the relationships among fault concepts and is optimized toward annotating HVAC faults.

The libraries will be continuously evolving, so that new types of faults and symptoms in VAV HVAC systems can be included and the community can incorporate the latest results for their applications.

2.文献综述2. Literature review

为了支持开发适合 HVAC 系统 FDD 相关应用的本体，我们回顾了建筑领域本体开发和应用的现有研究。该审查确定了本体的开发进展并揭示了现有本体的差距。同时，有助于确定某些本体在FDD-ON的开发中是否可以重用。To support the development of the ontology tailored to the HVAC system FDD-related applications, we reviewed existing research on the development and applications of ontologies in the building sector. The review identifies the ontology development progress and uncovers gaps in existing ontologies. Meanwhile, it helps to determine whether certain ontologies can be reused in the development of FDD-ON.

行业基础类（IFC）提供了一组类（实体）、属性、关系、属性集和其他实体的定义，以实现建筑信息模型（BIM）软件平台之间的语义互操作性[24]。尽管IFC本体提供了HVAC领域设备的全面标准表示，但它缺乏FDD知识的表示，而FDD知识需要描述HVAC系统中的故障机制和相关症状状态以及后续影响。The Industry Foundation Classes (IFC) provides a set of definitions for classes (entities), attributes, relations, property sets and other entities to enable semantic interoperability among Building Information Model (BIM) software platforms [24]. Although the IFC ontology provides comprehensive standard representations of equipment in the HVAC domain, it lacks a representation of FDD knowledge, which needs to describe fault mechanisms and associated symptom statuses and consequent impacts in HVAC systems.

巴拉吉等人。引入了 Brick Schema，它提供了建筑物中各种系统的标准化、机器可读的描述 [25]。在 Brick Schema 中，定义了类层次结构，以将构建系统中物理、逻辑和虚拟资产之间的链接实体合并在一起。实体之间的关系可以通过系统内的物理连接来表示。多项研究工作将 Brick Schema 扩展或集成到各种应用程序中，以增强 HVAC 系统相关应用程序内的数据互操作性。例如，罗等人。扩展了 Brick Schema 来表示占用者的元数据 [26]。普伦尼格斯等人。扩展了用于自动舒适度诊断的 Brick Schema [27]。奇亚等人。报道将 Brick Schema 集成到插头负载控制策略中以减少负载 [28]。贾等人。使用 Brick 本体开发了一个用于评估冷水机组运行的语义框架 [29]。Balaji et al. introduced Brick Schema, which provides standardized, machine-readable description of various systems in buildings [25]. In Brick Schema, a class hierarchy is defined to incorporate linked entities across physical, logical and virtual assets in building systems. Relations between entities can be represented through physical connections within the system. Several research works extended or integrated Brick Schema into various applications to enhance the data interoperability within the HVAC system-related applications. For example, Luo et al. extended the Brick Schema to represent metadata of occupants [26]. Ploennigs et al. extended the Brick Schema for automated comfort diagnosis [27]. Chia et al. reported integrating the Brick Schema into the plug load control strategies for load reduction [28]. Jia et al. developed a semantic framework for evaluating chiller plant operation using the Brick ontology [29].

Haystack 项目是一个广泛采用的元数据框架，它使用标准化标签和关系来描述建筑资产、传感器、点和控件 [30]。它对于解决不同供应商特定的楼宇自动化系统 (BAS) 和建筑能源管理系统 (BEMS) 不一致的点命名约定以及支持跨供应商特定系统的可互操作建筑分析特别有用。尽管如此，Haystack 项目主要被设计为通用建筑元数据和标记框架，而不是 HVAC FDD 领域本体。其核心词汇不提供 HVAC 系统中的故障信息。因此，需要额外的FDD特定语义结构来以可重用和机器可解释的形式表示诊断知识。Project Haystack is a widely adopted metadata framework that uses standardized tags and relations to describe building assets, sensors, points, and controls [30]. It is particularly useful for addressing the inconsistent point naming conventions from different vendor-specific building automation systems (BAS) and building energy management systems (BEMS) and supporting interoperable building analytics across vendor-specific systems. Nevertheless, Project Haystack is primarily designed as a general building metadata and tagging framework rather than an HVAC FDD domain ontology. Its core vocabularies do not provide information of faults in HVAC systems. Therefore, additional FDD-specific semantic structures are needed to represent diagnostic knowledge in a reusable and machineinterpretable form.

此外，其他研究人员针对建筑系统操作和控制中的不同应用开发了各种本体。例如，李等人。开发了能源灵活性本体（EFOnt），它扩展了用于构建能源灵活性应用的现有术语、本体和模式[31]。施耐德等人。开发了控制本体（CTRLont）来定义 BAS 中的控制逻辑域[32]。邱等人。开发了建筑本体中的人类舒适度，以捕获与室内环境质量领域中的各种因素相关的人类体验，以便可以调整 HVAC 系统设置[33]。拉斯穆森等人。开发了建筑拓扑本体（BOT），它提供了建筑物拓扑的高级描述，包括楼层和空间、建筑元素和网络友好的 3D 模型 [34]。托马舍维奇等人。开发了一个设施本体来对所有静态知识进行建模，包括技术供应商数据、专有数据类型和机场设施管理活动中的通信协议[35]。Additionally, other researchers developed various ontologies for different applications in the building system operation and control. For example, Li et al. developed Energy Flexibility Ontology (EFOnt), which extends existing terminologies, ontologies, and schemas for building energy flexibility applications [31]. Schneider et al. developed a control ontology (CTRLont) to define the domain of control logic in BAS [32]. Qiu et al. developed Human Comfort in Building ontology to capture human experiences related to various factors in the indoor environmental quality domain so that HVAC system settings can be adjusted [33]. Rasmussen et al. developed Building Topology Ontology (BOT), which provides a high-level description of the topology of buildings including stories and spaces, the building elements, and web-friendly 3D models [34]. Tomašević et al. developed a facility ontology to model all static knowledge, including technical vendor data, proprietary data types, and communication protocols in the facility management activities of airports [35].

总体而言，文献强调了建筑物中使用本体的好处，即本体促进了解决方案供应商和系统之间的语义互操作性。同时，本体为高级分析和推理提供了基础，并促进复杂数据集的集成，这对于智能建筑的可扩展性和强大的自动化至关重要。Overall, literature highlights the benefits of ontologies used in buildings, i.e., ontologies promote semantic interoperability across solution vendors and systems. Meanwhile, ontologies provide a foundation for advanced analytics and reasoning, and facilitate the integration of complex datasets, which is essential for scalability and robust automation in smart buildings.

2.2 HVAC FDD本体2.2 Ontology for HVAC FDD

在FDD的背景下，语义本体可以支持高级推理和基于推理的检测[36]。然而，很少有研究开发 HVAC 系统 FDD 的本体或语义模型。现有本体和语义模型旨在为 HVAC 系统 FDD 任务实现一致的数据集成和自动推理。例如，陈等人。开发了三种类型 HVAC 设备的故障分类法，即空气处理机组、终端机组和组合式屋顶机组 [37,38]。在分类学中，作者不仅开发了标准化和结构化的命名系统来表示设备中组件不同位置的各种故障类型，而且还开发了定义的控制词汇来描述 HVAC 系统中的故障性质。然而，作者将故障症状分为故障类型（即基于行为的故障类型和基于结果的故障类型），导致参考过程和故障类型方面存在困难。In the context of FDD, semantic ontologies can support advanced reasoning and inference-based detection [36]. However, few studies have been conducted to develop ontologies or semantic models for HVAC system FDD. Existing ontologies and semantic models aim to enable consistent data integration and automated reasoning for HVAC system FDD tasks. For example, Chen et al. developed fault taxonomy for three types of HVAC equipment, i.e., air handling unit, terminal unit and packaged roof top unit [37,38]. In the taxonomy, the authors not only developed a standardized and structured naming system to represent various fault types in various locations of a component in equipment but also the defined control vocabulary to describe fault natures in HVAC systems. However, the authors classified fault symptoms into fault types (i.e., behavior-based fault type and outcome-based fault type), leading to difficulties in the reference process and fault types.

黄等人。将 Brick Schema 扩展为故障症状 Brick 元数据模式，即 FSBrick [39,40]。 在该模式中，FSBrick 提供了一个语义框架，将 HVAC 故障、症状、系统组件和可测量点联系起来，从而实现更加系统化和可解释的 FDD 结果。然而，该模式既没有完全捕获故障机制（例如成因和发生阶段），也没有完全捕获故障症状特征（例如症状方向和症状模式）。Hwang et al. extended Brick Schema to a Fault-Symptom Brick metadata schema, namely FSBrick [39,40]. In the schema, FSBrick provides a semantic framework that links HVAC faults, symptoms, system components, and measurable points, enabling more systematic and explainable FDD results. However, the schema did neither fully capture fault mechanisms, such as contributing causes and occurrence stages, nor fault symptom characteristics, such as symptom directions and symptom patterns.

布莱希曼等人。开发了AHU故障本体来描述故障发生位置和故障性质等部分故障特征[41]。使用建筑物中真实 AHU 的历史运行数据证明了所开发方法的有效性。但作者并没有完整说明本体的结构并提供故障类型库。Blechmann et al. developed fault ontology for AHUs to depict part of fault characteristics such as fault occurrence locations and fault nature [41]. The effectiveness of the developed approach was demonstrated using historic operation data of a real AHU in the building. However, the authors did not completely illustrate the structure of the ontology and provide a fault type library.

古拉巴帕斯等人。开发了 HVAC 自动化 FDD 本体，即 AFDDOnto [42]。 开发的AFDDOnto有两个功能：1）以知识模型的形式将BIM与BAS概念相结合，辅助AFDD模型开发； 2）从AFDD模型获得分析结果，以更新基于BIM的模型，以应用设施的DT。尽管该研究利用BIM和BAS的概念开发了语义模型，但缺乏对故障性质的详细描述，导致FDD诊断过程中难以实施参考。Gourabpasi et al. developed an ontology for automated FDD of HVAC, namely AFDDOnto [42]. The developed AFDDOnto has two functionalities: 1) integrating BIM with BAS concepts in the form of a knowledge model to assist AFDD model development; and 2) obtaining analytic results from AFDD models to update BIM-based model for the application of DT of the facility. Although the study developed a semantic model using concepts from BIM and BAS, it lacked a detailed description of fault natures, making it hard to implement reference during the diagnosing process in FDD.

普伦尼格斯等人。采用语义图从 HVAC 系统中的数据点创建诊断模型并识别潜在的因果关系 [43]。尽管该研究区分了故障类型和相关症状，但没有进一步说明每种故障类型和各种症状的内在机制。此外，它没有提出一种有助于简化 FDD 解决方案和 CMMS 中数据分析的数据格式。Ploennigs et al. employed semantic graphs to create the diagnostic model from data points in HVAC systems and identify potential cause-and-effect relations [43]. Although the study differentiated the fault type and associated symptoms, it did not further illustrate the inherent mechanisms of each fault type and various symptoms. Additionally, it did not propose a data format that can facilitate streamlining the data analytics in FDD solutions and CMMSs.

马拉克等人。开发了一个系统本体，由四个实体或类别组成，即办公楼、感知和控制、症状和故障类别[44]。该研究被证明可以有效地构建诊断直接图形以供故障诊断参考。 然而，该研究没有提供统一的结构化数据格式来表示开发的本体，这使得CMMS很难自动处理FDD结果。Mallak et al. developed a system ontology which consists of four entities or classes i.e., office building, sensory and control, symptoms and failures classes [44]. The study was demonstrated to be effective in building a diagnostic direct graphic for fault diagnosis reference. However, the study did not provide a unified and structured data format to represent the developed ontology, making it difficult for CMMSs to automatically process FDD results.

李等人。开发了一个领域本体，其中包括基于知识图谱的语义规则，用于建筑能源系统故障检测[45]。通过本体模型，可以基于规则的推理链来捕获故障动作机制。虽然该研究定义了五个顶级类别，即设备、传感器、数据点、位置和故障来说明故障检测过程，但缺乏对故障性质和其他类型测量（例如控制信号和仪表数据）的详细描述来表示故障症状和影响。Li et al. developed a domain ontology, which included semantic rules based on the knowledge graph for building energy system fault detection [45]. Through the ontology model, fault action mechanisms can be captured based on the inference chains of the rules. While the study defined five top classes, i.e., Equipment, Sensor, Datapoint, Location and Fault to illustrate the fault detection process, it lacked detailed descriptions on fault nature and other types of measurements such as control signals and meter data to represent fault symptoms and impacts.

2.3 现有工作的局限性2.3 Limitations of existing work

从上述研究中，我们确定了现有本体的三个主要局限性。他们是：From the above studies, we identified three major limitations of existing ontologies. They are:

(1)现有本体或语义模型对暖通空调系统故障和症状的机制和属性描述不充分。(1) Existing ontologies or semantic models have insufficient descriptions of mechanisms and attributes of faults and symptoms in HVAC systems.

(2)非本体提供开源故障库和症状库，涵盖HVAC系统中报告的故障类型。这阻碍了本体在其他应用程序开发中的可重用性。(2) Non ontologies provide an open-source fault library and symptom library that cover reported fault types in HVAC systems. This prevents ontology reusability for other application developments.

(3)现有本体除了FDD方式开发外，没有考虑各种应用，例如维护决策系统和容错控制解决方案的开发；因此，支持多种应用程序的推理能力较弱。(3) Existing ontologies do not consider various applications, such as the development of maintenance decision-making systems and fault tolerant control solutions, other than FDD approach development; and hence, have weak inference capabilities to support multiple applications.

3.1.1 应用范围识别3.1.1 Application scope identification

在本研究中，我们首先构建FDD领域各种应用的知识结构来确定应用范围。 FDD领域包括六个基本方向。In this study, we identify the application scope by first constructing the knowledge structure for various applications in the FDD domain. There are six fundamental directions that the FDD area includes.

(1)故障建模：(1) Fault modeling:故障建模是构建对硬件故障或软件故障的发生机制（包括故障发生位置和故障行为）以及相关故障症状和影响的准确表示[50,51]。为了改善故障建模结果，需要清楚地了解故障行为（例如故障发生位置）及其与故障症状的相关性（例如故障严重级别如何影响故障症状状态和幅度）。Fault modeling is to construct an accurate representation of occurrence mechanisms (including the fault occurrence location, and fault behaviors) of hardware faults or software faults, as well as the associated fault symptoms and impacts [50,51]. To improve fault modeling outcomes, it is needed to clearly understand fault behaviors (e.g., fault occurrence locations) and their correlations with fault symptoms (e.g., how fault severity level may affect fault symptom states and magnitudes).

(2)故障影响及影响分析：(2) Fault effect and impact analysis:故障行为和影响分析研究暖通空调设备和系统在故障操作下如何运行，从而揭示设备和系统中各种类型故障的特征和影响[3,52]。为了更好地评估故障影响和影响，必须明确故障症状和影响的属性范围。例如，在暖通空调系统中，对于同一类型的故障，在不同的运行条件下，其影响和影响会有很大差异。因此，在进行故障影响和影响分析时，应说明故障症状幅度和影响程度的不确定性。The fault behavior and impact analysis investigates how HVAC equipment and systems operate under faulty operations and consequently uncovers the characteristics and effects of various types of faults in the equipment and systems [3,52]. To better evaluate fault effects and impacts, it is essential to clearly scope the attributes of fault symptoms and impacts. For example, in an HVAC system, for the same type of a fault, effects and impacts vary significantly under various operational conditions. Therefore, the uncertainty of fault symptom magnitudes and impact levels should be illustrated during fault effect and impact analysis.

(3)FDD发展：(3) FDD development:FDD解决方案的开发通常包括两个阶段：i）异常检测过程是捕获系统或设备的运行异常； ii）故障诊断是指识别故障位置、区分故障类型并估计故障严重程度[53-55]。为增强发展The development of FDD solution often includes two phases as: i) the abnormality detection process is to capture the operational abnormalities of a system or equipment; and ii) the fault diagnosis is to identify the fault location, distinguish the fault type, and estimate fault severity levels [53–55]. To enhance the development

6

FDD 解决方案，尤其是推理驱动的故障诊断，需要建立对故障与相关症状之间关系的深入理解。of FDD solutions, especially for the inference-driven fault diagnostics, it is needed to establish a robust understanding of the relations between faults and associated symptoms.

(4)容错控制实现：(4) Fault tolerant control implementation:容错控制能够容忍 HVAC 系统中的故障，以提高可靠性和可用性，同时确保理想的运行性能[56-59]。当故障发生时，暖通空调控制系统可以隔离故障源，消除故障，并具有鲁棒性，可以减轻故障的负面影响[60]。为了制定容错控制策略，需要完全了解故障发生机制（例如故障发生位置、故障严重程度和故障行为）以及故障症状和影响。A fault tolerant control is capable of tolerating faults in the HVAC system to improve the reliability and availability while ensuring a desirable operation performance [56–59]. When a fault occurs, the HVAC control system can isolate fault source, eliminate the fault, and is robust to mitigate fault negative impacts [60]. To develop fault tolerant control strategies, it is necessary to completely understand fault occurrence mechanisms (e.g., fault occurrence location, fault severity level and fault behaviors), as well as fault symptoms and impacts.

(5)维护实践：(5) Maintenance practice:检测和诊断故障后，设施团队或技术人员应解决问题、修复故障或计划其他维护活动，以确保 HVAC 系统的运行性能。维护算法需要根据故障行为、故障影响程度、系统运行计划和设施团队安排等多种因素，有效支持各种维护策略（例如状态维护、预防性维护和预测性维护）[61,62]。为了支持暖通空调系统维护决策和实际维护工作，需要精确诊断故障类型，准确估计故障影响并推断故障原因。After faults are detected and diagnosed, the facility team or technicians should address the issue, fix the faults, or plan other maintenance activities to ensure the operational performance of the HVAC system. The maintenance algorithms need to effectively support various maintenance strategies (e.g., condition-based maintenance, preventive maintenance and predictive maintenance) based on various factors such as fault behaviors, fault impact magnitude, system operation schedules and facility team arrangement [61,62]. To support HVAC system maintenance decision-making and actual maintenance work, it is required to precisely diagnose fault types, accurately estimate fault impacts and infer fault contributing causes.

(6)故障诊断分析：(6) Fault diagnosis analysis:故障诊断分析调查暖通空调系统中的故障发生可能性、故障发生率、故障成因。它可以深入了解不同类型设备或系统在各种运行条件下的故障发生分布和模式。因此，分析结果支持多种应用，例如FDD方法的开发、设备改进和维护实践[7,63]。为了进行故障发生分析，需要从故障记录中获取完整的描述信息，包括故障类型、故障行为、故障影响以及如何检测和诊断故障。Fault diagnosis analysis investigates fault occurrence likelihood, fault occurrence rate, fault contributing causes in HVAC systems. It provides insight into fault occurrence distributions and patterns in different types of equipment or systems under various operational conditions. Hence, the analysis results support multiple applications such as the development of FDD approaches, equipment improvement and maintenance practices [7,63]. To perform fault occurrence analytics, it is required to obtain complete descriptive information from fault records including fault types, fault behaviors, fault impacts, and how the fault is detected and diagnosed.

(1) 症状检测Symptom detection：在系统运行期间，症状表现为特定测量中的异常模式，通过将实时间隔数据与预定义阈值或预期基线进行比较（例如，供应温度高于预期）来识别。因此，症状检测的目的是在超过阈值时识别并标记这些异常表现。成功的症状检测依赖于准确建立性能基线和适当定义检测阈值[67,81]。否则可能会出现漏检、误报的情况。然而，HVAC 系统中的症状表现通常是不确定的，因为它可能受到许多因素的影响，例如故障严重程度、室内和室外环境条件的变化以及操作顺序的变化[82]。例如，在冷却需求较少的过渡季节，AHU 冷却阀卡在低位可能不会产生强烈的故障症状[54]。: During system operation, symptoms are manifested as abnormal patterns in specific measurements identified by comparing the real-time interval data with predefined thresholds or expected baselines (e.g., the supply temperature is higher than expected). Accordingly, symptom detection aims to identify and flag these abnormal manifestations when thresholds are overpassed. Successfully symptom detection relies on accurately establishing performance baselines and appropriately defining detection thresholds [67,81]. Otherwise, missing detection and false alarms may occur. However, symptom manifestation in HVAC systems is often uncertain because it can be influenced by many factors such as fault severity levels, varying indoor and outdoor environmental conditions, and changes in operational sequences [82]. For instance, an AHU cooling valve stuck at a low position may not produce strong fault symptoms during the transition season, when cooling demand is less [54].

(2) 故障诊断Fault diagnosis：在 FDD-ON 中，故障是指由于非功能组件（例如，泄漏阀门）、不正确的软件设置（例如，不正确的比例积分微分 (PID) 参数设置）或设计不当的操作过程条件（例如，尺寸不当的泵）而导致与定义的操作目标的不良操作条件偏差。因此，故障诊断的目标是根据异常运行状况的性质和在系统中的位置来区分异常运行状况。故障诊断的主要挑战之一是不同的故障可能产生相似或重叠的症状模式[83,84]。例如，在 AHU 中，送风静压传感器中的负偏压故障和卡在较高打开位置的室外空气风门可能会在送风风扇速度、风扇功率和冷却​​盘管阀位置方面产生类似的异常。这种症状重叠使故障的准确识别变得复杂。: In FDD-ON, a fault refers to undesirable operational condition deviation from a defined operational goal, caused by non-functional component (e.g., a leaking valve), improper software settings (e.g., incorrect proportional-integral-derivative (PID) parameter setting), or ill-designed operational processes conditions (e.g., an improper sized pump). Consequently, the objective of fault diagnosis is to distinguish the abnormal operating condition in terms of both its nature and location in the system. One of the major challenges in fault diagnosis is that different faults may generate similar or overlapped symptom patterns [83,84]. For example, in an AHU, a negative bias fault in the supply air static pressure sensor and outdoor air damper stuck at a higher open position can produce similar abnormalities in supply air fan speed, fan power, and cooling coil valve position. Such symptom overlaps complicate the accurate identification of a fault.

(3) 贡献原因识别：Contributing cause identification:成因表示导致故障发生的根本条件、行为或环境情况。揭示故障原因的过程是揭示特定故障背后的“原因”或“如何”。识别故障的成因对于有效地开展维护活动（例如，估计维护人工工作量和财务成本）至关重要。对于同一故障，维护活动可能存在显着差异。例如，对于AHU风门卡住故障，如果导致该故障的原因是风门控制器的通信线断开，那么维护工作就是重新连接通信线，并且与此维护活动相关的维护财务成本非常少。相反，如果故障是由风门执行器故障引起的，则可能需要更换部件，从而导致维护成本大幅增加并增加劳动力。Contributing causes represent the underlying conditions, actions, or environmental circumstances that lead to an occurrence of a fault. The process of uncovering fault contributing causes is to reveal the "why" or "how" behind a specific fault. The identification of contributing causes of a fault is critical to effectively carry out maintenance activities (e.g., estimate maintenance labor efforts and financial costs). For the same fault, the maintenance activities can be significantly different. For instance, for an AHU damper stuck fault, if the contributing cause of this fault is disconnected communication wires of the damper controller, then the maintenance work is to reconnect the communication wires, and there is very little maintenance financial cost associated with this maintenance activity. In contrast, if the fault is caused by a malfunctional damper actuator, component replacement may be necessary, leading to substantially higher maintenance costs and increased labor efforts.

尽管其重要性，但很少有人努力调查 HVAC FDD 中的故障原因。部分原因是识别促成原因通常需要复杂的因果推理和领域专业知识，而使用现有的 FDD 工具无法轻松实现这些专业知识的自动化。此外，在实践中很少有记录故障原因的记录，这使得很难建立系统的知识和标准化的表示。Despite its importance, very few efforts have been made to investigate fault contributing causes in HVAC FDD. This is partly because identifying contributing causes often require complex causal reasoning and domain expertise that cannot be readily automated using existing FDD tools. In addition, records documenting fault contributing causes are rarely available in practice, making it difficult to establish systematic knowledge and standardized representations.

(4) 故障影响分析：Fault impact analysis:特定组件中发生的故障可能会通过暖通空调系统传播，并对运行性能、能源消耗、居住舒适度、维护活动和财务结果产生更广泛的影响。故障诊断后，故障影响分析旨在使用各种性能指标来量化这些后果[3]。尽管症状和影响之间的区别并不总是明确的，但症状通常代表异常操作的可观察指标，而影响则描述由此产生的性能下降或相关后果。例如，功耗的突然增加或冷却能力的立即减少既可以作为可观察到的症状，也可以作为故障影响的指标。然而，故障影响通常代表更广泛的影响，包括能源损失、维护要求增加、设备可靠性降低和居住者不适。当故障无法快速修复时，减轻其影响就变得至关重要，并且可能需要实施复杂的容错控制策略。Faults occurring in specific components can propagate through HVAC systems and produce broader impacts on operational performance, energy consumption, occupant comfort, maintenance activities, and financial outcomes. Following fault diagnosis, fault impact analysis aims to quantify these consequences using various performance metrics [3]. Although the distinction between symptoms and impacts is not always explicit, symptoms generally represent observable indicators of abnormal operation, whereas impacts describe the resulting performance degradation or associated consequences. For instance, abrupt increases in power consumption or immediate reductions in cooling capacity may serve both as observable symptoms and as indicators of fault impacts. Nevertheless, fault impacts typically represent broader effects, including energy penalties, increased maintenance requirements, reduced equipment reliability, and occupant discomfort. When faults cannot be fixed rapidly, mitigating their impacts becomes essential and may require the implementation of sophisticated fault-tolerant control strategies.

综上所述，潜在的因果链可以总结如下：In summary, the underlying cause-and-effect chain can therefore be summarized as follows:原因导致故障，故障通过症状表现，故障随后产生影响contributing causes lead to faults, faults manifest through symptoms, and faults subsequently result in impacts。相比之下，FDD 推理过程则相反，从症状检测开始，然后进行故障诊断、成因识别和故障影响分析。该推理框架为以下各节中开发的语义模型提供了概念基础。. In contrast, the FDD inference process proceeds in the reverse direction, beginning with symptom detection, followed by fault diagnosis, contributing-cause identification, and fault impact analysis. This reasoning framework provides the conceptual basis for the semantic model developed in the following sections.

3.2.3 受控词汇3.2.3 Controlled vocabulary

在HVAC FDD领域，不同的利益相关者使用许多技术术语和描述来表示相同类型的故障或相似的症状[37]。这增加了模糊性，并可能严重阻碍故障信息的可解释性。因此，有必要开发一套受控词汇表，以提供一套更加策划、标准化的术语集，以一致的方式定义故障模式和症状状态，以及故障与相关症状之间的关系。In the HVAC FDD area, many technical terms and descriptions are used by different stakeholders to represent the same type of faults or similar symptoms [37]. This increases ambiguity and can significantly hinder the interpretability of fault information. Therefore, it is necessary to develop a set of controlled vocabulary to provide a more curated, standardized set of terms to define both the fault mode and symptom status, as well as the relationships between faults and associated symptoms in a consistent manner.

症状模式对故障发生时症状状态的描述方式进行了分类。在FDD方法的开发中，故障症状行为的识别和高保真模式匹配对于实现故障推理至关重要。大多数现有的 FDD 方法依赖于简单的稳态阈值交叉方法来检测和诊断故障。例如，如果 AHU 的送风温度持续高于设定点或定义的阈值，则 FDD 方法会检测到并标记症状（或异常）。使用此检测到的症状和其他症状，FDD 方法可以推断出很可能是冷却盘管阀卡住故障。然而，症状行为可能比简单的稳态阈值跨越更复杂。以上述为例，AHU的送风温度可能会出现振荡，即送风温度可能频繁超过上限和下限，这表明控制器出现故障（例如PID设置故障）。尽管许多研究使用特征工程方法来提取或揭示症状模式，但很少有研究工作来揭示症状模式[3]。因此，症状模式的分类可以促进 FDD 方法的更复杂、多维模式识别的发展。Symptom patterns classify how symptom statuses are described when a fault occurs. In the development of FDD approaches, an identification and high-fidelity pattern matching fault symptom behaviors is essential to implement fault inference. Most existing FDD approaches rely on a simple steady state threshold-crossing approach to detect and diagnose faults. For example, if the supply air temperature of an AHU is constantly higher than the setpoint or the defined threshold, then a symptom (or abnormality) is detected and flagged by the FDD approach. Using this detected symptom and other symptoms, the FDD approach can infer that it is highly likely a cooling coil valve stuck fault. However, symptom behaviors can be more complex than a simple steady state threshold-crossing. Using the example above, the supply air temperature of an AHU can be oscillating, i.e., the supply air temperature may surpass the higher threshold and lower threshold frequently, indicating a fault in the controller (e.g., a PID setting fault). Very few research works have been conducted to uncover symptom patterns even though much research investigated using feature engineering approaches to extract or uncover symptom patterns [3]. Therefore, a classification of symptom patterns can enable the development of a more sophisticated, multi-dimensional pattern recognition of FDD approaches.

电平偏差的子类描述了稳态发散，其中信号的值显着超过其预期基线或参考值。例如，较高的送风温度被归类为“水平偏差”子类别下的“高”二级类别。The subclass of the level deviation describes steady-state divergence, where a signal’s value significantly surpasses from its expected baseline or reference values. For example, higher supply air temperature is classified into the ‘High’ second-level class under the ‘Level deviation’ subclass.

时间模式的子类描述了故障发生时症状的瞬态行为。这种类型的行为可以捕获信号随时间变化的异常情况。例如，冷却盘管阀门位置的缓慢响应被分类为“时间”子类下的“缓慢响应”第二级类别。该模式可以指示控制参数（即传播增益）设置故障。The subclass of temporal pattern characterizes the transient behavior of the symptom when fault occurs. This type of behavior captures anomalies in terms of how signal evolves over time. For example, the outdoor air damper position and control signal mismatch symptom pattern uncovers an outdoor air damper stuck fault.

统计模式的子类揭示了在特定故障类型下症状如何表现的统计特征。例如，排风温度过高的症状发生概率可以表明排风风机在长期运行过程中发生故障时出现排风温度异常的可能性[5]。The subclass of statistical patterns uncovers the statistical characteristics of how symptoms manifest under specific fault types. For example, the symptom occurrence probability of discharge air temperature being too high can indicate the likelihood of the discharge air temperature abnormality under discharge air fan fault during a long-term operation [5].

开发故障库和症状库的第一步是定义本体的命名约定。在这项研究中，我们遵循规则来表示特定的故障和特定类型的症状[37]。然而，与[37]相比，一个主要修改是大多数基于行为的故障都定义了故障症状，以确保清晰的因果关系并保证稳健的基于语义的推理。The first step to develop the fault library and symptom library is to define naming conventions in ontology. In this study, we follow the rules to represent both a specific fault and a specific type of symptom as developed [37]. However, a major modification compared to [37] is that most behavior-based faults are defined fault symptoms to ensure a clear causal-effect relation and to guarantee robust semantic-based inference.

制定故障类型和症状的命名约定时有两个主要考虑因素。There are two primary considerations when developing the naming convention of fault types and symptoms.

首先，命名约定应简洁、准确，以反映故障类型和症状状态的性质，例如发生故障的设备和组件位置以及表现症状的测量类型。这增强了数据的可解释性；因此，在开发 FDD 相关解决方案时减少歧义，并促进将 FDD 结果解释和转化为可操作的维护活动。因此，在故障名称和症状名称中，尽量避免使用部件类型、位置的缩写以及故障类型和症状状态的描述。相反，我们仅使用设备类型的缩写，例如，我们在名称中使用 AHU to 来表示与空气处理机组相关的故障类型或症状状态。First, the naming convention should be concise and accurate to capture the natures of fault types and symptom statuses, e.g., the equipment and component locations where a fault occurs, and the measurement types that manifest symptoms. 4.4 局限性和本体演化4.4 Limitations and ontology evolution

本体的发展是一个不断发展和迭代的过程，因为它需要新的知识和实践过程中的实践经验。目前的FDD-ON在知识表示和数据结构方面有两个主要限制：The development of ontologies is an evolving and iterative process because it requires new incoming knowledge and practical experience during practices. The current FDD-ON has two major limitations in terms of the knowledge representations and data structure as:

(1)我们只开发了一级分类法来对故障原因进行分类，这可能不足以在更细粒度的级别上识别故障原因。此外，我们没有考虑故障传播，即故障可能是其他类型故障的促成原因。虽然这简化了故障原因和故障之间的当前关系，但它可能会限制更复杂的分析。(1) We only developed one-level taxonomy to categorize fault contributing causes, which may be insufficient to identify contributing causes at a more granularity level. Additionally, we did not consider the fault propagations, i.e., a fault can be a contributing cause for other types of faults. Although this simplifies the current relations between fault contributing causes and faults, it may limit more complicated analytics.

(2)在当前的FDD-ON中，故障与症状、故障与影响之间的关系仅限于设备层面，也就是说，故障、症状和影响之间没有建立跨层级的因果关系。这可能会限制 FDD 方法的发展和执行故障影响分析。(2) In the current FDD-ON, the relations between faults and symptoms, and the relations between faults and impacts are restricted to the equipment level, that’s said, no cross-level casual effect relations among faults, symptoms and impacts were developed. This may limit the development of the FDD approach and perform fault impact analyses.

除了解决局限性外，研究团队还计划从三个角度对FDD-ON进行更新和演进。In addition to addressing the limitations, the research team plans to update and evolve FDD-ON from three angles.

(1) 我们将通过在 HVAC 系统中添加更多设备类型、故障类型以及相关症状和影响来发展 FDD-ON。此外，我们将根据数据管理和协作开发的需要添加新术语，从而扩展 HVAC 系统中故障和症状的表示。(1) We will evolve FDD-ON by adding more equipment types, fault types, as well as associated symptoms and impacts in the HVAC systems. In addition, we will expand the representation of faults and symptoms in HVAC systems with the addition of new terms as needed for data curation and collaborative development.

(2)我们将调查更多的故障机制和症状状态，然后扩大受控词汇表。(2) We will investigate more fault mechanisms and symptom statuses and then expand the controlled vocabulary list.

（3）研究维护活动相关知识，纳入FDD-ON。(3) We will study and include the knowledge related to maintenance activities into FDD-ON.

5. 结论5. Conclusion

VAV HVAC 系统的高效运维对于确保能源效率、提高室内舒适度、降低运营维护成本和延长设备使用寿命至关重要。这些目标的实现依赖于多种先进技术的融合，其中FDD发挥着关键作用。 FDD的成功部署和实施不仅需要能够准确识别异常和诊断故障的鲁棒算法，还需要支持基于DT的FDD应用、人工智能驱动的维护决策支持系统和容错控制策略的结构化数据表示和语义模型。这些技术的集成实现了连接故障检测、诊断、纠正措施和影响缓解的闭环流程，从而提高了 HVAC 系统运行的整体可靠性、效率和可持续性。然而，现有实践中缺乏本体论来覆盖知识丰富性并促进 VAV HVAC 系统的 FDD 知识共享。Efficient O&M for VAV HVAC systems are essential for ensuring energy efficiency, improving indoor comfort, reducing operational maintenance costs and extending equipment service lifespan. Achieving these objectives relies on the integration of multiple advanced technologies, among which, FDD plays a critical role. Successful deployment and implementation of FDD requires not only robust algorithms capable of accurately identifying abnormalities and diagnosing faults but also needs structured data representations and semantic models that support DT-based FDD applications, AI-driven maintenance decision-support systems, and fault tolerant control strategies. The integration of these technologies enables a closed-loop process that connects fault detection, diagnosis, corrective actions, and impact mitigation, thereby enhancing the overall reliability, efficiency, and sustainability of HVAC system operations. However, there is a lack of ontology targeting to cover the knowledge richness and promote FDD knowledge sharing for VAV HVAC systems in existing practice.

在本研究中，我们开发了一个本体论 - FDD-ON，它采用知识表示和语义建模来增强数据可解释性和语义互操作性，并促进 VAV HVAC 系统各种应用程序之间的知识共享。我们为 FDD-ON 提供了几个新颖的功能，旨在提供 VAV HVAC 系统中的故障和症状的开源和正式的本体表示：In this study, we develop an ontology – FDD-ON, which employs knowledge representation and semantic modeling, to enhance data interpretability and semantic interoperability, as well facilitate knowledge sharing across various applications for VAV HVAC systems. We empower FDD-ON with several novel features, aiming at providing an open source and formal ontological representation of faults and symptoms in VAV HVAC systems:

(1)在开发四级语义模型时，我们考虑了FDD可以集成的多学科环境和应用的要求。(1) We consider the requirements of multidisciplinary environments and applications, which FDD can be integrated into, when developing the four-level semantic model.

(2)我们开发了各种分类法来对与故障和症状相关的各种属性进行分类，以完整地表示与FDD相关的不同级别的信息，从而增强语义推理。(2) We develop various taxonomies to classify various attributes associated with faults and symptoms to completely represent information at different levels related to FDD and hence enhance the semantic reasoning.

(3)构建了开源故障库和症状库，提供HVAC系统故障类型和相关症状状态的统一定义。开发受控词汇项来统一故障类型和症状状态的语义表示，从而增强数据可解释性。故障类型和症状状态的标准化和结构化命名约定增强了数据的互操作性，使其适合各种应用。迄今为止，FDD-ON 包括跨 241 种组件和 137 种措施的 469 种故障类型和 468 种症状状态，以及 VAV HVAC 系统中的 447 种影响类型。(3) We constructed an open-source fault library and a symptom library, which provide the unified definitions of fault types and associated symptom statuses in HVAC systems. Controlled vocabulary items are developed to unify the semantic representations of fault types and symptom statuses so that data interpretability can be enhanced. The standardized and structured naming conventions for fault types and symptom statuses enhance the data interoperability, making it suitable to various applications. To date, the FDD-ON includes 469 fault types and 468 symptom statuses across 241 types of components and 137 measures, as well as 447 impact types in VAV HVAC systems.

(4)我们从多个角度对FDD-ON进行评估，表明现有版本的FDD-ON很好地捕获了FDD区域的信息。此外，FDD-ON 的性质使其能够演进以解决不同应用中的新差距。(4) We evaluate FDD-ON from multiple angles to show that the existing version of FDD-ON well captures the information in the FDD area. In addition, the nature of FDD-ON enables its evolution to address new gaps in different applications.

未来，除了解决上述局限性和不断演进FDD-ON之外，我们还将展示FDD-ON在DT部署、人工智能驱动的维护支持系统开发和容错控制策略方面的更多应用。In the future, in addition to addressing the limitations and evolving FDD-ON as discussed, we will showcase more applications of FDD-ON in terms of DT deployment, AI-driven maintenance-supporting system development and fault tolerant control strategies.
