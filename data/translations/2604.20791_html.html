摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地应用于医疗保健领域，但其与临床标准的沟通一致性仍没有得到充分量化。我们对结构化医学解释和现实世界的医患互动中的通用和专业领域法学硕士进行多维评估，分析语义保真度、可读性和情感共鸣。 基线模型放大了相对于医生的情感极性（非常负面：43.14-45.10% vs. 37.25%），并且在 GPT-5 和 Claude 等较大的架构中，会产生更高的语言复杂性（FKGL 高达 16.91-17.60，而医生撰写的响应中为 11.47-12.50）。以同理心为导向的提示可减少极端消极情绪并降低年级复杂性（GPT-5 最高可达 -6.87 FKGL 分），但不会显着提高语义保真度。 协作重写产生最强的整体一致性。改写配置可实现与医生答案的最高语义相似度（最多Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed in healthcare, yet their communicative alignment with clinical standards remains insufficiently quantified. We conduct a multidimensional evaluation of general-purpose and domain-specialized LLMs across structured medical explanations and real-world physician–patient interactions, analyzing semantic fidelity, readability, and affective resonance. Baseline models amplify affective polarity relative to physicians (Very Negative: 43.14–45.10% vs. 37.25%) and, in larger architectures such as GPT-5 and Claude, produce substantially higher linguistic complexity (FKGL up to 16.91–17.60 vs. 11.47–12.50 in physician-authored responses). Empathy-oriented prompting reduces extreme negativity and lowers grade-level complexity (up to -6.87 FKGL points for GPT-5) but does not significantly increase semantic fidelity. Collaborative rewriting yields the strongest overall alignment. Rephrase configurations achieve the highest semantic similarity to physician answers (up toμ=0.93\mu=0.93），同时不断提高可读性并减少情感极端。双重利益相关者评估表明，没有任何模型在认知标准上超越医生，而患者始终更喜欢重写的变体以提高清晰度和情绪基调。 这些发现表明，法学硕士作为协作沟通增强器而不是临床专业知识的替代品最有效。) while consistently improving readability and reducing affective extremity. Dual stakeholder evaluation shows that no model surpasses physicians on epistemic criteria, whereas patients consistently prefer rewritten variants for clarity and emotional tone. These findings suggest that LLMs function most effectively as collaborative communication enhancers rather than replacements for clinical expertise.

1 简介Introduction

随着人工智能系统的能力和自主性越来越强，确保其与人类价值观的一致性已成为一个实际问题，而不是纯粹的理论问题As AI systems become increasingly capable and autonomous, ensuring their alignment with human values has become a practical concern rather than a purely theoretical one[klingefjord2024humanvaluesalignai, röttger2025safetypromptssystematicreviewopen]。在面向患者的医疗应用中，偏差可能会导致沟通不清晰、不适当的保证或不安全的建议，从而直接影响患者的信任和临床决策。 这个问题在医疗保健领域尤其重要，大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地部署在面向患者的环境中，通常是在以脆弱性和情绪困扰为特征的环境中. In patient-facing medical applications, misalignment may result in unclear communication, inappropriate reassurance, or unsafe recommendations, with direct consequences for patient trust and clinical decision-making. This issue is particularly critical in healthcare, where Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed in patient-facing settings, often in contexts characterized by vulnerability and emotional distress[DBLP:journals/npjdm/ArmoundasL25]。最近的证据表明，150 多家机构的超过 150,000 名临床医生已经依靠人工智能驱动的系统来协助患者发送消息. Recent evidence shows that over 150,000 clinicians across more than 150 institutions already rely on AI-powered systems to assist with patient messaging111https://www.nytimes.com/2024/09/24/health/ai-patient-messages-mychart.html，从而重塑日常临床沟通实践, thus reshaping everyday clinical communication practices[han2024ascleai, shool2025systematic, DBLP:journals/npjdm/RazaVK24].

虽然现有的研究主要集中在医疗人工智能系统的事实准确性上，特别是解决幻觉和可靠性问题While existing research has largely focused on the factual accuracy of medical AI systems, particularly addressing hallucinations and reliability[garciafernandez2025trustworthyaimedicinecontinuous, DBLP:journals/npjdm/AsgariBDKBYP25]，有效临床沟通的其他决定因素仍然相对未得到充分探索。在这项工作中，我们重点关注临床互动中至关重要的三个沟通维度：语义正确性（保留医学意义）、可读性（非专业用户的语言可访问性）和情感适当性（情绪基调与患者需求的一致性）。 如果缺乏这些价值观，患者可能会感到困惑、焦虑以及对医疗保健提供者的信任受到侵蚀, other determinants of effective clinical communication remain comparatively underexplored. In this work, we focus on three communicative dimensions that are central in clinical interactions: semantic correctness (preservation of medical meaning), readability (linguistic accessibility to non-expert users), and affective appropriateness (alignment of emotional tone with patient needs). 6 结论Conclusion

这项工作对跨两种不同环境的临床交流中的大语言模型进行了多维评估：机构策划的医学解释（MedQuAD）和现实世界的医患咨询（iCliniqQAs）。我们在基线生成、同理心提示和协作重写下分析语义保真度、可读性、情感共鸣和利益相关者一致性。This work provides a multidimensional evaluation of large language models in clinical communication across two distinct settings: institutionally curated medical explanations (MedQuAD) and real-world physician–patient consultations (iCliniqQAs). We analyze semantic fidelity, readability, affective resonance, and stakeholder alignment under baseline generation, empathy prompting, and collaborative rewriting.

结果表明，基线法学硕士并没有系统地提高与医生创作内容相关的可访问性或情感一致性。语言复杂性通常超过临床医生的水平，特别是在较大的通用架构中。可读性的提高主要是在明确的对齐策略下出现的。Results show that baseline LLMs do not systematically improve accessibility or affective alignment relative to physician-authored content. Linguistic complexity often exceeds clinician levels, particularly in larger general-purpose architectures. Readability gains emerge primarily under explicit alignment strategies.

以同理心为导向的提示可以减少情感极端并适度提高可读性，而不会显着改变语义保真度。然而，协作重写始终能带来最强劲的整体改进。改写配置在两个数据集中实现了与医生答案的最高语义相似性。在 MedQuAD 中，GPT5_Rephrase 达到最强的概念对齐，而在 iCliniqQAs 中 MedPaLM_Rephrase 实现最高的语义保真度。重写还可以最大程度地降低语言复杂性，并最一致地提高患者的信任度和情绪基调。Empathy-oriented prompting reduces affective extremity and moderately improves readability without significantly altering semantic fidelity. However, collaborative rewriting consistently yields the strongest overall improvements. Rephrase configurations achieve the highest semantic similarity to physician answers across both datasets. In MedQuAD, GPT5_Rephrase reaches the strongest conceptual alignment, while in iCliniqQAs MedPaLM_Rephrase achieves the highest semantic fidelity. Rewriting also produces the largest reductions in linguistic complexity and the most consistent gains in patient-rated trust and emotional tone.

专家评估证实，在准确性和精确度等认知标准上，没有任何配置能够超越医生。关系的改善并不转化为认知优势。患者评估显示出对重写变体的更强烈偏好，特别是在对话式临床环境中，其中清晰度和情感支持是核心。Expert evaluations confirm that no configuration surpasses physicians on epistemic criteria such as accuracy and precision. Relational improvements do not translate into epistemic superiority. Patient evaluations reveal stronger preference for rewritten variants, particularly in conversational clinical contexts, where clarity and emotional support are central.

总而言之，研究结果表明法学硕士作为协作编辑工具而不是自主沟通者最有效。人类与人工智能的共同作者提高了清晰度和关系一致性，同时保留了临床意义，但它并不能取代医生的专业知识。Taken together, the findings indicate that LLMs function most effectively as collaborative editing tools rather than autonomous communicators. Human–AI co-authorship improves clarity and relational alignment while preserving clinical meaning, but it does not replace physician expertise.

未来的工作应该将该框架扩展到多轮交互，整合适应领域的情感模型，让经过认证的临床医生参与结构化评估，并将分析扩展到多语言和资源匮乏的医疗保健环境。此外，未来的研究应该通过对不同严重程度的反应进行分层来调查临床问题的重要性对法学硕士行为的影响。这将使语义保真度、可读性和情感一致性作为临床紧迫性的函数进行细粒度分析。一个关键的假设是，法学硕士可能在低关键性情况下表现出与医生反应更强的一致性，而在需要精确推理、风险校准和谨慎沟通的高关键性情况下表现出退化。这样的分析将澄清当前模型是否在所有临床需求中都具有稳健性，或者在低风险环境中是否过于可靠。Future work should extend this framework to multi-turn interactions, integrate domain-adapted affective models, involve certified clinicians in structured evaluation, and expand analysis to multilingual and low-resource healthcare contexts. This would enable a fine-grained analysis of semantic fidelity, readability, and affective alignment as a function of clinical urgency. A key hypothesis is that LLMs may exhibit stronger alignment with physician responses in low-criticality scenarios, while showing degradation in high-criticality contexts that require precise reasoning, risk calibration, and cautious communication. Such an analysis would clarify whether current models are robust across the full spectrum of clinical demands or disproportionately reliable in lower-stakes settings.
