2 背景及相关工作Background and Related Work

概率逻辑和反事实。Probabilistic logic and counterfactuals.

地面 ProbLog 程序由概率事实和确定性规则组成。在分布语义下，概率事实定义独立的外生选择，并且逻辑程序将每个外生分配映射到唯一的符号世界，前提是该程序具有唯一的支持模型A ground ProbLog program consists of probabilistic facts and deterministic rules. Under distribution semantics, probabilistic facts define independent exogenous choices and the logic program maps each exogenous assignment to a unique symbolic world, provided the program has unique supported models(De Raedt et al., 2007). Kiesel et al. (2023)展示如何解释 FCM 等程序并通过双网络转换回答反事实查询，证明与 Pearl 式反事实和 CP 逻辑的一致性show how to interpret such programs as FCMs and answer counterfactual queries by a Twin Network transformation, proving consistency with Pearl-style counterfactuals and CP-logic(Vennekens et al., 2009). Habib et al. (2026)相反，提出单一世界干预方案，遵循 SWIG 思想，即干预将节点分为随机事实部分和固定干预部分instead proposes single-world intervention programs, following the SWIG idea that an intervention splits a node into a random factual part and a fixed intervention part(Richardson and Robins, 2013)。我们的工作假设这些普通的 ProbLog 结果，并询问当神经谓词产生一些外源符号选择时它们如何扩展。. Our work assumes these ordinary-ProbLog results and asks how they extend when some exogenous symbolic choices are produced by neural predicates.

Inference is typically reduced to arithmetic circuits or WMC after evaluating neural outputs. Scallop and related systems similarly reduce neural-symbolic inference to provenance circuits or weighted logical inference(Li et al., 2023; Valentin et al., 2025)。该电路视图通过代数模型计数进行统一，将 WMC、梯度和相关任务转换为共享电路上的半环评估. We do not claim universal DML for arbitrary logic queries but we prove a plug-in sensitivity result for general DeepSWIP queries and use AIPW only in experiments where the target is a randomized binary-treatment population estimand.

4 DeepSWIP 转型The DeepSWIP Transformation

DeepSWIP 的目的是将神经感知与符号反事实推理分开。 DeepProbLog 神经谓词不作为神经网络进行干预，相反，对于固定参数和固定输入上下文，它会在符号值上产生有限分布。因此，我们冻结神经计算，仅将引发的符号不确定性暴露给逻辑程序。这将神经概率逻辑程序转变为普通的 ProbLog 程序，之后可以应用标准因果程序转换。The purpose of DeepSWIP is to separate neural perception from symbolic counterfactual inference. A DeepProbLog neural predicate is not intervened on as a neural network, rather, for fixed parameters and fixed input context, it induces a finite distribution over symbolic values. We therefore freeze the neural computation and expose only the induced symbolic uncertainty to the logic program. This turns a neural probabilistic logic program into an ordinary ProbLog program, after which standard causal program transformations can be applied.

7 讨论和限制Discussion and Limitations

DeepSWIP 的精确性是相对于学习到的物化 FCM 的精确性。如果神经谓词校准错误，则符号反事实相对于外部数据生成过程可能是完全错误的。这不是SWIP的缺陷；这是因果符号推理和统计估计之间的区别。中的商导数DeepSWIP’s exactness is exactness relative to the learned materialized FCM. If a neural predicate is miscalibrated, the symbolic counterfactual can be exactly wrong with respect to the external data-generating process. This is not a defect of SWIP; it is the distinction between causal-symbolic inference and statistical estimation. The quotient derivative inTheorem 5.4使得这种区别变得明显，因为只有主动的、非干预的神经概率才重要，并且它们的错误会被逻辑和makes this distinction apparent as only active, non-intervened neural probabilities matter, and their errors are amplified by the logic and by1/ΦE1/\Phi_{E}.

DML 结果是有意限定范围的。 HOV AIPW 实验是有效的，因为我们构建了具有已知倾向的随机二元处理并评估总体平均值/ATE。这并不意味着任意 DeepSWIP 反事实查询具有正交分数，也不意味着 AIPW 估计单个反事实结果。对于一般的特定路径、连续或关系反事实，推导有效影响函数是一个单独的统计问题。类似地，近似 WMC 方法可以加速推理，但商的近似误差需要分母感知控制；我们将近似的反事实 WMC 留给未来的工作。The DML result is deliberately scoped. The HOV AIPW experiment is valid because we construct a randomized binary treatment with known propensity and evaluate population means/ATEs. This does not imply that arbitrary DeepSWIP counterfactual queries have an orthogonal score, nor that AIPW estimates individual counterfactual outcomes. For general path-specific, continuous, or relational counterfactuals, deriving the efficient influence function is a separate statistical problem. Similarly, approximate WMC methods may accelerate inference, but approximation error in a quotient requires denominator-aware control; we leave approximate counterfactual WMC to future work.

最后，我们的 MPI3D 规则故意变得简单。该实验并不是对最先进视觉或物理学的宣称。它的作用是在神经谓词具体化时分解 NeSy 反事实，在单一世界中执行符号干预，并且 WMC 精确计算结果商。Finally, our MPI3D rules are intentionally simple. The experiment is not a claim about state-of-the-art vision or physics. Its role is to breackdown the NeSy counterfactuals as neural predicates are materialized, symbolic interventions are performed in a single world, and WMC computes the resulting quotient exactly.

8 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 DeepSWIP，一个神经概率逻辑程序中的反事实推理框架。该方法对神经谓词进行一次评估，将它们具体化为普通概率选择，应用单世界干预变换，并通过 WMC 计算结果。这会产生相对于学习到的具体化 FCM 的精确反事实，并将反事实查询公开为 WMC 多项式的商。商观点为程序增长、剪枝、干预清理、校准敏感性、稀有证据不稳定性以及 DML 的有限作用提供了常见的解释。 MPI3D 和 SUMO 上的实验支持这些说法，同时保持统计范围明确。因此，DeepSWIP 最好不要被理解为一种通用的因果发现方法，而是具有明确因果结构和学习概率机制的 NeSy 程序的精确反事实推理层。We presented DeepSWIP, a framework for counterfactual reasoning in neural probabilistic logic programs. The method evaluates neural predicates once, materializes them as ordinary probabilistic choices, applies a single-world intervention transformation, and computes the result by WMC. This yields exact counterfactuals relative to the learned materialized FCM and exposes the counterfactual query as a quotient of WMC polynomials. The quotient view provides a common explanation for program growth, pruning, intervention cleaning, calibration sensitivity, rare-evidence instability, and the limited role of DML. Experiments on MPI3D and SUMO support these claims while keeping the statistical scope explicit. DeepSWIP is therefore best understood not as a universal causal discovery method, but as a precise counterfactual inference layer for NeSy programs with explicit causal structure and learned probabilistic mechanisms.
