However, existing reinforcement learning methods often rely on trajectory-level supervision, limiting fine-grained credit assignment in long-horizon TIR scenarios. 简介Introduction

工具集成推理 (TIR) 使大型语言模型 (LLM) 通过将推理与外部工具交互交错来解决复杂任务Tool-Integrated Reasoning (TIR) enables large language models (LLMs) to solve complex tasks by interleaving reasoning with external tool interactions(Gou et al. 2024; Qu et al. 2025a, b)。 与单步生成不同，TIR 代理会重复选择工具、构造参数、观察执行结果并调整其后续行为。 这些交互极大地扩展了法学硕士的能力，但也产生了一个困难的时间信用分配问题：最终的任务结果揭示了轨迹是否成功，但几乎没有提供关于哪些中间工具交互有用、多余或有害的信息。. Unlike single-step generation, a TIR agent repeatedly selects tools, constructs arguments, observes execution results, and adapts its subsequent behavior. These interactions substantially expand the capabilities of LLMs, but also create a difficult temporal credit-assignment problem: the final task outcome reveals whether a trajectory succeeded, yet provides little information about which intermediate tool interactions are useful, redundant, or harmful.

具有可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR) 为训练 TIR 代理提供了一个自然的框架Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) provides a natural framework for training TIR agents(Chang et al. 2026; Jiang et al. 2026)。 不过如图所示. However, as shown in Figure1(a)，基于结果的方法（例如 GRPO）通常会将单一轨迹级优势传播到所有生成的操作，为具有截然不同因果效应的决策分配类似的功劳(a), outcome-based methods such as GRPO typically propagate a single trajectory-level advantage to all generated actions, assigning similar credit to decisions with very different causal effects(Shao et al. 2024; Zeng et al. 2025)。 最近的方法通过将生成的工具调用与注释或参考工具轨迹进行匹配来提供更密集的监督. Recent methods provide denser supervision by matching generated tool calls against annotated or reference tool trajectories(Qian et al. 2025; Qu et al. 2026)。 虽然有效，但这种监督假设参考动作在学习策略访问的状态下仍然是适当的。 这种假设在多轮交互中变得脆弱：一旦代理调用不同的工具，环境就会发生变化，后续状态可能会偏离参考轨迹。 因此，TIR 的有效信用分配需要细粒度且符合国家要求的监管。. Although effective, such supervision assumes that the reference action remains appropriate at the state visited by the learned policy. This assumption becomes fragile in multi-turn interaction: once the agent makes a different tool call, the environment changes, and subsequent states may diverge from the reference trajectory. Thus, effective credit assignment for TIR requires supervision that is both fine-grained and state-aligned.

按政策自蒸馏 (OPSD)On-policy self-distillation (OPSD)(Shenfeld et al. 2026; Li et al. 2026b; Kim et al. 2026)提供了一种获得这种监督的有前途的方法。 它使用仅在训练期间可用的特权视图来评估学生生成的行为，从而提供有关政策状态的反馈，而不是规定外部轨迹。 然而，将 OPSD 应用于多转弯 TIR 仍然很重要。 如图offers a promising way to obtain such supervision. It evaluates student-generated behavior using a privileged view available only during training, thereby providing feedback on on-policy states rather than prescribing an external trajectory. However, applying OPSD to multi-turn TIR is nevertheless non-trivial. As shown in Figure1(b)，现有方法通常可以丰富教师的全球信息背景，例如真实答案、参考资料或提炼技能(b), existing methods often enrich the teacher with globally informative context, such as ground-truth answers, reference rollouts, or distilled skills(Zhao et al. 2026; Hübotter et al. 2026; Lu et al. 2026; Ye et al. 2026b)。 这些信号描述了如何解决任务，但不描述工具交互在其发生的状态下是否合适。 这种不匹配在 TIR 中尤其明显，其中每个工具调用都会更改外部环境，并可能导致后续状态偏离参考路径。 在这种情况下，轨迹本身包含最符合状态的监督，工具执行结果作为事后信号。. These signals describe how to solve the task, but not whether a tool interaction was appropriate at the state where it occurred. This mismatch is especially pronounced in TIR, where each tool call changes the external environment and may cause subsequent states to diverge from the reference path. In this setting, the trajectory itself contains the most state-aligned supervision, with tool execution outcomes serving as hindsight signals.

然而，仅靠国家协调是不够的。现有的自蒸馏方法通常产生令牌级信号，而TIR的基本决策单元是完整的工具交互，包括推理、工具选择和论证构造。 在一个回合内，由于格式化标记和参数级别的变化，标记级监督的大小甚至符号可能会有所不同。 因此，独立优化这些信号可以在同一交互中分配冲突的信用。 这激发了我们的轮次制定，在交互层面聚合事后监督，以提供更连贯的信用分配。State alignment alone is insufficient, however. Existing self-distillation methods generally produce token-level signals, whereas the fundamental decision unit in TIR is a complete tool interaction, comprising reasoning, tool selection, and argument construction. Within a turn, token-level supervision can vary in magnitude or even sign due to formatting tokens and argument-level variations. Optimizing these signals independently can therefore assign conflicting credit within the same interaction. This motivates our turn-level formulation, which aggregates hindsight supervision at the interaction level to provide more coherent credit assignment.

在本文中，我们提出了 TurnSight，一种用于多转弯 TIR 的转弯级后视自蒸馏方法。 TurnSight 不依赖外部参考轨迹，而是直接根据政策工具执行结果构建事后监督。 为了更好地匹配多回合 TIR 的决策粒度，它首先将令牌级事后证据聚合为连贯的回合级信号。 然后，它构建具有不同前瞻视野的多个后见之明视图，并通过跨视野定向协议选择可靠的监督。 最后，选定的后见之明信号在同级推出中进行标准化，并用于构建有界的、符号感知的权重，这些权重可以在不改变其优化方向的情况下调节 RL 优势。 因此，TurnSight 没有引入辅助模仿目标，独立于底层奖励构建，并且可以与现有的策略梯度 RL 算法无缝集成。In this paper, we propose TurnSight, a turn-level hindsight self-distillation method for multi-turn TIR. Instead of relying on external reference trajectories, TurnSight constructs hindsight supervision directly from on-policy tool execution outcomes. To better match the decision granularity of multi-turn TIR, it first aggregates token-level hindsight evidence into coherent turn-level signals. It then constructs multiple hindsight views with different lookahead horizons and selects reliable supervision through cross-horizon directional agreement. Finally, the selected hindsight signal is normalized across sibling rollouts and used to construct bounded, sign-aware weights that modulate RL advantages without changing their optimization direction. As a result, TurnSight introduces no auxiliary imitation objective, is independent of the underlying reward construction, and can be seamlessly integrated with existing policy-gradient RL algorithms.

对域内和域外基准的大量实验验证了 TurnSight 的有效性及其泛化不同工具使用场景的能力。 总而言之，我们的主要贡献如下：Extensive experiments on both in-domain and out-of-domain benchmarks verify the effectiveness of TurnSight and its ability to generalize across diverse tool-use scenarios. In summary, our main contributions are as follows:

∙\bullet我们对多轮 TIR 中的信用分配提出了两个要求：监管应与策略执行状态保持一致，并在交互层面保持一致。We formulate two requirements for credit assignment in multi-turn TIR: supervision should be aligned with on-policy execution states and coherent at the interaction level.

∙\bullet我们提出了 TurnSight，它可以导出以执行为条件的多视野事后评估，以在不改变 RL 优势方向的情况下调节 RL 优势。We propose TurnSight, which derives execution-conditioned, multi-horizon hindsight assessments to modulate RL advantages without changing their direction.

∙\bullet对域内和域外基准的大量实验证明了 TurnSight 的有效性、稳健性和泛化能力。Extensive experiments on both in-domain and out-of-domain benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness, robustness, and generalization ability of TurnSight.

相关工作Related Work

工具综合推理Tool-Integrated Reasoning

最近的 RLVR 方法，特别是 GRPO 风格的算法Recent RLVR methods, particularly GRPO-style algorithms(Shao et al. 2024; Yu et al. 2025)，已成为通过任务级反馈直接优化模型来提高 TIR 的有效范例, have emerged as an effective paradigm for improving TIR by directly optimizing models with task-level feedback(Wang et al. 2025; Xue et al. 2026; Qu et al. 2025b)。 早期的方法通常依赖于稀疏的结果奖励. Early approaches typically rely on sparse outcome rewards(Li et al. 2025; Feng et al. 2025)或轨迹级监督or trajectory-level supervision(Qian et al. 2025; Zhang et al. 2025; Zeng et al. 2025; Wei et al. 2025)，其中轨迹内的所有推理回合都接收相同的优势信号，从而限制了细粒度的信用分配。 最近的努力通过基于法学硕士的评估引入轮流监督来改善这一点, where all reasoning turns within a trajectory receive identical advantage signals, limiting fine-grained credit assignment. Recent efforts improve this by introducing turn-level supervision through LLM-based evaluation(Wang et al. 2026a)或者通过将预测的工具调用与真实的工具执行进行比较or by comparing predicted tool calls with ground-truth tool executions(Qu et al. 2026)。 然而，这些方法依赖于带注释或参考的工具轨迹，使得优化更接近模仿学习并限制了探索。相比之下，TurnSight 将 RL 与策略自蒸馏相结合，以在保留探索的同时实现细粒度的信用分配，而无需地面实况轨迹。. However, these methods rely on annotated or reference tool trajectories, making optimization closer to imitation learning and limiting exploration. In contrast, TurnSight combines RL with on-policy self-distillation to achieve fine-grained credit assignment without ground-truth trajectories while preserving exploration.

符合政策的自蒸馏On-Policy Self-Distillation

策略自蒸馏使用从同一模型构建的特权教师视图和附加信息来评估学生生成的轨迹，从而对学生访问的状态进行密集监督On-policy self-distillation evaluates student-generated trajectories using a privileged teacher view constructed from the same model with additional information, providing dense supervision on the states visited by the student(Sang et al. 2026; Ma et al. 2026; Li et al. 2026a; Wang et al. 2026b)。 最近的方法不是直接匹配教师分布，而是利用师生分歧来重新衡量强化学习优势、分配细粒度学分或限制蒸馏干预. Rather than directly matching the teacher distribution, recent methods use teacher–student disagreement to reweight RL advantages, allocate fine-grained credit, or bound distillation interventions(Yang et al. 2026; Zhang et al. 2026; Ding et al. 2026; Zhou et al. 2026b, a)。 尽管取得了这些进步，现有方法通常依赖于全局信息丰富的教师信号和令牌级蒸馏，这可能无法在 TIR 的自然轮级粒度上提供状态一致的监督。 相比之下，我们提出了 TurnSight，它直接根据策略工具执行结果构建回合级事后监督，从而实现细粒度和状态一致的信用分配。. Despite these advances, existing methods often rely on globally informative teacher signals and token-level distillation, which may not provide state-aligned supervision at the natural turn-level granularity of TIR. In contrast, we propose TurnSight, which constructs turn-level hindsight supervision directly from on-policy tool execution outcomes, enabling fine-grained and state-aligned credit assignment.

结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们确定了 TIR 中信用分配的两个关键要求：与策略执行状态的一致性以及交互级别的一致性。 为了解决这个问题，我们提出了 TurnSight，这是一个回合级后见之明自蒸馏框架，可以从政策工具执行结果中得出执行条件、多层面后见之明评估。 通过利用这些评估来调整 RL 优势而不改变其优化方向，TurnSight 可以实现细粒度且可靠的信用分配，同时保留 RL 的探索能力。 对域内和域外基准的大量实验证明了 TurnSight 在不同的多转弯 TIR 场景中的有效性、稳健性和泛化能力。In this paper, we identify two key requirements for credit assignment in TIR: alignment with on-policy execution states and coherence at the interaction level. To address this, we propose TurnSight, a turn-level hindsight self-distillation framework that derives execution-conditioned, multi-horizon hindsight assessments from on-policy tool execution outcomes. By leveraging these assessments to modulate RL advantages without altering their optimization direction, TurnSight enables fine-grained and reliable credit assignment while preserving the exploration capability of RL. Extensive experiments on both in-domain and out-of-domain benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness, robustness, and generalization ability of TurnSight across diverse multi-turn TIR scenarios.
