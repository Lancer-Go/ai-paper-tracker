通过从婴儿到类人动物的运动重定向via Motion Retargeting from Babies to Humanoids

摘要Abstract

随着人形机器人能力的增强，从人类到类人机器人的运动重定向变得越来越重要。然而，大多数现有方法只关注再现运动学，而忽略了与人类运动相关的丰富的感觉运动体验。在这项工作中，我们提出了一个使用物理和虚拟人形机器人模拟婴儿多模式感觉运动体验的框架。我们的方法通过提取婴儿的骨骼结构并估计每帧的完整 3D 姿势，从单个视频中重建婴儿的身体配置。然后，我们将重建的运动映射到几个开发平台上：物理 iCub 机器人和虚拟模拟器 pyCub、EMFANT 和 MIMO。在这些实施例上重放重定向的运动产生模拟的多感官流，包括本体感觉（关节和肌肉）、触觉和视觉。对于最佳匹配的实施例，重定向实现了亚厘米精度，并实现了婴儿发育的丰富多模态分析以及增强的行为自动注释。该框架为了解婴儿的感觉运动体验提供了一个独特的窗口，为机器人、发育科学和神经发育障碍的早期检测提供了新工具。该代码可在Motion retargeting from humans to human-like artificial agents is becoming increasingly important as humanoid robots grow more capable. However, most existing approaches focus only on reproducing kinematics and ignore the rich sensorimotor experience associated with human movement. In this work, we present a framework for simulating the multimodal sensorimotor experiences of infants using physical and virtual humanoids. From a single video, our method reconstructs the infant’s body configuration by extracting its skeletal structure and estimating the full 3D pose from each frame. Then we map the reconstructed motion onto several developmental platforms: the physical iCub robot and the virtual simulators pyCub, EMFANT and MIMo. Replaying the retargeted motions on these embodiments produces simulated multisensory streams including proprioception (joints and muscles), touch, and vision. For the best-matching embodiment, the retargeting achieves sub-centimeter accuracy and enables a rich multimodal analysis of infant development as well as enhanced automated annotation of behaviors. This framework provides a unique window into the infant’s sensorimotor experience, offering new tools for robotics, developmental science, and early detection of neurodevelopmental disorders. The code is available athttps://github.com/ctu-vras/motion-retargeting/.

I 简介INTRODUCTION

人类认知发展的基础是在婴儿期奠定的，皮亚杰称之为感觉运动阶段The foundation of human cognitive development is laid down in infancy, in what Piaget called the sensorimotor stage[1]。人类婴儿可能显得无助. Human infants may appear helpless[2]但正是这个早期发展阶段为人类惊人的多功能性和身体智能奠定了基础，这是当今任何机器都无法比拟的。了解建立这些核心能力的过程是发展科学的关键，包括对非典型发育检测的临床影响，以及机器学习和机器人技术, but it is this early developmental stage that provides the groundwork for the amazing human versatility and physical intelligence, unparalleled by any machine of today. Understanding the processes in establishing these core competences is key for developmental science, including clinical implications for the detection of atypical development, but also for machine learning and robotics[3]。然而，我们对早期人类发育的理解是有限的，主要依赖于描述性方法，例如对婴儿自发运动的观察（具有不同的分类，如贝利量表）. However, our understanding of early human development is limited, relying primarily on descriptive approaches, such as observations of infant spontaneous movements (with different classifications like Bayley scales[4]），在诸如到达等任务中的表现), performance in tasks like reaching[5]或偏好的范例or preferential looking paradigms[6]。无法使用与儿童相同的结构化实验范式来测试婴儿. One cannot test infants with the same structured experimental paradigms used for children[7]从婴儿身上获取神经记录充其量也具有挑战性and obtaining neural recordings from infants is, at best, challenging[8].

有人认为，在婴儿期密集采样的长视频记录是有关发育轨迹的最佳信息来源It has been argued that long video recordings densely sampled across infancy are the best source of information about developmental trajectories[9, 10]。心理学的黄金标准是将行为手动评分为预定义的离散表示，例如婴儿会自发触摸哪些身体部位. The gold standard in psychology is manual scoring of the behaviors into predefined discrete representations, e.g. which body areas an infant touches spontaneously[11]。使用计算机视觉和机器学习技术，特别是自动姿态估计，可以获得有关运动轨迹的更丰富的信息。针对成人训练的算法已成功应用于婴儿. Richer information about movement trajectories can be obtained using computer vision and machine learning techniques, in particular automatic pose estimation. Algorithms trained on adults have been successfully applied to infants[12]，允许检测和跟踪 2D 图像坐标中的大约 20 个关键点（主要是关节和少量面部标志）。与 3D 形状一起估计姿势更具挑战性，但也是可能的, allowing the detection and tracking of around 20 keypoints (mainly joints and few facial landmarks) in 2D image coordinates. Estimating the pose together with 3D shape is more challenging but possible[13]。虽然自动姿势估计为分析大规模数据集开辟了新的可能性，但这仅提供了婴儿行为的外部视角。. While automatic pose estimation opens new possibilities for the analysis of large-scale datasets, this only provides an external perspective on the infant behaviors.

另一方面，最近人们对以自我为中心的视频训练模型的兴趣激增On the other hand, there has been a recent surge in interest in training models from egocentric videos[14, 15]。这些作品提供了关于认知发展的独特见解。以自我为中心的视频使我们能够从第一人称视角获得婴儿的视觉（也可能是听觉）体验。然而，此类设置需要头戴式摄像头. These works provide unique insights about cognitive development. Egocentric videos allow us to access the visual (and possibly auditory) experience of an infant from a first-person perspective. However, such setups require head-mounted cameras[16]，这对于密集采样发育轨迹来说太过侵入性。, which are too invasive for densely sampling developmental trajectories.

为了弥补现有的第三人称视频和第一人称观察的优势之间的差距，我们提出了一种新工具，通过将运动重定向到人形机器人来模拟婴儿的感觉运动体验。我们的方法通过提取婴儿的骨骼结构并估计每帧的完整 3D 姿势，从单个视频中重建婴儿的 3D 身体配置。然后将重建的运动映射到多个人形物体上。在人形实施例上重放运动会产生逼真的多感官流，包括本体感觉（关节和肌肉）、触觉、视觉和前庭信息。对发育机器人学和心理学特别感兴趣的是，我们将婴儿动作重新定位到三个婴儿类人身上：iCubTo bridge the gap between readily available third-person videos and the advantages of first-person observations, we present a new tool that emulates an infant’s sensorimotor experience via motion retargeting onto humanoid robots. From a single video, our method reconstructs the infant’s 3D body configuration by extracting its skeletal structure and estimating the full 3D pose of each frame. The reconstructed motion is then mapped onto multiple humanoids. Replaying the motions on humanoid embodiments produces realistic multisensory streams including proprioception (joints and muscles), touch, vision, and vestibular information. Of particular interest for developmental robotics and psychology, we perform the retargeting of infant motions onto three baby humanoids: iCub[17]（真实和虚拟），以及两个婴儿模拟器、MIMO(real and virtual), and two infant simulators, MIMo[18]和EMFANTand EMFANT[19]，如图所示, shown in Fig.3。然而，该方法超越了婴儿数据，并为丰富了触摸等关键维度的机器人基础模型提供了一条途径。我们的方法实现了亚厘米精度，并能够对婴儿发育进行独特的丰富多模态分析以及增强的行为自动注释。. Our method achieves sub-centimeter accuracy and enables a unique rich multimodal analysis of infant development as well as enhanced automated annotation of behaviors.

iCub 人形机器人The iCub humanoid robot[17]具有模仿 4 岁儿童的拟人化比例。这组感觉模式包括双眼视觉、听觉、前庭（惯性）感知、本体感觉，以及重要的触觉。 iCub 的身体大面积覆盖着压力敏感皮肤，总共有 4000 个感受器has anthropomorphic proportions modeled after a 4-year old child. The set of sensory modalities include binocular vision, hearing, vestibular (inertial) sensing, proprioception, and, importantly, touch. The iCub has large areas of its body covered with pressure-sensitive skin, with 4,000 receptors in total[23]。 iCub 是一个. The iCub is a事实上的de facto发育机器人学的标准平台，并已被用作具体计算模型来研究发育的各个方面standard platform in developmental robotics and has been employed as an embodied computational model to study various aspects of development[24, 25, 26].

重要的是，iCub 增加了将运动重新定位到物理儿童人形机器人上的可能性。这作为任何学习算法的最终测试平台都很有价值。然而，由于真实硬件的局限性主要来自限制可能配置空间的约束，因此我们还使用了机器人的模拟版本。我们利用 pyCubImportantly, the iCub adds the possibility of retargeting motions onto a physical child humanoid robot. This is valuable as an ultimate testbed of any learning algorithm. However, as the real hardware has limitations arising mainly from constraints that limit the space of possible configurations, we also used a simulated version of the robot. We utilize pyCub[27]，一个基于 python 的 iCub 模拟器。该平台允许从眼睛中提取有关场景、本体感觉、人工皮肤激活或第一人称视觉信息的视觉信息。与真实机器人相比，我们能够测试超出关节限制的配置、导致机器人链接穿透的配置或可能破坏真实机器人的配置。, a python-based simulator of iCub. The platform allows to extract visual information about the scene, proprioception, artificial skin activations or first-person visual information from the eyes. Compared to the real robot, we are able to test configurations that go outside of the joint limits, configurations that result in penetration of links of the robot or configurations that would possibly break the real robot.

对于 iCub 人形机器人，我们应用几何运动重定向，这是一种根据人体姿势估计获得的 3D 关键点确定相应关节角度的分析方法。该方法改编自For the iCub humanoid robot, we apply geometric motion retargeting, an analytical method that determines corresponding joint angles from 3D keypoints obtained from human pose estimation. The method was adapted from[28]通过额外的优化步骤来最大限度地减少姿势差异和额外的机器人约束（例如关节限制）。with an additional optimization step to minimize differences in posture and additional robot constraints like joint limits.

Ⅱ-CII-C 电磁辐射EMFANT

胎儿和 iNfanT 的具体模型 (EMFANT) 是在 MuJoCo 中实现的肌肉骨骼模拟平台，重点关注生物学合理性。其形态是通过分割从婴儿 CT/MRI 数据中得出的，产生解剖学上真实的皮肤、骨骼和肌肉路线。该模型结合了关节耦合（例如颈椎相互作用和肩肱节律）和适合婴儿的运动范围限制，提供了现实的解剖学约束。 EMFANT 包含 17 个刚体段和 37 个自由度。The Embodied Model of Fetus And iNfanT (EMFANT) is a musculoskeletal simulation platform implemented in MuJoCo with a strong focus on biological plausibility. Its morphology is derived from infant CT/MRI data by segmentation, yielding anatomically realistic skin, bones, and muscle routing. The model incorporates joint coupling (e.g., cervical interactions and scapulohumeral rhythm) and infant‑appropriate range‑of‑motion limits, which provide realistic anatomical constraints. EMFANT comprises 17 rigid body segments and 37 DOF.

传感器特性是参数化的并且可以变化。在本文使用的配置中，EMFANT提供：以90°视场和每只眼睛1000×1000像素渲染的双眼视觉； 37 DoF 的本体感觉，用于关节角度和来自 162 个肌肉肌腱长度的基于肌肉的信号；分布在整个身体的皮肤网格上的 5,000 个传感器进行触摸。所有感官流的频率均为 1 kHz，与模拟速率相匹配。Sensor characteristics are parameterized and can be varied. In the configuration used in this paper, EMFANT provides: binocular vision rendered with a 90° field of view and 1000×1000 pixels per eye; proprioception with 37 DoF for the joint angles and muscle‑based signals from 162 muscle–tendon lengths; and touch from 5,000 sensors on the skin mesh distributed across the whole body. All sensory streams are available at 1 kHz, matching the simulation rate.

对于重定向，我们使用关节空间 PD 控制：在每个时间步，控制器跟踪目标关节角度，MuJoCo 集成前向动力学并解析接触。仿真运行频率为 1 kHz，我们使用 MuJoCo 的多边形接触模型；接触力被分配到附近的皮肤传感器以进行触觉输入，如下For retargeting, we use joint-space PD control: at each time step the controller tracks the target joint angles, and MuJoCo integrates the forward dynamics and resolves contacts. The simulation runs at 1 kHz, and we use MuJoCo’s polygonal contact model; contact forces are distributed to nearby skin sensors for tactile inputs, following[29, 19]。为了重放婴儿运动，我们将 EMFANT 缩放到记录的婴儿的大小，并在 OpenSim 中求解具有生物力学约束的逆运动学. To replay infant motions, we scale EMFANT to the size of the recorded infant and solve inverse kinematics with biomechanical constraints in OpenSim[30]。然后，由关节空间 PD 控制的 EMFANT 模型在正向动力学中跟踪由此产生的关节角度，产生物理上一致的运动以及同步的视觉、触觉和本体感觉信号。. The resulting joint angles are then tracked by the joint-space PD‑controlled EMFANT model in forward dynamics, yielding physically consistent motion together with synchronized visual, tactile, and proprioceptive signals.

Ⅱ-DII-D 多输入多输出MIMo

多输入多输出MIMo[18, 31]是一个基于 MuJoCo 平台构建的多模式婴儿模型，其速度比实时模拟更快。因此，MIMO 具有由基本几何原始形状组成的简单形态，例如头部为球体，手臂和腿为胶囊（末端带有圆形“帽”的圆柱体）。在这项工作中，我们使用了 MIMO 的简化实施例，具有像手套一样的手和脚，具有 46 个自由度。 MIMO 的感觉流包括四种不同的感觉模式：本体感觉（关节角度、速度、扭矩和每个关节的驱动值）、前庭感觉（位于头部内部的加速度计和陀螺仪）、触觉（触摸传感器的虚拟皮肤）和视觉。is a multimodal infant model built on the MuJoCo platform for faster than real-time simulations. As such, MIMo features a simple morphology composed of basic geometric primitive shapes, such as a sphere for his head and capsules (cylinders with round “caps” at the ends) for his arms and legs. In this work, we use MIMo’s simplified embodiment with mitten-like hands and feet, which has 46 DOF. MIMo’s sensory stream includes four different sensory modalities: proprioception (joint angles, velocities, torques, and actuation values for each joint), vestibular (accelerometer and gyroscope located inside his head), touch (virtual skin of touch sensors), and vision.

由于其简单的形态和不断增长的尺寸，MIMO 允许高保真运动重定向。首先，MIMO 身体中的每个几何基元都可以缩放以匹配婴儿相应的身体部位。我们利用这一功能，通过校准 MIMO 的身体，根据所有身体姿势的平均值忠实地采用真实婴儿的结构。此外，尺寸不是由提取的关键点确定的，例如根据 Anthrokids 的测量，手臂和腿的宽度符合同龄婴儿的默认值Due to his simple morphology and growing size, MIMo permits high-fidelity kinematic motion retargeting. First, each geometric primitive in MIMo’s body can be scaled to match an infant’s corresponding body part. We make use of this feature by calibrating MIMo’s body to faithfully adopt the real infant’s structure from the average of all body poses. Additionally, sizes that are not determined by the extracted keypoints, e.g. the width of the arms and legs, are fit to the default values for an infant of the same age, according to the Anthrokids measurements[32]。同样，模拟的视觉输入适应相应年龄的婴儿视觉器官的成熟. Likewise, the simulated visual inputs are adapted to the maturation of the infant’s visual apparatus at the corresponding age[31].

使用 MuJoCo 的逆运动学解算器和辅助动作捕捉 (mocap) 体，在每个时间步调整 MIMO 的姿势。这些动作捕捉体对所有关节施加力，以最小化每个关键点与相应目标位置之间的距离。在仿真过程中，约束求解器计算最能满足这些位置约束的关节配置。通过这种“傀儡操作”，MIMO 可以重现婴儿的原始身体姿势。一旦优化达到平衡，MIMO 的位置​​就会被存储，并且优化会针对接下来的时间步进行更新。 MIMO 的多模式感觉流是并行记录的。MIMo’s pose is adjusted at every timestep using MuJoCo’s inverse kinematics solver with auxiliary motion capture (mocap) bodies. These mocap bodies exert forces over all the joints so as to minimize the distance between each keypoint and the corresponding target position. During simulation, the constraint solver computes the joint configurations that best satisfy these position constraints. As a result of this “puppeteering”, MIMo can recreate the original body pose of the infant. Once the optimization reaches an equilibrium, MIMo’s position is stored and the optimization is updated for the following timestep. MIMo’s multimodal sensory streams are recorded in parallel.

V 婴儿感觉运动体验的模拟SIMULATION OF SENSORIMOTOR INFANT EXPERIENCES

一旦婴儿的身体在人形平台上成功重建，就可以重放这些动作，通过人形的不同模式来模拟婴儿的感觉运动体验。这种第一人称模拟器提供了分析和理解婴儿发育的独特视角。在这里，我们讨论了一些有趣的用例，在不同的人形平台中模拟了四种不同的感官模式。Once the infant body is successfully reconstructed in a humanoid platform, the motions can be replayed to simulate, through the humanoid’s different modalities, the sensorimotor experience of the infant. 我们开发了一个框架，可以自动将婴儿的运动重新定位到婴儿模拟器和人形机器人上，使我们不仅可以从婴儿的眼睛看世界，还可以模拟婴儿的体感体验，即她感受到的触摸和有关她身体配置的本体感受信息。根据婴儿的单个视频，我们估计她的 3D 身体配置，并将其映射到四个目标平台之一：iCub（真实和模拟）、EMFANT 和 MIMO。然后，婴儿的动作会在类人机器人上重播，产生多模式感觉运动体验：视觉、触觉和本体感觉（关节角度和速度）。 iCubWe developed a framework to automatically retarget motions of infants onto infant simulators and humanoid robots, allowing us to not only look at the world from the baby’s eyes, but also emulate its somatosensory experience, i.e. the touch she feels and proprioceptive information about her body configuration. From a single video of an infant, we estimate her 3D body configuration and map it onto one of four target platforms: iCub (real and sim), EMFANT, and MIMo. The infant movements are then replayed on the humanoids, generating multimodal sensorimotor experiences: vision, touch, and proprioception (joint angles and velocities). The iCub[17]是一个身体结构模仿人类的物理机器人。它有 4 岁儿童的大小，作为一个实体机器人，它不会“成长”，并且会带来特定的运动学限制。然而，它可以作为物理世界的终极测试平台。多输入多输出is a physical robot with a body structure modeled after a human. It has a size of a 4-year old and as a physical robot, it is not “growing” and brings specific kinematic constraints. However, it can serve as an ultimate testbed in the physical world. MIMo[18]是一个“成长平台”is a “growing platform”[31]，可以在单独的录制会话中缩放到婴儿的尺寸。通过额外的优化，我们实现了极其准确的重定向（平均误差低于 0.5 厘米）。 EMFANT 是一个具有高度真实的生物力学约束的肌肉骨骼平台，它还可以在肌肉长度方面模拟本体感觉，并增加肌肉控制的可能性。除了重新定位的空间准确性之外，我们还展示了我们的工具如何提供对发育中婴儿的感觉运动空间的独特访问。通过跨实施例潜在对齐，我们演示了重定向到三个目标平台后的感觉运动空间如何共享共同的感觉运动流形。这些分析扩展了以下发现：, which can be scaled to the infant size in individual recording sessions. With additional optimization, we achieved extremely accurate retargeting (average error under 0.5 cm). EMFANT is a musculoskeletal platform with highly realistic biomechanical constraints, which can additionally emulate proprioception in terms of muscle length and adds the possibility of muscle control. Beyond the spatial accuracy of the retargeting, we showed how our tool provides unique access to the sensorimotor spaces of developing infants. Through cross-embodiment latent alignment, we demonstrated how the sensorimotor space after retargeting to the three target platforms shares a common sensorimotor manifold. These analyses extend the findings in[34]基于本体感觉，提供真正的多模态感觉流，包括视觉、触觉和前庭信号。我们还通过比较重定向后自动检测到的触摸分布与手动编码的触摸来验证触摸模拟。这说明了这项工作在行为科学中的一个重要应用：通过利用模拟的第一人称体验来自动执行繁琐的行为注释。这个想法的应用包括对身体触摸的半自动或全自动注释based on proprioception by providing a truly multimodal sensory stream encompassing vision, touch, and vestibular signals. We also validated the touch emulation by comparing the distributions of touches automatically detected after retargeting with manually coded touches. This illustrates one important application of this work for behavioral science: automating the laborious annotation of behaviors by exploiting the simulated first-person experience. Applications of this idea include the semi- or fully automated annotation of touches to the body[11, 35]或伸手致意or hand regard[33].

未来的工作有几个方向。首先，我们计划将该工具部署到更大的仰卧位婴儿视频记录数据集，以更好地了解感觉运动发育的机制以及早期检测非典型发育的可能性。其次，我们将测试这些工具如何推广到具有更复杂的全身运动和与物体交互的儿童和成人。除了在感觉运动发展和具身认知方面的应用之外，该工具可能与人形机器人相关，提供现实的多模式重定向并缩小人类和人形机器人之间的具体差距。There are several directions of future work. First, we plan to deploy this tool to much larger datasets of infant video recordings in supine position to better understand the mechanisms underlying sensorimotor development and the possibility of early detection of atypical development. Second, we will test how the tools generalize to children and adults with more complex whole-body movements and interaction with objects. Next to applications in sensorimotor development and embodied cognition, this tool may be relevant in humanoid robotics, providing realistic multimodal retargeting and narrowing the embodiment gap between humans and humanoids.
