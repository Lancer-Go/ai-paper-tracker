1 简介Introduction

因果大语言模型 (LLM) 不仅作为生成器占据主导地位，而且是庞大的专业变体生态系统的基础：代码Causal large language models (LLMs) are not only dominant as generators but serve as the foundation of a vast ecosystem of specialized variants: code(Lozhkov et al., 2024), 数学, mathematics(Shao et al., 2024)、安全, safety(Zhao et al., 2025a), 愿景, vision(Bai et al., 2025)，和音频, and audio(Shi et al., 2026)，共同代表了数百万 GPU 小时的开源知识。然而，表示任务仍然绑定到双向编码器, collectively representing millions of GPU hours of open-source knowledge. Yet, representation tasks remain bound to bidirectional encoders(Devlin et al., 2019; He et al., 2023; Boizard et al., 2025a)，使这些知识未被开发。因此，将因果模型重新利用到编码器中是一个令人信服的目标，最近的工作已经开始探索这个方向, leaving this knowledge untapped. Repurposing causal models into encoders is therefore a compelling goal, and recent work has begun to explore this direction(Ma et al., 2023; BehnamGhader et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024a; Babakhin et al., 2025; Gisserot-Boukhlef et al., 2026)。然而，这种适应格局仍然围绕三个核心问题支离破碎。 我们通过完全开源的框架解决每个问题，并在 Gemma3 和 Qwen3 上严格相同的条件下进行验证。. However, this adaptation landscape remains fragmented around three core questions. We address each through a fully open-source framework, validated under strictly identical conditions on Gemma3 and Qwen3.

Through controlled ablations (§​ 2, §​ 3），我们理清了这些因素，并表明通过掩蔽目标（经常被省略的步骤）实现双向注意力对于释放特定任务基准的性能至关重要，而对比目标主要驱动通用嵌入质量。), we disentangle these factors and show that enabling bidirectional attention via a masking objective (a step often omitted) is critical to unlock performance on task-specific benchmarks, while contrastive objectives primarily drive generic embedding quality.

  3. 适应的编码器可以与因果生态系统结合起来吗？Can adapted encoders compose with the causal ecosystem?当前的自适应编码器依赖于刚性管道，这些管道无法与源自同一主干的其他专门因果模型组合。由于忽略了从因果架构开始的庞大生态系统，这些方法导致数千小时的开源专业化 GPU 时间未被使用。我们通过突破权重合并的界限来解决这个问题（Current adapted encoders rely on rigid pipelines that fail to compose with other specialized causal models derived from the same backbone. By ignoring the vast ecosystem that motivates starting from causal architectures, these methods leave thousands of GPU hours of open-source specialization unused. We address this by pushing the boundaries of weight merging (§​ 6）。我们无缝集成来自专门因果变体的知识，将我们的编码器扩展到新的领域和模式（安全、音频、视觉），而不需要完整的管道重新训练。). We seamlessly integrate knowledge from specialized causal variants, extending our encoders to new domains and modalities (safety, audio, vision) without requiring full pipeline re-training.

8 结论Conclusion

在这项工作中，我们引入了一个统一的、完全开源的框架，用于将因果解码器 LLM 转换为跨越文本到多个模态域的双向编码器。通过系统比较，我们表明最近仅对比方法省略的掩蔽阶段实际上对于微调性能至关重要。为了在没有专有预训练数据的情况下扩展这种适应，我们采用线性权重合并和轻量级多域数据混合的双重策略，产生 BidirLM 模型系列。我们的框架不是构建不灵活的系统，而是将专门的生成模型与我们适应的编码器无缝地组合在一起，从而实现高效的跨模式和特定领域的适应，而无需重新训练整个管道，最终形成 BidirLM-Omni。In this work, we introduce a unified, fully open-source framework for transforming causal decoder LLMs into bidirectional encoders spanning text to multiple modality domains. Through systematic comparisons, we show that the masking phase omitted by recent contrastive-only methods is in fact critical for fine-tunig performance. To scale this adaptation without proprietary pre-training data, we employ a dual strategy of linear weight merging and a lightweight multi-domain data mixture, yielding the BidirLM model family. Rather than building inflexible systems, our framework seamlessly composes specialized generative models with our adapted encoders, enabling efficient cross-modal and domain-specific adaptation without retraining entire pipelines, culminating in BidirLM-Omni.

F.2 有关模型相似性的详细信息Details on Model Similarities

正如中所讨论的As discussed in§​ 4，我们的权重合并策略的成功依赖于对适应模型和因果模型在权重空间中保持接近的观察。在这里，我们将这一观察结果建立在先前的理论文献中，并将我们的分析扩展到本研究中探索的各种合并配置的逐层水平，提供了更细粒度的证据，表明重量位移保持有限且一致。, the success of our weight-merging strategy relies on the observation that the adapted and causal models remain close in weight space. Here, we ground this observation in prior theoretical literature and extend our analysis to a layer-by-layer level across the various merging configurations explored in this study, providing finer-grained evidence that weight displacement remains bounded and consistent.

合并的经验背景。Empirical context for merging.

先前的工作表明，从共享预训练检查点微调的模型通常保留在损失景观的同一盆地中，这种属性称为线性模式连接Prior work has shown that models fine-tuned from a shared pretrained checkpoint often remain in the same basin of the loss landscape, a property known as linear mode connectivity(Frankle et al., 2020)，使它们的凸组合表现良好, enabling their convex combinations to perform well(Wortsman et al., 2022a)。补充观察. A complementary observation byOrtiz-Jimenez et al. (2023)表明预训练会导致权重解缠，从而在权重空间中沿着近似正交的方向对不同的功能进行编码，从而减少模型组合时的干扰。虽然这些结果是为共享相同目标和注意力机制的模型建立的，但我们注意到我们的设置共享一个关键的有利条件：所有合并模型都源自相同的预训练主干。此外，我们的适应处理相对于原始预训练规模的一小部分标记，同时保持下一个标记预测目标，导致权重位移非常有限（Gemma 的平均余弦相似度为 0.78，Qwen 的平均余弦相似度为 0.97）。suggests that pretraining induces weight disentanglement, whereby distinct capabilities are encoded along approximately orthogonal directions in weight space, reducing interference when models are combined. While these results were established for models sharing the same objective and attention mechanism, we note that our setting shares a key favorable condition: all merged models derive from the identical pretrained backbone. Furthermore, our adaptation processes a small fraction of tokens relative to the original pre-training scale while maintaining next-token prediction objectives, resulting in remarkably limited weight displacement (mean cosine similarity of 0.78 for Gemma and 0.97 for Qwen).
