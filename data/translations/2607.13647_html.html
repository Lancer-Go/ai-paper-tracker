超越颜色几何：评估视觉模型中的类人颜色表示Beyond Color Geometry: Evaluating Human-Like Color Representations in Vision Models

Ayan Igali, Pakizar Shamoi
摘要Abstract

视觉模型能像人类一样看到颜色吗？现有的颜色表示评估通常将其与 CIELAB 等几何空间或离散颜色标签进行比较。这些参考捕获了感知距离或类别成员资格，但没有捕获人们组织颜色的分级方式。我们根据模糊感知模型评估色彩底色，该模型具有适合人类调查数据的 86 个分级类别。该框架可应用于任何图像编码器，并测量三个互补的属性：类别边界、类别紧凑性和超出颜色几何本身所能解释的分级对齐。在 11 个 Vision Transformer 编码器中，类别级结果大致相似，而分级对齐却有很大不同。掩码自动编码器实现了最强的超越几何对齐，其置信区间与其他编码器的置信区间不重叠。逐层分析进一步表明，掩模重建保留了输出的这种结构。在自然图像上，MAE 全局表示表面颜色，而语言监督模型则对与前景对象相关的颜色进行更强的编码。这些结果表明，类人色底有几个不同的方面，不应简化为单一分数。Do vision models see colors the way humans do? Existing evaluations of color representations usually compare them with geometric spaces such as CIELAB or with discrete color labels. These references capture perceptual distance or category membership, but not the graded way in which people organize colors. We evaluate color grounding against a fuzzy perceptual model with 86 graded categories fitted to human survey data. The framework can be applied to any image encoder and measures three complementary properties: category boundaries, category compactness, and graded alignment beyond what color geometry alone can explain. Across eleven Vision Transformer encoders, the category-level results are broadly similar, whereas graded alignment differs substantially. Masked Autoencoders achieve the strongest beyond-geometry alignment, with confidence intervals that do not overlap those of the other encoders. A layer-wise analysis further shows that masked reconstruction preserves this structure toward the output. On natural images, MAE represents surface color globally, while language-supervised models encode color more strongly in relation to the foreground object. These results show that human-like color grounding has several distinct aspects that should not be reduced to a single score.

I 简介Introduction

现代视觉和视觉语言模型被广泛用作计算机视觉中的特征提取器Modern vision and vision–language models are widely used as feature extractors across computer vision[9, 29]。对于以人为中心且可解释的人工智能，询问其内部颜色表示是否类似于人类颜色感知或主要反映像素级规律性是有用的。人类的颜色感知并不是作为统一的度量连续体组织的。人们将颜色分为具有软边界的重叠类别，并且一种颜色可以部分属于多个命名类别，例如红色、深红色、灰色或黑色. For human-centered and explainable AI, it is useful to ask whether their internal color representations resemble human color perception or mainly reflect pixel-level regularities. Human color perception is not organized as a uniform metric continuum. People group colors into overlapping categories with soft boundaries, and a color can belong partly to several named categories such as red, dark red, gray, or black[22].

Ⅲ-BIII-B COLIBRI：模糊感知色彩空间COLIBRI: A fuzzy perceptual color space

蜂鸟COLIBRI[20]是一种基于人类颜色命名数据的模糊颜色模型。它不是将每种颜色分配给单个标签，而是表示重叠语言类别中的分级成员资格。 RGB 颜色首先转换为色调-饱和度-强度 (HSI)，然后使用从 2,496 名参与者的调查中得出的三角形或梯形模糊集计算隶属度。is a fuzzy color model grounded in human color-naming data. Rather than assigning each color to a single label, it represents graded membership in overlapping linguistic categories. An RGB color is first converted to Hue–Saturation–Intensity (HSI), after which membership is computed using triangular or trapezoidal fuzzy sets derived from a survey of 2,496 participants.

V 讨论Discussion

为了更好地理解我们的发现的意义，我们将它们与最近关于神经网络中的类人颜色和视觉表示的研究进行了比较。To better understand the significance of our findings, we compare them with recent studies on human-like color and visual representations in neural networks.

在十一个编码器中，类别边界和簇紧凑度看起来几乎相同，但控制颜色几何后测量的分级对齐将它们分开，掩模重建远远高于其他编码器。由于这些模型在训练数据、规模、补丁大小和配方方面也有所不同，因此我们将其视为与训练制度的关联，而不是目标本身的因果效应。早期的工作也表明了同样的观点，发现训练数据和目标比架构更能塑造人类模型的一致性Across the eleven encoders, category boundaries and cluster compactness look much the same, yet the graded alignment measured after controlling for color geometry pulls them apart, with masked reconstruction standing well above the rest. Since these models also differ in training data, scale, patch size, and recipe, we read this as an association with the training regime rather than a causal effect of the objective on its own. Earlier work points the same way, finding that training data and objectives shape human–model alignment more than architecture does[16, 7].

一个合理的原因是重建必须保留低层细节。颜色正是这样一种属性，因此重建像素的目标可能比围绕语义类或图像文本匹配组织的目标更忠实地保留它。这符合 MAE 保持低级视觉结构，同时与针对高级神经表示调整的模型不同的证据A plausible reason is that reconstruction has to preserve low-level detail. Color is exactly such a property, so an objective that rebuilds pixels may retain it more faithfully than one organized around semantic classes or image–text matching. This fits evidence that MAEs keep low-level visual structure while diverging from models tuned for higher-level neural representations[11]。考虑到有多少训练选择同时发生变化，这仍然是一种解释，而不是因果关系。. It stays an interpretation rather than a causal claim, given how many training choices vary at once.

VI 结论Conclusion

我们引入了一个使用人类颜色类别的分级模糊模型来评估颜色底色的框架。结果表明了为什么色彩底色不应该用单一分数来概括。在 11 个 ViT 编码器中，类别边界和集群紧凑性大致相似。在控制颜色几何形状后，这些模型与分级人体结构的一致性更加明显。在此衡量标准上，两个 MAE 模型获得了最高分数，并且通过不重叠的置信区间与其他编码器分开。 这些发现很重要，因为它们表明模型可以类似地组织颜色类别，但在反映人类颜色感知的程度方面仍然存在差异We introduced a framework for evaluating color grounding with a graded fuzzy model of human color categories. The results show why color grounding should not be summarized by a single score. Across eleven ViT encoders, category boundaries and cluster compactness are broadly similar. The models differ more clearly in their agreement with graded human structure after color geometry is controlled. On this measure, the two MAE models achieve the highest scores and are separated from the other encoders by non-overlapping confidence intervals. These findings are important because they show that models can organize color categories similarly while still differing in how well they reflect human color perception

该研究有几个局限性。 330 个 WCS 芯片仅覆盖 86 个 COLIBRI 类别中的 50 个作为主导标签，并且刺激在统计上不是独立的。评估的模型在数据、规模、补丁大小、训练配方和目标方面有所不同，因此需要进行受控比较来隔离 MAE 结果的来源。自然图像实验使用整个图像嵌入，不包括对象掩模。最后，所有评估的模型都属于 ViT 系列。未来的工作应该在受控条件下改变训练目标，包括其他建筑系列，并评估对象分割的自然场景。The study has several limitations. The 330 WCS chips cover only 50 of the 86 COLIBRI categories as dominant labels, and the stimuli are not statistically independent. The evaluated models differ in data, scale, patch size, training recipe, and objective, so a controlled comparison is needed to isolate the source of the MAE result. The natural-image experiment uses whole-image embeddings and does not include object masks. Finally, all evaluated models belong to the ViT family. Future work should vary the training objective under controlled conditions, include other architectural families, and evaluate object-segmented natural scenes.
