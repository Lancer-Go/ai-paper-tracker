讨论与展望Discussion and outlook

这些结果对容错量子计算有几个影响。 首先，代码设计和资源估算不应将距离作为唯一的品质因数。 低权重不可纠正错误的稀疏性、逻辑运算符的权重分布和解码器精度都构成了有效的错误抑制，并且在具有相同的代码之间可能存在显着差异。These results have several implications for fault-tolerant quantum computation. First, code design and resource estimation should move beyond distance as the sole figure of merit. The sparsity of low-weight uncorrectable errors, the weight distribution of logical operators, and decoder accuracy all shape effective error suppression and can differ dramatically between codes with identical⟦n,k,d⟧\llbracket n,k,d\rrbracket参数。 捕获这些结构属性的特定于代码的模型将产生比基于标准距离的公式更有利的资源估计。parameters. Code-specific models that capture these structural properties will yield more favorable resource estimates than the standard distance-based formula.

其次，解码器应被视为容错架构协同设计的一个组成部分，而不是一个独立的子系统。 容量扩展结果（Second, the decoder should be treated as an integral component of fault-tolerant architecture co-design rather than an independent subsystem. The capacity scaling results (Fig. 4）揭示了表达能力的一个尖锐阈值：低于该阈值，解码器无法表示在中等噪声下占主导地位的复杂错误模式；在它上面，出现了接近最佳的性能。 这解释了为什么现有的解码器错过了瀑布，并强调解码器的能力直接决定了代码的纠错能力在实践中实现了多少。) reveal a sharp threshold in expressive capacity: below it, decoders cannot represent the complex error patterns that dominate at moderate noise; above it, near-optimal performance emerges. This explains why existing decoders miss the waterfall and underscores that decoder power directly determines how much of a code’s error-correcting capability is realized in practice.

第三，我们的解码器的几何归纳偏差（局部性、平移等方差和各向异性）使其成为一个通用框架，而不是特定于代码的解决方案。 相同的架构在表面代码和双变量自行车代码上实现了近乎最佳的准确性，这些代码族具有根本不同的连接性和编码率，而这些代码族在历史上需要完全不同的解码策略Third, our decoder’s geometric inductive bias (locality, translation equivariance, and anisotropy) makes it a general framework, not a code-specific solution. The same architecture achieves near-optimal accuracy on both surface codes and bivariate bicycle codes, code families with fundamentally different connectivity and encoding rates that have historically required entirely different decoding strategies[49, 50, 51, 5, 52, 53]。 由于这些归纳偏差适用于其检查共享公共局部结构的任何代码，因此我们期望 Cascade 能够扩展到其他高速 qLDPC 代码系列，包括提升乘积代码.
