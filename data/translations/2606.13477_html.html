我们为法学硕士引入了第一个主客体超分子化学基准，称为We introduce the first host–guest supramolecular chemistry benchmark for LLMs, called超长凳SupraBench，其中包含四个基本任务，, that contains four fundamental tasks,即i.e.、结合亲和力预测、顶部结合剂选择、溶剂识别和主客体描述，以及基于视觉的辅助分子识别任务。 此外，我们还发布了16M token的文本语料库，, binding affinity prediction, top-binder selection, solvent identification, and host–guest description, along with an auxiliary vision-based task for molecular identification. Frontier proprietary LLMs may have seen part of this distribution during pretraining, so the absolute performance numbers may overestimate true generalization to genuinely novel host–guest pairs. We do not run a controlled novel host or temporal-cut split to quantify this effect.

The dominant host families in current practice include cyclodextrins, cucurbiturils, calixarenes, pillararenes, and crown ethers, as well as extended porous architectures such as metal-organic and covalent-organic frameworks (MOFs and COFs).
