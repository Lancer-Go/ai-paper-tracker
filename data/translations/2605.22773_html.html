灵活作业车间调度问题 (FJSP) 是一组作业到机器的最优分配。 FJSP 仍然存在两个主要挑战：未来工作的不可预测性和问题的组合复杂性，这使得传统的混合整数线性规划求解器难以处理。 本文提出了一种基于事件的The Flexible Job Shop Scheduling Problem (FJSP) is the optimal allocation of a set of jobs to machines. Two primary challenges persist in FJSP: the unpredictable arrival of future jobs and the combinatorial complexity of the problem, rendering it intractable for conventional mixed-integer linear programming solvers. This paper proposes an event-based深度强化学习（DRL）Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL)解决具有随机作业到达的 FJSP 的方法。具体来说，我们采用近端策略优化算法并使用轻量级多层感知器来训练approach to solve FJSP with random job arrivals. Specifically, we employ the Proximal Policy Optimization algorithm and use lightweight Multi-Layer Perceptrons to train the日间强化学习DRL用于最小化所有作业的总完成时间的代理。我们将状态表示设计为可直接从环境访问，并限制学习代理从一组完善的调度规则中进行选择。模拟表明我们的agent for minimizing the total completion time of all jobs. We design the state representation to be directly accessible from the environment, and limit the learning agent to selecting from among a set of well-established dispatching rules. Simulations show that our日间强化学习DRL该方法优于具有不同异质性和工作到达率的数据集上的任何单独调度规则。我们对标我们的approach outperforms any of the individual dispatching rules on datasets with varying heterogeneity and job arrival rates.
