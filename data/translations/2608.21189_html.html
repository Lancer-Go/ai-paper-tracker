摘要Abstract

目标： 人工耳蜗 (CI) 是一种仿生假体，通过对听神经进行电刺激来恢复听力。混合 CI 使用电声刺激 (EAS)，将残余低频听觉听觉与 CI 电刺激相结合。由于植入物的存在而形成的耳蜗内纤维化可能会阻碍残余听力功能并逐渐降低 EAS 的功效。因此，研究啮齿动物耳蜗纤维化的形成是一个转化目标，目的是减少纤维化负担并改善 CI 患者的预后。Objective: Cochlear implants (CIs) are bionic prostheses that restores hearing via electrical stimulation of the auditory nerve. Hybrid CIs, which use electroacoustic stimulation (EAS), combine residual low-frequency acoustic hearing with CI electrical stimulation. Intracochlear fibrosis, which forms in response to the presence of the implant, may impede residual hearing function and gradually reduce the efficacy of EAS. It is therefore a translational objective to study the formation of cochlear fibrosis in rodents, with the goal of reducing fibrotic burden and improving outcomes for CI patients.

方法： 我们生成并注释了来自长期植入豚鼠的光学相干断层扫描（OCT）图像的新数据集，作为正在进行的专注于植入诱导纤维化研究的一部分。客观地评估该模型中的纤维化负担，具有高分辨率和可重复性，为计算机视觉方法提供了一个明显的用例。Methods: We generate and annotate a novel dataset of optical coherence tomography (OCT) images from chronically implanted guinea pigs as part of an ongoing study focused on implant induced fibrosis. Objectively assessing fibrotic burden in this model, with high resolution and repeatability, presents an obvious use case for computer vision methods.

结果： 我们展示了几种最先进的语义分割模型的结果，并使用新的手动分割 OCT 图像库比较了它们识别耳蜗纤维化和其他相关注释的功效。Results: We present the results of several state-of-the-art semantic segmentation models and compare their efficacy for identifying cochlear fibrosis and other relevant annotations, using a new library of manually segmented OCT images.

结论： 我们发现，通过使用著名的 UNET 架构（我们称之为 2D-OCT-UNET）的修改版本来实现最佳性能，该架构在升级的 OCT 输入分辨率上运行。Conclusions: We find that the best performance is achieved by using a modified version of the well-known UNET architecture (which we term 2D-OCT-UNET) that operates on the upscaled OCT input resolution.

意义： 我们首次成功地将计算机视觉技术应用于植入纤维化耳蜗的 OCT 数据集。正如我们在实验部分验证的那样，使用这种深度学习模型，可以可靠地进行耳蜗纤维化负担计算。数据集和项目代码可在以下位置获取：Significance: For the first time, we have successfully applied computer vision techniques to an OCT dataset of implanted cochleae with fibrosis. Using this deep learning model, the cochlear fibrotic burden calculation can be reliably carried out as we verify in our experimental section. I 背景Background

人工耳蜗（CI）是仿生假肢领域的一个重大成功案例。对它们的发育、功能以及与人体生理学相互作用的研究一直是生物医学工程领域的兴趣所在。Cochlear implants (CIs) are a major success story in the field of bionic prostheses. Study of their development, function, and interaction with human physiology is of continued interest to the field of biomedical engineering e.g.[1]。 CI 由插入耳蜗鼓阶 (ST) 的灵活电极阵列组成，来自耳罩式接收器的声信号根据声频率转换为脉冲串，传送到单个电极。 CI 可以恢复聋哑患者的部分听力。其他具有残余低频听力的人可以接受所谓的电声刺激 (EAS)，该刺激将助听器低频声信号的放大与高频耳蜗的电刺激结合起来. The CI is comprised of a flexible array of electrodes inserted into the scala tympani (ST) of the cochlea, and acoustic signals from an over-ear receiver are transduced into pulse trains delivered to an individual electrode depending upon the acoustic frequency. CIs restore some hearing in deaf patients. Others with residual low frequency hearing can receive so-called electroacoustic stimulation (EAS), that combines amplification of low frequency acoustic signals from a hearing aid with the electrical stimulation of the cochlea at high frequencies[2]。与传统 CI 患者相比，EAS 患者通常表现出噪声中语音清晰度和音乐性的改善. EAS patients often demonstrate improved speech-in-noise intelligibility and musicality when compared to conventional CI patients[3], [4].

不幸的是，高达 50% 的 EAS 患者在 CI 后数月至数年内丧失残余听力Unfortunately, up to 50% of EAS patients lose their residual hearing in months to years following CI[5], [6]。这种残余听力损失的原因尚不清楚并且是多因素的。原因可能包括螺旋神经节神经元丢失、毛细胞丢失、手术创伤和免疫反应. The causes for this residual hearing loss are unknown and multifactorial. Causes may include spiral ganglion neuronal loss, hair cell loss, surgical trauma, and immune response[7], [8], [9], [10].

关于纤维化形成、其对耳蜗力学的影响以及它是否是残余听力损失的原因的研究正在进行中，并且最近在 CI 研究中采用光学相干断层扫描 (OCT)The study of fibrotic formation, its effects upon cochlear mechanics, and whether it is a cause of residual hearing loss is ongoing, and the adoption of Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) in CI research is recent[17]。 OCT 等新颖的成像策略有可能产生大型数据集。这些数据集为机器学习驱动的分割算法提供了一个用例，以便客观、快速地生成高分辨率的耳蜗纤维化负担估计。我们展示了几种最先进的基于 CNN 和 Transformer 的图像分割架构的性能，并得出结论：我们的方法 2D-OCT-UNET 是计算机视觉技术首次成功应用于包含 CI 诱导的耳蜗内纤维化的新型 OCT 数据集。该工具可用于以低分辨率地面实况分割作为输入来评估多种成像模式。. Novel imaging strategies such as OCT have the potential to produce large datasets. These datasets present a use case for machine learning driven segmentation algorithms in order to objectively and rapidly produce estimates of cochlear fibrotic burden with high resolution. We present the performance of several state-of-the-art CNN and Transformer-based image segmentation architectures and conclude that our approach, 2D-OCT-UNET, is the first successful application of computer vision techniques to a novel OCT dataset containing CI-induced intracochlear fibrosis. This tool can be used to assess multiple imaging modalities with low-resolution ground truth segmentation as an input.

V 讨论Discussion

我们开发了性能最佳的模型并将其应用于耳蜗纤维化负担计算，这对于理解小动物模型中的残余听力损失途径及其转化为人体解剖学特别重要。We developed and applied the best performing model to the cochlear fibrotic burden computation, which is of particular importance for understanding the residual hearing loss pathway in small animal models and its translation to human anatomy.

一个挑战是噪声标签的可能性，这是基于深度学习的图像分割中的一个众所周知的问题A challenge was the possibility of noisy labels, a well-known issue in deep learning based image segmentation[48], [49]。在基底膜-纤维化界面难以识别的情况下，出现了噪音标记的情况。尽管存在噪声标记的情况，该模型通常似乎能够正确识别该界面，并且不会将现有组织标记为纤维化。未来噪声标记的风险可以通过更多的分段器对成像体积的经验来减轻，或者可以通过对两个或多个人类测量值进行平均来避免。此外，噪声标签提供了训练我们的 2D-OCT-UNET 更积极地控制问题的机会。. Instances of noisy labeling cropped up where the basilar membrane-fibrosis interface was difficult to identify. Despite instances of noisy labeling, the model often appeared to correctly identify this interface and not label existing tissue as fibrosis. Future risk of noisy labeling can be mitigated by more segmenter experience with the imaging volume or can be avoided by averaging two or more human measurements. Further, noisy labeling offers an opportunity to train our 2D-OCT-UNET to more actively control for the issue.

We hypothesize that this performance of the 2D-OCT-UNET on the OCTV1L volume is due to the presence of more noisy labels in that volume and the more imbalanced nature of the semantic class annotations. Whether this outcome means that 2D-OCT-UNET can detect extraneous or unusual features within our dataset remains to be tested.

2D-OCT-UNET 的设计考虑了特定的用例：分析豚鼠耳蜗 OCT 扫描中的纤维化负担。目前，我们的数据集很小，只有 5 只动物，并且 2D-OCT-UNET 的功效仅限于批量训练。然而，我们认为 2D-OCT-UNET 的性能值得与其他 CI 和计算机视觉研究人员分享。我们在迄今为止分析的（在撰写本文时）相对较小的成像数据集上提出了分割功效作为概念证明，同时正在收集和分析更多数据。因此，在我们未来的工作中，我们将扩大科利耳 OCT 数据集的大小，并追求 2D-OCT-UNET 的泛化以跨卷工作。我们还将预期通过两个不同的分割器对每个图像进行注释，然后对注释进行平均以最小化地面实况误差。2D-OCT-UNET was designed with a specific use case in mind: to analyze fibrotic burden in OCT scans of guinea pig cochleae. Currently, our dataset is small at 5 animals, and the efficacy of 2D-OCT-UNET is limited to within-volume training. However, we felt that 2D-OCT-UNET’s performance was worth sharing to other CI and computer vision researchers. We have presented segmentation efficacy as a proof of concept, on the so-far analyzed (at the time of writing), relatively small imaging dataset, while more data are being collected and analyzed. Thus, in our future work we will expand the size of the Cochlear OCT dataset, and pursue generalization of 2D-OCT-UNET to working across volumes. We will also anticipate annotating each image by two different segmenters and then averaging the annotations to minimize ground truth error.

目前尚不清楚，但令人不得不考虑 2D-OCT-UNET 将如何处理来自其他物种（包括人类）的成像。解剖学变异性的增加——更不用说 CI 作为治疗潜在病理的手段而不是作为受控研究工具的存在，使这种适应变得复杂。考虑到这些注意事项，我们推测 2D-OCT-UNET 可能适用于其他成像模式，例如微型计算机断层扫描，其具有比标准 OCT 更高的分辨率和穿透深度。测试 2D-OCT-UNET 在人类颞骨成像中的实用性将会非常有趣。It is unclear but compelling to consider how 2D-OCT-UNET will perform on imaging derived from other species, including humans. Increased anatomical variability - not to mention the presence of the CI as a means to treat an underlying pathology and not as a controlled research tool, complicate this adaptation. With these caveats in mind, we speculate that 2D-OCT-UNET may be applicable to other imaging modalities, such as micro computed tomography, which has superior resolution and penetration depth to standard OCT. It would be of great interest to test 2D-OCT-UNET’s utility for human temporal bone imaging.

VI 结论Conclusion

我们的三个主要贡献是：Our three main contributions are:

  • •

    我们从长期植入耳蜗并表现出耳蜗内纤维化的豚鼠中收集了首个带注释的耳蜗 OCT 数据集。We curate a first-of-its-kind annotated Cochlear OCT dataset from chronically cochlear implanted guinea pigs exhibiting intracochlear fibrosis.

  • •

    我们引入了耳蜗语义分割的新任务来研究和量化耳蜗纤维化负担。We introduce a new task of cochlear semantic segmentation to study and quantify cochlear fibrotic burden.

  • •

    我们设计了 2D-OCT-UNET 模型，并展示了其在耳蜗纤维化量化任务上的优越性能。We design a 2D-OCT-UNET model and show its superior performance on the cochlear fibrosis quantification task.

这项工作的目的是在新收集的存在 CI 诱导的耳蜗内纤维化的耳蜗 OCT 数据集上评估不同最先进的深度学习模型在耳蜗语义分割新任务上的性能。The aim of this work is to evaluate the performances of different state-of-the-art deep learning models on the new task of cochlear semantic segmentation on the newly collected Cochlear OCT dataset with CI-induced intracochlear fibrosis present.

由于感兴趣的三个对象类别的形状、大小和外观的高度可变性，这项任务具有挑战性。所有这些属性自然会导致数据集极度不平衡。此外，2D-OCT-UNET 仅针对植入的耳蜗进行训练 - 在没有纤维化的体积上训练模型会引入更大的数据集不平衡，但这是模型优化的必要下一步。This task is challenging owing to the high variability in shapes, sizes and appearances of the three object classes of interest. All these properties naturally result in the extremely imbalanced dataset. Additionally, 2D-OCT-UNET was only trained on implanted cochleae - training the model on volumes without fibrosis would introduce greater dataset imbalance, but is a necessary next step in model optimization.

我们对六种不同的基于 CNN 和基于 Vision Transformer 的最先进模型进行了基准测试，包括基础分段任意模型 (SAM)。尽管任务复杂，但性能最佳的模型是我们自己的 2D-OCT-UNET 配置，它实现了 0.8874 的多类 DSC。定性结果验证了语义分割的高质量。We have benchmarked six different CNN-based and vision Transformer-based state-of-the-art models including the foundation Segment Anything Model (SAM). Despite task complexity, the best performing model was our own 2D-OCT-UNET configuration which achieved multiclass DSC of 0.8874. Qualitative results verify the high quality of semantic segmentation.

人工耳蜗植入后残余听力损失的机制尚未被发现，并且已经提出了几种数值和数学模型Which mechanisms underlie residual hearing loss after cochlear implantation remains undiscovered and several numerical and mathematical models have been proposed[50], [51]。我们希望我们的研究结果能够推动未来探索耳蜗纤维化与残余听力损失之间关系的研究。. We hope that our findings could advance future studies on exploring the relationship between cochlear fibrosis and residual hearing loss.
