9 局限性Limitations

我们的工作有一些局限性，需要进行公开讨论。Our work has several limitations that warrant an open discussion.

  1. 1.

    所有内部和外部评估主要依靠法学硕士法官来评估非显而易见性、健全性、清晰度、可行性和多样性。为了减轻评估者特定的偏见，我们采用了两个独立的大型判断模型，并以相同的权重汇总它们的分数。同样，为了校准关键的稳健性评估，我们对 5 个独立分数进行了采样，并将它们汇总以供下游使用，以减少任何意外偏差。此外，我们的主要指标是All internal and external evaluations rely primarily on LLM-based judges to assess non-obviousness, soundness, clarity, feasibility, and diversity. To mitigate evaluator-specific bias, we employ two independent large-scale judge models and aggregate their scores with equal weightage. Similarly, to calibrate the crucial soundness evaluation, we sample 5 independent scores and aggregate them for downstream usage to mitigate any accidental biases.
