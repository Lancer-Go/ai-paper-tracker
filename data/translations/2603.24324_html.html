摘要Abstract

为协作多智能体系统设计有效的辅助奖励仍然是一项不稳定的任务；不一致的激励措施可能会导致协调不佳，尤其是在稀疏的任务反馈无法提供足够基础的情况下。这项研究引入了一种自动化奖励设计框架，该框架利用大型语言模型从环境仪器中合成可执行的奖励程序。该程序将候选程序限制在正式的有效性范围内，并通过在固定的计算预算下从头开始训练策略来评估其功效；选择完全取决于稀疏任务的返回。该框架通过四种不同的 Overcooked-AI 布局进行评估，其特点是不同的走廊拥堵、切换依赖性和结构不对称。迭代搜索一代始终会产生出色的任务返回和交付计​​数，其中最明显的收益发生在交互瓶颈占主导地位的环境中。对合成塑造成分的诊断分析表明，动作选择中的相互依赖性增加，协调密集型任务中的信号对齐得到改善。这些结果表明，寻找基于目标的奖励计划可以减轻手动工程的负担，同时产生与有限预算下的合作学习兼容的塑造信号。Designing effective auxiliary rewards for cooperative multi-agent systems remains a precarious task; misaligned incentives risk inducing suboptimal coordination, especially where sparse task feedback fails to provide sufficient grounding. This study introduces an automated reward design framework that leverages large language models to synthesize executable reward programs from environment instrumentation. The procedure constrains candidate programs within a formal validity envelope and evaluates their efficacy by training policies from scratch under a fixed computational budget; selection depends exclusively on the sparse task return. The framework is evaluated across four distinct Overcooked-AI layouts characterized by varied corridor congestion, handoff dependencies, and structural asymmetries. Iterative search generations consistently yield superior task returns and delivery counts, with the most pronounced gains occurring in environments dominated by interaction bottlenecks. Diagnostic analysis of the synthesized shaping components indicates increased interdependence in action selection and improved signal alignment in coordination-intensive tasks. These results demonstrate that the search for objective-grounded reward programs can mitigate the burden of manual engineering while producing shaping signals compatible with cooperative learning under finite budgets.

1 简介Introduction

奖励规范仍然是强化学习（RL）的主要瓶颈。在许多领域，预期目标本质上是稀疏的或延迟的；因此，经验性能通常更多地取决于辅助反馈的设计，而不是优化算法本身的改进。在协作多智能体强化学习（MARL）中，这一挑战进一步放大：智能体在马尔可夫博弈中进行交互Reward specification remains a primary bottleneck in reinforcement learning (RL). In many domains, the intended objective is inherently sparse or delayed; consequently, empirical performance often depends more on the design of auxiliary feedback than on improvements to the optimization algorithm itself. In cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL), this challenge is further amplified: as agents interact within a Markov game[11]，辅助奖励不仅影响信用分配和探索，还影响协调所需的激励。因此，在一种环境中加速学习的奖励塑造可能会在另一种环境中引发脆弱的策略，包括最大化代理信号但无法提高真实任务回报的行为。, auxiliary rewards influence not only credit assignment and exploration, but also the incentives necessary for coordination. As a result, reward shaping that accelerates learning in one setting can induce brittle strategies in another, including behaviors that maximize a proxy signal while failing to improve the true task return.

基于势的奖励转换提供了奖励塑造的正式框架，该转换通过定义为跨转换的潜在函数的贴现差的成形术语来增强奖励。众所周知，这种转换可以在标准假设下保留一组最优策略，例如使用一致的折扣因子和依赖于状态的潜在函数A formal framework for reward shaping is provided by potential-based reward transformations, which augment the reward with a shaped term defined as the discounted difference of a potential function across transitions. Such transformations are known to preserve the set of optimal policies under standard assumptions, such as the use of a consistent discount factor and a state-dependent potential function[13]。这种策略不变性属性阐明了在不改变真实目标的情况下可以引入辅助学习信号的条件。尽管随后的研究扩展了框架以包含更丰富的功能，例如状态-行动公式. This policy-invariance property clarifies the conditions under which an auxiliary learning signal can be introduced without altering the true objective. Although subsequent research expanded the framework to incorporate richer features, such as state-action formulations[8]，并将其扩展到多代理设置, and extended it to multi-agent settings[5]，这种形式上的进步并不能减轻指定有效潜在函数的实际负担。此外，它们未能涵盖应用系统中常用的启发式事件奖励和惩罚的广泛类别。, such formal advances do not alleviate the practical burden of specifying an effective potential function. Furthermore, they fail to cover the broad class of heuristic event bonuses and penalties commonly used in applied systems.

除了理论限制之外，深度强化学习对函数逼近和有限训练预算的依赖使得优化动态对辅助奖励高度敏感；因此，理论上政策不变的塑造在复杂的多主体环境中仍可能产生混合的经验结果Beyond theoretical limitations, the reliance of deep RL on function approximation and finite training budgets renders optimization dynamics highly sensitive to auxiliary rewards; accordingly, theoretically policy-invariant shaping may still yield mixed empirical outcomes in complex multi-agent settings[4]。因此，奖励塑造通常需要大量的手动调整，并且奖励错误指定的可能性仍然是一个反复出现的问题。这一挑战的核心在于所实施的奖励与预期目标之间的根本不匹配，这可以通过逆向奖励设计来正式解决。通过将指定的奖励视为需要上下文解释的意图证据，这种方法揭示了优化未指定的代理可能会导致意想不到的副作用. Consequently, reward shaping typically requires extensive manual tuning, and the potential for reward misspecification remains a recurring concern. At the core of this challenge lies a fundamental mismatch between the implemented reward and the intended objective, which is formally addressed by inverse reward design. By treating the specified reward as evidence of intent that requires contextual interpretation, this approach reveals that optimizing an underspecified proxy can lead to unintended side effects[7]，因此需要针对真实目标而不是它们生成的辅助信号使用自动奖励设计方法。, thereby necessitating that automated reward design methods be used against the true objective, rather than the auxiliary signals they generate.

为了减轻对手动工程的依赖，数据驱动范例尝试通过直接从演示或反馈中学习功能表示来自动化奖励设计。逆强化学习（IRL）通过从专家轨迹推断潜在奖励函数来实现这一点To mitigate the reliance on manual engineering, data-driven paradigms attempt to automate reward design by learning functional representations directly from demonstrations or feedback. Inverse Reinforcement Learning (IRL) operationalizes this by inferring underlying reward functions from expert trajectories[14, 17]，而基于偏好的学习通过轨迹段的成对比较来恢复奖励信号, while preference-based learning recovers reward signals through pairwise comparisons of trajectory segments[3]。虽然这些方法在可以访问高质量演示或标签时非常强大，但它们需要大量的数据收集和接口开销，而这在复杂、模拟繁重的 MARL 环境中往往令人望而却步。此外，即使提供了塑造奖励，其功效也常常因状态空间而异。这种不一致需要确定何时以及在多大程度上依赖整形的方法. Although these approaches are powerful when high-quality demonstrations or labels are accessible, they entail substantial data collection and interface overhead, which often proves prohibitive in complex, simulation-heavy MARL environments. Furthermore, even when a shaping reward is provided, its efficacy often varies across the state space. This inconsistency requires methods that determine when and how much to rely on shaping[9].

大型语言模型 (LLM) 作为数据密集型方法的可扩展替代方案出现，提供了一条自动生成奖励的途径，而无需人机交互标记的开销。 Transformer 促进稳健的条件生成Large Language Models (LLMs) emerge as a scalable alternative to data-intensive methods, offering a path toward automated reward generation without the overhead of human-in-the-loop labeling. Transformers facilitate robust conditional generation[15]，并且大规模预训练模型展示了从结构化上下文进行程序合成的广泛能力, and large-scale pretrained models demonstrate broad competence in program synthesis from structured context[1]。代码生成方面的最新进展进一步支持了从自然语言规范生成重要可执行工件的可行性. Recent advances in code generation further support the feasibility of producing nontrivial executable artifacts from natural language specifications[10]。基于这些功能，最近的框架利用法学硕士提出奖励计划，通过强化学习环境中的经验反馈进行改进. Based on these capabilities, recent frameworks utilize LLMs to propose reward programs that improve through empirical feedback in RL settings[12]。法学硕士还有助于根据语言条件的任务描述制定有形的奖励计划. LLMs also facilitate the production of shaped reward programs from language-conditioned task descriptions[16]。然而，自主奖励生成提出了两个主要的技术挑战：生成的函数需要基本的正确性和有界性，而候选奖励需要目标一致的评估，以减轻优化虚假代理的风险。. Nevertheless, autonomous reward generation presents two primary technical challenges: generated functions require basic correctness and boundedness, while candidate rewards require objective-aligned evaluation to mitigate the risk of optimizing spurious proxies.

为了满足这些要求，这项工作引入了一个 LLM 指导的合作 MARL 奖励塑造搜索框架。所提出的方法将奖励函数候选表示为可执行程序，通过针对固定基线的端到端训练对其进行评估，并仅根据稀疏任务目标选择最佳候选。通过这样做，该框架确保塑造信号始终立足于真正的任务目标，同时利用法学硕士的生成先验。我们在跨多个任务的标准合作协调基准上评估这种方法，并分析所得的奖励候选者，以表征其对多智能体协调的影响。To address these requirements, this work introduces an LLM-guided reward shaping search framework for cooperative MARL. The proposed approach represents reward function candidates as executable programs, evaluates them through end-to-end training against a fixed baseline, and selects the optimal candidates based solely on the sparse task objective. By doing so, the framework ensures that the shaping signals remain grounded in the true mission goal while leveraging the generative prior of LLMs. We evaluate this method on a standard cooperative coordination benchmark across multiple tasks and analyze the resulting reward candidates to characterize their impact on multi-agent coordination.

这项工作的贡献是三重的。首先，我们为合作 MARL 引入了一个自动奖励搜索框架，该框架利用基于目标的选择来确保与全球目标保持一致。其次，我们提供了四个协调任务的实证验证，结合激励诊断来表征候选奖励引起的信号。最后，我们提出了系统的诊断分析，说明综合奖励函数如何塑造特定的协调行为并影响潜在的多智能体学习动态。The contributions of this work are threefold. First, we introduce an automated reward search framework for cooperative MARL that leverages objective-grounded selection to ensure alignment with global goals. Second, we provide empirical validation across four coordination tasks, incorporating incentive diagnostics to characterize the signals induced by candidate rewards. Finally, we present a systematic diagnostic analysis that illustrates how synthesized reward functions shape specific coordination behaviors and influence the underlying multi-agent learning dynamics.

2 相关工作Related Work

人们从多个角度研究了奖励工程的减少，包括合作 MARL 中的奖励塑造和信用分配、数据和交互的奖励构建或优化，以及使用法学硕士的程序化奖励生成。奖励塑造仍然是加速稀疏目标学习的常见实用工具，但它引入了可以改变学习动态的设计选择，并且在多智能体环境中可以改变激励措施。 策略不变整形为在不改变基本目标的情况下添加辅助信号提供了原则性基线Reduction of reward engineering has been studied from several perspectives, including reward shaping and credit assignment in cooperative MARL, reward construction or optimization from data and interaction, and programmatic reward generation using LLMs. Reward shaping remains a common practical tool for accelerating learning with sparse objectives, but it introduces design choices that can change learning dynamics and, in multi-agent settings, can change incentives. Policy-invariant shaping provides a principled baseline for adding auxiliary signal without altering the underlying objective[13]，后来的工作扩大了基于势的构造的类别, and later work broadens the class of potential-based constructions[8]。 在合作式 MARL 中，奖励信号还充当信用分配机制，激励设计更好地反映每个智能体对团队绩效的贡献；基于潜力的差异奖励就是这个方向的一个例子. In cooperative MARL, the reward signal also serves as a mechanism for credit assignment, motivating designs that better reflect each agent’s contribution to team performance; potential-based difference rewards are an example of this direction[5]。这些工作阐明了辅助奖励可能是有用的，但它们并没有消除为给定域指定有效塑造术语的需要，也不能确保启发式选择的基于事件的塑造将避免代理优化。. These lines of work clarify that auxiliary rewards can be useful, but they do not remove the need to specify effective shaping terms for a given domain, nor do they ensure that heuristically chosen event-based shaping will avoid proxy optimization.

第二组方法旨在通过修改或学习训练期间如何使用奖励来减少手动调整。 一些方法明确优化奖励函数，以便在固定的学习程序下，诱导的行为更好地匹配目标对齐概念A second set of methods aims to reduce manual tuning by modifying or learning how rewards are used during training. Some approaches explicitly optimize the reward function so that, under a fixed learning procedure, the induced behavior better matches a target notion of alignment[6]。 其他工作研究当塑造质量在状态空间中变化时，代理如何学习有选择地依赖塑造奖励. Other work studies how an agent can learn to rely on shaping rewards selectively when shaping quality varies across the state space[9]。这些方法激发了当前工作中采用的两种设计选择：通过固定基线下的端到端训练来评估奖励候选者，并使用真实的任务目标而不是成形信号来执行选择。奖励规范也可以通过从演示或反馈中推断目标来解决。 逆强化学习从专家行为推断奖励函数. These approaches motivate two design choices adopted in the present work: reward candidates are evaluated through end-to-end training under a fixed baseline, and selection is performed using the true task objective rather than the shaped signal. Reward specification can also be addressed by inferring objectives from demonstrations or feedback. Inverse RL infers a reward function from expert behavior[14, 17]，基于偏好的学习从人类比较中推断出奖励, and preference-based learning infers rewards from human comparisons[3]。 此类方法减少了手工制作塑造术语的需要，但需要演示、偏好或交互式监督。 目前的工作目标是一种补充机制，其中可以使用重复模拟和环境仪器，并且可以通过培训和评估来测试奖励候选者，而无需收集额外的人类标签。. Such methods reduce the need to handcraft shaping terms, but require demonstrations, preferences, or interactive supervision. The present work targets a complementary regime in which repeated simulation and environment instrumentation are available and in which reward candidates can be tested by training and evaluation without collecting additional human labels.

最近的工作利用 LLM 生成可执行工件，并通过基于 Transformer 的条件生成来实现Recent work has leveraged LLMs to generate executable artifacts, enabled by Transformer-based conditional generation[15, 1]，并在通用代码合成中进行了演示, and demonstrated in general-purpose code synthesis[10]。 基于这些能力，法学硕士已被用来提出奖励计划，并通过强化学习中的经验反馈进行改进. Building on these capabilities, LLMs have been used to propose reward programs that are improved through empirical feedback in RL[12]，并根据任务描述生成有形的奖励计划, and to generate shaped reward programs from task descriptions[16]。 我们的方法在对合作 MARL 至关重要的两个方面与这些框架有所不同：候选奖励是仪器信号上的可执行程序，并且只有当候选奖励在固定的多代理学习基线下改善稀疏任务目标时才会被接受，这是为了限制代理优化效果. Our approach diverges from these frameworks in two aspects critical to cooperative MARL: candidate rewards are executable programs over instrumentation signals, and candidates are accepted only when they improve the sparse task objective under a fixed multi-agent learning baseline, which is intended to limit proxy optimization effects[7].

3 方法Method

该方法的总体框架如图所示The general framework of the proposed method is illustrated infig.˜1。该系统实施了一个迭代的闭环奖励工程流程，利用了 LLM 的生成能力和 MARL 的评估精度。为合作 MARL 描述了一种基于客观的奖励搜索过程。在高层次上，法学硕士根据结构化任务描述和先前结果的档案提出可执行的奖励候选人。每个候选者都受到有效性信封的约束，该信封增强了可实现性并阻止了可利用的代理。有效的候选者用于在固定预算下使用固定的 MARL 学习器从头开始训练策略，并且仅使用稀疏任务目标来评估其性能。此外，描述性激励诊断是根据首次展示计算出来的，并用作后续候选生成的结构化反馈。. The system implements an iterative, closed-loop reward engineering process that leverages the generative capabilities of LLMs and the evaluative precision of MARL. An objective-grounded reward search procedure is described for cooperative MARL. At a high level, an LLM proposes executable reward candidates conditioned on a structured task description and an archive of prior outcomes. Each candidate is constrained by a validity envelope that enforces implementability and discourages exploitable proxies.Valid candidates are used to train policies from scratch with a fixed MARL learner under a fixed budget, and their performance is evaluated solely with the sparse task objective. In addition, descriptive incentive diagnostics are computed from rollouts and used as structured feedback for subsequent candidate generation.

5 结论Conclusion

提出了一种基于客观的方法来合成合作 MARL 的自主奖励计划。在此框架中，大型语言模型根据环境检测生成候选塑造程序。这些候选人仍然受到正式有效性范围的限制，并在固定的 MAPPO 学习器下接受评估。连续代之间的选择仅取决于稀疏任务返回；因此，性能改进与预期目标直接相关，而不是与辅助整形信号本身相关。An objective-grounded approach to autonomous reward program synthesis for cooperative MARL has been presented. In this framework, a large language model generates candidate shaping programs from environment instrumentation. These candidates remain constrained by a formal validity envelope and undergo evaluation under a fixed MAPPO learner. Selection across successive generations depends solely on the sparse task return; consequently, performance improvements are directly related to the intended objective, rather than to the auxiliary shaping signal itself.

在四种 Overcooked-AI 布局中，后来的搜索世代表现出更高的稀疏回报和增加的交付数量。最明显的收益出现在稀疏反馈下由交互瓶颈主导的布局中。塑造组件的诊断趋势表明，在协调繁重的任务中，动作耦合增加，塑造信号的一致性更高。此外，在切换驱动的布局中，单个代理的成形集中度明显降低。这些结果表明，基于目标的奖励计划搜索可以减轻手动奖励工程的负担，同时产生与有限训练预算下稳定的合作学习兼容的塑造信号。Across four Overcooked-AI layouts, later search generations exhibit higher sparse returns and increased delivery counts. The most pronounced gains appear in layouts dominated by interaction bottlenecks under sparse feedback. Diagnostic trends for the shaping component indicate increased action coupling and higher alignment of shaping signals in coordination-heavy tasks. Furthermore, a reduction in the shaping concentration on a single agent is evident in handoff-driven layouts. These results suggest that objective-grounded reward program search can mitigate the burden of manual reward engineering while it yields shaping signals compatible with stable cooperative learning under finite training budgets.
