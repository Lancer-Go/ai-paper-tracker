2 连接的价值The value of connection

Frontier AI 已经扩展了单一思维。但即将到来的现实是一个网络：数百万 异构代理——不同的基础模型、工具和专业知识——连接起来一起工作。 主导问题不再是“一名特工有多优秀？”而是“创造的价值是什么？ 他们之间有什么联系吗？”Frontier AI has scaled a single mind. But the coming reality is a network: millions of heterogeneous agents—different base models, tools, and expertise—connecting to work together. The governing question is no longer “how good is one agent?” but “what is the value created by the connections between them?”

互联网用三个定律回答了这个问题的版本The Internet answered its version of this question with three laws[14, 11, 13, 4, 8]，每一个对应一个拓扑 （图, each corresponding to a topology (Figure2).

我们的论文：相同的顺序管理代理网络，并且至关重要的是，可以构建一个网络 自适应地占据手头任务的最佳状态，而不是锁定于一种拓扑。Our thesis: the same ordering governs agent networks, and—crucially—a network can be built that adaptively occupies the best regime for the task at hand, rather than being locked to one topology.

7 Outlook——扩展智能的第二个轴Outlook — a second axis for scaling intelligence

十年来，人工智能的进步意味着一件事：让单个模型变得更大。该轴是真实的，并且它 还远未耗尽。但这并不是唯一的一个。互联网并没有重塑世界，因为任何单一的 计算机变得强大——它重塑了世界，因为计算机For a decade, progress in AI has meant one thing: make a single model larger. That axis is real, and it is far from exhausted. But it is not the only one. The Internet did not reshape the world because any single computer became powerful—it reshaped the world because computers已连接connected，以及价值迁移 从节点到网络。我们相信智力也将采取同样的行动，并且科学 连接才刚刚开始。, and value migrated from the node to the network. We believe intelligence is about to make the same move, and that the science of the connection is only beginning.

随着数亿个异构的、用户拥有的代理上线——每个代理都有自己的模型， 工具、记忆和专业知识——决定性的问题不再是As hundreds of millions of heterogeneous, user-owned agents come online—each with its own model, tools, memory, and expertise—the decisive question stops being一名代理人的能力如何？how capable is one agent?和 变成and becomes他们的联系创造了多少价值？how much value do their connections create?本文从字面上回答了这个问题。的 涌现交叉表明，连接具有其自身的缩放法则：超过一个小阈值， 由廉价、多样化的思想组成的网络比复制一个强大的思想更有价值——价值来自于多样性 连接的内容，而不是任何节点的大小。反身性的结果更奇怪，但也更有希望： 一个网络，只要解决它自己的问题，就可以重新发现管理它并自我组织的法则 因此。创造价值的结构不需要从上面强加——它可以从 之内。This paper takes that question literally. The emergence crossover shows that connection carries a scaling law of its own: past a small threshold, a network of cheap, diverse minds is worth more than a copy of a strong one—value comes from the diversity of what is connected, not the size of any node. The reflexivity result is stranger and more hopeful still: a network, handed only its own problem, can rediscover the very law that governs it and organize itself accordingly. The structure that creates the value need not be imposed from above—it can be grown from within.

如果这种情况在规模上持续下去，其后果将超越人工智能。像 ANet Patu-1 这样的协议让一群人 将普通主体转变为适应性集体，分解问题、形成联盟并达到目标 没有中央权威、没有瓶颈的共识——不属于任何人的集体智慧的基础 一个并向所有人开放。If this holds at scale, the consequences run past AI. A protocol like ANet Patu-1 turns a crowd of ordinary agents into an adaptive collective that decomposes problems, forms coalitions, and reaches consensus with no central authority and no bottleneck—a substrate for collective intelligence owned by no one and open to everyone.
