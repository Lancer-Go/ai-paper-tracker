摘要Abstract

在这项工作中，我们介绍了 Certus 口径分类枪支数据集 (C3GD)，这是一个为分析枪支枪口爆炸声音而开发的可公开访问的数据集。该数据集旨在提供各种枪支、口径、弹药筒、麦克风和麦克风位置，以及比当前可用的详细元数据。它包含从 16 种口径的 28 种枪支现场收集的 8000 多个数据点。由于现场数据收集成本高昂，现有的大部分研究都是使用从互联网收集的枪声音频完成的，这增加了低质量数据和标签噪声的风险。该数据集主要专注于口径分类，但也可用于枪声检测、音频分离和音频信号处理，提供多样化的真实参考。该数据集旨在提供足够的多样性，以便能够推广到更多现实世界的应用程序，同时还为详细的学术分析提供足够的元数据。In this work, we introduce the Certus Caliber Classification Gunshot Dataset (C3GD), a publicly accessible data set developed for the analysis of firearm muzzle blast sounds. The dataset aims to provide a wide variety of firearms, calibers, cartridges, microphones, and microphone locations with metadata detailed beyond what is currently otherwise available. It comprises more than 8000 field-collected data points from 28 firearms across 16 calibers. Because data collection in the field is costly, much of the existing research has been done using gunshot audio collected from the internet, which increases the risk of low-quality data and label noise. This dataset is primarily focused on caliber classification, but can also be used for gunshot detection, audio separation, and audio signal processing, providing a diversified and real-world reference. The dataset aims to provide enough diversity to be able to generalize to more real-world applications while also providing enough metadata for detailed academic analysis.

背景BACKGROUND

与其他传感方式相比，声学传感具有明显的优势。与光学传感器不同，声学传感器不需要足够的照明或视线，并且在范围和方向性方面都超过了许多其他传感器类型Acoustic sensing offers distinct advantages over alternative sensing modalities. Unlike optical sensors, acoustic does not require adequate lighting or line-of-sight, and it surpasses many other sensor types in both range and directionality[1]。此外，录音设备在现有基础设施中广泛使用，包括智能手机和安全摄像头，增加了支持性的辅助功能组件。这些特性使得声学传感特别适合枪声检测。枪支会发出响亮、脉冲的声音，并具有特有的声学特征. Moreover, audio recording devices are widely available across existing infrastructure, including smartphones and security cameras, adding a supportive accessibility component. These properties make acoustic sensing particularly well-suited for gunshot detection. Firearms produce loud, impulsive sounds with characteristic acoustic signatures[12]可以长距离传播。枪支的枪口爆炸传播的距离比射弹本身要远得多，这为在现实世界场景中进行实时检测和分类创造了机会。that propagate over long distances. The muzzle blast of a firearm travels considerably farther than the projectile itself, creating opportunities for real-time detection and classification in real world scenarios.

根据声学特征对枪支进行自动分类在安全行动、军事环境、法医调查和公共安全方面有着广泛的应用。不仅能够区分枪声的存在，还能够区分口径、枪械类型和枪械型号等特征，可以为紧急响应和威胁评估提供关键的战术情报Automatic firearm classification from acoustic signatures has numerous applications in security operations, military contexts, forensic investigation, and public safety. The capability to distinguish not only the presence of gunfire but also characteristics such as caliber, firearm type, and firearm model can provide critical tactical intelligence for emergency response and threat assessment[11]。因此，实时声学分类模型的开发是增强恶劣环境中的安全和态势感知的重要优先事项。. Consequently, the development of real-time acoustic classification models represents an important priority for enhancing safety and situational awareness in hostile environments.

尽管有这些令人信服的用例，但由于多种因素，声学枪声分类仍然具有挑战性。枪口爆炸产生的巨大脉冲很容易被误认为是其他响亮的声音，例如烟花、建筑工程、汽车回火，甚至塑料袋爆裂声Despite these compelling use cases, acoustic gunshot classification remains challenging due to several factors. The loud impulse caused by the muzzle blast is easy to mistake for other loud sounds such as fireworks, construction work, cars backfiring, and even plastic bags popping[2]。此外，枪口爆炸事件的短暂持续时间限制了可用于分析的时间信息. Furthermore, the brief duration of muzzle blast events limits the temporal information available for analysis[9]，对不以高采样率录音的麦克风提出了更大的挑战, presenting an even greater challenge on microphones which do not record at a high sample rate[10] [6]。为了开发能够在不同声学环境中实现稳健泛化的检测和分类模型，必须对模型进行各种声学条件（例如混响和回声）的训练. To develop models for detection and classification that achieve robust generalization across diverse acoustic environments, the models must be trained on a variety of acoustic conditions such as reverberation and echo[12]、不同信噪比的环境背景噪声以及麦克风削波引起的信号失真, ambient background noise of varying signal-to-noise ratios, and signal distortion caused by microphone clipping[8].

The proliferation of high-quality microphones, coupled with advances in efficient neural network architectures, enables deployment on resource constrained platforms. This dataset is specifically designed to support the development of machine learning algorithms for gunshot classification that can be deployed on edge devices and mobile platforms[1].

收集方法和设计COLLECTION METHODS AND DESIGN

数据收集DATA COLLECTION

该数据集是在几个月的时间内在不同的室外地点进行的三个独立的记录会话中收集的。选择受控的室外环境来捕获干净的枪口爆炸特征，同时将环境污染降至最低，同时确保不同环境下的声学变化。图 1-3 说明了每个收集事件的麦克风放置配置。The dataset was collected across three separate recording sessions conducted at distinct outdoor locations over a period of several months. Controlled outdoor environments were selected to capture clean muzzle blast signatures with minimal environmental contamination while ensuring acoustic variability across different settings. Figures 1-3 illustrate the microphone placement configurations for each collection event.

每个记录会话都采用一致的协议。记录设备（如表 1 所示）被放置在距射击者位置不同距离的位置，主要放置在下行方向以捕获声学传播。数据集元数据中提供了每个记录设备的完整规格，并在表 1 中进行了总结。每支枪支均使用分配给它的所有弹药进行射击，并在测试部分（例如特定平台或口径）结束时手动记录元数据。Each recording session employed a consistent protocol. Recording devices, detailed in Table 1, were positioned at various distances from the shooter location, with primary placement in the downrange direction to capture acoustic propagation. Complete specifications for each recording device are provided in the dataset metadata and summarized in Table 1. Each firearm was fired using all the ammunition allotted to it and the metadata was recorded manually upon conclusion of a section of testing, such as a particular platform or caliber.

收集协议涵盖 16 种不同的口径，其中许多口径包含不同的弹药，以及多种枪支品牌和型号，以确保枪管长度、枪机类型和配置的多样性。每支枪械都经过多次射击，以捕捉现实世界中声学特征的变化，同时保持可靠训练数据的可重复性。The collection protocol encompassed 16 distinct calibers, with many calibers containing different variations of ammunition, as well as multiple firearm makes and models to ensure diversity in barrel lengths, action types, and configurations. Each firearm was discharged multiple times to capturing real-world variability in acoustic signatures while maintaining repeatability for reliable training data.

数据处理DATA PROCESSING

每次收集事件后，所有记录的数据都从单独的记录设备传输到计算机，并导入 Audacity 进行处理和质量评估。处理管道由三个主要阶段组成：元数据注释、多通道同步和时间分段。Following each collection event, all recorded data was transferred from individual recording devices to a computer and imported into Audacity for processing and quality assessment. The processing pipeline consisted of three primary stages: metadata annotation, multi-channel synchronization, and temporal segmentation.

数据中添加了枪支型号和口径等元数据，并将大致同步的数据导出为多轨音频文件，使数据更易于处理，因为一整天的录音可以超过 40 GB。所有以 48 kHz 或以上频率收集的音频都被重新采样为统一的 48 kHz 采样率，以确保具有不同本机采样率的设备之间的一致性。Metadata, such as gun model and caliber, was added to the data and the roughly synchronized data was exported as multi-track audio files to make the data easier to process, as a full day of recording can exceed 40 GB. All audio, which was collected at or above 48 kHz, was resampled to a uniform 48 kHz sampling rate to ensure consistency across devices with varying native sampling rates.

通过手动识别每个记录中的枪口起始点来实现跨记录设备的同步。事实证明，这一步骤对于全面的枪声提取至关重要，因为质量较低的麦克风或远处的录音位置有时会产生幅度不足以进行可靠的自动检测的信号，类似于 Kabealo 等人使用的过程。 (2023)Synchronization across recording devices was accomplished by identifying the muzzle onset in each recording manually. This step proved essential for comprehensive gunshot extraction, as lower-quality microphone or distant recording positions occasionally produced signals with insufficient amplitude for reliable automatic detection, similar to the process used by Kabealo et al. (2023)[4]。通过将所有录音同步到最高质量的参考通道，我们确保了所有设备的时间一致性一致，同时保留了每个录音位置和设备类型特有的声学特性，使可用于检测的数据集更加成熟，并提高了对基于位置、口径和设备类型的细微检测的理解。. By synchronizing all recording to the highest-quality reference channel, we ensured consistent temporal alignment across all devices while preserving the acoustic characteristics unique to each recording position and device type, maturing the datasets available for detection and improving the understanding of nuanced detection based on position, caliber, and device type.

然后使用自定义 Python 脚本处理同步录音，该脚本旨在自动提取单个枪击事件。提取算法在参考通道上运行，以查找高于特定阈值的幅度峰值（The synchronized recordings were then processed using a custom Python script designed to automatically extract individual gunshot events. The extraction algorithm operates on the reference channel to find the peaks in amplitude above a certain threshold (80 %80\text{\,}\mathrm{\char 37\relax}），然后回滚选定的时间段（大约 0.1 秒），以确保整个枪口爆炸位于数据将被切片的 1 秒窗口内。), and then rolls back a selected period of time (about 0.1 s) to ensure the entire muzzle blast fits within the 1 second window into which the data will be sliced.

一旦在参考通道中识别出事件，就会从所有同步通道中提取相应的时间片段，确保所有记录设备同时捕获每一次枪声。使用此枪声提取过程，音频被修剪为不重叠的剪辑，其中包含持续时间不超过 1 秒的一个或多个不同的枪声。一秒提供足够的时间上下文，同时保持模型训练的计算效率。Once events were identified in the reference channel, the corresponding temporal segments were extracted from all synchronized channels, ensuring that each gunshot was captured across all recording devices simultaneously. Using this gunshot extraction process the audio was trimmed to non-overlapping clips which contain one or more distinct gunshots of a duration not to exceed 1 second. One second provides sufficient temporal context while maintaining computational efficiency for model training.

特征提取是关键的预处理步骤，它将原始音频波形转换为更适合机器学习模型的表示。已经探索了几种用于声学分类任务的方法；基于短时傅里叶变换 (STFT) 的幅度谱图是一种常见的选择，因为它们通过以固定间隔将波形分解为其组成频率来捕获信号随时间变化的频率内容。虽然有效，但幅度谱图在线性频率范围内运行，与人类感知声音的方式不太相符。梅尔标度谱图通过将频率映射到梅尔标度上、压缩较高频率并扩展较低频率来解决这个问题。这种方法已被证明适用于各种问题，例如枪支模型分类Feature extraction is a critical preprocessing step that transforms raw audio waveforms into representations more suitable for machine learning models. Several approaches have been explored for acoustic classification tasks; Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) based magnitude spectrograms are a common choice, as they capture the frequency content of a signal over time by decomposing the waveform into its constituent frequencies at fixed intervals. While effective, magnitude spectrograms operate on a linear frequency scale that does not align well with how sound is perceived by humans. Mel-scale spectrograms address this by mapping frequencies onto the mel scale, compressing higher frequencies and expanding lower frequencies. This approach has been shown to applied to various problems such as firearm model classification[5]一、歌唱技巧分类, singing technique classification[18]和肺音, and lung sounds[3]。而同时使用多种特征提取方法可以实现更高的性能. Whereas it is possible to achieve higher performance using multiple feature extraction methods concurrently[3]，我们建议将 log-mel 声谱图作为枪支声学分类的合理起点，这与 Raponi、Oligeri 和 Ali (2022) 的研究结果一致, we suggest log-mel spectrograms as a reasonable starting point for firearm acoustic classification, consistent with the findings of Raponi, Oligeri, and Ali (2022)[15]。该数据集以其原始音频形式呈现，允许自由选择特征提取方法，并且存储库中包含用于生成频谱图的示例代码。. This dataset is presented in its raw audio form to allow for freedom of choice on feature extraction methods, and example code to produce spectrograms is included in the repository.

验证和质量VALIDATION AND QUALITY

所有录音都经过严格的多级质量控制，以确保元数据的准确性、数据集的可靠性和音频质量。质量保证流程贯穿于数据收集和处理阶段。在收集活动期间，认真记录每组放电的现场元数据，包括枪支品牌、型号、口径、弹药类型和任何值得注意的观察结果。记录模式中内置了冗余，其中在音频中记录了发射的口径和枪支，以确保书面元数据的准确性。录音过程是在受控条件下进行的，以最大限度地减少车辆、飞机、风或人类言语的外来污染。枪支元数据（包括制造商、型号、口径和弹药规格）与制造商文档和弹道数据库进行交叉引用，以最大限度地减少元数据错误。所有收集活动均使用至少五个麦克风进行，其中至少包括两个高质量参考麦克风。所有数据均使用 Audacity 进行目视检查，以确保存在枪声且不存在明显的无关声音。此外，最终数据集的一部分经过手动检查以确保质量。All recordings underwent rigorous multi-stage quality control to ensure metadata accuracy, dataset reliability, and audio quality. The quality assurance process was integrated throughout data collection and processing phases. During collection events, field metadata was diligently recorded for each set of discharges, including firearm make, model, caliber, ammunition type, and any notable observations. Redundancy is built into the recording schema where the caliber and gun being fired is noted in the audio to ensure accuracy with written metadata. Recording sessions were conducted in controlled conditions to minimize extraneous contamination from vehicles, aircraft, wind, or human speech. Firearm metadata including make, model, caliber, and ammunition specifications were cross referenced against manufacturer documentation and ballistic databases to minimize metadata errors. All collection events were conducted with a minimum of five microphones including at least two high quality reference microphones. All data was visually inspected using Audacity to ensure the presence of gunshots and absence of significant extraneous sounds. Furthermore, a portion of the final dataset was manually inspected to ensure quality.

潜在用例Potential Use Cases

虽然该数据集特别关注基于口径的分类，但它可用于多个子问题，例如平台或墨盒分类。此外，通过与外部数据的结合，它可以用于枪击检测或其他问题。 存储库中包含有关如何将数据集与外部数据组合以增强数据集大小的详细信息。While this dataset is specifically focused on caliber-based classification, it can be utilized for several sub-problems, such as platform or cartridge classification. Furthermore, with the combination with external data it can be used for gunshot detection or other problems. Details are included in the repository on how to combine the dataset with external data to enhance the size of the dataset.

研究人员应该注意到，半控制记录环境缺乏现实世界部署中存在的显着混响和背景噪声，这意味着仅根据这些数据训练的模型在没有领域适应的情况下可能无法很好地概括。为了弥补这一差距，我们建议在训练期间应用音频增强来模拟更多样化的声学条件。工具如Researchers should note that the semi-controlled recording environments lack the significant reverberation and background noise present in real-world deployments, meaning models trained solely on this data may not generalize well without domain adaptations. To bridge this gap, we recommend applying audio augmentations during training to simulate more diverse acoustic conditions. Tools such asaudiomentations为音频增强提供简单的管道，包括背景噪声、模拟混响的房间脉冲响应卷积和时频掩蔽。结合这些增强功能可以显着提高模型的稳健性，并缩小受控记录与实际部署条件之间的性能差距。provide a straightforward pipeline for audio augmentation including background noise, room impulse response convolution to simulate reverberation, and time-frequency masking. Incorporating these augmentations can substantially improve model robustness and reduce the performance gap between controlled recordings and real-world deployment conditions.

局限性和普遍性Limitations and Generalizability

该数据集被组装到我们的解决方案中，以表示更真实的类分布。然而，确切的型号和口径取决于我们的可用性，因此可能无法完全代表所有用例，例如军事、民用或执法。根据此数据集训练的机器学习模型做出现实决策时应谨慎行事。The dataset is assembled to our solution to representing a more realistic distribution of classes. However, the exact models and calibers are based on our availability, thus may not be fully representative of all use cases, such as military, civilian, or law enforcement. Caution should be exercised when making real-world decisions based on machine learning models trained off this dataset.

该数据集提供干净的音频，包含最少的削波或背景噪音。然而，枪声声音非常大，在严重失真和背景噪音的情况下也可以听到。该数据集的用户可以将自己的噪声添加到数据中，以根据他们感兴趣的噪声类型制作噪声版本。如收集方法中所讨论的，数据收集的位置由室外场等区域组成。因此，该数据集不能代表城市或室内环境，因为无法使用该数据集产生音频信号的回声和失真。此外，即使非城市环境中的某种程度的结构或景观特征也会增加显着的回声，这可能会增加问题的难度。This dataset is presented with clean audio, containing minimal clipping or background noise. However, gunshot audio is very loud and can be audible in situations with significant distortion and background noise. The users of this dataset can add their own noise to the data to make a noisy version based on the type of noise they are interested in. As discussed in the collection methods, the location of the data collection consists of outdoor field like areas. Therefore, the dataset is unrepresentative of urban or indoor environments as the echo and distortion of the audio signals cannot be produced using this dataset. Furthermore, even some level of structures or landscape features in non-urban environments can add significant echoes which may add significant difficulty to the problem.

尽管存在这些限制，我们的数据集仍显着提高了枪声音频的可用性。具有比所有公开可用的数据集更广泛的麦克风、平台和口径。Despite these limitations, our dataset significantly advances the availability of gunshot audio. With a wider range of microphones, platforms, and calibers than all publicly available datasets.

数据集扩展Dataset Expansion

未来的工作将侧重于扩展数据集以包含更多口径和枪支，以及更详细的元数据，例如环境条件。数据集扩展的目标是支持枪声检测、冲击波检测等相关问题Future work will focus on extending the dataset to include more calibers and firearms, as well as include more detailed metadata, such as the inclusion of environmental conditions. The goal of dataset expansion is to support related problems such as gunshot detection, shockwave detection[12]，等等。此外，我们的目标是记录更详细的环境条件，以进一步分析空气密度或温度的影响。对数据集的分析揭示了枪支类别的进口特征。超音速弹药通常会产生枪口爆炸和弹道冲击波，而亚音速弹丸则缺乏冲击波。当前没有包含枪支产生的超音速冲击波注释数据的数据集。, and more. Furthermore, we aim to record more detailed environmental conditions to further analyze the effects of air density or temperature. Analysis of the dataset reveals import characteristic across firearm categories. Supersonic ammunition typically produce muzzle blast and ballistic shockwave whereas subsonic projectiles lack the shockwave. There are no current datasets which contain annotated data on firearm produced supersonic shockwaves.
