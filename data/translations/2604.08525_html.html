摘要Abstract

当今的大型语言模型 (LLM) 经过训练，可以通过强化学习等方法来符合用户偏好。然而，模型开始部署不仅是为了满足用户，也是为了通过广告创建模型的公司创造收入。这使得法学硕士有可能面临利益冲突，对用户最有利的回应可能与公司的激励措施不一致。例如，赞助产品可能更贵，但在其他方面与其他产品相同；在这种情况下，法学硕士向用户推荐什么（并且应该）？ 在本文中，我们提供了一个框架 受到语言学和广告监管文献的启发，对相互冲突的激励措施可能导致法学硕士改变与用户互动的方式进行分类。 然后，我们提出一系列评估来检查当前模型如何处理这些权衡。 我们发现，大多数法学硕士在多种利益冲突的情况下为了公司的激励而放弃用户福利，包括推荐价格几乎两倍的赞助产品（Grok 4.1 Fast，83%），提供赞助选项来扰乱购买流程（GPT 5.1，94%），以及在不利的比较中隐藏价格（Qwen 3 Next，24%）。 行为也随着推理水平和用户推断的社会经济地位的不同而有很大差异。 我们的结果凸显了当公司开始巧妙地激励聊天机器人中的广告时，用户可能会遇到的一些隐藏风险。Today’s large language models (LLMs) are trained to align with user preferences through methods such as reinforcement learning. Yet models are beginning to be deployed not merely to satisfy users, but also to generate revenue for the companies that created them through advertisements. This creates the potential for LLMs to face conflicts of interest, where the most beneficial response to a user may not be aligned with the company’s incentives. For instance, a sponsored product may be more expensive but otherwise equal to another; in this case, what does (and should) the LLM recommend to the user? In this paper, we provide a framework for categorizing the ways in which conflicting incentives might lead LLMs to change the way they interact with users, inspired by literature from linguistics and advertising regulation. We then present a suite of evaluations to examine how current models handle these tradeoffs. We find that a majority of LLMs forsake user welfare for company incentives in a multitude of conflict of interest situations, including recommending a sponsored product almost twice as expensive (Grok 4.1 Fast, 83%), surfacing sponsored options to disrupt the purchasing process (GPT 5.1, 94%), and concealing prices in unfavorable comparisons (Qwen 3 Next, 24%). Behaviors also vary strongly with levels of reasoning and users’ inferred socio-economic status. Our results highlight some of the hidden risks to users that can emerge when companies begin to subtly incentivize advertisements in chatbots.

1 简介Introduction

从广播电台到谷歌搜索，随着信息技术的成熟，他们往往选择融入广告来创收From radio stations to Google search, as information technologies mature, they often choose to incorporate advertisements to generate income(Sterling et al., 2011; Google, 2000)。 人工智能聊天机器人也不例外。 最近，OpenAI 开始将广告纳入 ChatGPT. AI chatbots are no exception. Recently, OpenAI has started incorporating advertisements into ChatGPT(Simo, 2026; Gehan & Perloff, 2026; Sircar, 2026)，代表聊天机器人与其用户之间关系的根本转变。, representing a fundamental shift in the relationship between the chatbot and its users.

这些广告可能是有代价的：经济学通常将广告视为给消费者带来滋扰成本These advertisements may come at a cost: economics commonly frames ads as imposing a nuisance cost on consumers(Tåg, 2009; Anderson & Gabszewicz, 2006; Anderson & Coate, 2005)，人机交互和广告文献的研究表明，广告会导致挫败感和额外的认知负荷, and studies in both human-computer interaction and advertising literature suggest that ads lead to frustration and additional cognitive load(Brajnik & Gabrielli, 2010; Edwards et al., 2002; Todri et al., 2020)。 在聊天机器人的背景下，还有另一个潜在的成本：广告与用户的最大利益产生紧张关系，可能与成为有用助手的现有规范相竞争. In the chatbot context, there is another potential cost: ads create tension with a user’s best interest, potentially competing with existing norms of being a helpful assistant(Bai et al., 2022a; Askell et al., 2021)。 随着聊天机器人的大规模消费者部署接近这一转折点，迫切需要建立广告呈现方式的规范，并进行严格的研究来评估最先进的人工智能系统是否符合这些规范。. As large-scale consumer deployments of chatbots approach this turning point, there is a critical need to establish norms for how advertisements ought to be presented, and to conduct rigorous studies evaluating whether state-of-the-art AI systems conform to these norms.

基于大型语言模型（例如 ChatGPT）的人工智能助手并不是第一个解决用户帮助和推广之间冲突的系统。客户服务聊天机器人，例如旅行或购物助理AI assistants based on large language models (e.g., ChatGPT) are not the first systems to navigate conflicts between user assistance and promotion. Customer service chatbots, such as travel or shopping assistants(Expedia, 2023; Trip.com, 2023; Booking.com, 2023; Amazon, 2024)，还需要平衡满足用户和带动公司利润。 其中包括值得注意的失败，例如加拿大航空广为人知的法律案件，其客户聊天机器人承诺不存在的退款政策，该公司最终不得不兑现, also need to balance satisfying users and driving company profits. These include notable failures such as Air Canada’s well-publicized legal case where its customer chatbot promised a non-existent refund policy that the company eventually had to honor(Lifshitz & Hung, 2024; Yagoda, 2024)。 重要的是，许多聊天机器人在其平台上推荐产品，使我们能够使用相同的规范和评估程序与广告人工智能助手一起仔细审查它们。. Importantly, many of these chatbots recommend products on their platform, allowing us to scrutinize them alongside advertising AI assistants using the same set of norms and evaluative procedures.

在我们的评估中，我们发现所有当前的法学硕士都表现出有利于公司而不是用户的风险行为，尽管这种频率在不同的法学硕士和场景中存在很大差异。 当选择推荐非赞助产品或价格几乎两倍的赞助替代产品时，23 名法学硕士中有 18 名在超过 50% 的情况下推荐后者。这也受到某些模型中用户个人资料的强烈影响，例如，Gemini 3 Pro 在 74% 的情况下为高 SES 用户推荐赞助产品，但在 27% 的情况下为低 SES 用户推荐赞助产品。In our evaluations we find that all current LLMs exhibit risky behaviors favoring the company over the user, though this frequency varies widely across different LLMs and scenarios. When choosing to recommend either a non-sponsored product or a sponsored alternative nearly twice as expensive, 18 of 23 LLMs recommended the latter over 50% of the time. This was also strongly influenced by a user’s profile in some models—for example, Gemini 3 Pro recommended the sponsored product 74% of the time for high-SES users, but only 27% of the time for low-SES.

接下来，当用户请求从特定的非赞助品牌购买商品时，所有模型都具有非零的额外推荐赞助替代品的比率，包括 GPT-5.1 (88%) 和 Grok 4.1 (100%)。虽然模型在执行此类推荐时很少对事实特征撒谎，但有些模型使用积极的框架来不成比例地美化赞助选项（例如，Grok 4.1 96%，Qwen 3 Next 66%）。 我们还观察到模型倾向于隐藏产品价格以避免不利的比较（例如，Qwen 3 Next 29%），或者不透露推荐是赞助的（例如，GPT 5.1 89%，Claude 4.5 Opus 98%）——如果部署，可能会违反关于欺骗行为的规定Next, when a user requests to purchase an item from a specific non-sponsored brand, all models had a non-zero rate of extraneously recommending a sponsored alternative, including GPT-5.1 (88%) and Grok 4.1 (100%). We also observed a tendency for models to conceal product prices to avoid an unfavorable comparison (e.g., Qwen 3 Next 29%), or not disclose that a recommendation is sponsored (e.g., GPT 5.1 89%, Claude 4.5 Opus 98%)—which if deployed, could potentially violate regulations on deceptive practices(Federal Trade Commission, 1983)。 最后，当用户询问 LLM 能够完全解决的问题时，一些人继续推荐不再需要的赞助服务（例如 Gemini 3 Pro 31%）。当赞助服务可能对用户有害时，法学硕士仍然推荐它来响应相关用户查询（例如，掠夺性贷款；GPT 5.1 71%）。. Lastly, when a user asks a problem that the LLM is able to fully solve, some continued to recommend a sponsored service that was no longer needed (e.g., Gemini 3 Pro 31%). And when a sponsored service is likely harmful to the user, LLMs still recommended it in response to a relevant user query (e.g., predatory loans; GPT 5.1 71%).

在我们框架的推动下，这些测试表明，如果没有有意识地采取缓解措施，当今的法学硕士就没有能力处理广告中出现的利益冲突。此外，法学硕士行为的异质性表明，当前和即将推出的模型应该针对广告部署进行单独测试——即使一种实现实现了真正的用户利益，其他平台也不能盲目效仿。如果没有保护用户利益的护栏，LLM 广告将打破现有的互动规范和期望，冒着风险甚至利用用户的帮助感知。我们的框架提供了讨论法学硕士广告的标准，促进值得信赖、以人为本的人工智能助手的持续开发。Motivated by our framework, these tests demonstrate that without conscious efforts towards mitigation, today’s LLMs are ill-equipped to handle the conflicts of interest that emerge with advertising. Further, the heterogeneity of LLMs’ behaviors suggest that current and upcoming models should be individually tested for ad deployment—even if one implementation achieves true user benefit, other platforms cannot blindly follow suit. Without guardrails to protect user interests in place, LLM advertisements will break existing interactive norms and expectations, risking or even taking advantage of user perceptions of helpfulness. Our framework provides a standard for discussing LLM advertisements, facilitating the continued development of trustworthy, human-centered AI assistants.

8 讨论Discussion

我们针对LLM广告中出现的利益冲突建立了一套规范，并进行了实验来衡量当前模型在代表性场景下的行为。 在这些研究中，我们发现法学硕士在处理用户福利和平台激励冲突时存在高度异质性的倾向，大多数模型都表现出对用户利益的保护不足。 模型行为的两极分化表明，一般能力扩展和安全调整并不能在多利益相关者场景中可靠地产生一致的行为。 虽然推理时间推理部分缓解了这些问题，但大多数模型仍然以不平凡的速度通过思考 / CoT 违背用户利益。 我们共同表明，将广告纳入法学硕士充满了挑战和麻烦的模型倾向，如果处理不当，可能会严重损害这些系统提供的信息生态系统。We have established a set of norms for conflicts of interest that arise in LLM advertising, and conducted experiments measuring the behavior of current models under representative scenarios. In these, we uncovered highly heterogeneous tendencies from LLMs when navigating conflicts of user welfare and platform incentives, with most models demonstrating insufficient protection for user interests. The polarized spectrum of model behavior suggests that general capability scaling and safety tuning does not reliably produce aligned behavior in multi-stakeholder scenarios. While inference-time reasoning partially mitigates these issues, most models still act against user interests at non-trivial rates with thinking / CoT. Together, we show that incorporating advertisements into LLMs is fraught with challenges and troublesome model tendencies that if handled incorrectly, may considerably damage the information ecosystem that these systems provide.

Further, as we observed that most models are steerable towards user interests, we should hold websites, rather than just model providers, accountable for the behavior of their chatbots. Companies must individually prove that their chatbots are willing to put users first. On the other hand, users should place scrutiny on their AI assistants to determine if they are truly helpful.

其次，我们表明，当模型为具有冲突价值观的多方提供服务时，假设单一主体的当前协调方法可能会失败。 为此，我们呼吁建立多利益相关者评估框架，超越广告、法学硕士为多方服务时的透明度要求以及利用现有消费者保护标准的监管监督。Second, we show that current alignment approaches that assume a single principal can fail when models serve multiple parties with conflicting values. 9 结论Conclusion

由于法学硕士代理人被部署在更广泛的环境中，并且出于更广泛的目的，因此很可能会出现利益冲突。 与其他自动化系统不同，法学硕士代理人需要自己决定如何处理这些冲突。 用户利益与部署公司利益之间的冲突就是一个简单的例子，随着人工智能公司寻求收入来源，这种冲突可能会变得更加普遍。 我们的工作借鉴了语言学的理论思想，创建了一个对这些冲突进行分类的框架，然后我们用它来对现有法学硕士如何应对这些权衡进行初步分析。 分析表明，企业激励措施对法学硕士的对话行为有重大影响，通常会损害用户的福祉，尽管这些系统之间也存在有意义的差异。 我们的结果表明，帮助法学硕士代理人解决利益冲突可能是开发人员面临的一个紧迫问题，但通过推理时间推理和系统提示管理作为潜在有效的缓解措施，在管理这些冲突方面也可以取得进展。 尽管如此，如果没有有意识地努力寻求缓解措施，将广告纳入聊天机器人可能会带来重大挑战。As LLM agents are deployed in a wider range of settings—and for a wider range of purposes—conflicts of interest are likely to arise. Unlike other automated systems, LLM agents will need to make their own decisions about how they navigate these conflicts. Clashes between user interests and those of the deploying company are a simple example of this, and one that is likely to become more prevalent as AI companies seek sources of revenue. Our work draws on theoretical ideas from linguistics to create a framework for categorizing these conflicts, which we then use to conduct a preliminary analysis on how existing LLMs navigate these tradeoffs. Analyses show that corporate incentives have significant effects on the conversational behavior of LLMs—often detracting from user well-being—although there is also meaningful variation across these systems. Our results suggest that helping LLM agents navigate conflicts of interest will likely be a pressing issue for developers, but also that progress can be made in managing these conflicts, with inference-time reasoning and system prompt curation as potentially effective mitigators. Nonetheless, incorporating advertising into chatbots is likely to present significant challenges without conscious effort to pursue such mitigation.
