By developing a triadic collaboration system to support K-12 writing learning, a multidimensional evaluation framework grounded in Systemic Functional Linguistics and the suggestion trajectory tracing pipeline, this paper contributes a large-scale empirical dataset involving57,95457,954论文来自essays from10,19510,195对面的学生students across120120学校两年以上。我们的研究结果证实了该系统通过战略分工提高写作质量的有效性：法学硕士作为减轻教师倦怠的生成引擎，教师充当教学看门人和桥梁以保证反馈质量。虽然法学硕士和教师对于技能提高都至关重要，但我们发现了天花板效应，即过度的语言扩展会导致边际效用递减。这些表明，随着学生熟练程度的提高，法学硕士与教师之间的协作是动态适应性的。schools over two years. 5 结论Conclusion

本研究实施并部署了一个三元协作模型，将法学硕士、教师和学生整合到现实的 K-12 写作环境中。通过进行关于迭代过程的第一个大规模经验数据集并量化这种合作中语言技能的提高，本研究提供了证明所设计的三元合作模型有效性的经验证据。This study implements and deploys a triadic collaboration model, integrating LLMs, teachers and students, within real-world K-12 writing environment. By conducting the first large-scale empirical dataset regarding the iterative process and quantifying the linguistic skill improvement from this collaboration, this study provides the empirical evidence demonstrating the efficacy of the designed triadic collaboration model.

更重要的是，该系统实现了有效的分工：法学硕士作为高效的生成引擎，可以减轻教师的倦怠，而教师的干预则转变为战略把关人和建议采纳的桥梁。此外，虽然法学硕士和教师都有助于提高写作技能，但我们发现了基于熟练程度的天花板效应：虽然语言扩展对新手作家有很大帮助，但熟练程度较高的学生会遇到边际效用递减的情况，过度的复杂性可能会适得其反。这些发现提倡建立三元协作系统，使法学硕士成为 K-12 教育的利器。More importantly, the system achieves an effective labor division: the LLM functions as a highly efficient generative engine to mitigate instructor burnout while teacher intervention shifts to a strategic gatekeeper and bridge for suggestion adoption. Moreover, while both LLM and teacher contribute to writing skill improvement, we identify a proficiency-based ceiling effect: while linguistic expansion significantly aids novice writers, higher-proficiency students encounter diminishing marginal utility where excessive complexity can become counterproductive. These findings advocate for a triadic collaboration system making LLMs sharp tools for K-12 education.

局限性Limitations

虽然这项研究为 K-12 学生实施法学硕士时的三元合作模型提供了经验证据，但有几个局限性值得考虑：While this study provides empirical evidence for the triadic collaboration model when implementing LLMs for K-12 students, several limitations warrant consideration:

首先，用于量化语言增长的 SFL 框架的普遍性可能因写作类型而异。虽然 SFL 框架已广泛应用于语言评估，但特定语言维度的权重可能会有所不同。探索这些维度的影响如何因流派（例如，争论性、说明性、叙述性、分析性）而变化，对于完善人工智能反馈策略非常有价值。First, the generalizability of the SFL framework used to quantify linguistic growth may vary across writing genres. While the SFL framework has been widely adopted in linguistic evaluation, the weight of specific linguistic dimensions might manifest differently. Exploring how the impact of these dimensions varies across genres (e.g., argumentative, expository, narrative, analytical) would be highly valuable for refining AI feedback strategies.

其次，这项研究的重点是我们三方合作模式的短期影响。我们没有充分考虑长期的纵向影响，例如自动化偏差的潜在出现。例如，教师可能会变得越来越过度依赖法学硕士的生成引擎，这可能会降低我们确定的“战略把关”和“教学桥梁”角色。Second, this research focuses on the short-term impact of our triadic collaboration model. We did not fully consider the long-term longitudinal effects, such as the potential emergence of automation bias. For example, teachers might become increasingly over-reliant on the LLM’s generative engine, potentially degrading the “strategic gatekeeping” and “pedagogical bridge” roles we identified.

此外，我们的研究受到基础法学硕士的具体能力的限制。在现实的 K-12 环境中，频繁更新或切换底层模型在技术上或经济上通常是不可行的。未来的工作应该研究所建议的动态自适应三元系统的实施，以测试其有效性和鲁棒性。Additionally, our study is bounded by the specific capabilities of the underlying LLMs. In real-world K-12 environments, it is often technically or financially unfeasible to frequently update or switch underlying models. Future work should investigate the implementation of the suggested dynamically adaptive triadic system to test its efficacy and robustness.

最后，一些外部因素（例如教师的人工智能素养和学校环境的社会经济多样性）可能会影响结果。虽然我们利用固定效应来控制这些因素的影响，但评估我们系统在这些变量上的公平性将非常有价值。Finally, several external factors (such as the AI literacy of teachers and the socio-economic diversity of school environments) may influence the results. While we employ the fixed effect to control these factors’ impact, assessing the equity of our system across these variables would be highly valuable.

道德考虑和设计Ethical Consideration and Design

在 K-12 教育环境中负责任地部署大型语言模型 (LLM) 至关重要。我们确定并解决对我们的三方合作模式至关重要的三个主要道德领域：The responsible deployment of Large Language Models (LLMs) in K-12 educational settings is paramount. We identify and address three primary ethical domains critical to our triadic collaboration model:

首先，教育 NLP 的一个核心伦理问题是法学硕士有可能取代人类教育工作者。我们的研究通过证明三元模型重新定义而不是消除教师的角色，为反对这种“自动化”叙述提供了强有力的经验证据。通过将重复的语言起草工作交给法学硕士（“生成引擎”），该系统为教师创造了空间，让他们能够专注于高风险的教学把关和桥梁工作。我们的结果表明，教师和法学硕士的干预对于学生的发展都是不可或缺的；然而，教师仍保持较高的采用率和较高的边际改进系数。这证实了法学硕士的实施必须旨在减轻教师的倦怠，同时在学习过程中保持人的责任感。First, a central ethical concern in educational NLP is the potential for LLMs to displace human educators. Our study provides strong empirical evidence against this “automation” narrative by demonstrating that the triadic model redefines, rather than removes, the teacher’s role. By offloading repetitive linguistic drafting to the LLM (the ”Generative Engine”), the system creates space for teachers to focus on high-stakes pedagogical gatekeeping and bridging. Our results show that both teacher and LLM interventions are indispensable for student development; however, teachers maintain a higher adoption rate and a higher marginal coefficient for improvement. This confirms that LLM implementation must be designed to mitigate teacher burnout while maintaining human accountability at the center of the learning process.

其次，为了防止认知脱离，我们的模型避免生成“原始”完整写作。相反，法学硕士充当助教，提供需要学生评估和修改的细致建议。教师充当教学桥梁，过滤这些建议以确保与特定的教育目标保持一致。此外，我们对基于熟练程度的天花板效应的发现可以作为一种道德保障：它警告了语言过于复杂的“过度支架”高级学习者的风险。通过倡导动态自适应系统，我们保护学生的能动性，并确保法学硕士干预支持真正的技能获取，而不仅仅是自动完成。Second, to prevent cognitive disengagement, our model avoids generating “raw” complete writing. Instead, the LLM acts as a teaching assistant, providing granular suggestions that require student evaluation and revision. The teacher acts as a pedagogical bridge, filtering these suggestions to ensure alignment with specific educational objectives. Furthermore, our discovery of the proficiency-based ceiling effect serves as an ethical safeguard: it warns against the risks of “over-scaffolding” advanced learners with excessive linguistic complexity. By advocating for a dynamically adaptive system, we protect student agency and ensure that LLM intervention supports genuine skill acquisition rather than mere automated completion.

第三，这项研究是在参与学校管理部门的批准下进行的，并且被认为免于机构审查委员会（IRB）的审查，因为它不涉及对学生的直接干预。尽管如此，我们严格遵守标准 IRB 道德和隐私要求。我们专门使用去识别化的文本数据，确保不会处理任何个人身份信息 (PII)。所有分析均在安全、访问受限的环境中进行，有效控制数据泄露的任何潜在风险。Third, this study was conducted with the approval of participating school administrations and was deemed exempt from Institutional Review Board (IRB) review as it did not involve direct intervention on students. Nevertheless, we strictly adhered to standard IRB ethics and privacy requirements. We utilized exclusively de-identified textual data, ensuring no Personally Identifiable Information (PII) was processed. All analysis was conducted within a secure, access-restricted environment, effectively controlling for any potential risks of data leakage.
