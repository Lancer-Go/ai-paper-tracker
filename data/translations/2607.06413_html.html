摘要Abstract

大型语言模型编码代理越来越多地执行开放式数据建模和分析。这些代理是随机的和自适应的，因此它们的自主模型发现行为不能通过单个基准运行来充分表征。在这项工作中，我们提出了一个实验设计和分析框架，用于系统地评估这一发现过程，量化其可变性并确定重要因素。所提出的框架将这些代理视为随机模型发现运算符，它将特定于任务的发现数据和优化目标映射到拟合模型。具体来说，我们在受控实验因素（包括智能体的推理工作、任务、优化指标和训练数据的组成）下研究了两个这样的算子 Codex 和 Claude Code。对于每个代理-任务-度量组合，针对多个响应（例如输出质量、美元成本、挂钟时间和流程复杂性）进行回归模型和推理。此外，我们开发了一种与效用一致的规范分解来表征推理努力效应的主导方向，并评估该方向是否与性能成本效用方向一致。所提出的框架在网络构词游戏的测试平台上进行了演示，并在成本和流程复杂性方面的推理工作中获得了富有洞察力的发现。Large language model coding agents increasingly perform open-ended data modeling and analysis. These agents are stochastic and adaptive, and therefore their autonomous model discovery behavior cannot be adequately characterized by a single benchmark run. In this work, we propose an experimental design and analysis framework for systematically evaluating this discovery process, quantifying its variability, and identifying important factors. The proposed framework treats these agents as stochastic model-discovery operators, which map task-specific discovery data and an optimization target to a fitted model. Specifically, we investigate two such operators, Codex and Claude Code, under controlled experimental factors including agent’s reasoning effort, task, optimization metric, and composition of training data. For each agent-task-metric combination, regression models and inference are conducted for multiple responses such as output quality, dollar cost, wall-clock time, and process complexity. Furthermore, we develop a utility-aligned canonical decomposition to characterize the dominant direction of the reasoning-effort effect and to assess whether that direction aligns with a performance-cost utility direction. The proposed framework is demonstrated on a testbed of networked word-forming games with insightful findings on reasoning effort with respect to cost and process complexity.

6 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusion

6.1 调查结果摘要Summary of Findings

主要的实际意义是，所要求的推理工作更像是 资源控制参数比可靠的路径更好的模型。更高的努力会产生 运行时间更长、成本更高且更复杂。它不会在模型中产生匹配增益 质量。我们的多变量分析清楚地反映了这种模式。作为单一响应的性能分析 仅靠努力就会错过这种模式，因为努力的主要变化出现在成本和 过程而不是质量。通过将成本和流程视为多重响应坐标，我们可以 询问努力效应是否朝着有用的方向发展。在这个实验中，定向 效用斜率表明事实并非如此。The main practical implication is that requested reasoning effort acts more like a resource-control parameter than a reliable path to better models. Higher effort produces longer, costlier, and more complex runs. It does not produce a matching gain in model quality. This pattern is clearly reflected through our multivariate analysis. An analysis of performance as a single response alone would miss this pattern, because the main changes with effort appear in cost and process rather than in quality. By treating cost and process as multiple response coordinates, we can ask whether the effort effect moves in a useful direction. In this experiment, the directed utility slopes show that it does not.

所提出的设计框架还阐明了这些代理的三个操作特征。一、代理商 专注于他们的评分指标。目标指标的收益可能会随着较弱的指标而增加 另一个指标的表现，因此目标的选择本身就是一个必然的设计 决定。其次，当目标奖励与坚持的结果分布相匹配时，更多的训练数据会有所帮助。对于其他目标，全部和部分发现 制度只显示出很小的差异。第三，两个代理达到了可比较的主要指标 质量，但它们的估计美元成本可能相差约七倍。在这个试验台上， 因此，质量和成本在代理之间并不紧密耦合。The proposed design framework also clarifies three operating features of these agents. First, the agents specialize to the metric they are scored on. Gains on the target metric can come with weaker performance on the other metric, so the choice of objective is itself a consequential design decision. Second, more training data helps mainly when the objective rewards matching the held-out distribution of outcomes. For the other objectives, the full and partial discovery regimes show only small differences. Third, the two agents reach comparable primary-metric quality, but their estimated dollar costs can differ by about a factor of seven. In this testbed, quality and cost are therefore not tightly coupled across agents.

另一个警示性的发现涉及自我评估。在预测任务上，代理人的 自我报告的分数接近他们的评估分数。在生成任务上，代理 常常很乐观。所有四次丢弃的运行都来自生成距离条件。 附录Another cautionary finding concerns self-assessment. For deployment, an agent’s self-report should not replace an external evaluation check, especially for generative tasks.

6.2 局限性和未来的工作Limitations and Future Work

当前的工作存在一些限制。首先，所提出的设计没有考虑复制， 因此逐层推理是基于阶乘结构而不是重复执行 相同配置的。这可能会限制不确定性估计的稳定性。注意复制 在我们的环境中，对于代理人工智能来说，规模更大的成本是昂贵的。我们最昂贵的单程运行 实验需要 70 分钟以上，代理工作费用超过 33 美元 单独而言，模拟和验证流程进一步增加了时间投入。更大规模的重复研究 因此留待以后的工作。其次，我们目前的工作仅调查一个人身上的两个代理人 一系列发现任务。因此，所提出的框架被证明是一种技术，而不是对一般代理人的调查。第三，一名代理人的美元成本是根据标价的代币数量估算的，而不是 比直接计量。因此，跨代理成本比较描述了部署 经济学并不是一个受控的对比。第四，主导的规范方向也是 在单一层内的识别度较弱。因此，我们对待效用失调结果 作为描述性的，由八层中其符号的一致性所支持。There are several limits in the current work. First, the proposed design has not considered replications, so the per-stratum inference is based on the factorial structure rather than repeated executions of the same configuration. This may limit the stability of the uncertainty estimates. Note that replication at larger scale is costly for agentic AI in our setting. The most expensive single run in our experiment required more than 70 minutes and more than 33 metered dollars for agent work alone, and the simulation and validation pipeline adds further time effort. Larger replicated studies are therefore left to future work. Second, our current work investigates only two agents on one family of discovery tasks. The proposed framework is therefore demonstrated as a technique, not as a survey of agents in general. Third, the dollar cost for one agent is imputed from token counts at list prices rather than metered directly. For this reason, the cross-agent cost comparison describes deployment economics and is not a controlled contrast. Fourth, the leading canonical direction is also weakly identified within a single stratum. We therefore treat the utility-misalignment result as descriptive, supported by the consistency of its sign across the eight strata.

未来的工作有几个方向。人们可以研究更广泛的代理组 和发现领域来测试推理努力和结果之间的模式将如何 概括。未来的实验还应该记录计量成本，以便分析价格 更直接。另一个方向是更详细地研究记录的跑步轨迹。这可能 表明额外的推理努力用在了哪里，以及为什么它几乎没有带来效用。更多 一般情况下，可以通过进一步的实验和分析，联合综合考察代理经营者在绩效、质量、成本、流程等方面的行为。There are several directions for future work. One can study a wider set of agents and discovery domains to test how the pattern between reasoning effort and outcomes will generalize. Future experiments should also record metered cost, so that price can be analyzed more directly. Another direction is to study the recorded run traces in more detail. This may show where the extra reasoning effort goes and why it produces little gain in utility. More generally, further experiments and analyses can be conducted to investigate agentic operators on their behaviors with respect to performance, quality, cost and process in a joint and comprehensive manner.
