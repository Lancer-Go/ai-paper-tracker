脑电图和肌电图作为神经再生的非侵入性生物标志物：中枢和周围神经系统损伤和再生的回顾Electroencephalography and Electromyography as a Non-Invasive Biomarker of Neural Regeneration: A Review of Central and Peripheral Nervous System Injury and Regeneration

Maryam Kheyrollah1, Reza Khanbabaie1, 2, Chris Ullrich 3,
Mohammad Moulaeifard4,*
1Department of Physics, University of Ottawa, Ottawa, Canada
2Congnixion Inc., Toronto, Canada
3Congnixion Inc., Santa Barbara, CA, USA

摘要Abstract

损伤后神经系统的再生仍然是一个重要的治疗目标，特别是在中枢神经系统（CNS）中，其中再生受到神经元限制和不利的细胞外环境的限制。相反，周围神经系统（PNS）在支持性雪旺细胞（SC）和促生长刺激的存在下表现出增强的再生能力。虽然结构和分子机制已被彻底了解，但能够非侵入性实时监测再生的功能生物标志物仍然有限。在这篇综述中，我们讨论了脑电图 (EEG) 和肌电图 (EMG) 作为实时、非侵入性生物标志物监测 CNS 和 PNS 环境中神经损伤和再生的前景。Regeneration of the nervous system after injury remains an important therapeutic objective, especially in the central nervous system (CNS), in which regeneration is restricted by both neuronal limitations as well as adverse extracellular environments. Conversely, the peripheral nervous system (PNS) displays enhanced regenerative capability in the presence of supportive Schwann cells (SC) and pro-growth stimuli. While the structure and molecular mechanisms are thoroughly understood, functional biomarkers that can non-invasively monitor regeneration in real time are limited. In this review, we discuss the promise of electroencephalography (EEG) as well as electromyography (EMG) as real-time, non-invasive biomarkers to monitor damage to nerves and regeneration in both CNS and PNS contexts.

首先，我们对比 CNS/PNS 损伤的生物和电生理指标，显示 EEG 信号（包括振荡功率、连通性和诱发电位变化）如何反映损伤导致的功能障碍以及神经可塑性重组。此外，肌电图可以直接洞察肌肉激活和外周输出，为神经肌肉通路的完整性和重新激活提供有用的脑电图补充。在中枢神经系统损伤（例如中风、脊髓损伤 (SCI)）中，脑电图通常显示整体减慢、半球间一致性破坏以及较高频率的部分恢复。对于 PNS 损伤，脑电图可以通过重建外周连接来捕获皮质重新映射和体感诱发反应的返回。肌电图反过来又可以监测神经支配和功能性运动输出恢复。First, we contrast biological and electrophysiological indicators of CNS/PNS injury, showing how EEG signs, including oscillatory power, connectivity, and evoked potential changes, reflect dysfunction due to injury as well as neuroplastic reorganization. Also, EMG provides direct insight into muscle activation and peripheral output, providing useful EEG complementation in neuromuscular pathway integrity and reactivation. In CNS injuries (e.g., stroke, spinal cord injury (SCI)), EEG typically shows global slowing, disrupted interhemispheric coherence, and partial recovery of higher frequencies. For PNS injuries, EEG can capture cortical remapping and return of somatosensory evoked responses with re-establishment of the peripheries’ connectivity. EMG, in turn, enables monitoring of reinnervation and restoration of functional motor output.

本综述提出了双系统视角，将脑电图和肌电图不仅定位为诊断工具，而且定位为神经再生的功能性生物标志物，从而在电生理学、可塑性和临床恢复之间架起桥梁。This review presents a dual-system perspective, positioning EEG and EMG not only as diagnostic tools but also as functional biomarkers of neural regeneration, thereby bridging electrophysiology, plasticity, and clinical recovery.

1 简介Introduction

神经系统损伤会在中枢和外周区引起截然不同的再生反应。成年哺乳动物中枢神经系统的修复能力严重有限，常常导致持续的神经损伤。相比之下，PNS 具有显着的轴突再生能力，可以产生显着的功能恢复Injuries of the nervous system elicit vastly different regenerative responses within central and peripheral compartments. The adult mammalian CNS has a severely limited capacity for repair, often resulting in persistent neurological impairments. In contrast, the PNS has a significant capacity for axonal regrowth, which can yield significant functional recovery(Cooke et al., 2022)。鉴于这些差异，人们对开发可靠的、非侵入性的神经再生生物标志物及其在指导康复过程中的应用产生了浓厚的兴趣。脑电图在这方面已成为一种有前途的工具，肌电图现在被视为记录外周神经支配和运动恢复的补充工具。脑电图和肌电图都是非侵入性测量，分别记录大脑和骨​​骼肌的电活动，因此可以立即获取有关神经功能的实时信息。此外，这些方法相当容易获得且价格低廉，这些特性有助于推动它们作为神经恢复的电生理测量和功能结果的预测测量. Given these disparities, strong interest has developed in the development of credible, non-invasive biomarkers of neural regeneration and their use in guiding processes of rehabilitation. EEG has emerged as a promising tool in this regard, and EMG is now seen as a supplementary tool for recording peripheral reinnervation and motor recovery. EEG and EMG are both non-invasive measures that register electrical activity in the brain and skeletal muscles, respectively, therefore granting immediate access to real-time information about neural functioning. Moreover, these approaches are fairly available and inexpensive, features which have helped fuel their use as electrophysiological measures of neurological recovery and predictive measures of functional outcome(Shigapova and Mukhamedshina, 2024).

脑电图本身可以直接测量大脑的电节律，并且可以反映受伤后恢复过程中的神经可塑性适应。定量脑电图测量与多种中枢神经系统损伤中的神经可塑性有关。例如，在 SCI 患者中，脑电图频谱功率变化（例如 α/θ 比率）与康复过程中运动和平衡功能的改善有关EEG itself measures the brain’s electrical rhythms directly and can mirror neuroplastic adaptations underlying recovery following injury. Quantitative EEG measures have been related to neuroplasticity within diverse CNS injuries. For instance, in patients with SCI, EEG spectral power changes (e.g., the alpha/theta ratio) have been related to locomotor and balance function improvement during a course of rehabilitation(Simis et al., 2021)。同样，中风后获得的皮质激活和连接的脑电图测量也与运动结果相关. Similarly, EEG measurements of cortical activation and connectivity obtained after stroke have also been related to motor outcomes(Delcamp et al., 2024)。脑电图在这些情况下有几个实际优势；它是无害的，床边状态就绪，并且能够记录长时间后的动态大脑网络重组。通过识别适应性可塑性的皮质标记，脑电图是中枢恢复的重要标记. EEG has several practical advantages within these scenarios; it is nonharmful, bedside status ready, and is capable of documenting dynamic brain network reorganization after extended time periods. By identifying cortical markers of adaptive plasticity, EEG is an important marker of central recovery(Bareham et al., 2018).

相比之下，肌电图可测量肌肉电活动，并为了解脊髓和周围神经的完整性提供了一个窗口EMG, by contrast, measures muscle electrical activity and offers a window into the integrity of the spinal and peripheral nerves(Sun et al., 2020)。对于周围神经损伤，肌电图是评估神经损伤严重程度和跟踪再生的支柱. For peripheral nerve injury, EMG is a pillar used to assess the severity of nerve injury and follow regeneration(107)。当再生轴突重新支配肌肉纤维时，肌电图可以检测到运动单位电位 (MUP) 的恢复，并且本质上记录通过侧支萌芽和轴突生长进行的重新支配. When regenerating axons reinnervate muscle fibers, EMG can pick up on the recovery of motor unit potentials (MUPs) and, essentially, document reinnervation by collateral sprouting and axonal growth(Lam et al., 2024)。对于伴有运动障碍的中枢神经系统损伤，肌电图可有利地跟踪随意肌功能的恢复. For CNS injuries with motor impairment, EMG is used advantageously to follow the return of voluntary muscle function(Sharma et al., 2023)。 EMG 已在 SCI 模型中用于测量恢复时恢复的运动功能. EMG has been utilized within models of SCI to measure restored locomotor function at recovery(Pilkar et al., 2020)。因此，虽然脑电图测量中枢神经可塑性，但肌电图测量效应器水平的外周肌肉激活，从而测量神经再生的最终产物. Accordingly, although EEG measures central neural plasticity, EMG measures peripheral muscle activation at the effector level and hence the end product of neural regeneration(Balbinot, 2022).

值得注意的是，一些神经生理学测试结合了脑电图和肌电图来评估通路的连接性和功能传递。一个经典的例子是诱发电位测试，其中使用脑电图从皮层记录体感诱发电位（SSEP），并使用目标肌肉的肌电图记录运动诱发电位（MEP）。这些测试分别客观地测量上升和下降路径的完整性。值得注意的是，SSEP 和 MEP 反应的改善已被证明与神经损伤后功能性运动恢复并行。例如，在不完全脊髓损伤后，SSEP 和 MEP 振幅随时间逐渐增强（以及潜伏期减少）与更好的运动和功能结果相关。这些观察结果表明，基于脑电图/肌电图的综合措施如何监测损伤连续过程中神经回路的恢复，从皮层信号起源到肌肉激活Notably, some neurophysiological tests combine EEG and EMG to evaluate the connectivity of pathways and functional transmission. A classic illustration is evoked potential testing, where somatosensory evoked potentials (SSEPs) are recorded from the cortex using EEG, and motor evoked potentials (MEPs) are recorded using EMG from target muscles. These tests objectively measure the integrity of ascending and descending pathways, respectively. Notably, improvements in SSEP and MEP responses have been shown to parallel functional motor recovery after neural injury. For instance, after an incomplete spinal cord lesion, progressive enhancement of SSEP and MEP amplitudes (and decrements in latencies) with time are associated with better locomotor and functional outcomes. Such observations show how EEG/EMG-based integrated measures can monitor the recovery of neural circuits along the injury continuum, from cortical signal origination to muscle activation(Li et al., 2021b).

2 CNS 和 PNS 的神经损伤和再生Nerve Injury and Regeneration in the CNS and PNS

PNS 和 CNS 的神经损伤在修复的生物学潜力以及我们如何进行功能恢复方面存在很大差异。最常见的是，由于抑制环境，中枢神经系统神经元在受伤后不会再生，例如，神经胶质疤痕和髓磷脂相关抑制剂实际上阻碍了大脑和脊髓内的轴突生长Nerve injuries to the PNS and CNS vary considerably in their biological potential for repair and in how we follow functional recovery. Most often, CNS neurons do not regenerate after being injured because of an inhibitory environment, for instance, glial scars and myelin-associated inhibitors actually retard axon growth inside the brain and spinal cord(Silver and Miller, 2004; Varadarajan et al., 2022)。相比之下，三七总皂甙拥有更有利的再生环境。周围神经损伤后，SC 会清除髓磷脂碎片并释放生长因子，引导轴突芽穿过损伤距离. By contrast, PNS possesses a more favorable milieu for regeneration. Following peripheral nerve injury, SCs remove myelin debris and release growth factors, directing axonal sprouts across the injury distance(Brosius Lutz et al., 2017; Deumens et al., 2010)。这些根本差异表明，中枢神经系统损伤通常会导致持久的缺陷，而三七总皂苷损伤有时可以在适当的条件下实现显着的神经支配和功能恢复。除了生物学机制之外，每个系统损伤和恢复的神经生理学生物标志物也存在差异. These fundamental differences suggest that CNS injuries often result in lasting deficits, whereas PNS injuries can, at times, achieve remarkable reinnervation and functional restitution under proper conditions. Beyond the biological mechanisms, there are also differences in the neurophysiological biomarkers of injury and recovery in each system(Pleasure, 1999; Kuffler and Foy, 2020)。脑电图已全面应用于探索中枢神经系统损伤和大脑活动变化. EEG has been comprehensively applied to explore CNS injuries and brain activity changes(Rayi and Murr, 2020; Feng et al., 2025)，而 EMG 代表了一种检查肌肉激活的配套方法，因此可以检查 PNS 和 CNS 损伤中的神经回路输出, while EMG represents a companion method to examine muscle activation and, therefore, neural circuitry output in both PNS and CNS injuries(Brambilla et al., 2021)。接下来，我们对比 PNS 和 CNS 损伤，强调它们的再生反应以及用于追踪神经和功能恢复的 EEG/EMG 测量之间的区别。. In what follows, we contrast PNS and CNS injuries, underscoring both distinctions between their regenerative response and also EEG/EMG measurements for tracing neurological and functional recovery.

2.1 中枢神经系统损伤与再生CNS Injury and Regeneration

在成人中枢神经系统中，创伤性损伤通常会引发一系列抑制轴突再生的事件。受损的中枢神经系统轴突表现出内在缺乏强大的再生能力，而中枢神经系统环境中的外在因素又加剧了这种情况In the adult CNS, traumatic injury commonly triggers a cascade of events that inhibits axonal regeneration. Damaged CNS axons exhibit an intrinsic lack of robust regenerative capacity, and this is compounded by extrinsic factors in the CNS environment(Huebner and Strittmatter, 2009; Shafqat et al., 2023)。此外，少突胶质细胞和髓磷脂碎片释放的髓磷脂相关抑制分子（如 Nogo、MAG 和 OMgp）会激活排斥信号机制，导致生长锥塌陷并抑制新的延伸. In addition, myelin-associated inhibitory molecules like Nogo, MAG, and OMgp that are released from oligodendrocytes and myelin debris activate repulsive signaling mechanisms that result in growth cone collapse and inhibit new extension(Chambel and Cruz, 2023; McKerracher and Rosen, 2015)。总的来说，这些内在和外在的障碍创造了一个高度抑制的环境，导致中枢神经系统的再生能力受到限制。. Taken together, these intrinsic and extrinsic barriers create a highly inhibitory environment that is responsible for the restricted regenerative capacity of the CNS.

因此，病变上方和下方的神经元通常无法重新建立连接，从而表现出慢性神经缺陷As a result, neurons above and below the lesion are often unable to re-establish connections and thus exhibit chronic neurological deficits(Fink and Cafferty, 2016; Kvistad et al., 2024)。相比之下，被切断的周围神经会经历华勒变性和碎片清理，然后是支持生长的 SC 形成邦纳带以指导轴突生长. By contrast, peripheral nerves that have been severed experience Wallerian degeneration and cleaning up of debris, followed by growth-supportive SC forming Bungner bands to direct growing axons(Stoll and Müller, 1999; Panzer et al., 2020)。中枢神经系统中没有类似的支持过程，事实上，中枢神经系统髓磷脂和疤痕组织实际上阻碍了再生. There is no similar supportive process in the CNS, in fact, CNS myelin and scar tissue actually retard regeneration(Gaudet et al., 2011; Fu et al., 2025)。因此，从生物学的角度来看，中枢神经系统损伤通常是永久性的，而三七总皂苷损伤至少有解剖和功能恢复的机会. Thus, from a biological standpoint, CNS injuries are generally permanent, whereas PNS injuries have at least a chance of anatomical and functional recovery(Balakrishnan et al., 2021).

尽管成人中枢神经系统的再生能力有限，但损伤后仍可能发生显着的神经可塑性。幸存的回路经常会重组，相邻的皮质区域可以承担受损区域的功能。同时，可以加强备用通路，例如皮质脊髓束、网状脊髓束或本体脊髓束，以补偿失去的连接Despite the limited regenerative capacity of the adult CNS, significant neuroplasticity can occur after the injury. Surviving circuits often restructure, and adjacent cortical areas can assume the functions of damaged regions. Meanwhile, spared pathways, such as the corticospinal, reticulospinal, or propriospinal tracts, can be reinforced to compensate for lost connections(Filipp et al., 2019; Gao et al., 2022)。脑电图可以对皮质重组进行无创监测，如中风或 SCI 患者运动任务期间振荡模式的改变（例如 α 节律减慢和 β 带活动改变）、功能连接中断以及非典型事件相关电位。. EEG enables non-invasive monitoring of cortical reorganization, as indicated by altered oscillation patterns (such as slowed alpha rhythms and altered beta-band activity), disrupted functional connectivity, and atypical event-related potentials during motor tasks in stroke or SCI patients(Lacerda et al., 2024; Li et al., 2020)。脑电图用于监测运动康复期间的皮质-皮质连接性和皮质-肌肉一致性，发现随着患者恢复运动，特定的脑电图指标会发生变化. EEG was used to monitor cortico-cortical connectivity and cortico-muscular coherence during motor rehabilitation, finding that specific EEG metrics change as patients recover movement(Krauth et al., 2019)。此类脑电图生物标志物为大脑重组以及中枢神经系统通路的完整性提供了非侵入性的视角。. Such EEG biomarkers present a non-invasive perspective on the reorganization of the brain as well as the integrity of the CNS pathways.

作为脑电图的补充，肌电图可以提供对中枢神经系统损伤后运动输出能力的重要洞察。上运动神经元病变（节段以上），周围神经和肌肉没有直接损伤；因此，与 PNS 损伤不同，静息时的肌电图通常不显示去神经电位As a complement to EEG, EMG provides crucial insight into the motor output capacity after a CNS injury. In upper motor neuron lesions (above the segmental level), the peripheral nerves and muscles are not directly injured; hence, EMG at rest typically shows no denervation potentials, unlike in PNS injuries(Benecke et al., 1983)。相反，肌电图上纯中枢神经系统损伤的标志是运动单位的自主募集受损。在完整的 SCI 中（没有下降信号），病变下方的 EMG 记录中缺少自愿 MUP。然而，相比之下，在不完全损伤中，某些 MUP 仍然可以被唤起，通常数量较少，但具有正常的配置和幅度，表明部分完整的通路. Instead, the hallmark of a pure CNS lesion on EMG is impaired voluntary recruitment of motor units. In a complete SCI (with no descending signals), voluntary MUPs are missing on EMG recordings below the lesion. In contrast, in incomplete injuries, however, certain MUPs remain evocable, frequently at lower numbers but with normal configuration and amplitude, indicating partly intact pathways(Robinson, 2022)。因此，中枢神经系统病变下方肌肉中任何意志肌电图活动的存在是残余下行运动束连接的重要指标。事实上，敏感的肌电图记录已经揭示了一些被诊断为临床完全性 SCI 的患者中“隐藏的”残余自愿输出。在一项研究中，表面肌电图显示，大多数慢性运动完全性 SCI 受试者在尝试运动时会出现微妙但一致的肌肉激活，这表明在许多情况下存在损伤以下的意志肌肉活动. Therefore, the presence of any volitional EMG activity in muscles below a CNS lesion is an important indicator of residual descending motor tract connectivity. Indeed, sensitive EMG recordings have revealed "hidden" residual voluntary output in some patients diagnosed as clinically complete SCI. In one study, surface EMG revealed subtle but consistent muscle activations in a majority of chronic motor-complete SCI subjects when they attempted movements, indicating that below-injury volitional muscle activity was present in many cases(Heald et al., 2017)。这些发现证明了肌电图如何揭示临床检查中未见的剩余运动单位和下降信号。. These findings demonstrate how EMG can reveal spared motor units and descending signals not seen in clinical exams.

肌电图是确定下行通路完整性的重要工具。通过经颅磁刺激，可以诱发 MEP 并在肌电图上进行验证，甚至可以通过脊柱病变提供皮质脊髓传导的证据。在完全瘫痪的患者中，MEP 主要出现在发生痉挛的个体中，这表明与痉挛活动相关的下行通路得以保留EMG is a valuable tool for determining the integrity of the descending pathways. Via transcranial magnetic stimulation, MEPs may be evoked and verified on EMG, providing evidence of corticospinal conduction even through spinal lesions. In those with complete paralysis, MEPs mainly appear in individuals who develop spasticity, suggesting preserved descending pathways linked to spastic activity(Sangari et al., 2019)。这意味着，当肌电图记录中枢神经系统病变下方的肌肉活动（自愿或诱发）时，它表明运动轴突保留并可作为积极的预后标志，因为自愿肌电图在检测 SCI 后保留的运动功能方面高度敏感. This implies that when EMG records muscle activity (voluntary or evoked) below a CNS lesion, it indicates sparing of motor axons and serves as a positive prognostic marker, as voluntary EMG is highly sensitive in detecting preserved motor function post‑SCI(Santamaria et al., 2021)。此外，肌电图可以表征异常激活模式，例如不自主痉挛和经常伴随痉挛的异常收缩. Moreover, EMG can characterize abnormal activation patterns, such as involuntary spasms and abnormal co‑contractions that frequently accompany spasticity(Balbinot, 2022; Sheng et al., 2022)。脑电图反映大脑功能，而肌电图通过神经信号监测肌肉激活，通过显示皮质变化和运动执行状态在中枢神经系统损伤中相互补充，从而揭示下行通路的完整性. While EEG reflects brain function, EMG monitors muscle activation from neural signals, complementing each other in CNS injuries by showing cortical changes and motor execution status, thus revealing the integrity of descending pathways(Debenham et al., 2024).

2.2 PNS 损伤与再生PNS Injury and Regeneration

与中枢神经系统损伤相比，周围神经损伤（如臂丛神经牵拉、神经撕裂或压迫性神经病）具有非常不同的愈合反应和恢复情况。当远端神经回路（神经内管）得到维持时，PNS 轴突以每天 1-3 毫米的速度再生Peripheral nerve injuries (such as brachial plexus traction, nerve lacerations, or compression neuropathies) have a very different healing response and recovery profile compared to CNS injuries. The PNS axons regenerate at the rate of 1-3mm/day when distal nerve circuits (endoneurial tubes) are maintained(Carvalho et al., 2019; Sulaiman and Gordon, 2013)。周围神经损伤后，远端节段发生华勒变性. After peripheral nerve damage, the distal segment experiences Wallerian degeneration(Rotshenker, 2011)。在此过程中，轴突分解，产生的髓磷脂被吞噬细胞去除。 SC 去分化、增殖、在神经内管内排列（形成 Büngner 带）. In this process, the axon disintegrates, and the resulting myelin is removed by phagocytes. SC dedifferentiate, proliferate, line up inside endoneurial tubes (to form Bands of Büngner)(Nevmerzhytska et al., 2025)，并产生生长因子分子（例如 NGF、BDNF）来引导轴突生长, and produce growth factor molecules (e.g., NGF, BDNF) to guide growing axons(Rotshenker, 2011; Nevmerzhytska et al., 2025)。如果神经残端之间的间隙不太大（或通过手术桥接），来自近端残端的轴突芽可以进入远端雪旺细胞通道，并最终重新支配目标肌肉和感觉受体. If the gap between nerve stumps is not too large (or is surgically bridged), axonal sprouts from the proximal stump can enter the distal Schwann cell channels and eventually reinnervate target muscles and sensory receptors(Sulaiman and Gordon, 2013)。然而，由于再生轴突的误导，功能恢复并不完全. Functional recovery is not complete, however, due to misdirection of regenerating axons(Nguyen et al., 2002), 运动终板退化, degeneration of the motor endplates(Huang et al., 2022)和去神经萎缩, and denervation atrophy(Carlson, 2014)尽管存在这些情况，PNS 神经元固有的再生能力和有利的细胞外环境意味着，与 CNS 不同，在许多周围神经损伤后存在有用的部分恢复的真正潜力Despite these circumstances, the inherent regenerative capability of PNS neurons and favorable extracellular environment mean that, unlike in the CNS, there is genuine potential for useful partial recovery following numerous peripheral nerve injuries(Grinsell and Keating, 2014; Gordon, 2020).

电生理方法在周围神经再生的发展中发挥着重要作用。虽然 PNS 损伤发生在大脑外部，但在这些情况下基于脑电图的方法并不那么重要；然而，在某些情况下，脑电图可以发挥作用。例如，临床医生可以利用 SEP 来评估修复后感觉通路的恢复情况。通过电刺激受伤的神经并记录体感皮层的脑电图反应，皮层诱发电位的再现表明病变部位的感觉纤维成功再生Electrophysiologic methods play an important role in the development of peripheral nerve regeneration. While PNS injuries are outside of the brain, EEG-based methods are less important in these cases; however, there are instances where EEG can play a role. For example, SEPs can be utilized by the clinician to evaluate the recovery of the sensory pathway after repair. By electrically stimulating the injured nerve and recording EEG responses over the somatosensory cortex, the reappearance of cortical evoked potentials indicates the successful regeneration of sensory fibers across the lesion(Synek, 1987)。由于中枢放大，皮质 SEP 出现在周围神经反应之前. Cortical SEPs appear before peripheral nerve responses due to central amplification(Eisen, 1988)。因此，EEG SEP 测试可以确认传入信号早期进入大脑，表明在外周结果变得可见之前 PNS 中的生理重新连接. Accordingly, EEG SEP testing can confirm early access of afferent signals to the brain, indicating physiological reconnection in the PNS before peripheral outcomes become visible(Synek, 1987)。脑电图通过运动皮层映射变化或不同的运动想象信号表明慢性外周损伤后的皮层重组，中枢可塑性具有次要意义. EEG indicates cortical reorganization following chronic peripheral injury via motor cortex mapping changes or different motor imagery signals, and central plasticity is of secondary significance(Amini et al., 2018; Li et al., 2021a)。肌肉的神经支配和恢复的肌电图模式，例如从小的新生运动单位到稳定的多相 MUP 的进展，直接反映了神经的外周恢复情况. EMG patterns of reinnervation and recovery of the muscle, such as the progression from small nascent motor units to stable polyphasic MUPs, reflect the peripheral restoration of the nerve directly(Krarup et al., 2016).

严重的三七总皂甙损伤（轴索断裂或神经断裂）后早期，肌电图会出现一系列特征性变化。在受伤后的前1-2周内，由于运动终板残留的乙酰胆碱和沃勒变性的延迟，运动神经和运动纤维可能仍会传导一些冲动，因此肌电图和神经传导研究最初可能显示很少或没有变化Early after a severe PNS injury (axonotmesis or neurotmesis), there is a characteristic sequence of EMG changes. In the first 1-2 weeks following injury, the motor nerve and motor fibers may still conduct a few impulses due to the remaining acetylcholine at the motor endplate and the delay of Wallerian degeneration, so EMG and nerve conduction studies may show little or no change initially(Panzer et al., 2020; Chaney and Nadi, 2020)。受伤后 2-3 周，在华勒变性完成且肌肉已去神经支配后，肌电图通常显示出强烈的急性去神经支配模式，并且相应的肌肉在休息时表现出自发性颤动电位和正向锐波，表明肌纤维已失去神经支配。这些自发电位通常在轴突变性到达运动终板时出现，并且是电诊断过程中公认的去神经支配标志物. 2-3 weeks following injury, after Wallerian degeneration is completed and the muscles have been denervated, the EMG typically reveals strong patterns of acute denervation, and the respective muscles demonstrate spontaneous fibrillation potentials and positive sharp waves on rest, indicating that the muscle fibrils have lost innervation. These spontaneous potentials usually appear once axonal degeneration reaches the motor end plate and are well-recognized markers of denervation during the electrodiagnostics(Ginsberg et al., 2020; Stålberg et al., 2019)。完全去神经的肌肉中最初不存在 MUP，而在部分损伤中，募集明显减少。该肌电图提供了轴突丢失的早期确认，对于区分神经失用症（无法区分纤维颤动）和轴突断裂或神经断裂（需要轴突再生）的快速恢复至关重要. There are initially no MUPs present within completely denervated muscles, whereas in partial injuries, recruitment is markedly reduced. This EMG profile provides early confirmation of axonal loss and is central to distinguishing neuropraxia where no fibrillations can be distinguished and rapid recovery is achieved, from axonotmesis or neurotmesis, where regrowth of the axons is required(Kamble et al., 2019; Preston and Shapiro, 2012).

这些特征反映了早期的神经支配，每个多相 MUP 都是一个由萌芽轴突重新支配的运动单位，但具有不成熟的神经肌肉接头和重塑神经末梢，导致波形的变异性These features reflect early reinnervation, and every polyphasic MUP is a motor unit that is reinnervated by a sprouting axon, but with immature neuromuscular junctions and remodeling nerve terminals, leading to variability in the waveform(Kamble et al., 2019)。随着时间的推移，随着成熟的再生轴突在肌纤维上实现更稳定的突触连接，MUP 发生变化：宽度扩大，持续时间变长，并且随着运动单元恢复更正常的状态，波形变得不那么多相（更稳定）。例如，在最初的几个月里，人们可能会在恢复的肌肉中捕获到越来越大的幅度和越来越长的持续时间的 MUP，并反映出再生的轴突重新支配了更多的纤维并且神经肌肉传输效率增加了这一事实。 EMG 的这种连续体，从自愿 MUP 的丢失到小型多相新生单元的出现，最终到更大更稳定的运动单元，提供了周围神经再生的体内电生理时间表。连续肌电图和神经传导研究为临床 PNS 损伤提供了相关的预后信息。肌电图上神经支配潜力的开始提供了恢复的早期证据，通常在明显的肌肉收缩之前几周。例如，肌电图可能会在患者体验到明显的自愿力量之前就显示出由侧支萌芽产生的新生多相 MUP. Over time, as the maturing regenerating axons achieve more stable synaptic connections on muscle fibers, the MUPs change: widths expand, durations become longer, and waveforms become less polyphasic (more stable) as the motor units regain a more normal state. For instance, in the first few months, one may capture increasingly larger-amplitude and longer-duration MUPs in a recovering muscle and reflect the fact that the regenerating axons have reinnervated more fibers and neuromuscular transmission effectiveness has increased. This continuum on EMG, from lost voluntary MUPs to the onset of small polyphasic nascent units and ultimately to larger more stable motor units, offers an in vivo electrophysiological timetable of peripheral nerve regeneration. Serial EMG and nerve conduction studies offer pertinent prognostic information in PNS injuries clinically. The onset of reinnervation potentials on EMG offers early evidence of recovery often preceding noticeable muscle contraction by several weeks. For instance, EMG may demonstrate nascent polyphasic MUPs produced by collateral sprouting well before patients experience discernible voluntary strength(Stålberg et al., 2019; Kamble et al., 2019; Preston and Shapiro, 2012)。如果几个月后肌电图上没有出现 MUP，则表明再生失败或神经间隙过大，可能需要进行神经移植等手术修复. When no arising MUPs appear on EMG after several months, failed regeneration or too great a nerve gap exists and surgical repair such as nerve grafting may be necessary(Sulaiman and Gordon, 2013; Kimura and Strakowski, 2025)。肌电图还通过评估运动单位形态和募集模式来区分神经病性和肌病性恢复. EMG also differentiates neuropathic and myopathic recovery by evaluating motor unit morphology and recruitment patterns(Campbell and DeJong, 2005)。此外，肌电图可以测量神经肌肉传输的质量：不成熟的神经支配运动单位最初表现出抖动和变异性，但随着时间的推移，随着突触的成熟，干扰模式变得稳定. Additionally, EMG can measure the quality of neuromuscular transmission: immature reinnervated motor units display jitter and variability at first, however over time interference patterns become stabilized as synapses mature(Stålberg et al., 2019)。总体而言，肌电图提供了周围损伤中运动单位恢复的直接证据，并始终作为结合临床检查观察神经再生的金标准. Overall, EMG offers direct evidence of the recovery of motor units in peripheral injuries and persists as the gold standard for the observation of nerve regeneration in conjunction with the clinical examination(Kimura and Strakowski, 2025).

请记住，脑电图主要揭示大脑中发生的情况，而肌电图则揭示肌肉中发生的情况。它们共同提供了神经损伤恢复的全貌Keep in mind that EEG primarily reveals what occurs in the brain, and the EMG reveals what occurs in the muscle. Together, they offer the whole picture of nerve injury recovery(Makeig et al., 2004)。这对于 PNS 来说尤其如此：大脑可能想要移动（典型的脑电图活动表示想要移动），但只有当周围神经纤维再次开始生长时，肌肉中的肌电图才会表现出真正的运动. This holds particularly for the PNS: the brain may desire to move (typical EEG activity for the desire to move), but only when peripheral nerve fibers begin growing again will the EMG in the muscle demonstrate genuine movement(Farina et al., 2014)。脑电图和肌电图在监测肌肉恢复时相互补充，一个识别大脑的信号，另一个验证肌肉的反应。正是出于这个原因，更多的神经康复研究结合了脑电图和肌电图记录。例如，同时检查脑电图和肌电图可以量化受伤后大脑控制肌肉的程度。该综合措施被提议作为中风或脊髓损伤患者运动功能恢复的良好指标. The EEG and the EMG complement each other when monitoring recovery in the muscles, one identifies the brain’s signals, and the other verifies the muscles’ response. More research in neurorehabilitation incorporates both EEG and EMG recordings precisely for this reason. For instance, examination of EEG and EMG concurrently may quantify the degree to which the brain may control muscles following injury. The combined measure has been proposed as a good indicator of the recovery of motor function in individuals who’ve had strokes or spinal cord injuries(van Wijk et al., 2012; Mima and Hallett, 1999; Grosse et al., 2002)。表. 3 中枢神经系统损伤后脑电图/肌电图的变化EEG/EMG Changes Following CNS Injury

中枢神经系统创伤（例如中风、创伤性脑损伤 (TBI) 或 SCI）后，脑电图描记显示出独特的干扰。此类损伤的一个共同特征是背景节奏减慢。作为一个典型的例子，TBI 患者通常会在创伤后不久出现后主导节律减慢和弥漫性 θ 活动增强。同样，在中风或脑病的情况下，弥漫性 θ 和 δ 减慢为整体脑功能障碍提供了证据Following CNS trauma, such as stroke, traumatic brain injury (TBI), or SCI, EEG tracings demonstrate unique disturbances. One common feature of such injuries is the slowing of the background rhythms. As a case in point, TBI patients will often present a deceleration of the posterior dominant rhythm and enhancement of diffuse theta activity shortly after the trauma. Similarly, in the case of stroke or encephalopathy, diffuse theta and delta slowing provide evidence for global cerebral dysfunction(136; 72)。缺血性中风后，脑电图几乎总是显示出主导 α 节律（8-12 Hz）减少，受损区域出现缓慢的 δ 和 θ 活动，表明皮质出现急性功能障碍. Following ischemic stroke, the electroencephalogram almost invariably demonstrates a decrease in the dominant alpha rhythm (8-12 Hz) and the appearance of slow delta and theta activity in the damaged areas, indicating acute dysfunction in the cortex(Peterson et al., 2025)。这些慢波缺陷反映了皮质功能障碍和受损大脑中的神经回路受损. These slow-wave defects reflect dysfunction in the cortex and impaired neural circuitry in the damaged brain(Fanciullacci et al., 2017)。支持上述发现的定量脑电图测量通常表明，慢波增强的增强和快波功率的降低预示着不良的临床结果. Supporting the above findings, quantitative EEG measures routinely demonstrate that increased enhancement of slow waves and reduced power in the fast waves predict adverse clinical outcomes(Ajčević et al., 2021; Sutcliffe et al., 2022)。严重的病例确实表现出局灶性脑损伤和相应的局部脑电图抑制或半球不对称导致的局部减慢. Severe cases indeed demonstrate focal brain lesions and corresponding localized EEG suppressing or localized slowing by asymmetry of the hemispheres(52).

除了光谱减慢之外，中枢神经系统病变也会损害大脑网络的动态。急性中风等局灶性病变会引起病变部位外的整体功能连接改变Spectral slowing apart, the dynamics of brain networks are also impaired by CNS lesions. Focal lesion such as acute stroke induce global functional connectivity alterations outside the lesion site(Alia et al., 2017)。急性中风脑电图功能网络连接不紧密，集成度较低：与对侧半球相比，同侧半球显示出较低的 α 和 β 波段连接性. Acute stroke EEG functional networks less intensely connected become less integrated: the ipsilesional hemisphere shows lower alpha- and beta-band connectivity as compared to the contralesional hemisphere(Zhang et al., 2024)。尽管脑组织完好无损，但 SCI（中枢神经系统损伤）也会引起皮质脑电图变化。慢性 SCI 患者表现出脑电图 α 功率降低和峰值频率降低，而 β 功率却相反增加。 SCI 中上升的感觉输出损失和下降的运动输出损失通常也会导致脑电图上 SSEP 的缺失或转变. SCI (lesion of a tract in the CNS) also induces cortical EEG changes despite the intactness of the brain tissue. Chronic SCI patients have exhibited decreased EEG alpha power and lower peak frequency and paradoxically increased beta power. Ascending sensory and descending motor output loss in SCI also commonly result in absent or transformed SSEPs on EEG(Doruk et al., 2017).

肌电图提供了有关中枢神经系统损伤（包括中风、TBI 和 SCI）运动通路破坏导致的外周表现的宝贵信息。在急性中风中，即使病变仅在大脑中，针状肌电图也能显示出瘫痪肌肉下运动神经元功能障碍的证据。例如，约 40% 的患者表现出去神经电位，其特征是存在正向锐波和颤动，以及中风后 72 小时肌电图上运动单位的异常募集模式。这些去神经支配指数证实，严重中风确实会诱发次级运动神经元或废用性萎缩，并与发病时较大的缺陷和虚弱相关EMG provides valuable information regarding the peripheral manifestation resulting from the disruption of CNS lesions, including stroke, TBI, and SCI, on the motor pathways. In acute strokes, needle EMG characteristically shows evidence for lower motor neuron dysfunction in the paretic muscles even when the lesion is only in the brain. For example, about 40% of the patients show denervation potentials, marked by the presence of positive sharp waves and fibrillations, along with abnormal recruitment patterns in the motor units on EMG at 72 hours after stroke. Such indices of denervation confirm that severe strokes do indeed induce secondary motor neuron or disuse atrophy and are associated with larger deficits and weakness at onset(Bitencourt et al., 2022)。在 SCI 中，即使肌肉的明显明显运动不明显，例如，肌肉在 MMT 上评分为零，高灵敏度表面肌电图有时也会发现大脑下行控制输入的中断。这些证据主张使用肌电图来测量不完全损伤组织的状态. In SCI, disruption of the descending control inputs by the brain may at times be unearthed by high-sensitivity surface EMG even when frank overt movement by the muscle is not evident, for instance, muscles graded as zero on the MMT. Such evidence advocates the utility using EMG to gauge the status of incompletely injured tissues(Balbinot, 2022; Heald et al., 2017)。最后，通过 EMG 观察到，慢性 SCI 会导致肌肉特性发生典型改变：病变水平以下的长期废用会导致肌肉萎缩、纤维构成改变以及被动僵硬增加. Finally, chronic SCI causes typical alterations in muscle properties as observed through EMG: chronic disuse below the lesion level causes muscle atrophy and alterations in the makeup of the fibers and increased passive stiffening(Shields and Dudley-Javoroski, 2007; Gorgey and Dudley, 2007)。临床上，前者表现为肌电图-力关系异常以及反射引起的反射兴奋性（痉挛）增加。. Clinically, the former is manifested by the abnormal EMG-force relations and increased reflex excitability (spasticity) by the reflexes(Grosse et al., 2002)。总体而言，在中枢神经系统受损的情况下，肌电图通过解释中枢驱动丧失后产生的外周表现，为脑电图提供了额外的信息，包括严重中风中观察到的急性去神经支配，以及脊髓损伤中残留和长期持续的神经肌肉适应的记录。. Overall, EMG provides additional information to the EEG in the case of the injured CNS by accounting for the peripheral manifestation resulting following the loss of the central drive ranging through the acute denervation observed in severe strokes to the documentation of residual and long-term persistent neuromuscular adaptation in SCI(Heald et al., 2017; Shields and Dudley-Javoroski, 2007).

4 PNS 损伤后脑电图/肌电图的变化EEG/EMG Changes Following PNS Injury

与 CNS 病变相反，单纯的 PNS 病变（例如周围神经横断或神经病变）通常不会引起脑电图观察到的脑节律弥漫性减慢。发生这种情况是因为完整且功能正常的大脑没有表现出直接的皮质破坏来释放异常的 delta/theta 波In contrast to CNS lesions, pure PNS lesions (e.g., peripheral nerve transections or neuropathies) do not usually induce the diffuse slowening of brain rhythms observed on EEG. This occurs because the intact and functioning brain proper shows no direct cortical destruction to release anomalous delta/theta waves(Nuwer, 1997)。相反，PNS 创伤主要表现为外周电生理学，其中神经传导研究和肌电图提供了轴突完整性和神经支配最敏感的指标。周围神经损伤仍然依赖于神经传导研究（NCS）和肌电图作为最重要的诊断工具。完全横断的周围神经在远端肌肉不会产生诱发反应，并且表现出感觉神经动作电位缺失. Rather, PNS trauma mainly presents in peripheral electrophysiology, in which nerve conduction studies and EMG offer the most sensitive indices of axonal integrity and reinnervation. Peripheral nerve injury still rests and relies on nerve conduction studies (NCS) and EMG as the most important tools for diagnosis. A peripheral nerve that is completely transected yields no evoked response at the distal muscles and exhibits absent sensory nerve action potentials(Kimura and Strakowski, 2025; Preston and Shapiro, 2020).

在针 EMG 上，受伤后 1-2 周，失去神经支配的肌肉中出现去神经变化，例如颤动电位的存在和正尖波，证实运动轴突的丧失，与具有外周表达的 CNS 损伤后的去神经模式相似On needle EMG, by 1-2 weeks after the injury, denervation changes such as the presence of fibrillation potentials and positive sharp waves appear in muscles deprived of innervation, confirming the loss of motor axons, similar appearing to denervation patterns following CNS lesions with peripheral expression(Kamble et al., 2019; Dumitru et al., 2002).

几个月后，无论是通过轴突生长还是侧枝发芽，肌电图上都显示出成功的神经支配，通过新产生的神经肌肉链接的存在和自愿运动单位募集的逐渐增加，形成多相 MUP。By months, successful reinnervation, whether by axonal growth or collateral sprouting, is revealed on EMG by the development of polyphasic MUPs by the presence of newly created neuromuscular links and gradual voluntary motor unit recruitment increase by months(Kimura and Strakowski, 2025).

有趣的是，纯粹的外周病变的中心后果是有限的。然而，外周重组和可塑性可能通过改变感觉输入而产生，例如，在肢体横断或神经损伤后，体感皮层可能会与生长到传入耳聋区域的相邻身体区域重新映射Of interest, the central consequences of a pure peripheral lesion are restricted. However, peripheral reorganization and plasticity may arise through changing sensory inputs, e.g., following limb transection or nerve lesioning, the somatosensory cortices may become remapped with adjacent body territories growing into the deafferented region(Li et al., 2021a)。在临床和实验领域，SSEP 用于跟踪此类改变。长期以来，人们观察到的一个经典发现是，严重的外周病变或整个脊髓的横断通过切断上行通路来抑制病变水平以下的 SEP 信号. In the clinical and experimental realms, SSEPs are employed to follow such alterations. It has long been observed as a classic finding that severe peripheral lesions or transections of the whole spinal cord suppress SEP signals below the lesion level by cutting ascending pathways(Nordmark and Johansson, 2020)。在临床上完全脊髓损伤的个体中，SSEP 的缺失通常可靠地代表上行传入通路的破坏，而保留或早期终止的 SEP 则预示着功能性感觉纤维的存在以及更好的神经功能恢复和可能的恢复. In individuals presenting clinically complete spinal cord injuries, the absence of SSEPs conventionally and reliably represents disruption of ascending afferent pathways and contrasts when preserved or early-terminating SEPs predict the presence of functioning sensory fibers and better neurological recovery and possible recovery(Kim et al., 2014; Chrysanthakopoulou et al., 2025)。对于部分周围神经病变，保留的 SEP 是典型的，但表现出延迟延迟或幅度减小，并表明传导缓慢或功能纤维数量减少. For partial peripheral nerve lesions, preserved SEPs are typical but demonstrate delayed latencies or diminished amplitude and indicate slow conducting or diminished numbers of functioning fibers(Kamble et al., 2019)。除了诱发反应之外，PNS 创伤还可以引起长期的皮质适应，例如，个体可能更多地依赖视觉反馈或对侧肢体控制，并且 FMRI 和脑电图连接性研究检测到细微的感觉运动网络变化，而不是总体损伤. Beyond evoked responses, PNS trauma can furthermore induce long-term cortical adaptations, e.g., individuals may depend more upon visual feedback or contralateral limb control, and FMRIs and EEG studies of connectivity detect nuanced sensorimotor network shifts and not gross impairments(Li et al., 2021a; Nordmark and Johansson, 2020)。重要的是，PNS 病变不会引起明显的整体脑电图减慢或中枢神经系统病变中通常观察到的关联之间的广泛分离；相反，PNS 损伤的电生理学特征在于局部信号丢失和肌电图证据，表明去神经和神经支配以及中枢大脑网络除了细微的重组外仍保持完整. Importantly, PNS lesions do not induce the notable global EEG slowing or widespread dissociation among the associations usually observed in the context of CNS lesions; rather, the electrophysiological signature of PNS lesions resides in localized signal loss and EMG evidence for denervation and reinnervation and central brain networks remaining intact apart from nuanced reorganization(Preston and Shapiro, 2020; Nuwer, 1997)。这种差异强调了这样一个事实：弥漫性 EEG/BOLD 损伤是中枢损伤的特征，而外周病变最充分地由局部外周电生理学改变来定义。. This difference emphasizes the fact that diffuse EEG/BOLD impairments are the signatures of central injuries and whereas peripheral lesions are most adequately defined by local peripheral electrophysiological alterations.

5 CNS 和 PNS 再生过程中的 EEG 和 EMG 生物标志物EEG and EMG Biomarkers During CNS and PNS Regeneration

在中枢神经系统修复过程中，例如中风或脊髓损伤后，脑电图测量显示出反映功能恢复的典型变化。一项可靠的发现是频谱平衡偏移，其特征是病理性慢波活动减少以及 α 和 β 波段节律相对增强，而运动功能有助于并改善During CNS repair, such as following stroke or SCI, EEG measures show typical alterations that mirror functional recovery. One reliable finding is spectral balance shift, characterized by decreased pathological slow-wave activity and relative enhancement of the alpha- and beta-band rhythms, while motor function contributes and improves(Wu et al., 2015)。此外，当患者恢复随意运动时，感觉运动皮层的 mu（8-12 Hz）和 beta（13-30 Hz）频段的事件相关去同步（ERD）期间与运动相关的脑电图动态变得更大. Moreover, movement-related EEG dynamics during event-related desynchronization (ERD) in the mu (8-12 Hz) and beta (13-30 Hz) bands across the sensorimotor cortex become greater when patients recover voluntary motion(Pichiorri et al., 2015)。这种脑电图改变反映了内在的皮质重组：更大的ERD/ERS反应强度反映了受损感觉运动皮层更大的重新激活，而更好的半球间β相干性反映了运动区域之间更多生理振荡协调的恢复. Such EEG alterations mirror intrinsic cortical reorganization: greater ERD/ERS response strength reflects greater reactivation of the lesioned sensorimotor cortex while better interhemispheric beta coherence reflects recovery of more physiological oscillatory coordination among motor areas(Wu et al., 2015; Bönstrup et al., 2018)。作为脑电图测量的补充，肌电图提供了与中枢重组逐步对应的神经肌肉恢复的直接证据。通过表面肌电图测量，可以追踪瘫痪肌肉运动单位活动的重新出现和增强。中风后，运动尝试过程中肌电图振幅和复杂性逐渐增加，这表明运动单位的募集和放电率正在恢复，并反映了患者自主收缩能力和神经肌肉协调能力的提高。请注意，通过肌电图导出的测量值（例如振幅和运动单位复杂性），肌肉力量增益和功能性运动恢复之间存在精确的相关性. Supplementing such EEG measures, EMG offers direct evidence of neuromuscular recovery in stepwise correspondence with central reorganization. By surface EMG measures, the re-emergence and enhancement of motor unit activity in paretic muscles can be tracked. Following stroke, gradual EMG amplitude and complexity increases during movement attempts signify that recruitment and discharge rates of motor units are recovering and reflect the patient’s improving capacity for voluntary contraction and finer neuromuscular coordination. Note that by EMG-derived measures such as amplitude and motor unit complexity precise correlations exist across muscle strength gains and functional motor recovery(Li et al., 2021a; Al-Ayyad et al., 2023; Stefanovic et al., 2022)。在 SCI 中，肌电图测量足够精细，即使无法观察到明显的运动，也能辨别残余或重新激活的肌肉激活，因此可能为病变下方运动通路的亚临床重新激活提供证据。在康复过程中，看似潜在的肌肉可能会开始通过小的早期 MUP 表现出 EMG 活动。这代表了在出现明显运动之前神经支配和皮质脊髓重新连接的初步证据. In SCI, EMG measures are delicate enough to discern residual or re-activating muscle activation even when overt movement cannot be observed and may consequently provide evidence for subclinical re-activation of motor pathways below the lesion. In the process of rehabilitation, muscles that appear latent may begin to demonstrate EMG activity by small, early MUPs. This represents the preliminary evidence of reinnervation and corticospinal reconnection prior to the development of noticeable movement(Balbinot, 2022; Heald et al., 2017; Shields and Dudley-Javoroski, 2007).

联合脑电图-肌电图分析提供了有价值的指示，揭示了神经恢复的中枢和外周方面。一项广泛使用的指标是皮质肌肉一致性 (CMC)，它检查脑电波 (EEG) 和肌肉活动 (EMG) 协作的程度。中风等脑损伤后，CMC（尤其是 β 范围）急剧下降，显示大脑和脊髓之间的关联较弱。但在恢复和再训练的过程中，CMC值逐渐增加，表明运动皮层与肌肉的关联性改善Joint EEG-EMG analyses provide valuable indications that reveal both central and peripheral aspects of nerve recovery. One widespread indicator is cortico-muscular coherence (CMC), which examines the degree to which brain waves (EEG) and muscle activities (EMG) collaborate. Following brain lesions such as a stroke, CMC, particularly the beta range, is sharply decreased, displaying weak associations among the brain and spinal cord. But during the process of recovery and retraining, the values of CMC gradually increase, indicating that the association among the motor cortex and the muscles improves(Krauth et al., 2019; Mima and Hallett, 1999)。关于中风后恢复的早期研究表明，手腕运动期间的脑电图-肌电图一致性在开始时较低，然后随着运动能力的增强而上升，有时达到或超过对照中观察到的值. Early research on recovery following a stroke revealed that EEG-EMG coherence during the movement at the wrist was low at the beginning and then rose as motor abilities grew and sometimes reached or exceeded values observed in controls(Krauth et al., 2019; Pichiorri et al., 2023; Mima et al., 2001)。这些发现证实了这样的假设：肌肉和大脑之间更大的联系是神经重组的重要指标。当执行运动任务时，同步的脑电图和肌电图反应是明显的。例如，在随意运动期间，明显的脑电图不同步与肌电图爆发同时发生，这表明肌肉输出和大脑激活在恢复系统中有效协作。这种同步的脑电图-肌电图反应，包括与肌电图显示的肌肉活动同时发生的运动相关的β带ERD，证明了中枢神经系统和肌肉之间反馈和前馈回路的逐渐重建. Such findings confirm the hypothesis that greater associations among muscles and the brain are a significant indicator of nerve reorganization. When performing motor tasks, synchronized EEG and EMG responses are evident. For instance, a noticeable EEG desynchronization occurring concurrently with an EMG burst during voluntary movement reveals that muscle output and brain activation collaborate effectively in the recovering system. Such synchronized EEG-EMG responses, including movement-related beta-band ERD occurring concurrently with muscle activity revealed by EMG, demonstrate the gradual re-establishing of feedback and feedforward loops among the CNS and the muscles(Krauth et al., 2019; Mima and Hallett, 1999; Pichiorri et al., 2023).

在 PNS 中，肌电图仍然是再生的关键生物标志物。周围神经横断或压迫会导致肌肉去神经支配，但生长的轴突最终可以重新支配肌肉纤维。针肌电图提供了该过程的一些第一个客观证据，并且观察到小的多相 MUP 代表了早期神经支配的标志In the PNS, EMG continues to be a key biomarker of regeneration. Peripheral nerve transections or compressions induce muscle denervation, but growing axons can ultimately reinnervate muscle fibers. Needle EMG offers some of the first objective evidence of the process, and the observation of small, polyphasic MUPs represents a signature of early reinnervation(Kamble et al., 2019; Kimura and Strakowski, 2025)。这些新的多相 MUP 通常在受伤后的最初几个月观察到，表明新生长的轴突已经实现了新的神经肌肉接头，并开始激活肌纤维，即使收缩太弱而无法产生可辨别的运动。随着神经支配的进展，肌电图模式发生变化：MUP 变得更大、持续时间更长，反映出运动单位通过侧支萌芽而扩大，并且运动单位的募集变得更强、更协调. These new polyphasic MUPs, usually observed in the first few months following injury, indicate that newly growing axons have achieved new neuromuscular junctions and began to activate muscle fibers even when contractions are too feeble to create discernible movement. As reinnervation progresses, EMG patterns change: MUPs become larger and longer in duration, reflecting the enlargement of motor units through collateral sprouting, and recruitment of motor units grows stronger and more coordinated(Sulaiman and Gordon, 2013; Kamble et al., 2019; Kimura and Strakowski, 2025)。与此同时，自愿肌电图活动变得越来越强。肌电图可用作神经肌肉恢复的标志，出现较早，通常在通过观察出现临床强度之前检测运动单位激活和神经肌肉传递的增强. At the same time, voluntary EMG activity grows stronger and stronger. EMG can be used then as a marker of neuromuscular recovery, appearing early, usually detecting enhancement in activation of motor units and neuromuscular transmission prior to clinical strength appearing by observation(Šádek and Otáhal, 2024; Rodney et al., 2024)。总的来说，肌电图和脑电图的结合可以更好地理解再生的情况，脑电图显示皮质活动如何重组，肌电图确认那些恢复运动信号的外周执行，从 MUP 的重新出现到新肌肉收缩的构建。. Overall, the combination of EMG and EEG allows for a better understanding of the picture of regeneration, EEG shows how the cortical activity reorganizes, and the EMG confirms the peripheral implementation of those recovering motor signals, ranging from the reemergence of the MUPs to the construction of the new muscle contractions.

6 PNS 和 CNS 损伤和恢复中的 EEG 和 EMG 变化EEG and EMG Alterations in PNS and CNS Injury and Recovery

周围神经损伤会扰乱大脑和肢体之间正在进行的信息交换，并导致皮质处理发生根本改变。脑电图研究表明，外周创伤对传入和传出输入的破坏会引发感觉运动皮层区域的神经可塑性重组（例如，重新映射、改变连接性）Peripheral nerve injury disrupts the ongoing exchange of information between the brain and the limb and produces radical alterations in cortical processing. EEG research shows that the disruption of afferent and efferent input with peripheral trauma initiates sensorimotor cortical areas’ neuroplastic reorganization (e.g., remapping, altered connectivity)(Fraiman et al., 2016)。这种破坏可以表现为皮质振荡活动的变化，例如光谱功率或稳定性变化，或者表现为受损神经支配的皮质区域的激活减少，并反映了传入神经阻滞后大脑的适应性重组。同时，肌电图 (EMG) 记录提供了周围去神经支配的肌电图证据：自愿性 MUP 在创伤发生后很快消失，无意识颤动电位和正尖波通常在创伤发生后两到三周后很快变得明显. This disruption can be revealed as a shift in oscillatory activity in the cortex, e.g., spectral power or stability change or as reduced activation in the cortical regions innervated by the damaged nerve and reflective of the adaptive reorganization by the brain following deafferentation. Meanwhile, the electromyographic (EMG) records provide electromyographic evidence of peripheral denervation: voluntary MUPs disappear soon after trauma onset, and involuntary fibrillation potentials, and positive sharp waves, typically become evident soon after two to three weeks following trauma onset(Kamble et al., 2019; Han et al., 2020)。这些各自的神经生理学发现共同概述了神经创伤后不久的中枢和外周相互作用。. These respective neurophysiological findings collectively outline the central and peripheral interactions soon after nerve trauma.

在恢复阶段，当周围神经重新支配目标肌肉并重新充盈时，脑电图和肌电图测量都会发生变化以反映功能恢复。更具体地说，传入感觉反馈和运动驱动的恢复能够使皮质重新激活；以前被抑制的皮质活动区域可能会在脑电图上表现出更高的激活和重组，表明运动控制的恢复。此外，肌电图的电生理测量表明神经-肌肉传递的逐步进展；例如，在临床前制剂中使用聚乙二醇 (PEG 3350) 已被证明可以通过缩短复合肌肉动作电位 (CMAP) 潜伏期和提高振幅（两者均表明功能性神经支配）来增强电生理功能，从而促进恢复During the recovery phase when peripheral nerves reinnervate target muscles and refill, both EEG and EMG measures change to reflect functional recovery. More specifically, recovery of afferent sensory feedback and motor drive enables cortical re-activation; formerly-suppressed cortically active areas may demonstrate heightened activation on EEG and reorganization indicative of the recovery of motor control. Moreover, electrophysiological measures by EMG indicate stepwise advances in nerve-muscle transmission; e.g., administration of polyethylene glycol (PEG 3350) in preclinical preparations has been shown to advance recovery by increasing electrophysiological function by shortening compound muscle action potential (CMAP) latency and by boosting amplitude both indicative of functional reinnervation(Ni et al., 2023; Tunç et al., 2025)。事实上，当不断生长的神经连接并发展出持久的功能时，运动皮层会迅速进行重塑，通过皮层可塑性过程重新获得有效的肌肉控制. Indeed, when a growing nerve connects and develops lasting function, the motor cortex quickly undergoes remodeling to re-acquire effective muscle control by processes of cortical plasticity(6; 44)。同时，肌电图显示了神经支配的明确证据：自主运动单位激活首先表现为侧枝萌芽产生的多相、小振幅 MUP，随着新轴突发展突触接触，潜在值逐渐成熟并发展更大的振幅和持续时间。. Meanwhile, EMG demonstrates clear evidence of reinnervation: voluntary motor unit activation re-establishes itself by first appearing as polyphasic, small amplitude MUPs resulting from collateral sprouting, potential values that increasingly mature and develop bigger amplitude and duration as new axons develop synaptic contacts(Bateman et al., 2025, 2023)。有趣的是，创伤后三到四个月多相电位的出现与正在进行的功能恢复一致. Interestingly, the appearance of the polyphasic potentials three to four months following trauma coincides with ongoing functional recovery(Bateman et al., 2025)。总之，外周增益经常伴随着脑电图上皮质值的增强，并表明 PNS-CNS 通信电路的修复。相比之下，中风或脊髓损伤等中枢神经系统病变提供不同但互补的脑电图-肌电图模式。在皮质水平，脑电图通常显示异常的感觉运动节律，运动尝试或运动成像期间的 µ/β ERD 改变，发生在中风后的亚急性和亚慢性损伤和恢复期之后，并且在 SCI 受试者中出现类似的情况. Together, peripheral gains frequently accompany reinforced cortical values on EEG and indicate repair of the PNS-CNS communication circuit. By contrast, CNS lesions such as stroke or SCI provide different but complementary EEG-EMG patterns. At the cortical level, EEG generally shows abnormal sensorimotor rhythms, µ/β ERD alterations during movement attempt or movement imaging, that follow impairment and recovery sub-acute and sub-chronic periods after stroke and appear similarly in SCI subjects(Kumari et al., 2023; Milani et al., 2022)。同时，肌电图对于运动损伤和恢复的评估也是不可或缺的：表面肌电图可以揭示瘫痪肌中残留的随意肌输出，甚至在明显运动较小时辅助运动意图解码. At the same time, EMG is indispensable in the evaluation of motor impairment and recovery: surface EMG can reveal residual voluntary muscle output in paretic muscles and even assist movement intent decoding when overt movement is small(Meyers et al., 2024)。它还定义了痉挛和协调性、客观 sEMG 测量（例如 RMS/iEMG）以及共同收缩指数测量张力过高和病理性共同激活，并显示与中风后或 SCI 患者群体的临床评分量表（例如 MAS）的相关性. It also defines spasticity and coordination, objective sEMG measures such as RMS/iEMG and the index of co-contraction measure hypertonia and pathological co-activation and display correlations with clinical rating scales (e.g., MAS) in the patient populations for post-stroke or SCI(Leszczyńska and Huber, 2023; Xie et al., 2024)。病理性肌肉共同激活的模式是可量化的：共同收缩的肌电图指数将中风后的个体与对照组分开，并且可以跟踪与干预相关的变化并证明评估协调障碍的实用性. The patterns of pathological muscle co-activation are quantifiable: EMG indices of co-contraction separate individuals after stroke from controls and can follow the intervention-related alterations and demonstrate the utility for evaluating coordination impairments(Bandini et al., 2023)。脑电图和肌电图的结合提供了更多信息。同时记录表明中风后皮质肌肉一致性（EEG-EMG 同步性）降低，但皮质肌肉一致性增加与运动恢复相关，并被证明是皮质脊髓耦合恢复的标志. The combination of EEG and EMG provides further information. The simultaneous registration demonstrates decreased cortico-muscular coherence (EEG-EMG synchrony) following stroke but increasing cortico-muscular coherence correlating with motor recovery and proving to be a marker of the restored corticospinal coupling(Krauth et al., 2019; Pichiorri et al., 2023).

从机械角度来看，较高水平的异常共同收缩反映了皮质脊髓束功能障碍，因此验证了皮质脊髓束作为中风康复中病理生理学锚定措施的状态Mechanically, a higher level of abnormal co-contraction reflects corticospinal tract dysfunction and hence verifies the status of the corticospinal tract as a pathophysiologically anchored measure in stroke rehabilitation(Sheng et al., 2022)。同样，时间锁定的运动相关脑电图反应的恢复，例如运动相关皮层电位（MRCP）和. Likewise, the recovery of time-locked movement-related EEG responses, like movement-related cortical potentials (MRCPs) andμ\mu/β\betaERD，反映运动通路的重建。纵向脑电图研究表明，运动前 δ/β 振荡的反复出现与中风后的行为恢复有关，而之前的 ERD 评估可以为以后的运动恢复提供预测因素ERD, reflect the re-establishment of motor pathways. Longitudinal EEG investigations demonstrate that premovement delta/β oscillations recur in association with behavioral recovery after a stroke, while preceding assessment of ERD may provide predictors of later motor recovery(Antonioni et al., 2024)。此外，MRCP 在中风患者下肢任务期间有效地监测步态阶段的执行和时间安排，并支持 MRCP 作为恢复运动执行和规划的时间锁定措施的价值. Additionally, MRCPs during lower-limb tasks in stroke patients efficiently monitor the execution and timing of gait phases and support the value of MRCPs as time-locked measures of restored motor execution and planning(Phang et al., 2024)。脑电图-肌电图模式的组合（例如同时使用脑电图-肌电图提取的皮质-肌肉相干性）强调了动态水平上的中枢-外周关系，并用于神经康复技术的开发，例如混合脑机接口技术的部署. Together, the combination of EEG-EMG modes (e.g., cortico-muscular coherence extracted by using EEG-EMG concurrently) underscores the central-peripheral relationship at the dynamic level and is employed for the development of neurorehabilitation techniques, such as the deployment of hyrid BCI techniques(Pichiorri et al., 2023).

7 讨论Discussion

脑电图在 CNS 和 PNS 损伤中通常显示出作为网络中断和恢复的非侵入性动态指标的实用性。对于中风或脊髓损伤等中枢神经系统损伤，脑电图通常会在受影响的网络中表现出广泛的减慢和不同步。中风后，静息状态记录通常显示 α/β 功率降低，并伴有病变半球 θ/δ 值升高，并且 qEEG 总指数（例如 δ-α 比率）与急性-亚急性期的临床状况相关EEG in both CNS and PNS injuries routinely demonstrates utility as a non-invasive, dynamic indicator of network disruption and recovery. For CNS injuries like stroke or SCI, EEG generally presents widespread slowing and desynchronization in affected networks. Following stroke, rest-state recordings routinely display decreased alpha/beta power by accompanying elevations in theta/delta over the lesioned hemisphere, and aggregate qEEG indices (e.g., delta-alpha ratios) relate to clinical condition in acute-subacute periods(Zhang et al., 2024; Sood et al., 2024)。在 SCI 中，感觉运动节律改变和 alpha/theta 度量变化也参与其中，证实了使用脑电图监测损伤和康复相关改变的有效性. In SCI, sensorimotor rhythm alterations and alpha/theta metric shifts are also implicated, confirming the validity of using EEG to monitor impairment and rehabilitation-related alterations(Simis et al., 2021; de Albuquerque et al., 2023)。肌电图补充描述了中枢病变的终末期运动结果，并显示自愿运动单位募集减少、整个任务阶段激活时间异常不规则、过度激动剂-拮抗剂共同收缩和痉挛爆发，与下降驱动力受损和适应不良反射控制相一致. EMG complementarily describes the end-stage motor outcomes of central lesions and demonstrates decreased voluntary motor-unit recruitment, abnormally irregular activation timing throughout task phases, excessive agonist-antagonist co-contraction and spastic bursts, in concordance with compromised descending drive and maladaptive reflex control(Bitencourt et al., 2022; Bandini et al., 2023; Hohsoh et al., 2024).

相反，对于 PNS 损伤（例如周围神经横断/卡压），皮质结构完整，脑电图改变主要通过传入神经阻滞和废用产生，而不是直接皮质损伤；实验和人体研究证实，感觉运动皮质过度兴奋/重组是由外周输入的丢失和恢复引起的In contrast, for PNS injuries (e.g., peripheral nerve transection/entrapment), the cortex is intact structurally and EEG alterations arise mainly through deafferentation and disuse, rather than direct cortical lesion; experimental and human research confirm sensorimotor-cortical hyperexcitability/reorganization motivated by loss, and restitution, of peripheral input(Nordmark and Johansson, 2020; Meijs et al., 2024).

SEP 允许跟踪体感通路，并且通常在根/神经受压后的急性期表现出反应减弱/缺失的结果，并在功能恢复或通过释放缓解压力时得到改善； SEP 幅度和时频功率按病变大小进行缩放，并在函数返回时进行归一化SEPs allow tracking the somatosensory pathways and generally demonstrate diminished/absent response findings in the acute period following root/nerve compression and improve when function returns or when compression is relieved by the release; SEP amplitude and time-frequency power scale by lesion size and normalize when function returns(Sun et al., 2023; Jo et al., 2021).

同时，肌电图假定外周完整性的主要生理终点。轴突缺失后早期，针肌电图显示去神经支配、颤动电位和正尖波。相反，随着神经支配的恢复，新的多相、低电压运动单位电位出现，然后随着侧支萌芽和轴突生长恢复运动单位，其大小和持续时间增加Concurrently, EMG assumes the major physiological endpoint of peripheral integrity. Early after axonal loss, needle EMG demonstrates denervation, fibrillation potentials, and positive sharp waves. In contrast, with reinnervation, new polyphasic, low-voltage motor-unit potentials appear and then increase in size and duration as collateral sprouting and axonal growth restore motor units(Krarup et al., 2016; Debenham et al., 2025; Fidanci et al., 2023).

然而，强烈的三七总皂苷传入阻滞可导致皮质节律紊乱，类似于中枢神经系统病变后的情况；在慢性 SCI 人群中，静息脑电图表现出频率降低和 β 活性改变，这是丘脑皮质节律失常的典型特征However, intense PNS deafferentation can bring about cortical rhythm disorders similar to those following CNS lesions; in chronic SCI populations, the resting EEG demonstrates shifts to lower frequencies and changed beta activity, characteristics typical for thalamocortical dysrhythmia(Simis et al., 2021; Boord et al., 2008)。总体而言，中枢神经系统损伤通常会导致脑电图的总体变化，而三七总皂甙损伤主要激活局灶性皮质重组，表明大脑对外周输入变化的可塑性反应。. Overall, CNS lesions usually result in gross EEG change, and PNS injuries mainly activate focal cortical reorganization, indicative of the plastic response by the brain to the peripheral input change.

最重要的是，脑电图生物标志物与神经可塑性转化和功能恢复有关。较大的 δ-α 和 DTABR 比值以及较低的 α/β 功率指数与亚急性缺血性中风中更大的损伤和组织灌注不足有关，纵向研究表明随着恢复进展，这些指标会正常化Most importantly, EEG biomarkers are related to neuroplastic transformation and functional restoration. Larger delta-alpha and DTABR ratio and lower alpha/beta power index are connected with greater impairment and tissue hypoperfusion in subacute ischemic stroke, and longitudinal studies demonstrate normalization of the measures with recovery progress(Ajčević et al., 2021; Sood et al., 2024)。任务相关脑电图还显示运动相关 ERD/ERS 的重建和增强与运动恢复同步。类似地，肌电图特征随着恢复而变化：随着分级招募的改善，自愿振幅上升；随着受试者的康复，拮抗剂共同收缩减弱；随着侧支萌芽和新轴突生长的继续，新的小幅度多相运动单元电位进化为更大、持续时间更长、更稳定的单元. Task-related EEG also shows the re-establishment and enhancement of movement-related ERD/ERS in lockstep with motor recovery. Similarly, EMG signatures change with recovery: voluntary amplitudes rise as more graded recruitment improves; antagonist co-contraction diminishes as the subject undergoes rehabilitation; and new, small-amplitude polyphasic motor-unit potentials evolve to larger, long-duration, more stable units as collateral sprouting and new axonal growth continue(Bandini et al., 2023; Negro et al., 2020).

在 SCI 恢复中，顶叶/感觉运动 α/θ 比率增加与更好的步态/平衡功能有关； qEEG 研究表明，α/θ 比率反映了治疗过程中功能恢复的进展，与任务相关的 β 功率也意味着亚急性 SCI 中的步态恢复In SCI recovery, parietal/sensorimotor alpha/theta ratio increases are related to better gait/balance function; qEEG studies show the alpha/theta ratio reflects the progression of functional recovery during the treatment process, and beta power in relation to tasks also signifies gait recovery in sub-acute SCI(Simis et al., 2021, 2020)。与此同时，β带皮质肌肉一致性（CMC）通常在中风后不久就会下降，但在恢复过程中会增加，并与有效皮质脊髓驱动和运动控制的恢复相关. At the same time, beta-band cortico-muscular coherence (CMC), typically decreased shortly after stroke, increases during the process of the recovery period and associates with the recovery of effective corticospinal drive and motor control(Krauth et al., 2019; Delcamp et al., 2023; Parmar et al., 2024)。相应地，在中风中，功能连接的脑电图指数显示在恢复过程中受损运动网络中半球内耦合的加强和适应不良的半球间优势的减少，与恢复功能相当。肌电图的连贯性和任务肌电图模式通常反映脑电图网络的变化，并提供外围证据，表明皮层协调性的增强会改善肌肉的激活；异常的脑电图-肌电图耦合（β带皮质-肌肉一致性降低）在亚急性中风组的运动恢复过程中增强，协调性增强. Correspondingly, in stroke, EEG indices of functional connectivity show the strengthening of intra-hemispheric coupling and reduction of maladaptive inter-hemispheric dominance in damaged motor networks during the recovery process, comparable to regained function. Coherence and task-EMG patterns by EMG typically mirror EEG network changes and provide peripheral evidence that increased cortical coordination produces improved muscle activation; aberrant EEG-EMG coupling (reduced beta-band cortico-muscular coherence) augments during the process of motor recovery and increased coordination in sub-acute-stroke groups(Zhang et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2023).

同时，脑电图标记的细化、阿尔法功率的增加、区域间连接的恢复以及尝试行动时更大的运动相关 ERD，反映了功能恢复和上肢功能In sync, EEG marker refinement, increased alpha power, recovery of inter-areal connectivity, and greater movement-related ERD upon attempted action, reflect functional recovery and upper-limb function(Zhang et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2024)。一致的肌电图测量显示出更大、更有选择性的募集和更少的与练习的共同收缩，而增加皮质肌肉一致性则可以重建有效的皮质脊髓驱动. Coincident EMG measures demonstrate greater, more selective recruitment and less co-contraction with practice, whereas increasing cortico-muscular coherence measures the re-establishing effective corticospinal drive(Bandini et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024)。结合起来，EEG-EMG 特征与通过连接性和经颅磁刺激 (TMS)-EEG 测量以及功能电路的协调重建来评估的网络级重组相匹配. In conjunction, the EEG-EMG signatures match network-level reorganization assessed by measures of connectivity and transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS)-EEG and concordant re-establishment of functional circuits(Bai et al., 2023; Pirovano et al., 2022)。脑电图在监测神经再生方面具有以下几个独特的优点：它直接测量毫秒级的神经活动，便携/便宜（包括床边和院前），并且可以频繁重复以获得即时可塑性；此外，定量脑电图指数（例如 DAR、DTABR、α/β 功率）提供了中风后残疾和结果的预后信息. EEG offers the following several distinctive merits in the monitoring of neural regeneration: it measures directly the neural activity at the millisecond scale, is portable/inexpensive (including bedside and prehospital), and may be repeated frequently to derive moment-to-moment plasticity; besides, quantitative EEG indices (e.g., DAR, DTABR, alpha/beta power) offered prognostic information on disability and outcomes following stroke(Sood et al., 2024; Shen et al., 2024; Wilkinson et al., 2020).

肌电图还提供床边可重复的功能输出测量，汇聚以确认中枢增益正在转化为肌肉激活，在外周修复后进行早期神经再支配（颤动电位/正尖波的出现）EMG also offers a bedside, repeatable measure of functional output, converging to confirm that central gains are translating to muscle activation, picking up early reinnervation following peripheral repair (emergence of fibrillation potentials/positive sharp waves by∼\sim2-3周），并测量中枢神经系统损伤组的异常协同作用或痉挛状态2-3 weeks), and measuring abnormal synergies or spasticity in the CNS injury groups(Kamble et al., 2019; Leszczyńska and Huber, 2023)。临床上，有益的静息脑电图模式（例如 DAR 减少或 α/β 恢复）可能会建议进行更高强度的训练，而异常持续的节律则需要改变方法；同时，自愿肌电图活动的开始或募集阈值的降低为进展到更困难的任务和目标肌肉的选择性肌肉再教育提供了支持. Clinically, a beneficial resting EEG pattern (e.g., reduced DAR or recovered alpha/beta) may recommend progression to higher intensity training, while abnormally ongoing rhythms necessitate change of approach; concurrently, the onset of voluntary EMG activity or decreased recruitment threshold provides support for progression to greater difficulty tasks and selective muscle re-education by target muscles(Wang et al., 2021)。重要的是，脑电图和肌电图的改变通常先于明确的行为改善，从而提供关于电路功能是否正常运行的早期洞察. Importantly, both EEG and EMG alterations often antedate explicit behavioral improvements, providing an early insight as to whether circuits are going the right way functionally(Kamble et al., 2019).

脑电图生物标志物在现实生活中的临床应用正在加快步伐。神经康复使用 qEEG 测量（例如 δ-α 比值、DTABR、α/β 功率）作为替代终点来监测与治疗相关的大脑变化并指导预后，回顾性和前瞻性研究表明，中风后急性期慢/快比升高和 α/β 降低可预测不良结果，而正常化伴随着恢复Clinical applications of EEG biomarkers in real life are gaining pace. Neurorehabilitation uses qEEG measures (e.g., delta-alpha ratio, DTABR, alpha/beta power) as surrogate end-points to monitor brain alterations related to therapies and guide prognosis, retrospective and prospective studies demonstrate that elevated slow/fast ratios and reduced alpha/beta in the acute period after stroke predict poor outcomes, whereas normalization accompanies recovery(Shen et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2021; Benghanem et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025)。最有潜力的变革是脑机接口（BCI）和其他闭环系统中脑电图和肌电图的结合。由于脑电图无创地代表运动意图，BCI 输入可以控制功能性电刺激 (FES) 或外骨骼，随机对照试验/荟萃分析记录了中风运动的改善以及 SCI 中令人鼓舞的发现. Most potentially transformative is the combination of EEG and EMG in brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) and other closed-loop systems. As EEG non-invasively represents motor intent, BCI inputs can control functional electrical stimulation (FES) or exoskeletons, and randomized and controlled trials/meta-analyses document motor improvements in stroke and encouraging findings in SCI(Biswas et al., 2024; He et al., 2025)。双肌电图验证交付并提供生物反馈/设备控制；在系统评价和对照研究中，与标准刺激相比，肌电图激活刺激方案（例如肌电图神经肌肉电刺激 (NMES)/对侧控制神经肌肉电刺激 (CCNMES)）可增强上肢功能和皮质激活. Dual EMG verifies delivery and provides biofeedback/device control; EMG-activated stimulation protocols (e.g., EMG-neuromuscular electrical stimulation (NMES)/contralaterally controlled neuromuscular electrical stimulation (CCNMES)) enhance upper-limb function and cortical activation compared with standard stimulation in systematic reviews and controlled studies(Yang et al., 2023; Shen et al., 2022).

PNS 损伤/神经转移后，肌电激活刺激有助于肌肉保存和再训练，同时神经支配继续进行，肌电假肢（通常在目标肌肉神经支配之前）允许恢复期间的功能控制Following PNS injury/nerve transfer, EMG-activated stimulation facilitates muscle preservation and retraining while reinnervation advances, and myoelectric prostheses (after often preceding targeted muscle reinnervation) allow functional control during the recovery period(Lin et al., 2025; Costello et al., 2023)。通过 SEP 进行辅助感觉通路跟踪，记录传入传导随功能恢复的损失和恢复. Ancillary sensory pathway tracking by means of SEPs records loss and recovery of afferent conduction as function returns(Fuseya et al., 2025)。这种闭环脑电图-肌电图系统共同发挥作用，作为强大的治疗辅助手段，将中心意图转录到周围效应器，并提供反馈本体感受输入以支持依赖于使用的可塑性. Such closed-loop EEG-EMG systems functioning altogether serve as powerful therapies’ adjuncts, transcribing central intent to peripheral effectors and providing feedback proprioceptive input to support use-dependent plasticity(Ferrero et al., 2023)。尽管脑电图生物标志物功能强大，但也并非没有局限性：空间分辨率较弱，深度/焦点发生器仍然难以定位；估计很大程度上依赖于预处理和特征选择；主体间的差异并非微不足道；并且信号非常容易受到运动、肌肉和眼部伪影的影响. Although powerful, EEG biomarkers are not without limitations: the spatial resolution is weak and deep/focal generators are still difficult to localize; estimates are heavily reliant on preprocessing and feature selection; inter-subject difference is non-trivial; and signals are very vulnerable to motion, muscle, and ocular artifacts(Kuroda et al., 2024; Gomez-Tapia et al., 2025)。肌电图也有局限性：由于它是终末器官信号，它不能单独区分中枢驱动故障和外周传导阻滞，并且幅度不是神经驱动的直接测量（例如，幅度抵消效应）。容易受到串扰和电极定位的影响，并且容易受到疲劳的影响；尽管深思熟虑的协议和高密度阵列减少了一些困难，但单肌肉记录忽略了补偿模式. EMG also has limitations: since it is an end-organ signal, it cannot alone distinguish central drive failure and peripheral conduction block, and amplitude is not a direct measure of neural drive (e.g., amplitude-cancellation effects). It is vulnerable to crosstalk and electrode positioning, and confounded by fatigue; single-muscle records omit compensatory patterns, although thoughtful protocols and high-density arrays reduce some difficulties(Bedoy et al., 2025; Wimalasena et al., 2022)。因此，在为诊断和康复决策提供信息时，电生理生物标志物应作为临床检查和影像学的辅助手段，而不是替代品。. 为了弥补这些限制，多模态系统将脑电图与互补技术结合起来。 EEG-fMRI 将毫秒动态与空间映射测量（例如大规模激活或失活）结合在一起，以便在中风人群中进行有针对性的、生物标志物引导的康复和神经反馈。 EEG + 扩散 MRI (DTI) 参考与皮质脊髓束完整性（例如 FA）相关的振荡修改，后者跟踪运动恢复，参考网络生理学到结构基质To compensate for such limitations, multimodal systems couple EEG with complementary techniques. EEG-fMRI brings millisecond dynamics together with spatially mapped measures (e.g., wholesale activation or deactivation) to allow for targetable, biomarker-guided rehab and neurofeedback in stroke populations. EEG + diffusion MRI (DTI) references oscillatory modifications relative to corticospinal-tract integrity (e.g., FA), the latter tracking motor recovery, referencing network physiology to structural substrate(Butet et al., 2025)。通过皮质-肌肉一致性的脑电图-肌电图耦合实际上可以实时测量下行通路功能和通过康复进行的恢复. EEG-EMG coupling through cortico-muscular coherence actually measures descending pathway function and restoration through rehabilitation in real time(Parmar et al., 2024)。同样，混合 EEG-fNIRS 系统通过在 EEG 定时/频率内容中添加血流动力学背景来增强运动意图和恢复跟踪. Similarly, hybrid EEG-fNIRS systems enhance motor intent and recovery tracking through the addition of hemodynamic context to the EEG timing/frequency content(Li et al., 2023)。最后，TMS-EEG/EMG 协议纵向询问回路兴奋性和可塑性，TMS 诱发的脑电图电位测量皮质反应性/连接性，而 MEP 报告恢复期间的皮质脊髓驱动. Lastly, TMS-EEG/EMG protocols interrogate circuit excitability and plasticity longitudinally, TMS-evoked EEG potentials measure cortical reactivity/connectivity while MEPs report corticospinal drive during recovery(Bai et al., 2023)。一般来说，多模态监测将单模态差距联系起来：虽然脑电图缺乏良好的空间分辨率，但成像可以定位；脑电图不确定，肌电图或血流动力学验证；肌电图显示没有中央签名的输出，而脑电图则揭示意图和网络状态。对于未来，多个优先事项可能允许最充分地利用脑电图（和肌电图）的效用。首先，电生理生物标志物的协调，从光谱比例（例如，Delta/Alpha 比率（DAR）和 Delta + Theta/Alpha + Beta 比率（DTABR））和连接指数到 β 波段皮质肌肉一致性和运动单位测量，将提高可比性和临床转化；最近的中风研究表明，qEEG 比率可以测量镜像预后，强调了在商定的定义和流程中的实用性. In general, multimodal monitoring links single-modality gaps: while EEG lacks good spatial resolution, imaging localizes; while EEG is indefinite, EMG or hemodynamics verify; and while EMG displays output without central signatures, EEG unravels intent and network state. For the future, multiple priorities may allow for the fullest exploitation of the utility of EEG (and EMG). First, harmonization of electrophysiological biomarkers, from spectral proportions (e.g., Delta/Alpha Ratio (DAR) and Delta + Theta / Alpha + Beta Ratio (DTABR)) and indices of connectivity to beta-band cortico-muscular coherence and motor-unit measures, would elevate comparability and clinical translation; recent studies in stroke demonstrate that qEEG ratio measures mirror prognosis, highlighting the utility in agreed-on definitions and pipelines(Sood et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2021)。其次，通过高密度脑电图和高级源模型以及用于运动单元分解的高密度肌电图（HD-EMG）增强空间映射，可以加强生理学和解剖学之间的联系，并测量运动单元水平的神经支配. Second, enhanced spatial mapping through high-density EEG and advanced-source models, and by high-density EMG (HD-EMG) for motor-unit decomposition, can strengthen links between physiology and anatomy and measure reinnervation at the motor-unit level(Grison et al., 2025)。第三，必须进行将 EEG/EMG 轨迹与成像（例如，皮质脊髓束的 DTI）、神经传导/SEP 测量和结果相匹配的纵向多模态队列，以阐明电生理恢复如何反映整个有机体水平的组织再生. Third, longitudinal multimodal cohorts that match the trajectory of EEG/EMG to imaging (e.g., DTI of the corticospinal tract), nerve-conduction/SEP measures, and outcomes must be undertaken to elucidate how electrophysiological recovery reflects tissue regeneration at the whole-organism level(Schulz et al., 2021)。第四，脑机接口的发展，例如自适应脑电图-肌电图控制和闭环刺激，应重点关注适应不良的模式和家庭使用的规模；使用多个脑电图系统和便携式高清肌电图控制器的新兴工作展示了家庭辅助和治疗的前景. Fourth, BCI developments, such as adaptive EEG-EMG control and closed-loop stimulation, should focus on maladaptive patterns and scale to home use; nascent work using multiple EEG systems and portable HD-EMG control demonstrates the promise for home assistance and therapy(Yang et al., 2025; Qiu et al., 2022)。最后，从运动终点扩展到感觉系统（例如，使用 SEP 来标记外周恢复）和疼痛/认知领域将增加广度，其中 SCI 可能是最大的受益者，因为振荡脑电图特征与神经病理性疼痛表型相关，并且可以纵向追踪. Finally, broadening beyond the motor endpoints to sensory systems (e.g., using SEPs to mark peripheral recovery) and the pain/cognition realms will increase breadth, of which SCI may be the greatest beneficiary since oscillatory EEG features are related to the neuropathic-pain phenotype and may be tracked longitudinally(Nawaz et al., 2024; Chiu et al., 2024)。脑电图和肌电图作为神经再生生物标志物的优点和局限性详见表. As part of this system, EEG will measure cortical reorganization and network connection and EMG will confirm peripheral execution and reinnervation, ultimately delivering a unified readout of CNS and PNS regeneration.

8 结论Conclusion

简而言之，脑电图和肌电图共同提供了神经再生过程的补充视图，包括大脑的实时电信号和三七总神经系统和中枢神经系统对创伤做出反应和重新调整时运动命令的外周表现。对于 CNS 和 PNS 情况，此类生物标志物代表了恢复的关键过程：EEG 识别大脑平衡振荡、耦合和皮质重新映射的重建，而 EMG 识别随意肌肉激活的恢复、运动单元募集细化和皮质肌肉一致性恢复。它们共同将神经元水平的活动与明确的功能结果联系起来，反映了构成患者恢复能力的回路的重组。In short, EEG and EMG collectively offer a complementary view into the process of neural regeneration, both the real-time electrical signature of the brain and the peripheral manifestation of motor commands as the PNS and CNS respond and readjust to trauma. For both CNS and PNS situations, such biomarkers represent key processes of recovery: EEG identifies the re-establishment of balanced oscillations, coupling, and cortical re-mapping by the brain while EMG identifies the recovery of voluntary muscle activation, motor-unit recruitment refinement, and cortico-muscular coherence restoration. Together, they tie the activity at the level of the neuron to explicit functional outcomes, reflecting the reorganization of circuits that constitutes the recovering abilities of the patient.

作为非侵入性、经济且实时的监测手段，脑电图和肌电图为临床医生和研究人员提供了一套有价值的工具来观察神经恢复过程并为康复提供实时指导。两者一起可以监测恢复路径，提供预后指导​​，并进行个体化治疗，无论是通过调整治疗方案来响应不断变化的大脑节律，通过调节运动来响应肌肉的募集模式，还是通过使用闭环 BCI 和 FES 系统来主动募集潜在路径。这种多模态方法通常适用于中枢（中风、TBI、SCI）和周围（神经损伤、横断）病变，因此通常适用于需要恢复神经元或其组成的回路的任何地方。As non-invasive, economical, and real-time monitoring agents, EEG and EMG provide clinicians and investigators with a valuable tool set to observe neural restoration in progress and to offer real-time guidance for rehabilitation. Together, the two can monitor the path of recovery, provide prognosis guidance, and individualize therapy, whether by adapting a regimen in response to changing brain rhythms, by modulating exercises in response to recruitment patterns by muscle, or by using closed-loop BCI and FES systems to actively recruit latent pathways. This multi-modality method applies generally across central (stroke, TBI, SCI) and peripheral (nerve lesions, transections) lesions and thus applies generally wherever restoration of the neurons or the circuits they compose is desired.

未来，随着技术进步和多模态技术变得更加普遍，脑电图-肌电图整合将成为一种日益强大的生物标志物系统，不仅可以观察再生，而且实际上可以促进再生。在科学和临床实践中采用这两种模式将继续扩大我们改善康复的能力，并利用大脑和肌肉自身的信息来启发和加速神经层面的修复过程。In the future, as technology advances and multimodal techniques become more common, EEG-EMG integration will become an increasingly powerful biomarker system, yet one that not only observes regeneration but actually promotes it. Embracing both modalities in science and clinical practice will continue to expand our ability to both improve recovery and take advantage of the brain’s and muscles’ own messages to enlighten and hasten the process of repair at the neural level.
