摘要Abstract

大型语言模型越来越多地用于高风险决策——招聘、刑事判决、贷款、医疗分类——但它们对虚假特征的敏感性在整个决策领域仍然没有得到很好的描述。我们介绍Large language models are increasingly deployed for high-stakes decisions—hiring, criminal sentencing, lending, medical triage—yet their susceptibility to spurious features remains poorly characterized across decision domains. We introduce冰卫士ICE-Guard，一个应用干预一致性测试来系统地检测三种类型的虚假特征依赖的框架：, a framework that applies intervention consistency testing to systematically detect three types of spurious feature reliance:人口统计demographic（姓名/种族交换），(name/race swaps),权威authority（凭证/声望交换），以及(credential/prestige swaps), and框架framing（正面/负面重述）。在跨越 10 个高风险领域的 3,000 个决策片段中，我们评估了来自 8 个模范家庭的 11 名经过教学调整的法学硕士，发现 (1) 权威偏见（平均 5.8%）和框架偏见（平均 5.0%）大大超过了人口统计偏见（平均 2.2%），挑战了社区对人口统计的狭隘关注； (2) 偏见集中在特定领域——金融领域存在 22.6% 的权威偏见，而刑事司法只有 2.8%； (3) 结构化分解，其中 LLM 提取特征并由确定性评分标准决定，将翻转率降低高达 100%（Kimi-K2.5：5.5%）(positive/negative restatements). Across 3,000 decision vignettes spanning 10 high-stakes domains, we evaluate 11 instruction-tuned LLMs from 8 model families and find that (1) authority bias (mean 5.8%) and framing bias (mean 5.0%) substantially exceed demographic bias (mean 2.2%), challenging the community’s narrow focus on demographics; (2) bias concentrates in specific domains—finance exhibits 22.6% authority bias while criminal justice shows only 2.8%; (3) structured decomposition, where the LLM extracts features and a deterministic rubric decides, reduces flip rates by up to 100% (Kimi-K2.5: 5.5%→\to0.0%, 95% CI [0.0, 0.8]%; 9 个型号的中值降低了 49%）。我们进一步展示了 ICE 引导的0.0%, 95% CI [0.0, 0.8]%; median reduction 49% across 9 models). 6 讨论与结论Discussion and Conclusion

我们介绍了We introduced冰卫士ICE-Guard，一个用于检测和减轻跨 10 个高风险领域的 LLM 决策中的虚假特征依赖的框架。通过测试三种偏见类型——人口统计学、权威和框架——我们发现权威和框架的敏感性为 2-3, a framework for detecting and mitigating spurious feature reliance in LLM decision-making across 10 high-stakes domains. By testing three bias types—demographic, authority, and framing—we find that authority and framing susceptibility are 2–3×\times比人口统计学偏见更普遍，扭转了假设的等级制度。结构化分解为大多数模型提供了有效的缓解措施（尽管提取不可靠的模型可能会回归，但 9 个模型中的 7 个模型最多可减少 100%），并且 ICE 反馈循环可实现迭代修复。这些模式与 ICE-CoT 忠诚度测试并行more prevalent than demographic bias, reversing the assumed hierarchy. Structured decomposition provides effective mitigation for most models (up to 100% reduction for 7 of 9, though models with unreliable extraction may regress), and the ICE feedback loop enables iterative repair. These patterns parallel ICE-CoT faithfulness testingAnonymous (2026)：操作员敏感性较低的模型也显示出较少的虚假依赖，表明存在共享机制。: models with lower operator sensitivity also show less spurious reliance, suggesting a shared mechanism.

局限性。Limitations.

我们的小插图是合成的（尽管Our vignettes are synthetic (though≥80%{\geq}80\%验证与生产系统的功能重叠；附录feature overlap with production systems is verified; AppendixD），仅限英语，结构化分解需要特定领域的标题工程。此外，结构化分解以模型灵活性换取一致性：确定性规则无法处理超出预定义规则的边缘情况，使得这种方法最适合具有明确定义的决策标准的领域。我们的 COMPAS 验证将单个模型 (GPT-5.2) 与 11 个模型合并的合成率进行比较，限制了“保守估计”结论的普遍性。我们发布了 3,000 个插图基准、代码和结果。), English-only, and structured decomposition requires domain-specific rubric engineering. Additionally, structured decomposition trades model flexibility for consistency: deterministic rubrics cannot handle edge cases that fall outside predefined rules, making this approach best suited to domains with well-defined decision criteria. Our COMPAS validation compares a single model (GPT-5.2) against 11-model pooled synthetic rates, limiting the generalizability of the “conservative estimate” conclusion.
