走向一对多的时间基础Towards One-to-Many Temporal Grounding

Qi Xu    Yue Tan    Shihao Chen    Jiahao Meng    Anna Wang    Shunping Ji    Hao Fei    Jason Li
摘要Abstract

时间接地（TG）旨在定位与文本查询相对应的视频片段。先前的研究主要集中在单片段检索。然而，现实场景通常需要为单个查询本地化多个不相交的段——我们称之为设置Temporal Grounding (TG) aims to localize video segments corresponding to a textual query. Prior research predominantly focuses on single-segment retrieval. Real-world scenarios, however, often require localizing multiple disjoint segments for a single query—a setting we term一对多临时接地 (OMTG)One-to-Many Temporal Grounding (OMTG).

以前最先进的 MLLM 针对一对一设置进行了优化，但在这种情况下却举步维艰，由于缺乏事件基数感知，常常产生接近于零的分数。Previous state-of-the-art MLLMs, optimized for one-to-one settings, struggle in this context, often yielding near-zero scores due to a lack of event cardinality perception.

为了弥补这一差距，我们提出了一个具有三个关键贡献的系统解决方案。首先，我们建立了第一个全面的 OMTG 基准，引入计数精度 (C-Acc) 和有效时间 F1 (EtF1) 作为评估指标。To bridge this gap, we present a systematic solution with three key contributions. First, we establish the first comprehensive OMTG benchmark, introducing Count Accuracy (C-Acc) and Effective Temporal F1 (EtF1) as evaluation metrics.

其次，我们通过复杂的构建流程整理了包含 56k 个样本的高质量 OMTG 数据集。Second, we curate a high-quality OMTG dataset comprising 56k samples through a sophisticated construction pipeline.

第三，我们开发了专门为 OMTG 设计的新颖的时间和字幕奖励函数。Third, we develop novel temporal and caption reward functions specifically designed for OMTG.

特别是，字幕奖励利用密集视频字幕的思想链推理来明确指导策略优化，以实现精确性和完整性。In particular, the caption reward leverages Chain-of-Thought reasoning over dense video captions to explicitly guide policy optimization toward both preciseness and completeness.

大量实验表明，我们的模型在 OMTG Bench 上达到了 43.65% 的新的最先进 EtF1，分别比 Gemini 2.5 Pro 和 Seed-1.8 好 15.85% 和 15.61%。Extensive experiments show our model achieves a new state-of-the-art EtF1 of 43.65% on OMTG Bench, outperforming Gemini 2.5 Pro and Seed-1.8 by 15.85% and 15.61%, respectively.

3.2 指标Metrics

一对一时间接地基准One-to-One Temporal Grounding benchmarks(Gao et al., 2017; Krishna et al., 2017; Lei et al., 2021; Zhang et al., 2025b)主要采用Recall@1（R@1）和时间IoU（tIoU）阈值作为评估指标。 在一对一设置中，每个查询都与唯一的真实片段相关联，该指标就足够了，因为正确的检索同时满足精度和召回率。predominantly adopt Recall@1 (R@1) with a temporal IoU (tIoU) threshold as the evaluation metric. In the One-to-One setting, where each query is associated with a unique ground-truth segment, this metric is sufficient, as a correct retrieval simultaneously satisfies both precision and recall.

As illustrated in Fig.2，精确率和召回率之间的区别变得非常重要。 高召回率仅表明覆盖了一些真实实例，但无法惩罚冗余或幻觉的预测。此外，tIoU 度量虽然可以有效测量时间重叠，但对事件的结构组成不敏感。 例如，如果模型错误地将由短时间间隙分隔的两个语义上不同的事件合并为单个连续片段，则由于显着重叠，所得到的 tIoU 可能保持在欺骗性的高水平（例如 0.9），尽管模型无法区分多次出现并产生不正确的基数。, the distinction between precision and recall becomes non-trivial. A high recall score only indicates that some ground-truth instances are covered, but fails to penalize redundant or hallucinated predictions. Moreover, the tIoU metric, while effective in measuring temporal overlap, is insensitive to the structural composition of events. For example, if a model incorrectly merges two semantically distinct events separated by a short temporal gap into a single continuous segment, the resulting tIoU can remain deceptively high (e.g., 0.9) due to dominant overlap, despite the model failing to distinguish multiple occurrences and producing incorrect cardinality.

我们发现，现有最先进的 MLLM 尽管在标准设置方面取得了成功，但在感知事件基数和定位不相交片段方面仍存在很大困难。We reveal that existing state-of-the-art MLLMs, despite their success in standard settings, struggle significantly to perceive event cardinality and localize disjoint segments.

为了弥补这一差距，我们通过复杂的数据管道策划了 56k 个高质量的一对多训练样本，并进行了结合了时间和字幕奖励的 SFT+RL 训练，从而实现了最先进的结果。To bridge this gap, we curate 56k high-quality one-to-many training samples via a sophisticated data pipeline and conduct SFT+RL training that incorporates our temporal and caption rewards, achieving state-of-the-art results.

我们的研究为这种新颖的 OMTG 设置建立了强有力的基线，并促进了这一方向的未来研究。Our study establishes a strong baseline for this novel OMTG setting and facilitates future research in this direction.
