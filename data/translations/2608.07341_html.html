6 结论Conclusion

我们研究了污染缓解评估中的两个耦合问题：如何 衡量缓解策略实现的恢复以及如何设计 更好的策略。在度量方面，我们发现了两个缺陷 流行的 G-AP（离散读数，不能代表每个问题 性能，并在差分之前进行平均，让过度和 抑制不足取消）并在每个问题的水平上纠正它们 求解概率。为了防止策略将解决概率推向 clean模型的频繁值在同等权重下，我们进一步聚合 在干净模型的求解概率的组内，产生 SA-PPG。上 策略方面，我们提出了 RailCap，它在每个解码步骤进行检查 样本是否已经回到贪婪轨迹，一旦回到贪婪轨迹， 将下一个轨迹标记的概率限制为亚军的概率， 将污染判断从一次性事前估计转变为 生成过程中的逐步监督。跨越三个模型家庭和两个 污染形式，G-AP 系统性地高估了污染的恢复 之前的策略，并且 RailCap 在每种情况下都获得了最低的 SA-PPG。We studied two coupled problems in contamination mitigation evaluation: how to measure the restoration that a mitigation strategy achieves, and how to design a better strategy. On the metric side, we identified two flaws of the prevailing G-AP (a discrete readout that cannot represent per-question performance, and averaging before differencing that lets over- and under-suppression cancel out) and corrected them at the level of per-question solve probabilities. To keep strategies from pushing solve probabilities onto the clean model’s frequent values under equal weighting, we further aggregate within groups of the clean model’s solve probability, yielding SA-PPG. On the strategy side, we proposed RailCap, which checks at every decoding step whether a sample has fallen back onto the greedy trajectory and, once it has, caps the probability of the next trajectory token to that of the runner-up, turning the judgment of contamination from a one-shot pre-hoc estimate into step-wise supervision during generation. Across three model families and two forms of contamination, G-AP systematically overestimates the restoration of prior strategies, and RailCap attains the lowest SA-PPG in every setting.
