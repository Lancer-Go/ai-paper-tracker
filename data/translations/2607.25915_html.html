Penelope：高效结构化推理的局部潜在递归Penelope: Localized Latent Recurrence for Efficient Structured Reasoning

Yutong Chen, Shouqian Shi, Xinran Liu, Haochen Wang,
Jiaying Wang, Tianxing Xu, Yuanxi Wang, Zirui Ding
摘要Abstract

复杂的结构化推理任务通常需要额外的计算，但当前的语言模型主要通过增加参数规模或将中间步骤序列化为思想链（CoT）标记来获得它。前者增加了训练和部署成本，而后者将推理计算与自回归输出长度联系起来。我们引入了 Penelope，这是一种用于预训练的仅解码器 Transformer 的高效潜在推理框架，可将循环计算本地化到选定的解码器间隔。较低的解码器前缀被评估一次以构造问题条件边界存储器，然后在答案生成之前通过时间调制的 GRU 动态和循环读出状态迭代地细化该边界存储器。渐进式 CoT 到潜在课程将可见推理转移到这个内部循环路径中，允许在潜在空间中分配额外的计算，而无需重复执行完整的解码器或生成长中间跟踪。开源结构化推理基准的实验表明，在验证选择的潜在预算时，Penelope 相对于已建立的潜在推理模型获得了有竞争力的准确性，同时减少了测量的推理延迟。这些结果表明，潜在的细化可以局限于狭窄的解码器间隔，减少重复的全解码器执行，而无需生成长的可见推理轨迹，并为仅解码器的 Transformer 模型提供实用的准确性与效率权衡。Complex structured reasoning tasks often require additional computation, yet current language models obtain it mainly by increasing parameter scale or by serializing intermediate steps as chain-of-thought (CoT) tokens. The former raises training and deployment costs, while the latter ties reasoning computation to autoregressive output length. We introduce Penelope, an efficient latent-reasoning framework for pretrained decoder-only Transformers that localizes recurrent computation to a selected decoder interval. The lower decoder prefix is evaluated once to construct a problem-conditioned boundary memory, which is then iteratively refined through time-modulated GRU dynamics and recurrent readout states before answer generation. A progressive CoT-to-latent curriculum transfers visible reasoning into this internal recurrent path, allowing additional computation to be allocated in latent space without repeatedly executing the complete decoder or generating a long intermediate trace. Experiments on open-source structured-reasoning benchmarks show that, at validation-selected latent budgets, Penelope attains competitive accuracy relative to established latent-reasoning models while reducing measured inference latency. These results show that latent refinement can be localized to a narrow decoder interval, reducing repeated full-decoder execution without generating a long visible reasoning trace and providing a practical accuracy–efficiency tradeoff for decoder-only Transformer models.

1 简介Introduction

推理能力通常取决于问题的可用计算量。当前的语言模型主要沿着两个轴获得额外的推理计算。扩展会增加模型容量和推理​​成本，同时也会提高培训、部署和服务成本。相反，思想链 (CoT) 推理通过将中间计算表示为生成的标记来引入额外的自回归步骤Reasoning ability often depends on the amount of computation available for a problem. Current language models obtain additional reasoning computation primarily along two axes. Scaling increases model capacity and inference cost, while also raising training, deployment, and serving costs. Chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning instead introduces additional autoregressive steps by expressing intermediate computation as generated tokens(Wei et al. 2022)。 CoT 提供了明确且有效的推理轨迹，同时将添加的计算与顺序生成长度耦合。因此，这两条主要路线将推理计算绑定到模型大小或可见标记的数量。. CoT provides an explicit and effective reasoning trace, while coupling the added computation to sequential generation length. Consequently, the two dominant routes bind reasoning computation either to model size or to the number of visible tokens.

潜在推理将中间计算从生成的标记转移到隐藏状态。连续思维方法表明潜在表征可以替代文本轨迹的一部分Latent reasoning moves intermediate computation from generated tokens into hidden states. Continuous-thought methods show that latent representations can replace portions of a textual trace(Hao et al. 2025; Shen et al. 2025)，而循环深度模型将隐藏状态细化公开为测试时计算轴, while recurrent-depth models expose hidden-state refinement as a test-time compute axis(Dehghani et al. 2019; Geiping et al. 2025)。然而，许多方法重复执行大量的解码器部分。它们的潜在轨迹避免了长的可见痕迹，但边际细化成本仍然与广泛的模型执行相关。我们询问是否可以通过局部循环计算来保留类似的推理能力。. Many approaches, however, repeatedly execute substantial decoder portions. Their latent trajectories avoid long visible traces, but the marginal refinement cost remains tied to broad model execution. We ask whether comparable reasoning capability can instead be preserved through localized recurrent computation.

获得更多的推理计算并有效地分配它是不同的问题。现有方法主要解决第一个问题；我们询问固定的预训练主干如何支持潜在的细化，而无需重复执行完整的解码器。结构化推理提供了一种受控设置，因为证明搜索、图遍历和表达简化自然地演化出紧凑的内部状态。我们的目标是一个计算接口，它可以保留特定于问题的状态，同时在具有竞争力的精度-延迟操作点上本地化重复执行。Obtaining more reasoning computation and allocating it efficiently are distinct problems. Existing methods largely address the first; we ask how a fixed pretrained backbone can support latent refinement without repeatedly executing the complete decoder. Structured reasoning offers a controlled setting because proof search, graph traversal, and expression reduction naturally evolve compact internal states. Our objective is a computation interface that preserves a problem-specific state while localizing repeated execution at a competitive accuracy–latency operating point.

实现这种局部潜在计算需要解决三个耦合的挑战。足够的问题条件信息必须持续存在，而无需重复执行完整解码器，从而激发一次性较低前缀传递和具有缓存的提示侧上下文的持久边界接口。仅重用选定的解码器间隔必须产生有意义的、稳定的细化，而不是冗余状态更新，从而通过共享输出侧间隔激发固定大小的潜在内存、步骤调节和循环更新。最后，不断发展的轨迹必须能够被标准答案生成所访问，从而通过最终的接口传递激发循环读出状态和层一致的答案上下文构建。这些要求共同推动了以下设计。Realizing such localized latent computation requires addressing three coupled challenges. Sufficient problem-conditioned information must persist without repeated full-decoder execution, motivating a one-time lower-prefix pass and a persistent boundary interface with cached prompt-side context. Reusing only a selected decoder interval must then produce meaningful, stable refinement rather than redundant state updates, motivating fixed-size latent memory, step conditioning, and recurrent updates through a shared output-side interval. Finally, the evolving trajectory must remain accessible to standard answer generation, motivating recurrent readout states and layer-consistent answer-context construction through a final interface pass. These requirements jointly motivate the following design.

我们提出了 Penelope，一个用于仅解码器 Transformer 的本地化潜在推理框架。这个名字借鉴了佩内洛普的循环编织：较低的解码器前缀执行一次以建立稳定的、有问题的边界，而共享的输出侧间隔重复地将细化编织到这个持久的潜在接口中。经过单个潜在轨迹后，细化的界面条件答案生成。至关重要的是，Penelope 不仅仅是一个循环解码器块：它计算一次提示前缀，在选定的时间间隔内缓存提示端上下文，然后仅迭代固定大小的内存和读出状态。该组织将可重用的问题上下文与分配给细化的计算分开，减少了重复的解码器执行，而无需为各个层分配独特的推理语义。We present Penelope, a localized latent-reasoning framework for decoder-only Transformers. The name draws on Penelope’s recurrent weaving: the lower decoder prefix is executed once to establish a stable, problem-conditioned boundary, while a shared output-side interval repeatedly weaves refinements into this persistent latent interface. After a single latent trajectory, the refined interface conditions answer generation. Crucially, Penelope is not simply a looped decoder block: it computes the prompt prefix once, caches prompt-side context within the selected interval, and thereafter iterates only fixed-size memory and readout states. This organization separates reusable problem contextualization from the computation allocated to refinement, reducing repeated decoder execution without assigning unique reasoning semantics to individual layers.

2 相关工作Related Work

潜在的推理。Latent reasoning.

CoT 提示和自我一致性通过生成的推理轨迹分配额外的推理计算CoT prompting and self-consistency allocate additional inference computation through generated reasoning traces(Wei et al. 2022; Wang et al. 2023)。 Coconut 逐步用解码器反馈的连续状态取代文本推理步骤，而 CODI 将显式推理提炼为隐式轨迹. Coconut progressively replaces textual reasoning steps with continuous states fed back through the decoder, while CODI distills explicit reasoning into implicit trajectories(Hao et al. 2025; Shen et al. 2025)。相反，DART 将自回归轨迹提炼为并行的无声思维标记，并通过推理演化模块将它们对齐. DART instead distills an autoregressive trace into parallel silent-thought tokens and aligns them through a reasoning-evolvement module(Jiang et al. 2025)。循环变压器提供了理论和经验证据，证明有效的循环深度可以支持潜在的迭代计算，而无需按比例增加不同的参数. Looped Transformers provide theoretical and empirical evidence that effective recurrent depth can support latent iterative computation without a proportionate increase in distinct parameters(Saunshi et al. 2025)。 SeLaR 采用互补的选择性计算观点，在不确定的解码步骤激活软潜在表示，同时在置信的步骤保留离散解码. SeLaR takes a complementary selective-computation view, activating soft latent representations at uncertain decoding steps while retaining discrete decoding at confident steps(Fu and Luo 2026)。这些方法共同将潜在状态建立为可行的推理基础。佩内洛普研究了如何保留这样的内部轨迹，同时本地化用于细化它的解码器计算的分配问题。. Together, these methods establish latent states as a viable reasoning substrate. Penelope studies the allocation question of how to preserve such an internal trajectory while localizing the decoder computation used to refine it.

循环计算和记忆。Recurrent computation and memory.

自适应计算时间和 PonderNet 学习变量内部深度Adaptive Computation Time and PonderNet learn variable internal depth(Graves 2016; Banino et al. 2021)，而通用变形金刚共享一个跨深度的过渡, whereas Universal Transformers share a transition across depth(Dehghani et al. 2019)。递归混合将此类参数共享与分配动态递归深度的轻量级令牌级路由器相结合. Mixture-of-Recursions combines such parameter sharing with lightweight token-level routers that assign dynamic recursive depths(Bae et al. 2025)。块循环和循环内存变压器跨块或序列段维护状态. Block-Recurrent and Recurrent Memory Transformers maintain state across blocks or sequence segments(Hutchins et al. 2022; Bulatov et al. 2022)。最近的循环深度语言模型将潜在深度暴露为测试时计算轴. Recent recurrent-depth language models expose latent depth as a test-time compute axis(Geiping et al. 2025)。 LoopFormer 和 LoopCoder-v2 进一步探索语言和代码推理的循环计算. LoopFormer and LoopCoder-v2 further explore looped computation for language and code reasoning(Jeddi et al. 2026; Yang et al. 2026)。定点推理器通过预归一化和残差缩放来稳定深度循环，并使用收敛作为学习停止标准. Fixed-Point Reasoners stabilize deep loops with pre-normalization and residual scaling, and use convergence as a learned stopping criterion(Movahedi et al. 2026)。相反，佩内洛普通过将持久的潜在接口耦合到缓存的输出端间隔内的循环执行来适应预训练的解码器；令牌级路由和基于收敛的停止是对此处评估的固定深度系统的补充。. Penelope instead adapts a pretrained decoder by coupling a persistent latent interface to recurrent execution within a cached output-side interval; token-level routing and convergence-based halting are complementary to the fixed-depth system evaluated here.

局部复发。Localized recurrence.

现有研究表明，复发不需要跨越整个 Transformer。层内循环选择性地重复各个层Existing studies establish that recurrence need not span an entire Transformer. Intra-Layer Recurrence selectively repeats individual layers(Nguyen and Lin 2025)，而无需训练的环形变压器会湿润并重新应用连续的中间块, while Training-Free Looped Transformers damp and reapply a contiguous middle block(Chen et al. 2026)。 LOTUS 相反会循环潜在区块并使用黄金 CoT-step 代币来监督其位置. LOTUS instead loops latent blocks and supervises their positions with gold CoT-step tokens(Fan et al. 2026)。 Penelope 通过独特的与缓存兼容的潜在计算接口推进了这个方向：提示上下文化执行一次，而在答案生成之前，在选定的输出侧间隔内仅重复固定的内存和读出状态。布局分析将此区间评估为一个有效的操作点；确切的位置仍然依赖于主干。. Penelope advances this direction through a distinct cache-compatible latent computation interface: prompt contextualization is performed once, while only fixed memory and readout states recur within a selected output-side interval before answer generation. The placement analysis evaluates this interval as one efficient operating point; exact placement remains backbone-dependent.

6 结论Conclusion

我们提出了 Penelope，它通过在选定的解码器间隔内与缓存兼容的循环来分配潜在计算。持久的、以问题为条件的接口将可重用的上下文化与潜在接口的细化分开，而层一致的缓存则将细化的轨迹暴露给答案生成。在三个结构化推理任务中，Penelope 保持了相当的精确匹配性能，同时减少了重复的全解码器执行和较低的测量延迟。这是有效的潜在计算分配，而不是参数效率或单调深度缩放。We present Penelope, which allocates latent computation through cache-compatible recurrence over a selected decoder interval. A persistent, problem-conditioned interface separates reusable contextualization from latent-interface refinement, while layer-consistent caches expose the refined trajectory to answer generation. Across three structured-reasoning tasks, Penelope preserves comparable exact-match performance with less repeated full-decoder execution and lower measured latency. This is efficient latent-computation allocation, not parameter efficiency or monotonic depth scaling.
