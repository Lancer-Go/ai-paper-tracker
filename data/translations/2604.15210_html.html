摘要Abstract

幽默是为数不多的认知任务之一，正确的推理与正确的答案同样重要。虽然最近的工作在纽约客卡通字幕大赛（NYCC）等基准上评估幽默理解，但它在很大程度上将其视为黑盒预测，忽视了幽默理解背后的结构化推理过程。我们引入 IRS（不一致解决监督），这是一个将幽默理解分解为三个组成部分的框架：Humor is one of the few cognitive tasks where getting the reasoning right matters as much as getting the answer right. While recent work evaluates humor understanding on benchmarks such as the New Yorker Cartoon Caption Contest (NYCC), it largely treats it as black-box prediction, overlooking the structured reasoning processes underlying humor comprehension. We introduce IRS (Incongruity-Resolution Supervision), a framework that decomposes humor understanding into three components:不协调造型incongruity modeling，识别视觉场景中的不匹配；, which identifies mismatches in the visual scene;分辨率建模resolution modeling，它对这些不匹配构建了连贯的重新解释；和, which constructs coherent reinterpretations of these mismatches; and偏好调整preference alignment，它根据人类判断评估候选解释。 IRS 以不一致解决理论和专家字幕实践为基础，通过结构化痕迹监督中间推理过程，使从视觉感知到幽默解释的路径变得明确且可学习。在 NYCC 的 7B、32B 和 72B 模型中，IRS 在字幕匹配和排名任务中的表现优于强大的开放式和封闭式多模态基线，我们最大的模型在排名方面接近专家级表现。零样本转移到外部基准表明 IRS 学习了可概括的推理模式。我们的结果表明，监督推理结构（而不​​仅仅是规模）是以推理为中心的任务的关键。, which evaluates candidate interpretations under human judgments. Grounded in incongruity-resolution theory and expert captionist practice, IRS supervises intermediate reasoning process through structured traces that make the path from visual perception to humorous interpretation explicit and learnable. Across 7B, 32B, and 72B models on NYCC, IRS outperforms strong open and closed multimodal baselines across caption matching and ranking tasks, with our largest model approaching expert-level performance on ranking. Zero-shot transfer to external benchmarks shows that IRS learns generalizable reasoning patterns. Our results suggest that supervising reasoning structure, rather than scale alone, is key for reasoning-centric tasks.

1 简介Introduction

幽默是人类智力的一个要求很高的方面，需要视觉感知、文化知识和创造性推理的整合Humor is a demanding facet of human intelligence, requiring the integration of visual perception, cultural knowledge, and creative reasoning(Kazemi et al., 2025)。与标准基准任务不同，幽默不是由单个正确答案定义的，而是由推理过程定义的：识别期望和观察之间的不匹配，并以连贯但令人惊讶的方式解决它。这种不一致解决动态，在认知科学中得到了长期研究. Unlike standard benchmark tasks, humor is not defined by a single correct answer but by a reasoning process: identifying a mismatch between expectation and observation, and resolving it in a coherent yet surprising way. This incongruity-resolution dynamic, long studied in cognitive science(Suls, 1972; Attardo, 1994)，表明幽默理解从根本上来说是一个结构化推理问题，但大多数计算方法将其视为预测任务，训练模型来选择或排名字幕，而不对使字幕变得有趣的解释步骤进行建模。 卡通字幕使这一差距变得具体。强有力的标题不仅描述了一个场景，而且还描​​述了一个场景。它识别出显着的视觉张力并将其重新构建为有意义的妙语。这需要知道什么是不一致的地方，如何解决它，以及为什么一个解决方案比另一个解决方案更有趣；当前多模态语言模型仅部分捕获的能力，这反映在它们在幽默基准上与人类表现的差距, suggests that humor understanding is fundamentally a structured reasoning problem — yet most computational approaches treat it as a prediction task, training models to select or rank captions without modeling the interpretive steps that make a caption funny. Cartoon captioning makes this gap concrete. A strong caption does not merely describe a scene; it identifies a salient visual tension and reframes it into a meaningful punchline. This requires knowing what the incongruity is, how it can be resolved, and why one resolution is funnier than another; capabilities that current multimodal language models only partially capture, as reflected in their gap to human performance on humor benchmarks(Hessel et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2025).

While prior work primarily treats NYCC as a classification or ranking benchmark, it also offers insight into the reasoning processes underlying visual humor.

6 结论Conclusion

我们引入了不一致解决监督（IRS），这是一个框架，它将幽默理解建模为基于不一致解决理论和专家字幕实践的结构化推理过程。 IRS 不是单独监督字幕选择，而是通过领域自适应预训练、字幕推理痕迹以及与感知和风格奖励的偏好一致来监督使字幕变得有趣的中间步骤——识别视觉不匹配、构建连贯的重新解释以及根据人类判断评估候选解释。在 7B、32B 和 72B 模型中，IRS 在匹配和排名任务上始终优于强大的基线，最大的模型接近专家级性能并推广到域外基准。这些结果表明，明确监督推理结构，而不是仅仅依赖规模，是幽默等复杂、主观任务的关键。We introduced Incongruity-Resolution Supervision (IRS), a framework that models humor understanding as a structured reasoning process grounded in incongruity-resolution theory and expert captionist practice. Rather than supervising caption selection alone, IRS supervises the intermediate steps that make a caption funny–identifying visual mismatches, constructing coherent reinterpretations, and evaluating candidate interpretations against human judgments–through domain-adaptive pretraining, captionist reasoning traces, and preference alignment with perceptual and stylistic rewards. Across 7B, 32B, and 72B models, IRS consistently outperforms strong baselines on matching and ranking tasks, with the largest model approaching expert-level performance and generalizing to out-of-domain benchmarks. These results suggest that explicitly supervising reasoning structure, rather than relying on scale alone, is key for complex, subjective tasks such as humor.

附录EAppendix E局限性和道德考虑Limitations and Ethical Considerations

虽然我们的框架改善了多模式幽默理解，但仍然存在一些局限性，突出了基于推理的对齐中的开放挑战。While our framework improves multimodal humor understanding, several limitations remain that highlight open challenges in reasoning-based alignment.

视觉感知错误。Visual perception errors.

尽管强化学习具有感知奖励，但该模型可能会错误识别显着的视觉实体，这会在推理过程中传播并导致错误的标题选择。如图所示。Despite reinforcement learning with perceptual rewards, the model can misidentify salient visual entities, which propagates through the reasoning process and leads to incorrect caption selection. As shown in Fig.E.1，由于表面视觉相似性（例如武器形状），该模型将维京战士与死神（镰刀）混淆，导致感知场景与预期的不协调之间完全不匹配。将骨干网缩放至 32B 可以大大减少此类错误，这表明感知基础受益于表征能力的提高。然而，这种改进并不统一：程式化的卡通需要对稀疏和夸张的视觉线索进行抽象，而这些线索在标准视觉预训练语料库中仍然代表性不足。这表明仅靠推理监督无法弥补系统感知错误，并且需要视觉编码器或特定领域视觉预训练的进步来完全解决这一限制。, the model confuses a Viking warrior with the Grim Reaper (scythe) due to superficial visual similarity (e.g., weapon shape), causing a complete mismatch between perceived scene and intended incongruity. However, this improvement is not uniform: stylized cartoons require abstraction over sparse and exaggerated visual cues, which remain underrepresented in standard vision pretraining corpora. This suggests that reasoning supervision alone cannot compensate for systematic perception errors, and that advances in vision encoders or domain-specific visual pretraining are necessary to fully address this limitation.

文化和语言的特殊性。Cultural and linguistic specificity.

我们的数据集以《纽约客》卡通字幕大赛为中心，主要反映以英语和美国为中心的幽默。因此，学到的推理模式可能不会转移到其他文化背景，因为在其他文化背景中，幽默依赖于不同的惯例、参考资料和语言结构。将该框架扩展到多语言和文化多样性的数据集对于评估幽默感知推理的普遍性是必要的。Our dataset is centered on the New Yorker Cartoon Caption Contest, which reflects predominantly Anglophone and U.S.-centric humor. As a result, the learned reasoning patterns may not transfer to other cultural contexts, where humor relies on different conventions, references, and linguistic structures. Extending this framework to multilingual and culturally diverse datasets is necessary to evaluate the generality of humor-aware reasoning.

评价的主观性。Subjectivity in evaluation.

幽默本质上是主观的，即使在 NYCC 内部，群体判断和编辑选择也仅代表可能解释的一部分。我们的奖励函数通过感知和风格标准来近似这些偏好，但无法完全捕捉人类反应的多样性。这在自动评估和人类判断之间引入了不可避免的差距，激发了未来关于更强大和多样化的人类评估协议的工作。Humor is inherently subjective, and even within NYCC, crowd judgments and editorial selections represent only a subset of possible interpretations. Our reward functions approximate these preferences through perceptual and stylistic criteria, but cannot fully capture the diversity of human responses. This introduces an unavoidable gap between automated evaluation and human judgment, motivating future work on more robust and diverse human evaluation protocols.

数据访问和许可。Data access and licensing.

我们的 IM 语料库的部分内容源自受版权保护的来源（例如播客转录、出版的书籍）。由于许可限制，我们无法直接发布此材料，但我们将提供评估拆分和完整的预处理管道。这确保了可重复性，同时尊重知识产权。Parts of our IM corpus are derived from copyrighted sources (e.g., podcast transcripts, published boooks). Due to licensing restrictions, we cannot release this material directly, though we will provide evaluation splits and full preprocessing pipelines. This ensures reproducibility while respecting intellectual property.

尽管存在这些限制，我们的工作还是朝着将多模态推理模型与人类智能最难以捉摸的方面之一：幽默结合起来迈出了一步。解决文化、主观和计算挑战为未来的研究提供了肥沃的基础。Despite these limitations, our work represents a step toward aligning multimodal reasoning models with one of the most elusive facets of human intelligence: humor. Addressing cultural, subjective, and computational challenges offers fertile ground for future research.

道德考虑。Ethical considerations.

模仿幽默引发了额外的道德问题。幽默通常依赖于文化刻板印象、敏感话题或隐含的社会规范，而根据此类数据训练的模型可能会重现或放大偏见。此外，能够生成类似幽默的字幕的系统可能会被滥用来产生攻击性或不适当的内容。虽然我们的工作重点是受控评估设置，但在现实应用程序中部署此类模型需要仔细考虑内容审核、文化敏感性和用户背景。Modeling humor raises additional ethical concerns. Humor often relies on cultural stereotypes, sensitive topics, or implicit social norms, and models trained on such data may reproduce or amplify biases. Furthermore, systems capable of generating humor-like captions could be misused to produce offensive or inappropriate content. While our work focuses on controlled evaluation settings, deploying such models in real-world applications requires careful consideration of content moderation, cultural sensitivity, and user context.
