NuclearQAv2：结构化基准NuclearQAv2: A Structured Benchmark
用于评估领域科学能力for Evaluating Domain-Science Competence

摘要Abstract

大型语言模型 (LLM) 在广泛的任务中表现出了强大的性能，但确保其在高科技领域的可靠性仍然是一个重大挑战。在核工程中，解决问题通常不仅需要事实知识，还需要定量推理和概念理解。 为了满足该领域系统评估的需求，我们引入 NuclearQAv2，这是评估法学硕士核工程知识的基准。该基准包含大约 1,240 个问答对，涵盖三个类别：布尔型、数字型和语言型。 NuclearQAv2 使用混合管道构建，该管道结合了专家撰写的问题、现有数据集和法学硕士辅助的特定领域技术语料库生成。通过利用结构化提示进行自动问题生成和响应评估，所提出的框架可以实现可扩展的基准构建和评估。 我们使用 NuclearQAv2 评估一组不同的法学硕士，并观察到不同任务类型之间的显着性能差异。虽然这些模型通常在事实问题上表现良好，但定量推理和概念理解仍然更具挑战性。这些结果凸显了多方面评估框架的重要性，并将 NuclearQAv2 确立为评估技术领域法学硕士能力的可扩展基准。Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated strong performance across a wide range of tasks, but ensuring their reliability in highly technical domains remains a significant challenge. In nuclear engineering, problem solving often requires not only factual knowledge but also quantitative reasoning and conceptual understanding. To address the need for systematic evaluation in this domain, we introduce NuclearQAv2, a benchmark for assessing LLMs on nuclear engineering knowledge. The benchmark comprises approximately 1,240 question-answer pairs spanning three categories: boolean, numeric, and verbal. NuclearQAv2 is constructed using a hybrid pipeline that combines expert-authored questions, existing datasets, and LLM-assisted generation from domain-specific technical corpora. By leveraging structured prompting for both automated question generation and response evaluation, the proposed framework enables scalable benchmark construction and evaluation. We evaluate a diverse set of LLMs using NuclearQAv2 and observe substantial performance differences across task types. While the models generally perform well on factual questions, quantitative reasoning and conceptual understanding remain considerably more challenging. These results highlight the importance of multi-faceted evaluation frameworks and establish NuclearQAv2 as a scalable benchmark for assessing LLM capabilities in technical domains.

1 简介Introduction

尽管大语言模型 (LLM) 的受欢迎程度迅速上升，但它在广泛的语言理解和知识密集型任务中表现出了强大的性能，包括翻译、问答、多任务推理和文本生成Despite their rapid rise in popularity, large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated strong performance across a wide range of language understanding and knowledge-intensive tasks, including translation, question answering, multi-task reasoning, and text generation(Brown et al., 2020; Hendrycks et al., 2020; Achiam et al., 2023; Team et al., 2023)。因此，它们正在被研究并部署在许多具有高影响力的技术领域，例如医学、法律、金融和教育. Consequently, they are being investigated and deployed in many technical domains with high impact, such as medicine, law, finance, and education(Singhal et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2026)。 然而，众所周知，法学硕士表现出. However, LLMs are known to exhibit幻觉hallucination，生成看似合理但实际上不正确或无意义的输出，特别是在技术和特定领域的设置中, generating plausible yet factually incorrect or nonsensical outputs, particularly in technical and domain-specific settings(Rawte et al., 2023; Ji et al., 2023; Huang et al., 2025)。这个问题激发了人们大量的研究工作，以了解幻觉的根本原因. This issue has motivated substantial research efforts both in understanding the underlying causes of hallucination(Huang et al., 2025; McKenna et al., 2023)并针对其发生制定缓解策略and in developing mitigation strategies against its occurrence(Ji et al., 2023)。 在具有重大现实世界意义的科学和工程领域，例如核物理和核工程，正确可靠的反应通常不仅需要事实回忆，还需要精确的定量推理和对领域概念的清晰理解。这些上下文中的错误，无论是由于幻觉、公式和计算的错误应用，还是浅薄的语义理解，都会严重限制大型语言模型的实际用途。简而言之，当涉及技术主题时，如果法学硕士无法提供准确且可验证的信息，则会存在重大风险。. In scientific and engineering domains with significant real-world implications, such as nuclear physics and nuclear engineering, correct and reliable responses often require not only factual recall but also precise quantitative reasoning and a clear understanding of the domain concepts. Errors in these contexts, whether due to hallucination, incorrect application of formulae and calculations, or shallow semantic understanding, can significantly limit the practical utility of large language models. In short, when it comes to technical topics, there is significant risk if LLMs fail to provide accurate and verifiable information.

为了验证大型语言模型的性能和可靠性，研究人员开发了基准To validate the performance and reliability of large language models, researchers have developed benchmarks(Liang et al., 2022; Chang et al., 2024)。基准通常由旨在评估模型行为的特定方面的任务组成，每个任务旨在针对法学硕士响应行为的某个方面，包括复杂的上下文理解、多步骤逻辑推理和定量计算. Benchmarks typically consist of tasks designed to evaluate specific aspects of model behavior, each aiming to target a certain aspect of the response behavior of the LLMs, including complex contextual understanding, multi-step logical reasoning, and quantitative calculation(Hendrycks et al., 2020; Srivastava et al., 2023)。 此类基准可以对核心法学硕士能力进行系统评估。 然而，评估高技术和专业领域的法学硕士表现的一个核心挑战是缺乏高质量、可扩展的基准。虽然许多现有基准已经评估一般推理和领域知识，但它们往往无法捕捉技术实践所需的独特方法. Such benchmarks enable systematic assessment of core LLM capabilities. However, a central challenge in assessing LLM performance in highly technical and specialized domains has been the lack of high-quality, scalable benchmarks. While many existing benchmarks already evaluate general reasoning and domain knowledge, they often fall short in capturing the unique approaches required in technical practice(Huang et al., 2025)。有些主要关注数学问题的解决，没有特定领域的设置. Some focus primarily on mathematical problem solving without domain specific settings(Cobbe et al., 2021)，而其他人则依赖专家策划的小型数据集，这些数据集构建成本高昂且难以扩展, while others rely on small, expert-curated datasets that are costly to construct and difficult to scale(Singh and Singh, 2025)。在核工程领域，之前构建评估基准的工作有限. In the nuclear engineering domain, there has been limited prior work on constructing a benchmark for evaluation(Acharya et al., 2023)。它提供了一个重要的起点，但规模有限，并且需要大量的人力来创建和评估数据集。. It provides an important starting point, but is limited in size and requires substantial human effort for both dataset creation and evaluation.

这凸显了对可扩展、特定领域基准的需求，这些基准可以捕获高科技领域的概念理解和定量推理。在这项工作中，我们引入了 NuclearQAv2，这是一个新的基准，旨在通过结构化和可扩展的方法评估法学硕士的核工程知识。该基准由问答（QA）对形式的任务组成，用于评估法学硕士理解领域知识和执行相关技术执行的能力。我们设计的一个关键方面是使用多种问题类型来捕获语言模型领域能力的不同方面。具体来说，该基准由三类问答任务组成：（i）评估主题事实基础的布尔（是/否）问题，（ii）需要更复杂的多步骤逻辑演绎、计算和定量推理的数字问题，以及（iii）通过简短答案测试概念理解的口头问题。这三个类别旨在分别测试不同的能力，并共同提供比单一格式基准更全面的模型能力评估。This highlights the need for scalable, domain-specific benchmarks that can capture both conceptual understanding and quantitative reasoning in highly technical fields. In this work, we introduce NuclearQAv2, a new benchmark designed to evaluate LLMs on nuclear engineering knowledge through a structured and scalable approach. The benchmark consists of tasks in the form of question and answer (QA) pairs to assess the ability of LLMs to understand the domain knowledge and carry out related technical execution. A key aspect of our design is the use of multiple question types to capture different facets of domain competence of language models. Specifically, the benchmark consists of three categories of question answering tasks: (i) boolean (yes/no) questions that assess factual grounding of the subject, (ii) numeric questions that require more complex multi-step logical deduction, calculation, and quantitative reasoning, and (iii) verbal questions that test conceptual understanding through short-form answers. The three categories are designed to test different capabilities separately, and collectively provide a more comprehensive evaluation of model capabilities than single-format benchmarks.

为了实现可扩展的基准构建，我们开发了一个混合管道，它结合了人工编写的问题、现有数据以及从特定领域的文本语料库中自动生成的问答对。我们利用文档解析工具从技术材料中提取结构化文本，并使用大型语言模型在精心设计的提示约束下生成候选问答对。这种方法使我们能够有效地扩大数据集覆盖范围，同时保持对问题质量和相关性的控制。生成的 NuclearQAv2 数据集包含大约 1,240 个跨越三种问题类型的 QA 对。 另一方面，跨这些异构任务对模型输出进行有效评估会带来额外的挑战。虽然可以使用简单的准确性指标（对数字变化的容忍度）来评估布尔和数字问题，但口头回答需要处理语言变异性，例如同义词和释义。为了解决这个问题，我们采用基于 LLM 的口头问题评估方案，使用结构化提示方法来近似生成的答案和引用的答案之间的语义等价性。这使得能够自动评估模型响应和计算基准指标。 我们针对 NuclearQAv2 评估了几种流行的大型语言模型，并观察它们在问题类型中的表现。特别是，语言模型往往在布尔问题上获得更高的准确性，而在复杂的数字任务上的性能仍然较低，并且语言问题的性能因模型而异。这些结果凸显了多方面评估对于了解核工程法学硕士的优势和局限性的重要性。To enable scalable benchmark construction, we have developed a hybrid pipeline that combines human-authored questions, existing data, and automatically generated question-answer pairs derived from domain-specific textual corpora. We leverage document parsing tools to extract structured text from technical materials and employ large language models to generate candidate QA pairs under carefully designed prompting constraints. This approach allows us to efficiently expand dataset coverage while maintaining control over question quality and relevance. The resulting NuclearQAv2 dataset contains approximately 1,240 QA pairs spanning the three question types. On the other hand, efficient evaluation of model outputs across these heterogeneous tasks introduces additional challenges. While boolean and numeric questions can be assessed using straightforward accuracy metrics (with tolerance for numerical variation), verbal responses require handling linguistic variability such as synonyms and paraphrases. To address this, we adopt an LLM-based evaluation scheme for verbal questions, using a structured prompting approach to approximate semantic equivalence between generated and referenced answers. This enables automatic evaluation of model responses and computation of benchmark metrics. We evaluate several popular large language models against NuclearQAv2 and observe their performance across question types. In particular, language models tend to achieve higher accuracy on boolean questions, while performance on the complex numeric tasks remains substantially lower, and verbal question performance varies depending on the model. These results highlight the importance of multi-faceted evaluation in understanding the strengths and limitations of LLMs in nuclear engineering.

2 相关作品Related Works

越来越多的著作探索了使用特定领域基准对专业学科法学硕士的评估。这些基准旨在评估通用数据集之外的领域知识，通常依赖于专家策划的问答对和来自现实世界实践的任务。著名的例子包括医学 QA 基准，例如 MedQA 和 PubMedQAA growing body of works have explored the evaluation of LLMs in specialized subjects using domain-specific benchmarks. These benchmarks aim to assess domain knowledge beyond general-purpose datasets, often relying on expert-curated question–answer pairs and tasks derived from real-world practice. Notable examples include medical QA benchmarks such as MedQA and PubMedQA(Singhal et al., 2023)、LegalBench等法律推理基准, legal reasoning benchmarks such as LegalBench(Guha et al., 2023)，以及专家编写的专业推理数据集，例如 ExpertQA, and expert-authored datasets on professional reasoning such as ExpertQA(Malaviya et al., 2024)和 PRBenchand PRBench(Akyürek et al., 2025)，其中包含由领域专业人员设计的任务和评估标准。 在核工程领域，与 NuclearQAv2 最接近的工作是 2017 年开发的 NuclearQA 基准测试。, which incorporate tasks and evaluation criteria designed by domain professionals. (2023)。这项工作引入了一个基准，由人类专家针对核主题精心设计的 100 个 QA 对组成，涵盖多种问题类型。它既包括主要评估事实知识的简答题，也包括需要额外推理能力的开放式长答题。问题分为不同类型，包括数字问题和科学问题，或两者的组合。该基准的评估需要人工输入，并且针对每项任务都有一个从正确到无意义响应的五层记分卡。 虽然这些努力产生了高质量的基准数据，但它们通常规模较小且构建成本昂贵，限制了它们的覆盖范围和可扩展性。 NuclearQA 基准测试确实如此。基准构建和评估过程中专家参与的要求限制了基准测试过程的可扩展性和可重复性。. The requirement for expert involvement during both benchmark construction and evaluation limits the scalability and reproducibility of the benchmarking process.

最近的工作还探索了使用法学硕士进行自动化数据集构建，包括从文本语料库生成合成问答对Recent works have also explored the use of LLMs for automated dataset construction, including the generation of synthetic QA pairs from textual corpora(Alberti et al., 2019; Puri et al., 2020)。这些方法可以快速扩展数据集并减少对昂贵的人工注释的依赖，但也带来了与质量控制、多样性和对源材料的忠实度相关的挑战。在实践中，生成的 QA 对的质量可能会有很大差异，需要额外的过滤或选择机制来确保有用. In contrast, real-world technical tasks often require a combination of factual recall, numerical computation, and conceptual understanding. Existing benchmarks rarely capture this combination within a single evaluation framework(Liang et al., 2022; Huang et al., 2025)。 这在技术领域能力的评估中留下了重要的空白。 除了任务设计之外，评估自由格式或简短答案的响应仍然是一个重大挑战，特别是在由于释义或同义词而导致精确字符串匹配不足的情况下. This leaves an important gap in the assessment of technical domain competence. To address this, recent works have explored the use of large language models as automated evaluators, leveraging their ability to assess semantic similarity and overall response quality(Liu et al., 2023)。虽然这种方法可以实现可扩展且灵活的评估，但它对提示设计、模型偏差和判断的可变性很敏感. While this approach enables scalable and flexible evaluation, it is sensitive to prompt design, model bias, and variability in judgment(Zheng et al., 2023)。 这些观察结果推动了评估框架的开发，该框架将自动评估与特定于任务的评分机制相结合，以支持可扩展的基准测试。. These observations motivate the development of evaluation frameworks that combine automated assessment with task-specific scoring mechanisms to support scalable benchmarking.

3.3 设计原则Design Principles

为了确保基准的质量和有用性，并促进评估过程，我们在构建 NuclearQAv2 的 QA 对时采用了几个原则。To ensure the quality and usefulness of the benchmark, and to facilitate the evaluation process, we adopt several principles when constructing the QA pairs for NuclearQAv2.

我们在构建问题时遵循五个原则： (i) 每个问题都属于三种类型（布尔型、数字型或语言型）中的一种。这是为了防止任务制定和评估的预期答案出现歧义。 (ii) 问题的设计明确需要核工程知识或推理，并避免变得过于简单或肤浅。 (iii) 每个问题的表述都应有明确且明确的答案，最大限度地减少对外部环境的依赖。这对于口头问题尤其重要，因为需要明确定义有针对性的答案，而不是笼统的答案。 (iv) 预期答案必须简洁。这使得无需人工干预即可自动评估。 (v) 最后，问题的构建应与上下文无关。这意味着无需访问原始源文本即可回答这些问题。 这些原则在手动管理 QA 对和使用章节中的法学硕士进行自动生成期间得到执行。We follow five principles when constructing questions: (i) Each question belongs to exactly one of the three types (boolean, numeric, or verbal). This is to prevent ambiguity in both task formulation and the expected answer for evaluation. (ii) The questions are designed to explicitly require nuclear engineering knowledge or reasoning, and to avoid becoming overly simple or surface-level. (iii) Each question is formulated such that there exists an unambiguous and well-defined answer, minimizing dependence on external context. This is especially crucial for verbal questions, where targeted answers need to be clearly defined instead of generic. (iv) Expected answers need to be concise. This enables automatic evaluation without human intervention. (v) Finally, the questions should be constructed to be context independent. This means they can be answered without access to the original source text. These principles are enforced during both manual curation of QA pairs and automated generation conducted using LLMs in Section4.1，通过提示设计和过滤标准。这也有利于后续的评估阶段。, through prompt design and filtering criteria. This facilitates the subsequent evaluation stage as well.

布尔、数字和语言问题类型的组合为领域科学 QA 提供了多方面的评估框架。这种结构不是将理解领域知识视为单一能力，而是可以跨不同推理模式对模型性能进行更精细的分析。 特别是，布尔问题主要测试事实记忆，数字问题强调定量推理，口头问题评估概念理解。这种分离使我们能够识别模型行为的系统差异，而这些差异在单一格式基准测试中可能不可见。The combination of boolean, numeric, and verbal question types provides a multi-faceted evaluation framework for domain-science QA. Rather than treating understanding the domain knowledge as a single capability, this structure enables more granular analysis of model performance across different reasoning modes. In particular, boolean questions primarily test factual recall, numeric questions emphasize quantitative reasoning, and verbal questions assess conceptual understanding. This separation allows us to identify systematic differences in model behavior that may not be visible in single-format benchmarks.

7 讨论Discussion

在这项工作中，我们引入了 NuclearQAv2，这是一个通过结合事实、定量和概念问答任务来评估核工程知识大型语言模型的基准。该基准包括 1,239 个 QA 对，涵盖三个不同的任务类别，包括布尔、数字和语言，旨在评估领域科学能力的互补方面。 为了支持可扩展的基准开发，我们提出并实施了一个混合构建管道，该管道结合了专家撰写的问题、现有基准数据和法学硕士辅助的特定领域技术语料库生成。我们进一步开发了一个结构化的自动化评估框架，其中包括针对特定任务的评估方法，包括基于容忍度的数字响应评分和基于法学硕士的口头答案语义评估。In this work, we have introduced NuclearQAv2, a benchmark for evaluating large language models on nuclear engineering knowledge through a combination of factual, quantitative, and conceptual question-answering tasks. The benchmark comprises 1,239 QA pairs covering three distinct task categories, including boolean, numeric, and verbal, which are designed to assess complementary aspects of domain-science competence. To support scalable benchmark development, we have proposed and implemented a hybrid construction pipeline that combines expert-authored questions, existing benchmark data, and LLM-assisted generation from domain-specific technical corpora. We further developed a structured automated evaluation framework with task-specific assessment methods, including tolerance-based scoring for numeric responses and LLM-based semantic evaluation for verbal answers.

多个法学硕士的实验结果揭示了不同任务类型的绩效存在显着差异。尽管许多模型在事实问题上表现出强大的性能，但定量推理和概念理解仍然更具挑战性。这些发现表明，单一任务格式的表现不足以表征技术领域的模型能力，并强调了本工作中提出的多方面评估框架的重要性。 此外，所观察到的评估模型之间的性能差异表明 NuclearQAv2 能够区分核工程任务中有意义的变化，因此可以作为评估语言模型的有效基准。Experimental results across several LLMs reveal substantial differences in performance across task types. While many models demonstrate strong performance on factual questions, quantitative reasoning and conceptual understanding remain considerably more challenging. These findings suggest that performance on a single task format is insufficient for characterizing model capabilities in technical domains and highlight the importance of multi-faceted evaluation frameworks as proposed in this work. Furthermore, the observed performance differences among the evaluated models indicate that NuclearQAv2 is capable of distinguishing meaningful variations in nuclear engineering tasks and therefore serves as an effective benchmark for assessing language models.

当前工作的一些局限性激发了未来的研究。用于自动生成口头任务 QA 对的提示是基于手动质量评估而设计的。未来的工作将研究更系统的方法来促进优化和质量保证。 此外，自动生成目前依赖于较旧的语言模型，这可能会限制生成的问题的质量。另一方面，基于法学硕士的口头反应评估可能对评估者的选择和提示表述很敏感，未来的工作应该将自动判断与专家的人类评估进行比较。 最后，该基准测试目前仅处理文本信息。许多科学和工程学科严重依赖多模式信息，例如图表、图形、示意图和绘图。扩展基准以纳入多模态数据是未来发展的重要方向。Several limitations of the current work motivate future research. The prompts used for automatic generation of QA pairs for verbal tasks have been engineered based on manual assessment of quality. Future work will investigate more systematic approaches to prompt optimization and quality assurance. Additionally, the automatic generation currently relies on an older language model, which may limit the quality of the questions generated. On the other hand, the LLM-based evaluation of verbal responses may be sensitive to evaluator choice and prompt formulation, and future work should compare automated judgments against expert human assessment. Finally, the benchmark currently only handles textual information. Many scientific and engineering disciplines rely heavily on multi-modal information such as diagrams, figures, schematics, and plots. Extending the benchmark to incorporate multi-modal data represents an important direction for future development.

总体而言，NuclearQAv2 既提供了评估核工程 LLM 表现的实用基准，也提供了构建特定领域评估数据集的可扩展方法。这项工作还为科学和工程应用中更高效、更严格的基准构建和自动化法学硕士评估提供了一个框架，有助于为高影响力的技术领域开发更可靠的人工智能系统。Overall, NuclearQAv2 provides both a practical benchmark for assessing LLM performance in nuclear engineering and a scalable methodology for constructing domain-specific evaluation datasets. This work has also provided a framework for more efficient and rigorous benchmark construction and automated LLM evaluation in scientific and engineering applications, contributing to the development of more reliable AI systems for high-impact technical domains.
