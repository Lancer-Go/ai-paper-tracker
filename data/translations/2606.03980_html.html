Skill-RM：通过代理技能统一异构评估标准Skill-RM: Unifying Heterogeneous Evaluation Criteria via Agent Skill

摘要Abstract

奖励模型 (RM) 为 LLM 后期培训提供关键反馈信号，特别是在强化微调 (RFT) 和强化学习 (RL) 流程中。然而，当前的奖励评估依赖于异构标准，例如基于规则的验证器、真实参考、程序检查表和复杂的规则，其中尚未探索整合所有类型证据的统一机制。 为此，我们提出了技能奖励模型（Skill-RM），这是一个统一的框架，它将奖励模型重新表述为可重复使用的奖励评估技能的执行。通过将奖励计算视为结构化代理任务，Skill-RM 提供了一致的接口来编排异构资源，动态选择和聚合根据每个输入的特定要求定制的证据。这种方法使奖励模型超越静态评估，确保不同任务的一致性和透明度。对奖励基准和下游应用程序（包括 N 中最佳选择和强化学习）的广泛实验表明，Skill-RM 始终优于传统的判断基准。我们的研究结果表明，Skill-RM 不仅为奖励建模提供了统一的解决方案，而且还通过证据的战略和动态编排实现了卓越的性能。代码位于Reward models (RMs) provide critical feedback signals for LLM post-training, notably in reinforced fine-tuning (RFT) and reinforcement learning (RL) pipelines. However, current reward evaluation rely on heterogenous criteria such as rule-based verifiers, ground-truth references, procedural checklists, and complex rubrics, where a unified mechanism to integrate all types of evidences remains unexplored. To this end, we propose Skill Reward Model (Skill-RM), a unified framework that reformulates reward modeling as the execution of a reusable Reward-Evaluation Skill. By treating reward computation as a structured agentic task, Skill-RM provides a consistent interface to orchestrate heterogeneous resources, dynamically selecting and aggregating evidence tailored to the specific requirements of each input. This approach enables the reward model to move beyond static evaluation, ensuring consistency and transparency across diverse tasks. Extensive experiments on reward benchmarks and downstream applications, including best-of-N selection and reinforcement learning, demonstrate that Skill-RM consistently outperforms traditional judge baselines. Our findings suggest that Skill-RM not only provides a unified solution for reward modeling but also achieves superior performance through the strategic and dynamic orchestration of evidence. 1 简介Introduction

奖励模型 (RM) 在大型语言模型 (LLM) 的后期训练中至关重要Reward models (RMs) are crucial in the post-training of large language models (LLMs)(bai2022training; cheng2023everyone; zeng2024diversified; genrm; agenticrewardmodeling)，用于在强化学习 (RL) 过程中引导模型实现所需行为的主要监督机制, for their primary supervisory mechanism to steer models toward desired behaviors during reinforcement learning (RL)(ouyang2022training)和强化微调（RFT）and reinforced fine-tuning (RFT)(cheng2024adversarial; reft)。在 LLM 培训后，RM 的传统用法很简单，包括重新排列候选人的响应以进行 N 中最佳选择. Traditional usage of RMs in LLM post-training is straightforward, including re-ranking candidate responses for best-of-N selection(llama2)，整理高质量的训练数据, curating high-quality training data(wizardlm; skyworkrewardv2)，充当自动法官, acting as automated judges(zheng2023judging)，并生成下游对齐的反馈信号, and generating feedback signals for downstream alignment(rafailov2023direct; zheng2023judging; rlaif; rewardbench2).

LLM推理能力的快速提升The rapid advancement of LLM capabilities in reasoning(wei2022chain), 编码, coding(codex)，以及工具的使用, and tool-use(toollm)对奖励模型提出了日益复杂的要求raises increasingly sophisticated demands on reward modeling(agenticrewardmodeling; rewardbench2; tirjudge)。因此，监督这些模型的 RM 评估标准从简单的标量偏好演变为复杂的、资源密集型的验证过程。 例如，确保事实准确性现在需要外部参考或检索证据. Consequently, the RM evaluation criteria to supervise these models evolve from simple scalar preferences into complex, resource-intensive verification processes. For instance, ensuring factual accuracy now necessitates external references or retrieved evidence(nakano2021webgpt; factscore)。数学和编码任务需要根据基本事实进行可验证的执行. Mathematical and coding tasks demand verifiable execution against ground truths(lightman2024let)。代理训练需要通过额外的工具调用来验证多步工具轨迹. Agentic training requires validating multi-step tool trajectories with additional tool calling(toollm; lu2025search; tirjudge)。安全执行通常取决于严格的约束分解或基于政策的否决. Safety enforcement often hinges on strict constraint decomposition or policy-based vetoes(bai2022constitutional; jiang2024followbench)。这些任务表明，高保真奖励分配不再是一个单一的任务，而是一个涉及异构资源的多方面过程。. These tasks demonstrate that high-fidelity reward assignment is no longer a monolithic task but a multi-faceted process involving heterogeneous resources.

然而，现有的 RM 设计很难将这种多样化的资源依赖判断整合到一个连贯的框架中。标量 RM 将复杂的、以资源为基础的证据压缩为不透明的分数，从而使评估过程从根本上变得不可解释且不灵活However, existing RM designs struggle to integrate such diversified resource-dependent judgments into a coherent framework. Scalar RMs compress complex, resource-grounded evidence into opaque scores, rendering the evaluation process fundamentally uninterpretable and inflexible(armorm)。法学硕士法官系统提供自然语言推理能力. LLM-as-a-Judge systems offer natural language reasoning capabilities(liu2023g; zheng2023judging)。他们通常依赖于非结构化、扁平化的提示，其中标题、示例和工具被连接成一个提示。这种方法使关键方面（例如资源选择、证据跟踪和信号聚合）变得隐式且不受管理。虽然最近的努力引入了以标准为条件、以标准为中心或以工具增强的评估系统. They typically rely on unstructured, flat-prompting, where rubrics, examples, and tools are concatenated into a single prompt. This approach leaves critical aspects (such as resource selection, evidence tracking, and signal aggregation) implicit and unmanaged. While recent efforts introduce criteria-conditioned, rubric-centered, or tool-augmented evaluation systems(prometheus2; verif; agenticrewardmodeling; openrs; tirjudge)，它们主要关注孤立的机制，一次仅公开一种资源模式。因此，迫切需要一个统一的、可重用的抽象，它可以无缝地合成异构资源，根据输入自适应地选择证据，并促进跨不同评估场景的明确的、基于证据的奖励计算。, they primarily focus on isolated mechanisms, exposing only one resource modality at a time. Consequently, there is a critical need for a unified, reusable abstraction that can seamlessly synthesize heterogeneous resources, adaptively select evidence based on the input, and facilitate explicit, evidence-grounded reward computation across diverse evaluation scenarios.

另一方面，代理技能提供了一种自然的方式来实例化这种缺失的抽象。在最近的代理研究中，代理技能被定义为一个独立的、基于文件系统的程序逻辑包和 LLM 代理可以按需发现、加载和执行的支持资源。On the other hand, agent skills provide a natural way to instantiate this missing abstraction. This design fundamentally transcends both flat prompting (which merely adds context) and atomic tool-use (which exposes isolated executables); instead, a skill packages the orchestration logic that determines什么what资源咨询，resources to consult,当when调用它们，并且to invoke them, and如何how他们的输出应该综合代理人的最终决定their outputs should synthesize the agent’s final decision(claude2025skillsexplained; xu2026agentskills; jiang2026agenticskills)。连贯地，我们可以将这一原则直接映射到异构评估标准上：标题、参考、约束和验证器是模块化和可加载的资源，而“奖励技能”则控制它们的调用和合成。. 3 技能奖励模型Skill Reward Model

我们建议We propose技能奖励模型Skill Reward Model（Skill-RM），一个统一的框架，将奖励模型从单一的评分任务重新表述为(Skill-RM), a unified framework that reformulates reward modeling from a monolithic scoring task into a技能介导的执行程序skill-mediated execution procedure。 Skill-RM 不是通过不透明的基于参数的评分或非结构化、单调的提示来获得奖励，而是将奖励计算视为可重用的系统执行. 局限性和未来的工作。Limitations and Future Work.

尽管 Skill-RM 很有效，但仍有一些局限性值得进一步研究。首先，我们的评估目前仅限于基于文本的指令遵循和标准奖励基准。将技能介导的表述扩展到多模式环境、长期代理任务或高度开放的主观偏好调整，提出了一个具有挑战性但充满希望的前沿领域。其次，当前的设计依赖于手动策划的奖励评估技能。虽然这种模块化确保了高精度和可解释性，但工件构建的自动化和持续、自我改进的更新机制仍然是悬而未决的问题，可以显着降低采用的障碍。第三，与传统的单遍标量模型相比，以技能为中介的调用过程引入了额外的推理开销。未来关于自适应早期停止、证据缓存和高效工件修剪的研究对于协调评估保真度和计算效率之间的权衡至关重要。Despite the effectiveness of Skill-RM, several limitations warrant future investigation. First, our evaluation is currently scoped to text-based instruction-following and standard reward benchmarks. Extending the skill-mediated formulation to multi-modal contexts, long-horizon agentic tasks, or highly open-ended, subjective preference alignment presents a challenging yet promising frontier. Second, the current design relies on manually curated Reward-Evaluation Skills. While this modularity ensures high precision and interpretability, the automation of artifact construction and the mechanisms for continual, self-improving updates remain open problems that could significantly lower the barrier to adoption. Third, the skill-mediated invocation process introduces additional inference overhead compared to traditional single-pass scalar models. Future research on adaptive early stopping, evidence caching, and efficient artifact pruning will be crucial to reconcile the trade-off between evaluation fidelity and computational efficiency.
