F2LLM-v2：包容性、高性能且高效的嵌入F2LLM-v2: Inclusive, Performant, and Efficient Embeddings
多语言世界for a Multilingual World

摘要Abstract

我们推出了 F2LLM-v2，这是一个新的通用、多语言嵌入模型系列，有 8 种不同的大小，范围从 80M 到 14B。 F2LLM-v2 基于新近整理的 6000 万个公开可用的高质量数据样本进行训练，支持 200 多种语言，特别强调以前服务不足的中低资源语言。通过将基于 LLM 的两阶段嵌入训练流程与俄罗斯套娃学习、模型剪枝和知识蒸馏技术相集成，我们提出的模型比以前的基于 LLM 的嵌入模型更加高效，同时保留了有竞争力的性能。广泛的评估证实，F2LLM-v2-14B 在 11 项 MTEB 基准测试中排名第一，而该系列中的较小型号也为资源受限的应用树立了新的技术水平。为了促进开源嵌入模型研究，我们发布了所有模型、数据、代码和中间检查点。We present F2LLM-v2, a new family of general-purpose, multilingual embedding models in 8 distinct sizes ranging from 80M to 14B. Trained on a newly curated composite of 60 million publicly available high-quality data samples, F2LLM-v2 supports more than 200 languages, with a particular emphasis on previously underserved mid- and low-resource languages. By integrating a two-stage LLM-based embedding training pipeline with matryoshka learning, model pruning, and knowledge distillation techniques, we present models that are far more efficient than previous LLM-based embedding models while retaining competitive performances. Extensive evaluations confirm that F2LLM-v2-14B ranks first on 11 MTEB benchmarks, while the smaller models in the family also set a new state of the art for resource-constrained applications. To facilitate open-source embedding model research, we release all models, data, code, and intermediate checkpoints.

1 简介Introduction

文本嵌入模型是各种人工智能应用的基础，包括语义搜索、检索增强生成 (RAG)、文本分类和聚类。通过将非结构化文本映射到密集的向量空间，这些模型允许机器捕获复杂的语义关系，从而实现跨海量数据集的高效、准确的信息检索和数据分析。该领域最近已经从基于编码器的架构转变Text embedding models serve as the fundamental backbone for a wide array of AI applications, including semantic search, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), text classification, and clustering. By mapping unstructured text into dense vector spaces, these models allow machines to capture complex semantic relationships, enabling efficient and accurate information retrieval and data analysis across massive datasets. This field has recently transitioned from encoder-based architectures(2019BERT; 2019RoBERTa; 2020XLM-R)基于解码器的 LLM 嵌入to decoder-based LLM embeddings(2025Qwen3-Embedding; 2025NV-Embed; 2025f2llm)受益于大规模预训练中获得的广泛推理和语言能力，取得了显着的性能提升。, benefiting from the extensive reasoning and linguistic capabilities acquired during large-scale pre-training and achieving remarkable gains in performance.

尽管取得了这些进步，前沿嵌入研究的现状仍存在两个显着的局限性。首先，在模型训练和基准评估中普遍存在以英语为中心的偏见。虽然 MTEB 等基准在标准化评估方面发挥了重要作用，但其中的高资源语言子集（例如英语和中文）受到了不成比例的大量关注，导致大量模型在英语中表现良好，但无法提供全球实用性。其次，研究界内部出现了透明度差距。大多数性能最佳的嵌入模型，例如 Gemini-EmbeddingDespite these advancements, the current state of frontier embedding research is characterized by two significant limitations. First, there is a pervasive English-centric bias in both model training and benchmark evaluation. While benchmarks such as MTEB have been instrumental in standardizing evaluation, the high-resource language subsets therein - such as English and Chinese - receive a disproportionately large share of attention, resulting in an abundance of models that are performant in English but fail to provide global utility. Second, a transparency gap has emerged within the research community. Most top-performing embedding models, such as Gemini-Embedding(2025Gemini-Embedding)和 Qwen3-嵌入and Qwen3-Embedding(2025Qwen3-Embedding)，作为闭源 API 或开放权重模型发布，而不公开底层训练数据或方法。这种透明度的缺乏阻碍了可重复性，并限制了我们对如何构建真正具有包容性的通用嵌入系统的集体理解。, are released either as closed-source APIs or open-weight models without disclosing the underlying training data or methodologies. This lack of transparency hinders reproducibility and limits our collective understanding of how to build truly inclusive, general-purpose embedding systems.

为了直接应对这些挑战，我们引入了 F2LLM-v2，这是一个新的通用多语言嵌入模型系列，旨在解决这些严重的不平衡问题。我们仅从公开资源中收集了一个包含 6000 万个样本的庞大、高质量的训练语料库，涵盖 282 种自然语言和 40 多种编程语言。通过优先考虑实际数据可用性而不是特定于基准的优化，我们创建了一个模型系列，该模型系列在真正的全球范围内的应用程序中表现出色，包括那些涉及服务不足的语言的应用程序。除了语言包容性之外，我们还通过提供 8 种不同的模型大小（从 80M 到 14B 参数）来解决计算包容性。通过集成俄罗斯套娃表示学习 (MRL) 和通过模型剪枝和新颖知识蒸馏增强的两阶段训练管道，我们即使在资源有限的环境中也能确保高性能。广泛的评估证实，我们的 14B 模型在 11 个 MTEB 基准上取得了最先进的结果，为多语言嵌入能力树立了新标准，而较小的模型也优于以前具有类似尺寸的前沿模型。为了营造一个开放、公平的研究环境，我们发布了 F2LLM-v2 系列的完整训练方案、中间检查点以及所有相关代码和数据，旨在推动 AI 技术迈向更具包容性的未来。To directly tackle these challenges, we introduce F2LLM-v2, a new family of general-purpose, multilingual embedding models designed to address these critical imbalances. We curate a massive, high-quality training corpus of 60 million samples spanning 282 natural languages and over 40 programming languages solely from publicly available resources. By prioritizing real-world data availability over benchmark-specific optimization, we create a model family that excels across a truly global range of applications, including those involving underserved languages. Besides linguistic inclusivity, we also address computational inclusivity by providing 8 distinct model sizes, ranging from 80M to 14B parameters. By integrating Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL) and a two-stage training pipeline enhanced by model pruning and novel knowledge distillation, we ensure high performance even in resource-constrained environments. Extensive evaluations confirm that our 14B model achieves state-of-the-art results on 11 MTEB benchmarks, setting a new standard for multilingual embedding capabilities, while the smaller models also outperform previous frontier models with a similar size. To foster an open and equitable research environment, we release the complete training recipe, intermediate checkpoints, and all associated code and data for the F2LLM-v2 family, aiming to drive progress toward a more inclusive future for AI technology.

2 相关工作Related Work

上一代基于编码器的嵌入模型见证了以XLM-R为代表的支持数百种语言的大规模多语言嵌入模型的激增The previous generation of encoder-based embedding models witnessed a proliferation of massively multilingual embedding models supporting hundreds of languages, represented by XLM-R(2020XLM-R), mDeBERTaV3 (2023DebertaV3), 捷运, mBART(2020mBART)和 mT5, and mT5(2021mT5)。最近，基于解码器的嵌入模型已成为主导范式，这得益于其在大规模预训练过程中获得的广泛功能，正如 E5-Mistral 等最先进模型所验证的那样. Recently, decoder-based embedding models have become the dominant paradigm, benefiting from their extensive capabilities acquired during large-scale pre-training, as verified by state-of-the-art models such as E5-Mistral(2024E5-Mistral), NV-嵌入, NV-Embed(2025NV-Embed), Qwen3-嵌入, Qwen3-Embedding(2025Qwen3-Embedding)，和 Gemini 嵌入, and Gemini-Embedding(2025Gemini-Embedding).

然而，这一进步伴随着以英语为中心的评估的转变。这在 MTEB 中得到了证明However, this advancement has been accompanied by a shift toward English-centric evaluation. This is evidenced in MTEB(2023MTEB)，已被确立为最受认可的文本嵌入基准之一，涵盖 500 多个评估任务和 250 多种语言, which has been established as one of the most recognized text embedding benchmarks, covering over 500 evaluation tasks and more than 250 languages(2025MMTEB)。然而，实际上，MTEB 排行榜表现出明显的语言偏见。例如，在 MTEB-多语言基准测试中，131 项任务中有 35 项专门关注英语，这可能掩盖了模型真正的多语言功效。此外，与英语或多语言基准相比，许多特定语言基准受到的关注要少得多。举一个极端的例子，在我们提交模型之前，波兰 MTEB 基准测试只有一个具有完整结果的模型。. Yet, in reality, the MTEB leaderboards exhibit significant linguistic bias. For instance, in the MTEB-Multilingual benchmark, 35 out of the 131 tasks focus exclusively on English, potentially obscuring a model’s true multilingual efficacy. Furthermore, many language-specific benchmarks receive disproportionately less attention compared with the English or Multilingual benchmarks. As an extreme example, the Polish MTEB benchmark had only a single model with complete results before our models were submitted.

3 F2LLM-v2

3.1 训练数据Training Data

F2LLM-v2 的基石是庞大且多样化的培训语料库的汇编，旨在培养语言包容性和广泛的任务能力。我们汇总了来自 157 个公开来源的数据，创建了涵盖 282 种自然语言（由 ISO-639-3 代码标识）和 40 多种编程语言的 6000 万个训练样本集合。至关重要的是，我们的数据管理流程是由现实世界的数据可用性驱动的，而不是针对特定基准进行优化。例如，我们的数据集包含西班牙语、阿拉伯语、意大利语、印度尼西亚语和葡萄牙语的大量数据（图A cornerstone of F2LLM-v2 is the compilation of a vast and diverse training corpus designed to foster both linguistic inclusivity and broad task competency. We aggregate data from 157 publicly available sources, creating a collection of 60 million training samples that span 282 natural languages (as identified by ISO-639-3 codes) and over 40 programming languages. Crucially, our data curation process is driven by real-world data availability rather than optimizing for specific benchmarks. For instance, our dataset contains substantial data for Spanish, Arabic, Italian, Indonesian, and Portuguese (Figure2），尽管这些语言缺乏 MTEB 中的专用基准。这种方法还包括低资源语言的长尾和大量的代码，旨在构建一个具有真正全局实用性的模型，并与最近的开源数据集（例如 KaLM-Embedding 发布的数据集）形成鲜明对比), despite these languages lacking dedicated benchmarks in MTEB. This approach, which also includes a long tail of low-resource languages and a significant volume of code, aims to build a model with truly global utility and stands in direct contrast to recent open-source datasets such as the one released by KaLM-Embedding(2025KaLM-Embedding-V2)，严重偏向英文和中文（图, which is heavily skewed towards English and Chinese (Figure3）。我们在附录中提供了更全面的数据集语言细分). We provide a more comprehensive linguistic breakdown of our dataset in AppendixLABEL:appendix:data.

我们数据集的功能多样性对于训练通用嵌入模型同样重要。如图The functional diversity of our dataset is equally critical for training a general-purpose embedding model. As shown in Figure4，我们的集合涵盖了广泛的任务，从以检索为中心的问答和双文本挖掘到面向分类的情感分析和意图/领域分类。, our collection encompasses a wide spectrum of tasks, ranging from retrieval-focused question answering and bitext mining to classification-oriented sentiment analysis and intent/domain classification.

为了在统一的对比学习框架中利用这种异质性，我们遵循第一代 F2LLMTo leverage this heterogeneity within a unified contrastive learning framework, we follow the first generation of F2LLM(2025f2llm)并将所有数据合并为三种规范格式：and consolidate all data into three canonical formats:检索、聚类和双向分类retrieval, clustering, and two-way classification。这种整合允许模型通过跨不同数据源和任务结构优化单个一致的目标来学习通用的嵌入空间。对于检索格式，数据由（查询、肯定文档、硬否定）元组组成。我们利用批内负例（其中小批量中的其他文档充当负例），并明确提供硬负例（使用 Qwen3-Embedding-8B 挖掘）来创建具有挑战性且高效的训练信号。对于还摄取多类分类任务的聚类格式，元组是通过对锚点、同一类的正例和不同类的硬负例进行采样而形成的。最后，双向分类格式直接使用类标签，其中给定文本作为锚点，对应的标签文本为正例，相反的标签文本为负例。对于聚类和分类，仅使用硬阴性以避免从批次样本中引入假阴性。. This consolidation allows the model to learn a versatile embedding space by optimizing a single, consistent objective across disparate data sources and task structures. For the retrieval format, data consists of (query, positive document, hard negatives) tuples. We leverage both in-batch negatives, where other documents in a mini-batch serve as negatives, and explicitly provided hard negatives (mined using Qwen3-Embedding-8B) to create a challenging and efficient training signal. For the clustering format, which also ingests multi-class classification tasks, tuples are formed by sampling an anchor, a positive example from the same class, and a hard negative from a different class. Finally, the two-way classification format directly uses class labels, where a given text serves as the anchor, the corresponding label text is the positive, and the opposite label text is the negative. For both clustering and classification, only hard negatives are utilized to avoid introducing false negatives from in-batch samples.
