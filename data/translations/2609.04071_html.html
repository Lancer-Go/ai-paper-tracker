1 简介Introduction

数字病理学已经从特定任务的卷积管道迅速转向大型预训练视觉和视觉语言基础模型。 UNI、CONCH、Virchow、Prov-GigaPath、CHIEF、Hibou、GPFM 和 PathOrchestra 等模型已证明，大规模自监督、弱监督或多模式预训练可以在癌症分类、生物标志物预测、检索、预后和其他下游任务中产生可重复使用的组织病理学表示Digital pathology has moved rapidly from task-specific convolutional pipelines toward large pretrained vision and vision–language foundation models. Models such as UNI, CONCH, Virchow, Prov-GigaPath, CHIEF, Hibou, GPFM, and PathOrchestra have demonstrated that large-scale self-supervised, weakly supervised, or multimodal pretraining can produce reusable histopathology representations across cancer classification, biomarker prediction, retrieval, prognosis, and other downstream tasks[6, 19, 28, 30, 29, 23, 20, 32]。然而，正在形成的共识并不是单一的基础模型都完全优越。独立基准测试显示出显着的任务依赖性、队列依赖性、对适应策略的敏感性以及外部评估下的变化. The emerging consensus, however, is not that a single foundation model is uniformly superior. Independent benchmarking has shown substantial task dependence, cohort dependence, sensitivity to adaptation strategy, and variation under external evaluation[5, 16, 24, 2]。这使得部署效率和可靠性以及原始预测性能变得越来越重要。. This makes deployment efficiency and reliability increasingly important alongside raw predictive performance.

当代病理学基础模型的计算成本尤其重要。从系统的角度来看，这种不匹配在整个幻灯片规模上会被放大。富含组织的 WSI 可以在诊断放大倍率下生成数千到数万个候选图块，并且在幻灯片级聚合或下游决策逻辑之前重复调用编码器。因此，每个补丁变压器的计算量减少了三分之一，从而在整个 WSI 中实现复合，而不是仅节省单个前向传递。这一观察结果促进了补丁编码器本身的特定于任务的重组，而不是将效率分析限制于可训练的下游参数的数量。 Virchow2使用ViT-H/14尺度编码器，大约有632M参数The computational cost of contemporary pathology foundation models is particularly relevant. From a systems perspective, this mismatch is amplified at whole-slide scale. A tissue-rich WSI may generate thousands to tens of thousands of candidate tiles at diagnostic magnification, and the encoder is invoked repeatedly before slide-level aggregation or downstream decision logic. A reduction of one third in per-patch transformer compute therefore compounds across the entire WSI rather than saving only a single forward pass. This observation motivates task-specific restructuring of the patch encoder itself, rather than limiting efficiency analysis to the number of trainable downstream parameters. Virchow2 uses a ViT-H/14-scale encoder with approximately 632M parameters[28]，而其他领先型号也同样大。整张幻灯片应用程序会加重这种负担，因为单张幻灯片可能会生成数百或数千个补丁。即使大型编码器被冻结，其存储的参数和前向传递操作在推理时仍然存在。因此，参数高效的微调降低了优化成本，但不一定减少驻留编码器或补丁级推理负担。最近的医学图像研究越来越认识到轻量级设计是与参数高效适应不同的问题, while other leading models are similarly large. Whole-slide applications amplify this burden because a single slide may generate hundreds or thousands of patches. Even when a large encoder is frozen, its stored parameters and forward-pass operations remain present at inference. Parameter-efficient fine-tuning therefore reduces optimization cost but does not necessarily reduce the resident encoder or patch-level inference burden. Recent medical-image studies have increasingly recognized lightweight design as a distinct problem from parameter-efficient adaptation[15, 26, 12]。在计算病理学中，SUDA 通过将重型基础模型提炼为轻量级学生来明确解决这个问题. In computational pathology, SUDA explicitly targets this problem by distilling heavy foundation models into lightweight students[34]。蒸馏可能非常有效，但它改变了模型族，需要师生转移程序，并且可能需要针对新任务或领域重复进行。这就留下了一个重要的补充问题：. Distillation can be highly effective, but it changes the model family, requires a teacher–student transfer procedure, and may need to be repeated for new tasks or domains. This leaves an important complementary question:对于特定的下游任务，实际上需要多少已经预训练的大型病理学编码器？how much of an already pretrained large pathology encoder is actually necessary for a specific downstream task?

通用视觉研究表明，沿变压器深度和令牌序列内都可能存在大量冗余。结构化稀疏和令牌修剪方法通过删除对目标任务贡献不大的块、令牌或交互来减少计算Generic vision research suggests that substantial redundancy can exist both along transformer depth and within the token sequence. Structured sparsification and token-pruning approaches reduce computation by removing blocks, tokens, or interactions that contribute little to a target task[18, 31, 3, 1, 21]。然而，病理学基础模型并不是普通的 ImageNet 分类器。组织病理学鉴别证据可能占据较小的组织区域，随放大倍数和形态的不同而变化，并且对于低患病率类别尤其稀疏。因此，从 FLOP 的角度来看，激进的修剪看起来很有吸引力，但可能会抹掉诊断上有用的证据。期望的目标不仅仅是最大压缩，而是经过验证的帕累托操作点，可以保留任务效用，同时降低真正的编码器成本。设计理念类似于最近以效率为导向的视觉架构，明确优化参数和计算的准确性. Nevertheless, pathology foundation models are not ordinary ImageNet classifiers. Histopathological discriminative evidence may occupy small tissue regions, vary across magnification and morphology, and be especially sparse for low-prevalence classes. Aggressive pruning that looks attractive from a FLOP perspective can therefore erase diagnostically useful evidence. The desired objective is not maximum compression alone, but a validated Pareto operating point that preserves task utility while reducing genuine encoder cost. The design philosophy is similar to recent efficiency-oriented vision architectures that explicitly optimize accuracy against parameters and compute[22]，但这里的问题是通过重建预训练的病理学基础模型来解决的，而不是从头开始设计新的主干。, but here the problem is addressed by restructuring a pretrained pathology foundation model rather than designing a new backbone from scratch.

仅效率评估的第二个限制是，压缩的医学模型可以保留准确性，但会以聚合准确性无法揭示的方式变得不太可信。校准误差、负对数似然、Brier 评分、基于置信度的故障检测和选择性风险量化预测概率是否对决策支持有用的不同方面A second limitation of efficiency-only evaluation is that a compressed medical model can retain accuracy while becoming less trustworthy in ways that aggregate accuracy does not reveal. Calibration error, negative log-likelihood, Brier score, confidence-based failure detection, and selective risk quantify different aspects of whether predicted probabilities are useful for decision support[10, 25, 9]。最近的病理学工作同样强调了领域的稳健性、公平性以及对非生物扫描仪或实验室变异的脆弱性. Recent pathology work has likewise emphasized domain robustness, fairness, and vulnerability to non-biological scanner or laboratory variation[11, 14, 13]。因此，计算病理学中可信的压缩研究不仅应该确定准确性和计算减少，还应该确定不确定性是否仍然具有信息性、少数类别是否被明确表征以及锁定模型是否推广到独立队列。. Thus, a credible compression study in computational pathology should establish not only accuracy and compute reduction but also whether uncertainty remains informative, minority classes are characterized explicitly, and the locked model generalizes to an independent cohort.

TAP路径TAP-Path从预训练的 Virchow2 编码器开始，执行仅验证结构分析，以识别变压器块的任务相关子集。与冻结或屏蔽不同，选定的块物理保留在部署的编码器中，而未选定的块则被删除。然后，结构压缩模型与输入自适应标记修剪配对，保留前缀标记并使用组合的类标记相似性和特征量值分数保留与任务最相关的补丁标记。为了补偿因缩短深度和令牌序列而造成的信息损失，begins from the pretrained Virchow2 encoder and performs validation-only structural analysis to identify a task-relevant subset of transformer blocks. Unlike freezing or masking, selected blocks are physically retained in the deployed encoder while unselected blocks are removed. The structurally compressed model is then paired with input-adaptive token pruning that preserves the prefix token and retains the most task-relevant patch tokens using a combined class-token similarity and feature-magnitude score. To compensate for information loss from shortening both depth and token sequence,TAP路径TAP-Path从保留的层次结构中的四个位置收集多深度特征，并在分类之前通过学习的软门将它们融合。该模型完全是根据训练和验证数据选择的；内部测试集和 CPTAC 外部队列仅在配置锁定后进行评估。最终的单头模型使用三个随机种子进行确认，并通过精度、平衡精度、宏 F1、稀有类平衡精度、校准、概率损失、故障检测 AUROC、运行时间、参数、分析 FLOP 和冻结外部性能进行评估。collects multi-depth features from four locations in the retained hierarchy and fuses them through a learned soft gate before classification. The model is selected entirely on training and validation data; the internal test set and CPTAC external cohort are evaluated only after configuration locking. The final single-head model is confirmed with three random seeds and evaluated with accuracy, balanced accuracy, macro-F1, rare-class balanced accuracy, calibration, probabilistic losses, failure-detection AUROC, runtime, parameters, analytical FLOPs, and frozen external performance.

2.3 变压器稀疏化Transformer sparsification

Transformer 架构提供至少两个互补的冗余轴：深度和令牌。结构化方法删除层、头或通道，而令牌修剪方法则动态减少序列长度。重新审视了特定于视觉的标记修剪以进行密集预测The transformer architecture offers at least two complementary axes of redundancy: depth and tokens. 2.4 可靠性和选择性预测Reliability and selective prediction

Together, these measures provide complementary evidence of probability quality, error awareness, selective-prediction behavior, and reliability under external evaluation. Their clinical interpretation requires prospective validation under the intended workflow.

5.4 令牌保留行为Token-retention behavior
