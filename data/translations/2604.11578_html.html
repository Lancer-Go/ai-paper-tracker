摘要Abstract

基于测量的量子计算（MBQC）是一种量子信息处理框架，其中计算任务是通过对高度纠缠的资源状态进行单量子位测量来执行的。由于量子测量结果的不确定性，这些操作的随机结果如果不加以纠正，就会产生变分量子通道族。传统上，这种随机性是通过经典处理来纠正的，以确保确定性的单一计算。最近，引入了基于变分测量的量子计算（VMBQC），以利用这种测量引起的随机性在生成建模中获得优势。这种方法的局限性在于，相应的通道模型的参数数量是单一模型的两倍，缩放比例为Measurement-based quantum computation (MBQC) is a framework for quantum information processing in which a computational task is carried out through one-qubit measurements on a highly entangled resource state. Due to the indeterminacy of the outcomes of a quantum measurement, the random outcomes of these operations, if not corrected, yield a variational quantum channel family. Traditionally, this randomness is corrected through classical processing in order to ensure deterministic unitary computations. Recently, variational measurement-based quantum computation (VMBQC) has been introduced to exploit this measurement-induced randomness to gain an advantage in generative modeling. A limitation of this approach is that the corresponding channel model has twice as many parameters compared to the unitary model, scaling asN×DN\times D, 其中, whereNN是逻辑量子位的数量（宽度）和is the number of logical qubits (width) andDD是VMBQC模型的深度。这通常会使优化变得更加困难，并可能导致模型难以训练。在本文中，我们提出了一种受限 VMBQC 模型，该模型仅使用单个附加可训练参数将单一设置扩展到基于通道的设置。我们从数值和代数角度证明，这种最小扩展足以生成相应的单一模型无法学习的概率分布。is the depth of the VMBQC model. In this paper, we present a restricted VMBQC model that extends the unitary setting to a channel-based one using only a single additional trainable parameter. IV 结论与展望Conclusion and outlook

在这项工作中，我们研究了参考文献中介绍的基于变分测量的量子计算 (VMBQC) 生成模型框架。In this work, we investigated the variational measurement-based quantum computation (VMBQC) framework for generative modeling introduced in Ref.[13]。 VMBQC 利用 MBQC 中测量结果的内在不确定性来实现量子通道作为学习模型。具体来说，参考文献。. VMBQC leverages the intrinsic indeterminacy of measurement outcomes in MBQC to realize quantum channels as learning models. Specifically, Ref.[13]为 MBQC 中的每个量子位引入了独立的可训练参数，用于控制 MBQC 中的随机测量结果是否得到纠正。相比之下，这项工作最大限度地减少了额外训练参数的数量，同时保留了基于通道的模型相对于纯单一模型的表达优势。introduced independent trainable parameters for each qubit in the MBQC that govern whether or not random measurement outcomes in MBQC are corrected. In contrast, this work minimizes the number of additional training parameters, while preserving the expressive advantage of channel-based models over their purely unitary counterparts.

我们展示了我们的有效性We demonstrate the effectiveness of our简化的simplified通过各种学习任务，以代数和数值方式通道 VMBQC 方法，并将其与纯单一模型进行基准测试。我们的结果表明，基于通道的模型，即使仅具有单个可训练的校正概率，也可以生成单一模型无法准确学习的输出分布。重要的是，这些数值结果是稳健的：在相同范围内的（目标）模型参数的不同随机选择下，定性行为和结论保持不变，这表明观察到的优势不是特定实例的产物，而是反映了模型类的内在属性。channel VMBQC approach, both algebraically and numerically, through various learning tasks, benchmarking it against purely unitary models. Our results show that channel-based models, even when endowed with only a single trainable correction probability, can generate output distributions that cannot be accurately learned by unitary models. Importantly, these numerical results are robust: the qualitative behavior and conclusions remain unchanged under different random choices of (target) model parameters within the same range, indicating that the observed advantage is not an artifact of specific instances but reflects an intrinsic property of the model class.

此外，我们表明随机副产品的放置在确定其对输出分布的影响方面起着关键作用。通过战略性地选择哪些量子位保留副产品，人们可以利用 VMBQC 模型的通道特性，同时保持电路宽度和深度固定以及经典控制资源最少。总之，这些结果为量子生成模型提供了具体的设计原则，这些模型利用测量引起的随机性，在 MBQC 框架内只需要很少的经典控制资源。Moreover, we show that the placement of the random byproducts plays a critical role in determining their impact on the output distribution. By strategically selecting which qubits retain byproducts, one can utilize the channel characteristics of the VMBQC model while keeping the circuit width and depth fixed and the classical control resources minimal. Together, these results provide concrete design principles for quantum generative models that exploit measurement-induced randomness with very few classical control resources within the MBQC framework.
