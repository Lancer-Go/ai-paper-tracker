摘要Abstract

测试时间缩放（TTS）可靠地改善了大型语言模型的推理，但它是否转移到小型开放视觉语言模型仍不清楚。我们在 EXAMS-V（一种多语言视觉多项选择基准）上对此进行了检查，比较了自我一致性、描述然后推理与 PRM 引导束搜索以及 Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct 和 Qwen3.5-4B 中的两个事后选择器。重要的是 TTS 运行的条件，而不是搜索或验证机制。最大的因素是可解析性：早期的提示格式使许多链能够正确推理，但从未承诺回复信，而标准答案提示和引导修复步骤在很大程度上消除了这一点。更大的解码预算消除了其余部分：将每链代币限制从 1k 提高到 2k 可恢复 3.7 pp，而采样更多链（8 到 16）仅增加 0.15 pp。一旦链有完成空间，复杂的方法就几乎没有贡献：PRM 引导波束搜索以超过 8 倍的成本追踪简单的自一致性 0.39 pp，并且免训练的生成批评家和经过训练的多模态 PRM 都无法击败多数投票跨越两项政策。最大的收益反而来自于政策模型本身（Test-time scaling (TTS) reliably improves reasoning in large language models, but whether it transfers to small open vision-language models remains unclear. We examine this on EXAMS-V, a multilingual visual multiple-choice benchmark, comparing self-consistency, describe-then-reason with PRM-guided beam search, and two post-hoc selectors across Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct and Qwen3.5-4B. What matters is the conditions under which TTS runs, not the search or verification machinery. The largest factor is parseability: an early prompt format left many chains reasoning correctly yet never committing to an answer letter, which a standard answer cue and a guided repair step largely remove. A larger decoding budget removes the rest: raising the per-chain token limit from 1k to 2k recovers 3.7 pp, whereas sampling more chains (8 to 16) adds only 0.15 pp. Once chains have room to finish, elaborate methods contribute little: PRM-guided beam search trails plain self-consistency by 0.39 pp at over eight times the cost, and neither a training-free generative critic nor a trained multimodal PRM beats majority vote across both policies. The largest gain comes instead from the policy model itself (+11.4+11.4页）。我们的最佳配置在 ImageCLEF 2026 测试中达到 84.1%，在 Visual MCQ 排行榜上排名第一。pp). Our best configuration reaches 84.1% on the held-out ImageCLEF 2026 test split, ranking first on the Visual MCQ leaderboard.

1 简介Introduction

Real exam questions are a natural stress test of this combination, and the EXAMS-V benchmark[das2024examsv]涵盖十三种语言和二十种学校科目，呈现为图像中的文本面板。 2026年ImageCLEF多模态推理共享任务spans thirteen languages and twenty school subjects rendered as text-in-image panels. The 2026 ImageCLEF multimodal reasoning shared task[ImageCLEF2026, ImageCLEFMultimodalReasoningTaskOverview2026]修复了实际限制：单个 A40 GPU、最多 7B 个参数的开放权重策略以及可重复性要求。fixes the practical constraints: a single A40 GPU, open-weight policies with at most 7B parameters, and a reproducibility mandate.

6 结论Conclusion

我们研究了多语言视觉 MCQ 上开放 VLM 的测试时间扩展，将结构化搜索与完整 EXAMS-V 验证集上两种策略的平面自一致性进行比较。 TTS 提高了准确性：在 Qwen3.5-4B 上，从零射击到自一致性的完整进展跨越了 24 个 pp，但收益集中在每链代币预算上，而不是结构化搜索、更大的链计数或训练有素的选择器上：无论是生成批评家还是训练有素的 PRM 都无法在两个策略模型和两个池优势中击败多数票。分层分析证实，TTS 的收益集中在中等准确度的语言上，这些语言的政策是错误校准的而不是薄弱的，并且持续低于平均水平的阶层（波兰语、伊斯兰研究）抵制 SC，因为链集中在相同的错误答案上，没有留下多数投票来纠正的多样性。We studied test-time scaling for open VLMs on multilingual visual MCQ, comparing structured search against flat self-consistency across two policies on the full EXAMS-V validation set. TTS improves accuracy: the full progression from zero-shot to self-consistency spans over 24 pp on Qwen3.5-4B, but the gains concentrate on the per-chain token budget rather than on structured search, larger chain counts, or trained selectors: neither a generative critic nor a trained PRM beats majority vote across two policy models and two pool strengths. Stratified analysis confirms that TTS gains concentrate on mid-accuracy languages where the policy is miscalibrated rather than weak, and that persistent below-average strata (Polish, Islamic Studies) resist SC because chains converge on the same wrong answer, leaving no diversity for the majority vote to correct.

最重要的教训是，在我们的预算规模下，工程基础（解析器格式、解码预算）和策略选择主导着任何搜索或选择器方法。我们最好的配置，Qwen3.5-4B at SC-The overarching lesson is that at our budget scale, the engineering substrate (parser format, decoding budget) and the policy choice dominate any search or selector method. 7 局限性Limitations

我们的研究结果基于两个策略模型：Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct 和 Qwen3.5-4B，它们都来自同一系列，因此我们无法将小型开放 VLM 的典型行为与 Qwen 模型特有的行为区分开来。可解析性效果尤其取决于模型如何在给定的提示格式下发出答案字母，这对于其他开源 VLM 系列可能有所不同。切换策略带来的 11.4 个百分点的收益支持了我们的主张，即策略基础主导推理时间策略，但也表明第三种模型可能会改变局面；这种模式是否适用于整个家庭和 7B 以下范围尚未经过测试。完全验证成本过高的消融使用了 dev-200 子集，其Our findings rest on two policy models, Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct and Qwen3.5-4B, both from the same family, so we cannot separate behavior typical of small open VLMs from behavior specific to Qwen models. The parseability effect in particular depends on how a model emits answer letters under a given prompt format, which may differ for other open source VLM families. The 11.4 pp gain from switching policy supports our claim that the policy substrate dominates inference-time strategy, but also shows that a third model could shift the picture; whether the pattern holds across families and across the sub-7B range is untested. Ablations whose full-validation cost was prohibitive used the dev-200 subset, whose±\pm7 页威尔逊 CI 排除了排名主张，因此所有标题调查结果均基于 val-full 重新运行 (n=4,651)。分段 PRM 空值看起来是结构性的，因为 P(7 pp Wilson CIs preclude ranking claims, so all headline findings rest on val-full re-runs (n=4,651).
