In this study, we identify a fundamental limitation of that choice: non-uniform formats such as E2M1 inherently suffer from收缩偏差Shrinkage Bias，由其可表示箱的几何不对称性引起的系统负舍入误差。 我们表明，这种偏差在各层之间乘法累积，并通过随机 Hadamard 变换 (RHT) 放大，为现有基于 E2M1 的 FP4 配方中观察到的训练不稳定性提供了统一的解释。 相比之下，均匀网格（E1M2/INT4）绕过了这种网格几何误差，并更好地将 RHT 改进的存储桶利用率转化为更高的量化质量。 基于这一发现，我们提出了 UFP4，一种统一的 4 位训练方案，它将 RHT 应用于所有三个训练 GEMM，同时将随机舍入限制为, a systematic negative rounding error caused by the geometric asymmetry of their representable bins. 1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型 (LLM) 的持续扩展推动了模型功能的发展，但预训练的成本、内存占用和能耗快速增长，需要更高效的数值格式Continued scaling of Large Language Models (LLMs) has pushed the frontier of model capabilities, yet the rapidly growing cost, memory footprint, and energy consumption of pretraining demand more efficient numerical formats(Hoffmann et al., 2022; Zhao et al., 2026)。 因此，低精度训练成为降低训练成本的关键方向，FP8已被多个大型训练系统采用. Low-precision training has therefore become a key direction for reducing training cost, with FP8 already adopted by several large-scale training systems(DeepSeek-AI et al., 2024; Team et al., 2025)。 基本思想是利用现代加速器上的高吞吐量低精度矩阵乘法单元，在前向和后向传递过程中以较低精度表示激活、权重和梯度. The basic idea is to represent activations, weights, and gradients in lower precision during both forward and backward passes, leveraging the high-throughput low-precision matrix-multiply units on modern accelerators(Micikevicius et al., 2022)。 最近，NVIDIA Blackwell 等加速器公开了本机 FP4 计算路径. More recently, accelerators such as NVIDIA Blackwell expose native FP4 computation paths(NVIDIA et al., 2026)，为进一步减半精度、降低训练成本提供了新的机会。, offering a new opportunity to further halve the precision and reduce training cost.

目前的 FP4 训练接口主要是围绕 E2M1 设计的，E2M1 是一种 4 位浮点格式，具有 2 个指数位和 1 个尾数位。 例如，MXFP4Current FP4 training interfaces are largely designed around E2M1, a 4-bit floating-point format with 2 exponent bits and 1 mantissa bit. For example, MXFP4(Rouhani et al., 2023a)和NVFP4and NVFP4(NVIDIA et al., 2026)保持细粒度张量块的独立比例因子，通过不同的块大小和比例层次结构权衡动态范围、精度和硬件效率。 与 MXFP4 相比，NVFP4 通常通过更小的块和更精细的缩放设计来提供更好的训练精度。 然而，由于只有 16 个可表示的值，端到端 FP4 训练仍然具有挑战性：如果没有额外的稳定性，FP4 配方会遭受严重的收敛问题和相对于 BF16 的损失退化maintain independent scale factors for fine-grained tensor blocks, trading off dynamic range, precision, and hardware efficiency through different block sizes and scale hierarchies. Compared with MXFP4, NVFP4 typically provides better training accuracy through smaller blocks and a more refined scaling design. Nevertheless, with only 16 representable values, end-to-end FP4 training remains challenging: without additional stabilization, FP4 recipes suffer from severe convergence issues and loss degradation relative to BF16(Wang et al., 2025; Chmiel et al., 2026; NVIDIA et al., 2026)。 为了缓解这些问题，NVFP4 配方引入了随机 Hadamard 变换 (RHT) 和随机舍入 (SR) 来处理异常值并稳定梯度更新. To mitigate these problems, the NVFP4 recipe introduces the Random Hadamard Transform (RHT) and stochastic rounding (SR) to handle outliers and stabilize gradient updates(Ashkboos et al., 2024; Gupta et al., 2015)。 后续工作通过改进尺度设计、舍入或梯度估计方法、异常值控制和量化感知训练，进一步缩小 FP4 和 BF16 训练之间的差距. Subsequent work further reduces the gap between FP4 and BF16 training through improved scale design, rounding or gradient-estimation methods, outlier control, and quantization-aware training(Chen et al., 2026; Panferov et al., 2026; Cao et al., 2026; Cook et al., 2026; Li et al., 2026).

7 结论Conclusion

我们重新审视在基于 RHT 的异常值缓解下，默认使用 E2M1 进行 FP4 训练。 我们的分析表明，RHT 将量化机制从动态范围限制更改为局部分辨率限制，其中 E2M1 的不对称 RTNE bin 可能会引入收缩偏差。 这种效应在真实张量和 GEMM 诊断中是可见的，并且相同的格式依赖模式在长期密集和 MoE 训练中持续存在。 通过使用 E1M2/INT4 样式的均匀网格，将 RHT 应用于所有三个训练 GEMM，并仅在We revisit the default use of E2M1 for FP4 training under RHT-based outlier mitigation. Our analysis shows that RHT changes the quantization regime from dynamic-range-limited to local-resolution-limited, where the asymmetric RTNE bins of E2M1 can introduce Shrinkage Bias. This effect is visible in real tensor and GEMM diagnostics, and the same format-dependent pattern persists in long-run dense and MoE training. By using an E1M2/INT4-style uniform grid, applying RHT to all three training GEMMs, and using stochastic rounding only ond​YdY，与 E2M1 参考配方相比，UFP4 持续降低了 BF16 相对 FP4 损耗误差。, UFP4 consistently reduces the BF16-relative FP4 loss error compared with the E2M1 reference recipe.

我们的含义很狭窄，但可行：E2M1 应该继续适用于范围有限的工作负载，但它不应该是唯一一流的 FP4 训练格式。 未来的训练加速器应支持 E1M2/INT4 样式的统一 4 位网格作为一流的训练数据元素，从而使 UFP4 等配方能够将 RHT 后数值稳定性与本机 4 位矩阵吞吐量相结合。Our implication is narrow but actionable: E2M1 should remain available for range-limited workloads, but it should not be the only first-class FP4 training format. Future training accelerators should support E1M2/INT4-style uniform 4-bit grids as first-class training data elements, enabling recipes such as UFP4 to combine post-RHT numerical stability with native 4-bit matrix throughput.
