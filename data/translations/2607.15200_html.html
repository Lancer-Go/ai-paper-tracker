1 简介Introduction

强化学习已成为改进大型语言模型的强大工具Reinforcement learning has emerged as a powerful tool for improving large language models(Guo et al., 2025; Jaech et al., 2024)，应用程序跨越指令如下, with applications spanning instruction following(Ouyang et al., 2022a), 对齐方式, alignment(Ouyang et al., 2022b)，以及复杂的推理, and complex reasoning(Guo et al., 2025; Jaech et al., 2024)。这些方法的核心是策略梯度算法以自回归方式对组推出进行采样，并估计其在策略下的对数似然。他们成功的关键是自回归对数似然的易处理性和简单性：生成轨迹是一系列标记预测动作，对数似然是它们的乘积。. At the core of these methods, policy gradient algorithms sample group rollouts autoregressively and estimate their log-likelihoods under the policy. The key to their success is the tractability and simplicity of the autoregressive log-likelihood: the generation trajectory is a sequence of token prediction actions, and the log-likelihood is their product.

掩蔽扩散语言模型 (MDLM)Masked Diffusion Language Models (MDLMs)(Sahoo et al., 2024; Shi et al., 2024; Ou et al., 2024)提供自回归 LLM 的替代方案，用完全屏蔽序列的迭代取消屏蔽取代固定的从左到右的生成顺序offer an alternative to autoregressive LLMs, replacing the fixed left-to-right generation order with iterative unmasking from a fully masked sequence(Hoogeboom et al., 2022; Kim et al., 2025)。 这使得并行解码和灵活的生成顺序成为可能，MDLM 已被证明在文本生成、代码生成、数学推理和蛋白质序列生成方面非常有效. This enables parallel decoding and flexible generation orderings, and MDLMs have proven effective across text generation, code generation, mathematical reasoning, and protein sequence generation(Lou et al., 2024; Sahoo et al., 2024; Nie et al., 2025)。 然而，这种灵活性是有代价的：与自回归模型不同，MDLM 缺乏易于处理的对数似然估计，而是通过证据下界 (ELBO) 进行训练。 这使得将策略梯度算法适应 MDLM 变得更具挑战性。. However, this flexibility comes at a cost: unlike autoregressive models, MDLMs lack a tractable log-likelihood estimation and are instead trained via an evidence lower bound (ELBO). This makes adapting policy gradient algorithms to MDLMs significantly more challenging.

扩散语言模型。Diffusion Language Models.

用于文本生成的离散扩散模型已经沿着多个方向发展。 早期工作在连续嵌入空间中应用了扩散Discrete diffusion models for text generation have developed along several lines. Early work applied diffusion in continuous embedding space(Li et al., 2022; Gong et al., 2022)，而最近的方法直接在离散令牌空间中运行。 得分熵离散扩散 (SEDD), while more recent approaches operate directly in discrete token space. Score Entropy Discrete Diffusion (SEDD)(Lou et al., 2023)和掩蔽扩散语言模型（MDLM）and Masked Diffusion Language Models (MDLM)(Sahoo et al., 2024; Shi et al., 2024; Ou et al., 2024)建立了掩蔽扩散作为文本生成的竞争范式，并基于 ELBO 简化了训练目标。 将这些模型缩放到更大的尺寸，LLaDAestablished masked diffusion as a competitive paradigm for text generation, with simplified training objectives based on the ELBO. Scaling these models to larger sizes, LLaDA(Nie et al., 2025)和梦想and Dream(Gong et al., 2024)证明掩蔽扩散法学硕士可以在标准基准上接近自回归性能。 最近，扩散语言模型已扩展到多模式设置demonstrated that masked diffusion LLMs can approach autoregressive performance on standard benchmarks. More recently, diffusion language models have been extended to multimodal settings(Yang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025b; Swerdlow et al., 2025)，并且有一些工作通过缓存提高了推理效率, and several works have improved inference efficiency through caching(Ma et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2025a)和并行解码策略and parallel decoding strategies(Wu et al., 2025; Arriola et al., 2025).

语言模型的强化学习。Reinforcement Learning for Language Models.

基于经典策略梯度方法，强化学习已成为大型语言模型训练后的核心技术Reinforcement learning has become a central technique for post-training large language models, building on classical policy gradient methods(Williams, 1992; Sutton et al., 1999)。 早期工作将来自人类反馈的强化学习 (RLHF) 与 PPO 结合使用. Early work used RL from human feedback (RLHF) with PPO(Schulman et al., 2017)和TRPOand TRPO(Schulman et al., 2015)使模型与人类偏好保持一致to align models with human preferences(Christiano et al., 2017; Ouyang et al., 2022b)。 最近，强化学习已被应用于提高推理能力。 深度探索数学. More recently, RL has been applied to improve reasoning capabilities. DeepSeekMath(Shao et al., 2024)引入了组相对策略优化（GRPO），这是一种无批评的策略梯度方法，可以估计组推出的基线，从而无需价值网络。 DeepSeek-R1introduced Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), a critic-free policy gradient method that estimates baselines from group rollouts, removing the need for a value network. DeepSeek-R1(Guo et al., 2025)和基米K1.5and Kimi K1.5(Team et al., 2025)基于强化学习的大规模推理训练，证明具有可验证奖励的策略梯度方法可以带来强大的数学和编码性能。 随后的工作完善了 GRPO 目标，减少了偏见scaled RL-based reasoning training, demonstrating that policy gradient methods with verifiable rewards can elicit strong mathematical and coding performance. Subsequent work has refined the GRPO objective, reducing bias(Liu et al., 2025b),提高样品效率, improving sample efficiency(Cohen et al., 2025)，并引入序列级剪辑, and introducing sequence-level clipping(Zheng et al., 2025).
