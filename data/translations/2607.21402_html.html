1. 简介Introduction

脑电图 (EEG) 信号是人脑神经电活动的记录，通过放置在头皮表面的电极进行测量。由于脑电图信号揭示了大脑的物理状态，因此被广泛用于大脑功能研究、疾病诊断和脑机接口（BCI）应用，例如情绪识别Electroencephalogram (EEG) signals are recordings of the neural electrical activity in the human brain, which is measured by electrodes placed on the scalp surface. As they reveal the physical state of the brain, EEG signals are widely used for brain function research, disease diagnosis, and brain–computer interface (BCI) applications, such as emotion recognition(Liu et al., 2025; Pan et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2024), 癫痫检测, epilepsy detection(Shoeb, 2009; Zhou et al., 2025)、精神障碍诊断, mental disorder diagnosis(Zyma et al., 2019)、疲劳评估, fatigue assessment(Zheng and Lu, 2017)，以及步态预测, and gait prediction(He et al., 2018)。早期的研究主要依赖于传统的机器学习方法，需要基于专家知识的手工特征工程。随着深度学习的快速发展，已经开发了多种用于脑电图分析的深度模型。例如，脑电图网络. Early studies primarily relied on traditional machine learning methods, which required hand-crafted feature engineering based on expert knowledge. With the rapid advancement of deep learning, a variety of deep models have been developed for EEG analysis. For example, EEGNet(Lawhern et al., 2018), SPaRCNet (Jing et al., 2023), 反WR, ContraWR(Yang et al., 2021)和FFCLand FFCL(Li et al., 2022)采用卷积神经网络，而 EEGConformeremploy convolution neural network, while EEGConformer(Song et al., 2022), CNN-Transformer (Peh et al., 2022)和 ST 变压器and ST-Transformer(Song et al., 2021)介绍用于 EEG 信号建模架构的 Transformer。 尽管取得了相当大的成功，但这些方法通常是针对特定任务量身定制的，这限制了它们的泛化性，也阻碍了向新数据集或任务的迁移。introduce Transformer for architectures for EEG signal modeling. Despite their considerable success, these methods are often tailored for specific tasks, which limits their generalization and also hinders transfer to new datasets or tasks.

For instance, self-supervised foundation EEG models such as BIOT(Yang et al., 2023), 拉布拉姆, LaBraM(Jiang et al., 2024),脑电图, EEGPT(Wang et al., 2024)，和CBraMod, and CBraMod(Wang et al., 2025)在不同的脑电图数据集和任务中表现出了强大的性能和有希望的泛化能力。然而，这些进步主要转化为下游性能的提高，而没有解决表示学习的基本限制。具体来说，这些方法通常会产生低质量、同质或特定于任务的表示，而忽略了脑电图信号固有的层次动态。have demonstrated strong performance and promising generalization across diverse EEG datasets and tasks. Nevertheless, these advances primarily translate to improved downstream performance without addressing the fundamental limitations in representation learning. Specifically, these methods generally produce low-quality, homogeneous, or task-specific representations, ignoring the hierarchical dynamics inherent to EEG signals.

脑电图信号的多尺度性质是指神经信息在动态交互的过程中同时编码。The multi-scale nature of EEG signals refers to the fact that neural information is simultaneously encoded across dynamically interacting空间的spatial, 时间性的temporal, 和, and光谱spectral秤。在时间上，持续几毫秒的瞬态放电（例如癫痫尖峰）与持续数秒的慢波振荡共存。从频谱上看，不同的认知或病理状态与经典频带（δ、θ、α、β、γ）中的特征和耦合模式相关。忽视这种复杂的多尺度动态本质上会限制模型性能，导致表示既不够丰富也不能轻易转移到看不见的任务或数据集。例如，对于事件级检测（例如癫痫尖峰识别）有效的表示通常很难推广到长视野状态解码任务，例如睡眠分期或精神状态评估scales. Temporally, transient discharges lasting milliseconds (e.g., epileptic spikes) coexist with slow-wave oscillations that persist for seconds. Spectrally, different cognitive or pathological states are associated with characteristics and coupling patterns in the classical frequency bands (delta, theta, alpha, beta, gamma). Neglecting this complex, multi-scale dynamic inherently limits model performance, resulting in representations that are neither sufficiently rich nor readily transferable to unseen tasks or datasets. For instance, representations effective for event-level detection (e.g., epileptic spike recognition) often generalize poorly to long-horizon state decoding tasks, such as sleep staging or mental state assessment(Shoeb, 2009; Phan et al., 2019; Zyma et al., 2019)。 因此，如何在 SSL 中显式有效地建模和融合这些多尺度特征和交互是一个核心挑战，也是构建通用且强大的脑电图基础模型的关键机会。. Therefore, how to explicitly and effectively model and fuse these multi-scale features and interactions in a SSL represents a core challenge, and a key opportunity for building general and powerful foundation models for EEG.

为了解决这个限制，我们提出了 MSBraM，一种用于分层脑电图动态学习的多尺度结构化大脑基础模型。遵循 LaBraM 架构，MSBraM 通过两级管道在大规模数据集上进行了超过 2,400 小时的预训练。在第一阶段，多尺度神经分词器通过多尺度码本将原始脑电信号离散化为语义丰富的代码，有效地捕获从细粒度到粗粒度分辨率的多尺度模式。在第二阶段，我们引入了课程多尺度屏蔽策略，以解决不同时间尺度中固有的不同上下文依赖性。通过动态缩放学习兴趣，该策略使模型能够逐渐从局部特征学习到全局上下文。因此，MSBraM 是第一个将多尺度架构系统地集成到 EEG 信号通用 SSL 范式中的基础模型。本文的主要贡献总结如下：To address this limitation, we propose MSBraM, a multi-scale structured brain foundation model for hierarchical EEG dynamics learning. Following the LaBraM architecture, MSBraM is pre-trained on a large-scale dataset over 2,400 hours via a two-stage pipeline. In the first stage, a multi-scale neural tokenizer discretizes raw EEG signals into semantically rich codes by a multi-scale codebook, effectively capturing multi-scale patterns from fine-grained to coarse-grained resolutions. In the second stage, we introduce a curriculum multi-scale masking strategy to address the varying contextual dependencies inherent in different temporal scales. By dynamically scaling the learning interest, this strategy enables the model to learn from local features to global context gradually. As a result, MSBraM is the first foundation model to systematically integrate multi-scale architecture into a general SSL paradigm for EEG signals. 4.4. 限制和未来的工作Limitation and Future Work

尽管取得了这些令人鼓舞的结果，但仍然存在一些局限性。首先，MSBraM 目前仅在头皮脑电图记录上进行评估，其对其他神经传感方式的推广还有待检验。其次，当前的建模策略联合展平通道和时间维度以捕获跨通道依赖性，这导致计算开销增加，未充分利用脑电图空间结构，并可能限制高密度记录的可解释性和可扩展性。未来的工作将把 MSBraM 扩展到颅内脑电图，并探索更结构化、更高效的通道感知建模和多模态集成。Despite these encouraging results, several limitations remain. First, MSBraM is currently evaluated only on scalp EEG recordings, and its generalization to other neural sensing modalities has yet to be examined. Second, the current modeling strategy jointly flattens channel and temporal dimensions to capture cross-channel dependencies, which leads to increased computational overhead, underutilizes the EEG spatial structure, and can limit interpretability and scalability to higher-density recordings. Future work will extend MSBraM to intracranial EEG and explore more structured, efficient channel-aware modeling and multi-modal integration.

5. 结论Conclusion

在本文中，我们提出了 MSBraM，这是一种自我监督的大脑基础模型，专门用于捕获脑电图信号固有的多尺度动态。它利用具有多尺度密码本的多尺度神经分词器和课程屏蔽策略来学习丰富且可转移的多尺度表示，这些表示共同捕获跨多个时间分辨率的局部模式和远程全局上下文依赖性，从而在不同的脑电图解码任务中实现稳健且一致的性能。对涵盖 10 个代表性脑电图解码任务的 12 个公共数据集的广泛评估表明，MSBraM 在大多数基准测试中实现了最先进或极具竞争力的性能，凸显了其强大的泛化能力和对异构脑电图分析场景的适应性。In this paper, we propose MSBraM, a self-supervised brain foundation model explicitly designed to capture the inherent multi-scale dynamics of EEG signals. It leverages a multi-scale neural tokenizer with multi-scale codebooks, and a curriculum masking strategy to learn rich and transferable multi-scale representations that jointly capture local patterns and long-range global contextual dependencies across multiple temporal resolutions, enabling robust and consistent performance across diverse EEG decoding tasks. Extensive evaluations on 12 public datasets covering 10 representative EEG decoding tasks demonstrate that MSBraM achieves state-of-the-art or highly competitive performance on most benchmarks, highlighting its strong generalization capability and adaptability to heterogeneous EEG analysis scenarios.
