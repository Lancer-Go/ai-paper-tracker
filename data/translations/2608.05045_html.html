1 简介Introduction

近年来，在专家混合 (MoE) 等架构创新的推动下，大型语言模型 (LLM) 的性能取得了显着的飞跃Recent years have witnessed remarkable performance leaps in Large Language Models (LLMs), driven by architectural innovations like Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)(Cai et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2024)以及高级推理技术，例如思想链 (CoT)and advanced reasoning techniques such as Chain-of-Thought (CoT)(Wei et al., 2022)和可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）and Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)(Su et al., 2025; Mroueh, 2025)。这些进步显着增强了模型的推理能力，使其能够在数学推理、代码生成和因果理解等下游任务中实现强大的性能. These advances have significantly enhanced the reasoning capabilities of models, enabling them to achieve strong performance across downstream tasks such as mathematical reasoning, code generation, and causal understanding(Liu et al., 2024; Grattafiori et al., 2024; Achiam et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2026).

然而，法学硕士仍面临众多安全挑战However, LLMs still face numerous safety challenges(Shanghai AI Lab, 2025; Purpura et al., 2025; Qi et al., 2024a; Lin et al., 2025; Qi et al., 2026)。超越固有的 推理过程中的漏洞，例如安全深度不够 对准. Beyond the inherent vulnerabilities during inference, such as insufficient depth in safety alignment(Qi et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2026b; Wu et al., 2026)以及容易受到无法反驳的误导性提示的影响 有毒输入and susceptibility to misleading prompts that fail to counter toxic input(Melamed et al., 2024; Li et al., 2026; Zeng et al., 2026; He et al., 2026a, b)，他们在面对时也表现出明显的弱点 恶意人工智能训练师, they also exhibit significant weaknesses when confronted with malicious AI trainers(Huang et al., 2024b)。研究表明，少量的有毒训练 数据可以完全打破模型原有的安全一致性. Research has shown that a small amount of toxic training data can completely break a model’s original safety alignment(Wei et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2026).

现有的微调防御方法主要是针对微调即服务（FTaaS）范式而设计的Existing fine-tuning defense methods are primarily designed for the fine-tuning-as-a-service (FTaaS) paradigm(Huang et al., 2024b; Weng et al., 2025; Li et al., 2026; Zhang et al., 2026a; Wen et al., 2026)因此，与此处研究的提供商控制的 PPOW 发布设置不直接匹配，其中大多数权重在发布后仍可训练，而一小部分安全关键子集必须在提供商端强化下保持受保护and therefore do not directly match the provider-controlled PPOW release setting studied here, in which most weights remain trainable after release while a small safety-critical subset must remain protected under provider-side hardening(Qi et al., 2024b)。在相同的发布假设下，我们专注于低预算下游 SFT； FTaaS 可以被视为一种特殊情况，其中更强的提供商方控制使得受保护子集假设更容易满足。目前的防御方法可以分为三种类型. We focus on low-budget downstream SFT under this same release assumption; FTaaS can be viewed as a special case in which stronger provider-side control makes the protected-subset assumption easier to satisfy. Current defense approaches can be categorized into three types(Huang et al., 2024b)：基于对齐的方法: alignment-based methods(Rosati et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2024c, a)、微调阶段方法, fine-tuning stage methods(Bianchi et al., 2023; Zong et al., 2024)，以及后微调阶段方法, and post-fine-tuning stage methods(Casper et al., 2024; Yi et al., 2024)。对齐阶段防御，例如疫苗. Alignment stage defenses, such as Vaccine(Huang et al., 2024c)，只能防御特定类型的数据攻击，在更强的下游适配下可能会减弱。微调舞台防御，例如 SafeInstr, can only defend against specific types of data attacks and may weaken under stronger downstream adaptation. Fine-tuning stage defenses, like SafeInstr(Bianchi et al., 2023)，要求培训师使用特定的培训框架；然而，在这种 PPOW 设置中，下游微调器可以简单地为暴露的权重选择不同的训练管道。后期微调阶段防御，例如SafetyLock, require the trainer to use a specific training framework; however, in this PPOW setting, a downstream fine-tuner may simply choose a different training pipeline for the exposed weights. Post-fine-tuning stage defenses, such as SafetyLock(Zhu et al., 2024)，要求用户在训练后向安全方向执行激活转向，一旦模型发布就不能依赖。因此，这些方法有一个共同的局限性：它们取决于下游是否遵守额外的安全程序, require users to perform activation steering toward safe directions after training, which cannot be relied upon once the model is released. These methods therefore share a common limitation: they depend on downstream compliance with additional safety procedures(Huang et al., 2024b)。这激发了内置的安全机制，该机制在发布后仍保持活动状态，并减少对用户合作的依赖。. This motivates a built-in safety mechanism that remains active after release and reduces reliance on user cooperation.

为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了一种新颖的单向安全门（USG），这是一种用于 PPOW 设置的机制，而不是完全产品化的强化工件。受到零空间理论的启发To address this challenge, we propose a novel Unidirectional Safety Gate (USG), a mechanism intended for the PPOW setting rather than a fully productized hardening artifact. Inspired by null-space theory(Fang et al., 2024)，我们的 USG 可以作为插件模块插入到预训练模型中。在下游微调过程中，它充当选择性安全过滤器：对于触发有害行为的输入，其隐藏状态被投射到受保护的零空间区域。根据经验，非参与但分布 有害样本也往往表现出比安全样本更低的范数比值，这为防御者提供了选择释放时间阈值的空间，该阈值将阻止覆盖范围扩展到零空间构建集之外。这种机制可以防止梯度从被阻止的有害样本传播到模型的早期层，从而减少下游 SFT 期间的有害模式采集。, our USG can be inserted as a plug-in module into a pretrained model. During downstream fine-tuning, it acts as a selective safety filter: for inputs that trigger harmful behaviors, their hidden states are projected toward a protected null-space region. Empirically, non-participating but in-distribution harmful samples also tend to exhibit lower norm-ratio values than safe samples, which gives the defender room to choose a release-time threshold that extends blocking coverage beyond the null-space build set. This mechanism prevents gradient propagation from blocked harmful samples to earlier layers of the model, thereby reducing harmful pattern acquisition during downstream SFT.

Lower calibration thresholds reduce the risk of unnecessarily gating safe data but weaken harmful blocking coverage, whereas higher calibration thresholds improve harmful blocking at the cost of gating more safe data. USG should therefore be understood as a release-time calibrated threshold mechanism implemented as a backward gate, whose persistence depends on blocking coverage of harmful fine-tuning signals.

局限性Limitations

零空间的灵敏度。Sensitivity of Null Space.

零空间对输入空间敏感；即使是微小的干扰也会使其失效。因此，依赖于零空间的硬梯度阻塞，例如直接且完全向后阻塞梯度，非常容易受到输入数据的格式和形式的变化的影响。更准确地说，我们的防护措施的持久性取决于有害的训练样本是否继续被阻止。与其他表示空间防御一样，USG 依赖于为有害数据保留特定的隐藏状态几何结构。当一些有害数据超出防御语料库的覆盖范围或以其他方式逃脱阻止时，就会出现主要漏洞：那些未阻止的有害更新逐渐使用于阻止的原始零空间结构变形，并可能将有害表示推回到有效攻击的流形。基于零空间的软梯度阻塞代表了未来研究的一个有前途的方向。The null space is sensitive to the input space; even minor perturbations can render it ineffective. Therefore, hard gradient blocking that relies on the null space, e.g., directly and completely blocking gradient backward, is highly susceptible to variations in the format and form of the input data. More precisely, the persistence of our safeguard depends on whether harmful training samples continue to be blocked. Like other representation-space defenses, USG relies on preserving a specific hidden-state geometry for harmful data. The main vulnerability arises when some harmful data fall outside the coverage of the defense corpus or otherwise escape blocking: those unblocked harmful updates gradually deform the original null-space structure used for blocking and can drive harmful representations back toward an attack-effective manifold. Soft gradient blocking based on the null space represents a promising direction for future research.

对逆分支可用性的依赖。Dependence on Inverse-Branch Availability.

我们最强的前向保护主张与本文中强调的逆适配器加扩展零空间机制相关，其中实际上不存在精确的零，因此逆分支仍然为基本上所有样本定义。如果未来的部署要在更接近精确零空间限制的情况下运行，那么一些隐藏状态可能会崩溃到严格的零，并且解析逆将不再在这些位置上唯一定义。在这种情况下，为每个样本保留精确的基本模型输出将需要超出此处使用的解析逆的额外设计。Our strongest forward-preservation claim is tied to the inverse-adapter-plus-extended-null-space regime emphasized in this paper, where exact zeros are practically absent and the inverse branch therefore remains defined for essentially all samples. If a future deployment were to operate closer to the exact-null-space limit, then some hidden states could collapse to strict zero and the analytic inverse would no longer be uniquely defined at those positions. In that regime, preserving exact base-model outputs for every sample would require an additional design beyond the analytic inverse used here.

F.2 扩展零空间选择的样本限制Sample Limit for Extended Null Space Selection

对于扩展的零空间选择，通过用最小奇异值方向替换零奇异值方向来减轻上述精确的零空间简并性。因此，其样本限制不再是通过是否存在精确的对称矩阵构造来最佳定义的，而是通过在固定的安全要求下仍然可以保留多少有用的安全行为来定义。For extended null-space selection, the exact null-space degeneracy described above is alleviated by replacing zero-singular-value directions with smallest-singular-value directions. As a result, its sample limit is no longer best defined by whether the exact symmetric matrix construction exists, but rather by how much useful safe behavior can still be preserved under a fixed safety requirement.

因此，我们对扩展设置的样本限制采用了面向效用的操作定义。具体来说，我们首先确定一个构建时间阈值，对参与的有害样本强制执行 95% 的有害阻止率，然后评估由此产生的保留安全通过率。该指标提出了一个具体问题：随着更多有害样本被用来构建扩展的零空间，在维持严格的安全目标的同时，仍然可以保留多少安全数据效用？Accordingly, we adopt a utility-oriented operational definition of sample limit for the extended setting. Specifically, we first determine a build-time threshold that enforces a 95% harmful block rate on the participating harmful samples and then evaluate the resulting held-out safe pass rate. This metric asks a concrete question: as more harmful samples are used to construct the extended null space, how much safe-data utility can still be retained while maintaining a stringent safety target?

根据这个定义，更大的有害构建集并不自动更好。如果为满足构建时间 95% 有害块标准而选择的阈值导致保留安全通过率下降，则构建的可用容量将接近其极限。因此，我们使用固定 95% 安全目标下的安全通过率曲线来表征扩展零空间选择的样本限制，并将其与精确零空间选择进行比较，以确定哪种方法可以更好地延迟性能下降的发生。我们还报告了相应的保留有害数据块率，以验证较低比率趋势也足以超出构建集，足以支持对保留有害数据进行基于阈值的阻止。Under this definition, larger harmful build sets are not automatically better. If the threshold chosen to satisfy the build-time 95% harmful block criterion leads to a declining held-out safe pass rate, then the usable capacity of the construction is approaching its limit. We therefore use the safe-pass-rate curve under the fixed 95% safety target to characterize the sample limit of extended null-space selection, and compare it against exact null-space selection to determine which method better delays the onset of performance degradation. We additionally report the corresponding held-out harmful block rate to verify that the lower ratio tendency also extends beyond the build set strongly enough to support threshold-based blocking on held-out harmful data.
