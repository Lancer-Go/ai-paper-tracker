Static benchmarks cannot probe and existing interactive medical benchmarks each compromise on at least one of them. 1 简介Introduction

大型语言模型现在在医疗执照考试中接近或超过专家的表现Large language models now approach or exceed expert performance on medical licensing examinations(Singhal et al., 2023; Nori et al., 2023)和结构化临床问答and structured clinical question answering(Jin et al., 2021)。然而，真正的住院实践并不是从列举的选项中选择答案。主治医生在住院期间进行导航，其中信息不完整，必须积极寻求，决策是连续且不可逆转的，每个决策都涉及最终得到验证的药物、程序和诊断 对照患者记录的病程。静态多项选择基准无法探测这些属性中的任何一个。. Yet real inpatient practice is not the selection of an answer from enumerated options. An attending physician navigates a hospitalization in which information is incomplete and must be actively sought, decisions are sequential and irreversible, and each decision spans medications, procedures, and diagnoses that are eventually verified against the patient’s recorded course. Static multiple-choice benchmarks cannot probe any of these properties.

Medical evaluation has begun a similar shift, but existing interactive benchmarks each make a structural compromise. Data-interaction benchmarks(Lee et al., 2022; Jiang et al., 2025)将 EHR 视为可查询的数据库并对原子操作进行评分，衡量数据访问技能而不是临床推理。诊断对话基准treat the EHR as a queryable database and score atomic operations, measuring data-access skill rather than clinical reasoning. Diagnostic-conversation benchmarks(Schmidgall et al., 2024; Luo et al., 2026)依靠法学硕士模拟的患者，将基本事实与简短的考试片段结合起来，并通过法学硕士作为法官进行评分。编码式可执行基准测试rely on LLM-simulated patients, bounding ground truth to short exam vignettes and scoring via LLM-as-judge. 7 结论Conclusion

我们提出了We presented临床环境ClinEnv，它评估法学硕士作为主治医师的真实、多阶段入院情况。在其纵向住院模拟范例下，病例是根据 EHR 时间线自动构建的，临床信息会被保留，直到模型主动查询四个代理之一，并且每个决策都会与覆盖范围和成本的流程指标一起进行确定性评分。在评估的模型中，最强者仅获得适度的决策 F1，并且结果质量与模型收集信息的效率脱钩。困难集中在管理决策和后期阶段，即使覆盖范围保持不变，准确性也会崩溃，这表明临床推理，而不是信息访问，是具有约束力的约束。, which evaluates LLMs as attending physicians over real, multi-stage admissions. Under its Longitudinal Inpatient Simulation paradigm, cases are built automatically from EHR timelines, clinical information is withheld until the model actively queries one of four agents, and every decision is scored deterministically alongside process metrics for coverage and cost. Across the evaluated models, the strongest attains only modest decision F1, and outcome quality is decoupled from how efficiently models gather information. 8 局限性Limitations

因为Because临床环境ClinEnv依据每次入院实际记录的管理决策，衡量与实际临床实践的一致性，而不是针对单一的最佳答案；因此，临床上合理的替代方案可能会被评为失败。此属性是从真实 EHR 轨迹得出的任何基准所固有的，并且限制了分数的读取方式，但不影响相同固定案例上模型的相对比较。grounds every decision in the management actually recorded for each admission, it measures concordance with real clinical practice rather than against a single optimal answer; a clinically reasonable alternative may therefore score as a miss. This property is intrinsic to any benchmark derived from real EHR trajectories and bounds how scores should be read, but does not affect the relative comparison of models on the same fixed cases.

病例来自美国一家学术医疗中心，使用英文注释和美国编码和定价系统（ICD、ATC、CMS 临床实验室费用表和 NADAC）；对其他设置、语言和编码标准的推广留待未来的工作。最后，构建和一些评分步骤使用 LLM，但所有结构化基本事实（药物名称、ICD 代码）都是从 EHR 而不是从模型输出确定性导出的，这使评估目标独立于被测模型。Cases come from one US academic medical center and use English notes with US coding and pricing systems (ICD, ATC, the CMS Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule, and NADAC); generalization to other settings, languages, and coding standards is left to future work. Finally, construction and some scoring steps use LLMs, but all structured ground truth (drug names, ICD codes) is derived deterministically from EHRs rather than from model output, which keeps the evaluation targets independent of the models under test.
